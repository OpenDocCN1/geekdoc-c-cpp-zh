It follows, of course, that if the k-tape Turing machine halts on input I of length |I| in t(|I|) steps, then the one-tape emulator will halt in O(t²|I|) steps.

A k-tape Turing can easily simulate the behavior of a one-tape Turing machine. It simply ignores its extra tapes. This completes the proof that one-tape and k-tape Turing machines are equivalent.
