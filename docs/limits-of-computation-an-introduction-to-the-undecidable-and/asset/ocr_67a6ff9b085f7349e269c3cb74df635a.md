Note that a yes or no instance of VC consists of a graph and a number m. We construct the mapping by letting

|V'| = m = n + 2c

where n is the number of variables in the Boolean expression and c is the number of clauses in the expression.

A typical graph made from a Boolean expression will often have components made from the Boolean variables x₁, ..., xₙ A standard trick is to make a "bar" or "variable gadget" corresponding to each xi; a bar for xᵢ is just the two vertices x, and ¬xᵢ, connected by an edge, as in Figure 9.4.

For each of the clauses in 3-CNF-SAT, construct a triangle (clause gadget) labeled with the three terms from the clause. Some triangles may have multiple identical labels. Some labels are negated variables, others are not.

For example, the clause (x₁ ∨ xᵢ ∨ ¬x₁) would correspond to the triangle in Figure 9.5.
