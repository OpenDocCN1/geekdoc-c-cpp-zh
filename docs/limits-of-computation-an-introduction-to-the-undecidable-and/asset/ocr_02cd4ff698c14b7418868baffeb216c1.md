# 9.10 SUMMARY AND REPRISE

We have seen a variety of NP-complete problems from areas of logic, graph theory, and number theory. There seems to be little of making blanket categorizations, except that NP-complete problems involve finding a solution from an exponential number of possibilities, and for these problems, no good shortcuts have been found.
