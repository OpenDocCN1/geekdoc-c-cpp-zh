# More NP-Complete Problems

# 9.1 ADDING OTHER PROBLEMS TO THE LIST OF KNOWN NP-COMPLETE PROBLEMS

The next order of business will be adding members to the collection of NP-complete problems. No other proof will be that of Cook-Levin, beginning with arbitrary members of the class NP and depending on the definition of NP-completeness. Instead, all these proofs use polynomial reducibility.

Recall to show that Y is NP-complete, show that:

+   1. Y is in NP.
2. X ⩽ Y, for some known NP-complete problem X (to begin with, there is no choice, X is conjunctive normal form satisfiability {CNF-SAT}).
