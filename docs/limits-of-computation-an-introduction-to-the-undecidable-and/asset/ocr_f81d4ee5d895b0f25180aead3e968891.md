+   3. To finish the proof, we need to show that if the graph—constructed has a vertex cover, then the Boolean expression is satisfied (that is, yes comes from yes). This proof relies on the constraints on how the vertex cover can be chosen. While there are many ways to choose m = n + 2|C| from G, the only possible way to be a vertex cover is to have a V' that includes one vertex from each bar, and two vertices from each triangle (there is simply no other way to have a cover of a triangle!). If you can have a successful vertex cover, then the lines interconnecting the bars and triangles must have one end in the vertex cover. Two of the interconnecting lines are covered by the two vertices chosen from each triangle, but the third must be covered by the vertex chosen from the bar—and that means setting that vertex to be TRUE will guarantee at least one true variable in each clause.
