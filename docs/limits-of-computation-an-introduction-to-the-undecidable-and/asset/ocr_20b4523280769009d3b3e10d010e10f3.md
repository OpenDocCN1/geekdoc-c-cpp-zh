We will also add the turn$\vdash$ to the alphabet to separate the contents of the k tapes.
Thus, in a three-tape machine with $\Gamma = \{a, b, c, B\}$, we construct a one-tape machine with $\Gamma' = \{a, b, c, a', b', c', B, B'\}$.
If the three tapes contain: abba (head on first b), caa (head on c), and cc (head on first c), they would look as in Figure 5.5.
