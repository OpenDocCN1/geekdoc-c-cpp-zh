vector mix equations work great and are simple to implement--why reinvent the wheel? AUSynth treats the dread of oscillators as a smaller of vector mix surfaces. That, each group of four waveforms comprises one vector mix surface. The name for group of four oscillators is the active cell. At any time, the output of the synth is a combination of the four oscillators in the cell that contains the mix point, calculated with the standard equations. Figure 11.34 shows cells in the rectangular array of waveforms. Notice that any pair of adjacent cells will share two wavables. In order to make the calculations simpler, and to re-use equations and the joystick control, AUSynth uses a square of wavables instead of rectangular. If you modified the joystick control to be rectangular, in addition, it is easier to see how AUSynth works by using the non-joystick coordinate system, where the mix diamond is laying on its side as a square.

# 128 Wavetable Grid

When the mix point is outside one of the cells, the cell becomes activated and its vertices are treated as the A, B, C, and D vertices of the VS surface. As the point moves around due to the path/router, the active cell switches around. Since each adjacent cell shares a common pair of vertices, it is just a matter of mapping the proper wavables into the voice object. The AUSynth object is similar to the VectorSynth object in has still oscillators, each reading from a sample file. Figure 11.36(a) shows a mix point in the first cell, moving towards the cell. Notice that the vertices are indexed. In Figure 11.36(b) the mix point has touched the D-C boundary, where its mix ratios to 50% C, 50% D and everything else.

Now suppose the mix point crosses over the D-C boundary into the cell below (cell 3) as shown in Figure 11.37. When it occurs, the voice object must switch out the current wavables for the next cell. However, two are shared—the wave tables 4 and 5 (D and C) become A and B in the next cell. At the boundary, the D and C wavables are re-named A and B because of their location Notice the mix ratios are now 50% A, 50% B and zero of everything else, since the wavables are shared, the cell switches without a glitch or discontinuity. If the path goes directly through a vertex, then mix value will be at 100%, the wavables will be distributed, and the mix continues into the next cell (at 100% level).
