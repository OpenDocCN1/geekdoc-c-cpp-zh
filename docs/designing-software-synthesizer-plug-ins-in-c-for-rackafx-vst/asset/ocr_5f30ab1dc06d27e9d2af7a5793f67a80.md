# 6.5 Digital Envelope Generators

When digital synths arrived in the 1980s, manufacturers and computer musicians alike realized they were not bound to a capacitor or could create envelope generators with any shape and number of segments they wanted. Since the curves are created digitally, we can implement an envelope generator that is linear-in-dB or follows any curve or equation we like. The Yamaha DX7 envelope generators were implemented digitally using counters that mimicked exponential behavior.

For digital synthesis, we represent the capacitor voltage VCAP with our maximum value 1.0, and therefore the sustain voltage VSUS will be represented with a sustain level LSUS that is less than 1.0. When designing an EG, it is often common to represent the segments as lines rather than curves and plotted on the y-axis labeled "dB—linear-in-dB". In addition we are free to use either the analog/release rates or the absolute times for the segments. Figure 6.15 shows these digital versions with segments plotted in dB.
