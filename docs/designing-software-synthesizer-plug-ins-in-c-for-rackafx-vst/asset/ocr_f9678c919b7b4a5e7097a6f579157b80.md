Next, setup the Patch control using the underscore (_) to separate strings in your list (you can have spaces in an enumerated name). You can take advantage of a feature of the LCD Control: When the LCD uses them, it replaces the underscores with white-space titles. When you enter an enumerated name, this allows you much more flexibility than the rotary or normal sliders. So, use underscores between the words Heavy_Lead, Lush_Pad, and Space_Cad.
