With any nonlinear waveshaping, aliasing will occur; the extent of the damage will depend on the saturation function and settings you choose. Any of the other filters in this chapter will have the same issue with aliasing. Because the oversampling will require some extra CPU processing, you may decide to leave out the nonlinear processing or just live with the aliasing.

