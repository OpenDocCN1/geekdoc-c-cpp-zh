The synthesized unrivalaped waveform version has issues; it will alias at high frequencies and high
saturation settings. You are aware that BLEP and PolyBLEP may only be used to correct a kind of
dcontinuity at C1 unity. This means the magnitude and direction of the vectors of top and
bottom of the point are identical; this occurs in the pure sawtooth, square, and bipolar shaped
sawtooth, but not for the unrivalaped shaped waveform. We observe only aliasing in the unipolar
sawshaped sawtooth with lower saturation values than the normal sawtooth.

In unison unrivalaped waveform produces DC offset that may produce problems when unfilled
waveforms are summed; the higher the value, the higher the DC. This can be remedied with a
tight high filter with a cutoff~5 Hz or so. Since a bipolar blep filter preserves its zeros at DC, it will completely
remove the DC component but will allow the wave shape shift. We found the shaped sawtooth waveforms for
fund and interesting. You might experiment with modulating the saturation value in time to produce
dynamic spectrum. In particular, adjusting the saturation in the bipolar shaped sawtooth moves the shape from saw
and square, producing a very nice modulation.
