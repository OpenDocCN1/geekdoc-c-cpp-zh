Now suppose the mix point crosses over the D-C boundary into the cell below (cell 3) as shown in Figure 11.37. When it occurs, the voice object must switch out the current four wavables for the next cell. However, two are shared—the wave tables 4 and 5 (D and C) become A and B in the next cell. At the boundary, the D and C wavables are re-named A and B because of their location Notice the mix ratios are now 50% A, 50% B and zero of everything else, since the wavables are shared, the cell switches without a glitch or discontinuity. If the path goes directly through a vertex, then mix value will be at 100%, the wavables will be distributed, and the mix continues into the next cell (at 100% level).
