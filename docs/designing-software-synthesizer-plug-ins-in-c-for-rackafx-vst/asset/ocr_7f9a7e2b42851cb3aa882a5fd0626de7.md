# 5.21 Bandlimited Wavetable Oscillators

To produce non-sinusoidal waveforms that are alias-free, you need to
preload tables with bandlimited versions of the waveforms. The
classical waveforms like sawtooth, triangle and square, which you Fourier
synthesis—the summing of individual harmonic components.

Here are the three Fourier synthesis for the waveforms. The
sawtooth waveform has both even and odd harmonics scaled to
(1/k)

The triangle waveform has only odd harmonics. The (-1)k alternates
signs of the harmonics. The harmonic amplitude drop off a rate given
1/(2k+1)², which is exponential in nature.

The square wave is also composed of odd harmonics like the triangle wave.
The harmonic amplitude drop off a rate of 1/(2k+1), which is not
severe as the triangle wave. Therefore, the square wave has higher
sounding waveforms and a more grittier texture.
