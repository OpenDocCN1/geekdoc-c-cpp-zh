The AHSR EG is used for sample-based synthesizers that come online in the 1980s. In these years, the attack and decay are usually built-in to the transient of the sample, and often the EG's attack is set to 0.0 to let the sample's attack come through. The hold time allows the complete transient portion of the sample to be passed through unaffected. All four of the EGs in Figure 6.17 have an attack decay that rises to an amplitude of 1.0, and thus all other levels must be lower than the attack.
