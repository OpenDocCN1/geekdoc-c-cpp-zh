# **Problem 2**

Write a program to input two 3*3 matrices and print the addition of both the matrix.

# **Solution**

The usual matrix addition is defined for two matrices of the same dimensions. The sum of two *m*-by-*n* matrices A and B, denoted by A + B, is again an *m*-by-*n* matrix computed by adding corresponding elements. For example: If we want to add the following matrices:

$$
\begin{bmatrix}0 & 1 & 2\\9 & 8 & 7\end{bmatrix}
+
\begin{bmatrix}6 & 5 & 4\\3 & 4 & 5\end{bmatrix}
$$

Add the pairs of entries, and simplify for the final answer:

$$
\begin{bmatrix}0+6 & 1+5 & 2+4\\9+3 & 8+4 & 7+5\end{bmatrix}
=
\begin{bmatrix}6 & 6 & 6\\12 & 12 & 12\end{bmatrix}
