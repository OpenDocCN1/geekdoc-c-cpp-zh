# Walking Mechanics

The four basic positions of a walk are the two stride positions and the two crossover positions.

# The Stride Positions

The two basic keys of a walk are at the extremes of each stride. To complete a walk cycle you need two strides. The exact arm and leg positions will be dependent on the emotions of your character and the external influences on them. However, one thing that is generally true of these stride keys is that the shoulder of the leading arm will be twisted forwards of the shoulder of the trailing arm, whereas the hip joint of the leading leg will be twisted forwards of the hip joint of the trailing leg. In other words, opposite shoulders and hips lead.
