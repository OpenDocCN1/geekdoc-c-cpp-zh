With a standard blink, the eyelid will close slowly and open quickly. By making the eyelids pop open quickly, the character looks alive and awake.

![](bbox=[0.116, 0.156, 0.835, 0.48])

When somebody is sleepy or not very bright, the eyelids will shut slowly and open very slowly, with a lot of drag on the lids. This gives the impression that opening the eyes is a huge effort.
