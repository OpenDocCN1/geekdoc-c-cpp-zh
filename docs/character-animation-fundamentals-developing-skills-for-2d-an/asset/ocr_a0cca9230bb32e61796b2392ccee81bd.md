When this is done in quick succession, the ball will move along the arc. You are now ‘flicking’. Sometimes it helps to put a rubber band over the pins on the peg bar to stop the paper slipping off. If the ball in drawing 9 doesn’t appear to be in the correct position, rub it out and redraw it. Keep flicking and redrawing until it looks right. (See ‘flicking animation’ in movies001, Chapter 1 on the website)
