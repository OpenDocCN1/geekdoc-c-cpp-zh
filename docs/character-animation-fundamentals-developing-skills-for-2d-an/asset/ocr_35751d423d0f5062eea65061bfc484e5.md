The field guide has 'North', 'South', 'East' and 'West' printed at the top, bottom, right and left. It consists of 24 columns and 24 rows in a grid. The columns are half an inch wide. By using these compass points and grid references you can specify any area on your paper that you want to be shot.

The illustration right shows an oblong area at the top right of the paper that is 5 inches wide. This would be 5 field at 7 east/7 north of 12-field center. Using the field guide, you work out where the center of the oblong is in relation to 12-field center (the center of the field guide). To find the center you would count along seven lines east and seven lines north from the center of the field guide (12-field center).
