Sit at street cafés or in parks and draw the people around you. This is a great way to find out about human nature. How do people talk to each other? How do they walk, sit, run and play?

![](bbox=[0.083, 0.153, 0.949, 0.691])

The most important thing about drawing is that it makes you sit down and look at the world around you in detail. Things that you would not normally notice – the way people pick things up, the faces they pull, the body language they adopt – become more apparent to you. A sketchbook is valuable reference material for your animation.
