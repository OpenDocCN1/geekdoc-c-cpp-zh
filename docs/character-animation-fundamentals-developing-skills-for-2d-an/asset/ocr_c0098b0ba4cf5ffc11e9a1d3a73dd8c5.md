# Follow-Through of Inanimate Objects

This is the animation of hair, manes, coat tails, ties, sleeves, strings, general drapery, and so on. Generally, inanimate objects connected to your character will follow a path similar to the part of the character they are attached to. Depending on the size and flexibility of the object, it will flap out from the end of this path to varying degrees.

![](bbox=[0.232, 0.202, 0.846, 0.526])

Depending on the ‘stiffness’ of the object, you will get a slightly different type of follow-through. Generally a piece of drapery will demonstrate a wave running along its length as it is pulled through the air. At its most simple, this is like a flag cycle. See `flag_cycle.avi`, Chapter 4 on the website.
