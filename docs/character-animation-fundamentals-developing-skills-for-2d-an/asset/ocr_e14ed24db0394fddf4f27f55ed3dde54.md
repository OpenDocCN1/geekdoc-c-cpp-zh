Attend life drawing classes and focus on short poses (less than 10 minutes). If the model is going to pose for an hour or two, draw them from one angle for a short period of time and then move around the room and draw them from another angle. This teaches you to capture the essence of a pose with a few simple lines. Concentrate on getting the structure, weight and balance correct.
