# Changing the Pace and Mood in a Walk Exercise in 3D

Start by building a floor for the man to walk on. Make it out of a grid, plane, box or cube. It should be about 100 units wide for Maya or 1000 units wide for 3D Studio Max and 30 units deep for Maya or 300 units deep in 3D Studio Max. Don’t make it any taller than 1 unit in Maya or 10 units in 3D Studio Max. Position it so that the majority of its length is in front of our man.
