# The Spine

At the center of the human skeleton is the ‘S’-shaped spine. It is made up of 24 individual bones, called vertebra, which are linked by discs of cartilage. This combination gives the spine great flexibility, allowing forwards and backwards and side-to-side movement and a degree of twisting.

![](bbox=[0.16, 0.66, 0.86, 0.88])

At the top of the spine are the seven vertebrae that make up the neck.
