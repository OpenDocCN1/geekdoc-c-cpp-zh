# Forwards Body Postures

In these postures the character is leaning forwards and pointing toward something with a fist or a finger. They indicate that a character is actively accepting or rejecting a message given to them. They are involved, absorbed or passionate about what they feel.

# Backwards Body Postures

In these postures the character may be leaning backwards, looking up at the ceiling or engaged in some other activity such as cleaning glasses, winding a watch or doodling on a pad. They indicate that a character is passively involved or ignoring a message given to them.
