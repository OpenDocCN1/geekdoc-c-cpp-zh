# The Animation of Liquids

# *A Drip*

Liquids, unlike solids, have the ability to break apart and reform easily. As it falls, a drop of liquid takes on a tear shape (a bit like a balloon). Surface tension creates a ‘membrane’, which holds the majority of the water at the bottom of the drop. When the drop hits the ground, the surface tension is broken and the water breaks apart. As the first amount of water hits the ground it bounces outwards and upwards at an angle. It is prevented from bouncing directly upwards by the water that follows.
