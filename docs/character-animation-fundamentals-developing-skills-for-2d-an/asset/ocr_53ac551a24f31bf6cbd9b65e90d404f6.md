Tex Avery and Chuck Jones: my favorite cartoon characters as a kid were Screwy Squirrel and Droopy (both Tex Avery creations), closely followed by Daffy Duck and Bugs Bunny (generally the incarnations of these characters in Chuck Jones' films).

The list of animators I'm in awe of is almost endless: Brad Bird, John Lasseter, Hayao Miyazaki, Nick Park, Joanna Quinn, Jan Švankmajer – I could go on and on.

Hopefully this book will inspire you.
