# MAKE YOUR GOOGLE DRIVE FOLDER PUBLIC

Because you will be adding new files to your Google Drive folder, it is a good idea to make the entire folder public so that you can share your entire web site.

> > By making this folder public, everyone in the world can see what is inside. It is very important that you do not put any personal information in this folder. Make sure that you show an adult everything you plan to put on the Internet and that you ask permission to do that.

+   1. Go to [http://drive.google.com](http://drive.google.com) and log in to your Google account.
+   2. Right-click your project folder and click **Share**.
