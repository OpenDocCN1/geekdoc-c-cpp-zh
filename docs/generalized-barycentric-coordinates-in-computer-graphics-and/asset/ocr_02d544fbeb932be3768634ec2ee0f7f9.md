For a better comparison we group all coordinates by the first two properties. The first group includes Wachspress (WP), discrete harmonic (DH), and the complete family (CF) of coordinates. These coordinates are well-defined for convex polygons only and have a closed form. The second group consists of mean value (MV), positive mean value (PM), metric (MT), Poisson (PS), Gordon-Wixom (GW), and positive Gordon-Wixom (PG) coordinates that are well-defined for arbitrary simple polygons and have a closed form. In the third group we list harmonic (HM), maximum entropy (ME), and local (LC) coordinates that are also well-defined for arbitrary simple polygons, but can be obtained only numerically.
