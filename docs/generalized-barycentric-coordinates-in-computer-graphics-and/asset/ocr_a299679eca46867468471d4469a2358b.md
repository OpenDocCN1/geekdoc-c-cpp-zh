while the corresponding body force \(\boldsymbol{f} = [f_x, f_y, f_z]^T\) reads as
\(f_x = -\frac{(1+x)\cos(z)(3\kappa - 2\mu)}{3}, \quad f_y = 0, \quad f_z = -\frac{(1+x)\sin(z)(3\kappa - 2\mu)}{3}.\)

Throughout, we set the initial shear and bulk moduli \(\mu\) and \(\kappa\) as 3 and 10, and consider two error measures, the \(L^2\)-norm and the \(H^1\)-seminorm of the displacement error. More specifically, the \(L^2\)-norm and the \(H^1\)-seminorm, respectively, are given by
\(E_{0,\boldsymbol{u}} = \|\boldsymbol{u} - \boldsymbol{u}_h\| \quad \text{and} \quad E_{1,\boldsymbol{u}} = \|\nabla \boldsymbol{u} - \nabla \boldsymbol{u}_h\|,\)

where the \(L^2\)-norm, \(\|\cdot\|\), is evaluated using a fifth order triangulation rule.
