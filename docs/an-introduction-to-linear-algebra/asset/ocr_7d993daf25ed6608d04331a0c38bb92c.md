# ## 4.13. Consider the tridiagonal matrix of order \( n \),

\( A_n(x, y) = \left( \begin{array}{ c c c c c c }
1 + x & -1 &  &  &  &  \\
-1 & 2 & -1 &  &  &  \\
 & -1 & 2 & -1 &  &  \\
 &  & \cdots & \cdots &  &  \\
 &  &  & -1 & 2 & -1 \\
 &  &  &  & -1 & 1 + y
\end{array} \right), \)

where \( x + y + (n - 1)xy \neq 0 \). Show that \( A_n^{-1}(x, y) = (b_{ij}) \) is symmetric and

\( b_{ij} = \frac{[1 + (i - 1)x][1 + (n - j)y]}{x + y + (n - 1)xy}, \quad i \leq j. \)

# ## 4.14. Consider the tridiagonal matrix of order \( n \),

\( A_n(x, y) = \left( \begin{array}{ c c c c c c }
1 & -y &  &  &  &  \\
-x & 1 & -y &  &  &  \\
 & -x & 1 & -y &  &  \\
 &  & \cdots & \cdots &  &  \\
 &  &  & -x & 1 & -y \\
 &  &  &  & -x & 1
\end{array} \right), \)

where \( x \geq 0, y \geq 0 \). Show that

+   (i) its determinant \( D_n \) is

\( D_n = \left\{ \begin{array}{ l l }
1, & xy = 0 \\
(xy)^{n/2} \frac{\sinh(n + 1)\theta}{\sinh \theta}, & \cosh \theta = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{xy}}, \quad 0 < xy < \frac{1}{4} \\
\frac{n + 1}{2^n}, & xy = \frac{1}{4} \\
(xy)^{n/2} \frac{\sin(n + 1)\theta}{\sin \theta}, & \cos \theta = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{xy}}, \quad xy > \frac{1}{4}
\end{array} \right. \)

+   (ii) the elements \( b_{ij} \) of its inverse matrix \( B \) are

\( b_{ij} = \frac{1}{D_n} \left\{ \begin{array}{ l l }
y^{j-i}D_{i-1}D_{n-j}, & i \leq j \\
x^{i-j}D_{j-1}D_{n-i}, & i \geq j.
\end{array} \right. \)
