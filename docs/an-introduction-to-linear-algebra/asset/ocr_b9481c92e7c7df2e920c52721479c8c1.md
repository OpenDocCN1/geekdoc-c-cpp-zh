**1.4.** (i) Let \(v^1, v^2 \in U + W\), where \(v^1 = u^1 + w^1\), \(v^2 = u^2 + w^2\). Then,
\(v^1 + v^2 = u^1 + w^1 + u^2 + w^2 = (u^1 + u^2) + (w^1 + w^2)\). Now since \(U\) and \(W\) are

\(U = \left\{ \left( \begin{array}{c}

\(U \cup W = \left\{ \left( \begin{array}{c}

Clearly,
\(\left( \begin{array}{c} 
1 \\
0 \\
0 \\
\end{array} \right) \in U \cup W, \quad \left( \begin{array}{c} 
0 \\

1 \\
\end{array} \right) \in U \cup W, \quad \text{but} \quad \left( \begin{array}{c} 
1 \\
0 \\
1 \\
\end{array} \right) \notin U \cup W.\)
