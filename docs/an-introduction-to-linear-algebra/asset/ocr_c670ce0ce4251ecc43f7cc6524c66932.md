**4.9.** Let $A$ be a skew-symmetric matrix and $I-A$ is nonsingular. Show that the matrix $B=(I+A)(I-A)^{-1}$ is orthogonal.

**4.10.** Let $W$ be a $1\times n$ matrix (row vector) such that $WW^t=1$. The $n\times n$ matrix $H=I-2W^tW$ is called a *Householder matrix*. Show that $H$ is symmetric and orthogonal.

**4.11.** Let $A,B$ be real square matrices, and let the matrix $P$ be orthogonal and $B=P^{-1}AP$. Show that $\operatorname{tr}(A^tA)=\operatorname{tr}(B^tB)$.
