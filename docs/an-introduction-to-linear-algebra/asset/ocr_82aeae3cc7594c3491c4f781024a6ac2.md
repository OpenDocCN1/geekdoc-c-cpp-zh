# ## Example 3.6.

From the definition of determinants it is clear that for a given \( n \times n \) matrix \( A(x) = (a_{ij}(x)) \) of differentiable functions in an interval \( J \), the function \( \det A(x) \) is differentiable in \( J \). We shall compute \( (\det A(x))' \) by using the expansion of \( \det A(x) \) given in (3.1). Since

\(\det A(x) = \sum_{j=1}^n a_{ij}(x) \alpha_{ij}(x)\)

it follows that

\(\frac{\partial \det A(x)}{\partial a_{ij}(x)} = \alpha_{ij}(x)\)

and hence

\((\det A(x))' = \sum_{j=1}^n \sum_{i=1}^n \frac{\partial \det A(x)}{\partial a_{ij}(x)} \frac{da_{ij}(x)}{dx} = \sum_{j=1}^n \sum_{i=1}^n \alpha_{ij}(x) a'_{ij}(x),\)

which is equivalent to

\((\det A(x))' = \begin{vmatrix} a'_{11}(x) & \cdots & a'_{1n}(x) \\ a_{21}(x) & \cdots & a_{2n}(x) \\ \cdots & \cdots & \cdots \\ a_{n1}(x) & \cdots & a_{nn}(x) \end{vmatrix} + \begin{vmatrix} a_{11}(x) & \cdots & a_{1n}(x) \\ a'_{21}(x) & \cdots & a'_{2n}(x) \\ \cdots & \cdots & \cdots \\ a_{n1}(x) & \cdots & a_{nn}(x) \end{vmatrix} + \cdots\)
\(+ \begin{vmatrix} a_{11}(x) & \cdots & a_{1n}(x) \\ a_{21}(x) & \cdots & a_{2n}(x) \\ \cdots & \cdots & \cdots \\ a'_{n1}(x) & \cdots & a'_{nn}(x) \end{vmatrix}.\)
