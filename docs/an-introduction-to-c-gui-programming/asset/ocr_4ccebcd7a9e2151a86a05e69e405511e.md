If you look at the top of the right-hand column, you'll see the hierarchy of widgets being displayed - a GtkWindow, containing a GtkBox containing a GtkLabel and a GtkButton. If you click on one of the widgets in the hierarchy, the options displayed in the tabbed area at the bottom of the right-hand column will be those for that widget.

Click on the GtkLabel and scroll through the options on the 'General' tab, you'll find a section labelled 'Edit label appearance', including the entry 'Label' - use this to set the text in the label to whatever you want.
