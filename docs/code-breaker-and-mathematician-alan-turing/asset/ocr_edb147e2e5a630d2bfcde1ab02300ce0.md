Adolf Hitler (right) became the leader of Germany in 1933.

While Turing was studying at King's College and Princeton, Adolf Hitler rose to power to become the dictator of Germany. Hitler and the Nazi Party had plans to expand into other countries through military force. Britain was likely to go to war against Germany. Like many others throughout Europe and the world, Turing was scared. But he knew that knowledge of cryptography could be useful in a war.
