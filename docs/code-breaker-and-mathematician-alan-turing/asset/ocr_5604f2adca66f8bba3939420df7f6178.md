# FINAL CONTRIBUTIONS

In 1952, Turing turned his attention to biology and how things grow. His interest in the subject went back to his childhood, when he’d been fascinated with plants. As an adult, he had new ideas that explained how patterns in living things develop mathematically. He published a paper on his ideas, focusing on daisies.
