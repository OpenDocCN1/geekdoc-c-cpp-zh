Britain recognized Turing’s efforts. He was honored as an Officer of the Most Excellent Order of the British Empire. After the war, he continued working in his field. At the National Physical Laboratory in London, Turing designed the Automatic Computing Engine. It was the first computer design that could store digital programs.
