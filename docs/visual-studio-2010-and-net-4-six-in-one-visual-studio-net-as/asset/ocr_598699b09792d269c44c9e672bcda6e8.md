# Pie and Doughnut Charts

A pie chart is a circular chart divided into sectors, illustrating percentages. In a pie chart, the arc length of each sector (and, consequently, its central angle and area) is proportional to the quantity it represents. The pie chart is perhaps the most ubiquitous statistical chart in the business world and in the mass media. Pie charts of a slice with the whole pie can be an effective way of displaying information, in particular if the intent is to compare the slices among each other.
