# Transaction-Handling Activities

One of the key success factors of modern workflow applications is the capability to manage longrunning transactions. Long-running transactions are those transactions where you cannot use either the begin-commit-rollback pattern generally implemented by database management systems, or the two-phase commit pattern used by distributed systems. There might be many reasons why these patterns cannot be applied, the most frequent being that one (or more) of the parties involved in the transaction do not support any of the two previously described.

WF 4.0 uses a transaction model compensating transaction. This model does not ensure atomicity (that is, your transaction is either executed or rolled back to the starting point as nothing happened). Instead, it provides that the transaction is either entirely done (committed), or in the case it cannot be entirely committed, changes are compensated.

A common example is when you register for a conference and separately book an air ticket and hotel. If everything's okay, all fees are withdrawn from your credit card account. If the conference is cancelled, your registration fee is returned, and, in many cases, others are put back to your account. If you buy tickets with special conditions or you cancel your hotel too to the check-in date, a part of the fee is withheld as a cancellation fee. In this case, your original transaction is not rolled back — you do not get all your money back, only part of it. You are compensated, partially fairly.

Table 11-8 describes the activities WF 4.0 uses to implement the compensating transaction model.

TABLE 11-8: Transaction-Handling Activities

| ACTIVITY | DESCRIPTION |
|---|---|
| CompensableActivity | This activity is the core compensation activity in WF 4.0. Any activities that perform work that may need to be compensated are placed into the Body of a CompensableActivity. It also allows the developer to optionally specify a compensation and confirmation activity to schedule appropriate business logic in the event of errors or successful completion of the Body. Compensation and confirmation of a CompensableActivity is invoked by using the CompensationToken returned by the CompensableActivity. If the Body is cancelled before it is finished, the CancellationHandler activity is executed. The ConfirmationHandler activity is executed when subsequent activities fail and the workflow is aborted, and it is used to undo the work of the Body if the Body has completed. The ConfirmationHandler activity is executed when the activity has been confirmed. By default, confirmation happens automatically when the workflow instance has completed.
