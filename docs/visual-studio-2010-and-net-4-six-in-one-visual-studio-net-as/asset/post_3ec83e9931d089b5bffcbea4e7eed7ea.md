# ABOUT THE AUTHORS

**ISTVÁN NOVÁK**
István Novák worked in the IT industry for more than 14 years. He is the founder and lead architect of Eyedee Ltd., a small independent software vendor (ISV) specializing in Rich Internet Application (RIA) development. Before founding his own company, he worked for 6 years at Microsoft as an Application Development Consultant, and later as a Senior Consultant in Microsoft Services. He specialized in .NET-based custom development projects. In 2004, he was awarded an Most Valuable Professional (MVP).

**ANDRÁS VELVÁRT**
András Velvárt is a Silverlight MVP, with a passion for user experience. As an accomplished speaker, he talks at numerous conferences about Windows Presentation Foundation (WPF) and Silverlight. He has taught many classes and workshops. He is also the owner of Response Ltd. (response.hu), a small company in Hungary.

**ADAM GRANICZ**
Adam Granicz is the CEO of IntelliFactory, a leading provider of F training, development, and consulting services, as well as technologies that enable rapid, reactive web development. As one of the first F users, he is a key community member and an F evangelist. He has co-authored two F books with Don Sime, the designer of the language.

**GYÓRGY BALÁSY**
György Balássy teaches web development as a lecturer at Budapest University of Technology and Economics. He is a founding member of the local MSDN Community Center (MSDNCC), having an important role in evangelizing the foundation of the Hungarian .NET community as a speaker, book author, and consultant. He provided leadership in Microsoft events, technical forums, and as the head of the Portal Tool Group in the MSDNCC. He is a regular speaker on academic and industrial events, presenting in-depth technical sessions on .NET, ASP.NET, Office development, and ethical hacking, for which he won the Best Speaker and the Most Valuable Professional (MVP) Awards in SharePoint and ASP.NET multiple times. He was selected to be a member of the ASPInsiders group. Since 2005, he has been the Microsoft Regional Director in Hungary.

**ATTILA HAJDRIK**
Attila Hajdrik is a member of the ASPInsiders group. He is a regular speaker at developer conferences and various industry events.
