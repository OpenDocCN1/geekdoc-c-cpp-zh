# Area Charts

An area chart graphically displays quantitative data. It is based on the line chart. The area between axis and line are emphasized with colors, textures, and hatchings. Commonly, you use an area chart to compare two or more series. Figure 14-26 shows an example of an area chart with two series.

You have two kinds: one based on a line chart, and the other on a spline chart (which is named SplineArea). While putting more series to a line chart generally does not deteriorate the value of individual series, putting more on the same area chart debase the value of the chart. In Figure 14-26, Series #2 enrouds a large portion of Series #1. You can use transparent coloring to get rid of this situation.
