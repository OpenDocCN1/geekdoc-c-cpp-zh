At the snippet level you invoke the snippet using the Tab or the Enter keys, or by using a mouse-click.

Using these ways of inserting snippet is practical when you are just getting acquainted with the variety of available snippets, but really useful until you know exactly which snippet you want to invoke. A much more efficient way of accessing snippets is through shortcuts. Of course, that you must remember the shortcut — or at least the first few characters. Let's have a look at how this method works.
