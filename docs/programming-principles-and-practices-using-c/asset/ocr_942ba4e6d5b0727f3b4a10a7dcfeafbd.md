Can you think of any more steps that a person would assume, but that a computer would not? Add them to your list. This is a good start in "thinking like a computer." Warning: For most people, "go to the bathroom" is a perfectly adequate instruction. For someone with no experience with houses or bathrooms (imagine a stone-age person, somehow transplanted into your dining room) the list of necessary instructions could be *very* long. Please don't use more than a page. For the benefit of the reader, you may add a short description of the layout of the house you are imagining.
