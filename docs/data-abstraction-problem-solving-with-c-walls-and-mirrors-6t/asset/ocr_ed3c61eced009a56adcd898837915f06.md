One of the most frequently performed computing tasks is the maintenance of a collection of data in some specified order. Many examples immediately come to mind, such as students placed in order by their names, baseball players listed in order by their batting averages, and corporations listed in order by their assets. The items in these examples are in sorted order. In contrast, the items on a grocery list might occur in the order in which they appear on the grocer's shelves, but they are probably not sorted by name. These items are ordered simply by their positions within the list.
