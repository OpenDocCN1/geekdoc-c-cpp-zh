4. Suppose an ammonia solution consists of 15 gallons of water mixed with 4 gallons of ammonia. An additional \(x\) gallons of ammonia are added to the mixture.

+   a. In terms of \(x\), how many gallons of ammonia are now in the mixture?

+   b. In terms of \(x\), what is now the total volume of liquid in gallons?

+   c. The concentration of ammonia in the mixture is defined to be the ratio of the volume of ammonia to the volume of liquid. This is a function of \(x\), the number of gallons of ammonia added to the mixture. Find a formula for \(C(x)\), the concentration of ammonia in the mixture.

+   d. Find the equation for the horizontal asymptote of \(C(x)\) from Part c. Explain what this asymptote means about the mixture.
