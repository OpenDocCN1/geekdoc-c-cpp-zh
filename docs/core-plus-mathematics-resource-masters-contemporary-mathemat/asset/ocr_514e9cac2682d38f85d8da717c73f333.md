# TEST-TAKING TIP

**Factoring a quadratic expression often makes a question transparent.**

College entrance examination authors think it is important for students to be able to factor several simple quadratic expressions such as \(ax^2 + bx\), \(x^2 + 2ax + a^2\), and \(x^2 - y^2\). However, you are seldom directly asked to factor. Rather, you are asked a question that is simple once you see that factoring a quadratic expression is involved.

**Example**  Look back at Item 8. To use factoring, notice that in \(m^2 - 4n^2 = 32\), the left-hand expression is the difference of two squares, \(m^2\) and \((2n)^2\). Thus, \(m^2 - 4n^2 = (m + 2n)(m - 2n) = 32\). Since \(m + 2n = 8\), \(m - 2n = 4\). So, the answer is (d).
