1. Two major forces determine the rise or fall of a hot air balloon, a lifting force and a drag force due to gravity. A formula for the force \(F\) in pounds on a spherical balloon is given by

\[F=\frac{4}{3}\pi bR^3-4\pi wR^2-c,\]

where \(R\) is the radius in feet of the balloon, \(b\) is the lifting force in pounds per cubic foot, \(w\) is the drag force in pounds per square foot, and \(c\) is the weight in pounds of the equipment including the basket and its occupants.

+   a. Assuming that \(w\) and \(c\) are both positive numbers, explain why the last two terms in the formula for \(F\) are preceded by minus signs.
+   
+   b. The balloon will rise or fall depending on whether \(F\) is positive or negative. Ignore the weight of the equipment, that is, assume \(c=0\). What is the minimum radius \(R\) of the balloon in terms of \(b\) and \(w\) that will keep it steady (neither rising nor falling)? Show or explain your work.
+   
+   c. Suppose that for a certain balloon, \(b=0.1\,\text{lb/ft}^3\) and \(w=0.2\,\text{lb/ft}^2\). What maximum radius of the balloon is required to lift equipment weighing \(c=750\) pounds?
