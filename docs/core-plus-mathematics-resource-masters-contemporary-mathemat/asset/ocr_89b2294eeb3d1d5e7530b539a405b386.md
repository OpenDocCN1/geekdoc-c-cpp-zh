# # Summarize the Mathematics

In this investigation, you learned how to add and subtract rational expressions.

+   a. Adding rational expressions is easy when the two expressions are related in a particular way. What is that relationship? If it holds, how do you add the expressions?

+   b. If the most convenient relationship between rational expressions does *not* hold, what can you do to make addition or subtraction of the expressions possible?

*Be prepared to explain your ideas to the class.*
