# Forgiveness In The Work Place

You hear laughter and hushed voices; poke your head out of your cubicle to see what holds many of your coworkers' attention, always happy to hear a good joke... Your eyes widen as you see them, gathered by the water cooler, mocking some of your famous hand gestures. One is even throwing on an accent very similar to yours.

What do you do?

Your first thought could be to get out of your cubicle, walk with great purpose to the water cooler, get yourself a drink and then throw it in their faces. You can picture yourself standing triumphant as they drip away, embarrassed to ever mocked you.

Your second thought could be to simply stick your back in your cubicle and pretend you did not hear them, only to shoot an angry stare as they walk by. Or, perhaps say something cryptic to let them know that you know.

If either one of these ideas sounded like a good one, keep reading.

The workplace is filled with gossip and distrust. It is human nature at its most competitive. Tempers will flare and egos will bruise. What you must learn how to deal with these types of challenges, realities and problems through forgiveness.
