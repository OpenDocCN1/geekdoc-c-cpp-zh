# Step Four: Track, Trim, and Target

After you pay attention to your spending habits for a little while, you may realize that you are spending more money than you are actually making. You will also see where you are spending too much money.

In some cases, it is then also NOT too hard to cut expenses!

In others, though it is and can be very hard and challenging. For example, you have to pay your rent and electricity bill, but you do *not* have to buy a new pair of purple suede shoes with a *really* cute silver buckle. (and the “must have” handbag to match!)

Once you get used to only spending as much as you have coming in, you will probably feel much more relaxed. You will not feel the pressure of dealing with bills that you do not have the money to pay for.

And you might not have enough left over to treat yourself from time to time, or to save some money for the future.

**SIDEBAR:** When I was not making a lot of money early on, it meant more to me to have something saved in the bank, than a great pair of jeans. I would rather know I had something to fall back on.

You can always get jeans later, but sometimes you need that savings as a safety net when when things happen that you were not expecting and when you are on your own, things always come up.
