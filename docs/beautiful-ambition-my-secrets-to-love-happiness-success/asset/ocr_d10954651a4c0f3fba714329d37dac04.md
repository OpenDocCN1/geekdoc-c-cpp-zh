# Healthy Inside and Out

Like everyone, I love a good tan, I am also aware of and concerned about risks like sun damage. Thank goodness, there are so many great selftanners on the market that you really do not have to spend time in the sun to get great color.

When looking for a self-tanner, my advice is to purchase a product that goes on color, so you can see if you have missed any spots.

I recommend using an exfoliating product in the shower before you apply the self-tanner. You can use a salt or sugar scrub at any department store, drugstore or even health-food store. They make some with great fragrances. (To save money, mix your own with equal amounts of sea salt and baby oil. It works wonders!)
