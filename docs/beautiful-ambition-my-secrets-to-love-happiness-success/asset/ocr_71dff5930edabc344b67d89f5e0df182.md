# 78 CHAPTER FOUR The Decisions You Make Today...

+   - There are seven primary rules to good decision-making. By following them carefully, you'll make a habit of them and good decision making will become a natural part of your everyday life.

+   - Good decision making relies on a positive attitude. If you are not making decisions with a positive outlook, then you will not be taking yourself down the right path with your choices.

+   - Working on your own self-confidence is an important part of the good decision making process. When you have strong self confidence, you are much better prepared to gauge the outcomes of different decisions, and to choose what is best for you.

+   - There are sixteen things that you should ask yourself before making any major decision. Answer them honestly, and look over the results to help guide you in the right direction.
