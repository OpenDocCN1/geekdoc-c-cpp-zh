# Chapter 6

# # The Sisterhood
Now, Now Ladies. Can We NOT ALL Just Get Along?

> "For there is no friend like a sister, in calm or stormy weather, to cheer one on
the tedious way, to fetch one if one goes astray, to lift one if one totters down,
to strengthen whilst one stands."

> —Christina Rossetti

Mean girls. Catfights. Queen bees...!
There are many different ways to describe the way that women
can be jealous, envious and all-round nasty to and with one another.
By the time a young girl is eleven years old, she might very well already ‘know’
what type or category she falls into or would be stereotyped as.
