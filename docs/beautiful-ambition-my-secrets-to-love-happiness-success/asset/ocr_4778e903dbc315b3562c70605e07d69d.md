# Saving

Saving money is something most of us want to do, but it is also something we do not always do. Saving is easier than you think, as long as you know what you are doing with the basics of savings and what interest can do for you.

Interest calculations Simple Interest Calculation—With the simple interest calculation, interest is calculated on the original amount of your deposit, year after year.

The formula is:

Original dollar amount x Interest rate x Length of time (in years) = Amount earned
