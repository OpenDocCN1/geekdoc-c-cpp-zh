Let tell you ladies; do NOT listen to the women that say they don't exist, because they do!

I had just turned twenty-three years old. I was independent and taking care of myself. To top it off, I had reached my goal to be a working model and now a spokeswoman that others could look up to—others to whom I could now lend a voice, which they had none before.

In fact, I was working as a spokes-model for a business that had created a diet product for another organization. (The latter owned by my very-soon-to-be husband).

My boss had asked me to fly into Las Vegas for a meeting with some-or-other nutrition company. He said that they wanted to meet me. Well, when I walked into the Bally's hotel for the meeting, a very tall and extremely handsome guy came to me and said: “Are you in our group?”

I affirmed and said yes. He then escorted me to the table at the restaurant where we would all be meeting. He seated me right across him and throughout the course of the meeting, he kept kicking my foot. Of every time he did this, I would look up and smile, so he was clearly getting the response he wanted. (Long after I found out that my boss was trying to play match maker between the two of us.
