# BEAUTIFUL AMBITION 87

With today's fast-moving society that is always demanding more, and putting a great deal of strain and stress on our patience, it becomes quite a challenge to maintain that forgiving outlook on life. Well, in case you needed more motivation to try, researchers have now discovered that living a consistently forgiving lifestyle, is actually good for your physical health.

It has been discovered that people living a forgiving lifestyle also experience lower cases of heart disease, cancer, and autoimmune diseases (to name a few) due to the lower levels of stress experienced for this conscious choosing of forgiveness over bitterness and resentment, or harbored anger.

As you can see, it is extremely beneficial to your health to reduce the number of grudges that you hold, and begin making a regular habit of forgiving people whenever possible. This is not to say that you should become a doormat, because that is not what forgiving is all about. What it does mean, is that when someone says they are sorry, and you believe that they mean it, forgive them, both by telling them that they are forgiven, and by meaning what you say!

You will feel much less stress, a lighter overall view on life, and feel much more content about the world around you, and, as you can see, you have a healthier life while you're at it. Avoiding an unforgiving life, also means avoiding much more than grudges—it is the emotional equivalent to an apple-a-day needed for regular health and happiness (to keep the doctor away).
