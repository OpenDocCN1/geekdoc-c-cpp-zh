# 15 QUESTIONS TO ANSWER BEFORE DECIDING

You can make all of your own decisions on your own. From starting a business, to changing careers, buying a house to choosing a vacation. Any decision is and can be really easy to make, following a couple of easy steps!

First, list all of your options. Once you have listed out your options, find the answers to these 16 questions for each of your options. You will know some of these answers and find out the others (as time goes on).

+   - Somewhere along the line, your best correct decision will be obvious.

+   - What is the goal or purpose of each option?

+   - How do the purposes of each option align with your goals?

+   - What are the statistics for each choice? Each of your options has statistics.

+   - Finances? Two vital questions: What will each option cost? How much money will each return? The cost is not a barrier if the predicted return is greater than the cost.

+   - Sequences? Most people forget to look at the exact steps involved with each solution. For example, you are notified by mail, "Congratulations! You have won either a deluxe AM/FM radio, $500 cash, a 60" TV or a cruise to Alaska!" You decide to go claim your prize. You never read the fine print or ask what steps are involved.
