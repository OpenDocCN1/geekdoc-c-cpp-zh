# 160 CHAPTER EIGHT Finding Mr. Right and Keeping Him

He also treated me like a princess. So in my case, I had to decide whether I was going to let fear cause me to sabotage our relationship, or if I should look fear in the eyes and take a leap of faith. I took that leap, and it has been almost thirteen years of marital “bliss”. We also share the same faith which has kept our lives balanced and on track.

We are partners in everything we do. We laugh, have a great time with each other, and of course, he is my best friend. However, I also have a life with our children, my girl friends, and the professional projects that I still like to tackle.

When I told Jason that I really wanted to write this book for younger women, in order to help and empower them to reach their full potential and dreams, he was completely supportive.

There have been times that I have been afraid, but he kept me on track, reminding me of my passion and commitment to be my ‘best’ self, and give my utmost and best in everything that I do.

When you find Mr. Right, it is especially important to be with someone that brings out your best qualities, not yours. We will all have good things about our personalities, as well as lesser positive things; ensure that you are with someone that motivates you to reach your fullest potential, instead of holding you back.

It is up to you to act the same way!
Do not be a dream killer——be a dream builder.

You want the person that you are with to live a full and happy life. Do not be afraid or insecure about that fact. This life of ours is so precious, and we only get one chance here on earth; so care for each other, loving and kind. Say nice things, and do not use harmful or hurtful words. Build each other up!
