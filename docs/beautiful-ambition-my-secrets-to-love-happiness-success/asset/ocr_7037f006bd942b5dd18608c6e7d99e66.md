# A GREAT EXAMPLE: HILARY SWANK

We do not all make perfect decisions for ourselves, but it is important to keep on top of what we want. This is to ensure that the decisions that we are making, and the decisions that we have made in the past, continue to work their magic for us.

Hilary Ann Swank has a great life-story to share and inspire, especially those getting a rough start in their lives, with the odds stacked against them!

She is a wonderful example of someone who has made the type of decisions that have worked well for her, serving her dreams positively, a helping at a time, throughout her life.

Learning from an early age, that small steps, overcoming mistakes and pulling through, beating the odds to speak, matter, and can make a difference.
