Instead of watching hours of TV, read for 15-20 minutes from a book that will teach you something new. Try to make it something that interests you and that you will enjoy, but also something that you will be able to use in your life.

+   - It is nice to take an after-dinner walk; especially if you have dogs because remember, they need their exercise to.
