# 104 CHAPTER SIX The Sisterhood

She is a tremendous example of somebody who took, addressed and overcame the obstacles in her life, in order to achieve what she wanted from it and then some. She is highly successful, ambitious and diligent.

After all, though she had little experience in the fashion industry aside from her modeling career, and had no fashion design background, she was able to take her former career, and her marriage to Russell Simmons (of Def Jam fame) and create a fashion business of incredible popularity, scope and depth, to great success.

Kimora’s life has been challenging since birth. She grew up in a home where her father was a convicted felon and served time in a Federal Prison for drug offenses and charges. This led to continual financial issues in her home, despite the fact that her mother had a well-paying job with the Social Security Administration. Little did she know that her two racial backgrounds and extreme height—5'10” at ten years old, and 6' by fourteen years old—would help her to begin a profitable modeling career!

This career started at thirteen years old, to assist her mother’s financial troubles. When she reached her full mature height at fourteen years old, she became a muse for Karl Lagerfeld (of the House of Chanel).

Though she achieved only her high school education—her academic career being cut out by her modeling career—she is merry advocate of continuing education, and has donated large amounts of money to causes focused on overcoming educational deficiencies.

It was her marriage to Russell Simmons, and their two children, would become the inspiration for the Kimora Baby Phat Kids Collection. Kimora was not, however, the founder of Baby Phat, as many would believe. This company, called Phat, was actually already in existence, having been founded by Russell Simmons. It was the Baby Phat design that was created by Kimora, and she became creative director for clothing-line, label and brand.
