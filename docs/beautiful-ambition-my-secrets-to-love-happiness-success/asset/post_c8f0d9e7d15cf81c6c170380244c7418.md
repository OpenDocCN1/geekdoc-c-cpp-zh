## Things That Women Do That Drive Men Away Early On

So ladies, here are some of the things that you do that drive men away early-on and they share these things with their male friends just like you do with the girls! It may surprise you, but it is true. (Believe me, men chat it up too! They talk about the way we act (all the time).)

If any of these things apply to you, take notice, and make the necessary adjustments and corrections accordingly—DO NOT self-sabotage or undermine your own efforts and chance with the guys or Mr. Right!

The dating game is exactly what it seems or appears to be: A GAME!

Depending how you play this game determines if you will win the man of your dreams (alias Mr. Right!) or be destined to live alone with way too many cats to count, thinking everybody else has it all wrong.

- Do you make too many calls and emails to him when you just met and are in the getting-to-know-one-another phase?
- Do you talk about all of your problems early on, letting him right off the bat that you do not have it together? You make the first move thinking he will find that "sexy" when really you killed the chasing game that men love to play. Ladies, they are hunters! Do not kill the excitement for them. Let them chase you. They love it! This is the best part for them. Work and play a little hard-to-get and challenge them. This does not mean treat them badly or break dates, but let them email you and call you! Let them take the lead and set up dates in the beginning.
- Do not call him at work and while he is out with his friends constantly—this makes you look insecure and needy.
- While on your first date at dinner, offer to pay for your half of the bill. This will tell you a lot about the guy already. If he allows you to pay, then guess what, get used to it. If he refuses and picks up the tab, then he is a true gentleman and most likely will always be generous. You are not taking advantage here, but you are looking for all the right characteristics and there is nothing wrong with that. Plus, he will find it really sweet that you offered to pay and that you are not just looking for a meal ticket.
- Do not ask him too many questions about his Ex. Do not say "are you still in love with her?" or about what they did in the bedroom. Too many women obsess about the ex and previous relationships. The first thing you need to know is that it is his ex and that they are not together anymore, so quit bringing her to his attention all the time.
- Understand what he likes and do not try to drag him to things you know he hates. For example, he really might not want to go shopping with you. Most men hate shopping and he does want to watch you spend your money on things that he finds frivolous like "shoes". He has only three pairs and will never understand why you need 100 pairs. Plus, you are only showing him how you are going to be spending his hard-earned money if you were to get married or live together, and it will freak him out. No, he also does not want to see the latest chicklick or tearjerker. It is simply not his thing. That is what your girlfriends are for!

During the months of August through January they are going to be very focused on, yes, "Football Season", That means college games are on Saturday afternoon. Monday night football is like religion to them so respect it! Here is what you do: Get to know his favorite team and go online to check out who the star players are for that team. You may hate football but your man loves it, so it would behoove you to learn about it just a bit. You may have to fake it. Then he will find it really sweet and cute that you know the star players. You will gain major points and if you look at the schedule a week at a time, you can schedule events that are important to you around the games. Just thinking of you, go fun with the boys.” He will be eating out of your hand in no time flat!

They are not wired like women and do not work or think that way. YES or NO usually works well for them and they typically DO NOT make decisions that are “emotional” like we do. Try to understand the basics of how men think and tick, because it will serve you all around, every time!
