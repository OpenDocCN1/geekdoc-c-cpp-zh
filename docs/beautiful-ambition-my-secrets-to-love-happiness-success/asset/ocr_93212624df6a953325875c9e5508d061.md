Now, here are examples of things that YOU can do NOW, to start building your self-esteem:

Write out ten things that you like yourself (it can be anything from your eyes) for your sense of humor. Be creative when you are making the list and really give it some thought).

Now that you have your list, take a mirror and begin to tell yourself the following things aloud:

+   - I am beautiful

+   - I am ambitious,

+   - Start telling yourself all the things that made your list—do this every day for a month (** NOTE: At the end of the month, you will begin to see a change in how you feel about yourself).

+   - This is NOT arrogant at all. You are starting to develop and realize your self worth. Do not SELF-SABOTAGE or let anyone take that from you; especially you!

+   - Start complimenting others on the things that you see in them that are GREAT—even if you DO NOT know them.

+   - Smile when you pass by others. (You never know what that smile can do for someone who is feeling down).

+   - If you see someone that has beautiful eyes, tell them, let them know! It will make them feel great, but you get the real blessing.

+   - Practice more patience! We all hate to wait in lines, but sometimes that is the way it goes. We do not all get the red-carpet treatment and that is okay too.
