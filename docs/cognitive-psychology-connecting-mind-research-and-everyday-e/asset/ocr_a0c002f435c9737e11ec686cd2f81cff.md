**Figure 11.16** The Branigan et al. (2000) experiment. (a) The subject (right) picks, from the cards laid out on the table, a card with a picture that matches the statement read by the confederate (left). (b) The subject then takes a card from the pile of response cards and describes the picture on the response card to the confederate. This is the key part of the experiment because the question is whether the subject on the right will match the syntactic construction used by the subject on the left. *(Source: Based on H. P. Branigan, M. J. Pickering, & A. A. Cleland, Syntactic co-ordination in dialogue, Cognition, 75, B13–B25, 2000.)*
