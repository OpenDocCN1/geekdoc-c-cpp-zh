
- **Whole report method** Procedure used in Sperling's experiment on the properties of the visual icon, in which participants were instructed to report all of the stimuli they saw in a brief presentation. See also **Partial report method**; **Sensory memory**. *(5)*
- **Word frequency** The relative usage of words in a particular language. For example, in English, *home* has higher word frequency than *hike*. *(11)*
- **Word frequency effect** The phenomenon of faster reading time for high-frequency words than for low-frequency words. *(11)*
- **Word length effect** The notion that it is more difficult to remember a list of long words than a list of short words. *(5)*
- **Word superiority effect** The idea that letters are easier to identify when they are part of a word than when they are seen in isolation or in a string of letters that do not form a word. *(11)*
- **Working memory** A limited-capacity system for temporary storage and manipulation of information for complex tasks such as comprehension, learning, and reasoning. *(5)*
