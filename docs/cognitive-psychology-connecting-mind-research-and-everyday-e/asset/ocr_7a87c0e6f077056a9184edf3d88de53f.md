(b) When a word has one dominant meaning and one less dominant (biased dominance), only the most frequent meaning of a word is activated, which results in fast access. (c) and (d) illustrate situations in which context precedes a word with biased dominance: (c) If the less dominant meaning is indicated by the context, it is activated, but so is the more dominant meaning, even though it doesn't fit the context.
