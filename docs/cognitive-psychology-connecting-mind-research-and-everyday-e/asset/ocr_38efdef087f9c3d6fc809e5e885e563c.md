This famous student is Prince William, studying in the library at St. Andrews University in Scotland, where he received a degree in geography in 2005. No matter who you are, being a student involves taking in information and being able to remember it later. This chapter describes encoding—how to get information into memory—and retrieval—how to get it out later. Encoding and retrieval can be described in terms of both psychological and physiological processes. Research on these processes has resulted in insights about ways to study more effectively.
