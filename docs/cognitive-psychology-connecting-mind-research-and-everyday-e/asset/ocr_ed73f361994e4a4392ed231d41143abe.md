This picture of the brain as seen from the bottom has been artistically embellished with colors and patterns that don’t exist in real brains. The beauty of this fanciful image is in the way it symbolizes the mysteries of the brain’s operation. Cognitive neuroscience, the study of the physiological mechanisms of cognition, has revealed what is happening in the brain during cognition, and has provided insights that have added to what researchers have learned from purely behavioral experiments. This chapter provides the foundation for understanding the physiological research that you will read about in the chapters that follow.
