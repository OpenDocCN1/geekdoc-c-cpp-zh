Here’s something to think about: One male is selected randomly from the population of the United States. That male, Robert, wears glasses, speaks quietly, and reads a lot. Is it more likely that Robert is a librarian or a farmer? This is one of the questions posed in an experiment we will discuss in this chapter. The way subjects answered this question, plus the results of many other experiments in which people were asked to make judgments, has helped us understand the mental processes involved in making judgments. This chapter also considers the mental processes involved in the closely related topics of decision making and reasoning.
