Each of these projections has a near plane and a far plane. The near plane is typically very close to the camera. Anything between the camera and the near plane is not visible onscreen. This is why games have objects partially disappear if the camera gets too close to them. Similarly, the far plane is far away from the camera, and anything past it is not visible. Games sometimes allow the player to reduce the “draw distance” to improve performance. This is often just a matter of pulling in the far plane.
