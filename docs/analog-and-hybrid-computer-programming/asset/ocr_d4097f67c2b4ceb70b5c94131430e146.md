MICHAELIS-MENTEN kinetics is a classic model of enzyme kinetics and takes the form

$S + E \underset{k_1}{\stackrel{k_2}{\rightleftharpoons}} \varepsilon \xrightarrow{k_3} P + E,$

with $S$, $E$, $\varepsilon$, and $P$ denoting the concentrations of a substrate, the enzyme, the enzyme-substrate complex, and the final product of the reaction. The free enzyme on the right-hand side is available for new enzyme-substrate complexes, i. e. the enzyme is not consumed during the overall process.⁷⁷ This system is described by the following set of equations:⁷⁸

```
ṡ = -k₁ e s + k₂ ε
ė = -k₁ e s + (k₂ + k₃) ε
ε̇ = k₁ e s - (k₂ + k₃) ε
ṗ = k₃ ε
```
