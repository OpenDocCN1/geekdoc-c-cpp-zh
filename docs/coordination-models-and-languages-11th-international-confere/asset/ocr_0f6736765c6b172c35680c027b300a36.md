The authors comment that although the reaction enthalpy is moderate, it is the potential exothermic decomposition of the product 104 that is problematic. Side reactions can also arise from oxidation of the starting material 105 and other components of the reaction mixture, such as careful control of the reaction conditions is required.

To perform the organic nitration, the substrate 105 and solvent were brought together in a microstructure that afforded a fine emulsion; this was followed by the addition of neat nitric acid 95 at which point the reaction started immediately.
