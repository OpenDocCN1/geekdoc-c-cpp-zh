In addition to the seven deoxygenation and dehalogenation reactions demonstrated, the authors adapted the methodology to the hydrosilylation of a series of alkynes and alkenes. To conduct such reactions, a premixed solution of the alkyne or alkene (1.00 M), tris(trimethylsilyl) silane 61 (1.2 M) and AIBN 62 (10 mol%) in toluene was passed through the heated microreactor (130 °C) at a flow rate of 200 μL min⁻¹, affording a residence time of 5 min.

As Table 5 illustrates, compared to batchwise reactions the increased heat and mass transfer obtained within the microfluidic reactor afforded enhanced *cis/trans* selectivity for reactions employing alkynes and more generically a dramatic reduction in reaction time (cf. conventional batch reactions).
