Multiphase microflows are dominated by pressures (Aota et al., 2007a, 2009a). One important parameter needed to describe the multiphase microflows is the pressure that drives the fluids. The pressure decreases in the downstream part of the flow because of the fluids’ viscosity. When two fluids in contact with one another have different viscosities, the pressure difference (ΔP_Flow) between the two phases is a function of the contact length and the flow velocity. Another important parameter is the Laplace pressure (ΔP_Laplace) caused by the interfacial tension between two phases. The position of the interface is fixed within a point in the microchannel by the balance established between the ΔP_Laplace and ΔP_Flow.
