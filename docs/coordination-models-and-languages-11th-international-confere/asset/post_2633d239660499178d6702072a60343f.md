## 4. DISCUSSION AND CONCLUSIONS

The basic methodologies and applications for microfluidics were introduced. In Chapter 2, the general concepts (MUO and CFCP) for microintegration and applications based on CFCP will be discussed. CFCP approach can be applied to develop more complicated chemical processing system. If CAD for microchemical processing, which includes microfluid simulation, reaction time analysis, extraction time analysis, and microchannel structure design, the time of development for microchemical systems would be drastically shortened. In Chapter 3, fluid control methods will be discussed. The viscous force and the interfacial tension are effective forces in microspaces. In order to control these forces, surface chemistry and microchannel structures play important roles. Since the basic methodologies have already been developed, development of microfluidic systems for practical applications is required in the next stage.
