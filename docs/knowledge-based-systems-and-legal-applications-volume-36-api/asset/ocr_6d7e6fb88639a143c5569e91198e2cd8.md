# 14.1. POLICY MAKING WITHIN THE DSS — THE NATURE OF THE PROBLEM

It is with the policy system that we are particularly concerned in this chapter as it is only in this application that a significant amount of group working needs to be catered for.

The DSS is a very large organization with about 93,000 staff administering roughly five million claims each year from approximately 500 offices around the UK. There are great many people involved in monitoring and formulating policy on behalf of the minister in central policy branches and the regional directorate. In the policy branch concerned with formulating the policy for income support, one of the main social security benefits, there were as many as 300 people involved.

Most of the day-to-day work of a policy worker involves the explanation of policy. Queries about the way the legislation is working or the justification for legislation arise as a result of questions being asked in Parliament, from pressure groups, from concerned individuals, or from reviews within the policy branches themselves.
