Lastly, in terms of their social impact, it is probably important to maintain a reasonable perspective on the dangers associated with these systems. The chances of systems failing due to insufficient consideration of human issues are considerable. The likelihood of undesirable social consequences resulting from these systems is more questionable. Certainly, focusing on the technology per se is likely to serve little purpose when the organizational stereotypes and managerial expectations have by far the greater impact. The objective of organizational analysts should be to demonstrate the options available and the formative context in which decisions are made so that the political nature of the decisions is not hidden behind the inevitability of the technology.
