Each policy worker would have direct access to statistics and policy argument databases and could browse, on-screen, any of the acts and regulations, commissioners' decisions, case law and reports that he or she chose to. The system would have links out to statistics branch, treasury, regional directorate, office of the chief adjudication officer, solicitors branch, operations research branch, and perhaps elsewhere: pressure groups, other departments or agencies, perhaps even to the minister.
