One motivation for these various extensions is that sentences in a typical text vary in their character. Some definitions of legal concepts in more or less precise terms. Others express norms of conduct. Still others describe how other parts of the text should be read, or state exceptions and modifications to general rules appearing elsewhere. The list is not exhaustive. It would clearly be desirable to attempt some kind of classification, perhaps developing a particular construct or set of constructs for each type. [Bench-Capon et al. 1987] and [Routen 1989] make some specific suggestions along these lines.
