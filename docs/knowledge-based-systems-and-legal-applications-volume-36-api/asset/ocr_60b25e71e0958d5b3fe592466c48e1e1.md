Allen's normalized form is based on a disciplined use of the propo-

Allen's normalized form has had some influence on the way that

describes the experience of encountering a statute in normalized form. Allen and
some of the practical difficulties that they encountered. Saxon

a form which supports only the meaning intended.

Allen and Saxon [1986] have recommended that legal provisions

#  111.2. Commentary

The only computer-aided drafting tools based on Allen's recommendations
(of which I am aware) are the normalization aids referred to earlier.
These help to translate a document into normalized form, but they do
ot attempt to proceed beyond this, to process the resulting representa-
s further.
