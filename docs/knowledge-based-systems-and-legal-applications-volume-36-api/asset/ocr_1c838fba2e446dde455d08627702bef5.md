# 15.2.3. Knowledge analysis

Knowledge analysis is frequently thought of being the responsibility of the more technically-oriented knowledge engineer than the organizational analyst. While there are many aspects of knowledge analysis which are so oriented, it is naive and dangerous to believe that decisions about the scope of knowledge to be included in a system, the form of its presentation and its organization, and the procedures for maintaining and updating it, do not arise organizational issues of considerable importance.
