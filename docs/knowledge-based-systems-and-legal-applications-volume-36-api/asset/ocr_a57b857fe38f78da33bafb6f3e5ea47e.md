It would be interesting to examine the nature of the mixed initiative interaction with other types of computer system, especially those that differ from ones presented here. It remains to be seen what factors can be identified to describe mixed initiative systems that are less ‘conversational’; those where turns take place simultaneously, those where turns are spread around a computer screen and those where turns appear on different media.
