# Display 5.24 Interactions test, main effects test, and mean calculations for a 2×2 experiment

```
data twoway;
    input response factor1 $ factor2 $ @@;
    datalines;
5 1 1   7 1 1
10 1 2   11 1 2   12 1 2
8 2 1    9 2 1   10 2 1
13 2 2   14 2 2   15 2 2
;
run;

ods rtf bodytitle;
proc means data=twoway maxdec=1;
    class factor1 factor2;      * calculate cell means;
    var response;
run;

proc means data=twoway maxdec=1;
    class factor1;              * calculate factor 1 level means;
    var response;               * (pool over factor 2 levels);
run;
proc means data=twoway maxdec=1;
    class factor2;              * calculate factor 2 level means;
    var response;               * (pool over factor 1 levels);
run;

proc glm data=twoway;
    class factor1 factor2;
    model response = factor1 | factor2;
    means factor1 factor2;
    lsmeans factor1 factor2;
run;
ods rtf close;
```

The output from the three invocations of PROC MEANS is shown in Display 5.25. The first table includes the four cell means. For example, the mean of the (1,1) cell is 6=(5+7)/2. The mean of the (1,2) cell is 11=(10+11+12)/3. The second table includes the means of all five observations in the first level of FACTOR1, and all six observations in the second level of FACTOR1. The mean of the first level of FACTOR1 is 9=(5+7+10+11+12)/5. The mean of the second level of FACTOR1 is 11.5=(8+9+10+13+14+15)/6. The third table contains analogous summaries for the levels of FACTOR2.
