The LSMEANS statement reports a value of 8.5 for the same level of the same factor. Why do these values differ? The MEANS statement reports the mean of n11+n12=5 observations in the first level of FACTOR1 (9 =(5+7+10+11+12)/5). The LSMEANS statement reports the mean of the sample means of the two cells in level 1 of FACTOR1 ((6+11)/2=8.5).
