# Spherify modifier

The Spherify modifier distorts an object into a spherical shape. The single Spherify parameter is the percent of the effect to apply. Figure 11.20 shows the hammer with Spherify values of 10, 20, and 30 percent.

The Spherify modifier is different from the Push modifier. Although they both are applied to the entire object, the Push modifier forces all vertices continuously outward, and the Spherify modifier uses a sphere shape as a limiting boundary. The visible difference is that the Spherify modifier creates a bulging effect.
