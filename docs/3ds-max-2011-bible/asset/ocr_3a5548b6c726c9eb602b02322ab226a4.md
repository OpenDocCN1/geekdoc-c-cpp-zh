# FIGURE 7.18

The Snaps panel includes many different points to snap to depending on the object type.

![](bbox=[0.077, 0.654, 0.586, 0.828])

After snap points have been defined, the Snap buttons on the main toolbar activate the Snap feature, or you can press the K key. The first Snap button consists of a flyout with three buttons: 3D Snap toggle, 2.5D Snap toggle, and 2D Snap toggle. The 2D Snap toggle button limits all snaps to the active construction grid. The 2.5D Snap toggle button snaps to points on the construction grid as well as projected points from objects in the scene. The 3D Snap toggle button can snap to any points in 3D space.
