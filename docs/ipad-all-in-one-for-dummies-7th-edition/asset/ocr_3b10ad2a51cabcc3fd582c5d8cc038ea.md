Almost everybody on Earth has heard of the iPod — that small, portable, music-playing device from Apple that's seemingly glued to the ears of many. The iPad includes an iPod-like app Music that allows you to take advantage of the iPad's pretty amazing little sound to play your favorite style of music or podcasts or audiobooks.

In this chapter, you can get acquainted with the Music app and its features that enable you to find music and control playback from your iPad. You also get an overview of the AirPlay feature, which you can use to play music on your iPad over a home network. Finally, I introduce you to iTunes Radio.
