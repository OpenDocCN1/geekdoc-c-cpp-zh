# Being Entertained by iPad

One of the joys of the iPad is its use as a media consumption tool. Playing music and watching videos are very entertaining uses of iPad, indeed. bundled Music and Videos apps make playing media easy to do. In addition, by using the Books app can use your iPad as an e-reader, and the Podcasts app opens up a world of audio content.

# Playing around with Music

Unless you've been living in a cave without 3G/4G or satellite TV for the last years, you know perfectly that the iPod has historically been the Apple-preferred reader. On your iPad, the Music app is your media player with a heavy emphasis on music. You can use Music to play music, audio podcasts, or earbuds.

One of the nicest things about Music on the iPad is that the iPad comes with a nifty sound system and stereo speakers, so listening or without headphones will be a pleasing experience for everyone who added to MP3 listening devices. You can also browse your music by a variety of criteria, such as artist, album, song, or genre.

# Watching Videos

You can use the Videos app to play TV or movies, and it offers some useful features for controlling video playback.

The Videos app is a media player like Music, but it specializes in videos and offers a few more features, such as breakdowns of the various chapters in video and information about related movies and casts for media you got from iTunes. You can move between widescreen and full-screen display, and it shines showing high-definition content, especially with the third-generation (and later) iPad's Retina display. See Book II, Chapter 3 for more on the Videos app.
