# Making 3G/4G settings

When you use a Wi-Fi model of iPad, in addition to modifying Wi-Fi settings as covered in the preceding section, you can make changes to your cellular data in Settings. Cellular Data settings enable you to manage your roaming and account information (see Figure 1-3). You can use these settings to sign up for a data plan by entering your name, phone number, and credit card information; when you submit this, your account is set up with your provider with a unique phone number associated with the account. You need this phone number to speak to customer representatives about your account.

You can find the following items under the Cellular Data category in Settings:

+   * Cellular Data: Turn 3G/4G off or here.

+   * Enable LTE: To enable your iPad to search for and connect to LTE networks, work on this setting. Data may load faster using LTE, but remember that downloading still takes a toll on your battery life.

+   * Data Roaming: Data Roaming is a feature of cellular networks that takes advantage of other carrier' networks, if you're out of range of your carrier's network. Using data roaming can result in additional charges, so you may want to turn it off when you don't need it.

+   * View Account: Tap this setting to view account information.

+   * Add a PIN: You may want to protect the data on your SIM card from prying eyes. Beyond a setting to unlock your iPad, you can assign a PIN to your SIM card. When you've assigned a PIN, whenever try to use a new SIM card or restart your Wi-Fi or 3G/4G iPad, you have to enter the password. Don't forget your PIN, or you can't unlock your SIM card.

With Wi-Fi and 3G/4G model iPad, you also have access to the Airplane Mode in both the Settings and Control Center. This allows you to disable wireless features of your device by airline regulations when you're on an airline flight. With Airplane Mode turned on, you won't be able to browse, email, sync, buy online, or stream videos (which would only be able to do with a 3G/4G model miles up in the sky) unless the airline provides Wi-Fi. If it does, you can use the Wi-Fi setting in the Control panel to enable this, but do turn it off before takeoff and landing. Also, the GPS locator of Maps won't work, but you can play content that you've downloaded, play games, and create documents in apps like Pages and Numbers. Just don't forget to turn off the Airplane Mode setting when you land!
