# Going to an App from Notification Center

+   1. Swipe down from the top of the screen to display Notification Center.

+   2. Tap Today (see Figure 4-12) to show all reminders and other items you've selected to display in Notification Center (see the earlier section, "Setting Notification Types") that occur today.

Note that you can also swipe to the right to move to the next tab.

+   3. Tap the Notifications tab to see items for today and tomorrow missed and other future items.

# Going to an App from Notification Center

To easily jump from Notification Center to any app that causes an alert or reminder to be displayed, follow these steps:

+   1. Swipe down from the top of the screen.

Notification Center displays.

+   2. Tap any item in a category, such as Reminders or Stocks.

The item opens in its originating app.

If you've tapped an email message, you can reply to the message using the procedures described in Book I, Chapter 7.
