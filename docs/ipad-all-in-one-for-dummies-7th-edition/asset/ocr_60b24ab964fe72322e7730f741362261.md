# Understanding All Siri Can Do

Siri also checks with Wikipedia, Bing, and Twitter to give you the information you ask for. In addition, you can use Siri to tell iPad to play your voicemail, open and search the App Store, or control iTunes Radio playback.

Siri is the technology behind the iPad's Dictation feature. If you have the onscreen keyboard, note that it contains the Dictation key, which you can tap to dictate and tap to end dictation. This works in any app that uses the onscreen keyboard.

When you ask a question about, setting up an appointment, Siri responds to you verbally and with text information, in a form as email (see Figure 6-5) or in a graphic display for some items such as maps. When a result appears, you can tap it to make a choice or open a repeated run.

With iOS 8, you can activate Siri hands-free. With your iPhone plugged into a wall outlet, car power port, or computer's USB port, say "Hey, Siri" and Siri opens up ready for a command. In addition, with voice recognition, Siri displays with text what it's hearing as you speak, so you can verify that it understood you instantly. This streaming feature makes the whole process of interacting with Siri faster.

With iOS 8, Siri now supports 22 languages, so you can finally show off language lessons you teach in high school.
