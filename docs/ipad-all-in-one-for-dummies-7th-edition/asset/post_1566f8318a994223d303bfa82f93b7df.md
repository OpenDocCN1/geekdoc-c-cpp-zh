
# Connecting to an Enterprise

## Connecting with Personal Hotspot or iPhone Tethering

If you want to connect your Wi-Fi-only iPad to your smartphone to go online out of range of a Wi-Fi network, you can tether the iPad to the phone using Verizon's, T-Mobile's, Sprint's Personal Hotspot and AT&T's iPhone 3G/4G Tethering. Tethering with any of these services allows your iPad to tap into your phone's data connection to go online. You can connect anywhere your phone gives a connection — even if you have a Wi-Fi-only model.

Tethering involves a few challenges. You don't go online instantly; you have to complete a few steps first, the connection speed might be a tad slow, and it can drain your phone battery quickly.

After you're comfortable with the trade-offs of the tethering option, you need to do two things:

- 1. **Add the tethering option for your carrier on the carrier's website logging in to your account and adding the feature.**

This can come with a cost; with AT&T you might pay for tethering support (based on GB of usage) anywhere from $50 to $100 at the time of this writing.

- 2. **Make the setting on your iPad on the tethering service by doing the following:**

    - a. Tap the Settings icon.
    - b. Tap Wi-Fi.
    - c. Tap the tethering service in the Choose Network and make settings specific to your carrier.

Sprint, T-Mobile, and Verizon pricing and features for iPad Air 2/mini 3 example. Verizon offers an individual plan at $45 a month and a family plan called Everything for up to 4 lines. AT&T offers 3GB of data for $30 a month and 40GB of data for a family for $150. You need both a basic data plan and a tethering plan which could cost an additional $50, which can add up. Still, if you often wander into areas that have no Wi-Fi and you don't have a 3G or 4G iPad, it may — or may not — be more cost effective to use this feature.

# Connecting to an Enterprise

Exchange support opens possibilities for connecting an enterprise-network and its data remotely. If you work at a company using Microsoft Exchange and use an iPad, this is very good news.

Thanks to these enterprise features, here are some of the things you can do to connect to your organization's network and data.

## Setting up a configuration profile

Microsoft Exchange is a messaging standard that allows the exchange of information between networks. With a configuration profile in place, you can set up a Microsoft Exchange account on your iPad. **A configuration profile** is a way for the network administrator at your enterprise to set up the systems in your company, via Microsoft Exchange or a VPN (virtual private network), which controls access to corporate email or contacts.

Your administrator should check out the Enterprise Deployment Guide and the iPad Configurator utility from Apple (www.apple.com/support/ipad/enterprise). After setting up a configuration profile is in place, it can be emailed to you or placed on a secure web page. Alternatively, your company's network administrator can install a configuration profile for you.

When you receive a configuration profile, you can install it yourself by opening the message and tapping the file. Tap Install and enter any information that's requested, such as your password.

## Setting up a Microsoft Exchange account

Many companies use Microsoft Exchange to exchange their email, contacts, and calendar information with devices. iPhone has supported Microsoft Exchange for a while now, and iPad carries on the tradition. You can use Microsoft Exchange to wirelessly sync that information to your iPad from your corporate network.

![](img/2a8a8d89daeb1b3896bd38ebf18af126_311_0.png)

One benefit of connection to Microsoft Exchange is that your IT administrator can wipe the data and settings of a device remotely using a command in Exchange if it's lost or stolen, keeping confidential business contacts private. Another option for erasing content on a lost device is to use iCloud's Find My iPad feature, covered in more detail in Book 1, Chapter 9.

To set up Microsoft Exchange, follow these steps:

- 1. Tap Settings.
- 2. Tap Mail, Contacts, Calendars.
- 3. Tap Add Account.
- 4. Tap Exchange.
- 5. Enter your information shown in Figure 1-3 and tap Next.
- 6. Tap Save.

**Figure 1-3:** Exchange settings.

## Setting up an LDAP account

You can also use Lightweight Access Protocol (LDAP) accounts which many organizations use to store data. Here's how to set up on iPad:

- 1. In Settings, tap Mail, Contacts, Calendars.
- 2. Tap Add Account.
- 3. Tap Other.
- 4. Tap LDAP.
- 5. Enter your information (see Figure 1-4) and tap Next.
- 6. Tap Save.

![](img/2a8a8d89daeb1b3896bd38ebf18af126_313_0.png)
**Figure 1-4:** LDAP settings.

When you've set up this account, contacts appear as a group in iPad. To see these, your iPad has to be connected to the Internet because they aren't stored locally. If any necessary, check with your network administrator for LDAP requirements.

## Setting up a virtual private network

If you want to connect to your organization's network, you may be able to use a virtual private network, or VPN. A VPN, which works over Wi-Fi and 3G/4G cellular connections, allows you to access data securely whether you're using a network outside your company — such as at home or in a café — to access servers inside your company. After a VPN is set up at your company, you can use VPN settings on your iPad.

**TIP** You might need to ask your network administrator for information on how to make settings for a VPN. If you set up on your computer, you should be able to use the same VPN settings on your iPad.

Add a new VPN configuration, go to Settings > General > VPN > Add VPN configuration. The settings shown in Figure 1-5 appear. Fill in the information requested and tap Save.

![](img/2a8a8d89daeb1b3896bd38ebf18af126_314_0.png)
**Figure 1-5:** Add Configuration settings.

You can have multiple VPNs set up, for example if you consult with companies with their own VPNs. Tap the one you want to use, and then set the VPN switch to On. To switch VPNs, disconnect from one, select the new one, and then turn VPN back on. Only one VPN can be active at any time. To delete a VPN configuration, tap the arrow to the right of the configuration in the General settings and tap Delete VPN.
