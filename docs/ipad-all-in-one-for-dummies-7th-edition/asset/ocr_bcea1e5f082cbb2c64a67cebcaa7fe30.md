# 224 Experiencing Twitter and Facebook on iPad

# Twitter or posting to Facebook

When you have your Twitter account set up, you can be in a compatible app such as Safari, Photos, Camera, and Maps, and choose Twitter in the screen that appears when you tap the Share button. You see a Tweet like the one shown in Figure 5-3. Just write your message in the form and then tap Send. Note that a number in the bottom-left corner lets you know how many characters you have left to use in your tweet.

To post to Facebook, just tap Facebook in the Share menu (refer to Figure 5-2), and follow pretty much the same procedure as Twitter, except that you are allowed to choose who can see your posting: the general public, your friends, just you, or all friends (which includes friends of friends).

When you're filling in the tweet or Facebook posting, you can tap Location and then tap Enable to allow that to turn on Location Services, which allows Twitter to ascertain your location and share it with others. Be cautious about using this feature: if many people follow you on Twitter, they can be able to track your movements and where you live, where you go to school, where you socialize, and so on. This information can be used to commit a variety of online and offline crimes.
