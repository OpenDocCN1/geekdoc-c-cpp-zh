+   5. **Continue to change the effects and options until you're satisfied.**

Options vary depending on the effect you've selected. You'll probably want to try the combinations by tapping the Play button in the upper-right side of the popover several times.

+   6. **Tap Done in the upper-right corner of the screen to apply the transition and return to the navigator.**

After you apply a transition to a slide, the Transitions button appears next to the slider when you select that slide in the navigator (see Figure 4-11). You can tap the Transition option to open the Transitions popover to edit the effect.
