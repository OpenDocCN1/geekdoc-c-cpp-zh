# Understanding Multitasking Basics

Multitasking means that you can easily switch from one app to another without closing the first one and returning to the Home screen. This is accomplished by viewing all open apps and jumping from one to another, and quitting an app by simply swiping upward. Follow these steps to multitask:

+   1. Open an app.
2. Press the Home button twice.
3. On the App Switcher view that appears (see Figure 2-15), scroll to the left or right until you find the other open app that you want to switch.
4. Tap an app to switch to it.

Press the Home button to close the App Switcher and return to the app you were working in.
