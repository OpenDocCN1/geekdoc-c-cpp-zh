You can also mirror or stream what's on your iPad connected to your HDTV's HDMI port.

Mirroring shows what's on your screen, such as a Keynote slide. You can zoom in and rotate your screen on your iPad, and that's what you'll be mirrored on your TV. As you move through your Keynote presentation on your iPad, the presentation on the TV changes right along with it.
