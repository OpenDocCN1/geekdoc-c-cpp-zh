# 184 Looking Over Your Library of Music

# Viewing the library contents

You can easily view the contents of your library collections, which you may have synced from your computer or shared over a network, pushed to your device through iCloud, or downloaded directly using iPad's Wi-Fi or Wi-Fi+ capability. (See Chapter 1 in this minibook for details.)

Take a tour of your Music library collections by following these simple steps:

+   1. Tap the Music app, located in the Dock on the Home screen.
The music library appears (see Figure 2-1).

![](bbox=[0.296, 0.343, 0.703, 0.638])

Figure 2-1: The Music library showing your music by Artists.

+   2. Tap the Playlists, Artists, Songs, Albums, Genres, Compilations (such as the Best of British Rock), or Composers at the bottom of the library to view your music according these criteria. (Refer to Figure 2-1.)

The Purchased button appears only if you've already obtained content on iTunes.

+   3. Tap the Radio button (see Figure 2-2) to view this feature, discussed in more detail later in this chapter.
