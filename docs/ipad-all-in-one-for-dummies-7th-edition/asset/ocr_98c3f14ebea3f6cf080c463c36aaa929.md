# Using Stopwatch and Timer

Sometimes life feels like a countdown or a ticking clock counting up the minutes you've spent on a certain activity. You can use the Stopwatch tab of the Clock app to time the length of an activity such as a walk. Use the Timer feature to do a countdown to a specific time such as the moment when chocolate chip cookies are done baking.
