8. Use the Shift keys just as you would on a regular keyboard to type uppercase letters.

Tapping the Shift key once causes just the next letter you type to be capitalized.

9. Double-tap the Shift key to turn on the Caps Lock feature so that all letters you are capitalized until you turn the feature off.

Tap the Shift key to turn it off.

You can control whether this feature is available in Settings, for the General pane's Keyboard section.

10. To type a variation on a symbol (for example, get alternative symbols when you hold down the dollar sign on the numeric keyboard), and hold the key until a set of alternative symbols appears (see Figure 2-10).

Note that displaying variations on symbols works only when on some symbols.

11. Tap the Dictation key (refer to Figure 2-9) to activate Dictation feature, and shake your input.

This works in several apps, as Mail, Notes, and Maps. Tap the Dictation key (or tap in the note) to turn off the Dictation feature.

12. Tap the Emoji key (a smiley face to the left of the Dictation-button) and select from a set of smiley symbols to insert in your document. Tap tabs along the bottom to display other graphic icons such as pictures from nature or city skylines.
