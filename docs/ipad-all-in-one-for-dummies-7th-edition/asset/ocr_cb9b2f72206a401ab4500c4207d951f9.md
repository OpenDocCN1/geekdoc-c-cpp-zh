His chapter delves into the Pages app, which is the word-processing and page-layout tool in the iWork suite of apps. Pages gives you powerful tools for creating documents of all kinds.

In this chapter, I tell you how to work with text and text boxes, format a document, and use the simple-to-use Pages interface.

To find basic instructions for managing documents and sharing them with WebDAV, iCloud, email, or iTunes, see Chapter 1 in this minibook.
