# # Using bookmarks and highlights

Bookmarks and highlights in your e-books are favorites that you may be familiar with saving in your web browser: They enable you to revisit a favorite or refresh your memory about a character or plot point. Note that iBooks retain these bookmarks and highlights across iDevices and Macs.

To add and use bookmarks and highlights, follow these steps:

+   1. With a book you want to read, tap the Bookmark (the red ribbon) in the upper-right corner, as seen in Figure 7-15.

A colored bookmark is placed on the right-hand page.
