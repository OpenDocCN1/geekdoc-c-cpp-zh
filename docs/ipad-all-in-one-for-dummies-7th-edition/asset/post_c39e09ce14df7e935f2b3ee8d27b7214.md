# Shopping Anywhere Else

Many stores have added iPad-friendly videos to their collections, so you do have alternatives to iTunes for your choice of movies or TV shows. You can also shop for music from sources other than iTunes, such as Amazon.

You can open accounts at these stores using your computer or iPad's Safari browser and then following a store's instructions for purchasing and downloading content. Keep in mind that costs will vary. For example, one provider is Hulu. To get iPad-friendly content from Hulu, you have to sign up for the Hulu Plus service and pay a monthly fee (less than $10). Then, download the app directly from Hulu and start watching.

Here are some online stores and content providers that offer iPad-compatible content:

- TV (www.tv.com) provides its online television and movie content to iPad.
- Ustream (www.ustream.tv) has a mobile app for streaming sports and entertainment programs to mobile devices.
- ABC (http://abc.go.com) and CBS News (www.cbsnews.com) stream live TV programming to the iPad.
- Netflix (www.netflix.com) makes movies and TV shows available that can be streamed to the iPad.
- PBS (www.pbs.org), the king of documentary and arts movie programming, has several apps for iPad including Nova Elements iPad App, Kids iPad app, and PBS for other programming.
- Imp4hub (www.imp4hub.com) is all free MPEG-4 movies for iPad, iPhone, and Android devices.

Additionally, if you get content on your Mac or Windows machines, you can stream it to your iPad using Air Video ($2.99) and Air Video Server, and let it do on-the-fly conversion. For more information, go to www.method.com/air-video/index.html. Another free utility for Windows, OS X, and Linux that converts movies to an iPad-friendly format is HandB. Go to http://handbrake.fr for more information.

There are also stream content that you pay a subscription fee or are an existing customer. Two good ones are Xfinity TV from Comcast and TWCable from Time Warner.

# 179 Enabling Autodownloads of Purchases from Other Devices

# Enabling Autodownloads of Purchases from Other Devices

With iCloud, after you set up an iCloud account, either during the initial setup of your device or through iPad Settings, you can make a purchase or download free content on any of your Apple devices that have been set up for auto-download, and you can have these automatically copied onto or any of your Apple devices. To enable this auto-download feature on iPad, follow these steps:

- 1. Tap Settings on the Home screen.

To use iCloud, first set up an iCloud account. See Book I, Chapter 5 for detailed coverage of iCloud, including setting up your account.

- 2. Tap iTunes & App Store.

- 3. In the options that appear, the On/Off switch to turn on any category of purchases you want to auto-download from your iPad devices: Music, Apps, or Books (see Figure 1-11).

At this point, Apple doesn't offer an option of auto-downloading video content using these settings, probably because video is a memory and bandwidth hog. You can always download video to your iPad through the iTunes App store or sync to your computer using iTunes to get the content or through the iTunes U for educational content.

# 180 Setting Up Family Sharing

# Setting Up Family Sharing

Family Sharing is a new feature that allows up to six people to share whatever anybody in the group has purchased from iTunes, eBooks, and App Stores even though you don't share Apple accounts. Your family must use one credit card to purchase items, but you can approve purchases by younger children. You can also share calendars, photos, and a family calendar (see Book V, Chapter 3 for information about Family Sharing and Calendar, and Book II, Chapter 6 for information on sharing Photos in a Family album).

Start by turning on Family Sharing:

- 1. Tap Settings.
- 2. Tap iCloud and then Set Up Family Sharing.
- 3. Tap Started.
- 4. Tap Continue.
- 5. On the Share Purchases screen, tap Share Purchases from a different account to use another Apple account.
- 6. Tap Continue and check the payment method you want to use.
- 7. Tap Continue.
- 8. On the screen that appears, tap Add Family Member.
- 9. Enter the person's name (assuming this person is listed in your contacts) or email address (see Figure 1-12).
