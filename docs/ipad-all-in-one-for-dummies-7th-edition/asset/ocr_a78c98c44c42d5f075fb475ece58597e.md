# Checking Words in the Dictionary

I know that people just skip over words they don't understand when reading, but if you are a writer, I like to know what word means. If you do, you'll appreciate the iPad's built-in Dictionary. As you read a book, if you come across unfamiliar words, don't skip them — take the opportunity to learn a word! The built-in dictionary in iBooks recognizes many proper names, such as historical figures and geographic locations.

Follow these steps to look up a word in the dictionary:

+   1. Open a book, press your finger on a word and hold it until the meaning is shown in Figure-7.
