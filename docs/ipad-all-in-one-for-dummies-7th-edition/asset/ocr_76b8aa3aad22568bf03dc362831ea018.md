contacts is the iPad equivalent of the address book on your cellphone. In fact, if you know an iPhone, it's similar to your Contacts app. The Contacts app on iPad is simple to set up and use, and it has powerful features beyond simply storing names, addresses, and phone numbers.

For example, you can pinpoint a contact's address in the iPad Maps application. You can use your contacts to quickly address emails, messages, and tweets. If you store a contact record that includes a website, you use a link in Contacts to view that website instantly. And, of course, you can easily search for a contact by a variety of criteria, including people related to you by family ties or mutual friends, by groups you create.

In this chapter, you discover the various features of Contacts, including how to save yourself time by syncing your email lists to your iPad instantly.
