# Adjusting the volume

Although individual applications such as Music and Videos have their own volume settings, you can set your iPad system volume as to control the level of system settings and sounds in apps that don't have their own volume control. This system setting is the max against which Music volume settings work; if you set volume to 80% here, for example, Music's 100% volume setting will actually be the maximum system volume, or 80%.

You can simply press the volume rocker controls on the top-right side of your iPad (when it's in portrait orientation) to increase or decrease volume. However, this doesn't change the volume of ringers and alerts unless you change one additional setting. To adjust the volume from the Settings pane, tap and drag the slider to increase the volume or to the left to lower it. Note that you can also adjust system volume in the Control Center, you display by swiping up from the bottom of the iPad.

Under General settings, make a choice from the Side Switch To set the side switch on your iPad to mute all sounds. If you prefer to that switch to scroll rotation, another way to mute your iPad is to hold down the volume switch (below the Side switch on the top-right corner of the device) until the sound is effectively off. This method assumes that you have set this switch to control volume.
