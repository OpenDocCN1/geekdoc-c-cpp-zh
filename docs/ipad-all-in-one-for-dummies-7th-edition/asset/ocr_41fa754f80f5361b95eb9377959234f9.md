# Sharing a Note

Today, it's all about sharing content. If you want to share what you wrote with a friend or colleague, or even access your note on your main computer to move it into a word-processing program and flesh it out, you can easily share the contents of a note by following these steps:

+   1. With a note displayed, tap the Share button on the top of the screen.
2. In the pop-up menu that appears, tap Mail.
3. You can also using AirDrop by tapping that button and tapping on the nearby device you want to send the note to this point.
4. In the email form that appears (see Figure 2-10), type one or more email addresses must appear in the To, Cc, or Bcc field.
