# Deleting Contacts

Remember that iPad's storage capacity is limited compared to your typical computer. Though a contact record is tiny compared to a TV show, there's no sense keeping lists of old records when you no longer them. When you need to remove a name or two from your Contacts, doing so is easy.

Follow these steps to delete contacts on your iPad:

+   1. With Contacts open, tap the contact you want to delete.

+   2. On the information pane on the right, tap the Edit button.
