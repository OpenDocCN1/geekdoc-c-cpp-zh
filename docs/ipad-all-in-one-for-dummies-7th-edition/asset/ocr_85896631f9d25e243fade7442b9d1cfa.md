# Buying a selection

Although you can find freebies out there, and you can rent certain movies rather than buy them, you'll have to buy the music, video, or audio that you enjoy. Buying authorizing the purchase and downloading the content to your iPad (which is done automatically after the purchasing part is complete).

Buying and downloading a movie while running your iPad on a 3G/4G network can also eat up your data allocation and take a while—if such a large file can download all. You're better off downloading this content while connected to a Wi-Fi network. To play it, disable cellular download for the iTunes Store, go to Settings>iTunes & App Store and turn off the Cellular Data switch.

When you want an item to buy, here is how to make your purchase:

+   1. Tap the button that shows either the price (if it's a selection available for purchase; see Figure 1-8) or the word Free (if it's a selection available for free).

The button label changes to Buy X, where X is the particular content you're buying, as a song or album.

![](bbox=[0.235, 0.523, 0.735, 0.853])

+   2. Tap the Buy X button.

The iTunes Password dialog appears. (Refer to Figure 1-1).
