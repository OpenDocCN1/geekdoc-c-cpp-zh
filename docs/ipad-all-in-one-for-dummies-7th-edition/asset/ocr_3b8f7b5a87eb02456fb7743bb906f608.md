# Setting Brightness

Especially when using iPad as an e-reader, you may find that a slightly-bright screen reduces strain on your eyes. To adjust brightness, fol-these steps:

+   1. Tap the Settings icon on the Home screen.

+   2. In Settings, tap Display & Brightness.

+   3. To control brightness manually, tap the Auto-Brightness On/Off switch (see Figure 3-1) to turn off this feature.

![](bbox=[0.262, 0.443, 0.759, 0.737])

Figure 3-1: Lower brightness on your screen can also save battery life.

+   4. Tap and drag the Brightness slider (refer to Figure 3-1) to the right to make the screen brighter or to the left to dimmer it.

+   5. Press the Home button to close Settings.

If glare the screen is a problem for you, consider getting a screen protector. This thin film not only protects your screen from damage but can also reduce glare.
