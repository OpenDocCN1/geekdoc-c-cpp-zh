8. Follow the instructions on the next couple of screens to enter your password and verify your payment information if this is the first time you have signed in to your account from this device.

If you have added friends to your account (see the preceding section), you can go to the Friends page and view games that your friends have downloaded. To purchase one of these games, tap it in your friend's list.
