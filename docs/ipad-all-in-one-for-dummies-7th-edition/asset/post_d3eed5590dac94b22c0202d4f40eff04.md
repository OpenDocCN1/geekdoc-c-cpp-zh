## Opening Mail and Reading Messages

The whole point of email is to send and receive messages. Mail offers a simple interface for reading your emails. It displays an email and a pane that you can use to show inbox contents or change to a different folder. In landscape orientation, the Mailboxes/Inbox pane is always displayed, but in portrait orientation, you display it by tapping the Inbox.

When you tap the Mail app to open, it automatically heads out and checks for any new email. (If you have Microsoft Exchange or iCloud, you can turn on push settings to have your email host initiate a check for new messages to download them to your iPad).

Here are some common actions in Mail:

- To follow a link in a message, simply tap it. Note that tapping links opens them in Safari, and tapping address lines opens the Maps app with a map to that address displayed. You can also tap phone numbers to call that person, or flight code links to check on a flight's status in Safari.

- To open an attachment, tap it, and Mail presents you with a menu to select the one you want to open it.

- To open a meeting invitation, tap the meeting icon (what it looks like depends on the application; example, Outlook.com uses a little calendar sync).

iPad supports many file types—including those that run on multiple platforms, such as PDF and text; those available on Macs, including Pages and Numbers; those for Windows users (also available in Mac versions), including Microsoft Word and Excel; as well as most common graphics and audio formats.
