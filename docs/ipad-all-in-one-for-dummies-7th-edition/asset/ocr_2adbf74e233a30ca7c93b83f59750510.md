Here are the steps to set up iCloud backup on your iPad :

+   1. Open the Settings app, tap iCloud, and then tap Backup.

+   2. In the next screen (see Figure 5-7), tap the On/Off switch to turn on iCloud Backup.

To perform a manual backup, tap Back Up Now.

An alert may appear asking whether you want to allow iCloud to use the location of your iPad. Tap OK.

Your account is now set up.
