# Viewing a Contact's Location in Maps

If you entered a person's street address in Contacts, you have a shortcut for viewing that person's location in the Maps app. Again, this is useful for places you need to visit for the first time, such as several clients' offices on next business trip.

Follow these steps to pinpoint your contacts in the iPad Maps app:

+   1. To open the Contacts app, tap its Icon on the Home screen.
2. Tap the contact you want to view to display her information.
