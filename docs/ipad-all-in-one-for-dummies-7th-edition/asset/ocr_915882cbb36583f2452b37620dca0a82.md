# Great presentation pointers

Here are a few presentation pointers:

+   **Avoid the dark.** If you're using a projector, use the least possible amount of roomdarkening for your presentation. Make sure you can see your audience.

+   **Use question-and-answer sections.** Frequent, short Q&A sections in your presentations to influence your audience as much as possible.

+   **Use a map.** Let people know where they're going in your presentation and where they are at any moment. When you're reading a book, the head of theunread pages gives you an idea of how far you areve. With a presentation, one slide after another can come out of the deck with no clue to how each one fits into the presentation.

+   **Use font sizes.** Have you ever looked closely at the presentations given by the late Steve Jobs and others at Apple? Consider using 60 points for the font size.

+   **Check out your setup.** Leave time to check out your equipment and make sure everything works properly.
