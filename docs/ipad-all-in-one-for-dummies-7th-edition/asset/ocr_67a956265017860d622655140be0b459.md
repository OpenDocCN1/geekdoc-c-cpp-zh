# Working with multiple calendars

You can choose a calendar — for example, Business, Personal, Parties, Classes, and Volunteering — for every event. If you're synchronizing calendars with your Mac or PC by using iCloud, Google Calendar, Microsoft Exchange, or AOL, each source has its own calendar. You can also sync a local calendar on your Mac or Windows PC via iTunes. On your iPad, tap Calendars at the bottom of the screen in the Calendar app. Every calendar is listed with a different color.
