# # Navigating an e-book

Here are the simple steps involved in making your around an e-book using the iBooks reader:

+   1. Tap iBooks, and if your Library (the bookshelf) isn't already displayed, tap the My Books button.
2. Tap an e-book to open it.

The book opens, as shown in Figure 7-7. Note that if the book is stored online, you can discern by the disc and down-arrow icon in top-right corner, downloading it to your iPad may take a few moments. (If you hold your iPad in portrait orientation, it shows one page; if it's in landscape orientation, it shows two by default.)

![](bbox=[0.288, 0.3935, 0.712, 0.6735])

Figure 7-7: Landscape orientation on the iPad.

+   3. Take any of these actions to navigate the book:

+   * To go to the book's Table of Contents near the top-left corner of the page (it looks like a little bulleted list) to go to the book's Table of Contents (see Figure 7-8) and then tap the name of a chapter to go to that chapter.
* To turn to the next page: Tap your finger anywhere on the right edge of a page.
