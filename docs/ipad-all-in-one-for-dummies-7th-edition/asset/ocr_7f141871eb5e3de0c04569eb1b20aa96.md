# Staying Connected

When're on the road, staying connected with your office or family is important. That's why you should be sure to take advantage of two cool features, FaceTime and Personal Hotspot. Here's what you can do with these:

+   FaceTime: This video and audio-calling app (covered in Book II, Chapter 4) allows you to take advantage of the video camera in the iPad (except for the original iPad) to call others who have the FaceTime feature on their iPad, Mac, iPhone, or iPod. You can touch the front camera to show the person what you call your bright smile, or switch to the rear camera to let them see what you're looking at now. Great way to make others jealous when you snagged sales cases in Hawaii!

+   Personal Hotspot: Gives you the ability to use your iPhone's 3G/4G connection (in cases for a fee to your service provider) to take your Wi-Fi-only iPad online when Wi-Fi is out of reach. Personal Hotspot, can, perform this service for up to five devices on the Verizon, T-Mobile, and AT&T versions of iPhone. This is a very useful feature for driving roads who can't sit in a car or hotel lobby to get online or make calls. (I cover Personal Hotspot in more detail in Chapter 1 of this ebook)

Don't forget that you are using the iCloud storage and sharing service, you can have the content on your other devices automatically synced to your iPad. If your spouse downloads some new music or a movie to your iPhone and you both use the same Apple ID, with a Family Sharing account, for example, you can access movie on your iPad wherever you are.
