+   1. Tap the Playlists button at the bottom of the iPad screen.

+   2. Scroll up the screen and tap New Playlist in the options that are revealed.

+   3. In the dialog that appears, enter a name for the playlist and in the list

+   4. Tap an album as Recently Played or Purchased, and in the list

ant to include.

The Add symbol on selected items turn gray.

+   5. Tap the Done button.

+   6. Tap the Playlists button.

Your playlist appears in the Playlists list, and you can play it by tapping
the name to reveal its songs and then tapping the Play button in the
playback controls at the upper left.

You can use the Genius Playlist feature in iTunes Store to set up playlists of
recommended content in your Music library. Based on items you've pur-
ased, iTunes Store suggests other playlists that would go well with your
collection. It's a way to get you to buy more music, but if you're build-
ing music collection, it could be worth a try! Visit the iTunes site at
www.apple.com/itunes for more information.
