# Sharing a Photo Using AirDrop

AirDrop provides a way for those with fourth-generation iPad, iPad mini, and later iPads to share content as photos others who are nearby have an AirDrop–enabled device:

+   1. Follow the steps in the previous task to locate a photo you want to share and tap the Share button.

If an AirDrop-enabled device is in your immediate vicinity (within 10 feet), see the device listed at the top of the Share popover.

+   2. Tap the device name and photo it is sent to.

![](bbox=[0.143, 0.597, 0.223, 0.657])

Note that the device you have has AirDrop enabled and be a compatible Mac or iOS device. To enable iOS, use the Control Center (swipe up from the bottom of any screen) and tap the AirDrop button in the bottom center of the Control Center. On a Mac, open a Finder window or then click AirDrop in the Sidebar. Whatever method you use, choose Contacts or Everyone to specify whom you can interact with AirDrop.

# Sharing Photos Using Photo Stream

Photo Stream allows you to automatically sync copies of any new photos to any of your iCloud devices and to share photo streams with others when you connect to a Wi-Fi network. You can also subscribe to another person’s photo stream if he she shares it with you. First you should set up Photo Stream. Follow these steps to share your photos using Photo Stream:

+   1. Tap Settings: Photos & Camera. Tap the On for My Photo Stream to share among your devices; tap Photo Sharing to share with others.
