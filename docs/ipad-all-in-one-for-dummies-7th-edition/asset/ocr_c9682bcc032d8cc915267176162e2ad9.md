# Creating your first Keynote slide and adding text

The templates in iWork apps provide you with useful starting points. In Keynote, you're almost certain to have prebuilt slides you can use, and then it's only a matter of organizing your data. To create a presentation, follow these steps:

+   1. Tap Keynote on the Home screen.
2. On the Documents window, tap the plus sign (+) to create your new presentation from a template or from another presentation, as described in Chapter 1 of this minibook.
3. Tap the Standard or Wide tab, depending on the appropriate scale of slides for your presentation, and then tap a theme to use it on your new presentation.

A new presentation based on that theme opens.
