# Managing iCloud Settings

A virtual private network (VPN) allows you to set up an encrypted connection with a private network, such as one at your office, over the Internet. A VPN lets you make such a connection securely, and your iPad allows you to make settings for activating your connection through General settings.

![](bbox=[0.137, 0.196, 0.203, 0.263])

For more about connecting to your company network remotely or off, choosing the network for a VPN, see Book III, Chapter 1.

The Wi-Fi settings include simply turning Wi-Fi on or off, choosing the network to connect, and activating a feature that recognizes networks automatically.

I can't forget 3G and 4G. If you have a 3G/4G iPad, you can make settings for your 3G/4G connections by tapping Settings and then Cellular Data. See Book III, Chapter 1 for more about these settings.

![](bbox=[0.137, 0.401, 0.203, 0.468])

Note that you can also use the Airplane Mode to turn your Wi-Fi and Bluetooth signals off when in flight and quickly turn them back on when you're safely on terra firma. This setting is also available through Control Center.

![](bbox=[0.137, 0.495, 0.203, 0.562])

Another setting you can make in Privacy Settings to protect when you're online is to turn Location Services on or off. Turning this on lets apps like Maps find your current physical location. If you turn this feature off and an app needs Location Services to function, it prompts you to turn it on.

# Managing iCloud Settings

iCloud is your online storage and sharing service. Chapter 5 of this minib takes you through the steps involved in setting up an iCloud account, one of the things you can do using the iCloud pane in the Settings app. (See Figure 8-7.) Here are the three main things you can control for iCloud from Settings:

+   - Turn various apps on or off for inclusion in iCloud sync. For example, you can Set Settings to sync contacts stored in your iCloud account. Your iCloud account can sync Mail, Contacts, Calendars, Reminders, and Notes in your iCloud account.
- Control Storage & Backup. This includes keeping your available storage, buying additional storage, or turning iCloud Backup on or off.
- Set Up Family Sharing. Use this to set up an account that up to six people can use to make and share purchases using a single credit card.
