# Setting Ringtones and Text Tones

If you want to hear a unique tone whenever you receive a FaceTime call or a text from a particular contact, you can set this feature in Contacts. For example, if you want to be sure that you know when your spouse is calling, a sick friend, or your boss is calling, set a unique tone for that person by following these steps:

+   * To add a new contact or select a contact from the list of contacts, and tap Edit.
* Tap the Ringtone field.

If you want to assign Text Tones, make that choice in this step. A list of tones appears (see Figure 5-14). You can also tap Buy More Tones to buy additional ringtones from Apple or a variety of third-party sites, or create your own.
