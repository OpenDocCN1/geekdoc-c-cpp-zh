## Charging the Battery

You've heard the awesome ten-hour battery life on your iPad, and it's all true. My iPad showed up almost charged from the Apple Store, even if you got yours shipped, it should've been at least partially charged. But batteries run down eventually (the little icon in the iPad Status bar tells you when you're running low), so one of your priorities is to know how to recharge your battery. This is a pretty obvious procedure, given the few items that you carry with your iPad, but in case you need help, you can follow these steps to get battery meter up to 100 percent:

- 1. Gather your iPad, Lightning to USB cable (fourth generation and later) or Dock Connector to USB Connector (earlier models), and Apple USB power adapter.
- 2. Gently plug the USB end of the Lightning to USB Cable (or the Dock Connector USB Cable) into the USB Power Adapter.
- 3. Plug the other end of the cord (see Figure 2-3) into the Lightning Connector (or Dock Connector slot) on the iPad.

## Turning the iPad On and Registering It

The first time you turn on your iPad, you need to go through a sequence of setup steps. The best way to do this is to be in range of a Wi-Fi network.

When you're ready to get going with your new toy, be sure you're within range of a Wi-Fi network that you can connect with, and then hold the iPad with one hand on either side, oriented like a pad of paper. Follow the Lightning to USB Cable with your device into your iPad and plug the other into a USB port on your computer, in case you lose your battery charge during the setup process.

Follow these steps to set up and register your iPad:

- 1. Press and hold the Sleep/Wake button on the top of your iPad until the Apple logo appears. Another moment, a screen appears with a cheery Hello on it.
- 2. Slide your finger to the right on the screen where it Slide to Set Up.
- 3. Enter a password if you have an Apple ID, choose how other people can reach you with text messages through the Messaging app or video calls through FaceTime (choose your phone number, iCloud account, or email account), and whether to upload to iCloud Drive (an online storage service).

![](img/2a8a8d89daeb1b3896bd38ebf18af126_41_1.png)

Be sure to utilize iCloud Drive on all your devices, such as iPhone, Mac, or Windows PC to make sure your iCloud-stored documents continue to sync to them all.

- 4. You may see a series of screens that involve settings such as language, country, Wi-Fi network, and use of iCloud.

Follow the series of screens to respond to questions and make initial settings, all of which can be changed later in iPad Settings.

- 5. After you deal with all the setup steps, a Welcome to iPad screen appears; tap Get Started to display the Home screen.
