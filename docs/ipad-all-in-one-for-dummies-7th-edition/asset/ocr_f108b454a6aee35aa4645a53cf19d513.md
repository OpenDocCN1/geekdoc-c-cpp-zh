# Setting up a virtual private network

If you want to connect to your organization's network, you may be able to use a virtual private network, or VPN. A VPN, which works over Wi-Fi and 3G/4G cellular connections, allows you to access data securely whether you're using a network outside your company — such as at home or in a café — to access the servers inside your company's network. After a VPN is set up at your company, you can use VPN settings on your iPad to connect to your company network.
