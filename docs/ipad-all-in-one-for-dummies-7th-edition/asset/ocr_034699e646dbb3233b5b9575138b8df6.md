If your having problems with your iPad, you can use the Update feature to try to restore the current version of the software. Follow the preset of settings and then click the Restore button instead of the Update button in Step 6. Typically, restoring an OS to another version does run the risk of going back to original settings, so be aware of going in.

# Restoring sound

Coincidentally, the very morning I wrote this chapter, my husband was puttering with his iPad when sound suddenly disappeared. We gave ourselves a quick course in recovering sound, so now I have these tips with you. Make sure that

+   You haven't touched the volume controls on a physical keyboard connected to your iPad via Bluetooth. They're on the right side of the top-right side of your iPad when holding it in portrait orientation. Be sure not to touch one and inadvertently lower the sound 'til it is essentials.
