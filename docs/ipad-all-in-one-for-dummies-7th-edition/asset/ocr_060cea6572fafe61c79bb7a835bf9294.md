VIP List is a way to create a list of special senders. When any of these senders send you an email, it's flagged in your Inbox. You can view the mailbox to just see messages from VIP contacts in one place. Also, you get a distinct notification sound when a VIP message arrives. Be sure that in Notifications settings, you have set your Mail accounts to use Notification Center for VIP mail. Then, in the Mailboxes list of Mail, tap the Information button to the right of VIP (see Figure 7-20) and follow these steps to design a contact as VIP:

+   1. Tap Add VIP, and your Contacts list appears.
