# Deleting Notes

1. Tap the Notes app on the Home screen to open it.

2. With the iPad in landscape orientation, tap a note in the Notes List to open it.

3. Tap the Trash Can icon at the top of the screen, as shown in Figure 2-11.

![](bbox=[0.267, 0.253, 0.732, 0.372])

**Figure 2-11:** With a note displayed, tap the Trash Can to delete it.

4. Tap the Delete note button that appears (See Figure 2-12).

The note is deleted.

![](bbox=[0.267, 0.484, 0.572, 0.626])

**Figure 2-12:** Go ahead; delete whatever you don't need with a tap of this button.

> Notes is a nice little app, but it's limited. It offers very few formatting tools and only one font for your text. (Actually, you can, but can't paste pictures into it—what appears is the filename, not image.) So if you've made some notes and want to graduate to building a more robust document in a word processor, you have a couple of options. One way is to use the Pages-word processor for iPad, which comes with newer iPads, and copy your note (using the Copy and Paste feature discussed earlier in this chapter). Alternatively, you can send the note to yourself in an email. Open the email and copy and paste the text into a fully-fledged word processor, and you're good to go.

> If you want to move beyond Notes and use a note-taking app that lets you write with a stylus, check PaperDesk for iPad ($3.99) or Penultimate (Free) in the App Store. Both make drawing and writing notes easy and fun. Pogo Sketch and Pogo are also good tools to check out at (www.tenonedesign.com).
