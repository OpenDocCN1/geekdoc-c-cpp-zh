To adjust the volume, follow these steps:

+   1. Tap Settings on the Home screen and then tap Sounds.
2. In the Sounds pane that appears (see Figure 3-7), tap and drag the Ringer and Alerts slider to increase the volume of your audible attention grabbers, or to the left to lower it.

![](bbox=[0.273, 0.266, 0.762, 0.558])

**Figure 3-7** If you want to control ringer and alerts from the volume buttons, tap Change with Buttons.

+   3. Press the Home button to close Settings.

In the Sounds pane, you can turn on or off the sounds that iPad makes when certain events occur (such as receiving new mail or calendar alerts). These sounds are turned on by default.

# Setting Up Subtitles and Captioning

Closed captioning and subtitles help folks with hearing challenges enjoy entertainment and educational content. Follow these steps to turn them on:

+   1. From the Accessibility pane in Settings, tap the Subtitles and Captioning option.
2. On the following screen, shown in Figure 3-8, tap the On/Off switch to turn on Closed Captions and SDH (Subtitles for the Deaf and Hard of Hearing).
3. If you'd like, you can also tap Style and choose a text style for the captions.
