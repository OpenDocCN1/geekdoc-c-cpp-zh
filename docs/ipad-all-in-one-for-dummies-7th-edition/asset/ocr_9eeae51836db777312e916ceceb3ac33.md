You can use Siri to play music hands free. Just press and hold the Home button, and when Siri appears, say something like "Play Take the A Train" or "Play The White Album."

The Home Sharing feature of iTunes allows you to share music among as many computers that have Home Sharing on (iOS devices and Apple TVs, second generation or later, don't count that). To use the feature, each device must have the same Apple ID for Home sharing. After you set up the feature via iTunes, you can retrieve music and videos from your shared library to any of the devices. For more about Home Sharing — which is discussed further in the later section, "Using AirPlay and Home Sharing— visit this site: www.apple.com/support/homesharing.

# Shuffling music

If you want to play a random selection of the music you've purchased or synced through iCloud or from your computer to your iPad, use the Shuffle feature. With the Music app, tap the Songs or Albums button at the bottom of the screen. Next, tap the Shuffle button shown in Figure 2-7. Your contents play in random order.
