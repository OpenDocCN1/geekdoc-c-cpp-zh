# 368 Introducing Numbers

The simple idea of making tables into an actual part of the Numbers application rather than letting people create them any which leads to a major change in the way you can use Numbers when compared to other spreadsheets. Because a table is an entity of its own and not a range of cells, you don't break the tables you've organized in a spreadsheet when you reformat part of the spreadsheet. You can work within any table, and you can reorganize tables in a sheet, but you can break tables by simply adding a row or column to your spreadsheet.
