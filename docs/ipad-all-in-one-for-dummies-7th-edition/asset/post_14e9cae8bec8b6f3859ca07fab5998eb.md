## Making Notes

Notes is a simple notepad app in which you can enter text or cut and paste text from a website or email. You can't much create and edit your notes, or email or print them — the app has no features for formatting text or inserting objects. You'll find Notes handy, though, for simple scribbles on the fly.

You'll feel right at home with the clean interface Notes operates. The fonts also have a clean, sans-serif look, and the icons along the top are pretty straightforward. You can move to the previous note, edit a note, trash a note, or move to the next note. You can even tap Dictation on the onscreen keyboard of an iPad (for the original iPad and iPad 2) and speak your content.

## Keeping yourself on track with Reminders

Reminders are a handy electronic-do list in which you can enter tasks or pull tasks from the Calendar app. You can organize reminders into custom categories and see them in a list or organized by date, which also displays a useful calendar on one side of the screen.

Of course, an obvious feature of an app called Reminders is that you can set it to remind you of an event. Location-based reminders work most efficiently with the always-on 3G or 4G connection on iPad (3G/4G models) prompt your iPad to remind you about an event based on your location; for example, set a reminder to buy milk when you get to a specific location, and you receive the alert when you arrive at the market. This feature uses Location Services to track your whereabouts at any point in time.

## Staying on time with Clock

This app is a simple utility that is especially useful to all you travelers. Clock allows you to display clocks from around the world side by side, so if you're in Paris, you can easily see what time it is in New York.

Clock also has a feature that lets you set alarms to keep your day on schedule. Finally, you can use timer and stopwatch features. The timer lets you do things like the cookies you put in the oven, and the stopwatch lets you add the minutes of your morning jog or workout. You can use the lap-timer feature to keep track of multiple laps in a workout, such as those runs around the track or swimming pools.

## Playing around with Music

Unless you've been living in a cave without 3G/4G or satellite TV for the last years, you know perfectly that the iPod has historically been the Apple-preferred reader. On your iPad, the Music app is your media player with a heavy emphasis on music. You can use Music to play music, audio podcasts, or earbuds.

One of the nicest things about Music on the iPad is that the iPad comes with a nifty sound system and stereo speakers, so listening or without headphones will be a pleasing experience for everyone who added to MP3 listening devices. You can also browse your music by a variety of criteria, such as artist, album, song, or genre.
