# Understanding iCloud Tabs 115

Follow these steps to use iCloud Tabs:

+   1. Check to make sure both devices are using the same iCloud account by going to Settings: iCloud and checking the account name.
2. In Settings, tap Safari and make sure that Do Not Track is turned off; this allows sharing to take place among devices.
3. Open Safari on your iPad and click the Show All Tabs button in the upper-right corner.
4. Tap another device.

All items in your other device's browsing history are displayed (Figure 6-22).
