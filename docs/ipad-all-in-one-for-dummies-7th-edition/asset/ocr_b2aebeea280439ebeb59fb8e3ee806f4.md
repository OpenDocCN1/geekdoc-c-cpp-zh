# Setting the date and time

By default, your iPad is set to Cupertino — Cupertino, California, that is, home to Apple. If you have occasion to reset the time zone and date time, because you live somewhere other than Apple headquarters or travel around your iPad, here's how you control the time setting on your iPad:

+   1. Tap Settings on the Home screen.
2. Tap General.
3. Tap Date & Time.
