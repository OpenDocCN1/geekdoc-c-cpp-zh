Here are some additional tips for using Messages:

+   **Talking with multiple people:** You can address a message to more than one person by simply choosing more recipients in Step 4 in the preceding list.

+   **Canceling a message:** If you begin a conversation and change your mind before sending your message, tap the Cancel button near the top right of the screen.
