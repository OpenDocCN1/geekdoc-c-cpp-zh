# Keeping in touch with Contacts

Today, it's all about who you know and staying in touch. Contacts is the built-in book feature (see Figure 4-3) for the iPad that lets you just do that. You can use Contacts to enter contact information (including phones, if you use from Photos app) and share contact information via Mail or Messages. You can also use a search to find contacts easily.

Contacts is another app that you can sync with your iPhone or computer to save you tedious reentry of information. Read more about this in Book V, Chapter 5.

Want to find your contact in the real world? Tap that person's address in Contacts, and the iPad Maps shows you how to get there! You can also add an address that you in Maps and add it to Contacts.

# Making Notes

Notes is a simple notepad app in which you can enter text or cut and paste text from a website or email. You can't much create and edit your notes, or email or print them — the app has no features for formatting text or inserting objects. You'll find Notes handy, though, for simple scribbles on the fly.

You'll feel right at home with the clean interface Notes operates. The fonts also have a clean, sans-serif look, and the icons along the top are pretty straightforward. You can move to the previous note, edit a note, trash a note, or move to the next note. You can even tap Dictation on the onscreen keyboard of an iPad (for the original iPad and iPad 2) and speak your content.
