# Sharing Photos

Part of the fun of taking photos is sharing those images with others. It's easy to share photos stored on your iPad by posting them to Facebook, tweeting, sending via iMessage, posting to a Flickr account, sharing via iCloud Photo Sharing, or sending them as email attachments. You will have to go to Facebook or Twitter by using a browser and set up an account before you can use this feature. Follow these steps to share photos:

+   1. Tap the Photos app on the Home screen.
2. Tap the Photos or Album tab and locate the photo you want to share.
3. Tap the photo to select it and then tap the Share button (which looks like a box jumping out of it).

The menu shown in 6-8 appears.

![](bbox=[0.265, 0.427, 0.738, 0.74])

+   4. Tap additional photos across the top to share if you want; then tap Message, Mail, iCloud Photo Sharing, Twitter, Facebook, or Flickr option.
5. In the message format that appears, make any modifications that apply in the To, Cc/Bcc, or Subject fields for email, or enter your Facebook posting or tweet text.
