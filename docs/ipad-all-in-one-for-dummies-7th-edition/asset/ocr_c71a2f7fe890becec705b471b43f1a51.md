# Remembering Where You've Been

Why reinvent the (mapping) wheel? One of the great capabilities of a mapping program is the ability to store locations you go to for future reference. In Maps, you can do this in a few ways. You can drop a pin on a map, which marks a beginning point for getting directions from one site to another. Or you can place a bookmark for a site you want to revisit. You then access your bookmarks by tapping Recents in the Search field; tap Favorites to see your bookmarks.

# Dropping a pin

With the iPad, pins act like the pin you might place on a paper map to note routes or favorite locations. Also, pins are markers: Green pin marks a start location; red pin marks search results; a pin you drop yourself in a lovely purple; and a blue pin (referred to as the blue marker) marks your iPad’s current location.

Use these to try the pin feature of iPad:

+   1. Display a map that contains a spot where you want to drop a pin to help you get directions to or from that site.

If necessary, you can zoom in to a more detailed map to get a better view of the location you want to pin using techniques I cover in the earlier “Zooming in and out” section.

+   2. Press and hold your finger on the screen at the location where you want to place the pin.

The pin appears, together with an information bar (shown in Figure 2-8).
