# # Using Safari Reading List

Remember when you were in school and had lots of reading assignments to keep track of all your classes? Well, after graduation, reading lists don't go away; whatever you're staying up-to-date in your chosen field or just keeping up with the latest articles and information, being able to assemble a list of online reading can be a great help. The Safari Reading List provides a way to save content you want to read at a later time so you can easily visit it.

You can save not only links to sites but also the pages themselves, which allows you to read the content even when you're offline.

To use Reading List, follow these steps:

+   1. With a site displayed that you want to add to your Reading List, tap the Share button.
2. On the menu that appears, tap Add to Reading List.
nThe site is added to your list.
3. To view your Reading List, tap Bookmarks and then go to the Reading List tab (the eyeglasses icon).
4. In the Reading List that appears (see Figure 6-12), tap the content you want to revisit and resume reading.
