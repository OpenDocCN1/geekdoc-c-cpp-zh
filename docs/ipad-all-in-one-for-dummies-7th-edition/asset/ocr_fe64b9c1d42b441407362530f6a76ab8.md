# ## In This Chapter

+   * Discovering how iPad differs from other e-readers
* Finding and buying books at iBooks
* Navigating a book
* Working with bookmarks, highlights, and the dictionary
* Organizing books in collections
* Experiencing periodicals with Newsstand

Apple has pushed the iPad as a great e-reader, so though it isn't a dedicated e-reader device like the Amazon Kindle Paperback, you won't miss out on this cool functionality.

Apple's preinstalled iBooks app is what turns your iPad into an e-reader. iBooks is an application that enables you to buy and download e-book sources from Apple's iBooks Store as well as read PDF files. You can also use several other e-apps, for example Kindle or Stanza, to download books to your iPad from a variety of online sources, such as Amazon and Google, so you can read to your heart's content.

Another preinstalled app, Newsstand, has a similar look and feel to iBooks, but its focus is on subscribing and reading magazines, newspapers, and other periodicals.

In this chapter, you discover the options available for reading material, and I will tell you how to buy books and subscribe to periodicals. You also find out how to navigate a book or period and adjust the brightness and type, as well as how to search books and organize your iBooks and Newsstand libraries.
