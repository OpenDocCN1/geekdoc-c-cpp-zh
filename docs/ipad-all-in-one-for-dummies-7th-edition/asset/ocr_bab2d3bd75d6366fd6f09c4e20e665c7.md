# Using iMessage

iPad's instant messaging is called Messages. This app allows you to use the iMessage service to swap text messages with others in real time using your cellphone number or email address. You can forward your conversations to others as well.

You can use the Messages settings to turn on the app or allow others to get a read receipt when you read their messages. You can also use the data in your Contacts app to addressing messages quick and easy. New with iOS 8 comes the ability to send audio or video messages.

# Shooting and Organizing Photos

All iPads (except the original iPad) include front- and rear-facing cameras. You can use the cameras to take photos or still videos (both of which are covered in Book II, Chapter 4). To review and play around with photos you snap or bring onto the iPad from another source, you have the Photos app.

# Examining the iPad cameras

The third-generation and later iPads have a super-clear Retina display and rear-facing Sight camera for viewing and taking the iPad-generated photos and video yet.

Take a quick look at your camera's features by tapping the Camera icon on the Home screen.

You can use the controls from the front and rear cameras to

+   - Switch from still-camera to video-camera operation by using the Camera/Video slider.
- Take a picture or start recording a video.
- Turn on a grid to help you autofocus on still photo subjects.
