# Playing around with Photo Booth

Photo Booth is a photo-manipulation app that goes hand in hand with the still camera built into the iPad (except for the original iPad) hardware. You can use this app to capture photos using various fun effects, such as Kaleidoscope effects and X-Ray. You can copy or email photos from within Photo Booth.

Note that photos you take with Photo Booth open automatically and are saved to your photo app's Camera Roll.

Here's how to take photos using Photo Booth effects:

+   1. Tap the Photo Booth icon on the Home screen.

The different possible effects that can be used in the current view of the camera appear. (See Figure 4-4).

+   2. Tap an effect.

+   3. Tap the Capture button.

An image using that effect is captured. (See Figure 4-5).

To return to the various effects, tap the Effects button in the bottom-left corner of the screen.
