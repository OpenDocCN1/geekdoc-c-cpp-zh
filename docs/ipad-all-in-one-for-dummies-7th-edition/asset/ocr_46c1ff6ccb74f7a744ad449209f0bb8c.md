# Adding contacts

You, of course, enter your contacts the old-fashioned way, by typing their names, addresses, phone numbers, and other information in a form.

Follow these steps to create a new contact record:

+   1. Tap the Contacts icon on the Home screen to open the application.

If you haven't entered a contact, you see a blank address book with the Add (plus) icon at the top of the Contacts List, as shown in Figure 5-1.
