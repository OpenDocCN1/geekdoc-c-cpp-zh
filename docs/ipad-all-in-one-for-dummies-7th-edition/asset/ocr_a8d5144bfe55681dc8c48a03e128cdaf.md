| Gesture | Effect |
| Flick right or left. | Select the next or preceding item. |
| Tap with two fingers. | Stop speaking the current item. |
| Flick two fingers up. | Read everything from the screen. |
| Flick two fingers down. | Read everything from the current position. |
| Flick three fingers up or down. | Scroll one page at a time. |

| Flick four fingers up or down. | Go to the first or last element on a page. |
| Flick four fingers right or left. | Go to the next or preceding section (as on a web page). |

# Making Additional Vision Settings

Several Vision features are simple on/off settings, so rather than giving you the steps to get those settings repeatedly, I provide this useful list of additional features that you can turn on or off after you tap Settings>General>Accessibility:

+   * Zoom: The Zoom feature enlarges the contents displayed on the iPad screen when you double-tap the screen with three fingers. The Zoom feature works almost everywhere in iPad: in Photos, on web pages, and on Home screens, in your Mail, and in Videos — give it try!
* Invert Colors: The Invert Colors reverses colors on your screen so that backgrounds are black and text is white.

![](bbox=[0.126, 0.863, 0.193, 0.921])

The Invert Colors work well in some places and not in others. For example, in the Photos application, pictures appear almost as photo negatives. Your Home screen image will likewise look a bit strange. However, don't even think about playing a video with this feature turned on! If you need help reading text, Invert Colors can be useful in several applications.
