
# 170 Exploring the iTunes Store

So, it's time you get to know the iTunes Store: the easiest way to grab all movies, TV shows, and music that you love for viewing and listening to on your iPad.

## Visiting the iTunes Store

Using the iTunes Store from your iPad is easy with the preinstalled iTunes Store app. You just tap the iTunes app on the Home screen.

Now you can roam around different options. You have music, movies, TV shows, and audiobooks to choose from. If you begin to purchase or rent an item and you're not already signed in with your Apple ID, the dialog shown in Figure 1-1 appears, asking for your sign-in information. Enter your ID and password (you may have created one when you set up your iPad or may have already done) and tap OK.

![](img/2a8a8d89daeb1b3896bd38ebf18af126_187_0.png)

Tap here and enter your password.

Figure 1-1: Log in to the iTunes Store.

Start exploring musical selections by tapping the Music button in the row of buttons at the bottom of the screen, if it's not already selected, as shown in Figure 1-2. Tap the See All link in any category of music to display more selections.

Tap the Music button in the upper-left corner to go back to the featured musical selections; then tap the Genres button at the top left of the screen. This step displays a list of music genres that you can choose from. Tap one. Items in the genre are organized by criteria, such as New and Noteworthy Albums and iTunes Essentials. Tap any listed item to see more details about, as shown in Figure 1-3.

The navigation techniques in these steps work essentially the same in any of the content categories (the buttons at the bottom of the screen), which include Music, Movies, Audiobooks, and TV Shows. Just tap one to explore it.

> If you want to use the Genius playlist feature, which recommends additional purchases based on the contents of your library, open the iTunes Store on your iPad and tap the Genius button at the bottom of the screen. If you've made enough purchases, song and album recommendations appear on those purchases. You also see content in your Match library (a fee-based service) if you have one.

## Finding a selection

You can look for a selection in the iTunes Store in several ways. You can use the Search feature, search by genres or categories, or view artists' pages. Here's how these work:

- Search: Tap in the Search field shown in Figure 1-4 and enter a search term which could be a music genre, the name of an album or song, an artist's name, for example, using the onscreen keyboard. Tap the Search button on the keyboard, or if one of the suggestions appeals to you, just go ahead and tap it. Search results are divided into categories such as Songs and Albums. Flick to scroll through them and find what you need. Tap Cancel to return to Music selections.

![](img/2a8a8d89daeb1b3896bd38ebf18af126_189_0.png)

- Link: On a description page that appears when you tap a selection, you can find more by people involved, such as a singer or actor, if you tap the Related tab, as shown in Figure 1-5.

![](img/2a8a8d89daeb1b3896bd38ebf18af126_190_0.png)

Figure 1-5: Search for artist.

If you select a song you like, tap the Share button at the top of the description page to share your discovery with a friend via Mail, Message, or Facebook. For all sharing choices but AirDrop, a message appears with a link that your friend can click to view the selection. Enter an address in the To field and tap Send. Your friend now knows.

## Previewing music, movie, or an audiobook

Because you've already set up an iTunes account (if you haven't yet, see Chapter 5), when you choose to buy an item, it is automatically charged to your credit card or against any allowance you have from iTunes gift.

However, you might just want to preview an item before you buy it to be sure it's a good fit. If you like it, buying and downloading are then easy and quick. Follow these steps to preview your content:

1. Open the iTunes Store and locate a selection you want to buy using any of the methods outlined earlier.
2. Tap the item to see detailed information about it, as shown in Figure 1-6.
3. Sample the content:
   - For a movie or audiobook, tap the Trailer button (see Figure 1-6) to play a preview.
   - For a TV show, tap an episode to get further information.

![](img/2a8a8d89daeb1b3896bd38ebf18af126_191_0.png)

Figure 1-6: Why not preview before you buy?

- If you want to listen to a sample of a music selection, tap the track number or name of selection with a red preview icon to the right of it, as shown in Figure 1-7. A small square appears and the track plays. Tap the square to stop the preview.

![](img/2a8a8d89daeb1b3896bd38ebf18af126_191_1.png)

Figure 1-7: You can also preview music before you rent or buy.

If you like what you hear or see, when you're ready to buy, it brings you to the next section.

## Buying a selection

Although you can find freebies out there, and you can rent certain movies rather than buy them, you'll have to buy the music, video, or audio that you enjoy. Buying the purchase and downloading the content to your iPad is done automatically after the purchasing part is complete.

Buying and downloading a movie while running your iPad on a 3G/4G network can also eat up your data allocation and take a while—if such a large file can download. You're better off downloading this content while connected to a Wi-Fi network. To play it, disable cellular download for the iTunes Store: go to Settings > iTunes & App Store and turn off the Cellular Data switch.

![](img/2a8a8d89daeb1b3896bd38ebf18af126_192_0.png)

When you want an item to buy, here is how to make your purchase:

1. Tap the button that shows either the price (if it's a selection available for purchase; see Figure 1-8) or the word Free (if it's a selection available for free). The button label changes to Buy X, where X is the particular content you're buying, as a song or album.

![](img/2a8a8d89daeb1b3896bd38ebf18af126_192_1.png)

2. Tap the Buy X button. The iTunes dialog appears.

3. Enter your password and tap OK. The item begins to download and is automatically charged to your credit card or against your balance.

![](img/2a8a8d89daeb1b3896bd38ebf18af126_193_0.png)

When the download is complete, you can view the content using the Music or Videos app, depending on the type.

## Renting movies

In the case of movies, you can either rent or buy content. If you rent, which is less expensive but makes the content yours for only a short time, you have 30 days from the time you rent to begin to watch. After you have begun to watch, you have 24 hours remaining to watch as many times as you like (but only on the same device). To rent a movie, follow these steps:

1. With the iTunes Store open, tap the Movies button.
2. Locate the movie you want to rent and tap it open, as shown in Figure 1-9.
3. Tap the Rent button. The Rent button changes to a Rent Movie button (see Figure 1-10). At this point, you may be asked to enter your Apple ID and password.
4. Tap the Rent Movie button to confirm the rental. The movie begins to download immediately and your account is charged the rental fee.
