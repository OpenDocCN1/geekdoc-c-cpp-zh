# # Deleting Photos

You might find that it's time to get rid of some of old photos of the family reunion or the last project you worked on. If a photo was not transferred from your computer but instead was downloaded or captured as a screenshot on the iPad, you can delete it using this procedure:

+   1. Tap the Photos app on the Home screen.
2. Tap the Albums or Photos tab; if you tap the Album tab, then tap an album to open it.
3. Tap Select and then either an individual photo or multiple photos.
4. Tap the Trash button, as shown in Figure 6-11.
5. Tap the Delete Photo button that appears to finish the deletion.
