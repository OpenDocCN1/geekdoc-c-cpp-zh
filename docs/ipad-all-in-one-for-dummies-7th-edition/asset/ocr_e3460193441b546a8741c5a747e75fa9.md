# Capturing Your Own Videos with the Built-in Cameras

In iPads, two video cameras that can capture video from either the front or rear of the device make it possible for you to take videos that you can then share with others.

To record a video, follow these steps:

+   1. Tap the Camera app on the Home screen.

The Camera app opens.

+   2. Tap and slide the line of words below the red Record button so that Video becomes the orange selection.

This step switches from the still camera to the video camera (as shown in Figure 4-6).
