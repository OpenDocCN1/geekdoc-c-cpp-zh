# Assigning a Photo to a Contact

It's always helpful to associate a face with a name. In Contacts, you can do just by adding a person's photo to their contact record. You can add photos from various sources, such as

+   * The Photos' albums
* Digital cameras
* Email attachments
* iPad's camera
* Photo collections stored on your computer or in iCloud (see Book II, Chapter 6, for more about working with the Photos app)
