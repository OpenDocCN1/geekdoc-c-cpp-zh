# Making Private Browsing and Cookie Settings

Apple has provided some privacy settings for Safari that you should consider. Private Browsing automatically removes items from the download list, stops Safari from using AutoFill to save information to complete your entries in the Address/Search field as you type, and erases some browsing history. These features can keep your activities more private. The Accept Cookies settings allow you to stop the downloading of cookies (small files that document your browsing history so that you can be recognized by a site the next time you go to or within site) to your iPad.

You can control both settings by choosing Safari in Settings. Tap to turn the Do Not Track feature on or off (see Figure 6-20). Tap the arrow to Block Cookies and choose to never save cookies, always cookies, or save cookies only from the current website or visited third-party and advertiser sites (the default setting).
