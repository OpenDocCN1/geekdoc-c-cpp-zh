# 450 Syncing Calendars

You can choose calendars on this list to display the events from several calendars at one time, including calendars you sync from your PC or Mac, only your PC calendar, your Mac calendar, cloud-based calendars, or only your iPad calendar. With the Calendar app open, tap the Calendars button, and in the Calendars dialog, tap to select or deselect the calendars you want to display: All from My <Account>, Birthday Calendar, My Calendar, or US Holidays. If you have synced from other sources, you can choose them by tapping to select or deselect them; for example, tap All iCloud to show all your iCloud-synced calendars. Tap Done, and your chosen calendar displays. When you add a new event, tap the Calendar field to choose the calendar to add the event.

# Syncing Calendars

If you use a calendar from an online service, such as Yahoo! or Google, you can sync to that calendar to read events that are saved in your iPad. To sync a calendar, follow these steps:

+   1. Tap the Settings icon on the Home screen.
2. Tap the Mail, Contacts, Calendars option on the left.
3. Tap Add Account.
4. Tap an email choice, such as Outlook, Gmail, or Yahoo!
5. In the settings that appear (see Figure 3-13), enter your name, address, and email account password.
6. Tap Next.
7. On the following screen, tap the On/Off switch for the Calendars field.
8. Your iPad retrieves data from your calendar at the interval you have specified, to review these settings, tap the Back button in the top-left corner of the phone, and tap the Fetch New Data option in the Mail, Contacts, Calendars.
9. In the Fetch New Data pane that appears (see Figure 3-14), be sure that the Push option's On/Off switch is set to (green) and choose the frequency you prefer your iPad to use for querying the server for new data: every 15 or 30 minutes, hourly, or manually.
