# Adding alerts

Once upon a time, helpful assistants placed printed schedules of the day's activities in front of their bosses to remind them of scheduled commitments. Today, I'm not sure but the highest-paid CEOs get this type of service. Instead, most people set up alerts in calendar programs to remind them where to be, and when.

Luckily, in the iPad Calendar app, you can easily set up alerts by following these steps:
