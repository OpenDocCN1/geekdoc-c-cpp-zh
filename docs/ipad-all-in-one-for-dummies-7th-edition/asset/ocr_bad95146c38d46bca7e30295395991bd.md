Before you set off on your trip to cities such as New York, San Francisco, or Chicago, you may download one of these apps to get transit system maps, schedules, and more.

Transit Maps is free and comes with one transit map, but it enables you to download transit maps as graphics files from the Internet using your browser fellow. The beauty of this app is that it helps you download large image files and view them offline, so you can check a map if you're not able to get online.

iTransitBuddy is a series of apps costing $0.99 for metro areas; they're designed for the iPhone but usable on the iPad. These apps have a helpful feature for looking up free transfers and schedule updates. Maps are downloaded to your device, so you don't have to have an Internet connection to use them. iTransitBuddy is handy for commuters as well as those who travel to metro areas on business or pleasure.
