If you want to buy music, you can open the description page for an album and click the album price, or buy individual songs rather than the entire album. Tap the price for a song and then proceed to purchase.

Note the Redeem button on many iTunes Store screens. Tap this button to redeem any iTunes certificates you might have gotten from your generous friends, or from yourself. Your purchases are debited against your credit balance; if the items cost more, your credit is charged for the remainder.

If you don't want to allow purchases from within apps (Music or Videos) but rather want to allow purchases only through the iTunes Store, you can tap Settings>General>Restrictions and enable Restrictions and enter a passcode. After you set a passcode, you can tap individual apps to turn on restrictions for them, as for actions such as deleting apps, sharing via AirDrop, or using Siri.

# Renting movies

In the case of movies, you can either rent or buy content. If you rent, which is less expensive but makes the content yours for only a short time, you have 30 days from the time you rent to begin to watch. After you have begun to watch, you have 24 hours remaining from time to watch as many times as you like (but only on the same device). To rent a movie, follow these steps:

+   1. With the iTunes Store open, tap the Movies button.

+   2. Locate the movie you want to rent and tap it open, as shown in Figure 1-9.

+   3. Tap the Rent button.

The Rent button changes to a Rent Movie button (see Figure 1-10). At this point, you may be asked to enter your Apple ID and password.

+   4. Tap the Rent Movie button to confirm the rental.

The movie begins to download to your iPad immediately, and your account is charged the rental fee.

After the download is complete, you can use the Videos app to watch it. (See Chapters 2 and 3 in this minibook to read how these apps work.)
