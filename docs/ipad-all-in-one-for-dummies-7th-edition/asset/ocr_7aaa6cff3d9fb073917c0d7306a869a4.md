# Sharing contacts

After you've entered contact information, you can share it with others in an email message. Sharing is especially handy when you store locations in Contacts, such as your favorite restaurant or movie theater.

Here's how to share your contacts' information:

+   1. **With Contacts open, tap a contact name to display its information.**
2. **On the information page, tap the Share button.**
3. If you can, select AirDrop, tap the name of a device near you to send the contact information wirelessly to that device.
4. In the popover that appears, shown in Figure 5-20, tap Mail or Message.
The New Message form appears. Both the email and message form let you enter a message and include the contact information as an attachment to be sent to the recipient. Figure 5-21 shows the email form.
