So how do you choose? If you want to wander around the world or to - take long drives with your iPad finally connected to the Internet - 3G/4G and the price. But if you use your iPad at home or using a Wi-Fi hotspot (a location where Wi-Fi access to the Internet is available, such as at an Internet cafe, an office, or a library), don't bother with 3G/4G. And frankly, you can find lots of hotspots out there, including restaurants, hotels, and malls.

Because 3G/4G iPads are GPS devices, they know where you are and can get you from here to there. The Wi-Fi-only model uses a digital compass and triangulation method for locating your current position, which is less accurate; no constant Internet connection, it won't help you to get around. Getting accurate directions when you're on the go is one iPad feature that excites you, get 3G/4G and then see Book III, Chapter 2 for more about the Maps app.
