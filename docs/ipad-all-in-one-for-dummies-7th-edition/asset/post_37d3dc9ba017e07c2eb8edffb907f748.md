# Replying to or forwarding email

Replying to or forwarding emails is pretty dearny with iPad as well. In fact, there's a handy button for replying or forwarding. There's also a Print command on this to make printing your emails to a compatible printer easy.

You can attach a photo or video to new email messages when you create them by pressing in the message body and then inserting Photo or Video. You can also use features in apps as Photos, Contacts, Notes, iWork Pages, and Maps to share individual documents via email.

Here's how to use the simple Reply/Forward functions in iPad:

- 1. **With an email open (see the preceding section), tap the Reply button, as shown in Figure 7-9.**

If you are the only recipient, there will be no Reply option; if available, this option allows you to reply to the sender and all other recipients.

# Creating and sending a new message

You're probably an old pro at creating and sending emails messages, but it's a quick trip through the iPad's Mail feature and its approach to writing an email using the onscreen keyboard.

Note, that by default, your emails have a signature that says, "Sent from iPad." This will impress your geekliest friends, but if you want to change it to a little more usual, just go to Settings. In the Mail section of Mail, Contacts, Calendars settings, tap Signature. You can then enter any signature text you want. If you have multiple email accounts, Signature displays options to use the usage for All Accounts or Per Account. If you choose Per Account, settings for each account appear appear as each.

Follow these steps to create and send:

- 1. With Mail open, tap the New Message button. A blank message form (see Figure 7-12) appears.

# Formatting Email

You can apply some formatting to text. You can use bold, underline, and italic. To use the indent, you need to make sure it's on. Settings, tap Mail, Contacts, Calendars; next, tap Quote Level.

- 1. In the message, tap a word or select all.
- 2. In the pop-up that appears (Figure 7-13), tap Bold, Italics, or Underline.
- 3. To change the indent, tap at the beginning of a line and tap Quote Level.
- 4. Tap Increase to indent the text or Decrease to move text towards the left.
