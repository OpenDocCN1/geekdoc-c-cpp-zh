# 86 Buying Apps or Downloading Apps

2. At this point, you have several options for finding apps:

+   * Tap in the Search field, enter a search term, and tap the Search button on the onscreen screen to see results.
* Tap and swipe the screen to scroll down to see more selections.
* Tap the Categories button at the top of the screen to see different categories of apps.
* Tap the Top Charts button at the bottom of the screen to see which free and paid apps other people are downloading.
* Tap the Explore option to display apps in categories such as Great Games, Get Stuff Done, and Interactive Kids Stories. Tap a category to display apps that fit within it.
* Tap the Popular Near Me button at the top of the Explore to view apps that are popular among people in near your location. (This may not produce useful results, depending on where you are.)
* Tap the Purchased button to view apps you've already purchased, as shown in Figure 5-10. An item with a cloud symbol to the right of it means you need to download it to use it.
* Tap the See All button in any category in the Featured view to view all items in that category.
* In the Charts view, use the Paid, Free, and Gross tapping to narrow down your search.
