If you set up multiple accounts, you can then switch between accounts by tapping an account name in the upper-left corner of the displayed inbox and then tapping Accounts and choosing which account to display. Or you can use the consolidated inbox and check your mail from all active accounts on one page.

If you have iCloud, Microsoft Exchange (mostly used for business accounts), Gmail, Yahoo!, Outlook (this includes Microsoft accounts from Live, Hotmail, and so on), AOL account, iPad pretty much automates the setup. Here are the steps to get you going with any of these email providers:

+   1. Settings on the Home screen.
2. Settings, tap Mail, Contacts, Calendars.
The settings shown in Figure 7-1 appear.

![](bbox=[0.269, 0.406, 0.742, 0.701])

**Figure 7-1:** Mail settings allow you to set up multiple email accounts.

+   3. Tap Add Account.
The options shown in Figure 7-2 appear.

+   4. Tap iCloud, Exchange, Gmail, Yahoo!, AOL, or Outlook.com and enter your account information in the form that appears (a description is optional). (See Figure 7-3).

+   5. Tap Next.

+   6. After iPad takes a moment to verify your account information, tap any On/Off switch to have Mail, Contacts, Calendars, Notes, and/or Reminder services synced with iPad.
