# Working with Selection Buttons

vary depending on which element is selected. Some selection buttons may look familiar to you from other apps that you may have used on the iPad and the Mac (as well as on other types of computers), including the Cut, Copy, and Paste commands.

When you select a chunk of text, you may also see these options:

+   - **Delete**: Delete the selected text or object.

+   - **Highlight**: Highlight selected text with a felt-tip marker effect.

+   - **Comment**: Insert a comment where you can enter a note about items in your document or comment on the work of others that you're reviewing.
