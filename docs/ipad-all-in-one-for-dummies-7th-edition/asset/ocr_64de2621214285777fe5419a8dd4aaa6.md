**Figure 3-7:** Use this cool slot-machine-like interface to set up event details.

+   - Place your finger on the date, hour, minute, or AM/PM column, and swipe your finger to scroll up or down.

+   - (Optional) To add notes, use your finger to scroll down in the New Event dialog; tap in the Notes field.

+   - After all items are set correctly, tap Add.
