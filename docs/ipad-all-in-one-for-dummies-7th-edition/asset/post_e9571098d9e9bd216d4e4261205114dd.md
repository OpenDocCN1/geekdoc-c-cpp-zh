## Remembering Where You've Been

Why reinvent the (mapping) wheel? One of the great capabilities of a mapping program is the ability to store locations you go to for future reference. In Maps, you can do this in a few ways. You can drop a pin on a map, which marks a beginning point for getting directions from one site to another. Or you can place a bookmark for a site you want to revisit. You then access your bookmarks by tapping Recents in the Search field; tap Favorites to see your bookmarks.

## Dropping a pin

With the iPad, pins act like the pin you might place on a paper map to note routes or favorite locations. Also, pins are markers: Green pin marks a start location; red pin marks search results; a pin you drop yourself in a lovely purple; and a blue pin (referred to as the blue marker) marks your iPad’s current location.

Use these to try the pin feature of iPad:

- 1. Display a map that contains a spot where you want to drop a pin to help you get directions to or from that site.

If necessary, you can zoom in to a more detailed map to get a better view of the location you want to pin using techniques I cover in the earlier “Zooming in and out” section.

- 2. Press and hold your finger on the screen at the location where you want to place the pin.

The pin appears, together with an information bar (shown in Figure 2-8).

## Adding and viewing a favorite

Favorites are a tried-and-true way to save a destination so that you can display a map or directions to spot quickly. You've probably used a similar bookmark favorite in a web browser. With Maps, you can save favorites and access those locations from the search field.

Here's how to add a favorite in Maps:

- 1. Place a pin on a location, as described in the preceding section.
- 2. Tap the Information bar to display the Information dialog.
- 3. Tap the Share button. (See Figure 2-10.)

## Deleting a favorite

If you want to delete a favorite, follow these steps:

- 1. Tap the Search field, then tap Favorites.
- 2. Tap Edit.
- 3. Tap the minus icon next to the favorite.
- 4. Tap Delete.

The favorite is removed.

## Getting Directions, Information, and More

Maps can provide all kinds of information, from directions from one location to another. You can get directions by using pins.

Here are the steps to get directions in maps using pins:

- 1. With at least one pin on your map in addition to your current location, tap Directions.
- 2. Tap the Drive or Walk tab to get the kind of directions you prefer.

img/2a8a8d89daeb1b3896bd38ebf18af126_328_0.png

**Figure 2-14:** Go from pin to pin on your map to get directions.

- 3. You can also enter two locations in the boxes at the top of the page to get directions from one to the other.

Here are the steps to get directions by typing the addresses in the Search fields:

- 1. Tap the Directions button in Maps and then tap the field labeled Start.
- 2. Enter a different starting location.
- 3. Tap in the End field and enter a destination location. Tap Route.
