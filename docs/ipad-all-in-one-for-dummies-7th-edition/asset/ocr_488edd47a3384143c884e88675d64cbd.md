# # Exploring the iTunes Store

![](bbox=[0.795, 0.785, 0.84, 0.84])

Whether it is your favorite shopping store or not, the iTunes Store is set up to be your most convenient store for content on your iPad at the moment. Sure, you can get content from other places, but the iTunes Store app comes preinstalled on your iPad — Apple makes it easy to access it from various Apple devices or your PC.
