# 16 Considering iPad Accessories

iPad Smart Case/Smart Cover: Your iPad isn't cheap, and unlike a laptop computer, it has an exposed screen that can be damaged if you drop or scratch it. Investing in the iPad Case or Smart Cover is a good idea if you intend to take your iPad out of your house — or if you have cats or grandchildren. The iPad Smart Cover costs $40 for polyurethane, and the Smart Case costs $70 for leather. Other cases vary in price depending on design and material.

![](bbox=[0.148, 0.263, 0.223, 0.333])

The official iPad Smart Case has an ingenious little slot on the back. You can flip the cover back and tuck it into the slot to make the case rest on your desk or counter at a very handy angle for viewing and typing. You can also prop the case in a kind of U-shaped configuration to presentations to others.

iPad Camera Connection Equipment: Because there's no USB port on an iPad, you can use a USB connection to upload photos from your camera or media card to your iPad. If you want to add this functionality, you can use a MicroUSB camera connector and a MicroSD reader, each of which will set you back about $30 for the privilege.

Stands: The Apple Store offers several stands, including the Origami Workstand at $29.95 and The Mobile Upstand for iPad at $49.95. The Twelfth Hover Stand for iPad costs $16.95, and attaches to your computer. Twelfth SouthBook is another good option at $29.99. For a truly special handcrafted stand, visit http://www.webskit.com/accessories.html to find a one-of-a-kind tablet stand that will make your choice of words haunt.

Apple EarPods with Remote and Mic: This device offers in-ear listening and remote control of your audio for about $29.

Apple Digital AV Adapter: To use of the HDMI technology that allows you to connect devices to output high-definition media through HDMI ports in TVs, monitors, and projectors, you can use this adapter (Figure 1-3) for $49.

AirPrint Printers: There are printers available from HP, Canon, Brother, Lexmark, and Epson that support Apple's AirPrint feature. Prices range from $129 to $399. HandPrint and Printopia are Macintosh apps that can print any printer shared over a network to your iPad.

It's a good idea to have a spare cable available to charge your iPad in case one gets mislaid.

# Checking out what others have to offer

If you want to explore third-party accessories, there are many, and appear all the time. Just perform a web search for iPad accessories. You'll find that there are suede, leather, neoprene, aluminum, and canvas cases; a variety of stands; carrying bags; screen protectors; and external batteries to supplement iPad's impressive ten-hour battery life.
