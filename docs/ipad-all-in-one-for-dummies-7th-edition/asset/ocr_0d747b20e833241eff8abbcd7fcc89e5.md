# Creating Stations

Are you the creative type? Do you like more than one kind of music? You'll be glad to know that you can create a radio station of your own.

+   1. Tap Music in the Dock.
2. Tap Radio. From the iTunes Radio Home page, tap the New Station button with a bright red cross on it (see Figure 2-11).
3. Tap a category of music such as Classic Rock or Dance. (See Figure 2-12)
4. If subcategories are then offered, tap the one you prefer and tap the Add New button that appears to the right of the category.

The category is added to your stations.
