# Saving photos from the web

The web offers a wealth of images that you can download to your Photo Library on the iPad. The built-in Safari browser makes it simple to save any image you come across to your iPad.(Of course, you should be careful not to violate copyright when using pictures you've grabbed.

Several search engines have advanced search features that allow you to search for only nonlicensed images, at least theoretically, that you don't have to pay for the images. It's always a good idea to get written permission to use an image, especially if you intend to use it to make money (with a company brochure or online course).

Follow these steps to save images from the web:

+   1. Open Safari and navigate to the web page containing the image you want.

+   2. Press and hold the image.

A popup appears, as shown in Figure 6-1.

+   3. Tap Save Image.

The image is saved to your Camera Roll album in the Photos app.
