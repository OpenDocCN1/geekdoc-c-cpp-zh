4. If you wish to change the language Siri uses, tap Language and choose a different language in the list that appears.

5. To change the gender of Siri's voice from female to male, tap Voice Gender and then tap Male.

6. If you want to be able to activate Siri free when your iPhone is plugged in by saying “Hey, Siri,” set Voice Activation to On.

7. Tap My Info and select your contact information in the Contacts list that appears.

Siri now knows your name, phone number, website, contacts who are related to you and their relationship, and so on, so it can respond to requests that require that information.

You can make a setting that allows Siri to be used when the Lock screen is displayed, but Siri can then bypass your passcode if required. If somebody else gets your iPhone on your iPad and that could compromise your data security. To change this setting under General Settings, tap Passcode Lock. When the lock is set to on, you can use the Siri field to access account via Siri on or off.
