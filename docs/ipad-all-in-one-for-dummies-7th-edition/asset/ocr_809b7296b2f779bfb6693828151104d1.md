# # Making Wi-Fi settings

Book 1, Chapter 5 gives you the information you need to connect to a Wi-Fi network, a matter of simply signing in to the network, with a password, if necessary. Now it is time to go over the finer points of your Wi-Fi connection.

A basic Wi-Fi setting tells iPad to automatically search for and join networks that are in range. If several networks are in range, using this setting, iPad joins the one that you used most recently.

After iPad joins a network, a little Wi-Fi icon appears in the status bar. The number of dots indicates the strength of the signal, just as you're used to seeing on your cellphone.

To get to Wi-Fi settings, tap Settings on your Home screen and tap Wi-Fi (see Figure 1-1).

![](bbox=[0.262, 0.44, 0.72, 0.735])

**Figure 1-1**: Wi-Fi settings.

Here's a rundown of the items you can work with in Settings for your Wi-Fi configuration:

+   **Wi-Fi On/Off:** Simply tap this switch to On to turn on Wi-Fi. If you want it off, perhaps to avoid battery drain or because you're on an airplane and the pilot tells you to, set this to **Off**. (Note that iPads also have an Airplane Mode setting, which I discuss in the next section.)

+   **The network you're connected to:** The current connection is listed. Tap it to change any settings for it or to disconnect.
