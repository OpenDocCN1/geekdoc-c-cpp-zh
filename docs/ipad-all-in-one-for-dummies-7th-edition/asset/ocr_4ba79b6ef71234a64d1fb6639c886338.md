# Deleting Video Content

Media files, as you know, take up lots of space in a computing device. Even if you bought the iPad model with the largest amount of memory, its memory can fill up fast. You can always download it from the iTunes Store, and if you're not planning on watching an item again soon, deleting it from your iPad and freeing up some space is a good idea. Or, sync with iTunes on a computer to get the content back on your iPad. If you want to delete content that you didn't purchase from the iTunes Store, be sure to back it up to your computer first; otherwise, you can't get it again when it's gone from your iPad.
