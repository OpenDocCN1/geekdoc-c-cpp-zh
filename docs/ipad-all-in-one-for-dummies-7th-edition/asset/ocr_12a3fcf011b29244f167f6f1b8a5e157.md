# 22 Taking First Look at the Gadget

Here's the rundown on what these things are and what they do:

+   - **(The all-important) Home button:** On the iPad, press this button to go back to the Home screen to find just about anything. The Home screen displays all your installed and preinstalled apps and gives you access to /your iPad settings. No matter where you are or what you are doing, press the Home button and you're back at home base. You can also double-press the Home button to bring up a viewable list of apps called the App Switcher so that you can quickly move from one app to another that you don't want or have to remove (Apple refers to this multitasking). If you press and hold the Home button, you open Siri, the iPad voice assistant. Finally, on recent iPad models, the Home button contains a fingerprint reader with the Touch ID feature.
- **Sleep/Wake button:** You can use this button to power up your iPad, in sleep mode, wake it up, or power it down (more about this is in the final section of this chapter).

+   - **Lightning connector:** This is where you plug in the Lightning connector at the USB end of the power adapter to charge your battery or use it without the power adapter to sync with your computer (which you will find out about in Chapter 5 of this minibus). You can also use this slot for the Camera Connection Kit or connect connect any AV adapters.
- **Cameras:** iPads (except for the original iPad) offer front- and rear-facing cameras that you can use to shoot photos or video. The rear one is the top-right corner, and you need to be careful not to put your thumb over when taking shots. (I have several very nice photos of my thumb already.)

+   - **Side switch:** In case you haven't heard it, the iPad screen rotates to the angle you're holding it. If you want to stick with one orientation even you spin the iPad in circles, you can use this little switch on the iPad mini 3 to lock the screen (Note that some apps can override this functionality if they require one orientation or the other). iPad Air 2 no longer has a side switch, but you can change Rotation and Volume settings in the Control Center (swipe up from the bottom of the screen to open Control Center).

+   - **(Tiny, mighty) speakers:** One surprise when I first got my iPad Air 2 was hearing a great little stereo system inside it and how much sound can come from these tiny speakers. The speakers are located along one side of the iPad Air 2 and iPad mini 3.

+   - **Volume:** This is a volume rocker that you use like any other volume rocker: Press up for more volume and down for less. You can also use this rocker as a camera button when the camera is activated.
