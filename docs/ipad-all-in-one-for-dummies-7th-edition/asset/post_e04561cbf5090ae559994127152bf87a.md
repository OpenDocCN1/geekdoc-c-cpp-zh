### Getting there by air

This list is a mix of travel-booking tools and apps that help you check on your seat assignment or flight status. Try these:

- Expedia Hotels & Flights: This free, nicely designed app, shown in Figure 3-3, lets you book travel online, see itineraries, and get flight information. My favorite feature is the ability to explore destinations by great deals, coupons, or available travel guides. You can even check hotels and sort the results by distance, price, and rating.

![](img/2a8a8d89daeb1b3896bd38ebf18af126_339_0.png)

**Figure 3-3:** Expedia is free and offers lots of travel-booking options.

- Seat Alerts: You know how you always get out that your seat will be fine, only three miles from the restroom or stuck in the middle of a seven-seat row on an international flight? By using Seat Alerts, which has data from more than 60 airlines and many plane models, you get the advice you need to choose the best seat to make your travel more enjoyable. This is free and well worth downloading in in-flight comfort.

- AirportStatus: Get information about flight status and delays all the United States and parts of Canada with this $0.99 app. (See Figure 3-4.) You even get information about the cause of delays, such as weather and airport closures.

![](img/2a8a8d89daeb1b3896bd38ebf18af126_340_0.png)

### Renting a car

If you're renting a car, you may want to compare prices and find the best deal. There are many apps that help you find and book a rental car. Try these:

- Expedia Hotels & Flights: This free, nicely designed app, shown in Figure 3-3, lets you book travel online, see itineraries, and get flight information. My favorite feature is the ability to explore destinations by great deals, coupons, or available travel guides. You can even check hotels and sort the results by distance, price, and rating.

![](img/2a8a8d89daeb1b3896bd38ebf18af126_342_0.png)

**Figure 3-3:** Expedia is free and offers lots of travel-booking options.

- RentalCars: This app allows you to compare rental car prices from many different companies. You can also search by location, date, and car type. It's a great way to save money on your rental car costs.

- Kayak: This app allows you to compare prices for flights, hotels, and rental cars. It's a great tool for finding the best deals on your trip.

### Finding your way

If you're exploring a new city, you may want to find your way around. There are many apps that help you navigate and find public transportation. Try these:

- Maps: This free app provides maps and directions for all over the world. You can also download maps for offline use, which is very helpful.

- Transit Maps: This app provides you with real-time information on public transportation, including buses, subways, and trains. It's a great way to get around a new city.

- Citymapper: This app allows you to find local attractions, restaurants, and more. It's a great tool for discovering what there is to do in a city.

### Finding Hotels

If you're planning a trip, you may want to find a place to stay. There are many apps that help you find and book hotels. Try these:

- Booking.com: This app allows you to search for and book hotels all over the world. You can also read reviews from other travelers to help you make a decision.

- Airbnb: This app allows you to search for and book hotels and vacation rentals. It's a great tool for finding the best deals.

- Expedia: This app allows you to search for and book hotels, flights, and rental cars. It's a great tool for planning your entire trip.
