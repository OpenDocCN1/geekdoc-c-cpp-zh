Turn on the feature in Settings by following these steps:

+   1. Tap Settings on the Home screen.
2. On the screen that appears, tap General > Accessibility and then tap Subtitles & Captioning.
3. In the menu that displays on the right side of the screen (see Figure 3-4), tap the Captions & SDH On/Off switch to turn on the feature.

Now when you play a movie with closed-captioning support, you can click the Audio and Subtitles button to the right of the playback controls to manage these features.

# Going to a Movie Chapter

You know that movies you view on a DVD or online are usually divided into chapters, so you can jump from one part to another quickly. Jumping to another chapter using the Videos app is a pretty simple procedure. Follow these steps:

+   1. Tap Videos on the Home screen.
2. Tap the Movies tab if it isn't already displayed.
