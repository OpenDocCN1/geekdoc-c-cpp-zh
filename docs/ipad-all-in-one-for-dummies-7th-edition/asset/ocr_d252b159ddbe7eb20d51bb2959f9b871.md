# Troubleshooting Your iPad

Everyone wants to think that his or her iPad is a perfect magical machine, unburied with the vagaries of crashing and system software bugs, but that's not always the case.

Here are some common issues that come up with your iPad, along with ways to deal with them.

# Dealing with a unresponsive iPad

If your iPad goes dead on, it's most likely a power issue, so the first thing to do is to plug the Lightning to USB Cable (or Dock Connector to USB Cable for pre-fourth-generation iPads) into the 10W USB Power Adapter, plug the 10W USB Power Adapter into a wall outlet, plug the other end of the Lightning to USB Cable (or Dock Connector to USB Cable) into your iPad, and charge the battery.

Another thing to try — especially if you think that an app might be hanging up the iPad — is to press the Sleep/Wake button for a few seconds. Then press and hold the Home button.
