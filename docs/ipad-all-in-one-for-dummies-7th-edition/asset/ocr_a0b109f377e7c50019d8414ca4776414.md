# Locating the Perfect Restaurant

What's a road warrior without his lunch? Hungry, that's what. So, why not use your iPad to find food? It's a selection of some intriguing restaurant-listing apps for you to explore:

+   - Vegetarian-Restaurants: Do you avoid meat in your diet? If so, check out the free Vegetarian-Restaurants app (shown in Figure 3-7). Look for vegetarian restaurants worldwide as well as vegetarian recipes and information about the vegetarian lifestyle.
