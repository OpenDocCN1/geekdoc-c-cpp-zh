# 253 Exploring other e-readers 

Though iBooks can handle content from many ePub sources, such as Google and Project Gutenberg, you may prefer to explore the tools offered by other e-readers. Your iPad is capable of using other e-readers to display book content from bookstores, like iStores. To do so, first download another e-reader application such as Kindle from Amazon or the Barnes & Noble app. You can also download a non-specific app such as Bluereader, which handles eBook and PDF format, as well as all formats that public libraries use. Use that application's features to search for, purchase, and download content.

The Kindle e-reader application is shown in Figure 7-4. After downloading the free app from the App Store, you open the app, go to the Amazon Kindle Store and enter the email address associated with your Amazon account and password. Any you have already bought from Amazon for the Kindle is archived and can be downloaded using your Kindle on your iPad to read any time. Use features such as changing the background to a sepia tone or changing the font to enhance your reading experience. Tap the Device to see titles stored on your iPad. From this e-reader, just push the title with your finger, and Remove an from Device buttons.

# Buying iTunes

If you set up an account with iTunes, you can buy books in the iTunes Store easily using the app (See Chapter 5 for more about iTunes.) To buy a book, follow these steps:

1. When you find a book in the iTunes Store that you want to buy, tap it, and then its price button, as shown in Figure 7-5.

![](img/2a8a8d89daeb1b3896bd38ebf18af126_271_0.png)

Figure 7-5: Tap the price to begin the buying process.

2. Tap the Buy Book (or Get Book) button. If you haven't already signed in, the iTunes dialog appears.

3. Enter your password and tap OK.

The book appears on your bookshelf, and the cost is charged to the payment method you specified when you opened your iTunes account — or the cost is deducted from your store credit if you have a balance. If you have signed in, your purchase is accepted immediately. No returns are allowed, so tap carefully! With Family Sharing, as many as six people can make purchases for iOS devices, including e-books, using the same credit card. See Chapter 1 for more about setting up Family Sharing.

After you have an e-book in iTunes, put on some music, settle back in a comfortable chair, and read it. Luckily, reading an e-book is just as easy (or perhaps easier than) reading a paperback. The next section shows how to do it.
