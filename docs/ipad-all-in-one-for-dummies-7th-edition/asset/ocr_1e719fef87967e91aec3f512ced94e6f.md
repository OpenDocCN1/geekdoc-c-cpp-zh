# Organizing Your Library

+   1. Tap Select.
2. Tap now one or more books and tap Map.
3. In the Collections popup shown in Figure 7-21, tap New Collection at the bottom (you may need to scroll down to see it).
4. On the blank line that appears, type a name.
5. Tap Done.

The popup closes and returns you to your Library.
