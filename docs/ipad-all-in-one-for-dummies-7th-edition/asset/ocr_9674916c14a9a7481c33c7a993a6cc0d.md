# # Introduction

Apple turned the computer world on its ear when it introduced the iPad. This small wonder, which weighed less than a pound and a half, sold many tens of millions of units in its first few years for good reason: It's well designed and feature rich, and it opens a world of apps and media viewing in a highly portable format.

# ## About This Book

iPad All-in-One For Dummies, 7th Edition, has one aim: to be the ultimate reference on the coolest device of the day. If you are reasonably computer savvy, you can use this book to get up quickly the day you buy your iPad, and then you can pick up the book anytime you feel like getting your knowledge of the iPad to the next level. Even if you've been puttering with your iPad for a while, you'll still find things between these that you didn't know.

Though the iPad is relatively simple to use, a lot is packed there, and you can get even more from it by downloading apps to do everything under the sun. This book approaches the iPad from every angle: from the basics to powerful roadwarrior tools.

You can read step-by-step instructions for basic tasks, hot tips for getting the most out of iPad, and tips about apps that can take your iPad experience further.

# ## Foolish Assumptions

To offer you a book that gives insight into the powerful ways you can use iPad, I have to assume that you are computer and mobile-phone literate. You don't have to be an iPhone user to use this book (though iPhone has a great similarities to iPad and shares many apps with it). You don't have to be a Mac-oriented person — PC people do just fine with the iPad.

I also assume that you want to take your use of the iPad to the next level by getting helpful tips and well as discovering several apps that are useful in expanding your iPad experience. You may use the iPad for personal pressure or to get your work done — both. If you travel a great deal or use the iPad to work, you'll find extra benefits in the material covered in Books III and IV.
