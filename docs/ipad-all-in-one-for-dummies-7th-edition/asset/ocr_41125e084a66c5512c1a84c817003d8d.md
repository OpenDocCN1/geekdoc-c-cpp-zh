If you're a frequent flyer, check out the Air Milage Calculator app. (Make sure exclude the e in milage when searching the Apps Store for this app.) For $0.99, you can get the mileage for travel between airports worldwide with up to three legs for each trip. You can also have the app calculate bonuses on your frequent-fyer status and the class of travel.

If packing is a challenge for you and you don't have a helper to help you out (mine last week), use Packing Pro. Keep track of lists what you might need, including your passport, clothing, and vital accessories such as an umbrella for that trip to Seattle. It costs $2.99, but if you're organization challenged, it might save your neck as you prepare for business or family trips.
