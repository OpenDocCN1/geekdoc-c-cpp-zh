# 230 Using Messages to Create and Send Text, Audio, and Video Messages

+   1. Tap the New Message button in the top-right corner.
2. Tap the camera icon on the left of the message entry field; then, in the popup that appears (see Figure 5-9), tap Photo Library and select a photo.
You are returned to the message.
3. If you prefer to capture a photo or video, with the choices in Figure 5-9 displayed, tap Take Photo or Video and tap the Capture button to take a photo.

Alternatively, you can move the slider at the bottom right to select Video. Move the phone around to take a video and tap the red Stop button when you've recorded what you want to record. Tap the Use Photo button. Your photo or video is attached to your message.

+   4. To send multiple photos or videos, repeat Step 3 for each one.
5. Tap Send to send your message and attachments.
