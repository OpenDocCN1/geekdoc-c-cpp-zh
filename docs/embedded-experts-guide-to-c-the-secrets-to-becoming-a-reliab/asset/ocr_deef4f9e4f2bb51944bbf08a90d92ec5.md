One area of (mostly pointless) debate among programmers, for no particularly good reason, is the bracing and indentation style that should be used. There are people with strong opinions on the subject and this has resulted in such nonsense as one style being dubbed the “One True Bracing and some programmers boasting of “correcting” the style of others. Becoming evangelical about such things is pointless; there are multiple styles and all result in valid code. Personally, I prefer to place opening and closing braces on their own line, indented to the same level as the preceding (or following) code, with additional indentation between the braces; this is the style I use in examples throughout this As far as I am concerned, you are free to pick any style which appeals to you. If you work as a team, agree a style and then have the team use it consistently. Ultimately, the style you use hardly matters, but consistency does.

There is less debate around the placement of parentheses after function names and control statements, although some people do still have strong feelings about this too.
