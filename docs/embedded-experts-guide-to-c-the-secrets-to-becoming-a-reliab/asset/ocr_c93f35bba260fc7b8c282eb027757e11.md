# Linker errors

As with any other part of the build process, linkage can fail or, worse, succeed but produce a result is not what the programmer intended. Linker errors can be particularly frustrating to resolve since they often have little or nothing to do with the source code you are trying to build. Many linker errors are caused by incorrect configurations, in which case you will need to check your linker documentation to determine the solution. Other than these, the two main types of error you are likely to come across are:

+   Multiple definitions of an identifier,

+   Missing definitions of an identifier.
