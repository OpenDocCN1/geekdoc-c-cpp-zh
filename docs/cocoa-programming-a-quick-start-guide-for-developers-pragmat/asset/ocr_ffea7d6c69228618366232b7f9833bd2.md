As if you don’t have enough options, you can also make these connections in the opposite direction. For example, choose the Back button and look at its Connections inspector. In the Sent Actions section, you should see the single entry, selector:⁶ Select this, drag the connection back to the WebView, and let go. A gray box with all the web view’s actions should appear.

Choose goBack:. You have now successfully made the connection in the opposite direction. You will soon develop a rhythm around your favorite method and direction. Stick with whatever you are most comfortable doing.
