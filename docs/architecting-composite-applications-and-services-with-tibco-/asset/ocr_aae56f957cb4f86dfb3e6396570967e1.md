# Accounts and Funds Transfers

There are three types of bank accounts involved in the claims payment process: Payer Accounts, Provider Accounts, and Settlement Accounts. Each insurance policy has an associated Payer Account from which claims against the policy are paid. Each health care service provider being paid through funds transfers has a Provider Account.
