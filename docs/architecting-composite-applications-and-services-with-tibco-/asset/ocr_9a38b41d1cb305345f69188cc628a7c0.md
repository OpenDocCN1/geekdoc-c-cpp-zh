Using benchmark results requires understanding the relationship between the benchmark test design and your component design. Unless you understand the benchmark test design in sufficient detail to be able to reproduce the test results, it may be difficult to determine the extent to which the test results can be interpreted in terms of your design. When in doubt, run your own benchmark test, particularly for components having performance that is critical for the overall solution performance.
