Aggregation: The hollow diamond at the “whole” end indicates that the “part” at the other end should be considered a part of the whole (e.g., each DepositFundsRequest includes an AccountReference). A multiplicity of 0..1 or 0..n indicates that the part is optional. A multiplicity of 1 indicates that it is required.

When the part doesn’t make any sense without the whole, a solid diamond is added at the “whole” end of the association (Figure A-6).

# Generalization

Sometimes you will encounter a situation in which there is a core concept and then a number of variations (Figure A-7). A UML Generalization relation is used to indicate this type of relationship. Generalization indicates that each of the subclasses has all of the characteristics (attributes, relationships, structure, etc.) of the parent class. If you don’t mean this, don’t use generalization! Typically each of the subclasses has additional characteristics that are unique to the subclass.
