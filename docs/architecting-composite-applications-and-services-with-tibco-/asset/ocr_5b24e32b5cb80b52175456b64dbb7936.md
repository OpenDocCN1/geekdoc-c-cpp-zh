# Payment Manager Specification: Process Overview

On the surface, the payment manager looks simple. It has a simple interface (Figure 4-6).

It also has a seemingly simple relationship to other processes. Process Claim tells it to pay a claim and Manage Payments either sends a check or does an electronic funds transfer (Figure 4-7). But this apparent simplicity is deceiving. To understand this, let’s take a look at a health care claim.
