# Summary

There are three views of an architecture that are essential for successful IT projects: process model, architecture pattern, and process-pattern mapping. The process model describes how the work of the business process is organized and the information (and other artifacts) that are involved. The architecture pattern shows the participants in the process (human and system) along with the communications channels between them and any constraints upon their use. The process-pattern mapping shows how the work is allocated to the participants and the resulting pattern of interactions between the participants.
