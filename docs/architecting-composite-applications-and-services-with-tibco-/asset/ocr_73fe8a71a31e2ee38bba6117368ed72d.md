# ActiveMatrix Adapters

As with ActiveMatrix BusinessWorks, there are two design and deployment options for ActiveMatrix adapters (Figure 2-6). When deployed in ActiveMatrix nodes, the adapter service engine is installed in the ActiveMatrix environment, and the TIBCO Business Studio™ is used to configure the adapters. The adapters, however, do not include TIBCO ActiveMatrix Service Bus as part of the product. Thus a separate installation (and license) of either ActiveMatrix Service Bus, Service Grid, or BusinessWorks is required.
