# P.R Averaging

We finally illustrate the value of averaging. The parameter estimates used to create our histograms were averaged according to the formula (8.10b), with $(N-N_0)/N=0.4$. Figure 8.7 shows histograms of the normalized parameter error for smoothed estimates. In the prior experiments, the columns are distinguished by $\rho$, and the two rows are distinguished by choice of gain $g$ in the step-size rule $\alpha_n=g/n^p$. The results are not so sensitive to choice of gain. Theory Section 8.7.3 tells us that the Polyak-Ruppert averaging will result in the optimal convergence rate for the MSE, provided the step-size $\alpha_n=g/n^p$ is used with $\rho \in(0.5,1)$ and $g > 0$.
