## 9.11 Temporal Difference Methods

As surveyed earlier in Section 5.9, the TD-learning algorithms developed by Sutton and Barto in the 1980s were designed to obtain approximations of value functions within a finite-dimensional parameterized class, with emphasis on linear function classes and neural networks. Sutton's dissertation contains early insights on temporal difference methods and some of the first TD algorithms [26, 339, 340] (see also Williams [376] for more early references). A fuller history of RL origins can be found in [338, 347]. The seeds planted by the RL explorers prompted a flurry of analysis in the 1990s (much of it led to Tsitslis and his students at MIT), along with many new algorithms and analytical techniques; more on the MIT school can be found in Section 10.10.
