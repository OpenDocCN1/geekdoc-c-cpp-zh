The notion of \"state\" is flexible in both control theory [15] and reinforcement learning\n[337, 338]. The motivation is the same in each field: For purposes of online decision\nmaking, replace the full history of observations at time $k$ by some finite-dimensional\n"sufficient statistic\" $x(k)$. One constraint that arises in RL is that the state process must\ndirectly observable; in particular, the belief that arises in partially observable\nMarkov decision processes (MDPs) requires the (model-based) nonlinear filter, and is\nnot directly useful for free-free RL. The practice, \"RL state\" is specified as\nsome compression of the full history of observations – see [338, 17.3] for further\ndiscussion.
