If $x(n) = 7$, then the coefficient of $	heta_n^s$ becomes negative, remember $x(n) = 7$ occurs only $1/7$ of the time.
In this example, TD($\alpha$) can be expressed as the linear recursion

```
$\theta_{n+1} = \theta_n + \alpha_{n+1}A_{n+1}\theta_n , \quad A_{n+1} = \zeta_n \{-\psi(x(n)) + \gamma\psi(7)\}^	op$.
```

The matrix (5.4) becomes

```
$A = \mathbb{E}_\varphi[A_{n+1}] = \frac{1}{7} \sum_{n=1}^7 A_n$.
```

Based on the recursion for $	heta_n^s$, it may not be surprising that $A$ is not Hurwitz for all values of $\gamma$. Figure 5.6 shows results from two experiments with $\gamma = 0.9$, two values of $\lambda$, and fixed step-size $\alpha_n = \alpha_0$.

The parameter estimates diverge in these two experiments, and the behavior is as predicted from the eigenvalues of $A$. With $\lambda=0$, the eigenvalues in the right half plane are complex, and in this case the eigenvalues oscillate to infinity.
