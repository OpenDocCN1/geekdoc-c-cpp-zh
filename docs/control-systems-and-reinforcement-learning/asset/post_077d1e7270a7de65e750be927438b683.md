- 4.9 Oja's Algorithm. This is a famous ODE technique, designed to estimate the eigenvectors of a $N \times N$ matrix $W$. Suppose that the matrix is positive definite, so that the eigenvectors of $W$ are nonnegative. Fix an integer $N_m \leq N$, and suppose there is a "spectral gap" in the sense: If the eigenvalues of $W$ are ordered that $\lambda_1 \geq \lambda_2 \geq \cdots \lambda_{N_m} > \lambda_{N_m+1}$. Our goal is to identify these first eigenvectors, along with the subspace $S$ spanned by the first $N_m$ eigenvectors. 

Let $m_t$ denote an $N \times N_m$ matrix whose columns are intended to approximate elements of $S$. Oja's subspace algorithm expressed as the polynomial differential equation:

```
\frac{d}{dt}m_t = (I - m_t m_t^T)W_t. \quad (4.154)
```

where $m_0$ is given as initial condition. It is known that for "most" initial conditions, the solution to the ODE is convergent and the limit $m_t$ lies in $S$ (see [91] and also [59, 278, 321]).
