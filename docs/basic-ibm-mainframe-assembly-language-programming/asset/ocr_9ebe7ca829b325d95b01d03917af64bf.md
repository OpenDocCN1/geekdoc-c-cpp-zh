On line 3 the assembler is told that this is the start of a control section and that its name is ADD (from the label on the SUBENTRY). A CSECT is an assembler directive and does not generate code. It means control section.

On line 4 the STM (Store Multiple) causes registers R14, R15, R0, R1, ... R11, R12 to be saved in successive 4 byte full words beginning with the fourth full word in the calling program's (R13) save area. Word 4 begins at offset 12 from the start of the calling program's save area whose address is in R13 and thus the displacement of 12 past R13.
