# 7.12.5 Projects

+   1. This project calculates a very simplified golf handicap. The idea of a golf handicap came in 1911. The handicap would allow golfers who had different abilities to play together in a more evenly-matched way. The formula to calculate a handicap is as follows:

> (Score – CourseRating) × 113/SlopeRating

To calculate your handicap you need at least 18-hand-scores or 9-hand-scores. A scorecard will look something like the following:

| Hole | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 | OUT |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---||
| Par | 5 | 4 | 3 | 4 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 36 |
| Score | 5 | 5 | 3 | 5 | 8 | 6 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 47 |

| Hole | 10 | 11 | 12 | 13 | 14 | 15 | 16 | 17 | 18 | IN | TOTAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---||
| Par | 4 | 4 | 3 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 3 | 5 | 3 | 6 | 72 |
| Score | 5 | 6 | 4 | 5 | 5 | 7 | 5 | 3 | 7 | 9 | 96 |

Par indicates the predetermined number of strokes that a 0-handicap golfer should take to complete the hole. Although a course's actual rating might be different than the total par the score card, we will assume the Course Rating = Total par the course. The Slope Rating shows the difficulty of a course for an average golfer. We will assume that your course's rating is 130. Indicate how many strokes you need to complete the hole.

First, you will need to record the scores. Next, you will need to determine your Adjusted Gross Score (AGS). We will assume that the maximum number of strokes you can take on any hole is 8; so, you will need to adjust the scores accordingly (both per hole score and the total for each round). Now that you have the AGS, you will need to determine your handicap differential. The equation for a Handicap Differential, or (AGS – CourseRating)x113/SlopeRating For example if your AGS is 96, the Course Rating is 72, and the Slope Rating is 130. You would have the equation (AGS – CourseRating)x113/SlopeRating, or (96 – 72)x113/130, which results in a Handicap Differential of 12.1. Next, we need to calculate your handicap index. The formula is sum of your differentials divided by the number of differentials, multiplied by 0.96, or (SumOfDifferentials/NumberofDifferentials)x0.96. Now that you have your Handicap Index, you can total up your Handicap using the formula: (HandicapIndex)x(SlopeRating)/113. Round to the nearest whole number.
