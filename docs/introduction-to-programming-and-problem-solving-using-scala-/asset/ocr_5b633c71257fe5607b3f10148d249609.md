+   5. Blackjack is one of the most popular card games in a casino. In this project, you will write a two person blackjack game in which the player will try to beat the dealer (computer) in one of three ways: 1) The player gets 21 points on their first two cards, without the getting blackjack (note that the dealer deals without excluding 21); 2) The player gets a final score higher than the dealer; 3) the dealer's hand exceeds 21. The computer deals out cards to both the player and the dealer (itself). The player's cards are both face-up, but the dealer only one card face-up and the other is face-down. Next, the deals if either the player or the dealer gets a blackjack. If no got blackjack, the player gets to add some cards to their hand. The player must decide whether to try "Hit", which means the dealer deals another card which is added to their hand, or to "Stand", which means to stop dealing cards to the player. If the player "Hits" and they go 21, then they lose. If not, the player gets to decide whether to "Hit" or "Stand". This process continues until the player loses or wins. If the player has not go over 21 or "Stands", then the dealer gets to draw cards. The general rule of the game that a dealer must follow is if the dealer's hand is less than or equal to 16, the dealer must "Hit" and add another card to their hand. The dealer must continue until the dealer's hand is greater than equal to the player's total (in which case the dealer wins), or the dealer has gone over 21 (in which case the player wins).
