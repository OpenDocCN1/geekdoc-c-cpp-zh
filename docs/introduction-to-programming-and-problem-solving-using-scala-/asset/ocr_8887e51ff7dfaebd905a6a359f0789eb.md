Some of the most fundamental things that we do on computers is to sort data to certain values and search through data for various things. These activities are known as SORTING and SEARCHING, respectively. Due to their importance in solving many different types of problems, a lot of work has gone into doing them as quickly and efficiently as possible. In this chapter we will start your exploration of the topics by working through some of the simpler approaches.

# ## 13.1 Basic Comparison Sorts

There are many different ways to sort data. The most general ones do not have many requirements on the nature of the data. All require the ability to compare elements to see if one should before another. These comparison sorts are the ones that are used most frequently, mainly because they are so flexible. There are limits on how fast they can be because they are not customized to the data, but typically their generality tr speed.

At this point we are not even going to consider the fastest comparison based sorts. Instead, we will start with some of the conceptually simpler sorts.
