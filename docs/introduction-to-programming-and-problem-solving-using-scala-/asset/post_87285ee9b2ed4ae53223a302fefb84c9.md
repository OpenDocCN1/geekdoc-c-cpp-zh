# Preface

Welcome to “Introduction to Programming and Problem Solving Using Scala”. This book is intended to be used in first-semester college classrooms to teach students programming. To accomplish this task, the Scala language is used. The book was constructed with a focus on the topics students need to know. These topics were woven into a format that was deemed the best way to communicate these ideas to students. You are about to embark on a field of study that will hopefully open your mind to new ways of thinking and impact the way you view everything around you. For students who intend to major in Computer Science and make careers working with technology, this is your first step in learning to communicate with the computer and to instruct the computer in how it should solve problems.

Programming is fundamentally problem solving. It is about figuring how to solve problems and express them in many ways that the computer can understand. Learning to program will help you develop and hone this ability. There are more direct benefits as well. The world you live in is extremely dependent on computing. Many of the things you do during an average day are enabled by computer power and digital processing is only going to grow over time. Even if you never have to write programs yourself, there is a good chance that you will interact with those who do and gain a competitive advantage by improving the information you use to do work. Code samples in this book were compiled and tested using Scala 2.12.

Even a basic knowledge of programming will help you understand what people are doing and communicate effectively with them. Those who do not intend to make a career in programming might occasionally encounter problems where they could save a lot of time if they used a computer to do it. When that happens, even a basic knowledge of programming can make your life much better. Data is becoming a bigger part of the business world; the vast amounts of data created and collected by modern technology cannot be processed by humans manually. To sift through this data means using machines, and software. You can either pay someone to write it or you can write it yourself. That is only an option if you know how to program.

While many might be tempted to view programming as an advanced concept, the reality is this book is going to take you back to your early education. The reason for this is you spent a lot of time in early years learning how to solve certain basic problems became second to you and you no longer think about them. In the context of programming you will have to go back and examine how you solve problems, even ones you have been doing for a long time. The reason for this is that you now have to tell a computer to do those same things, and the computer will need detailed instructions. The reality is the programming presented in this book is not an advanced technique, it is basic logic and problem solving done at a level you probably have not worked at you were much younger.

We often refer to programming as an art. Programming shares many features with traditional creative writing and painting. The programmer first creates an image in mind of what he or she wants to bring into existence. This is followed by a period of work bringing the mental image into a more formal result. The end is something new, born from the imagination of the creator, that can be experienced by others. The digital medium in some sense, seems less real than paint, clay, or stone, but it is also remarkably dynamic. You can create things in a computer that you have no means of creating in the real world. All the software you interact with every day, whether to get things done or just for entertainment, was written by programmers. The exercises and projects in this book have been created with the goal of giving you the ability to express your creativity.

Programming has another characteristic common in other creative arts: if you want to be good at it, you need to practice. There are many ways to program the solution to any problem. That introduces a lot of nuance. This book will strive to teach you the strengths and weaknesses of different approaches; to really understand how to write code to solve a problem, you need the experience of solving similar ones. Imagine an artist who never draws or paints for class projects, not doodling in notes, or a creative major who never writes a story except the ones required for class. Hopefully these hypothetical people seem to you. A computer science major who never writes code what is assigned for class is exactly the one. So explore the art of programming. Have fun with it and try to do what interests you.

Scala is a fairly new language that has gained a lot of momentum since it was adopted by Twitter in 2009, but there are other languages that are used more for professional development. Scala is newer than languages Java and Python, and that means that it has integrated lessons learned from those languages. Those languages are struggling to integrate. In particular, Scala has found significant use in developing web sites that have lots of users and doing data analysis.
