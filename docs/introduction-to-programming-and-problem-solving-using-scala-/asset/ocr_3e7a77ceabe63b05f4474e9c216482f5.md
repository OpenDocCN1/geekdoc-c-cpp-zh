+   This project has you write another simple "game" that you are likely familiar with sliding tiles in a 4x3 grid where one piece is missing. The tiles might have numbers or parts of a picture. Your goal is to move them around by sliding pieces into the one open space until they are in the desired order.

Write a GUI that shows the 4x3 grid. You can choose to use numbers of parts of an image. If you want to use parts of an image, you can get the small sections by loading the full image, drawing it into smaller images with offsets that so not the part you want for a piece appears in the smaller image.

The user should interact with the game by clicking on the piece that they want to slide. In the simplest implementation, they have to click on a square immediately adjacent to the open square. A bit of an extra challenge you make it so that they can click on any square in the same row or column as the one piece and multiple pieces can be moved at the same time.
