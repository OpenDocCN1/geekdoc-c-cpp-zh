Those who do not intend to make programming a career might of time occasionally encounter problems where they realize they could save a lot of time if they used a computer to do it for. When that happens, even a basic knowledge of programming can make your life a whole lot better. Data is becoming a bigger and bigger part of the business world. The vast amounts of data created and collected by modern technology can not be processed by humans manually. To sift through this data to find means using machines, and that means software. You can either pay someone to write it or you can write it yourself. That is only an option if you know how to program.

While many might be tempted to view programming as an advanced concept, the reality of this book is going to take you back to your early education a few times. The reason for this is you spent a lot of time in early years learning how to solve certain basic problems. The approaches became second to you and no longer think about them, you do them. In the context of programming you will have to go back and examine how you solve problems, even ones you have been doing for a long time. The reason for this is that you now have to tell a computer to do those same things, and the computer will need detailed instructions. The reality is, the programming presented in this book is not an advanced technique, it is basic logic and problem solving, done at a level you probably have not worked at you were much younger.

We often refer to programming as an art. Programming shares many features with traditional creative writing and painting. The programmer first creates an image in her mind of what heshe wants to bring into existence. This is followed by a period of work bringing mental image into a more formal result. The end is something new, born from the imagination of the creator, that can be experienced by others. The digital medium in some sense, seems less real than paint, clay, or stone, but it is also remarkably dynamic. You can create things in a computer that you have no means of creating in the real world. All the software you interact with every day, whether to get things done or just for entertainment, was written by programmers. The exercises and projects in this book have been created with the goal of giving you the ability to express your creativity.

Programming has another characteristic common in other creative arts, if you want to be good at it, you need to practice. There are many ways to program the solution to any problem. That introduces a lot of nuance. This book will strive to teach you in regards to the strengths and weaknesses of different approaches, to really understand how to write code to solve a problem, you need the have of experiencing similar ones. Imagine an artist who never draws or paints for class projects, not doodling in notes, or a creative major who never writes a story except the ones required for class. Hopefully these hypothetical people seem to you. A computer science major who never writes code what is assigned for class is exactly the one. So explore the art of programming. Have fun with it and try to do what interests you.
