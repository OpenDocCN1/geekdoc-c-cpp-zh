# Aims and Scope

This series covers various areas of computing, as well as related technical areas, such as software engineering, artificial intelligence, computer engineering, information systems, and technology. The series will accommodate textbooks for undergraduate and graduates, generally adhering to worldwide curricula from professional societies. The editors wish to encourage new and innovative ideas and proposals, and are keen to help and encourage new authors. We welcome proposals that: provide groundbreaking and imaginative perspectives of computing; present topics in a new and exciting context; open up opportunities for emerging areas, such as multi-media, security, and mobile systems; capture new developments and applications in emerging fields of computing; and address topics that provide support for computing, such as mathematics, statistics, life and physical sciences, and business.
