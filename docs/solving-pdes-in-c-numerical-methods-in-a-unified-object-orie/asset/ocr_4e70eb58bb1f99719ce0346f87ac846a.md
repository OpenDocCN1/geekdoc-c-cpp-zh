Then, the contributions from the different monomials are multiplied by the corresponding (unknown) coefficients in \tilde{x} and summed up. Thus, an element in B must be a particular partial derivative of a particular monomial, evaluated at a particular corner, edge midpoint, or side midpoint of T.
