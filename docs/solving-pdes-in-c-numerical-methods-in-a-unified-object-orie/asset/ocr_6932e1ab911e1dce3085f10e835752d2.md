6. The nodal basis functions in the unit tetrahedron are the polynomials of three variables that have the value 1 at one corner and 0 at the other three corners:

$$p_0(x,y,z)=1-x-y-z,$$
$$p_1(x,y,z)=x,$$
$$p_2(x,y,z)=y,$$
$$p_3(x,y,z)=z.$$

Use the above "integral()" function to compute the integral of these function over the unit tetrahedron, and show that it is the same:

$$\iiint_T p_0\,dx\,dy\,dz=\iiint_T p_1\,dx\,dy\,dz$$
$$=\iiint_T p_2\,dx\,dy\,dz$$
$$=\iiint_T p_3\,dx\,dy\,dz$$
$$=1/24.$$

7. Use the above "integral()" function to show that the integral of the square of these functions is also the same:

$$\iiint_T p_0^2\,dx\,dy\,dz=\iiint_T p_1^2\,dx\,dy\,dz$$
$$=\iiint_T p_2^2\,dx\,dy\,dz$$
$$=\iiint_T p_3^2\,dx\,dy\,dz$$
$$=1/60.$$

8. Use the above "integral()" function to show that the integral of any product of two distinct nodal basis functions is the same:

$$\iiint_T p_0p_1\,dx\,dy\,dz=\iiint_T p_1p_2\,dx\,dy\,dz$$
$$=\iiint_T p_2p_3\,dx\,dy\,dz$$
$$=\iiint_T p_3p_0\,dx\,dy\,dz$$
$$=\iiint_T p_0p_2\,dx\,dy\,dz$$
$$=\iiint_T p_1p_3\,dx\,dy\,dz$$
$$=1/120.$$
