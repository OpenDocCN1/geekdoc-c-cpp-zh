Thanks to the extensive “Dirichlet” data, this relatively easy problem can be solved rather rapidly to supply the required initial guess for the Newton iteration on the current mesh.

Once the Newton iteration on the current mesh is complete, the resulting discrete solution is used to refine the current mesh and produce the next finer mesh. This completes the refinement step.

On the finer mesh, the value of K₁, the coefficient in the nonlinear term, may increase slightly in magnitude (in the spirit of Section 21.5). This way, one may solve more and more nonlinear and difficult problems on finer and finer meshes. Furthermore, on the finest mesh, K₁ may increase further in magnitude in each Newton iteration, in the spirit of [4].

In (semi-)implicit time marching in time-dependent problems, this procedure should be used in the first time level only. In further time levels, the initial guess for the Newton iteration can be taken from the previous time level.
