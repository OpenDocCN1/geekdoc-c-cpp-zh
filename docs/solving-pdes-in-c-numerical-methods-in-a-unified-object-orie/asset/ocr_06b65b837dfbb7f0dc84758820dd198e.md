1. Use the "integral()" function in Section 24.11 above to compute the area of the unit triangle t in Figure 5.5 by integrating the constant polynomial p(x, y) ≡ 1 over it:

2. The nodal basis functions in the unit triangle are the polynomials of two variables that have the value 1 at one vertex and 0 at the other two vertices:

p₀(x, y) = 1 − x − y,
 p₁(x, y) = x,
 p₂(x, y) = y.

Use the above "integral()" function to compute the integral of these functions over the unit triangle, and show that it is the same:

∬ₜ p₀ dx dy = ∬ₜ p₁ dx dy
 = ∬ₜ p₂ dx dy
 = 1/6.

3. Furthermore, show that the integral of the square of these functions is also the same:

∬ₜ p₀² dx dy = ∬ₜ p₁² dx dy
 = ∬ₜ p₂² dx dy
 = 1/12.

4. Furthermore, show that the integral of any product of two distinct nodal basis functions is also the same:

∬ₜ p₀ p₁ dx dy = ∬ₜ p₁ p₂ dx dy
 = ∬ₜ p₂ p₀ dx dy
 = 1/24.
