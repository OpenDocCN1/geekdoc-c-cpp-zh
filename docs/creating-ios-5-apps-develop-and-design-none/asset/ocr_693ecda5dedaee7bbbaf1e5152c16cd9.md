This chapter covers all the tasks needed
to polish our application and get it ready
for sale in the App Store. We’ll look at the
details that go into a finished app, from
icons and startup screens to localization and
build settings. Finally, we’ll examine the process
of building the app for distribution—both for
ad hoc distribution and for sale in the iTunes
App Store.
