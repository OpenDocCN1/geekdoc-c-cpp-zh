There are two important changes. First, we're only using a 0.15-second duration. Second, we added the UIViewAnimationCurveEaseIn option.

By default, Core Animation will interpolate the animations evenly over the duration. This makes the animation appear at a constant duration. UIViewAnimationCurveEaseIn causes the animation to begin slowly, then speed up over the animation's duration.
