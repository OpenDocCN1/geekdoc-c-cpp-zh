### Evidence for the portfolio

The practice manager was keen to know if the PHCT and the PPDP processes were useful. Evaluation of the first workshop indicated that since the PHCT meetings had been abandoned, this was a good opportunity for the team to review the practice. The opportunity for the team to discuss and contribute to the future of the practice was the most positive and valuable outcome of the exercise.
