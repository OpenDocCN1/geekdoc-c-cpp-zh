+   ### The ‘what and why’ of identifying priorities and priority areas
+   
+   - Make sure everyone is aware of the ‘external’ agenda such as the PCT’s priorities and NSFs (see Box 5).
+   - If the team has carried out individual SWOT analyses, these should be co-ordinated and a compilation of this evidence is required so that areas requiring attention should be included in the list (see Box 6).
+   - Do not forget to include the ‘must-do’s, or the ‘it-would-be-foolish-not-to-do’s such as addressing health and safety issues or performance targets/indicators. This is where it is important to spend some time reaching agreement on two or three priority areas for improvement to be worked on over the coming year.
+   - Remember one small change that brings about improvement is worth more than half a dozen good intentions (see Box 7).
