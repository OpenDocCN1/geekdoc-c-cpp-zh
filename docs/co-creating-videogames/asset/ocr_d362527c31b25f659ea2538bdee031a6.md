It started out at the University of Queensland as a PhD research project back in 1997 and there I need to especially thank my friend and mentor Tony Thwaites and supervisor Tom O'Regan.

Thanks to the management and staff at Auran, especially Greg Lane, Tony Hilliam, and Graham Edelsten, and the members of the *Trainz* and *Fury* development teams (especially former Auran colleagues Rob Shaw, Rachel Nixon, Ieuan Hyde, Stephanie Bond, Jason Robson, Alex Weekes and Dan Gray). Colleagues at this institution have provided support, critical feedback, encouragement and the collegial exchange of ideas that makes doing research a rewarding vocation.
