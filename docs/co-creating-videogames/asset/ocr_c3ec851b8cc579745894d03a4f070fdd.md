The salient point introduced by the social network market model is that this is not a static or closed situation in which we can always clearly and definitively identify what the pay-offs are: market or non-market motivations, incentives or behaviours. Instead, these emergent co-creative practices potentially redefine our understandings of what markets are and how they operate in relation to social and cultural networks. These markets are because exchange occurs, but it is social connections and recommendations, access and attention that perform the coordinating function, not just price.
