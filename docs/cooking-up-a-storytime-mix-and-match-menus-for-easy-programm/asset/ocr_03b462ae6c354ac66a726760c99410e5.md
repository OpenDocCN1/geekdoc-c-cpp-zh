*Big Rigs on the Move* by Candice Ransom (Lerner, 2011). Photographs accompany simple text in a book with great appeal for preschoolers.

*This Train* by Paul Collicutt (Farrar, Straus and Giroux, 2001). Simple text, lots of repetition, and some wonderful math words make this a winner for storytime.
