# Transition Rhymes

Transitioning from one activity to the next can be a challenging feat for young
children at storytimes. Transition rhymes can be an excellent way to get standing
children back into their seats and ready to listen. Here are some rhymes written
for that purpose. The movements to accompany them should be self-explanatory.

# I Can Hop

I can hop,
I can leap,
I can clap,
I can sleep,
I can turn around,
I can blow a kiss,
I can sit right down,
Just like this!

# Laughing Clap

Laughing clap, *(clap hands)*
Happy slap, *(cross hands to slap opposite shoulders)*
Smart I am with a tap tap tap. *(tap index finger against temple)*
Flying flap, *(flap arms like wings)*
Sleepy nap, *(yawn and drop to the floor)*
Hands I fold in my lap lap lap. *(fold hands into your lap)*

# Quivering

I shake my hands,
And wiggle my nose,
I jiggle my knees and my elbows.
I wobble my backside
As fast as it goes.
I'll flutter my lips
Then plop on the floor,
And then I'll be ready
To listen some more!

# This Is My Head

This is my head,
And these are my toes.
I can turn around twice, I suppose.
