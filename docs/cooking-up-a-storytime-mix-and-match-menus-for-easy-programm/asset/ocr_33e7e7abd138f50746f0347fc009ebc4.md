The rat took his bat,
Swing it at the gnat,
But he only knocked the hat
Off the fine, fat cat.
Drat!

# *Supplies*

+   - small amounts of felt in a variety of colors
+   - scissors

# *Directions*

Download patterns 5.9 through 5.14 from alaeditions.org/webextras. Using the patterns, cut the cat, rat, hat, bat, mat, and gnat out of your chosen colors of felt.

# *Telling*

With each line, place the appropriate flannelboard piece on the board and pause before naming it aloud, giving the children the opportunity to finish the rhyme. You may move the figures to illustrate the story in the latter portion.
