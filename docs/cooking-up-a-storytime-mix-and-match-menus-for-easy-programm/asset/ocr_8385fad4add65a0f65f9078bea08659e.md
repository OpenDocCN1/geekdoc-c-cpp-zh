I may look like one,
But
I'm not a horse.
What am I?
*(zebra)*

I like to eat grass
And leaves and bugs,
And you'd never call me dumb!
I swing from trees,
I chatter and scream,
And I have an opposable thumb.
What am I?
*(monkey)*

My skin is rough and scaly.
I can wait for my food
A long while.
And though I have
Impressive teeth,
You should stay
*Far away*
From my smile!
What am I?
*(crocodile)*

I have a big horn
On the end of my nose
(Though it's mostly made of hair.)
I'm not very smart,
But I'm really quite fierce,
And I *charge*, so you'd better beware!
What am I?
*(rhinoceros)*
