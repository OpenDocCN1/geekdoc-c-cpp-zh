# Chinese Journal of Aeronautics, 26(2): 427–436, 2013

A comparative study of two kinds of vortex-muzzle

The vortex-muzzle with a tangential intake has a higher discharge coefficient and more stable flow field than the normal one with a guide vane.

| Type | Discharge coefficient | Flow field stability |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tangential intake | Higher | More stable |
| Guide vane | Lower | Less stable |

The experimental results show that the vortex-muzzle with a tangential intake has a higher discharge coefficient and more stable flow field than the normal one with a guide vane.

The vortex-muzzle is a kind of fuel injector, which is widely used in aerospace engines.

In this paper, two kinds of vortex-muzzle are compared: one is with a tangential intake, the other is with a guide vane.

The discharge coefficient and flow field stability are measured and analyzed.
