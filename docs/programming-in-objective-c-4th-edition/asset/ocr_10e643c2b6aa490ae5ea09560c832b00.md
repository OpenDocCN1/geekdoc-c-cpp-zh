Blue guide lines appear as you move the label around inside the window. Sometimes they will appear to help you align objects with other objects previously placed in the window. At other times, they appear to make sure your objects are spaced far enough apart from other objects and and from the edges of the window, to be consistent with Apple’s interface guidelines.

You can always reposition the label in the window at any time in the future by selecting it and dragging it to another spot inside the window.

Notice that the Inspector pane gives you information about the currently selected object in your window.
