PP 3.6 Write an application that reads the lengths of the sides of a triangle from the user. Compute the area of the triangle using Heron’s formula (below), in which s represents half of the perimeter of the triangle and a, b, and c represent the lengths of the three sides. Print the area to three decimal places.

Area = √[s(s − a)(s − b)(s − c)]
