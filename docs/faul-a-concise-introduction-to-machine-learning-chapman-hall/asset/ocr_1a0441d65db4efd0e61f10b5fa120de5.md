Obviously, the elements of \(\sigma(\mathbf{a})\) sum to 1. The softmax function is most often used in the final layer, where the number of synapses is the same as the number of classes, \(K\). It gives a probabilistic interpretation to which class the sample, which passed through the neural network, belongs.

However, the construction of specific activation functions as in (5.3) is not in the spirit of machine learning, where as many tasks as possible shall be completed by the machine. Looking at Figure 5.16, we see that the region can actually be described as points on the right of the thin line and on the left of the bold line shall output 1. We denote the *hidden* variables, also known as *latent variables*, by \(z_1\) and \(z_2\). This is a two layer neural network, since a layer are the synapses from one set of neurons to another.
