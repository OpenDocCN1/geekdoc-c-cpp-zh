The inverse Wishart distribution \(W^{-1}(\Sigma|\Psi, \nu)\) is the conjugate prior for the covariance matrix \(\Sigma\) of a multivariate normal distribution with a known mean. We can assume that the mean of this distribution is zero, since we can re-centre the data at zero by subtracting the mean from all samples.
