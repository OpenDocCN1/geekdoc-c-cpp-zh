+   * Deep learning: We put our experiences in a wider context and draw conclusions.
+   * Supervised learning: Here we have some external input, but are also allowed to discover things by ourselves.
+   * Teaching: This is completely governed by external input.

Once we have learned something, we can recognize anomalies. A child below a certain age will not be impressed by a levitation act. However, once it has discovered gravity (though in a more rudimentary way than Galileo), it wants to experiment on it again and again and again as any parent picking up the toy the child has dropped from its highchair for the thousandth time will testify.

Machine learning is all about transferring the various modes of learning we have identified here to machines. We are already very good at writing procedural or object oriented programs, teaching machines this way. But what about the other modes?
