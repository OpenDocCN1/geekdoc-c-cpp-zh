If the input signals to V on X1 and Y1 and 0 to X2 and Y2 are now changed, the polarization on the electromagnets changes to that shown in Figure 17.28b. The two north poles at the top of the drawing will be repel, and the north pole on the rotor will be attracted to the south pole on the right-hand side of the stator. The rotor will thus move 90° in the clockwise direction (see Figure 17.29).

| V | 0 | V | 0 | 90° |
