**Propagation Velocity** **Propagation Delay** **in/ps** The Propagation Velocity (distance per time) is the inverse of the propagation delay (delay per distance). Recall that the delay depends upon the dielectric constant of the surrounding medium. The propagation velocity, \(\Theta_p\), is in in/ps and given as Eq. (21.24). The propagation delay, \(\tau_p\), is in ps/in and is given as Eq. (21.25).

\(\Theta_p = \frac{1}{\sqrt{LC}}\) (21.24)
\(\tau_p = \sqrt{LC}\) (21.25)
