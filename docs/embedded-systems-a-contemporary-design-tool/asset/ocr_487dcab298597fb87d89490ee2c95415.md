In the early days, the software for embedded applications was written either in machine code or in assembler. The C language was developed to expedite and to simplify the design and development process. Consequently, several of the features and capabilities we find in the language were targeted specifically for such applications. In this chapter, we will examine four of these: bitwise operators; functions, pointer variables, and structs. We will also look at two important uses of pointers: pointers to functions and interrupting events.
