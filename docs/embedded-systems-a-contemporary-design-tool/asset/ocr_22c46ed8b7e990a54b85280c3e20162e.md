The difference between the guaranteed maximum output low voltage and the maximum recognized input low voltage is called the low-level noise immunity or noise margin. These specifications ensure that with a minimum output signal of 2.5 VDC from a driving device, the receiving device will tolerate up to 0.5 VDC of noise on its input and still interpret the signal as a logical 1.
