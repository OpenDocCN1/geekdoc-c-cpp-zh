# CMOS

DC The top or bottom transistor is OFF, which gives very low power consumption. In the logic low output state, the sink current is coming from the gate circuit of a following device (or devices). In the logic high output state, the source current is going to the gate circuit of a following device (or devices).

AC Both devices are ON for a short interval as shown in Figure 14.35 and the sink and source currents now go through both devices to ground.

# TTL

DC The top or bottom device is OFF. However, power consumption is higher than with CMOS. With a logic low output, the sink current is coming from the emitter or a diode circuit of a following device. With a logic high source, the current going to the emitter or diode circuit of a following device.

AC Both devices are ON for a short interval as shown in Figure 14.36. The sink and source currents now go through both devices to ground.
