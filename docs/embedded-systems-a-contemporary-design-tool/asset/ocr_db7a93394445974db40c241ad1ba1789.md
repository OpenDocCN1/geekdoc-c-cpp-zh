Today, a wide variety of protocol standards are available. Although a proprietary network and protocol must sometimes be used, given a choice one should opt for one of the standards. The general objective of each standard is to facilitate message exchange in a specific application context such as small computer networks (EIA-232 or USB), simple local area networks (Firewire, Bluetooth, P C), automotive networks (Controller Area Network [CAN] bus), or manufacturing environments (CAMAC).
