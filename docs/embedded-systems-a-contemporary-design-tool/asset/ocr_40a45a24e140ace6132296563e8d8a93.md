To enhance both their usefulness and their flexibility, PLDs are designed with a variety of output configurations. Typically, these include the standard unidirectional combinational output. This configuration may be extended to support bidirectional input and output (which may also be programmable) as well as tristate control. These outputs may also be invertible through a programmable XOR path. Latched or registered outputs support basic storage and permit the design and implementation of sequential logics. These are illustrated in the circuit fragments in Figures 19.3 and 19.4.
