# 16.6 Enablement: Value Prototyping

Value prototyping provides the best possible high-level solution design and custom-tailored prototype system, combining business, industry, solution, and technology into one. Comprising this holistic approach and focusing on fast tangible results, it provides in-depth insight and confidence before committing to the real world. Both business and IT can explore and address such uncertainties as ambiguous and changing requirements, technical complexity and unexpected errors, and even skepticism from business users. This helps reduce guesswork and solve critical problems early, before the project goes too far. In other words, we scaffold opinion leaders and power users to design, build together based on their live testimony, and prove it by their experience rather than by analysis with logical assumptions.
