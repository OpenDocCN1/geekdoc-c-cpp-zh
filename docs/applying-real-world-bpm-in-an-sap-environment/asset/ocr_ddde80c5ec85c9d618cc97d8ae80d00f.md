# Praise and Criticism

We hope that you enjoyed reading this book. If it met your expectations, please do recommend it, for example, by writing a review on http://www.sap-press.com. If you think there is room for improvement, please get in touch with the editor of the book: florian.zimniak@galileo-press.com. We welcome every suggestion for improvement but, of course, also any praise!

You can also navigate to our web catalog page for this book to submit feedback or share your reading experience via Facebook, Google+, Twitter, email, or by writing a book review. Simply follow this link: http://www.sap-press.com/H3134.
