# 13.4 Business Add-Ons to ASAP

The business add-ons to ASAP extend the ASAP methodology with modular business implementation content and additional methodology and governance frameworks. The business add-ons provide proven implementation content for implementation of various industry solutions, solutions packages, and other related areas such as agile methodology, BPM, SOA, Value Management, EA governance, and strategy frameworks. The business add-on concept is the gateway to executing process-based and easier-to-consume solutions including enabling simpler, shorter, and less disruptive implementations. The first business add-ons to ASAP were released to the SAP community on June 7, 2010.
