# 16.9 Enablement: Communities

It delivers an unparalleled depth and breadth of knowledge, insight, and rich content about SAP solutions and services in a collaborative environment that encourages innovation and sharing of best-run business practices.

When you join SCN, you'll connect directly with a diverse member base that engages in an unparalleled level of knowledge sharing.
