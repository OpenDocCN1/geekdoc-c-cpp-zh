Extracted data is then checked for duplicates within its own data and cleansed. In the new process, this step begins automatically, without any human intervention or setup. Similar to the as-is process, some ambiguous matches need human intervention to resolve. Data analysts are then given the task of resolving ambiguous matches, and appropriate line-of-business managers (according to region) are given the task of approving the proposed resolution by data stewards, as depicted in Figure 7.12.
