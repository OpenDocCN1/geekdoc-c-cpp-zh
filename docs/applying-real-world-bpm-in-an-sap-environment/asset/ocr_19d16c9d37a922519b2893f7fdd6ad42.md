# 2.3.2 Case Study: Arvato Lead Logistics Services

Arvato Logistics Services is a subsidiary of Arvato AG, the international media service provider of the Bertelsmann Group. As a lead logistics provider (also a fourth-party logistics provider), they design, orchestrate, and manage significant portions of the outsourced supply chain on behalf of their customers [Arvato, 2010; ARC, 2005].

The goal was to benefit from blurring the borders between the out-sourcing manufacturer, in-sourcing logistics service providers, and their partner/supplier ecosystem and move into more sophisticated, more complex, higher-margin logistics services (refer to Table 2.2).

Arvato Logistics Services was founded in 1964 as Bertelsmann Distribution. They have transformed their business model and now act as a global supply chain manager. Their service offering comprises end-to-end supply chain solutions including customer service, sourcing, production, procurement, warehousing, order-entry, distribution, after-sales services, and financial services. Their core competency is the orchestration of various logistics offerings (transportation, warehousing, value-added services) from several specialized providers without necessarily owning the related assets. Customer value is created through end-to-end process visibility and orchestration.
