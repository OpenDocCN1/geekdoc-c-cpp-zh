The value driver field (VDF) between the core business and technology delivery (technology management) is not managed consciously in many organizations. Thus, the core business misses an area to be optimized, and the evaluation and running costs between system and core business are consequently unadjusted and off balance.

# Lessons Learned from the Industry

Often current operations follow well-known organizational patterns clinging to already known and tried practices for how things are done. The cost is unchanged and follows the price development in the market, the market potential is decreasing, and the executives have three options, or a combination of these:

+   - Drop out of the market
+   - Use more money for perfection
+   - Optimize the processes
