Main (management or support) processes with focus on whole company

Core processes with focus for example on department level

![](bbox=[0.35, 0.534, 0.64, 0.63])

+   - Identify process ownership (eventually)

![](bbox=[0.35, 0.63, 0.64, 0.726])
