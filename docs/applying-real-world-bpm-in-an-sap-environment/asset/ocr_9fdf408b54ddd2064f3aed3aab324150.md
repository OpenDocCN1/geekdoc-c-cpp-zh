# 7.2 Ericsson: Using Business Rules to Enable Globalization of Supplier Master Data Governance

> The many country-specific requirements make it difficult to consolidate supplier master data governance globally. By leveraging BPM and business rules to guide users through these requirements, the project has delivered a robust and user-friendly solution.
> 
> – Stacey Drinan, IT Platform Area Manager, Ericsson AB

Ericsson, a global supplier of telecommunications equipment, has been engaged in a multi-year program of continuous improvement of its master data processes. In its latest project, Ericsson sought to add an additional level of usability to its supplier master data processes. The company incorporated business rules into their process automation to guide correct user input of supplier data, which had specific and individual needs for the countries where the suppliers are located.
