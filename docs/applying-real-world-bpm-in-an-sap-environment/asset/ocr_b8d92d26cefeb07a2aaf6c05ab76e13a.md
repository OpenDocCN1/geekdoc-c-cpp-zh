# 11.2.5  BPM and Core Business

The VDF that will facilitate the connection between core business and BPM is usually not given enough attention. It has not really been given a proper name yet. A term like *balancing* has been suggested, for example, ensuring that purpose and performance of intraorganization activities can be managed in a way that makes it possible to deliver the end product to a customer at a price that can compete on the market and delivers as much profit to the organization as possible.
