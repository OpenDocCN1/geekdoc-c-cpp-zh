Combining BPM principles with EA principles in a CoE gives the EA approach practical value. One value is that it gives something to EA that is one of the weakest points within any existing EA framework or method. It doesn’t matter if you look at a Zachman Framework, the Object Management group, the Gartner EA method, or the TOGAF Framework Enterprise Architecture methods and frameworks; they are missing a continuous improvement perspective and approach in this day and age. This is where the BPM process management lifecycle (PML) can help (see Figure 11.7).
