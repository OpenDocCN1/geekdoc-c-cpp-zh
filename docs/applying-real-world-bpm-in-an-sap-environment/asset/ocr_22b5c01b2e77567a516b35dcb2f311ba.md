# 11.1 Company Profile

Hospira is a global specialty pharmaceutical and medication delivery company with headquarters in Lake Forest, IL. Hospira’s portfolio includes generic acute-care and oncology injectables and integrated infusion therapy and medication management systems. Hospira’s portfolio of products is used by hospitals and alternate site providers such as clinics, home healthcare providers, and long-term care facilities.
