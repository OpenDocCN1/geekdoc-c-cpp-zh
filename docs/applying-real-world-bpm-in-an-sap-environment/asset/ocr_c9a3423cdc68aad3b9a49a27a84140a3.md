These models are appropriate for many scenarios but often have difficulty expressing the variability common in many processes that are executed collaboratively by knowledge workers, processes such as creating a product vision or executing a corporate merger. These processes are repeated but typically adjusted based on the business context by leveraging the collective experience of the team executing them; that is, they are executed based on practices rather than traditional formal business processes.
