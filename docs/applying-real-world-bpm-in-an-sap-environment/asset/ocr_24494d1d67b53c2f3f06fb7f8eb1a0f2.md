The chief of defense is the head of the Danish Armed Forces and is head of the Defense Command, which is managed by the Ministry of Defense, which has three purposes and six tasks. Its primary purposes are to prevent conflicts and war, preserve the sovereignty of Denmark, and secure the continuing existence and integrity of the independent Kingdom of Denmark and further peaceful development in the world with respect to human rights. Its primary tasks are NATO participation in accordance with the strategy of the alliance; detection and repulsion of any sovereignty violation of Danish territory (including Greenland and the Faroe Islands); defense cooperation with non-NATO countries, especially Central and East European countries; international missions in the areas of conflict prevention, crisis-control, humanitarian mission, peacemaking, and peacekeeping; participation in “total defense” in cooperation with civilian resources, and maintenance of a sizeable force to execute these tasks at all times.
