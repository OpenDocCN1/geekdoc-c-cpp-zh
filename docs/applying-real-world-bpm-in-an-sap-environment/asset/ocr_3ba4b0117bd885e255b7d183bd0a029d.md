Documentation of enterprise services is an important activity for SOA-based solutions. Because the reuse of enterprise services is one of the cornerstones of a successful service-oriented architecture, even services that are modeled in the best way possible need to be accompanied by thorough documentation to ensure widespread reusability.

Enterprise services documentation should describe the technical contract between the service provider and the consumer. This is required to understand the services’ behavior in detail. Typically, these contracts group the service description documents and technical diagrams and may be supplemented by other, nontechnical documents.

The service models in ES Repository, which are created in the design activity, are a binding contract between the consumer and the service provider.
