Both LM Wind Power and SAP agree that the implementation has been successful and use of hybrid methodology based on traditional ASAP combined with a Scrum approach is promising to open new ground for successful projects. LM Wind Power has decided to use the same implementation methodology for the remaining parts of the scope. SAP has leveraged the lessons from this and other agile projects and reflected them in both core ASAP methodology and the business add-on to ASAP delivery of agile methodology.
