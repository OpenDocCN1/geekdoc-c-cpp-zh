To see the big picture and the interdependencies, architects have to understand the various factors that influence an enterprise, including people, process, customers, technology, and so on. Having visibility across all of these factors helps architects and decision makers better predict the butterfly effects (how one infinitesimal action can have a huge effect on the course of a much larger event).

BPM is an integral part of architecture because it allows IT to properly enable business capabilities using the right technologies and to measure performance in the context of the business. By integrating BPM with the architecture practice, organizations can achieve the level of transparency across the enterprise that is required to directly trace IT organizations’ value to the business and measure it (see Figure 11.2).
