# 2.6 Summary

Over the last couple of decades, the theories of group sequential testing have been well-established, particularly in the context of clinical trials. The US Food and Drug Administration (FDA) released a draft guidance for industry on "Adaptive Design Clinical Trials for Drugs and Biologics" in 2010 [31] and stated that "group sequential designs are among the other adaptive designs considered well-understood designs, offering valid approaches to clinical trial implementation." This chapter has provided both theoretical and practical aspects of designing and monitoring a clinical trial adopting the sequential testing procedure. The sequential testing methodologies we have surveyed are able to preserve the type I error under nominal α level. With an independent DSMB, it is possible to maintain study integrity during the course of a trial. The SAS procedures and the examples discussed in this chapter provide a solid introduction to the sequential testing framework and programming tools for scientists, clinicians, statisticians, and programmers to investigate if group sequential methods can be effectively used in their clinical studies.
