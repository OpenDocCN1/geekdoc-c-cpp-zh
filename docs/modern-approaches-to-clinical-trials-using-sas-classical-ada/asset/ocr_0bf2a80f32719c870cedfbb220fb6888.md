As described above, this analysis approach is to strike a balance between providing evidence on CV safety to reassure patients and excessive delay of novel therapies reaching the marketplace. An important point for any analysis strategy, one that utilizes sequential methodologies or not, is to maintain confidentiality of the CV results at the end of Stage 1 to minimize bias for the ongoing or additional trials needed to assess cardiovascular safety at the 1.3 margin. This includes the public, because knowledge of the point estimates and confidence intervals may affect the enrollment of current or future trials, but also includes sponsor personnel, since trial integrity may be compromised. It is recommended that blinded sponsor personnel be independent of the study team, and limited to those individuals needed to initiate activities. Additional details on the confidentiality of interim results of CVOT trials for T2DM are available in [27, 28].
