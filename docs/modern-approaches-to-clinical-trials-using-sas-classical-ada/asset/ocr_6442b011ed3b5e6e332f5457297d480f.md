The promising zone approach gives the additional flexibility so that the sample size is increased only if the results are promising at an interim analysis. This example is just an illustration on how to evaluate the promising zone approach. In practice, extensive simulations aligned to the project needs should be conducted to ensure desirable properties. For example, the minimum and maximum conditional power for the promising zone can be altered or an additional futility boundary can be added.
