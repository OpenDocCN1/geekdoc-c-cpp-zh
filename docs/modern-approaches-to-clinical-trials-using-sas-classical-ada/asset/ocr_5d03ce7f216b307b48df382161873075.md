Once the posterior distributions are computed, mcmc computes the point estimates of the parameters, along with the standard deviation and percentiles. By default, only the quantiles are provided but one can specify percentiles using the stats(percent =) option in the proc mcmc procedure also generates graphs of the posterior samples such as generated by default with ods deviance information criterion (dic), which could be used as a model diagnostic tool when multiple chains are used (not shown), and highest posterior density (HPD) intervals (not shown). The mcmc trace plots, auto-correlation plots, and kernel density plots. These are generated on as shown in Figure 8.1 Diagnostic Plots for e0, Figure 8.2 Diagnostic Plots for Emax, Figure 8.3 Diagnostic Plots for tau, Figure 8.4 Diagnostic Plots for Ned50, and Figure 8.5 Diagnostic Plots for var_subjid. The trace and autocorrelation plots provide a good visualization of the convergence. The trace plots for the parameters e0, emax ed50 indicate that the Markov chain has stabilized and that the chain has good mixing properties. The auto-correlation plot shows a low degree of auto-correlation among posterior samples for the three parameters (potentially due to the thinning of the posterior samples).
