The primary endpoint was locally assessed progression-free survival (PFS), defined as time from randomization to first radiologically documented tumor progression or death according to Response Evaluation Criteria In Solid Tumors (RECIST, v1.0). It could be assumed that the median PFS for the placebo group was 6 months, while for the treatment group was 8.25 months, about 37.5% improvement. If the trial was designed to have a total follow-up time of 2 years and an accrual time of 1 year, under the assumption of exponential survival curve and specification of log-rank test and equal allocation, the total sample size under a fixed-design can be approximated to $N=508$ achieving a power of 90%. The type I error was set at 0.025. Example Code 2.3 Sample Size for mTOR Inhibitor Trial calculates this sample size. The results of this everolimus combination therapy trial are displayed in Results of Trastuzumab-Resistant Breast Cancer Trial for Everolimus on PFS.

Example 2.3 Sample Size for mTOR Inhibitor Trial

```
proc power;

totaltime=24
power=0.9
alpha=0.025 sides=1
total=.;
```
