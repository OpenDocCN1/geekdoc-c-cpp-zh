Example Code 9.7 Randomized Play-the-Winner Rule carries out the randomized play-the-winner rule to determine the next treatment assignment. For example, suppose that 20 patients have been assigned to treatment A and 13 assigned to treatment B. Among the treated patients, 14 from treatment A and 5 from treatment B have a favorable response. We start with an empty urn (i.e., $m=0$), and set $\alpha=1$ and $eta=0$. The `RPWrandomize` macro can be utilized to determine the treatment group for the next patient.
