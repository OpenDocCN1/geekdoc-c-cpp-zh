Since it is within the stopping boundaries, the trial will continue to the second stage with the accrual of an additional 15 patients to a total of 32 according to the original design plan. It is important to note that the boundary values for the subsequent stages have been updated in boundaryinfo2 based on the same information suggested by infoday=one. After collecting the data on the second cohort, the second interim analysis produces a new MLE estimate of 0.4805. When compared to the MLE boundary values (0.0227, 0.3964), it is determined that the MLE estimate has crossed the upper boundary; therefore, the null hypothesis will be rejected and no more patients will need to be recruited in this trial. This psoriasis trial is declared a success, meaning that the investigative anti-TNF product is superior to the placebo in improving the hfPAG outcome.
