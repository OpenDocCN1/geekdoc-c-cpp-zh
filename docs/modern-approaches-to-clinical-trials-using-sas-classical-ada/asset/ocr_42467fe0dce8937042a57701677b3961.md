In Example Code 2.8 Efficacy and Utility Boundaries for the Psoriasis Trial, we designed a sequential trial with the objective of comparing the anti-TNF treatment to a placebo. The clinical outcome is a binary response. In the design statement, this modified design has four stages with power at 86% (beta=0.14) as in the fixed-sample design. Additionally, we want conduct a trial with options for early rejection or acceptance of the null hypothesis; therefore the option stop is used. However, the monitoring approach for efficacy is based on the Pocock boundary while for utility is the OBF boundary: method(alpha)=poc and method(beta)=ocf, respectively. In the samplesize statement, we specify the twosamplefreq option for testing two proportions with a null proportion of 7% and an alternative proportion at 40%. The test available in proc seqdesign is the binomial test (test=prop), which relies on normal approximation to the binomial distribution.
