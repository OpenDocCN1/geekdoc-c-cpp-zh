The ed50 prior for 100 patients per group is flat between 0 and 20; this upper bound is reflected in the posterior distribution of ed50. As a sensitivity analysis, the same priors were run on the same simulated data but only changing the ed50 prior. The ed50 prior was set to the uniform (0, 81) indicating the complete dosing range instead of the uniform (0, 20), all other priors remaining the same. With the new prior, the mean (standard deviation) of the posterior distribution for ed50 was 53.24 (8.81) with a 95% credible interval (37.96, 68.82). This further elucidates the fact that priors may have influence on the results and careful discussion, as well as understanding that the clinical endpoint the biological justification around the prior is critical.
