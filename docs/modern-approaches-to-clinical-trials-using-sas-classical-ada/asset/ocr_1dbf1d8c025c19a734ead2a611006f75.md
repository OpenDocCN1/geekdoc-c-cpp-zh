In trials that use time-to-event outcomes such as progression-free survival (PFS) or overall survival (OS), the maximum number of events $D(c_j)$ is important to the determination the study’s statistical power, instead of the maximum sample sizes $n_j$ as in trials using normal or binomial outcome.
