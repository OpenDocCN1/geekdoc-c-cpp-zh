As a result, in July 2009, the FDA approved cetuximab for treatment of *KRAS*-wild type colon cancer. Similarly, in a prospectively specified analysis of data from a previous randomized phase III trial of panitumumab versus best supportive care [5], the hazard ratio for progression-free survival (PFS) comparing panitumumab vs with best supportive care in the *KRAS* wild type and mutant subgroups was 0.45 and 0.99, respectively, with a statistically significant treatment by *KRAS* status interaction (p<0.0001). Given the lack of activity in the *KRAS*-mutant group, the label was changed to include wild-type patients only in 2009.
