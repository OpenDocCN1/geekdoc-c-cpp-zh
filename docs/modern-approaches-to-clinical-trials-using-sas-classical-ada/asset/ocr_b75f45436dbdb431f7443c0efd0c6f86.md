4. cohortsize is the cohort size.

5. tau is the length of the follow-up period for full evaluation of toxicity.

6. a is the length between the recruitment times of two adjacent cohorts.

7. tostop is the threshold probability to early terminate the trial if the lowest dose level is too toxic.

8. ntrial is the number of trial replications.

As an example, Example Code 5.6 Simulation of fCRM and TITE-CRM Designs generates the results for scenario 2 in Table 5.9; the output variables are the same as those in the %sim.
