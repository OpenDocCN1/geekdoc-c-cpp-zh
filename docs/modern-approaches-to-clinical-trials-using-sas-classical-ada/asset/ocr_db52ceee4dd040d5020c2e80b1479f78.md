Typically, responses for clinical trials in the inflammation and immunology area are binary in nature, i.e. a success or failure of the outcome.
Although there are cinuous scales that are available in these disease areas, the clinicians prefer to define success on the basis of whether a subject has responded or remitted. Apart from a model for continuous endpoint we also provide an example for a Bayesian model for binary data.
