# 10.6.3 Misclassified Response

RAR requires access to accumulating primary outcome data from patients in the trial. These data must be of excellent quality to enable accurate estimation and adaptation throughout the trial. In some trials, binary responses may be misclassified and this may introduce bias into the design. Li and Wang in [60] considered a problem of finding optimal allocation for RAR designs where responses are subject to misclassification. While their approach can be useful for mitigating certain biases, efforts should always be made to ensure a high quality assessment of data in RAR trials.
