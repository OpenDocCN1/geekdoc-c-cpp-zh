# 4.3.1 Model for Future Survival Data

In this section, we discuss the meta-regression survival model under the general survival meta-experimental design setting discussed in 4.2 Meta Design Setting. Let $y_{jk}$ denote the subject-level time-to-event (failure time) and $v_{jk}$ denote the right censoring indicator such that $v_{jk}=1$ if $y_{jk}$ is a failure time and $v_{jk}=0$ if $y_{jk}$ is right censored, the th subject in the $j$-th arm and the $k$-th trial for $i=1, \dots, n_{jk}, j=1,2, \dots, K$. We write $\hat{y}_{jk}=\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{n_{jk}} y_{ijk}}{\sum_{i=1}^{n_{jk}} v_{ijk}}$ which denotes the total subject year duration and the total number of subjects, respectively, for $j=1,2, \dots, K$. We let $x_{jk}=0$ (control) or 1 for $j=2$ (experimental). Then, we have the observed aggregate meta-survival data denoted by $D_K=\{y_{jk}, v_{jk}, x_{jk}\}, j=1,2, \dots, K$.

We further assume that, individual-level failure time follows an exponential distribution with mean $1/\lambda_{jk}$, $Y_{jk}\sim Exp(\lambda_{jk})$, where $\lambda_{jk}>0$ is the hazard rate. The density function of $Y_{jk}|v_{jk}$ is given by

$f(y_{jk}|v_{jk}|\lambda_{jk})=\lambda_{jk}e^{-\lambda_{jk}y_{jk}}$. (4.2)
