The assay is not 100% perfect to dichotomize patients to biomarker positive and negative groups, the relative efficiency formulas can be generalized to account for errors, and such errors apply only to the enrichment design, in a negative way. Let λsens denote the sensitivity of the assay for diagnosing marker+ patients and let λspec denote the corresponding specificity, then the treatment effect for the enrichment study is dictated by

> $\λsens(1-γ) 
(1-λspec)γ + λsens(1-γ).

If 2NT denotes the number of patients randomized to the targeted trial, then (1-λspec)γ + λsens(1-γ) is the expected number of screened patients to enroll 2NT randomized patients.
