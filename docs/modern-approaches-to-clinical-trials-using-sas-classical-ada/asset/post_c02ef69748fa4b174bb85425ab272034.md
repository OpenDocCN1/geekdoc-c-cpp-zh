We discuss how to calculate the type I error and power discussed in [6]. Let $n_0$ and $a_0$ denote prespecified Bayesian credible level and discounting parameter for the historical meta-experimental survival data, respectively. We also let $\{\gamma^{(m)}, \ m=1, \dots, NMCS\}$ denote a Gibbs sample of $\gamma_1$ from the posterior distribution $\pi(\gamma, \theta_{D_K}, D_0, a_0)$, where $NMCS$ is the Gibbs sample size. ```

```
