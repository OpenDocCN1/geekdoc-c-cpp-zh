The left curve plotted in Figure 6.1 Possible Efficacy and Toxicity Dose-Response Curves can be viewed as an efficacy–response curve; that, the response (y-axis) represents some measure of the drug efficacy. In addition to efficacy response, there are also toxicity dose–response curves. We will assume, as shown in Figure 6.1, when the dose increases, both efficacy and toxicity increase. Based on these curves, the maximum tolerated dose (MTD) can be defined. The MTD is the maximal dose acceptably tolerated by a particular population.

With certain products, the efficacy curve and the toxicity curve are widely separated. When this is the case, there is a wide range of doses for patients to take. In other cases, the two curves may be very close to each other and physicians have to dose patients very carefully so while benefiting from the efficacy, patients do not have to be exposed to potential toxicity from the drug.
