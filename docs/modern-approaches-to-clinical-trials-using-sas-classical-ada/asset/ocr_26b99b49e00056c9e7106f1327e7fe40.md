In Sections 10.3.2, 10.3.3, and 10.3.4 we discuss several approaches to facilitate optimal RAR procedures. All these procedures possess the following important asymptotic properties:

+   1. The maximum likelihood estimators $\hat{\varrho}_{Em}$ and $\hat{\varrho}_{Cm}$ in (10.6) are strongly consistent for $\hat{ho}_E$ and $\hat{ho}_C$; they are asymptotically uncorrelated and have an asymptotically bivariate normal distribution.

+   2. The sample treatment allocation proportion $N_E(m)/m$ is strongly consistent for the target allocation $ho$ and has an asymptotically normal distribution with a known structure for the asymptotic variance.

Therefore, standard asymptotic estimators and tests can be used for statistical inference following optimal RAR procedures [35,36].
