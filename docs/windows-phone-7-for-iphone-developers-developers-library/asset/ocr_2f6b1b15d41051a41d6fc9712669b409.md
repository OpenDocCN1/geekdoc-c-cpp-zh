This chapter covers the increasingly important topic of security. Users of smartphone applications, especially those who use their smartphones for work, both expect and demand that the phones and the applications running on them are secure.

Unfortunately consumers and developers often have different definitions of security. To add to the confusion, device manufacturers have certain expectations about what developers will do to secure their applications, and developers have expectations about what device manufacturers will do to maintain the security of the device, the platform, and the operating system.

In this chapter I attempt to clear away some of the ambiguity surrounding the definition of secure applications, devices, and application platforms. In addition, I'll show you some of the built-in security features of the Windows Phone 7 platform and take you through some code samples for encrypting your application's data for secure storage.
