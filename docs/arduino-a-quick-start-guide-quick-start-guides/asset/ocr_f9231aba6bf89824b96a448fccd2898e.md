# 1.8 What If It Doesn't Work?

Don't panic! If it doesn't work, you've probably attached the LED in the wrong way. When assembling an electronics project, parts fall into two categories: those you can mount any way you like and those that need a special direction. An LED has two connectors: an anode (positive) and a cathode (negative). It's easy to mix them up, and my science teacher taught me the following mnemonic: the cathode is necative. It's also easy to remember what the negative connector of an LED is: it is shorter, minus, less than. If you are a more positive person, then think of the anode as being bigger plus more. You can alternatively identify a LED's connectors using its case. On the negative side the case is flat, while it's round on the positive side.
