> ## Scientific Applications Using Wii Equipment
> 
> Because of the Wii’s accuracy and cheap price, many scientists use Wii equipment for other things than gaming. Some hydrologists use it for measuring evaporation on a body of water.* Usually, you’d need equipment costing more than $500 to do that.
> 
> Some doctors at the University of Melbourne had a closer look at the Wii Balance Board, because they were looking for a cheap device to help stroke victims recover.† They’ve published a scientific paper verifying that the board’s data is clinically comparable to that of a lab-grade “force platform” for a tiny fraction of the costs.
