![](bbox=[0.1, 0.05, 0.9, 0.22])

As soon as the solder starts to flow, you're safer, because the solder distributes heat automatically. Feed some more solder (not too much!) until you have a nice, shiny solder joint. The whole process shouldn't take more than two to three seconds. When you're done, remove the iron tip quickly, and give the joint a few seconds to cool down.
