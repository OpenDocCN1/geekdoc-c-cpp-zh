it, you’re surrounded by stepper motors. You hear them when your printer, scanner, or disk drive is at work. Controlling stepper motors isn’t rocket science, but it is a bit more complicated than controlling DC motors and servos.

Servo motors are the most popular among hobbyists, because they are a good compromise between DC motors and steppers. They’re affordable, reliable, and easy to control. You can move standard servos only in a range of 180 degrees, but that’s sufficient for many applications. With continuous rotation servos, you can increase the range to 360 degrees, but you lose the ease of control.

In the next section, you’ll learn how easy it is to control standard servo motors with an Arduino.
