## About the Authors

Hi, my name is Bill Sempf, and I have been working in software development for over 30 years. I started with computers long before the internet existed. I am a graduate of Ohio State University with a bachelor’s degree in computer science, a Microsoft Professional, Certified Internet Business Strategist, and Certified Internet Webmaster. My company is Point of Innovative Technology (usually called POINT), and you can reach me at bill@point.net.

Chuck Sphar has been a technical writer for over six years, escaping Microsoft documentation camps. You can reach Chuck for praise or minor nits at cucksphar.com. His C# web page is at csharp.info.

Stephen R. Davis, who goes by Randy, lives with his wife and son near Dallas, Texas.
