# Managing Files

Setting up an ASP .NET project is not trivial, and there are a lot of wrong ways to do it. I can't tell you the right way — it depends on your circumstances. What I want to do is give you some of the best practices that will help you form your own path. The idea is to know how the tools works, in order to help you do your work.
