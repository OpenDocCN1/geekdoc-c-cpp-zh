**hypergraph:** Edges are a set of vertices(nodes).

**metric measure:** Adheres to the triangle inequality.

**multigraph:** May include multiple edges between nodes, or a node may have an edge to itself.

**subgraph:** A portion(a proper subset) of a graph.
