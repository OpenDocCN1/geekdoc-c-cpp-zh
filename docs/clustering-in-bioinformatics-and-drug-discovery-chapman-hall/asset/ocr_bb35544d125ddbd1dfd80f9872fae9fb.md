# ### 1.6 Glossary

**algorithm:** A set of steps in sequence or in parallel to calculate some result.

**asymptotic order notation:** The notation for the order of computational complexity of algorithms.

**bioinformatics:** Biological information and analysis.

**cheminformatics:** Chemical information systems and analysis.

**supervised learning:** Classes are known.

**unsupervised learning:** Classes are unknown.

+   2. Mechanical clustering. As late as the 1980s, determining the classification of a specific wood sample was done with a large, square, stack of cards known as a dichotomous key. All cards have a set of labeled holes along the edge of each card. The labels denoted specific properties of wood: gross properties, microscopic properties, etc. Each card represents a specific species and the property labeled holes are either open to the edge if the property for that species is not present, and closed if that property is present. A long pin or rod that fits through the holes is used to determine each property of a new species under consideration. The pin is placed through all of the holes of one property and the cards are drawn out with the pin. The process is repeated for each property on the successive smaller and smaller subset of cards.
