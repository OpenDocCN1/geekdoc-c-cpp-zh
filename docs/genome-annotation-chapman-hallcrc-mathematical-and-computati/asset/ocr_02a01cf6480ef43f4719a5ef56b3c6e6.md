Although these are typically genetically mobile elements, they may also be from paralogous genes. A tandem array is a contiguous series of three or more copies of a motif within a chosen distance threshold. A pseudo-satellite has copies, but the copies do not meet the distance threshold for tandem repeats. Short repeats of 1 to 6 base motifs (also known as microsatellites) are most sensitively detected by dedicated software such as SciRoCo (Kofler et al., 2007).

The overall diversity of repetitive elements is reflected in the fact that of 2011, there are 75 “repeat” related terms in the Sequence Ontology (Eilbek et al., 2005). The TEclass can help annotate the de novo repeat analyses at the top level of DNA transposon/SINE/LINE/LTR. REPBASE provides a rough classification system for known repeats as well.
