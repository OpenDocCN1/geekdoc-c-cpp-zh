# 9.2.2 Ensembl Genome Browser

The Ensembl Genome Browser (Flicek et al., 2011) is the main visualization tool for the Ensembl project, which generates databases for vertebrates and other eukaryotic genomes. As of Release 59 (August 2010), the Ensembl database holds 56 species, most of which are vertebrates.

The Ensembl Browser's individual pages contain statistical information, including the known protein-coding genes, protein-coding genes, pseudogenes, microRNA (miRNA) genes, rRNA, small nuclear RNA (snRNA), small molecular RNA (snoRNA), and other RNA genes as well as single nucleotide polymorphisms (SNPs). RNA gene annotation in Ensembl is accomplished by making use of partial cDNA or EST and protein-coding genes found in other organisms.
