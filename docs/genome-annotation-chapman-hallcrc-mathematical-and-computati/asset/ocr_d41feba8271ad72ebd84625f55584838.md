FIGURE 10.10 Seahawk's conditional service evaluation via a search/filter widget. (a) Filter conditions in Seahawk GUI and (b) corresponding filtering processes in a running Taverna workflow. (See color insert.)

Filters are tied to specific documents in Seahawk. Different conditions can be given at each browsing step. “else” conditions are selection inversions, based on toggling the keyword “if” to “unless” in the filter widget (Figure 10.10a, bottom left). This fulfills conditionals of type 1 listed above. In terms of the equivalent workflow, a filter processor with two output ports would fork the workflow (Figure 10.1b). In the demonstration, forking is accomplished by calling a service in a new tab (Shift+Click).
