# Contributor

Stephen Strain was born in Szeged, Hungary. He studied visual arts and music in Debrecen, Szeged, and Budapest. In 1981, he emigrated to Calgary, Alberta, Canada, where he lives and works as an artist. He is known for his interpretation of flamenco music (www.flamencelegante.com) and as a painter.

His painting Magpie is used as the cover design.
