In some cases, alternate splice forms of genes create so-called nonsense transcripts, which are never translated into protein. The ultimate validation of the protein expression of splice variants can be achieved through green fluorescent protein (GFP) fusions, mass spectrometry, and various other means. The fact that some variants are only expressed under very specific conditions can make this confirmation step all the more difficult.
