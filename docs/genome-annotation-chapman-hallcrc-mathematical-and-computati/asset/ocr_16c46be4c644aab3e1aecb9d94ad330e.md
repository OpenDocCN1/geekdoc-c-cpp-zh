As new sequencing technologies provide increased sequencing at a lower cost than before, existing read alignment and assembly viewers were improved to cope with the challenge of higher volumes of NGS reads. Consed has been expanded and Gap5 (Bonfield and Whittham, 2010) has been developed to deal with substantially larger data sets. On the other hand, several new visualization tools are also being developed specifically for the purpose of handling NGS data.
