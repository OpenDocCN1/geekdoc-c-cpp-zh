# Friction (Fr)

Friction is a force that acts between two surfaces that meet. When forces are applied to make the two surfaces slide over each other, there will be an opposing frictional force acting along (parallel to) the surfaces. It is a force that opposes the sliding of the surfaces. The surfaces may slide (then we call the frictional force dynamic) or they may not (then we call the frictional force static) . [15]
