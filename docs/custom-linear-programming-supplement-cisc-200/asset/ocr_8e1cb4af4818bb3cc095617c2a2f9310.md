# FIGURE 7.10 OPTIMAL SOLUTION FOR THE RMC PROBLEM

![](bbox=[0.31, 0.135, 0.79, 0.465])

Depending on the accuracy of your graph, you may or may not be able to determine the exact optimal values of F and S directly from the graph. However, refer to Figure 7.6 and note that the optimal solution point for the RMC example is at the intersection of the material 1 and material 3 constraint lines. That is, the optimal solution is on both the material 1 constraint line,

0.4F + 0.5S = 20 (7.6)

and the material 3 constraint line,

0.6F + 0.3S = 21 (7.7)

Thus, the values of the decision variables F and S must satisfy both equations (7.6) and (7.7) simultaneously. Using (7.6) and solving for F gives

0.4F = 20 - 0.5S

or

F = 50 - 1.25S (7.8)

Substituting this expression for F into equation (7.7) and solving for S yields

0.6(50 - 1.25S) + 0.3S = 21
30 - 0.75S + 0.3S = 21
-0.45S = -9
S = 20
