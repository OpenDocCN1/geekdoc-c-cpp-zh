# FIGURE 9-9 Context switching.

Obviously, the programmer must call the yield routine frequently, or else system response time may suffer. At a minimum, yields must be inserted in any loop where a thread is waiting for some external condition, as in Figure 9-10. A yield may also be needed inside other loops that take a long time to complete (such as reading or writing a file), or distributed periodically throughout a lengthy computation.
