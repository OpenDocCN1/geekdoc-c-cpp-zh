We are going to keep only the middle 64 bits, so we can discard the 32-bit upper half of A_hi B_hi and the 32-bit lower half of A_lo B_lo as shown in Figure 3-11.
All that remains (to compute the signed product A_x B_x) is to conditionally subtract the lower halves of A_62..0 and B_62..0 from the upper half of the unsigned product A_x B_u (see Figure 3-11). Note that the only difference between A and A_62..0 and between B and B_62..0 is the most significant bit position; this is irrelevant since we used only their lower halves in the subtractions. Thus, to convert the unsigned product into a signed product, simply subtract the lower half of B from the upper half of the unsigned product A_x B_u if A is negative (A_63 = 1) and subtract the lower half of A if B is negative (B_63 = 1).6
