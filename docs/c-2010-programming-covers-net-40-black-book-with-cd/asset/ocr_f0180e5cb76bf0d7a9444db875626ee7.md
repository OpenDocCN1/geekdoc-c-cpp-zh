In Listing 20.1, we have entered the Starting MyService message and the Stopping MyService message by using the WriteEntry() method of the EventLog class in the OnStart() and OnStop() methods, respectively. The OnStart() and OnStop() methods specify the actions that you want to perform when the service starts and stops running, respectively. You can also specify actions when the service is paused or continued, or when you shut down the computer using the OnPause(), OnContinue(), and OnShutdown() methods, respectively.
