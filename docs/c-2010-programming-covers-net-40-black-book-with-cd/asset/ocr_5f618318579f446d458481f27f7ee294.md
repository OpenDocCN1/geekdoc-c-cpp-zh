In Listing 12.45, we are using a MediaElement control, named mediaElement1, to play the music. The mediaElement1 control is controlled by the button1, button2, and button3 controls. The button1 control plays the music, the button2 control stops the music, and the button3 control pauses the music. The mediaElement1 control is using the slider1 control for increasing or decreasing its volume, and the slider2 control for increasing or decreasing the speed ratio of its played music.
