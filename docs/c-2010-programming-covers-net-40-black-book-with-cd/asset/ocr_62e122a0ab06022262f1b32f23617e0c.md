# Case Study:
Sports Website

# Background

The MySports company is one of the leading marketers of sports-related goods and products. It offers a wide range of good quality products and has established a strong presence in the market against various competitors. Apart from the apparels, footwear, and equipments of popular sports, such as cricket, tennis, and golf, this company also deals in accessories, such as bags, caps, sports eyewear, and swimming costumes. The increasing popularity and business of the MySports company has led to a growing number of clients. To cater to the demands of its customers more quickly and efficiently, the company decided to automate its account-keeping system.

# Problems with the Existing System

In the existing system, all the records of the company, such as list of products ordered and received, customers and their orders, price of various products, and delivery dates of the products are handled manually. This is a very cumbersome and error prone process and often leads to the following problems:

+   - Misplacing of purchase order, due to which products are not received from dealers on time
+   - Delay in maintaining records which in turn leads to the delay in fulfilling orders
+   - Errors in calculating price of the received or sent items, leading to a loss to the company

# Envisioned System

The management of the MySports company hired some software developers and discussed its problems. The software developers recommended that the company should create a website using the ASP.NET 4.0 and C# 2010, as they provide great functionality with lesser code. It was recommended that the website of the company should provide all the information about the available products and their prices to the customers. Apart from it, the website should also maintain records of various transactions made by the company. This website would allow the customers to purchase the products online, which could be then delivered at their doorstep.
