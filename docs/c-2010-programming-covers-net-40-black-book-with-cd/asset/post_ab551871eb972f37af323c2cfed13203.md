## Adding Cameras

A 3-D scene that you are creating is actually a 2-D representation of a 3-D object. It looks different depending upon the viewer angle of view; therefore, you must specify the angle of view. To do this purpose, the Camera class is used in WPF to specify the point of view of a 3-D scene. There are three types of camera available in WPF that are implemented by using the MatrixCamera, OrthographicCamera and PerspectiveCamera classes. In this section, we discuss about the two most important and widely used camera classes in WPF:

- OrthographicCamera
- PerspectiveCamera

## OrthographicCamera

The OrthographicCamera class describes the points that are parallel to the angle of view, instead of the one whose sides meet in the point of Camera. You have learned about the OrthographicCamera class in detail in the In Depth section of this chapter. Let’s learn the usage of the OrthographicCamera class.
