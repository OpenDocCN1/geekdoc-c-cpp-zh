The term *single homed* (used in the one BGP exam topic) refers to a particular Internet edge design with a single link to one ISP, as shown in Figure 12-4. A single-homed design has a single link between the enterprise and an ISP. You would typically find a single-homed Internet edge design used when connecting an enterprise branch office to the Internet, or for a simple connection from a core site at the enterprise.
