Taking Figure G-3 a step further, imagine if R1 had learned only one route to subnet X, say the route learned from R2. R1 would use that route because it is the only route R1 knows for subnet X. However, having learned three routes to subnet X, R1 picks the route with the best (lowest) metric, in this case the two-hop route through next-hop Router R7.
