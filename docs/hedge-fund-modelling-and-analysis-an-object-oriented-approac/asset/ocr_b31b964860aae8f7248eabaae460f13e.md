An investable Hedge Fund Reference Framework (HFRF) is designed to establish which hedge fund investment processes and strategies will be represented in the investable index. The HFRF is further supported by a series of index calculation, maintenance rules and guidelines relating to segment diversification, eligibility, concentration, investment capacity, and segment weight allocation.
MCI Hedge Fund Composite Index (HFCI) is used as a proxy for the hedge fund universe. The HFCI is an equally weighted index that measures the performance of a diverse portfolio of hedge funds (AuM greater than $15m) across the range of available investment strategies. MSCI investment methodology.
A review of the investable hedge fund index is conducted on a quarterly basis where funds are added or deleted and adjustments made to the constituent weights for available investment capacity. Segment weights are also realigned to the HFRF and checked to ensure that they still adhere to the general index construction and maintenance principles.
