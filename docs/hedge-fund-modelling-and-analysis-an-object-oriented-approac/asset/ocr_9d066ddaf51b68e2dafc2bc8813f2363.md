Clearly then, hedge indices are subject to a greater lack of representativeness than traditional indices. Such a problem goes beyond insufficient classification of investment styles. Instead, it concerns the actual managers themselves have a great degree of freedom and choice at their disposal.

By early 2000, many index providers have launched a series of investment indices offering a low-cost investment opportunity to gain exposure to the hedge fund industry. An index is valuable when the investors are able to replicate the index by maintaining and a certain level of tracking error². Generally based on platforms of managed accounts³, this new generation of indices has been able to provide investors with improved liquidity and a low-cost of gaining access to the hedge fund world. In addition, the composition, construction methodology and management principles are overseen by an independent committee and disclosed to the public, allowing increased transparency. To create an investment index, the index selects a range of hedge funds and structured products or derivative instruments that deliver the performance of the investable index. To make this, each hedge fund agrees to accept investments on the terms given by the index provider. When investors buy an investment index the provider makes the investments in the underlying hedge funds, making an index similar in many ways to a fund of hedge fund (FoHF⁵ investment. In addition, some refer to indices as merely FoHFs in disguise or with additional constraints. However, by their very construction, hedge fund indices are unable to replicate the hedge fund universe. Such indices cannot, for example, represent an open funds universe, since they are not composed of funds belonging to this subset of the complete universe. They contain partially closed hedge funds i.e. funds that accept new investments from investors who have reserved capacity. Hedge funds generally do have capacity issues as strategies only work within certain limits of investment capital. Indeed, many hedge fund refuse further investment of capital into the fund after they have reached a maximum level of AuM and them as closed funds. As a result, it is very difficult for hedge fund indices to remain investable when the composite funds have closed their doors to new investors. Most index providers argue to be a truly representative index that acts as a gauge for hedge fund performance, both open and closed funds should be included in the hedge fund index.
