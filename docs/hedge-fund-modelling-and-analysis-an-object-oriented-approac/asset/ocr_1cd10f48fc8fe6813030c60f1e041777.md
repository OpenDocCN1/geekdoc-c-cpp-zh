# Arbitrary

Each hedge fund return is given an arbitrary weight $w$, within the average which can be changed over time. However, the total of all the arbitrary weights must always sum to 100%.

Suppose we have a number of hedge funds, $N$ in a group where the return on fund $i$ is denoted by $r_i$ and respective weight $w_i$, then a performance measure (or index) for the group can be determined as the weighted average of the individual hedge fund returns, such that:

$r_{index} = \sum_{i=1}^{N} w_i r_i$

The index family currently consists of 17 indices, including a range of geographical and strategy-specific hedge fund indices. The current family includes:

+   * The DJCS AllHedge Index, formerly the CSFB/Tremont Sector Invest Index, is a diversified investable index comprised of an aggregate of all 10 DJCS AllHedge Strategy Indices and asset-weighted based on the sector weights of the Broad Index. The AllHedge Index was launched in October 2007, and any performance of the index predating October 2007 is simulated from runs on the underlying AllHedge Strategy Indices seeks to represent the hedge fund universe and encompasses around 80 funds as of November 2013;
* The DJCS Blue Chip Hedge Fund Index, formerly the CSFB/Tremont Investable Hedge Fund Index, is an investable index made up of the 60 largest hedge funds from the 10 style-based sectors comprising the Broad Index;
* The DJCS LEA Hedge Fund Index is an emerging market asset-weighted composite index covering the regions of Latin America, Europe, Middle East, Africa, and Asia economies. The index was launched in April 2008.
