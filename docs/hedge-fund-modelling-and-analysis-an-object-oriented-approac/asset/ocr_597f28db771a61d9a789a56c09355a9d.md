The flagship fund, the GAI Global Hedge Fund Index, is designed to reflect the dynamic nature of the hedge universe and does not have a fixed set of constituent funds. Instead, GAI attempts to include as many funds as possible, excluding all FoHs, based on monthly return data. Funds are excluded on the basis of size, location or other factors but must have a minimum three-month track record. Currently in excess of 2000 hedge funds are used to calculate the GAI Global Hedge Fund Index at month-end. Each hedge fund is categorized by investment strategy based on the information provided by the fund manager and according to the GAI hedge fund strategy definitions. In 2004, four Strategies were introduced in the GAI Global Hedge Fund Index, Namely Market Neutral Group, Long/Short Equity Group, Directional Trading Group and Specialty Strategies Group. In January 2010, 10 regional indices were introduced as a result of an increasing number of funds focused exclusively on specific geographic regions. The indices were created in two sets i.e. Developed Markets and Emerging Markets with each set containing five indices, namely Composite, Global, Asia, Europe and Americas.
