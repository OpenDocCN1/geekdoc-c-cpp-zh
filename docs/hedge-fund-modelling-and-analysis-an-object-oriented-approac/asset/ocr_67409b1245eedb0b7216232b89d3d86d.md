The three main components that drive hedge fund performance can be identified as:

+   1. Traditional beta
2. Alternative beta and
3. Alpha.

Traditional returns are based on long-only investment strategies and have exposure to traditional market factors, such as equity and credit risk that act as a proxy.¹¹¹ for well-known benchmark indices e.g. the S&P 500 and Russell 2000 indices. Alternative beta involves other factors, such as currency carry and equity momentum, that can be captured using various investment strategies and proxies through systematic trading.¹¹² Alpha is attributable to the skill and expertise of the hedge fund manager and is often difficult to capture.
