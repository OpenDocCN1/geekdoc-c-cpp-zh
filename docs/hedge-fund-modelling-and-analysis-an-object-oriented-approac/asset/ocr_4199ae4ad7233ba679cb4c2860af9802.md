a kurtosis of three is known as a mesokurtic distribution¹¹ e.g. the normal distribution
(See Figure 4.13). A higher kurtosis usually indicates that the variability in the data
dis due to a few extreme variations from the mean, rather than many relatively small
diffrences. As with skew, kurtosis has no units.

Mathematically, the sample excess kurtosis, k is given by:

```
k = \left[ \frac{n(n+1)}{(n-1)(n-2)(n-3)} \right] \sum_{i=1}^{n} \left( \frac{x_i-\bar{x}}{s} \right)^4 - \frac{3(n-1)^2}{(n-2)(n-3)} = (4.15)
```
