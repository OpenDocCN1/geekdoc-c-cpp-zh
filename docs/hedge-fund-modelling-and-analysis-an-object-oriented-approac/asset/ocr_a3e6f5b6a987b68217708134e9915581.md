Once the factors and proxies have been determined it is necessary to ascertain the combination of exposure and weights that replicate the hedge fund strategies. Investors can gain market exposure to the factors identified by the analysis using a variety of commonly traded financial instruments, such as index funds, Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs),¹³ swaps, listed futures and option contracts.
