More formally, the sample covariance of $n$ observations of $k$ variables is the k-by-k matrix with entries given by:

> $$\Sigma_{jk} = \frac{1}{n-1} \sum_{i=1}^{n} (X_{ij} - ar{X}_j)(X_{ik} - ar{X}_k) \quad (4.18)$
