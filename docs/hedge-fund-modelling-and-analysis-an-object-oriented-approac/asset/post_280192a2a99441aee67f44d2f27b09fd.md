An investable Hedge Fund Reference Framework (HFRF) is designed to establish which hedge fund investment processes and strategies will be represented in the investable index. The HFRF is further supported by a series of index calculation, maintenance rules and guidelines relating to segment diversification, eligibility, concentration, investment capacity, and segment weight allocation.

MCI Hedge Fund Composite Index (HFCI) is used as a proxy for hedge fund universe. The HFCI is an equally weighted index that measures the performance of available strategies. The investable index is conducted on a quarterly basis where funds are added or deleted. The HFRF is further supported by a series of index calculation, maintenance rules and guidelines.
