You can always hover over a passage to delete that passage, edit the passage, play the game from that passage, or—if you click on the three dots—determine if the passage is the start of your game or adjust how the passage looks (regular, wide, tall, or big).

Along the bottom is a menu that you'll use while working on your game.

![](bbox=[0.095, 0.25375, 0.904, 0.29625])

The home icon will take you out of your game and back to the main page with the story menu. Next to that is the name of your story, and if you click on it, you'll see a list of options. We won't get into them now, but just know they are there!

Quick find gives a search option that helps you to easily find a word or phrase in your passages and replace text next to the quick find will let you find and replace in your game. The next three icons show a grid of 9, 4, or 1 passages, and will change the way your story is displayed in the grid space.

The test icon, which looks like a bug, will allow you to debug your game and gives some extra tools to help. You'll become very familiar with this tool later, after you've started working on your game. The play icon will launch your game and let you see what it looks and plays like for your players. And lastly, the add passage button will—you guessed it—drop a new passage into the grid.

Go ahead and explore the interface. Don't worry about doing any damage—we'll be wiping the slate clean before we start.
