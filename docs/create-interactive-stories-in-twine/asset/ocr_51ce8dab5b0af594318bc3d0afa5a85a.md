Interactive storytelling is the basis for any game, and Twine gives users the tools to make their own choose-your-own-path games. Starting with the basics of storytelling, moving to Parsely games, and finally exploring Twine, readers will learn the ins and outs of making fun as well as engaging story-based games. The hands-on activities in this remarkable resource are uniquely designed to teach teachers the basics of computational thinking, variables, and the Harlow programming language—all while having fun making a game online.
