# Making It Your Own

25–30 minutes

# Activity Overview

That was a lot of learning! Luckily for us, Twine is a very well-designed piece of software. That means you'll just keep getting better and better the more you use it! And believe me—the possibilities of Twine are only limited by your own imagination.

# Instructions

Now it's your turn. You should have a good foundation to work in Twine. Take what we learned so far and start working on your own masterpiece! Maybe keep working on the game we started, use a CYOP or Parsely design, or be bold and design a new game from scratch.

Don't worry if it's not perfect or if you make mistakes along the way. All the errors and mistakes are a part of the design process! Be sure to check and test as you add new parts or make changes—if you catch them early, you'll have less of a headache later on, trust me. And remember—have fun!
