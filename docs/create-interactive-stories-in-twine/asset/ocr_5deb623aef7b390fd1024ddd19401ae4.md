# Instructions

To get started, think about the main types of actions that players might try in interactive fiction games. These are as follows:

+   * Look
* Take
* Drop
* Put
* Examine
* Search
* Inventory
* Open
* Close
* Lock
* Unlock
* Ask
* Tell
* Say
* Give
* Show

Thinking about how players may act in your game will help you as you think of obstacles and the things you want them to do in your game. It will also help you think of important rules for what happens in your game. Remember, you don't have to think of every possible thing players might try to do in your game! But it definitely helps to think of and make rules for the ones that are important or fun.

Brainstorm action words and create rules for the locations you have already written up in your game. Add one or two new rules for each, thinking about how to incorporate these common actions verbs.
