+   If players told you to “save,” then they can start the game over at the point they saved by by telling you to “load.” They will have all the things they had at that point and you will just continue the story like they were back at that point. It's up to you to remember where they saved last, or you can ask the players to remind you where that was. What you shouldn't do, though, remind them to save and load. Let them meet a horrible end a few times. They will quickly figure out that they need to double-check themselves, and their frustration is so much fun to watch!

You are now a Parsely game master! You should feel comfortable starting to build interactive fiction games in both CYOP and the Parsely formats. These are great ways to get into interactive design: they don't require much beyond some time, effort, and imagination.
