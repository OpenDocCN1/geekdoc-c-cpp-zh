+   * Players will take turns telling the “computer” what they want to do.

+   * Commands should be simple, two- or three-word combinations of a single action. They aren’t allowed to combine actions in a single command (e.g., “open the egg and drink the yolk”).

+   * The “computer” will respond with what happened as a result of their commands.

+   * Players can give advice and help each other, because they are working together.

+   * Players are permitted to “save” and “restore,” because horrible things may befall.

With out of the way, it’s time to start the game! To kick it off, read the description and provide the exits for the very first location. Then, allow the first player to give a command. Look at the rules and let the player know what happens. The next player gets to give a command next. Keep going from player to player until the group makes its way through the game!

Some important tips and tricks to keep in mind as you run your game:

+   * If the players try something that isn’t listed in the rules, feel free to have fun making up a response. It could be outrageous, funny, add more detail to the story, or cause the game to end in a horrible way!

+   * Also, have in fun with words. If players don’t use the right words, express confusion. For example, if the players find a box and then one of them says to you, “Open it,” you can respond and say, “I don’t see an it.” Players must be specific—they see a box, and that’s what they can open. It may sound annoying, but it can make for some fun times!
