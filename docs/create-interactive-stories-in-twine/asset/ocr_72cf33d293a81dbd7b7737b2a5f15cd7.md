# # Spreading Out

+   - 10–15 minutes
- Idea sheet, pencil

# ## Activity Overview

Now that you have your starting spark written down on your idea sheet, it's time to spread out and think about the world where your game is going to take place.

Remember those three categories you thought about for your starting spark? Well, you still need to think about them. They’re important parts of the story behind your game!

# ## Instructions

Continuing to work on the idea sheet that you created in the first activity, write down at least one idea for each of the other two categories that were not your story spark. You can write more if you like—don’t let me stop you! And don’t worry about whether they’ll all work, just get them down on paper. In the end, there’s really no chance you use every single one of them in your first project. But capturing your ideas on paper will help your story take shape and keep all of your great ideas in one place!
