# New Passage Links

10–15 minutes

# Activity Overview

Your story, at some point, is going to have to move forward. You want to your players to have some choices and new passages to explore and extend their games. These are like the decision points when we were building our CYOP game. Each decision will lead to a new passage, with a new part of your story. And we need to create a link between the two passages that will take us from a choice in the first passage to one of the new passages.
Thankfully, Twine has a quick and easy way to do this! When you add a new link, Twine will automatically create a passage with the title of the link. So let's make a link to a new passage and create the passage at the same time.

# Instructions

Open up to edit your starting passage. Leave a blank line after the last line and then enter the following:

```
[[Approach the House]]n[[Go around Back]]
```

Once you have that, close the passage. You should see two new passages. Can you guess what they're titled? One will be “Approach the House” and one will be “Go around Back.” There should also be two arrows, each leading from your starting passage to one of the new passages you created.
