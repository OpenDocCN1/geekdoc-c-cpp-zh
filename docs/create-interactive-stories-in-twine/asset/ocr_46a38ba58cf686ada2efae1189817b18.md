# # One Step Forward

15–20 minutes Idea sheet, sticky notes, pencil

# ## Activity Overview

With your bookends and touchstones in place, now you can start building your story and game by slowly filling in the connections between the starting block, the touchstones, and the ending block. Where you start is up to you. You can begin from the starting block, moving forward in the story, or you can start from the ending block and move backward. In fact, you'll probably do a little bit of both!

# ## Instructions

For now, let's start at the beginning of the story. To do this, you need to start thinking of decision points and what could happen next in your story. What are some things the reader can do?

Decisions are any of the things that the players may decide to do in that moment. The results of that decision will move the story forward and have them choose a new page to move to. The decisions can be actions, such as pressing a button or retrieving an item. They can be inactions, like not speaking, or waiting. As you get comfortable with making stories, you can get creative with what types of decisions you present to your readers!

For your first CYOP story, think of two things you could give the readers the option of doing. One would move them forward, while the other would either lead nowhere or take them to trouble.
