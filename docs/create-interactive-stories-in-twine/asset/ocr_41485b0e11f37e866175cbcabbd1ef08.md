The key from the chest will unlock the door.

If the players try to open the door, they will find it is locked. If the players have the key from the chest, they can use the key to unlock the door.

You may have noticed that there are two fonts being used in these examples. The words that are italicized are specifically for the “computer,” to let it know what will happen. The words in regular font can be shared with the players.
Your rules can include what the players will discover if they examine items. For example: “If the players examine the mirror, they will find a letter hidden behind the glass.” You can describe what happens if the players do something: “If the players open the door, a wolf attacks them.” You can also say what happens if they don’t have or do something: “If the players don’t have the coin, the gypsy casts a spell on them.”
Don’t be afraid to allow horrible and unsightly ends to come to your players! Remember, they can save their game and restore to where they saved. Part of the fun is knowing that—sometimes—horrible things will happen.
