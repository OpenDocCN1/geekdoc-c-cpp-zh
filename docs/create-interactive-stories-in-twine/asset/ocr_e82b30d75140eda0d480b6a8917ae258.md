# GLOSSARY

**action words** In Parsely games, verbs that allow players to advance the story.

**branch** The part of an interactive fiction story where a decision point leads to two different story paths.

**choose your own path** A style of story where the reader makes certain decisions that affect the outcome of the tale.

**conditional statement** In coding, a statement that checks whether certain conditions are met to determine what operation it executes.

**decision points** Areas in an interactive fiction story where the reader has to choose one or more options that have different outcomes.

**inline** In programming, describing code on the same line or without any breaks.

**interactive fiction** The general term for stories and other media in which the audience has some form of interaction with the outcome.

**interface** The form of a system or software that presents information to a user.

**iterative** Referring to the process of repeating something to improve it.

**links** In Twine, the connections between different passages and the way they are connected.
