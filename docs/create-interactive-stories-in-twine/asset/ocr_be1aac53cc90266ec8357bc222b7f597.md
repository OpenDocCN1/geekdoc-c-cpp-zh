# Narrative Sheet: Aka, Writing? Not So Much!

+   - 20–25 minutes
- Idea sheet, paper, pencil

# Activity Overview

Now that you have work done on your map, let's switch gears and look at the other part of creating a Parsely game: the narrative sheet.
On the narrative sheet, you're going to be writing up information for each location on your map. The person running your game will look between the map and the write-ups to run the game for the players. The order of locations on your narrative sheet is not important, but you should try to keep them—as best you can—in the order that the players will make their way through the game!
