# # Instructions

Testing means you get to try your game out. So grab a few friends and give them a chance to play the game you've made so far. Don't worry about how well you run the game—that's not your focus right now. Instead, pay attention to where players get stuck or what seems confusing for them. When you're in the middle of designing your game, you know how things go together and what they need to do. But these things may not be so obvious to your players! They have to puzzle things out on their own, and sometimes, that may be too hard for them to figure out. It's okay if it takes them some time to make connections or get past obstacles—as long as they're having fun. But if it's taking a really long time for them to figure things out, then you may want to make some adjustments.
