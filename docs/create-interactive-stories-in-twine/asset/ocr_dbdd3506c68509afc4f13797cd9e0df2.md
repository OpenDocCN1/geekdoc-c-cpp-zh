+   * Click-append (click-append: "text a")[texttext b]
* Anywhere "text a" appears in the passage, it will be
clickable and it will have [text b] added immediately
after. It doesn't matter where you put this macro in your
passage, though, again, it's usually better for readability to
add it at the end.
* Example:
    * I hate the circus because I hate clowns. They just
freak out me. I know clowns see nice, but I just
don't know. They seem to be hiding something
darker. (click-append: "clowns")|and feet
    * This time, clicking on "clowns" will produce: "I hate
the circus because I hate clowns and feet. They
just freak out me. I know clowns and feet seem
nice, but I just don't know. They seem to be hiding
sothing darker. I'll never trust clowns and feet."

+   * Click-prepend (click-prepend: "text a")[text b]
* Anywhere "text a" appears in the passage, it will be
clickable and it will have [text b] added immediately
before.
* Example:
    * I hate the circus because I hate clowns. They just
freak out me. I know clowns see nice, but I just
don't know. They seem to be hiding something
darker. I'll never trust clowns.
(click-prepend: "clowns")|monkey ]
    * With this macro, clicking on "clowns" makes the
passage read: "I hate the circus because I hate
monkeymon clowns. They just freak out me. I know
monkey clowns seem nice, but I just don't know.
