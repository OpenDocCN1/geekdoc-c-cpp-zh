Slowly, you make your way into the tunnel. The flashlight is helping you find your way. The tunnel does not look quite as dirty as the cave you fell in. The floor is well worn, as if something has moved across it over and over again, keeping it clean and creating a few small grooves.
Soon, you come across a great many cobwebs crossing your path—they make it impossible to move forward.
You turn to head back, but before you do, you see a large, shadowy shape emerge from the cobwebs directly in front of you. Suddenly, you realize it's a giant spider—and it leaps at you!
By some unknown instinct, you pull out the rusted dagger and thrust it out toward the spider. It skitters away with a horrifying sound, but you can tell it won't be coming back. Once the sound fades, you cut your way through the cobwebs and continue down the tunnel.
Soon, you stumbled out of the cavern and hear the sounds of a rescue party—you're saved!

The end.
