a large grid space, with a single square in the center. That square is the start of your story! In Twine, it's called a passage. Soon, you'll be adding more passages, filling up the grid space with your awesome story.

![](bbox=[0.038, 0.219, 0.963, 0.574])

You can click and drag that passage anywhere in the grid space. As the page fills in, it sometimes helps to rearrange how the passages are laid out, so they make sense to you and make it easier to find what you're looking for.

# Instructions

Go ahead and try dragging the square around. You may have noticed that when you hover your mouse over the passage, some options pop up.
