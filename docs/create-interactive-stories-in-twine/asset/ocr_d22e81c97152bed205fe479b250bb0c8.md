# Touchstones

10–15 minutes Sticky notes, pencil

# Activity Overview

You now have a start and an end to your game, but things need to happen between those points. We wouldn't have much of a game (or a story) otherwise! What goes between the middle and the end is called the plot.

# Instructions

For this activity, think about the main events that you want to happen in your story. Look through the ideas you put down on your story sheet and think what things need to happen in your story. In my example, the players will need to find the magic words of power to defeat the wizard. To get those, they will need to trade a piece of gold to an old elf, and they find gold in a chest buried on an island. Other things will happen in the story, but these are the main events that *must* happen. All these ideas need to go on their own sticky notes and be placed on the story sheet in order, from the beginning toward the end.

Because these are on sticky notes, you can move them around, make room for new ones, or take them away as your story takes shape. That’s the beauty of this process! Each of these events will be touchstones your players get to experience as they work through the game.
