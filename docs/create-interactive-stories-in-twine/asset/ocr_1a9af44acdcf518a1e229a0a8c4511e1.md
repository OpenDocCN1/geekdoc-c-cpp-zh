If the players rush out to check out the singing unprepared ... well, let's just say their stories will end quicker than they expected. They will need to examine the room first and find the walking stick in the cottage if they want to defend themselves.

So just to the right of the starting block, I will make two new blocks, one above the other. For each block, I will draw an arrow from the starting block. The direction of the arrow lets me remember where the decision is coming from and the result leads to.

One block will be to look around the cottage; the other will be to investigate the singing. To help me remember at a glance, I put the gist of the action as a title at the top of the block, and then a few sentences about what happened for each. As you can see, if the players choose to examine the cottage, they will find the walking stick. And if they choose to run outside, they will suffer a terrible fate (oh no!).
