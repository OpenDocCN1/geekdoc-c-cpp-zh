# GOING FURTHER

You didn't think I was done with with you already, did you? I hope you had fun building in Twine. Here at the end, I wanted to give you a few tools to use when you're creating your awesome future games. These are more complex topics that can extend and enhance a player's game.

To learn more—beyond what you'll discover here—be sure to check out the Twine Wiki at https://twinery.org/wiki. Also, some of the best lessons can be learned by playing other Twine games! You can find a lot on the Twine website, or on the Interactive Fiction Database: http://ifdb.tads.org/

Every activity in this chapter will require a computer and Twine, unless otherwise noted.
