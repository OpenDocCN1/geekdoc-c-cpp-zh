# Location, Location, Location

+   - 10–15 minutes Idea sheet, paper, pencil

Though your map probably looks similar to the story sheet you were working on when designing your CYOP game, locations on this map are different.

Locations are the different places that the players in your game can move to, explore, and make decisions. If they are in a house, the locations could be the different rooms and hallways they are exploring. If they're at the bottom of a tree and can climb to the top, those are two locations.

It's important to note that locations must not always have interesting things happening in them. But they do need to have movement or action decisions that need to be made. If players need to choose a direction or make a decision, it will be a distinct location.

For example, imagine you have a house as a part of your story, with several rooms connected to a hallway. Whenever the players need to choose a direction to go, that needs to be a location so the players can make a choice.
