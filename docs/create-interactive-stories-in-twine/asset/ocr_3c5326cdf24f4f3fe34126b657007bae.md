They seem to be hiding something darker. I'll never trust monkey clowns.

Just to hammer this point home: it's best to place click macros at the bottom of your passage. If you place the macro on a line by itself before or in between text, Twine will create a blank line. And if you happen to have more than one, you'll be faced with multiple blank lines. To avoid this, it's best to put these at the bottom of your passage so the text that your players are reading is up at the top and not moved around or spaced oddly.
