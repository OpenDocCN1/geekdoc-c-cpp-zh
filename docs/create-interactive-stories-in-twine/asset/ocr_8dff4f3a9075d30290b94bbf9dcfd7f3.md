Even though you know, as the designer, how things need to unfold, the players do not and will often go in circles trying to figure things out. You have to be aware that you are creating a more open space that the players get to explore, and you have to to plan for that. This freedom and the need to figure out what needs to happen makes Parsely games really fun and enjoyable. So let's get started with making one!

Slowly, you make your way into the tunnel. You can't see anything after a few feet, you have to use your hands to feel the wall, and you have to be careful as you step so you don't trip.
After about five minutes of wandering in the dark, you decide that maybe this wasn't a very good idea, and you start to head back.
As you turn, you feel something tickle the back of your neck.
Before you even have a chance to run, you feel two small needle-sharp things press into your neck. And then ...

Darkness.

The end.
