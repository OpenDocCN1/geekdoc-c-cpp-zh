# Passages

10–15 minutes

Passages are the main building blocks of Twine. They are the containers that hold your story and the bits needed to make the game run. As you know by now, they are represented by the blocks on the grid. Passages are small chunks of your story. They can represent locations, they can represent conversations, and they can connect to each other by links, which often present themselves as choices to the players. They will also reveal themselves one at a time to the reader, so a passage is like a page in a book or a location in a Parsely game.

That is why these passages should feel familiar. The work you did mapping your story out with sticky notes in the CYOP game and the location write-ups for the Parsely game are very similar to what you will be putting in your passages!
