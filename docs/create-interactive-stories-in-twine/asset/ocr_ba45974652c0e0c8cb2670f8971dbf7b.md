Spacing note: Like the link macro, the spacing between your text and where you put the macro matters!

Case: Your case (meaning the capital and lowercase letters) must be the same in your passage writing and "text a" section for Twine to see the two as a match and make the text clickable. For Twine, "dog" and "Dog" are two different words. On the other hand, if the word is a part of a larger word, Twine will recognize it—so be careful. That means if you were replacing dog with cat, the word "dogged" would become "catged." That's not right! Unless you have a character named Catged. But that seems unlikely.

+   Click-replace (click-replace: "text a")[text b]

+   - Anywhere "text a" appears in the passage, it will be clickable. And every instance of "text a" will be replaced by [text b].
