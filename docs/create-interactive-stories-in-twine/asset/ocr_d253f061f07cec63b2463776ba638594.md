It will probably make a bit more sense if you can see an example, so let's create an else-if, else statement for the old woman in the kitchen. Let's make her the players one of three things, depending on their titles. If they are “The Balloon Slayer,” she will give them a dearly departed balloon named Bob. If they are “The Fearful,” they will get a yellow binky, because they're scared. Otherwise, they'll receive a big giant glitter L (for “The Lame”) to wear on their foreheads.

How are you going to do all that? Edit the Kitchen passage. After the woman calls the players by their titles, insert the following:

> “I have something for you.”

(if: $title is 'The Fearful')[The woman reaches into her bag and pulls out a yellow binky. “I know how scary the world can be.”](else-if: $title is 'The Balloon Slayer')[The woman reaches into her bag and pulls out a piece of ribbon with something pink on the end. She sniffles “His name was Bob.”](else:) [The woman reaches into her bag and pulls out a large glittery L. “It is for your forehead, dear. Next time, make a name for yourself.”]

The End!
