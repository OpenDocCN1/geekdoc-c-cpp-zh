# Filling It In: Redux

25–30 minutes Idea sheet, paper, pencil

# Activity Overview

By now, you should have a good foundation to work on your own to add the rest of your locations, obstacles, rules, and descriptions to your game. The next step is to work on filling in the rest of your game!

# Instructions

Go through and create write-ups for each of the locations on your game map. As you're working, don't be afraid to add new locations or make changes! Design is a work in progress and great, new ideas can come as you are building your game. Remember, the most important thing is to have fun!

# Iterate and Refine

25–30 minutes Idea sheet, paper, pencil

# Activity Overview

Game design is an iterative process. Whoa now, there's a pretty big word! Luckily, it means something you already understand. In general, iterative means you will come up with something, try it out, make changes, and try again. This means—for you—that it's time to playtest and refine!
