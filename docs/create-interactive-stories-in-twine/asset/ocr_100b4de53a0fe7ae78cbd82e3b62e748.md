Also, as you write up your sticky notes, although they will start on their own pages, they may continue across multiple pages depending on how detailed and descriptive you are, or how much action is happening. That's fine!

As you write up your story, try to keep the pages in order of how the sticky notes flow as best you you can. After the first part of your story, put the two decision results immediately after. This will make it easier to find the parts needed as we start to mix up and number our pages in the final activity!

# Assembling Your Game

+   - 20–25 minutes
- Idea sheet, pencil

This is the home stretch! If you can believe it, you're almost done with your very first interactive fiction story! The last step is mixing all of the pages you have created and numbering the pages so that readers can play through your game.

Right now, your game should be mostly in order, with the decision pages following each decision. Now, you need to get to numbering the pages so the players' decisions lead to the right pages!
