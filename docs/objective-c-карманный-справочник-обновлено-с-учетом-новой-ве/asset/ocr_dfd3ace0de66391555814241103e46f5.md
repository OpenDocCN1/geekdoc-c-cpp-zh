One of the historical problems with Objective-C++ is the difficulty in interoperability collection types. Objective-C collections are intended to store Objective-C objects, and therefore, are not usable when storing C++ structures. C++ collections are a bit more flexible. They are implemented as templates; therefore, in theory they can store any type.
