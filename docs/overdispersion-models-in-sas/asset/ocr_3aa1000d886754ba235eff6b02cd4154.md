Another common plot is the Quantile-Quantile (Q-Q) Plot, which is a plot of the observed values of the residuals versus the expected value of the order statistics from a random sample from the standard normal distribution. In either case, plotted points should be positioned essentially along a straight line. Departures from the straight line indicate skewness in the data. Diagnostic plots are also very useful in identifying possible outliers and influential observations in the data set. There are many excellent books available in this topic—we refer the reader to Montgomery, Peck, and Vining (2007) for a gentle introduction.
