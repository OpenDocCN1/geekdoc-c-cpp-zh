# 7.11 Generalized Linear Overdispersed Models for Multinomial Responses–the Births Study Revisited

As in the case of Multinomial distribution, both DM and RCM can also be used in conjunction with covariates to obtain GLMs for the multinomial vector of probabilities. We restrict ourselves to the two most commonly used link functions, cumulative logit and generalized logit. The reader may also recall our discussion from Section 4.8, where we considered Generalized Linear Models (GLOM) for binary responses with appropriate link functions for the probability of success and the overdispersion parameter. These concepts can be extended to the Dirichlet-multinomial and Random-clumped Multinomial distributions in a natural way. First of all, note that the overdispersion parameter is constrained to the zero-one interval if so. The typical link functions used to model the probability of success of a Binomial distribution may be implemented to model the overdispersion parameter. The discussions from Sections 4.8 and 4.9 regarding the overdispersion parameters therefore apply here as well.
