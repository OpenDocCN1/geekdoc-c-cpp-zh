The programs for computing a GOF test (5.2) are given in Program 5.1. Under the Binomial model several SAS procedures can provide the MLE of $\pi$, which turns out to be the ratio estimator $\hat{\pi} = \sum_{j=1}^{n} \frac{t_j}{m_j}$. For this example, we make use of the SURVEYMEANS procedure to compute $\hat{\pi}$. The reader should aware that the standard error provided by SURVEYMEANS this estimator $\hat{\pi}$, is unbiased regardless of the distributional assumptions of $t_j$. Under the overdispersion assumption, as seen in equation (4.5), the estimate of the variance provided by SURVEYMEANS is consistent.
