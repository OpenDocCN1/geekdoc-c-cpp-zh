In this book, we present examples from a variety of application areas rather than focusing on a single area, we believe, because of the high quality of overspersersion, as well as versatility of the methods presented here. Towards that end, there are 30 sets discussed throughout this book. Some examples are: taxoplasmosis in sampled subjects in 33 cities of El Salvador, ossification of the phalanges on rats to environmental agents, forest pollen columns, chromosomal aberrations in the atomic bomb explosion in Hiroshima, Sikel's and Ebert's memory, urinary tract infections in HIV, hospitalization in significant neighborhoods, seizure counts for epilepsy, shoulder pain after surgery, etc.
