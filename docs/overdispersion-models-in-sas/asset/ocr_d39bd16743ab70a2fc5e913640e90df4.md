The coefficients of kurtosis (4.25) and (4.26) of the two distributions become $\{1+6r(1-\pi)\}/m\pi(1-\pi)+3$ when $ho=0$, which is the coefficient of kurtosis of the Binomial distribution. For the degenerate case $ho=1$, the coefficient of kurtosis of the Beta-binomial and the Random-clumped Binomial distribution is $1/{\pi(1-\pi)\}$. To meaningfully compare the kurtosis of the Beta-binomial and Random-clumped Binomial distributions, we consider the case $\pi=0.5$, for which both distributions are symmetric. That is, the coefficient of skewness is zero. For $\pi=0.5$, the coefficients of kurtosis of the Beta-binomial and Random-clumped distributions become

```
Kurtosis(Beta-binomial when $\pi=0.5$) = \frac{(3m-2)+\{3m(m-1)+2\}\rho^2}{m[1+(m-1)\rho^2\^2}$ (4.27)
```

and

```
Kurtosis(Random-clumped when $\pi=0.5$) = \frac{(3m-2)+\{2(m-1)(3m-4)\rho^2+(m-1)(m-2)(m-4)\rho^4}{m[1+(m-1)\rho^2]\^2}$ (4.28)
```

It can be shown that for $\pi=0.5$, the coefficient of kurtosis of the Random-clumped Binomial is less than that of the Beta-binomial $ho<1$. The tails of any symmetric Beta-binomial distribution are heavier than that of the corresponding Random-clumped Binomial distribution.
