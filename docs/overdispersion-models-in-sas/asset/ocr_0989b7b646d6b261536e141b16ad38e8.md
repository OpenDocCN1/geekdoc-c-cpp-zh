The SGPLOT procedure is implemented for creating the final plots (SAS 9.2 or later is needed). Plots 2.4 and 2.5 provide the plots under discussion. The difference between the two plots might be subtle. The reader can see that the cumulative normal curve approaches its limit more rapidly than the cumulative logistic curve. The logistic curve fits better in this example, as can be seen from the plots.
