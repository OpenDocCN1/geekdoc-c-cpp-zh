Table 9.1 shows the GEE results corresponding to two working correlation structures along with the estimates obtained under the Beta-binomial and the Random-clumped Binomial distributions. GEE is similar to Quasi-likelihood in the sense that neither approach is based on a likelihood function. While Quasi-likelihood adjusts the standard errors by a scale, GEE standard errors are obtained from the sandwich estimator of variance-covariance matrix. It will be instructive to the reader to obtain the Quasi-likelihood estimates for these data sets using codes similar to those given in Chapter 3, and likelihood based models are substantially different from the GEE. Finally, it is important to note that while we can perform goodness-of-fit tests described in Chapter 5 for the likelihood based models, we cannot test the validity of GEE model in the same way.
