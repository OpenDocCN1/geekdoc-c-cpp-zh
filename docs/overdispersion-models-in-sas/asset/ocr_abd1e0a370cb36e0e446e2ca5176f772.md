Table 5.6 provides the MLEs under the Beta-binomial and Random-clumped Binomial models. All the terms in each are highly significant (P-value < 0.0001). We found (not reported here) that cubic of the beta estimates were not statistically significant. The corresponding P-values were 0.17 under the Beta-binomial model and 0.8 under the Random-clumped Binomial. This seems reasonable to assume that the probability of observing a chromosome aberration is a quadratic function (through the logistic mean) of the standardized radiation dose received, and the degree of overdispersion varies (via the logistic function) as a linear function of the standardized radiation dose received. Higher order combinations can be tried by simple modification in Program 5.4.
