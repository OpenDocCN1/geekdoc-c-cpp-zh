However, the estimated errors are larger than those under the Binomial model, reflecting the fact that the data exhibits overdispersion. To fit a GEE model with an exchangeable correlation structure, the binomial-type of responses need be expanded into strings of zeros and ones. The programming statements below show this conversion of the responses and the implementation of the GENMOD procedure. Selected results are shown in the continuation of Output 9.2. Note that the beta estimates are not identical to obtained under the GEE with the independent working correlation matrix. The estimate of the exchangeability parameter turned out to be 0.295.
