# End of Journey

We end this journey by recalling McCullagh and Nelder's comment “. . overdispersion [phenomenon] is the norm in practice and nominal dispersion the exception. The ubiquity of overdispersion in practice demands that every practising statistician be very familiar with it and be equipped with tools to model this significant departure from the assumption of independence. The variety of models available to explain overdispersion, its close connection to mixed models, and its numerous theoretically and computationally intriguing aspects make overdispersion a very fertile area of research. In the preface, we promised to bring a “sampler platter” of overdispersion topics to the reader. We hope we fulfilled our promise.
