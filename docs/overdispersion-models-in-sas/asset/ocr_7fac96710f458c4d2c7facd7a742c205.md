Comparing the limiting results for the two distributions by (8.3) and (8.4), interesting observations emerge. First of all, note that the expression of the limiting expressions in (8.4) is simple to compute, whereas the limiting expressions in (8.3) involve digamma and trigamma functions. Furthermore, it can be shown that the error of approximation for the FIM of the DM is of order $m^{-1}$, whereas the corresponding error of approximation for the RCM is of order m. Thus, we can conclude that while approximations will perform well for large m, the approximation will work much better for the RCM model than the DM model. These observations are corroborated in a large scale simulation study, the results of which are reported in Appendix 8.1.

The limiting expressions in equations (8.3) and (8.4) are simple to compare to the elements of the associated Fisher information matrices. So, for large m, one may consider using them as approximations in the Fisher scoring algorithm of equation (8.1). Using the approximations we recommend the following Two-stage procedure:
