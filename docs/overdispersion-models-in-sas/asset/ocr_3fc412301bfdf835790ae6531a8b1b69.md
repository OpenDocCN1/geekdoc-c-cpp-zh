There seems to be no clear-cut criteria for choosing between these two analyses (Probit versus Logit). They may be thought of as modeling the event triggered by a latent variable distributed as to Normal or Logistic distribution, respectively. Cox and Snell (1989, pp. 21-22) point out that for most purposes the Logistic and the Normal distributions agree closely, except when interest is in extreme values, where the probability of success is either very small or very close. The cumulative normal curve approaches its limit more rapidly than the cumulative logistic curve, indicating that the latter has longer than the former.
