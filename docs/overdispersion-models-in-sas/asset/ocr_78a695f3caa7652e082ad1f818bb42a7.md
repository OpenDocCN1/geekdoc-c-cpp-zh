For the Cadmium-Zinc example, which consists of trinomial responses, i.e., d=2, the variance-covariance matrix in equation (7.20) turns out to be (for simplicity we remove the subscript “j")

$Var(T) = m \begin{pmatrix} \phi_1(1-\pi_1) & -\phi_1(1-\pi_1) \ -\phi_1(1-\pi_1) & \phi_1\pi_1^2(1-\pi_1)^{-1} + \phi_2\pi_2(1-\pi_2)^{-1} \end{pmatrix}, \quad (7.26)$

which has an intuitive interpretation in terms of the variability among the components of the Multinomial vector **T**. The variance of the first component $T_1$ is adjusted by the factor $\phi_1$. Writing the second element of **T**

$T_2 = \hat{T}_2 + T_{2,1},$

where $\hat{T}_2$ is the regression of $T_2$ on $T_1$, and $T_{2,1}$ is the corresponding residual, the variance of $T_2$ is seen as the sum of the variances of two orthogonal pieces $\hat{T}_2$ and $T_{2,1}$.
