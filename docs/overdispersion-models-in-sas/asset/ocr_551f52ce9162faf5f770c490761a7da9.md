The results of the omnibus GOF test under the Beta-binomial and Random-clumped Binomial distributions are reported in Table 5.4. The expected frequencies were rounded to the second decimal for simplicity. The algorithm discussed earlier started with 18 intervals. It ended with 10 intervals around the Random-clumped Binomial model and 9 intervals we combined the last two under the Random-clumped Binomial model so that Chi-square tests had the same degrees of freedom. The P-values of the GOF test under the Beta-binomial and Random-clumped Binomial distributions are 0.0014 and 0.2205. These tests are based on six degrees of freedom (number of intervals minus one, minus the number of parameters being estimated). The Random-clumped Binomial distribution fits the data well, while the Beta-binomial does not. The P-values were also estimated by bootstrapping the data parametrically with 20,000 replications. They turned out to be 0.0012 and 0.2124, those obtained by using the Chi-square distribution with six degrees of freedom. The QQ-plots 5.1 are for the residuals obtained observed minus expected frequencies. It is clear from the plots that the fit for the Random-clumped Binomial distribution is better than the Beta-binomial distribution.
