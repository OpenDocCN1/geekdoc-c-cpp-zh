# 10.1 Generalities and Fundamental Concepts of GLMMs

In this chapter, we pick up the discussion of Generalized Linear Mixed Models (GLMM) again and provide a more comprehensive view of how overdispersion models interact with GLMM. Several advanced examples given here illustrate how likelihood-based overdispersion models presented in this book can augment the estimation methodology provided in SAS procedures GLMIX and GENMOD. The diagram below depicts one of the models discussed in this chapter. We will provide Binomial, Poisson, and Multinomial GLMMs as examples. These are conditional models. We will also include four examples of Generalized Linear Overdispersion Mixed Models (GLOMM). They correspond to the Binomial, Random-clumped Binomial, Zero-inflated Poisson, and Zero-inflated Negative models with random effects. By studying these models, we complete all the hierarchical models we promised to cover in this book (see Appendix 1.1 of Chapter 1). We first start with a discussion of few fundamental concepts.
