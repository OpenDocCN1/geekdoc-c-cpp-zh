# 4.1 Introduction

In this chapter we will discuss several overdispersion models using the proper likelihood
functions for binomial data. These models, applicable to binomial responses (i.e., number of
uccesses “T” out of “m trials”), are obtained by postulating different sets of assumptions
corresponding to different mechanisms of underlying overdispersion. One family of the models
is obtained by assuming that the probabilities of success of responses from one respondent to
another, according to a certain distribution with domain in the zero-one interval. The
unconditional distribution of T is then an overdispersed distribution with a likelihood function
that allows for the joint estimation of probability of success and the overdispersion parameter.
