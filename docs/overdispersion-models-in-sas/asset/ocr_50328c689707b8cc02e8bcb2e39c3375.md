# Appendix 6.1: Generation of Data under the Negative-binomial Distribution

To generate a random sample $Y_j, j=1,2,...,n. Y_j \sim \text{Negative-binomial}(\mu,\kappa)$ where the mean of the distribution is $\mu$ and the dispersion parameter $\kappa$ proceeds as follows:

+   - Define $\alpha$ and $eta = \kappa\mu$ as $\alpha = \frac{1}{\kappa}$ and $\beta = \kappa\mu$.
- Generate a sample $U_j, j=1,2,...,n$ such that $U_j \sim \text{Gamma}(\alpha, \beta)$.
- Then, for each $j, j=1,2,...,n$, generate $Y_j \sim \text{Poisson}(U_j)$.

Recall the mean and variance of a Negative-binomial distribution are, respectively, $\mu$ and $\mu + \kappa\mu^2$.
