Table 8.1 provides the maximum likelihood and the Two-stage results for Mosimann's example under both the DM and RCM models. The results under the Two-stage procedure are practically impossible to differentiate from the exact maximum likelihood estimates. Note that the last column is not the difference between the exact and Two-stage standard errors but is instead the per-ncent relative error. In this example, the standard errors under both methods are practically indistinguishable.
