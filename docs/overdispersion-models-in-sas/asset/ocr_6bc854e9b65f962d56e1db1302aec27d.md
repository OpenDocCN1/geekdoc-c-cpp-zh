Rao and Scott (1981) proposed a test of homogeneity where the usual Chi-square statistic is defeated (overdispersion) by an estimate of the design effect. In contingency tables the test of homogeneity is the same as that for test if there is any association between the row and column variables. This test is available in the SURVEYFREQ procedure. The programming statements in the continuation of Program 9.7 show how to request Rao and Scott's Chi-square test. Note that subjects are treated as Primary Sampling Units (clusters). Excerpts of output SURVEYFREQ are shown in the continuation of Output 9.7. The value of the naive Chi-square statistic is 20.2281. The estimate of the design effect is 1.765. Thus, the value of $20.2281/1.769=11.4313$. Under the null hypothesis, this statistic is asymptotically distributed as a Chi-square with two degrees of freedom. The corresponding P-value is 0.003. Thus, we conclude that the distributions of the two products are different.
