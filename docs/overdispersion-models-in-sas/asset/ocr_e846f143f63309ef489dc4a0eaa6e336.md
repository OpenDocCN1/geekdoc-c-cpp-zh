The case where the $T_{tp}$ are believed to be binomially distributed ignores completely the litter effect and assumes litters are inside. To explain possible differences in $\pi_{tp}$ among the treatment groups, we will consider the following logit

```
$\ln\left(\frac{\pi_{tp}}{1-\pi_{tp}}\right) = \beta_0+\beta_1I(t=1)+\beta_2I(p=1)+\beta_3I(t=1,p=1), \quad (4.34)
```

where $I(.)$ represents an indicator function. The link function in (4.34) provides estimates of the teratogenic effects of the substances being used in the experiment and possibly its synergistic effect. It is common with teratological data to have varying degrees of intra-litter correlation. This problem has been properly addressed by Kupper et al. (1986) and Williams (1988).
