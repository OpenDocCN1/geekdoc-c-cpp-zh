# Program 10.2: Fitting a Poisson GLMM—Pump Reliability Data of Unit 1 of the Farley Nuclear Plant

```
/*
Pump Reliability at a Pressured Water Reactor Nuclear Power Plant
Gaver and O'Muirchheartaigh (1987)
Draper (1996) */

data pump;
  pump = _n t;
  input group y;
  logstd = log(t) - 2.5469400;
  datalines;
  1 1 94.320
  1 5 62.880
  1 14 127.560
  1 19 31.440
  2 1 15.720
  2 3 3.240
  2 1 1.048
  2 2 1.048
  2 4 2.096
  2 22 10.480;
```

```
ods html;
title "Pump Reliability at a Pressured Water Reactor Nuclear Power Plant";
proc glimmix data=pump method=quad;
  class group;
  model y = group logstd*group / noint dist=poisson link=log
    solution ddfm=residual;
  random it / subject=pump;
  estimate "Difference Y-intercepts" group 1 -1;
  estimate "Difference Slopes" group 1 -1;
run;
ods html close;
```

In Analysis of Covariance, with response Y and covariate X, the "MODEL Y = X GROUP X*GROUP," provides a test of parallelism of the straight lines among the groups (equality of slopes). A re-parameterization of the previous ANCOVA model as "MODEL Y = X*GROUP / NOINT," provides unbiased estimates of both the y-intercepts and the slopes of the regression lines. The latter re-parameterization has been requested in Program 10.2 through GLIMMIX for the Poisson GLMM described in (10.13). Output 10.2 provides excerpts from the output of Program 10.2.
