The negative coefficients indicate an increased risk of ossification. We are modeling the probability of ossification. So, a negative coefficient indicates the chemical impedes ossification and is toxic. Thus, the effect of the chemicals is to impede ossification. The Binomial model fails to recognize the litter effect and provides, as expected, estimated standard errors smaller than they should be.
