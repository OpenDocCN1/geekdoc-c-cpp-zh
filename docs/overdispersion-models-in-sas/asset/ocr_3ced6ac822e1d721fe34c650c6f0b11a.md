Since the logarithmic function is the most commonly used link function with the Poisson distribution, we provide diagnostic plots under both the identity and logarithmic functions for comparison purposes. The programming statements are provided in Program 2.6.

# Program 2.6: Analysis of Number of Failures of Electronic Equipment under Two Operating Regimes

```
/*
Number of Failures Electronic Equipment under Two Operating Regimes
Jorgensen (1961), and Frome et al. (1971)*
data failures;
input x1 x2 y;
datalines;
33.3 25.3 15
52.2 14.4 9
64.7 30.2 14
137.0 20.5 24
125.9 97.6 27
116.3 53.6 27
131.7 55.6 23
85.0 87.3 18
91.9 47.8 22
;
ods html on;
ods graphics on;
title *** Fisher Scoring optimization technique up to iteration 10 ****;
title2 *** Identity Link ***';
proc glimmix data=failures scoring=10 plots=residualpanel;
model y = x1 x2 / nint dist=poisson link=identity covb s;
run;
title *** Fisher Scoring optimization technique up to iteration 10 ****;
title2 *** Logarithm Link ***';
proc glimmix data=failures scoring=10 plots=residualpanel;
model y = x1 x2 / nint dist=poisson link=log covb s;
run;
 graphics off;
ods html close;
```
