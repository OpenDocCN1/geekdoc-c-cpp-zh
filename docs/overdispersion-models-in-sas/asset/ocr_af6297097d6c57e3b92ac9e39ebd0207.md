$O_s = \sum_{j=1}^{n_s} I\left(\frac{t_j}{m_j} \in A_s\right) \quad (5.5)$

and

$E_s = \sum_{j=1}^{n_s} \text{Pr}(T_j = t) I\left(\frac{t}{m_j} \in A_s\right), \quad (5.6)$

where $I(.)$ represents an indicator function and $\text{Pr}(T=t)$ is evaluated by the maximum likelihood estimates of $\pi$ and $\rho$. Note that $\sum_{s=1} O_s = \sum_{s=1} E_s = n$. If the clusters are all of the same

size, it can be shown that the expression (5.6) for frequentist reduces to

$E = m \text{Pr}\left(\frac{T}{m} \in A_s\right)$ and that the Chi-square statistic will bicome the usual Pearson's GOF

statistic.
