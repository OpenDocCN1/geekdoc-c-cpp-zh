We also discussed four examples with generated data to cover the Beta-binomial, Random-clumped Binomial, Zero-inflated Poisson, and Zero-inflated Negative-binomial GLOMMs. Examples of a Poisson and a Negative-

binomial GLMM, and a Zero-inflated Poisson and a Poisson Hurdle GLOM, are all found in Chapter 15 of Littell et al. (2006). An example of a Generalized Poisson GLOM is discussed in the SAS documentation for the GLMMIX procedure. The Generalized Poisson can be motivated as a Poisson mixture and hence provides an alternative to the Negative-binomial distribution (see Joe and Zhu, 2005). Further, fitting a Dirichlet-multinomial or a Random-clumped Multinomial GLOM might pose complex computational issues. We expect to provide guidance on these Multinomial GLOMMs in a future edition.
