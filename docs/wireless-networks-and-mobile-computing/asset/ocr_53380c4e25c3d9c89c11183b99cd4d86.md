# 8.6.3  Energy-efficient Communication Using TFNS

We apply TFNS to communication systems to save energy during communication. Digital data in binary format are first converted to decimal form and the decimal number is encoded to the tri-digit Fibonaccian number using the appropriate conversion algorithm. If digital data are transmitted in this encoded form, i.e., TFNS-based format, the numbers of 1’s and 2’s in such data streams for large values of data are substantially reduced. The numbers of 0’s, 1’s and 2’s in such data streams are found to be in the ratio 44 to 38.2 to 17.8, respectively as against 50 to 50 for 0’s and 1’s in normal binary coding.

# 8.6.3.1  Implementation of TFNS Protocol

The TFNS transmission scheme involves the recoding of an outgoing binary message to its equivalent decimal form and converting the decimal number into a tri-digit Fibonaccian number and transmitting it. The receiver then reconverts the received tri-digit Fibonaccian number to a decimal number and decodes into a binary number and passes it onto the higher layers of the communication stack for further processing. As in the case of TSS, we use a hybrid modulation scheme involving FSK and ASK. In the same way, the transmitter uses FSK modulation with two frequencies, such as *f_c* and *f_c + Δf* corresponding to the symbols 1 and 2, respectively. The transmitter is switched to *sleep* state during the transmission of the silent symbol (0’s).
