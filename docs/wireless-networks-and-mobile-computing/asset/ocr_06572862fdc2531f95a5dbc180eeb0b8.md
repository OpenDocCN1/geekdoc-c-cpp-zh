The first issue of deploying the sensors at strategic locations is important for several reasons depending on the application requirements. For example, for a video surveillance system using cameras as sensors in a shopping mall or a library, the visibility of all points of the mall or library by the set of camera sensors is essential. In another application of weather sensors, the placement should be such that all the sensors are able to communicate their sensed data to the sink location through other sensors using minimum energy for transmission and reception of all messages. We consider this aspect of placement in more details in Chapter 7.

The issues of communication protocols, energy and security are similar to those in ad hoc networks. However, depending on the typical application requirement of the sensed data, some specific energy-efficient algorithms can be developed for the application layer of the communication protocol stack in some special types of sensor networks by exploiting the impact of the computation process on the sensed data.
