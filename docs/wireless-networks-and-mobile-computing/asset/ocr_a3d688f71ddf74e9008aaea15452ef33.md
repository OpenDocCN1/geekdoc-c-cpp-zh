When an MN moves from one subnet to another, it has to register its new IP address (in the newly visited subnet) with its home agent. Thus, in effect, there are two IP addresses associated with every MN: one is the home address for its identification and the other is for finding its current location. The latter address is known as its _care of address (CoA)_. The association between the current CoA and the MN’s home address is maintained by some mobility binding rule with some lifetime period for this binding. Any packet destined for this MN is routed using the current CoA. The lifetime period of the binding with the CoA is determined at the time of MN’s registration at its home agent and after this period, this registration is deleted. The MN must re-register with its HA before the expiry of this time period to ensure continuous service (e.g., without interrupting ongoing calls) using this CoA. Thus, the MN may need to send a registration request message even when it is in its home subnet, i.e., the registration message may be sent directly to its HA (when it is still in its own subnet) or through an FA it is currently visiting.
