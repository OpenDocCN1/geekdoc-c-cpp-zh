Noting that in RBNSiZeComm the average transmitter power will be 6.0206 dB less than the peak power, it appears that the average transmitter power with RBNS will be about 0.057 dB higher than that with QPSK, which translates into 1.01321 times or about 1.32% more energy than the QPSK scheme. Thus, with coherent FSK-ASK detection, performance of RBNSiZeComm is comparable to that of coherent binary FSK, so far as energy consumption is concerned.
