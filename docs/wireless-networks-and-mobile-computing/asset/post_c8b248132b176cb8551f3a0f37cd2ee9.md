To satisfy the interference criteria, no frequency channel within the open intervals $(x-s_1,x)$ and $(x,x+s_1)$ can be assigned to any node of the subgraph including the central node itself, since $s_0 \ge s_1$. That is, there must be unusable gaps $(x-s_1,x)$ and $(x,x+s_1)$ before and after $x$, respectively.
