# 8.6  Tri-Digit Fibonacci Number System (TFNS)  
Communication Protocol

We describe another energy-efficient communication protocol based on a variant of Fibonacci number system, called the *Tri-digit Fibonacci number system (TFNS)*, coupled with silent communication of the dominant symbol of the encoded message.

In many engineering applications, the Fibonacci number system (FNS) has been used with remarkable success. Such applications include VLSI chip design (in minimizing crosstalk among the data buses inside a processor chip [37]), synchronization control [35], compression of large sparse bit strings [33] and robust transmission of binary strings [32]. Another possible application is in low-power wireless communication systems [34, 39, 6] where asymmetry in the numbers of symbols to be transmitted through the wireless channel is exploited to minimize the transmitter and/or receiver energy required. If the Fibonacci number system is used to give positional weights to binary digits for representation of decimal numbers, a marked asymmetry may be observed in the numbers of 0’s and 1’s in the resulting codewords. These asymmetric characteristics can be exploited for saving energy, particularly in low-power wireless sensor networks. However, if conventional FNS is used, the number of bits representing a given decimal number increases compared to the binary number system. As a result, the savings of energy for low-power wireless communication systems may not be substantial.
