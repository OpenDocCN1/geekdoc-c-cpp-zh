**WSNs for agriculture:** In WSNs deployed for monitoring crop health, information collected through sensor networks in the fields include climate data such as temperature, relative humidity, solar radiation and soil composition. Network architectures typically consist of a network of *acquisition stations* and a *gateway* node. Data acquisition is done through low-cost acquisition stations equipped with wireless transceivers and deployed in a mesh topology [24, 26]. The acquisition stations collect data such as temperature, relative humidity, solar radiation and soil composition, and also detect pest infestation and other data.
