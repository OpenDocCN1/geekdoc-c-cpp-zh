**Theorem 21.** *The demand sum* \(D_{10}^{(2)*}\) *is the optimal bandwidth requirement of a hexagonal cellular network having a heterogeneous demand vector.*

*Proof.* We established from Lemma 22 that it is necessary to consider at least a ten-node critical block to compute the minimum slot requirement of a cellular network. We now prove that the demand of a ten-node critical block gives the minimal bandwidth requirement of a hexagonal cellular network with a heterogeneous demand vector.

The nodes adjacent to the critical block have the least number of contributing nodes in the critical block to satisfy their demands. The further away a node is from the critical block, the higher is the number of contributing nodes in the critical block to satisfy its demand. Hence, proving that the demand of any Distance 2 clique adjacent to the critical block can be satisfied using only the slots assigned to the critical block, implies that the demand of all other Distance 2 cliques in the cellular graph can be satisfied using only the slots assigned to the ten-node critical block.
