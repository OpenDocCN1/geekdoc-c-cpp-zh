**Dynamically changing size of LA:** In this location update scheme, the LA size and hence the LA boundaries may be different for different MTs and may be dynamically changed even for the same MT depending on its recent mobility characteristics. For example, referring to Figure 2.6, [1] the LA size of an MT, say MT1 may consist of 2 × 2 square cells, whereas for another mobile terminal, say MT2, moving at a faster rate, the LA size may consist of 3 × 3 cells. Thus, essentially, the LA size is chosen on a per-user basis aiming at getting the optimum results in terms of the total overhead in time arising from location update and paging. Naturally, this scheme is more complicated than the others and is difficult to implement because of the dynamically changing LA size and LA boundaries for each individual MT. The scheme becomes even more complicated if the cells are hexagonal rather than square.
