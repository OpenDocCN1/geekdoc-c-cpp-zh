In addition to RBNSiZeComm [39], another protocol called RZE [5] was proposed by Sinha and Sinha. Comparing the performances of transmitter and receiver (at the front-end amplifier section) energy savings for non-coherent TSS, coherent TSS, RBNSizeComm and RZE protocols relative to non-coherent BFSK, we can see that while the transmitter side saving for TSS is less than that of RBNSiZeComm and RZE, it saves more energy at the front-end amplifier of the receiver section than both these schemes. Thus, considering the combined energy savings at the transmitter and the receiver, TSS is more suitable than RBNS or RZE for data communication in multi-hop wireless networks.
