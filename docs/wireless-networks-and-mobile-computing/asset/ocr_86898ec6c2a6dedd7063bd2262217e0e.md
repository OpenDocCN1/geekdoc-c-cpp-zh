We have presented several techniques for energy-efficient communication at the physical layer and it will be interesting to investigate further to find alternative means (possibly through source coding and/or channel coding) to achieve substantial savings in transmitter and receiver energy savings. Further investigations of energy-efficiency at the MAC layer, network layer and application layer are also very much needed.
