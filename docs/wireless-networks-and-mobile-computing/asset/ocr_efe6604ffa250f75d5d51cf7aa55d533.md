After the completion of second round channel assignment to all peripheral nodes $(a,b,c,e,f,g)$, if we assign a channel to the central node, the minimum gap would be $s_1$. For the next round, we are to keep another gap of length $s_1$ before assigning to node $a$. It would be more economical to complete the assignments of all $w$ channels to the peripheral nodes only, and assign the remaining $(w-1)$ channels to the central node.
