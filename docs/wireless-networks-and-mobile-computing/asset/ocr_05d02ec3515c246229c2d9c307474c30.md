$\nu'_1=F_{2n}+(2F_{2n-2}+F_{2n-3})+\cdots=F_1F_{2n}+(F_3F_{2n-2}+F_2F_{2n-3})$  
$\qquad +(F_5F_{2n-4}+F_4F_{2n-5})+\cdots +(F_{2n-1}F_2+F_{2n-2}F_1).$

The total number of 2’s in all the valid codewords is:

$\nu'_2=F_1F_{2n-1}+2F_{2n-3}+5F_{2n-5}+\cdots$  
$\qquad =F_1F_{2n-1}+F_3F_{2n-3}+F_5F_{2n-5}+\cdots+F_{2n-1}F_1.$

To express $\nu'_0$, $\nu'_1$ and $\nu'_2$ in closed form we proceed as follows.

Let $G(z)=F_0+F_1z+F_2z^2+F_3z^3+F_4z^4+\cdots$

Thus, $G(z)$ is given by $G(z)=\frac{z}{1-z-z^2}$ as in [36]. Now, $G(-z)=F_0-F_1z+F_2z^2-F_3z^3+F_4z^4+\cdots$.

Hence, we have:

$\frac{G(z)+G(-z)}{2}=F_0+F_2z^2+F_4z^4+F_6z^6+\cdots=H(z),\quad\text{(say).}$

That is,

$H(z)=\frac{z^2}{(1-z^2)^2-z^2}=\frac{z^2}{1-3z^2+z^4}$  
$\qquad=\frac{z^2}{(1-\alpha z^2)(1-\beta z^2)},\quad\text{(say)}$

where $\alpha$ and $\beta$ are given by:

$\begin{cases}\alpha+\beta=3\\\alpha\beta=1.\end{cases}\tag{8.54}$

Hence, $\alpha=\frac{3+\sqrt5}{2}=1+\phi$ and $\beta=\frac{3-\sqrt5}{2}=1-\bar{\phi}$. Also, $H^2(z)=(F_0+F_2z^2+F_4z^4+\cdots)^2$, in which the coefficient of $z^{2n+2}$ is $F_0F_{2n+2}+F_2F_{2n}+F_4F_{2n-2}+\cdots$.
