**Lemma 7.** The given CAP \(P\) and the coalesced CAP \(P'\) are equivalent if \(z=n\). For \(z<n\), the total search space of \(P'\) is always less than that of \(P\).

**Proof:** Clearly, if \(z=n\), problems \(P\) and \(P'\) have the same number of nodes having the same weights, and \(C=C'\). Hence, there is no reduction in search space after transferring \(P\) to \(P'\).

As explained in Section 3.1.3, the number of nodes and their demands in a CAP graph determines the total search space for a given CAP. Let the sum of demands on all cells for problems \(P\) and \(P'\) be \(\mathcal{T}\) and \(\mathcal{T}'\), respectively. Hence, the CAP graphs for \(P\) and \(P'\) will have \(\mathcal{T}\) and \(\mathcal{T}'\) nodes, respectively. If \(z<n\), \(\mathcal{T}'\) must be less than \(\mathcal{T}\) because the sum of the demands of all the cells in \(Y(i)\) has been replaced by the maximum of those to contribute to \(\mathcal{T}'\). Hence, the proof.
