**Pipelined Design C**: Another idea for reducing circuit size further is to perform only one iteration of KSA per cycle. In this approach, KSA will require 256 cycles to initialize the _S_-box and the read and write accesses to _S_-box will become two per cycle. By applying access sharing on top of that, the total read and write accesses to _S_-box are reduced to three and two, respectively. Furthermore, the number of read accesses to _K_-box also drops to one. The synthesis of this circuit also indicated a more compact design.

A sharp reduction in _S_-box and KSA areas for Pipelined Design C in comparison with the previous designs was observed. The reduction in the area for _S_-box is most prominent as Pipelined Design C directly reduces the area requirements for the address decoders and multiplexers, due to fewer _S_-box access ports. However, the reduction in _K_-box access ports costs 256 cycles for KSA instead of 128 as in the previous two designs.
