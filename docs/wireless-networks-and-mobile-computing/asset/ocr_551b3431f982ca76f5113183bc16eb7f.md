More difficult is finding optimal solutions for the other two Philadelphia instances: Problems 2 and 6 [15], [24]. For example, the assignment algorithm given in [16] required 165 hours of computing time for Problem 6 on an unloaded HP Apollo 9000/700 workstation, but produced only a non-optimal solution with 268 channels (optimal solution requires only 253 channels). Later, however, the authors in [15] proposed an algorithm which provided optimal solutions for Problems 2 and 6 with running times of 8 and 10 minutes, respectively on the same workstation. Among the later works, the FESR (frequency exhaustive strategy with rearrangement) algorithm in [17] and the randomized saturation degree (RSD) heuristic presented in [24] also produced only non-optimal solutions to the benchmark Problems 2 and 6. However, combining their RSD heuristic with a local search (LS) algorithm, the authors in [24] were able to find an optimal solution for Problem 2 but not for Problem 6.
