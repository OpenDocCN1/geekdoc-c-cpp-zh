# 2.3  Handoff Management

In Chapter 1, we mentioned three distinct stages for handoff: i) handoff initiation, ii) new connection generation and iii) data flow control. Actually, handoff initiation depends on the received signal strength by the MT from the BS and vice versa. This leads to two types of handoff situations: intracell handoff and intercell handoff. Intracell handoff may be initiated when the user moves even in the same cell, but the signal strength falls below the threshold value due to changed network surroundings (e.g., obstacles or reflecting walls, etc.) for reliable communication to be maintained between the BS and the MT. In this case, after the handoff, the ongoing call to the MT will be continued over a newly allocated channel through which the signal strength received by the MT from the BS and vice versa will be more to sustain a reliable communication. The intercell handoff is initiated when the MT moves from one cell to an adjacent cell so that the signal received from the old BS is of smaller strength than that from another BS in the direction of its movement.
