There will be a total of _n_ rounds in every phase and only one node will be transmitting during any round. Hence, there will be no collisions and the broadcast will be completed in _D_ phases. The upper bound on the number of rounds required for broadcasting by following this protocol will thus be _nD_. However, this simple protocol has the drawback that even when the maximum node degree Δ is very small (as is usually the case), only one node will be allowed to transmit in a round although it might have been possible to allow more than one node to transmit in a round without causing a collision. That is, for low values of Δ and large values of _n_, the number of rounds needed to complete the broadcast may be much smaller than _nD_.
