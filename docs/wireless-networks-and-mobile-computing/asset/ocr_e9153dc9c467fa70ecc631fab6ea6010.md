# 8.6.3.5  Energy Savings Analysis

Now we analyze the extent of energy savings in non-coherent detection using TFNS. We assume that the symbol duration of the TFNS-encoded message in TFNS is same as that in BFSK. It means that the transmission duration of the TFNS message will be smaller than that of the equivalent binary message due to encoding. Considering $T_b$ and $T_1$ as the transmission duration of the binary message and its equivalent ternary message, respectively, we can write $\frac{T_1}{T_b}=\frac{n}{m}$. This is asymptotically (for large $m$) equal to 0.72.

If we denote the total (scaled) energy in TFNS with non-coherent receiver as $E_1$ and the energy in non-coherent BFSK as $E_b$,

$$\frac{E_1}{E_b}=\frac{P_1\times T_1}{P_b\times T_b}=1.038\times 0.72=0.7474 \tag{8.65}$$
