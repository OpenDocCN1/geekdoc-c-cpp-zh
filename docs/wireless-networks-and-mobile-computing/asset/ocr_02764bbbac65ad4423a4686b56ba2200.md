# **Theorem 10.**

*Assuming an error of $\pm\epsilon$ in the coordinates of the beacon nodes and an error of $\pm\delta$ in the measured ranges, the error in the location of an unknown node due to the combined effect of these two errors is bounded by* $\pm\left[\frac{3\epsilon(1+\cos\theta)}{\sin\theta}+2\delta\right]$ *along the x-axis and* $\pm\left[\frac{2\delta(1+\cos\theta)}{\sin\theta}+3\epsilon\right]$ *along the y-axis.*

*Proof.* The projection of the edge $PO$ on the x-axis is $6\epsilon\cot\theta$. Similarly, the projection of edge $DA$ on the y-axis is $4\delta\cot\theta$. Now from Lemma 11, Minkowski’s sum is formed by placing the polygon $ABCD$ on all the points along the edges of the polygon $MNOP$ such that the intersection of the two diagonals of $ABCD$ lies on the edges of $MNOP$. Hence the projection of $ABCD$ along the x-axis is $4\delta$ and the projection of $MNOP$ along the y-axis is $6\epsilon$. Thus, the total span of the error along the x-axis due to the combined effect of the two errors is $\pm\left[\frac{6\epsilon}{\sin\theta}+6\epsilon\cot\theta+4\delta\right]$, and along the y-axis is $\pm\left[\frac{4\delta}{\sin\theta}+4\delta\cot\theta+6\epsilon\right]$.
