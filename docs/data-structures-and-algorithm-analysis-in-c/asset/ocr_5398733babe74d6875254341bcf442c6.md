Various solutions to the union/find problem can be found in [6], [9], and [11]. Hopcroft and Ullman showed an O(M log* N) bound using a nonrecursive decomposition. Tarjan [16] obtained the bound O(Mα(M, N)), where α(M, N) is as defined in Exercise 8.7. A more precise (but asymptotically identical) bound for M < N appears in [2] and [19]. A lower bound showing that under certain restrictions Ω(Mα(M, N)) time is required to process M union/find operations was given by Tarjan [17]. Identical bounds under less restrictive conditions have been shown in [7] and [14].
