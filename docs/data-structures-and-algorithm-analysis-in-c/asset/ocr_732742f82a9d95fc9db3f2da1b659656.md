The devil, of course, is in the details: How is combineSiblings implemented? Several variants have been proposed, but none has been shown to provide the same amortized bounds as the Fibonacci heap. It has recently been shown that almost all of the proposed methods are in fact theoretically less efficient than the Fibonacci heap. Even so, the method coded in Figure 12.48 on page 607 always seems to perform as well as or better than other heap structures, including the binary heap, for the typical graph theory uses that involve a host of decreaseKey operations.
