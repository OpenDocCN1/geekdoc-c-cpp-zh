A statistical assessment developed from a GIS raster representation of Great Mendoza’s distributions -using 350 x 350 m² grid elements and official census data collected between 1990 and 1992-, reveals the existence of two subsystems: one dense and central and the other rather diluted and peripheral (fig. 3). The former contributes to the characterization of the main urban agglomerate whereas the latter to its interphases of fluxion.
