# ## 1. Introduction

Some socioeconomic systems, it is desirable to provide real-time information or even a short-term forecast about dynamics. For instance, in stock markets it is advantageous to give a reliable forecast in order to maximize profit. In traffic flow, advanced traveler information systems (ATIS) provide real-time information about the traffic conditions to road users by means of communication such as variable message signs, radio broadcasts, or on-board computers (Adler & Blue, 1998). The aim is to help individual road users to minimize their personal travel time. Therefore traffic congestion should be alleviated, and the capacity of the existing infrastructure could be used more efficiently.
