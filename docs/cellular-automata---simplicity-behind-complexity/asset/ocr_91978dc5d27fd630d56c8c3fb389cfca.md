# 5. Calculation of the shift exponent

A shift exponent (φ) describes the deviation of the critical temperature from the critical temperature for the decoupled limit (Kz = 0). Some scaling theories were constructed to obtain the shift exponent (Oitmaa & Enting, 1975; Lipsowski, 1998; Horiguchi & Tsushima 1997; Abe, 1970; and Suzuki, 1971). It was shown that for the two-layer model there is a relation between the critical point and the shift exponent as follow

$$T_c(\xi)-T_c(0) \propto \xi^{1/\varphi}$$  \hfill (24)
