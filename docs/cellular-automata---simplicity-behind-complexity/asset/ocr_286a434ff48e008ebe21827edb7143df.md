In this section, we propose a cellular automaton model to study the dynamics of a cycling bus. The intrinsic fluctuations in the traffic dynamics are prescribed by the stochastic moving of bus (along the route) coupled with the stochastic arrival of passengers (at the bus stops). We observe the wide fluctuations of the recurrent schedule. When the passengers increase, the schedule diverges suddenly, i.e., the bus was delayed indefinitely and unexpectedly.
