Simulation outcomes are also influenced by the number and selection of external driving factors (Table 8). Using four factors generates the highest agreement with the reference map both in terms of overall agreement (0.058) and location (0.140). The best combination of three factors includes distance to main road, distance to city center and distance to river, which are expected to play a major role in the increase of built-up areas. Ground slope also appears to be an important factor as revealed by the coefficients of agreement that are slightly lower than the ones obtained with the previous three factors. It can be observed that the amount of land-use transitions remains the same with the different combinations of factors; however, their spatial distribution changes as indicated by Ktransloc.
