# ### Solution of Question 133

First occurrence of *using A::f* in global namespace marks *f* as a synonym for *A::f*; that is, for *A::f(int)*.
*f('a')* in *void foo()* calls *f(int)*, even though *f(char)* exists.
in the function *void bar()*, *using A::f* marks *f* as a synonym for *A::f*; that is, for *A::f(int)* and *A::f(char)* and *f('a')* calls *f(char)*.
