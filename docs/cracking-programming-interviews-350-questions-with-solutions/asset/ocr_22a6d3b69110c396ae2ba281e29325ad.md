# ## Solution of Question 149

It declares pmi, pmf, pmd and pmc to be a pointer to a member of X of type int, a pointer to a member of X of type void(int), a pointer to a member of X of type double and a pointer to a member of Y of type char respectively.

The declaration of pmd is well-formed even though X has no members of type double.

Similarly, the declaration of pmc is well-formed even though Y is an incomplete type. pmi and pmf can be used like shown in the program.
