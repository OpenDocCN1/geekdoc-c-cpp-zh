This reasoning leads us to think of 1.943 defects per module as the statistical limit. Poisson approximation thus allows us statistical control of defects. Any module with more than 1.943 defects is a Poisson outlier. Poisson distribution here serves as a quality-judge. (The use of this characteristic Poisson distribution is illustrated in Box 12.4.)

The CDF of the Poisson distribution, shown in Figure 12.9, is of special relevance to software defect management.
