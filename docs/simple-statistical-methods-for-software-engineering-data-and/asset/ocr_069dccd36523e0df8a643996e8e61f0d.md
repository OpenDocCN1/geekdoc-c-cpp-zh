Then we will apply a policy to decide whether the p value is less than the “critical value.” If the p value is less, then we would say that the data point is significantly different from the process. It is a round-about way of saying that p is outside and is certainly confusing to many who would rather have a simple and transparent answer.

The triangle presents a crystal clear view of process and facilitates straight decision making.
