We can find several applications of the Weibull application in the literature; here are a few instances.

The strength of yarn is not a single-valued property but a statistical variable. The statistical distribution of yarn strength is usually described by the normal distribution. As an improvement, Weibull statistics was used by Shi and Hu [2] as a tool to analyze the strength of cotton yarns at different gauge lengths to find the relationship between them.

Propagation delay in CMOS circuits is characterized by Weibull distribution. The experiments of Liu et al. [3] on large industrial design demonstrate that the Weibull-based delay model is accurate, realistic, and economic.

Fire interval data are known to belong to the Weibull family. A study by Grissino-Mayer [4] shows that two- and three-parameter Weibull distributions were fit to fire interval data sets. The three-parameter models failed to provide improved fits versus the more parsimonious two-parameter models, indicating that the Weibull shift parameter may be superfluous.
