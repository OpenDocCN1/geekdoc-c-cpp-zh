# Gamma Curves for Clarification Time Data

We can model clarification time data with gamma distribution. In software maintenance, clarification time depends mostly on the customer and is not under the direct influence of the project team. Let us consider data with descriptive statistics shown in Data 18.1.

Data 18.1 shows that the mean is 36.1622, the mode is 11, and the standard deviation is 39.3173.

We wish to mention two properties of gamma distribution,

Mean = scale × shape
= αβ
(18.2)

Variance = αβ²
(18.3)

To select the shape parameter, let us consult the histogram of clarification time data, as shown in Figure 18.3.

The shape of the histogram is closer to the first curve in Figure 18.1, with a shape factor of 1.2.

Substituting the values of mean (36.1622) and shape (1.2) in Equation 18.1, we obtain the value of scale as follows:

Scale = Mean / Shape = 36.1622 / 1.2 = 30.1352
