# Definition 10.7

Let Xₜ be a causal, stationary multivariate process that satisfies

Xₜ - Φ₁Xₜ₋₁ - ⋯ - ΦₚXₜ₋ₚ = α + aₜ - Θ₁aₜ₋₁ - ⋯ - Θ_q aₜ₋q

where the Φ′ₖ s and Θ′ₖ s are real-valued m × m matrices, and aₜ is multivariate white noise. Then, Xₜ is a VARMA(p,q) process.
