In Definition 11.4, we extend the idea of a fractional process to a FARMA process that can have both short and long-memory behavior.

# Definition 11.4

If Xₜ is a causal, stationary, and invertible process satisfying

$$\phi(B)(1 - B)^d(X_t - \mu) = \theta(B)a_t,\tag{11.6}$$

where aₜ is white noise, and where φ(z) and θ(z) are pth and qth-order polynomials, respectively, that share no common factors, then Xₜ is called a fractional ARMA (FARMA(p,d,q)) process.
