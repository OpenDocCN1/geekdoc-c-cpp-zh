+   1. XBOX image: A classic two-dimensional image used for illustrating two-dimensional wavelets is the XBOX image shown in Figure 12.22a. The XBOX image is ideally suited for showing the sense in which the d_{j,k,m}^v, d_{j,k,m}^h and d_{j,k,m}^d coefficients measure vertical, horizontal, and diagonal behavior, respectively. We reiterate the fact that the image in Figure 12.22b is a DWT, that is, it is a two-dimensional plot of the coefficients analogous to the DWT plots shown, for example, in Figures 12.7 and 12.8. That is, although the bottom left box has the appearance of an approximating function (such as S4 in Figure 12.9) it is simply a plot of the coefficients, which in this case has the appearance of the actual figure.
