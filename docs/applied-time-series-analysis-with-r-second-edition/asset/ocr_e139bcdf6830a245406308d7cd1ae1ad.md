# 13.4.1 Models for Generalized Linear Chirps

In this section, we discuss AR-type models for GLC processes that are similar to the Euler(p) and $G(p; \lambda)$ models. To begin the discussion, we define GLC white noise and then define the GLC(p) model. As before, we proceed formally assuming standard properties of the differential operator, $D$, since our purpose is to motivate the sampling scheme.

# Definition 13.10

Let $z(u)$ be zero mean white noise and let $u = a t^2 + b t$, $\quad t > 0$. Then $\varepsilon(t) = z(u)$ is defined as GLC white noise.

# Definition 13.11

$$\prod_{i=1}^{p}\left(\frac{1}{2 a t + b}D - \alpha_i\right)(X(t) - \mu) = \varepsilon(t),\tag{13.24}$$
