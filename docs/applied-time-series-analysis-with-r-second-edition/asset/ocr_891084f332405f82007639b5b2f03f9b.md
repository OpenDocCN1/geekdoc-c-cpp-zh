Equation 12.9 is an orthogonal collection of functions that form a basis for functions $g \in L^2(\mathbb{R})$ in the sense of Theorem 12.1 that follows. Comparing Figure 12.6a, c, and e it can be seen that as $k$ increases, the support of the Daublet(2$k$) wavelet (interval on which the wavelet takes on nonzero values) gets longer and the wavelet becomes smoother.
