# EXAMPLE 13.3: INSTANTANEOUS PERIOD AND FREQUENCY

Another concept is that of instantaneous period and instantaneous frequency (IF). Loosely speaking, the instantaneous period at time t is the length of the next period. The IF is then defined as the reciprocal of the instantaneous period. In Figure 13.3, we show an enlarged version of the plot shown in Figure 13.2c. From the plot, it is seen that the instantaneous period at t = 19 is about 5 while the instantaneous periods at t = 80 and t = 152 are about 11 and 18, respectively. Consequently, instantaneous frequencies at t = 19, 80, and 152 are about 0.2, 0.09, and 0.06, respectively. See Appendix 13A for a mathematical treatment of instantaneous period and frequency.
