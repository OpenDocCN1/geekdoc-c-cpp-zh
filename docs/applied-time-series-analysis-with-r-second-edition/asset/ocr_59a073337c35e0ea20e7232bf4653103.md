$X_t = \Phi X_{t-1} + V_t \quad \text{(state equation)},$
$Y_t = (0\ 0 \ \dots \ 1) X_t \quad \text{(observation equation)},$

where $X_t$, $\Phi$, and $V_t$ are the $pm \times 1$, $pm \times pm$, and $pm \times m$ matrices given, respectively, by

$X_t = \begin{pmatrix} Y_{t-p+1} \\ Y_{t-p+2} \\ \vdots \\ Y_t \end{pmatrix}, \quad \Phi = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & I & 0 & \dots & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & I & \dots & 0 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & \dots & I \\ \Phi_p & \Phi_{p-1} & \Phi_{p-2} & \dots & \Phi_1 \end{pmatrix}, \quad V_t = \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \\ \vdots \\ 0 \\ I \end{pmatrix} u_t$

# Example 10.11: STATE-SPACE FORM OF AN ARMA(p, q) PROCESS

A state-space formulation of the univariate, zero-mean ARMA(p,q) process

$Y_t - \phi_1 Y_{t-1} - \cdots \phi_p Y_{t-p} = a_t - \phi_1 a_{t-1} - \cdots - \theta_q a_{t-q},$

is given by

$X_t = \Phi X_{t-1} + V_t \qquad \text{(state equation)},$
$Y_t = (-\theta_{r-1}, -\theta_{r-2}, \dots, -\theta_1, 1) X_t \quad \text{(observation equation)},$

where

$X_t = \begin{pmatrix} U_{t-r+1} \\ U_{t-r+2} \\ \vdots \\ U_t \end{pmatrix}, \quad \Phi = \begin{pmatrix} 0 & 1 & 0 & \dots & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 & \dots & 0 \\ \vdots & \vdots & \vdots & & \vdots \\ 0 & 0 & 0 & \dots & 1 \\ \phi_r & \phi_{r-1} & \phi_{r-2} & \dots & \phi_1 \end{pmatrix}, \quad V_t = \begin{pmatrix} 0 \\ 0 \\ \vdots \\ 0 \\ 1 \end{pmatrix} a_t,$

where $\mathcal{U}_t$ is the AR(p) process given by $\phi(B)\mathcal{U}_t = a_t$, $r = \max(p, q + 1)$, $\phi_j = 0$ for $j > p$, and $\theta_j = 0$ for $j > q$. (See Problem 10.9.)
