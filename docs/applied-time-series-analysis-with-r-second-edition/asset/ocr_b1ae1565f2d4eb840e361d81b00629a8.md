The realization associated with $\phi_1 = 1.1$ demonstrates what Box et al. (2008) refer to as “explosive” behavior. The random wandering seen in Figure 3.7 associated with $\phi_1 = 1$ is typical of certain real data series such as daily stock prices. However, the explosive realization associated with $\phi_1 = 1.1$ is not similar to that of any data sets that we will attempt to analyze, being better modeled as a deterministic process.
