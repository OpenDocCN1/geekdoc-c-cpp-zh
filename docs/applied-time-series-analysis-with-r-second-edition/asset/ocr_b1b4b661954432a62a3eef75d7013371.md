+   2. Var $[X_t] = E[X_t X_t]$  
+       $= \sigma_a^2 \sum_{j=0}^{\infty} \psi_j^2 \text{(a constant)}$  
+       $< \infty \left(\text{since} \sum_{j=0}^{\infty} |\psi_j| \text{ converges}\right)$  
+   3. $E[X_t X_{t+k}] = \gamma_k$ (depends only on $k$ from Equation 2.7)

Thus, $X_t$ is stationary, and the proof is complete.

# 2.2.1 Spectrum and Spectral Density for a General Linear Process

Using Equation 2.3 and the fact that the spectrum for white noise is constant and given by $P_a(f)=\sigma_a^2$, the spectrum of the general linear process $X_t$ is given by  
$$P_X(f)=\left|\psi\left(e^{-2\pi i f}\right)\right|^2 P_a(f) \tag{2.8}$$  
$$=\sigma_a^2\left|\psi\left(e^{-2\pi i f}\right)\right|^2,$$  
and the spectral density is given by  
$$S_X(f)=\frac{\sigma_a^2}{\sigma_X^2}\left|\psi\left(e^{-2\pi i f}\right)\right|^2. \tag{2.9}$$
