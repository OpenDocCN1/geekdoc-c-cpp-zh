Time series analysis techniques are often classified into two major categories: time domain and frequency domain techniques. Time domain techniques include the analysis of the correlation structure, development of models that describe the manner in which such data evolve in time, and forecasting future behavior. Frequency domain approaches are designed to develop an understanding of time series data by examining the data from the perspective of their underlying cyclic (or frequency) content. The observation that the Pennsylvania temperature data tend to contain 12 month cycles is an example of examination of the frequency domain content of that data set. The basic frequency domain analysis tool is the power spectrum.
