Specifically regarding the autocorrelations in Figure 5.2, we note that both the damped exponential (first-order) behavior associated with the positive real root and the damped sinusoidal (second-order) behavior associated with the pair of complex conjugate roots are visible when α₁ = 0.8. However, as α₁ → 1, the damped exponential (first-order) behavior tends to dominate the autocorrelations, to the point that in Figure 5.2c, the autocorrelations simply have the appearance of a (very slowly) damped exponential. This suggests that in this case, the extended autocorrelations, ρ*_k, will be totally dominated by the first-order behavior, that is, they will satisfy a first-order difference equation.
