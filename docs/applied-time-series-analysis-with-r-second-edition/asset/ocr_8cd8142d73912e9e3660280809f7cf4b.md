$$\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} \psi_{\ell,m}(t)\psi_{j,k}(t)dt = \begin{cases} 0, & j \neq \ell \text{ or } m \neq k \\ \|\psi\|^2, & j = \ell,\ m = k \end{cases}$$

Consequently,

$$d_{j,k} = \frac{1}{\|\psi\|^2}\int_{-\infty}^{\infty} g(t)\psi_{j,k}(t)dt.$$
