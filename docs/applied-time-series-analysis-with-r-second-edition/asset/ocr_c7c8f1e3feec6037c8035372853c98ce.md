$\phi(B)\alpha^{-1}(B) = \phi_1(B)\alpha(B)\alpha^{-1}(B)$
$= \phi_1(B),$

that is $\phi_1(B)Y_t = \theta(B)a_t$, and $Y_t$ satisfies an ARMA $(p - k, q)$ model.

Case (2) $\alpha(B)$ is not a factor of $\phi(B)$ and has no common subfactors: In this case

$\phi(B)\alpha^{-1}(B)Y_t = \theta(B)a_t,$

or

$\phi(B)Y_t = \alpha(B)\theta(B)a_t,$

that is, $Y_t$ is ARMA $(p, q + k)$.
