# EXAMPLE 4.7: SEISMIC DATA

Figure 4.15 shows a seismic Lg wave (data set mm.eq in tswge) from an earthquake known as the Massachusetts Mountain earthquake (August 5, 1971) which was recorded at the Mina, Nevada station. See Gupta et al. (2005). The extended period of higher variability around t = 100 is similar to the behavior in Figure 4.14d around t = 700 and t = 1150 suggesting that an AR with ARCH errors might be an appropriate model for the seismic data in Figure 4.15.
