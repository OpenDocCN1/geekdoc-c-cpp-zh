More generally for the ARMA($p,q$) model

$\phi(B)X_t = \theta(B)a_t$,  (3.132)

we consider the problem of determining the model satisfied by $Y_t^*$, where

$Y_t^* = \alpha(B)X_t$

and $\alpha(B)$ is of order $k$, that is, $\alpha(B) = 1 - \alpha_1 B - \cdots - \alpha_k B^k$. Thus, $X_t = \alpha^{-1}(B)Y_t^*$, and Equation 3.132 can be rewritten as

$\phi(B)\alpha^{-1}(B)Y_t^* = \theta(B)a_t$
