# 3.2.4 Autocorrelations of an AR(p) Model

In order to find the autocorrelations of an AR(p) process, pre-multiply each side of Equation 3.17 by $X_{t-k}$ where $k > 0$, to obtain

$X_{t-k}\{X_t - \phi_1 X_{t-1} - \cdots - \phi_p X_{t-p} = a_t\}$ . (3.36)

Taking expectations in Equation 3.36 yields

$\gamma_k - \phi_1 \gamma_{k-1} - \cdots - \phi_p \gamma_{k-p} = 0$, 

where we have used the fact that, as in the AR(1) case, $E[X_{t-k} a_t] = 0$ for $k > 0$.

It follows that for $k > 0$, 

$\rho_k - \phi_1 \rho_{k-1} - \cdots - \phi_p \rho_{k-p} = 0$. (3.37)
