terms of the conditional variance in the model. Specifically, for the GARCH(p0, q0) model, Equation 4.23 is generalized to

$a_t = \sigma_{t|t-1} \varepsilon_t \tag{4.26}$

where

$\sigma^2_{t|t-1} = a_0 + \beta_1 \sigma^2_{t-1|t-2} + \cdots + \beta_{p_0} \sigma^2_{t-p_0|t-p_0-1} + \alpha_1 a^2_{t-1} + \alpha_2 a^2_{t-2} + \cdots + \alpha_{q_0} a^2_{t-q_0}$

and where the $\varepsilon_t$'s are i.i.d., zero mean, unit variance random variables. Brockwell and Davis (1991) introduced constraints on the parameters that ensure stationarity, and Nelson and Cao (1992) presented looser constraints. Corresponding to Equation 4.24, an alternative expression is given by

$a_t^2 = a_0 + (\alpha_1 + \beta_1) a^2_{t-1} + \cdots + (\alpha_{\max(p,q)} + \beta_{\max(p,q)}) a^2_{t-\max(p,q)} + w_t - \beta_1 w_{t-1} - \cdots - \beta_p w_{t-p} \tag{4.27}$

From Equation 4.27, the unconditional variance is given by

$\sigma_a^2 = \frac{a_0}{1 - (\alpha_1 + \beta_1) - \cdots - (\alpha_{\max(p,q)} + \beta_{\max(p,q)})} \tag{4.28}$

For details, see Box et al. (2008), Cryer and Chan (2008), and Shumway and Stoffer (2010).
