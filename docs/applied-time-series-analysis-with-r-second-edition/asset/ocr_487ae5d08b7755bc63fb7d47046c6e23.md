Calculations show that

H(e^{-2πi f}) = 1 + cos 2πf − i sin2πf,

and

|H(e^{-2πi f})|^2 = 2(1 + cos2πf).

Closer observation of Figures 2.2b and 2.3 shows that |H(e^{-2πi f})|^2 = 4 when f = 0.5 for the first difference filter and when f = 0 for the sum filter. The goal of a filter is to leave some frequency content unchanged and remove others.
