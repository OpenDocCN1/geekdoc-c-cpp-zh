# 3.2.8 Summary of AR(1) and AR(2) Behavior

Before proceeding to further examination of general AR(p) models, we summarize the information about AR(1) and AR(2) models

+   1. *Facts about the stationary AR(1) model* $X_t - \phi_1 X_{t-1} = a_t$: In this case, the characteristic equation is given by $1 - \phi_1 z = 0$, so that the root, $r_1$, of the characteristic equation is given by $r_1 = \phi_1^{-1}$. As a consequence, an AR(1) process is stationary if and only if $|\phi_1| < 1$. It is instructive to note that since $| r_1^{-1} | = | \phi_1 |$ the proximity of the root to the unit circle can be determined by simple examination of $\phi_1$.
