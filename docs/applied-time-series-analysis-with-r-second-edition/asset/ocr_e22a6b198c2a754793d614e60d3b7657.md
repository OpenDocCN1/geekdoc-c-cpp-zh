# 12A.2 Vector Spaces

Let $\mathbf{x} = (x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n)'$ and $\mathbf{y} = (y_1, y_2, \dots, y_n)'$ be two $n$-component real vectors.

+   1. The *inner product* (or dot product) of **x** and **y** is given by $\sum_{k=1}^n x_k y_k$.
+   2. The Euclidean *norm* of **x**, denoted by $\|\mathbf{x}\|$, is given by $\left(\sum_{k=1}^n x_k^2\right)^{1/2}$.
+   3. Vectors **x** and **y** are *orthogonal* if their inner product is zero.
+   4. Vectors **x** and **y** are *orthonormal* if they are orthogonal and have norm one.

# 12A.3 Function Spaces of Real-Valued Functions $f$: $[-\pi, \pi] \to \mathbb{R}$

+   1. The *inner product* of functions $f$ and $g$ is given by $\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} f(x) g(x) dx$.
+   2. The *norm* of $f$, denoted by $\|f\|$, is given by $\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} |f(x)|^2 dx$.
+   3. The real-valued function $f$ is said to be in $L^2[-\pi, \pi]$ if $\int_{-\pi}^{\pi} |f(x)|^2 dx < \infty$.
+   4. Functions $f$ and $g$ are *orthogonal* if their inner product is zero.
+   5. Functions $f$ and $g$ are *orthonormal* if they are orthogonal and have norm one.
