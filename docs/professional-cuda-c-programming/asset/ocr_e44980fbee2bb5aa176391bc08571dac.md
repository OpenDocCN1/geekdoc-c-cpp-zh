NVIDIA's CUDA 5.0 report shows varying performance of FFTs depending on data size, from as low ~30 GFLOPS up to nearly 250 GFLOPS. Performance evaluation by FFTW on a single core reports anywhere from ~1 GFLOPS to ~5.5 GFLOPS. Extrapolating from FFTW's highest reported performance of 5.5 GFLOPS and cuFFT ~100 GFLOPS at the same data size, you would require 200 cores to equal a single GPU running cuFFT. In this section, studying performance evaluations of cuSPARSE, cuBLAS, and cuFFT proved that the usability of CUDA libraries does not come at the cost of performance.
