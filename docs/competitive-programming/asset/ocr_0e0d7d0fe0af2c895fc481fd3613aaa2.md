+   5. The length of a **Chord** of a circle with a radius *r* and an angle *α* (in degrees) can be obtained with the **Law of Cosines**: \(2r^2 \times (1 - \cos(α))\) -- see the explanation of this law in the discussion about Triangles below.
+   
+   6. The **Area** A of a circle with a radius *r* is \(A = π × r^2\).
+   
+   7. The area of a **Sector** of a circle with an area A₁ and an angle α (in degrees) is \(\frac{α}{360.0} × A\).
+   
+   8. The area of a **Segment** of a circle can be found by subtracting the area of the corresponding Sector with the area of an Isosceles Triangle with sides: *r*, *r*, and Chord-length.
