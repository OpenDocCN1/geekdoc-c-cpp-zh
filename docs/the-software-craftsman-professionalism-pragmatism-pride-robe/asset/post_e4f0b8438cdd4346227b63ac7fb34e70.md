## Work-Life Balance

So I have been saying how it is to look after our careers and that we should devote time of outside working hours to learn. However, we all have family, friends, and other interests in life. I have one and two kids, who love it to bits and want to spend a lot of quality time with me. It is never easy to balance work and personal life because more than 50 percent of our working hours at home, including all the commuting to and from.

The common thing I hear everyone talk about investing in our careers outside working hours is, "I don't have time." And if you say you agree with that, you are probably right. That's what you decided to believe and, in turn, become your reality. But the truth is, we are just not good at optimizing it. Maybe we prefer to spend our time with things that may or not be as important as our careers.

Stop reading and think about what you did yesterday, the time you woke up to when you went to bed. What did you do the day before yesterday? Seriously and really think about it. Now about how much of it was spent on work, how much was it spent on family, how much on learning something new or how much on spending with your loved ones. If you take some time to rest, it is not unusual, especially after a day or a busy week. Our body needs to recharge and we should take that into account. Some people do not have a lot of time. I met a German speaker at a conference who told me he had a life and a few kids. Talking about learning and practicing working hours, he told me that he is very smart in how he uses the limited amount of time he has. His situation, managed at a conference.

## Creating Time

Quite often, lack of time is as an excuse for our learning. People are different. Some live in large cities, some in small villages, some have family, and some have friends. Some have hobbies. Some love to go out with friends; others prefer to stay at home. Some are morning people; some go to bed late and wake up early by. By not following the tips apply to everyone: they are just a collection of things that we can take and use it in our careers.

I realized that I waste a lot of time in front of it browsing the Internet, all the unintended time spent scrolling through social media, playing games, or watching sports. I decided to count down on the number of hours spent on these things. That doesn't mean I don't do them. It just means that I do it in moderation. Although they are great ways to relax and switch off, we do not need to do them every night, with all.

Your local coffee shop is your friend. Find a coffee shop near you. If it's not one, find one along the way. If you have a coffee shop at home, I would avoid that I could save up to two hours before starting your work. Use this time to code, read a technical book, blogs, or whatever you think you need to do and move towards your career.

Lunch is another great opportunity that we have to practice and learn something new. Or once or twice a week grab a sandwich and eat while working where it is quiet. It is amazing what we can do in such a small period of time when we are focused.

Check if there is a user group or tech community in your city and join them. User groups meet regularly, many on a monthly schedule. Make a commitment to the meetings at least once or twice a month. Meetings to attend from one to three hours is the vast majority of our days we can plan for. The advantage of user meeting groups is that we can learn a huge amount in a very short period of time, either from a presentation or speaking or coding with other developers. There is a limit to what we can learn on our own. Although we feel we can learn anything, sometimes it doesn't ever feel true. It's also remembering that it will be faster to learn from someone else than trying learning on our own.

Go to bed 30 minutes earlier than normal and use this time to read a book, look at blogs, or watch screenshots before falling asleep. This is something that works really well for me. Every single night I try to read at least a few pages, regardless of the time I go to bed.

Buy yourself a Kindle, iPad, or another e-reader, carry it with you at all times. Use it every time you have dead time, keeping occupied or waiting for your dentist, doctor, or hairdresser appointment.

I believe everyone has a smartphone today but if you do not have one, buy one. Use Twitter whenever you have a break or dead time. This is a great way to quickly read or keep up with the latest news and trends. Being able to go to a single page.

## Focus: The Pomodoro Technique

In order to use our time well, it is extremely important that we have focus. A good technique is to decide before what we want. It does not need to be perfect, but we must ensure we can. There are five basic steps to implement this:

- Decide on the task to be done.
- Set the Pomodoro (timer) to 25 minutes.
- Work on the task until the timer rings.
- Take a short break (normally 5 minutes).
- Every four "Pomodoros", take a longer break (15-30 minutes).

During a Pomodoro, we focus on the task and nothing else but if it needs to be interrupted (there is an important call we need to take), then the Pomodoro must be terminated and counted. If so, there are many tools available out there that can keep track of all the completed, interrupted Pomodoros, and many other statistics. I, personally prefer the simple ones but feel free to use that suits you better.

## Balance

Whatever you do, a sustainable pace is key. Keeping a healthy work-life balance is tough but not. If you are the type of person who says, "I don't want to work for a computer outside," you should think about your career choice; maybe development is not for you. For the majority of us, software development—besides being our job—is also a hobby, which makes it relatively easy to find enough time to practice and better ourselves.

Keeping your professional life is essential for a healthy life. An unhealthy professional life, where we are constantly worried about not being paid well, or we may not find another well-paid and interesting job if we are made redundant in the market, puts us in a good position not to worry too much about our professional lives and gives our families the attention they much deserve.
