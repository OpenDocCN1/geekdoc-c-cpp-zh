# Be Prepared

There are a few things you must have to want to change things now. The most important is courage. You cannot be afraid to engage in debates with managers, technical leaders, and fellow developers; discussions are unavoidable and you need to be prepared for them. You must be brave and say everything you think, no matter what. Honesty and transparency are core values for any software craftsman. You also need to believe that you will be able to convince others to do anything if they don't feel they know what you are talking about.
