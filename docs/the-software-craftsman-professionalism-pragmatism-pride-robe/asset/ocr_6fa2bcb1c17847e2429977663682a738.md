The problem with all the Agile transformations I have seen is that they were a partial transformation. Consultancies and Agile coaches were hired by companies, most often, to change their process. The Agile transformations focused on process; empowering people; reducing bureaucracy and waste; prioritization of work; and improving the flow. Effective processes work for the system. In order to achieve quality, we need to provide a good feedback loop, at all levels. This approach makes the system inefficient, compromising the entire project.

# Agile Coaches

Every profession has good and bad professionals and this is no different for Agile coaches. There are the ones who fully understand the original Agile message and when advising companies and teams on their Agile adoption, they emphasize the importance of process and technical excellence. Although the majority focus on process, they will often deal with or or replace other professionals who could help them with the technical disciplines. However the average Agile coach, due to a lack of technical knowledge, will overwhelm the process side (misleading the client) and, often will not mention the technical side at all.
