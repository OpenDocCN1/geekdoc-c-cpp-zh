# Enlightened Managers

Good managers understand that they are part of the team and need to work with the developers to the common
goal. There is no 'us' and 'them'. They must understand what the current state of the project is and work
the team to figure out what they will be able to achieve within a period of time.

Managers should appreciate when we can say no to certain features. Although that may be bad news, that
information helps avoid a major mistake. Transparency helps managers and prepare for such tasks
Raising the red flag as early as possible, indicating that things are wrong, allows all the team and people
involved to be pragmatic and up with alternative solutions. It frees people to be creative to solve the
problem. If we deliver what we promised then managers will be more inclined to trust us
when we say no.
