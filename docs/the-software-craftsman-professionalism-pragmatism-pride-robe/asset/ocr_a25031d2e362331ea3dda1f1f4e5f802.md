# So We Really Call All About That?

Besides the technical skills, software craftsman is also a role model for less-experienced developers. Software craftsmen are not always of being honest and transparent. They don't lie or deceive their customers, hiding problems to make them happy. Software craftsmen don't say what they want to; they speak their minds. Software craftsmen take responsibility for their actions and are not afraid to fight for what is best for the project.
