Save a phone interview for when it is the only option—interviewing candidates who are located in different countries

# Summary

Finding good developers is difficult and time-consuming. If we are investing time to interview, we should make it
count. Using braintesters or making the candidate look like a fool or feel inferior are good ways to make good developers
not want to work for or with you. Coding without the Internet or on a whiteboard or a piece of paper is pointless and
annoying. What we really want is a developer at the best. We want to pair with her, using good tools and
productive everything. We want to see tests architecture and code. We want to have a nice conversation and see
if we can work together well. We want to feel how it would be to work with that person.

There is a huge demand for good developers and if we want them to join our company, we must learn how to
interview them properly. We also need to remember that developers are not the only ones being interviewed. Good
developers are also interviewing companies and they will exercise their option to reject the bad ones.
