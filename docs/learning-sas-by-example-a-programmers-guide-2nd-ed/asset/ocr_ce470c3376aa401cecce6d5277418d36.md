# 15.1 Introduction

Although you can customize the output produced by PROC PRINT, there are times when you need a bit more control over the appearance of your report. PROC REPORT was developed to fit this need. Not only can you control the appearance of every column of your report, you can produce summary reports as well as detail listings. Very useful features of PROC REPORT, multiple-panel reports and text wrapping within a column, are often the deciding factor in choosing PROC REPORT over PROC PRINT.
