# 8.6 DO WHILE and DO UNTIL Statements

Instead of choosing a stopping value for an iterative DO loop, you can stop a loop when a condition is met or while a condition is true. Enter the DO UNTIL and DO WHILE statements.

Let's revisit the compound interest problem from earlier in this chapter. Instead of asking how much money you have after x years, want to know how many years you need to keep your $100 in the bank at 3.75% to double your money. The two programs solve this problem:

# Program 6.13: Demonstrating a DO UNTIL Loop

```
data Double;
  Interest = .0375;
  Total = 100;
  do until (Total ge 200);
    Year = Year + 1;
    Total = Total + Interest*Total;
    output;
  end;
  format Total dollar10.2;
run;

title "Listing of Double";
proc print data=Double noobs;
run;
```
