You can easily modify Program 14.13 to include subtotals and totals by including a SUM statement to your program, like:

# Program 14.14: Adding Totals and Subtotals to Your Listing

```
proc sort data=Learn.Sales out=Sales;
  by Region;
run;

title "Adding Totals and Subtotals to Your Listing";nproc print data=Sales label;
  by Region;
  id EmpID;
  var Totals Quantity;
  sum Quantity Totals;
  label EmpID = "Employee ID"
  TotalSales = "Number Sold";
  format Totals dollar$10.2 Quantity comma7.
run;
```
