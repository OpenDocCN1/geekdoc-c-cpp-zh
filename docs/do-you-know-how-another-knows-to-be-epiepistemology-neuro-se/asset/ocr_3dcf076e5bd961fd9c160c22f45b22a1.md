We have our unique ideas about a topic. These unique ideas are carried in our unique language structures. From our unique language structure comes our unique sentences, words and unique style of speaking on the topic. It is for this, that on the same topic, no two people will have the same sight and slant. Have you ever entered a conversation with another and after an exchange of three or four sentences, you suddenly realized that you and the other person were talking on totally different subjects?
