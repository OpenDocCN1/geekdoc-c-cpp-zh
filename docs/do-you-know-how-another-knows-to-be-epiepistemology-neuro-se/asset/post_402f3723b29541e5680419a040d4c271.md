### The Improbable and the Uncertain.

For us is the breathing space to err and to wend our way through a reality of the IMPROBABLE and the UNCERTAIN. Therefore, there are NO GUARANTEES, SURETIES or CERTAINTIES; and if this is true-to-actuality, there cannot be EXPECTATIONS and ENTITLEMENTS 23. These two holons speak to the actuality-of-life that our entire reality is IMPROBABLE and UNCERTAIN at this level. If this is so, there is no logic for:

- (a). this SHOULD BE
- (b). that SHOULD BE
- (c). this SHOULD NOT BE
- (d). that SHOULD NOT BE

Who has the Divine empowerment or the enactment in secular Law to be able to impose his SHOULD on another? We all know such a power lies in the office of a Judge, but not in simple folk such as thee and me. So, when are we going to stop using this word, SHOULD?

Thankfully, we do not live in a reality of ABSOLUTE CHAOS as we are guided by the Laws of Physics, on the one hand, and by our Holarchy of Paradigms, HOPs and our AnY9 on the other. It is for this that we live in a reality that is a challenge upon our personhood with respect to our character, lawfulness, morality and ethics, on the one hand and a constant taskmaster of the celerity of our wit and nimbleness of our mind, on the other. The alternative is a life by surety, guarantee and certainty. If this were to be so then, we assure you that it would be a life of BOREDOM.

One cannot guarantee how one will be at a party nor can one be sure and certain of what one might do or say at such a function. There are no assurances, oaths or avowals that you will behave, in a specific way, whatever the context. To accept such a fact-of-life may shake the fabric of your subjective reality. Yet, at some level, you have always known the truth of what is stated here. Who can GUARANTEE that one may not meet an accident on the road? Who has the SURETY that tonight when one goes to sleep one will wake up tomorrow morning. Who has the CERTAINTY that upon returning to one’s home it has not been burgled?
