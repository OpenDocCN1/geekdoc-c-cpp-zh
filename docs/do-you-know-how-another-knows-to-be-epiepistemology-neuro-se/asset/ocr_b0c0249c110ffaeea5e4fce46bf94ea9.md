In the above cycle, there is always a pause to it. With it pray there is a moment of calm. Then comes the next moment of perturbation or turbulence. Put simply we go from one event to another or as they say in the vernacular, life is full of its ups and downs.

All initial conditions with their perturbations will go through their cycle and end. It is from the initial conditions that one has to organize the perturbation and by it take oneself out of the initial confusion and bewilderment. Thus, every first-time new home owner, will be faced with the condition of a humongous perturbation regarding what furniture will be best for their principle bedroom, the guest rooms, the living room, the family room and the dining room. What curtains for each of the rooms, what decorations for the house, what knickknacks for the bathrooms, what soaps and toiletries for the bathrooms; and what decorations for the walls. We shall not review the matters for the kitchen, the garage and the requisites for the front and back yards.

The resolution of the perturbations cited above involves choices and differences of opinions between the principals, with respect to each individual item. Can you think about the form the exchanges might take? From the potential differences in preferences are polite exchanges to firm discussions, strong debates, angry arguments and thunderous fights. This is perturbation upon a perturbation.

For one who was taught the Theorem of Pythagoras and is tasked to prove it now, it is a tendentious perturbation. One is confused and bewildered with respect as to how to begin.
