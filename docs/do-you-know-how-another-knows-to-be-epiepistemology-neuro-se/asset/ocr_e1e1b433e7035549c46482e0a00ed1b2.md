# Chapter III: Applied Linguistics & Thinking

How we use our language is in the field of Applied Linguistics. Our thinking, and by extension our *being* and our *doings* in life, are derivatives of our Applied Linguistics. It is the view of NSP, that Applied Linguistics is a critical study for those who seek their professional futures in Religion, the Law, Medicine, Psychology, Politics and Business.

Our thinking is by language. Our language is a system. **All systems have grammars to them** to determine when and how they are actuated and how they operate. Put simply every system has its rules of governance.
