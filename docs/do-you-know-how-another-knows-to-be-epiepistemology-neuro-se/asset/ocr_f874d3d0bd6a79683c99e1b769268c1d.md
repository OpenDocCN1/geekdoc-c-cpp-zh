# # Glossary of Terms

**4-Tuple** is the term for Vision, Kinaesthetic, Audition, Olfaction.

**An^9** stands for a person’s ancillary life metrics. They include 1. default assumptions, 2. presuppositions, 3. beliefs, 4. assumptions, 5. Standards, 6. values, 7. criteria, 8. morality and 9. ethics.

**Aristotelian system of Cause and Effect** is a powerful language system that operates by a logical structure derived from its philosophy called Causality. It is also an all-encompassing life blueprint that determines the operations of all your General Paradigms and Specific Paradigms.

**Automatism** is said to be an entity that exists-in-actuality. It is never specified where its domicile is. It can take over a person, and make him do bad things. From an NSP point of view, this is utter rubbish. Yet in one case in England and a second in Canada, it was proposed as the cause for the plaintiff to do his crime. The jury accepted the argument in both cases. Each was discharged, as logically the defendant could not be held accountable or responsible for his wicked deed.
This was a reprise of the olde time argument: _The Devil made me do it_.

**Autopoiesis** is the attribute of a sentient entity that is autonomous and self-governing. It is focused upon the task to ensure its continuing power and influence in its environment. It is also alert and aware of any variable that may diminish its power and influence.

**Bodhisattva** is a person who has the insight to illume the life of another. Once, slavery was universal. Then one day a man offered to the world: _Love thy neighbour as thyself_. This idea was like a seed. As it spread and grew, it illumed humankind to see that slavery had to end. In our work, we can only help people to stop their smoking, alcoholism and addiction because we can illume them to end it.

**Bodhisattvastic** is the adjective derived from its noun.
