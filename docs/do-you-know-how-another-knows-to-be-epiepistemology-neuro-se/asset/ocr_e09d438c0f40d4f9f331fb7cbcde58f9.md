Let us use this example. We are lounging by the seaside and I see you picking your nose. Politely I ask you: "WHY are you picking your nose?" You reply: I have an itch in my nose. I riposte: "No, you were not scratching your nose. You were picking your nose. So, WHY were you doing that?" Quite honestly, if you think about it, what business is it of mine what you do with your nose? At this moment, do you not feel like screaming: Mind your own business! But this is the point. Whilst, it is your instinct to tell me: Mind your own business, in the world of the tyranny of PC, you cannot. So, you will desperately have to find some plausible story to shut me up. This is how it is today, everyone is having to come up with some plausible nonsense to palm the person off. For this, do you get the odour that PC is veering us off into a kind of ga-ga land? If what we allege here is true, do you honestly think that we are living in a cultured, educated and rational society?

People can be so automatically good at asking WHY or responding to it. Some are good at denying their feelings towards WHY or they are not aware of suppressing them. Occasionally people get their comeuppance for asking such an offensive and intrusive question. If that happens, respectfully and sincerely, all we can say to them is: "You deserve it".
