The Universe is turning on its axis but it is also on a certain trajectory. By the same structure, all the galaxies are rotating on their axis but they are also on their unique trajectories. Every solar system of every galaxy is rotating on its axis and it is on its own trajectory. There is no point in space that is constant. For this there is no fixed point that is an eternal unchanging position by which we can have a reference point. This is how everything is relative.
