In this passage and at this stage, we use the formal terminology for thinking. All thinking is by the process of our Internal Semantic Symbolic Codings, ISSC; and they are all in binary code. With binary language, exchanges can take place completely out of our awareness because it is at the speed of light. The best witness is of critical problems of high importance and significance. The scholar is utterly at a loss as to the solution. In the end, he gives up and shunts the problem to the back of his mind. Then one day, suddenly, the answer reveals itself. This is the story how Friedrich August Kerkule found the chemical structure of Benzene. In 1890, the German Chemical Society organized an elaborate appreciation to honour Kerkule for his discovery in 1865.

It is located between the Left Brain, LfB and the Right Brain, RtB. This conclusion is based on the anatomical fact that when just a portion of the cortex of the RtB is dissected away, it shows how the nerve fibres from the Corpus Callosum stream away to the LfB or of fibres arriving to the RtB from the LfB.
