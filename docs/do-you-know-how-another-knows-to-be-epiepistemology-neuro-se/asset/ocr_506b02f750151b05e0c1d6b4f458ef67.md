In this work:
All spelling will be in British English.
The male pronoun will apply to either gender.
The nominal pronoun will apply to the second author.

In the matter of examples, the number quoted will max at five in accord with our paraphrase of the Rule of Five by Richard Bandler:

+   - If you can/cannot do a thing, the first time, it is an event.
+   - If you can/cannot do a thing, the second time, it is a coincidence.
+   - If you can/cannot do a thing, the third time, it is luck.
+   - If you can/cannot do a thing, the fourth time, it is fate.
+   - If you can/cannot do a thing, the fifth, it is YOU.

Based on this, the need for 25,000 double blind trials, is just a waste of time and money.
