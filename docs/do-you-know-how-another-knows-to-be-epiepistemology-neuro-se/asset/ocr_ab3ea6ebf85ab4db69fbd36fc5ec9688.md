All events in our lives are by our living processing-in-action. However, our languaging biases all events to become mental items - words. All wars, for example, are terrible human doings in time and across time. There is never anything static about our warring. However, when we speak, we say:

+   (a). the Peloponnesian War
+   (b). Napoleonic War
+   (c). World War I
+   (d). World War II
+   (e). the American Vietnam War
+   (f). the forthcoming WW III

To speak in this manner of such events is to think and speak of them as nouns. Wars are not nouns or things. They are living activities or processes. In fact, in warring they are humongous activities or processes. To speak of them as nouns or things, we immediately think of them as inanimate; and immediately, there is automatic and concordant mental reduction and simplification of the magnitude of what is going on. When any warring is going on, there is nothing inanimate about it. There is killing, wounding, maiming, suffering and dying for both the soldier and the innocent civilian.

If there is no awareness of what a nominalization is, then it will automatically be accepted as a real thing. And this is exactly what is going on today. Now things become even more fantastic. In the reality of A, the nominalization, the noun, the reification becomes the Cause for the Effect. Once you accept this position the person is totally absolved of all responsibility for generating his feelings and his doings.
