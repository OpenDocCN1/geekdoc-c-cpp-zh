Here is an extraordinary example of human vindictiveness, malevolence, vengefulness and malice. The unfortunate Pope Formosus was disinterred and clad in his papal vestments, and seated on a throne to face all the charges from John VIII. The verdict was that the deceased had been unworthy of his pontificate. The *Damnatio memoriae*, an old judicial practice from Ancient Rome, was applied to Formosus. All his measures and acts were annulled and the orders conferred by him were declared invalid. His papal vestments were then torn from his body, the three fingers from his right hand that he had used in consecrations were cut off and the corpse was thrown into the Tiber River.
