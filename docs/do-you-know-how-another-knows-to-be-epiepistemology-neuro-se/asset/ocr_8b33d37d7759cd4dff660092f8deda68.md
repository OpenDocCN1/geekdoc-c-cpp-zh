# The Authors

Jennifer and Dennis Chong have co-shared a practice in Psychotherapy and Hypnotherapy from 1979 to the present. Over this time they inadvertently converted the practice into a research laboratory into the marvels and singularities of Ontology and subsequently Epistemology.

This led them into the fields of Applied Linguistics, Applied Philosophy, Formal Logic, Informal Logic, Non-Verbal Language, Science, Syntax, Grammar, Semantics, Religion, Systems, Structure, Human Relationships, AI, Human Communication, Fractals and Chaos Theory etc. This extended their knowledge base into the fields of, Law, Morality, Ethics, Games Theory, Plans, and Induction, etc.

Both were contributing presenters of the Ontario Society of Clinical Hypnosis, National Guild of Hypnotists and contributing authors of its journal, Hypnotism.

They are contributing workshop presenters to the International Association of Counsellors and Therapists and are contributing authors to their journal. They are workshop presenters to the International Medical and Dental Hypnotherapy Association and are contributing authors to their journal. They are contributing presenters of the American Board of Psychotherapy and to their journal.

They have lectured at various colleges and universities and in professional forums in Canada, US, England, Spain, Italy, Germany, Australia, Hong Kong and Malaysia.

Both are Fellows of the Society of Neuro-Semantics and Fellows of the American Board of Psychotherapy. Jennifer is a Fellow of the International Medical and Dental Hypnotherapy Association; and Dennis is a Diplomat of the International Medical and Dental Hypnotherapy Association. In May 2017 Dennis was inducted into the Hypnosis Hall of Fame.
