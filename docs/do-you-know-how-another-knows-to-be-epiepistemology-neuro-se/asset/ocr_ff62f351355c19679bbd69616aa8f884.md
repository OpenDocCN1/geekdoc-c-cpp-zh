If you are blind, you will be reading by Braille. In such a contingency, you will be sourcing your words by touch. If so then this will be the description of your state of consciousness:

V^/- , A^i, K^e/K^i, O^/-.

Let us consider another example. You are at the edge of Niagara Falls, Ontario. This will be the description of your consciousness:

V^e, A^e, K^i, O^e.

You will be V^e, as you are externally sourcing the sight of the Falls and A^e, the thunderous sound of the falling water and O^e, the smell of the water spray. However, your awareness will be K^i — your internal feelings of awe. It is these shifts in e or i that constitutes one's mental processing, i.e. thinking.
