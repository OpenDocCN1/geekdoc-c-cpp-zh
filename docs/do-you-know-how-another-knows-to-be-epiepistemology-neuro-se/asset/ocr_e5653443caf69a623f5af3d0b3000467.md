Living in the reality of RELATIVITY and HUMAN RELATING requires us to be constantly alert and consciously aware, in any context. We are required to be as accurate in our perception as we can be and that the quality of our meta function, our thinking, is as witty and as nimble as it can be. The price to pay if one is not, can sometimes be exacting.

An example was a man who married a woman of olde Ontario money. For the cognoscenti, this turn of phrase, old Ontario money means that the woman is incredibly-rich-by-inheritance. He never had to work again. Now at 75, she comes to see me. From the story, a matter of ethics arises in me and I put to her: I believe that my work for you may carry very serious consequences for your spouse. I wonder if you would let him know that I would like him to come to your consultation with me. She agreed. The appointment was set for the next week. To my concern, she never came back . . . until 8 weeks later. When she returned, she said: I wish to apologise to you for not returning earlier. I have spent these 8 weeks doing my best to get my husband to come. To my last effort, he riposted: I have no problems. It is you, who has them. You go to see him. I am not coming. With that, I immediately phoned for an appointment to see you. I then said to her: As my patient-in-Law, I will discharge my duty to you and I will do the best for you. This coming week-end is a 3-day piece of work that we call the Freedom Seminar. I wish you to come to it. Can you come? She replied: Yes.

She completed it. She was scheduled for her post seminar review on the following Wednesday. She never came back. I was shocked. I thought I had lost her.
