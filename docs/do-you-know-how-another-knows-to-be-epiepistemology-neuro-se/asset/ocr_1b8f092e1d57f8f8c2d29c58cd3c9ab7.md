The logical question that may have crossed your mind is, what is the epistemological hardwiring for our penchant to mass murder? Unless we know what it is and know how to cut the circuitry, we shall be doomed to our ontology of war. This is uniquely human because other species DO NOT mass murder their own kind, in the grand style that we do. The work of Jane Goodall has shown that a troop of chimps will attack another to cannibalize one or two of them for their dinner. But this is not mass murder of an entire troop. They do it for their “steak tartare”.

What is the structural basis for humans to take to war again and again despite the inspirational teachings of great human minds, minds far greater than ours? Great inspirational teachings are equivalent to sermons at great retreats that are supposedly to lead us to a good and pure life. None of these powerful and great sermons, as far as we know, have ever changed our ontological behavior in this domain.

So, in indexing our epistemological hardwiring in this area, we regretfully assure you that our end of this world is inevitable. Therefore, our ultimate goal is how to be more like other species, who we now know, are in at least one domain clearly much more advanced than we are.
