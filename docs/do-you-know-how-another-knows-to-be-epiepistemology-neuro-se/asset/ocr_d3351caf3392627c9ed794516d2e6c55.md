However, the ultimate sadness for us is that what we hoped for, the revival of Ontology, as a field of study did not happen. The task and the challenge to restore it remains. It continues in this work. We suspect that ontology is yoked to an implicate disregard and disdain of it by Science today. So, we pray this work will not be dashed on the boulders of silence¹. If we fail, we are sure, this matter will not end with us. Somewhere, sometime:
