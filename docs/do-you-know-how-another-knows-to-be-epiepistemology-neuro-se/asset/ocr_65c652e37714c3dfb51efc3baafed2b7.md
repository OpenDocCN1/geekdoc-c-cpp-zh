# (d).whom you respect

From a structural point of view, the other is an open target for your blame if he is inferior to you in station, office or power, someone who is not your equal, someone whom you do not love¹² or care for and someone whom you have no respect for. We were advised that the Nazis thought of the Jews as vermin. The fate of the Jews was, therefore, sealed by this mindset that the Nazis had for them.

In Ã, is the recognition that we do not know the other person. Therefore, in inter-relating you cannot stance from a position of worry, uncertainty, anxiety or fear. We have to begin from a disposition of courage and goodwill and the acceptance of the universal truth that we all have to clean ourselves; and this is the ultimate hallmark by which we know that we are equal with the other. If this is so, then we can only begin from a stance of respect and fearlessness. What we say here finds its corroboration in a universal analog:

> We all wipe ourselves.

We recognize that because of unique life experiences of trauma, there are people who believe that they are not worthy or that they are not good enough. This, in turn, means that they live in worry, anxiety and fear of their vulnerability to ridicule, mockery, scorn or contempt of others. Under such conditions, they relate with timidity and fear of what others may think about them. In turn, they are shy and they tend to retreat in social exchanges. What we cite here is a diagnostic for DSM-5.
