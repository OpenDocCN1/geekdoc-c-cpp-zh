+   (b). WHO is about information re persons.
+   (c). WHAT is about information re things.
+   (d). WHEN is about time.
+   (e). WHERE is about location.
+   (f). WHICH is about choice/options.
+   (g). WHOM is identity from a general set.
+   (h). WHOSE is about a specific identity.

All of us are habituated to using WHY. We accept and accommodate to its consequences with the result that we have no cognitive awareness of them. Consider this question:

> Do you know of anyone who has a Divine or legal warrant to automatically ask another to justify what he has done?

In our Law and Culture, the only place we know where you may lawfully and legitimately ask, “WHY” is in a Court of Law. As far as we know, our Constitution and Law has no provision for anyone to willy-nilly demand justification from another by WHY. Think of this instance. A reporter asked a celebrity who flew in on the Concorde into NYC, “Why have you come back?”

# Third Linguistic Holon:

# Y makes X

Across the span of one’s life, there are literally untold myriads of things that one does not understand which may include, for us:

> "WHY are you reading this book?"

Let us suppose your answer is:

> "I bought it to read because it was powerfully recommended to me to do so."

With this answer, I now understand WHY you are reading this book. However, with it I also know that:

> The powerful recommendation to read the book MADE you buy the book.
