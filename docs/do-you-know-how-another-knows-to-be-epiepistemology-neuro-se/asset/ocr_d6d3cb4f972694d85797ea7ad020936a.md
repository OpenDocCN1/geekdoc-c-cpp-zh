In a world of C→E, a.k.a. Y MAKES X, the search is for THE CAUSE of the problem. Thus, when a person is alleged to have committed a crime, all his counsel has to do is to find a plausible CAUSE that MADE him do it; if he can argue the “truth” of the CAUSE successfully, then it lifts from him all personal responsibility and accountability for his action. In other words, find what MADE him do it and there is no rational or logical basis to hold him responsible or accountable for what he, in fact, had decided to do in the first instance. The IT that MADE him do it, is the CAUSE.

What we cite above is the accepted pattern of supposedly normal logical thinking. NSP of course, is utterly unsure of its probity in logic.

Thus, many years ago, a jury in a South American trial agreed to the proposition that it was JEALOUSY that CAUSED the man to kill his wife. He was absolutely absolved of his crime. More latterly, an Edmonton jury agreed that it was AUTOMATISM that CAUSED an Alberta socialite to fire six bullets into her husband. Having found the CAUSE, she was absolutely absolved of all responsibility of her crime.
