# Orthodoxy & Being

In 1904, the German Government of South-West Africa, now known as Namibia killed off the Herero and Namaqua. In 1985, the United Nations' classified the aftermath as an attempt to exterminate the Herero and Nama peoples of South-West Africa, and, therefore, one of the earliest attempts at genocide in the 20th century. In 2004 the German government recognized and apologized for the events, but has ruled out financial compensation for the victims' descendants. In July 2015, the German government and parliament officially called the events a "genocide" and "part of a race war".

So, the question is:

> What is the embedded structure within our Orthodoxy that compels us to unfold as mass killers of our kith and kin from then, now and for all our possible tomorrows?

If we do not change in our orthodoxy, we cannot presume that there will be a tomorrow for any of us. If the structure does exist for us to become mass killers, then logically it is linked to or connected to our neurology.

All the above examples relate to religion, culture, race or political beliefs. However, our orthodoxy affects our personal problem states involving our beliefs about ourselves, others and our living experiences. Whatever the events are in our life we process the information through our Holarchies and our HOPs. Our orthodoxy, HOPs + AnY9, is at the root of our Being.

From an NSP point of view we are sure that in therapy, whilst a patient may present himself to his therapist wanting help, the orthodoxy of the patient is determined to ensure that he remains the same and continues in the way he has been and into his future. Therefore, the idea of a therapeutic alliance between a patient and the clinician to resolve a problem, is a nice idea, but, it is an oxymoron.
