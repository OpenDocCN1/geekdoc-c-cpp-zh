Question: When you killed him, did you do it impeccably well?
Answer: +.
Question: Was it a useful and worthwhile thing to kill him?
Answer: +.

At this stage, he is well on the way to being found innocent.

Question: Was it useful and worthwhile for him to be killed by you?
Answer: + to me
Question: However, his wife is a widow with three children. Was it useful worthwhile for him to be killed? Answer: —.
Question: When you killed him, was it ethical in that you did no harm to him or to yourself?
Answer: to me —, to him —.
Question: When you killed him did you do it with some love and compassion?
Answer: —. I hated him because he took the woman I loved.
Question: By killing him has it helped you to rise in your awareness?
Answer: —. If I had to, I would do it again.

For the last six negative answers, he will be found guilty; and you will note, he has condemned himself out of his own mouth.

Anyone can arrive at their own uniquely personal understanding or meaning of an item or a life situation. In other words, you can have your opinion! Mercifully, most of the time others are in the same ballpark with you. When that happens, you could not hope for better.
