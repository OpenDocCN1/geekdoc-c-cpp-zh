In other words, we are saying that our semantic structures, by actual anatomical wires known as the neurones, are attached to specific sites in our brain that contain the animalistic behavioural codes of our cave dwelling ancestors.

We have evolved to dine and eat with spoon, knife and fork. However, let us suppose we were to play a game of charades in which the chosen person is to be like one of our ancestral cave man dwellers. We have no problem to play such a role. Easily the person pushes aside any table or chair; he then roughly brushes aside all the nice crockery and cut glass on the table, rushes for and grabs the leg of lamb and tears at it with his teeth and grabs a pitcher of water to slosh it down.
