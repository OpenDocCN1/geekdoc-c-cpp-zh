# Be grateful for your lot.

However, there is an instinctive knowing that there is always someone out there, in life, who can puncture our **SELF-IMPORTANCE**, **SI**, stomp on our **SELF-ESTEEM**, **SE** and spit on our **EGO**. We all know this. For this, we know our SI, SE and EGO are, in fact, vulnerable, weak and undefendable items. So, what on earth are we hanging onto them for, when they are so worthless? **SELF-WORTH** is something that we have to earn in life.

How do we know we have earned our **SELF-WORTH**? It is very simple. We know when we ask ourselves: *Have I earned my self-worth today? Did I do the best I could?* Wait and the answer will well up in you. If it is: **Yes**, then you have the self-evidence and instinctively you will know that **NO ONE** can ever take it away from you. This is the only answer that matters. The opinions of other people about you are for the wind. As someone recently said “What you think of me is not my business.”
