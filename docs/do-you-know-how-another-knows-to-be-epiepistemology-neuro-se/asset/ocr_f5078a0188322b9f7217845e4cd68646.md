(d). So, whilst WHY as a question may appear to have a universal application, the critical question is this. Does any human being have a Divine authorization or a Legal warrant to demand JUSTIFICATION from any other human being, *ad lib*, and at any time of his fancy? We certainly know that we do not. For this, it is extra-ordinary that people keep using it, unthinkingly! We have an automaticity of kowtowing to it.

(e). Whilst we are all adapted to its use on others and its application on us, with a smile, at an unconscious level, people know that they are being required to justify and they do not like it. No one really likes this.

If you think about what we have noted above, you will realize that it is quite a rude and ill-mannered thing to do, strutting about and trotting here and there and pea shooting WHY at everyone. We have no foundation in Law and in Culture to require anyone to justify his action to us. Certainly, we are sure your God did not give you permission to do so. Yet in the world of C→E everyone is doing it, especially young children.

WHY seems to be *THE MOST NATURAL INTERROGATIVE* to use, but, in fact, it is the most unnatural of all the interrogatives. There are only two domiciles where it has its natural place. One on earth, is in a Court of Law. The other is in Heaven, when on the last day, God needs to know WHY you misused the gift of life that He gave you.

So now, we offer you the chance to vote on this. What do we really want? Is life to be by?
