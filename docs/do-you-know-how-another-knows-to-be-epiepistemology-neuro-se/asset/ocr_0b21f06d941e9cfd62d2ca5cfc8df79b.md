In the above diagram, the shift for SS to DLS is thinking. The shift from DLS to [ V K A O ] is also thinking. Thinking is a very complex process. As we go on, it becomes more and more complex. The wonderful thing is that we cannot go back to the days when thinking was apparently such a simple matter. For us the wonder is the discovery of how we do it.

All incoming atomic units of information will go to the Left Brain and the Right Brain. One of the more interesting discoveries of the late 20th Century was the Lateralization of Functions in the LfB and the RtB. It is for this that we now know that every item of incoming information will be sent to the LfB with a Time Tag, TT or Sequential Tag, ST; and those for the RtB will have a Geometric Tag, GT or Location Tag, LT. This then means that all DLSI, the mental chips of any value, will have their TT and GT. Each act of tagging is also thinking. Put in another way, each act of lateralization is a thinking step. Fortunately, this is done unconsciously for us.
