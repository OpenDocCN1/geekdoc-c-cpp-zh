# Justification versus Information

The only logical place for the pursuit of JUSTIFICATION is in an A Court of Law, where conflict issues over RIGHT and WRONG are being resolved. It is only in this context that the interrogative, WHY, finds it's true and natural place.

We all live today within the system of A. For this, in our secular life, from all our yesterdays till the present, everyone is using the language of it ad lib, from the highest personages in our society to our children. It is the most used automatic interrogative and it seems that it is OK for all and anyone, to ask WHY.

We are so inured to the unthinking, free and wanton use of WHY, that we have no awareness today that when it is put to us, we are being asked to JUSTIFY what we do! Having no awareness of what the question is really impinging on us, takes us to the very edge of the fantastic.

Today, in the reality of Ã, the Internet has taken us into the world of information. The hope is that we can access the highest quality of unbiased information. To get the information we must know how to ask questions, NOT by WHY but by HOW, WHO, WHAT, WHEN, WHERE, WHICH, WHOM and WHOSE. In a recent interaction with a student at a De-Imprinting Seminar, the student stalled when he was invited to use any of the Ã interrogatives. Thus, he could not construct a question with HOW.

Of course, there is also the presupposition that a person has a well-formed philosophy of life and a truly sound and rich knowledge base. If they do not, let us pray that they get an inclination to acquire it. Without such a base for gathering information, how can one ask good questions?
