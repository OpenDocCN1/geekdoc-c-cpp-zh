The critical question is:

Is it true that WHY has a sort of omnibus power by which it can miraculously change itself and take the place of doing what in linguistics are the natural functions of the questions of Ã?

The answer is NO!

+   (a). WHY really does not have any omnibus power to morph itself to take on the linguistic functions of any Ã interrogatives.
+   (b). The natural linguistic function of WHY is to get the information appertaining to JUSTIFICATION of any action implied by the verb in any sentence of the English language.
+   (c). In its pursuit for JUSTIFICATION it can demand it from any verb of any sentence in the English language. This is how, superficially, it seems to have a universal rational and logical place for any sentence. Say anything and do anything and I can ask you WHY? As examples: Why are you doing that? Why not?
