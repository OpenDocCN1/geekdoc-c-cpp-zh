It is the witness of our life experience that interpretations can err; now suddenly we realize how tenuous our hold of reality really is.

From this, we can derive that:

+   (a) our neuro-semantics, NS and our ontology, ON^T is a function of our perception P^N
+   (b) our neuro-semantics, NS and our ontology, ON^T is a function of our comprehension, C^N
+   (c) our comprehension is a derivative of our mf

From the above, we can derive these following mathematical equations:

```
P^N = f (NS + ON^T)
C^N = f (NS + ON^T)
C^N = f (mf)
```

Therefore:

```
P^N X C^N = (NS + ON^T) ^2 X mf
```

From this, it follows that:

```
C^N = [(NS + ON^T) ^2 X mf] / P^N
```

This is to say that our comprehension is a product of our neuro-semantics, ontology and mf divided by our perception in space.
