## We now come to the tenth logical level of A and Ã: SI, SE and Ego versus Self-Worth.

As we stated before a nominalization is a term out of the field of English Transformation Grammar. It refers to mental conversions of verbs into nouns or processes into things. Examples are:

- (a). to love or loving nominalized to Love
- (b). to dine or dining nominalized to Dinner
- (c). killing hundreds of thousands nominalized to War
- (d). to fly or flying nominalized to Flight
- (e). building a house nominalized to Construction

Clearly, the verb or the process is real-to-fact and the noun or thing is unreal-to-life. Thus, to feel Depressed is actual-to-experience and the noun Depression is an artefact-of-mind and therefore, it is unreal-to-life. There is a vast number of nominalizations, a.k.a. reifications. None of them exist-in-reality.

Every verb has its nominalization, at one stage in our NLP training, it was put to us by John Grinder:

> BE or BEING.
