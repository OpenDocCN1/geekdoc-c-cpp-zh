**multiorginal term** is a term of the English language which has a semantic place and value in a vast scan of contexts. We offer five examples:

+   (a) like: you can like a vast slew of items
+   (b) yes: you can say yes to a near infinity of items
+   (c) love: you can love a whole host of vegetables, animals, minerals, ideas and situations
+   (d) no: you can say no to any number of items
+   (e) present: you can use as a verb or as a noun and each can be used in an indefinite number of situations and to any number of people.
