# # CAUSE and EFFECT

At the logical level, Y makes X, one is functionally at CAUSE and EFFECT, a.k.a. C→E. So, if we ask: Is it true that the powerful recommendation to read the book CAUSED you to buy the book? The answer is inevitably a congruent affirmative.

On earth and in the universe, is an infinity of phenomena. Our embedded logic compels us to find their CAUSES. This is the *modus operandum* of Science. However, in life people search for what CAUSES another to do what he has done. In doing so, they are looking for the noun by which the doer need not be accountable or responsible for his actions and consequences. The noun is hailed as the initiator and doer of the deed. This kind of thinking is nonsense.
