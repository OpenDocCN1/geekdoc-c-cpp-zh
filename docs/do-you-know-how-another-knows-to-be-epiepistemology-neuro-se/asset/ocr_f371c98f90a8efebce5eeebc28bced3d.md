+   a verb into a noun
+   a process into a thing.

The verb or the process is actual-to-fact. The noun or thing is the artefact or product of the mental act of nominalizing. It is, therefore, unreal-to-fact. It is a fantasy of the mind. Incredibly, the elite and most of the population of Western nations believe that the noun or thing is real-to-life and actual-to-reality. By the sheer weight of numbers, it means the rest follow en suite. If you enter a room and even if you have no wish to dance but everyone else is, you will feel the pressure to do so.
