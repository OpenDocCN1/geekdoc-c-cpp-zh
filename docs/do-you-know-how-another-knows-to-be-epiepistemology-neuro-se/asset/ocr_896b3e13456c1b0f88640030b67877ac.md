# Test Operate Test Exit

A tote is a closed thinking/action sequence in which it is targeted to achieve an outcome. If it is not achieved, the thinking and the doing begins again until the outcome is achieved. Then, the thinking/action for the outcome stops; and the person is immediately open to begin again.

Totes can be stitched to form an enlarged tote. A waltz is a stitched set of sequences between two dancers. When the last note of the music is played, the tote is done. This is also true for a debate. It is a stitched sequence of sentences that form topical paragraphs. Then the moment comes when finally, one of the debaters delivers an oration that, to all present, it is obvious, he has demolished his opposition. The tote is done. The vote under such circumstances is a ritual gesture.
