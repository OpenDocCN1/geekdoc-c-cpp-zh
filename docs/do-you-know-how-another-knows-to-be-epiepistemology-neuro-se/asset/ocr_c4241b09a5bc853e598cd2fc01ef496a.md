Paul Watzlawick, John Weakland and Richard Fisch: CHANGE, PRINCIPLES OF PROBLEM FORMATION and PROBLEM RESOLUTION, W. W. Norton and Co., 1974, page 79.

These authors had the forthrightness to admit that "... this metalanguage is not necessarily available." Then it was as if the hand of Fate intervened to correct this situation. A year later after the publication of CHANGE, RB&JG, in 1975, published their work, Structure of Magic, SOM. In it was mapped out the first metalanguage which they named the Meta Model, 2M.

In its day, 2M, was the most powerful gathering information tool. By it, you could learn to understand a person, and derive where he was coming from.25 To possess a proficiency over 2M entailed changes in our ontology. The premier difference was our improved confidence as we approached people and their problem states. However, in time, we determined that there were certain structural flaws in the 2M. The main one was that 2M was initially created in Cause and Effect A.
