## We now come to the fifth logical levels of A and Ã:

### Should versus Improbable and Uncertain

This entire world operates by the universal thinking structure of C→E; and because every E is an inevitability of its C, this means that we live in a condition of human CERTAINTY. As there is a near infinity of C→E relationships, by this structure, we can naturally and logically conclude that we live in A REALITY OF CERTAINTY! It logically follows that this allows us to have our SHOULDs. It is from our SHOULDs that now flows our entire gamut of EXPECTATIONS and ENTITLEMENTS.

However, as we know, nothing in life is guaranteed, certain or assured. One event follows another event and there is a possibility of an outcome that appears to be certain but it is not always so. We cannot escape from the contradiction that this world is also replete with the UNKNOWN. We cannot even assure ourselves of how we shall behave at a party nor can we be sure how another will respond to us.

Once the space shuttle Challenger took off, it seemed another NASA success and then disaster came upon it. If ever anyone needed the evidence that in life there are no guarantees, sureties or certainties, what we cite here is the ultimate proof. And it is one that is buttressed by the near pandemic numbers of divorces, lethal road traffic accidents and killings.

Such is the fear of this world’s uncertainties, improbabilities and unknowns, that it will be no surprise that many a rich person can and has opted to live a life of a recluse. They can do so in their estates, mansions and gated communities. The billionaire, Howard Hughes was one example. We believe that one of the Vanderbilts and one of the Rothschilds were in a similar vein.

Certainty is the product of the structural thinking pattern of C→E. The corollary of any event’s certainty is that you can anticipate it. Out of this certainty is born either our SHOULD BE or SHOULD NOT BE. In my naïve youth, I thought everyone was like me: Punctuality is a hallmark of character. In my SHOULD without realizing it, was my EXPECTATION of the other to be ON TIME for any appointment. Now, if you just think on it for one moment: Are you not like me too, full right up to your attic with EXPECTATIONS. But, and this is a huge BUT, how on earth can you have even one teeny weeny EXPECTATION when the reality we live in has no guarantees, sureties or certainties. NSP is sure that to have even one EXPECTATION is a mark of insanity. Do you think this physical phenomenon is included in the Diagnostics of DSM-5? Of course not!
