Consider this sentence: I was walking on the Kelso Conservation Trail in the Niagara Escarpment. You can understand it. And because you understand it, I ask you now: What time of the day were you thinking I was on my trail walk. With your answer, you are affirming your understanding of the sentence that came from your visual tuple in which you saw me do the walk, at that time and it most certainly was not at 3.00 AM in February, at the height of a Canadian mid-winter. It was not because in your picture, the leaves of the trees along the trail were all green.
