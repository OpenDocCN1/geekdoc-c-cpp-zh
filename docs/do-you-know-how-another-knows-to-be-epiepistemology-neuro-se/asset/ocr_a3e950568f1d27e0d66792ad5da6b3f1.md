In time, we realized that there was a class of holarchies that were personal and unique to an individual. We named them the Specific Holarchies, SHs. They include Beliefs, Values and Criteria. Here are five examples:

+   (a). I must be sure no one makes a fool of me.
+   (b). Politicians are liars.
+   (c). Women are against me.
+   (d). Men are worthless.
+   (e). I am worthless

We concluded that all the General Holarchies and Specific Holarchies were under the governance of a Meta Holarchy, MH. This MH was the Aristotelian system of Cause and Effect, A, C→E.

There is an alternative MH. It is the Non-Aristotelian system of Relativity and Relatedness, Ã.
