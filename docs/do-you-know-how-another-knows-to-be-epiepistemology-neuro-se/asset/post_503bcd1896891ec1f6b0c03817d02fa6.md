**First Order Change, IOC**, is where the behaviour or problem remains the same. It involves coping with the situation or talking about it. As an example of First Order Change, involving a room you can:

- (a) move the chesterfield from one location to another
- (b) rearrange the chairs and tables differently
- (c) re-site the location of the television
- (d) change the fireplace into an automatic gas one
- (e) relocate the pictures on the wall to new places.

Despite all the changes the room is essentially still the same. For it to have the status of Second Order Change one might convert it into a sauna or hot tub.
