The study of Geometrodynamics or cosmology, since the launch of the Hubble telescope, has revealed the unbelievable marvels of our universe. It continues to unveil new phenomena that require the review of existing understandings of this cosmos. Thus, it has been discovered that black holes have firewalls around them. The mathematics of these firewalls have yet to be discovered.

Currently, there is no all-encompassing mathematical understanding of this universe that is carved in stone.14 It is sometimes somewhat disturbing to those who study .them because it may mean a volte-face re some treasured sub-theory of Cosmology. Every volte-face to an astrophysicist will be like an intellectual earthquake or a tsunami. No one likes such events.
