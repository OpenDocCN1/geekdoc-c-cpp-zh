## Fifth Linguistic Holon:

### SHOULD BE or SHOULD NOT BE

(+) (-)

When we had worked out the top 4 logical linguistic holons or language structures of A, we questioned:

*What would be the logical circumstances for a person to ask WHY?*
or
*When would a person know that he has to ask WHY?*
or
*How does a person know when he must ask WHY?*

The above questions are not the same as this question:

*What purpose would one use WHY for?*

This question is about intentionality.

The answer to the original questions above, finally came to us, after some time. It had to do with the structural issue as to whether an event SHOULD BE or SHOULD NOT BE and whether one understands it (+) or not, (-). Thus:

- (a) If Y, by your value system is a SHOULD BE and you understand it, (+), there is no basis to ask WHY.
- (b) If Y, by your value system, is something that SHOULD BE, but you do not understand, (-), you will ask WHY. Thus, when you were a child, your parents were doing things such as having to go away on business but you did not understand. For this, you asked WHY they were leaving you.
- (c) If Y, by your value system, is something that SHOULD NOT BE but (+), you will ask WHY. Later on, in your life, you concluded that there were things your parents were doing that you felt they should not be doing, such as getting divorced, even though you understood their actions because of their arguments. You then asked them, WHY.
- (d) If Y, by your value system, is something that SHOULD NOT BE and it is for you (-), you will ask WHY. There are many things that happen in life, that you most certainly feel that they should not happen and you do not understand them. You will certainly ask, WHY.

SHOULD as a word used to fall into a category of English words known as the Imperatives of the English language. They included these other words, MUST, HAVE TO, REQUIRED, IMPERATIVE and OBLIGATORY. Today all these words are said to belong to a class of words known as Modal Operators of Necessity, MONS, as opposed to Modal Operators of Possibility, MOPS.

Each MON carries its EXPECTATIONS. Thus: People SHOULD be on time, carries my expectation that the other be on time. If they are not, they deny my ENTITLEMENT not to be kept waiting.

It is a witness of experience that we live in the reality of the unknown in which there are no guarantees, sureties or certainties. Any driver in Ontario would wish there were guarantees, sureties and certainties when driving on the 401 Highway. In such a condition, is it at all logical or tenable to have EXPECTATIONS?. In Canada, do you know of any Parliamentary enactments in Law that give us ENTITLEMENTS? From an NSP point of view, it is futile and illogical to live by our MONs.

Now, to continue, if by your value system something SHOULD BE, but you do not understand it, (-), then you will ask WHY. You do so because you want the REASON, the EXPLANATION, RATIONALIZATION, the JUSTIFICATION or the EXCUSE⁸ for it. For this, you automatically know you need to use the question WHY. The powerful value of WHY is:

If you do not understand X, WHY gets you what you need.

So, for example, we are pleased that you have ownership of this book. However, let us say you are a famous and very busy astrophysicist holding positions in the Universities of Toronto, Harvard and Oxford. You really do not have the time to spend reading our book. From our point of view, respectfully, you SHOULD NOT be reading our book because of your university obligations. So now, can you feel the WHY question trundling up the railway line to you from us? It arrives! “WHY are you reading this work?” You answer, “I would have dropped it, if I could have done so, but I find it interesting and intriguing and oddly, personally enriching.” Now we have closure!

There is a whole host of things that SHOULD NOT BE and we certainly do not understand them, (-). Our worst example was the case of a van travelling along our major highway. A man in the van flung a child out of the window. Thankfully, he was caught and mercifully the infant was found uninjured, alive and well. When I read about this I did not understand it and for me it most certainly was something that SHOULD NOT BE. I was extremely angry at this monster. So:

## Sixth Linguistic Holon:

### RIGHT and WRONG

At this point in our language exploration of A, the question came to us.

How does a thing become a SHOULD BE and another a SHOULD NOT BE?

Without the accidental stumbling into the answer, we were initially in a bewildering state. You can imagine the time it took to find the answer. One day it suddenly came:

- (a) X is one that SHOULD BE because it is RIGHT
- (b) Y is one that SHOULD NOT BE because it is WRONG

Of course, it seems so simple now as you read it; but it was very different for us back then when we did not know it. In passing, we have come to realize that notwithstanding our years of labour to unravel the entirety of A, we have come to see that:

C→E as a system is incredibly and extraordinarily simplistic.

It is a very simple matter to see a SHOULD BE can only BE if it was RIGHT; and any WRONG was something that SHOULD NOT BE. And this was regardless of whether you understood it, (+) or not, (-).
