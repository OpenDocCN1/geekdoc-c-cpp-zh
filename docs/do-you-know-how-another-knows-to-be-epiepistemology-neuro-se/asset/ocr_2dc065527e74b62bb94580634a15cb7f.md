A man and his wife were desperate for their son who was 18 years old. He would not consider school and yet he was losing one job after another. They were desperate for the answer WHY? I told them the question was a futile one and gave them the following five examples beginning with a journalist asking a celebrity who was flying into NYC.

+   - <u>What</u> plans do you have for this visit?
+   - **WHY** is my son just hanging around?
+   - <u>What</u> hopes does your son have for his future?
+   - **WHY** are you shouting at your wife?
+   - <u>How</u> might you resolve your wife's concerns?
+   - **WHY** did you marry him?
+   - <u>When</u> you decided to marry him, what plans did you have?
+   - **WHY** did you have another child?
+   - <u>Whose</u> decision was it to have another child?
