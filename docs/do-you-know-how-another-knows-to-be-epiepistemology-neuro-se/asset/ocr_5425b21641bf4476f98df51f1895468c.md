What then is HUMILITY? It is when you know that you are as one with the other. Both you and everyone else is on the same boat. On this boat, there are no first-class passengers; and neither are there any third-class passengers.
Before God, we are all the same.

It is a maxim of NSP:

> Pride is the road to AGGRESSION.
> Humility is the road to POWER.

This may have been said by someone else. If this is so, we defer to this person. However, we have never come across this couplet anywhere in our reading.

It is only with POWER that we can live and fulfill the truth of our lives. Implicate to all this is about the CHARACTER of a person. Does he have a sound and well-formed CHARACTER? Or as Mrs. Bucket of Keeping up with Appearances said of one person, that he was: as weak as water. NSP is sure that no one can live being as weak as water.

There is no one who has either a divine injunction or a Parliamentary Warrant who HAS TO look after or support another. We only have our life and we have to live it. No one is going to do it for us. However, you might know of families in which there are children who even as adults, remain living at home and have never been out to do a day's work or to earn one cent. The quality of life for them is diminished by the love these parents claim they have for them.
