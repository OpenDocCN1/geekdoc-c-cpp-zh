All thinking is ABOUT self, others and things, and where things may be physical or abstract; or it may be ABOUT a context/situation such as how was the party you put on, for your family and friends.

4. The thinking ABOUT anything will result in thinking products that fall into any of these following categories:

| opinions | interpretations | views |
| ideas | evaluations | self-evaluations |
| judgements | determinations | conclusions |

Each category, e.g. an opinion, is ABOUT some person, thing or idea. Whatever is the ABOUT of another is by definition, meta to it. This is to say it is above it. It is for this that NSP concluded that the faculty of thinking is by the power of the meta function, mf.

What is the meta function? In Medicine, it is known as the ability to abstract. In Psychology, it is known as the Global Function. It is the faculty by which we can go above a topic of consideration and get a sight of it by which we can derive an abstraction, opinion or interpretation ABOUT it. At this logical level of consideration, the ability to think is about the ability to arrive at a transderivation about self, other and whatever is before us. The power of the meta function exists in our mind/brains and in our being/body.

5. The thinking ABOUT anything can never be the thing itself. This is a fundamental maxim of General Semantics. We utterly agree with it and so will you, when you reflect on it.

It also follows that one's talk ABOUT anything can never be the thing itself. It is critical to remember that when you are awed before a powerful charismatic presenter who is rendering his delivery ABOUT a topic, what he says can never be the thing itself.
