# What is a human entity that is HOPs-less?

When our first grandson was born, biologically, his brains, Left and Right, were sound and intact. However, in those first few days, it was obvious to all that his brains had nothing and he knew nothing.

In time, he began to acquire information and data and above all to recognize distinctions. The first main ones were “mama” and “dada” followed by “nana’ and “popa”. What is a computer if it has no software to it? What we were witnessing was the inputting into him by his parents, his brain’s software, his HOPs. In this way, his brains by their operations were manifesting the development of his mind. He is now ten and well on the way to having an adult mind. So, a HOPs-less person is one without a mind.

In a brain injury, it can happen that your HOPs may be stalled in its operations. In this instance, you will be amnesic. In certain instances, it can be erased and one can be like the person, described in the film, Regarding Henry, starring Harrison Ford. Everything he had in his mind was wiped out. Henry, knew nothing, not even who he was, what to do, where he was, when the time was. . . EVERYTHING. He had NOTHING in his mind.

In such a condition, you could not put to him the question, WHY. Such logical constraints in the reality of A, would have no meaning for him. But it has for us as, by our HOPs, we are constantly aware and alert as to when it will be required of us to JUSTIFY. We do not have to tell you what happens if we fail to do so. And this, in a very natural way, takes us to the Eighth Linguistic Holon.
