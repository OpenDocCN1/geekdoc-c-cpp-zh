If you want to know about yourself or others, this work is for you. It is for everyone.

As you read this work it will speak to you about a new way of thinking and being. It will open your eyes to a new approach to life situations.

You may not know that when you are:

- 1. at a cocktail party
- 2. attending a family gathering
- 3. meeting friends in a bar
- 4. on a flight to London in the first-class section
- 5. playing with one's grandchildren

that unconsciously you automatically know to know-to-be and so, you do not have to worry about how to be.

In other words, you do not have to work out how to be in any context of life, whether they are normal social situations or stressful professional ones. The fabric of our subjective reality and the structure of our subjective experience is somehow worked out for us.

At a deeper level, it is as if there is a psychological machine that works for us to produce our necessary way of being in any context.

This work is about the exploration of how each of us know to be, known as ontology-to-context. It is also about how another knows how you know-to-be, known as Epiepistemology. It is about how a therapist may use this knowledge to help others.

In this work, we invite you to learn about yourself and others by exploring your ontology.

## Origins

Everything begins somewhere. So, what and where are the origins of Neuro-Semantic Programming?

For us in Western Civilization, it began some 2600 years ago, when ancient Greek philosophers became interested in the physical phenomena about how a person knows-to-be a nail biter, hair puller, Bi-polar etc. They were therefore, exploring the nature of being. Their exploration would have sought the answers to these questions:

- (a) what constitutes the fabric/structure of subjective reality/experience?
- (b) how does one know-to unfold it in life?

They named their field of research:
ONTOLOGY.

The ancient Greek philosophers were to see that with just this ontological faculty, a human being would sit and luxuriate his life away in his ontological fantasies. They concluded that a person had to have this added faculty:
to-know-to know to be.

With this faculty, he would go out into life and express his ontology to get his needs, wants and desires. This opened a new field of study for them. They named it:
EPISTEMOLOGY.

Anyone would think that Ontology and Epistemology would be significant fields of study. Sadly, the evidence is that both have fallen from the high towers of contemporary academia. As a medical student of the 1960s, I was never taught the subject of Ontology. From our sources, we know it is not a subject that is taught in Medicine and Psychology as recent as 2016.

In our respective professional areas of focus, Psychotherapy and Hypnotherapy, it is clear to us that both are critical subjects. But, there are no critical papers or notices of any outstanding discoveries from ontological and epistemological research. The only exception was the time, between 1975 and the late 1980s, when the field of Neuro-Linguistic Programming, NLP, shared with the world its incredible ontological discoveries. They included:

- (a) structure of thinking
- (b) the nature of human awareness
- (c) the nature of human consciousness
- (d) how to calibrate
- (e) anchoring states
- (f) collapsing contrapuntal states
- (g) how to work out human strategies
- (h) how to alter the syntax of strategies
- (i) reframing
- (j) Manoeuvre of Change History.
- (k) Flying Phobia Cure
- (l) extension of (k) to cure for fear of insects and snakes
- (m) the definition of Hypnosis
- (n) the nature of hypnotic language
- (o) the concepts of linguistic well-formedness and ill-formedness
- (p) discovery of the first metalanguage, the Meta Model
- (q) etc.

All the above are of inestimable value. As a source of new ontological discoveries, it sadly, has ended. We wished that the founders of NLP might have been given the Nobel Prize for their research.

Perhaps in passing the public and politicians may wonder, to the Professions, what is happening that Ontology and Epistemology are not taught to students of Medicine and Psychology.

Then, in the early 1980s, a compelling question unfolded in us:

Can another know how a person knows-to-know-to-be.
