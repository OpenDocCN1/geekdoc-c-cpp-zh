# Twelfth Linguistic Holon:

# WE LIVE IN A MAD, MAD, MAD, MAD WORLD

We now come to the last logical holon of A. As a turn of phraseology, calling it a MAD, MAD, MAD, MAD WORLD was not our creation. It was first invented for the title of a 1963 film, directed by Stanley Kramer.

This twelfth Holon can only be true-to-life if its antecedent is, in fact, a universal style of life. What it means is that we live in a world in which the maxim of life is to disown the mantle of SELF-RESPONSIBLE AUTHORITY to live our lives. It is this that Western politicians are living exemplars of. Their creation of the Welfare State in Britain and in the Nordic states speak to this truth. The motif was the introduction of State Care which would be given to its citizenry from the cradle to the grave. The citizen did not have to take any responsibility for anything. The State was the All Knowing, the All Seeing, the All Understanding, the All Giving and All Providing.

In time, they were to discover that the state could not afford it. So, there is now a tilt to go back to basics.
