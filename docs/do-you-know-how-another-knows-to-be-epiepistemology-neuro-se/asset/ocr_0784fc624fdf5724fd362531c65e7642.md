What does this expression Y is a function of X, mean? It means that Y and X are two variables whose values change in tandem with each other. Every school boy knows this relationship, C = 2 π r. The value of C, the circumference of a circle changes as a function of the change in the value of the radius by a constant/factor of 2 π. The value of the radius DOES NOT MAKE OR CAUSE THE VALUE OF THE CIRCUMFERENCE or vice versa. Put simply as r increases, then C increases in value.

The Santiago Theory of Humberto Maturana, HM, and Francisco Varella, FV 6 describes a husband and wife to be in a socially structured relationship. NSP speaks of a husband and wife relationship as a conjugated relationship7. As the husband becomes more agitated and irate, the wife responds by feeling worse and worse. When the husband is more and more amorous, the wife responds by feeling happier and happier. Of course, if the husband suddenly explodes, the wife’s response is one of sudden alarm and helplessness. In structured or conjugated relationships, one party cannot make or cause another to feel the way they do. What and how they feel is their style of choice to respond to the context that they are in.
