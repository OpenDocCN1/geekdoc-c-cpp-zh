# Thinking by Being/Body

At one logical level, our **subjective experience** speaks to our emotions. For whatever is a given emotion, **it is our human state**. There are unique physiological metrics for it. These physiological metrics are reflected in the biochemistry of the blood. The relevant values of them, therefore, constitute the unique description of the given human state.

However, at the point when a unique emotion unfolds in a subject, the emotion has its characteristic physiological values. But they deviate from physiological norms. This instantly results in the activation of the body’s homeostatic mechanisms. The result is all deviations of the emotion are immediately rebalanced to the body’s norms. Consider this. If a man were to instantly flare into fury, his blood pressure and cardiac rate and respiration will soar up to the ceiling. This can only happen if his adrenal gland goes into overdrive. For this, his adrenocorticotrophic hormonal levels will soar; and then his Adrenaline blood level will be off the normal scale. The Wisdom of the Body will not allow this to be and in activating its homeostatic mechanisms, it will bring all these distorted levels back to their normal range as fast as possible. For this therefore, it is next to impossible to get a blood sample, that we can analyse, that will give us the actual description of a given emotion. The result is that we can never have the biometrics that are true for a genuine Depression or fury.

NSP proposes that the one way around this problem is to put a person into a trance state. Then to deepen it to the Esdaile State. In the Esdaile State, one can evince the problem ontology. And when it unfolds, take his blood sample and check its biometrics. For a true research scientist, he will instantly see that we are indexing a critically new domain of research.
