### Respect and Fearlessness

This is not a work on the physical phenomena of human RESPECT and dis-RESPECT. For this book, the question that we need to put is:

What is the necessary condition for an exchange to be mutually respectful?

It is when two people accept and value each other as equals. This is next to impossible when you know one of the two is sure he is MORE RIGHT and CERTAIN on the one hand; and on the other, is sure he is the BIG ME and the GREAT I. In such a situation, these two persons are not accepting each other as equals.

Yet, the fact of actuality is that we are all equal with each other, and that speaks to the truth of creation. No human being that was, is or ever will be, is born with the Divine Warrant that states:

> I, God hereby decree that XYZ is born superior and more exalted than any other human being.
> God
> Signature

Correspondingly, there is no human being here in Canada that carries this parliamentary enactment, stating:

> By this Act of Parliament, any Canadian who will not kowtow to you, XYZ, will be deemed to have committed a criminal offense punishable by 3 months incarceration at Her Majesty's pleasure.

John Smith
Prime Minister of Canada

What is the evidence that we are equal? The evidence is that:

- (a). we all must pay taxes
- (b). we all have to breathe each other's CO₂
- (c). we all need to clean ourselves.

However, the reality of this world is that there are people who, de novo believe that they are superior and exalted over others. This is a serious matter as everything has an origin. So, who or what planted the seed of superiority into a child or person from which grew the attitude of superiority and exaltedness over all others? In and of itself this is not the basis for any problem. The problem, comes about when, as an adult in the work force, he holds a position in which he is given the necessary power to discharge the functions of his office. What is the evidence for this? When you are to go into the Ontario licensing bureau to renew your license, you have to face those who are paid to serve you. Everyone has to be so nice to them.

Consider these next five examples:

- (a). Governor General of Canada
- (b). Prime Minister of Canada
- (c). Chief of Police
- (d). Air Commodore of Air Canada
- (e). Head Nurse of ICU.

The respective office holders cited above, all have this in common. They have POWER over their subordinates. This condition is inevitable because the grammar of any hierarchical system requires this. Those who are under those above them are to OBEY; and this is for the good of the operation of the system.

However, it is open to abuse. This is certainly true in the world of C→E. It is for this that a hierarchical system risks losing the potential creative contribution of subordinates. In the world of NSP we are sure that no hierarchical system can afford such a loss.

It is essential to have a meta system in which one can be aware of such possibilities; and a system of penalties be clearly delineated for such violations. Currently, the corrective mechanism to abuse is by whistleblowing. And wisely, the English have legalized it.

To have people OBEY you is heady wine; and this can warp a person’s ontology. In A, the sceptre of power is the:

- (a). ability and facility to blame the other
- (b). the power to sack the underling

He, who is blamed, will feel diminished and inferior. If he is flexible, he will learn to back down well and artistically. If he is rigid and inflexible, he will be sacked. If he does not want that outcome, then with seething resentment he will back down inelegantly and tastelessly. Thereafter, he will be marked and promotions will pass him by. In and of itself, people need to play the game of the system: to wit, TO OBEY and to do it well.

The meta problem is when people start to believe that they are truly inferior; they automatically look up to the blamer as a superior being. This ontology carries over to other negative ontological consequences that compound themselves. In the instance of a marriage the spouse now stances down:

- (a). Yes, darling, you have the right to walk all over me.
- (b). It is you I truly love because I worship you.
- (c). For you I do everything and anything you require.
- (d). Everything and anything will be as you wish.
- (e). If it pleases you, I hope that you acknowledge that I exist.

This is what it means to be a people pleaser or to do a Satir Stance 1 to another.
