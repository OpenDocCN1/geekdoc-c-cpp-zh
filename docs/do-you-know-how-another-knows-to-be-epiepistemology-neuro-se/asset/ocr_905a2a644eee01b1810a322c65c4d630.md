Now we come to the phenomenon of the cybernetic loop. Emotions or human feelings are products of human thinking. Emotions drive human metam entation by layering more metas on top of the base emotion. As an example, consider this. In a given context, the product of one’s evaluation is to feel angry at another. The next meta step is to feel that he is an idiot. The next is to conclude that he is a stupid idiot . . . and so the layering of thinking products can go on and on. They are driven by the base feeling.
