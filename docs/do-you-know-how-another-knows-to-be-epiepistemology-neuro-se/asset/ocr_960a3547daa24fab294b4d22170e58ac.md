When they make up and take you out for a dinner, your ontological change is one of happiness and it is a function of their ontological reconciliation. These changes can be described in equations or formulae.

For example, a thing can change in its value. Some do so extremely slowly. Thus, if you put a slab of granite inside a cavern all its atoms will be in atomic Brownian motion. One day one of its atoms will collide with another and it will be expelled from the general mass of that slab of granite; and it will never return. The mass of the granite has decreased by one atom. It will take eons of time and one day that slab of granite will be no more. This is a fact of Physics. Therefore, if there were no processes of wind and rain erosion, the pyramids would obey the Law of Entropy, S = k log W, and they would be no more one day.

For this we can distinguish these changes; and we can see how these changes have relationships with each other and with us.
