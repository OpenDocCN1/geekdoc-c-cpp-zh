# Introduction

This work:
Neuro-Semantic Programming, NSP,
is about a great story. The story is a part of Western Civilization. We note the work of the ancient Egyptians1 in the study of human ontology that was reflected in this quote in the text of the ancient Israelites who were ex-slaves of Pharaoh in their sacred text, the Torah:

> As a man thinketh in his heart so is he.

We acknowledge the critical pioneering work of the ancient Greek philosophers in their enquiries into human Ontology and above all, Epistemology.

We salute the heroic efforts of Robert Dilts, John Grinder, Richard Bandler, Leslie C. Bandler, and Judith DeLozier to revive the study of Ontology.

This is the genealogical track of the development of NSP:

+   - Ontology ... 515 BC. Parmenides et alia
+   - Epistemology ... other ancient Greek philosophers
+   - General Semantics ... 1933 Alfred Korzybski
+   - Neuro-Linguistic Programming .. 1975 Dilts, Grinder, Bandler, Bandler, DeLozier
+   - Neuro-Semantics ... 1980 Hall
+   - Neuro-Semantic Programming ... 2017 Chong and Chong

Above all it acknowledges the mystery of the lapse in the study of Ontology and Epistemology.

1 the ancient Egyptians: They were a great famed Mediterranean power with links to ancient Greece. Herodotus went to Egypt and in his Histories, records the wonders of it.
