To speak is to generate words in strings known as sentences. For this it is known as Generation. Whatever we generate, is by and through our DLSI. What is now clear is that all our DLSI are collated within the language structure of our HOPs. When we speak or write, all the words for our sentences are sourced from the language structure of the topic10.

Therefore, for the semantic topic within his language structure of Yorkshire pudding, will be all the person’s entire vocabulary for all possible sentences he may wish to use to participate in a discursive exchange on this subject. There will be sentences drawn from the files of, temperature, quantity, volume etc. with respect to his recipe. With aficionados on this subject, these can be very critical matters for serious debate. There will be sentences on whether to use oil or shortening and the consequences for using one and not the other; and so forth.

This, in turn, means that there is a distinction between the language structures versus our languaging. In other words, what we utter when we speak is not the same as the fountain head from which we source our words.
