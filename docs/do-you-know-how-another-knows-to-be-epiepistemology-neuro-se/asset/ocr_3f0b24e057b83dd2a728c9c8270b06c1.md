The meta problem is when people start to believe that they are truly inferior; they automatically look up to the blamer as a superior being. This ontology carries over to other negative ontological consequences that compound themselves. In the instance of a marriage the spouse now stances down:

+   (a). Yes, darling, you have the right to walk all over me.
+   (b). It is you I truly love because I worship you.
+   (c). For you I do everything and anything you require.
+   (d). Everything and anything will be as you wish.
+   (e). If it pleases you, I hope that you acknowledge that I exist.

This is what it means to be a people pleaser or to do a Satir Stance 1 to another. We recommend you get this work, People Making by Virginia Satir in which you will find the critical information relating to her concept of analog semantic stances, I, II, III, IV and V. With this information, your understanding of Ontology will expand.

The opposite, or the meta-meta downside, is when the person believes that he is indeed superior, an exalted being. Now, with power, he is a super-alpha ape. To be like a monkey you have to think like a monkey. How does a monkey think, especially the top monkey of the troop? This is how he thinks:

+   (a). Everyone under me, is below me.
+   (b). Everyone and everything is mine.
+   (c). Everything that I want, I want it now.
+   (d). I can do anything I wish.
+   (e). I will CRUSH everyone who opposes me.

The interesting thing is that a monkey cannot think like a human being but we can think like a monkey.
