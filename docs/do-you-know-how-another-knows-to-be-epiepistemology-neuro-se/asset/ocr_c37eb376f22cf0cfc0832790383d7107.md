We all live our lives by RIGHT and WRONG, and what or how Y SHOULD BE or NOT BE. RIGHT and WRONG are multi-ordinal terms. For this, they have a universal application across an infinity of semantic contexts. It can subtend the arc between being WRONG to pass wind at a private dinner, to being ever so RIGHT as to pay out over a million dollars to push for one's cause to have a person declared a saint, by the Roman Catholic Church. RIGHT and WRONG open our lives to an infinite vista in scope and range of considerations in the semantic domains and subdomains of Civilization, Culture, Law, Society, Politics, Religion, Science, Economics and Commerce etc. There is no semantic domain in which RIGHT and WRONG has no say.

However, there are two significant problems with RIGHT and WRONG. The first is a problem that everyone has missed. It is that:

It has no universality in space/time.

In other words, what may be RIGHT for one person may not be RIGHT for another. If it has no universality, you can infer RIGHT and WRONG has no Divine roots nor are they underpinned by any Laws of Physics. If that is so, then we are dealing with a MAN-MADE THING. It means that everything about RIGHT and WRONG is idiosyncratic and artificial.

The history of the world is bestrewn with the evidence that it is indeed MAN-MADE. What is today utterly impermissible in Law in Saudi Arabia, to wit drinking alcohol, is lawful in the West. What is permissible in Law in the West, such as kissing scenes in films is verboten in Bollywood films from Mumbai, India. Once upon a time, in Japan, seppuku was a requisite if you dishonoured yourself, a custom that had its parallel in ancient Roman and Greek culture. This is not permitted today by the Law of the West.
