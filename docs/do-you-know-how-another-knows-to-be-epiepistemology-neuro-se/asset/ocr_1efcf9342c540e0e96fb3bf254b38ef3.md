We are in the era of the new physics of Relativity and Relatedness. We are to accept the inevitable problems intrinsic to our lot, in the natural order of things. In the context of this Universe and the astounding things that are in it such is our fate. These are things that our mathematics help us to understand; but there are those unknowns that our mathematics have yet to work out for us. Today2017, the grayst of subjects concern Dark Matter, on the one hand, and on the other is whether we are alone in this Universe.

It is such a unique privilege to live in this era of time.33 It is one in which men and women of High Science have the automatic humility to admit:

+   (a). I do not know.
+   (b). I have a theory and a conclusion. Please prove that I am in error.

By and in: I do not know, is the willingness to commit oneself to find out. At one level, we do not live in fear of having a contrarian view. We recall that Einstein was a simple factor of the Swiss Patent Office. From that humble position, he wrote his paper, Special Relativity with the incredible conclusion that light does not travel in straight lines. This was heresy at that time! It defied the finding of Euclid that light travels in straight lines. The German scientific establishment of the time held their peace on this
