# (d) Meta-meta-meta . . . feelings or emotions:

From the work of Michael Hall, the father of Neuro-Semantics, was his sight that we can have feelings-about-feelings; and then, we can have feelings about feelings-about-feelings . . . and onto feelings-about-feelings-about-feelings-and-so-on. It was Michael’s finding that when this happens, the final feeling escapes its Lorentzian space/time constraints. The result is that the person knows to feel what he feels but NOT what it is about.

You would think that for such a crippling emotion, the person can answer the question:

> What are you depressed about?

Invariably, the bewildering response is:

> I don’t know.

If a Psychiatrist or Psychologist is not aware of Hall’s work, he will conclude that the condition of Depression is now a Primary emotion of psychopathological-aetiology that exists in and of itself. This means that it is an island unto itself. From Hall’s work this conclusion is not valid. By First Principle the emotion has to have a root source.
