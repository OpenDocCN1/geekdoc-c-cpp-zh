Now, to continue, if by your value system something SHOULD BE, but you do not understand it, (-), then you will ask WHY. You do so because you want the REASON, the EXPLANATION, RATIONALIZATION, the JUSTIFICATION or the EXCUSE⁸ for it. For this, you automatically know you need to use the question WHY. The powerful value of WHY is:

If you do not understand X, WHY gets you what you need.

So, for example, we are pleased that you have ownership of this book. However, let us say you are a famous and very busy astrophysicist holding positions in the Universities of Toronto, Harvard and Oxford. You really do not have the time to spend reading our book. From our point of view, respectfully, you SHOULD NOT be reading our book because of your university obligations. So now, can you feel the WHY question trundling up the railway line to you from us? It arrives! “WHY are you reading this work?” You answer, “I would have dropped it, if I could have done so, but I find it interesting and intriguing and oddly, personally enriching.” Now we have closure!
