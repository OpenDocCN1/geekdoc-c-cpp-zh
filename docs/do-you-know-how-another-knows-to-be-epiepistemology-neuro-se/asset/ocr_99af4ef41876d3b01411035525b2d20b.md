# # The Journey of Discovery

We were always taught that to read a book we were to do so by the Principle of Charity. For us, the book *Science and Sanity, AN INTRODUCTION TO NON-ARISTOTELIAN SYSTEMS AND GENERAL SEMANTICS, S&S* is an incredibly wonderful and seminal work. When S&S was first published, it was reviewed by the Journal of Philosophy. Most uncharitably, its reviewer described S&S as unreadable. Amazingly, when we bought our copy, it was in its 4th Edition and 6th Reprinting in 1980. When we published our work, *Don't Ask WHY?!* it had a critique which slammed it and in 3 years it was in its second reprint. The film Star Wars was severely critiqued when it was first relised and yet it soared to the stars.

There was already a profound shift in thinking in Dennis, by reading the work, *Structure of Magic, SOM*, by Richard Bandler and John Grinder. With S&S was the rocket that catapulted him into a wondrous new universe of ideas and concepts which he introduced to Jennifer. Then together, they worked on these concepts to take the form of this work. Thus, at a very significant level, we owe to S&S our discovery of Neuro-Semantic Programming, NSP. It is for this that we formulated the dedication of this work the way we have.

> > Truly, we thank AK for this.
> > It is one of a set of eternal debts that we can never repay.

If, as it seems the time has come to give acknowledgement to those who guided us and helped us to be here, we certainly must acknowledge the ancient Greek Philosophers. It was in a very real sense, they who found the road to Ontology and Epistemology. It was they who signposted these studies for us to trek, search, explore, find, unravel and discover.
