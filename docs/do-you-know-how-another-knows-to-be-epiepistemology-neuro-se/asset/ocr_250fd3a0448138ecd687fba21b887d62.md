All changes in our emotional values are functions of our responses to our understanding of nuanced changes that happen in a semantic context. Thus, one moment you are annoyed over something. Then the front door bell rings. On opening the door you discover your best friend, whom you have not seen for the last 10 years, is visiting you. So now which is more accurate:

> You respond with joy at his visit or His visit MADE you feel joy?

The shift from being annoyed to feeling joyful is not by C→E. There are no C→E phenomena about changes within us.
