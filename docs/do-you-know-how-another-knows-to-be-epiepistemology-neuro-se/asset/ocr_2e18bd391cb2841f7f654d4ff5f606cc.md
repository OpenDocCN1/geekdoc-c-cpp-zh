Yet, at some level, you have always known the truth of what is stated here. Who can GUARANTEE that one may not meet an accident on the road? Who has the SURETY that tonight when one goes to sleep one will wake up tomorrow morning. Who has the CERTAINTY that upon returning to one’s home it has not been burbled?

Theoretically, there are no guarantees, sureties or certainties in any Psychiatric or Psychotherapeutic encounter. Yet, with respect to the former, we have heard this complaint repeatedly: I had hardly begun to tell my problem and he was already writing out my prescription. If this is true, then for us, this allegation is bewildering. Our modest view is that only God can know, even before the patient sits down, what his problem state is and what is the drug to use.

Every diagnostic is an opinion. I do not have to tell you that the fastest thing that we can do is to arrive at opinions such as: He is an idiot. He is no good. He is a bane to his parents. He is a spendthrift. He is a womanizer. We do so at the speed of light! We are slightly slower when our opinions are in the positive and affirmative of another.

If we are not alert, aware and quick-of-mind, we can end up being stuck in our SHOULDS and SHOULD NOTS. We expect something will go one way and when it does not, we end up crying:
