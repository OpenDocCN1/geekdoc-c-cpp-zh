# It is better to say nothing

The problem is that people attempt to justify a situation or event. In the process of doing so, they give their opinions and reasons. As you know today’s world of political correctness, PC forbids you to tell a person who is waffling rubbish to “shut up” because you will offend him. The ultimate behavioural metric of PC is that you must not offend anyone. We are not sure if such an ideal can ever be attained; and if it is, it would mean that we are all in a blissful state where no one’s feelings are ever hurt.

It is only when you learn that people use blueprints of life to determine the semantics of their doings, that the fog of our un-understanding of another, clears.

Now, we wish to put this to you:

# All human actions have their consequences.

And the consequences are invariably never as we expect, want or anticipate. This is a fundamental teaching of NSP and it is a Law of Physics. Conventionally, the vogue today is to tar a person who immolates himself as unsane,5 and not responsible for his action. Thus, when a Paranoid Schizophrenic selects his car, decides where to locate it, buys the necessary amount of gasoline, decides the date and time to get into the car, then pour the gasoline at the exact chosen time and when to strike the match – his act will be deemed utterly unsane. This conclusion is the product of a simplistic logic by reductionism: This is something I would not do. I would not do this because I am sane. Because I am sane, he must therefore be unsane.

5 unsane is a term we prefer out of our respect for Alfred Korzybski who first coined it in his work S&S.
