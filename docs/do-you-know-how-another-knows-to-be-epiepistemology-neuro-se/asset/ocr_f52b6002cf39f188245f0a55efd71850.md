# Fourth Logical Holon of Ã and its linguistic holons of: How, Who, What, Where, When, Which, Whose and Whom?

Each of the above interrogatives have unique linguistic functions. They are to get INFORMATION appropriate to their category:

+   - How is for the method of doing things
+   - Who is for a person & his status, profession, or designation
+   - What is for a thing, device, intention, purpose, goal, etc.
+   - When is for time, past, present or future
+   - Where is for place, location, site in space, geography
+   - Which is about choices or options
+   - Whose is a general query about identity re ownership of an item
+   - Whom is about specific identity of the person.
