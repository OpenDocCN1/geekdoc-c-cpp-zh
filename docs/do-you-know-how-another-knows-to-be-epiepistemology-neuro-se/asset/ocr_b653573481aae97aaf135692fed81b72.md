This is the fact of Science today. The more they discover and learn, scientists of the highest order admit that they know less. This is HUMILITY. Not one scientist of any repute has ever claimed: I now know all. This is not the age to burn him at an auto da fé. But his claim would be met with laughter and, much worse, with sympathy and concern. For us, this is our concern with Editors and Editorial Boards of Scientific journals. They are so fixated in holding onto their orthodoxy; whilst those at the cutting edge of research have long surrendered theirs. Which do you think is the well-formed party?

In the Scientific American April 2015 Volume 312 Number 4, is the problem of “Firewalls” around black holes. These firewalls are apparently confounding General Relativity and Particle Physics. Here is an affirmation that not all is understood and certainly not all is known. Like the German establishment in its relating towards the “maverick” Einstein and his light waves not traveling in straight lines, everyone has to hold their peace until they find the mathematics about these Firewalls.
