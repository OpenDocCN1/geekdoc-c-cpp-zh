Therefore, all the elements of DLS are under the control of the logic of A. In turn, the logic of A determines the form of a person's ontological coherency and cogency. Coherency and cogency can be well-formed or ill-formed, logical or illogical and sane or insane. Put simply, A determines our human nature in any one of an infinity of possibilities.

The Non-Aristotelian Frame, as you have already read, is known as the Freedom Frame. This came about as it has been said to us many times that dissociating even momentarily from the world of Cause and Effect gives a sense of freedom to take responsibility for oneself and see things in a different light. It was as if they had stepped out from under a cloud that stopped them from moving forward.

It has helped many to see the horror and abomination of:

> A realization of the aimlessness of life lived to labour and to die, having achieved nothing but avoidance of starvation and thirst (our addition), and the birth of children also doomed to the same weary treadmill, has seized the minds of millions.

Alfred Korzybski: Science and Sanity, AN INTRODUCTION TO NON-ARISTOTELIAN SYSTEMS AND GENERAL SEMANTICS, The International Non-Aristotelian Library Publishing Company, 1980, Sixth Reprinting, page 49.

Famine is an act of Fate such as a massive drought. What AK speaks of in this quote, is the work of Man. There is nothing we can do about the former. We can, however, certainly amend the latter⁴ and the Chinese have proved it.

⁴ We can, however, certainly amend the latter: By the outrages of Mao Ze-dong, generations of Chinese lived the aimlessness of life. By their shift to a capitalist economy, they have lifted nearly all their people from this egregious fate.
