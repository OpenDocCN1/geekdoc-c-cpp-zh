## Ninth and Tenth Linguistic Holons:

### INTOLERANCE and PRIDE

### SELF-IMPORTANCE, SI, SELF-ESTEEM, SE and EGO

In the semantics of INTOLERANCE is embedded the idea that one is superior to the other. It is this relational difference that gives the carte blanche for the one above to dump the blame on, heap fault and slap guilt on the one below amidst a whole slew of possible negatives. If both are equal, this cannot happen. It is the problem of gravity, you see. Any mudslide flows down. Gravity will not allow anyone to avalanche negatives up to another who is superior to you.

In the stance of superiority one can look down on the other with extreme negative disrespect. Some examples are, she is a bitch, he is a bastard, she is no good and he is a narcissistic selfish dog.

Our Self Importance, SI, Self-esteem, SE and Ego is the mother of our exalted egocentrism. From this came that Christian iconic thinking that we are:

- (a). at the centre of our solar system and our universe
- (b). the only sentient beings that God created.

Times have changed. From an astronomic/scientific point of view we have long discarded our egocentric position in our solar system and the universe. But, it is NSP's view that in life, it is critical that we find out where the other person is coming from. We must shift our stance from:

Egocentric to exocentric.
