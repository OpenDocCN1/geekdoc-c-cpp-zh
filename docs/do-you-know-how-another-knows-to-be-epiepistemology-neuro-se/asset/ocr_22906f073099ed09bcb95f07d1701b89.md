We dearly and absolutely hold to Einstein’s axiom that God does not place dice. However, it is extremely sad for us that what we know now, we are not able to share with him, to wit, that:

> It is only within the structure of Divine Order that there is absolute order and certainty.

It is the inevitable logical sight of Joseph Campbell that we do not live in eternity, but in the Einsteinian space/time continuum. And space/time is the reality of opposites. For the utterly infinite inflexibility to be possible then its opposite on earth must prevail – the improbable and the uncertain. How else could or would human life be possible as we know it?

Let us consider these examples to demonstrate what we mean. There is nothing more rigid than the laws governing the game of Chess. Yet, it is within its laws that there is chance and improbability. There is nothing more unbending than the rules of a game of Tennis and yet within its rules are chance and improbability. Across the northern boundary of the city of Toronto is one of the world’s busiest highways, the 401. Everyone who uses it is required to drive in accord with the laws apropos to its use. Drive on it and witness the constant fact of chance and the improbable. Behold the heavens and see the Laws of the Divine in operation before you and be staggered, as astronomers and astrophysicists are, by the ever-new chance findings of the improbable. It is true that everything in a casino is weighted against the client. But, all casino games are played out in accord with their laws.
