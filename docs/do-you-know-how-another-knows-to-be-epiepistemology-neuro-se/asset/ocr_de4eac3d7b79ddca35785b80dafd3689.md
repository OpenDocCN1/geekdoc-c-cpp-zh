Its author had a singularly un-Anglo Saxon name, Alfred Korzybski. This really intrigued and excited our curiosity about this work. We felt we had to get it. In reading it we were introduced to the Non-Aristotelian system Ã. This reading of S&S was the most wonderful and fortuitous gift of our lives³.

There is no way we can ever repay the debt we owe Korzybski for illuming our lives. It was truly Bodhisattvatic. We know that without our reading of S&S, this work would never have been written.
