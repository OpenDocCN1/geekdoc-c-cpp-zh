Every verb has its nominalization, at one stage in our NLP training, it was put to us by John Grinder:

> "What is the ultimate nominalization?"

Given that every verb has its corresponding nominalization there are, therefore, a vast number of them. What Grinder wanted to know was what, from a semantic point of view, was the most aberrant of the lot. Everyone in the class desperately sought to find the answer. We all failed. Finally, he told us. It is:

+   I.

"I" is the reification of to be or being. To be or being is real-to-life. I, as a noun is created by the mind by a maneuver of English Transformation Grammar, ETG. It does not exist-in-actuality. If it does not, then "you" do not. Ouch! Therefore, the question is what is your being? What really is your true nature? It is only on the second day of the Freedom Seminar that it is revealed to the students. The alternative is to obtain the answer in our book: A Glimpse at Forever, A Chance for Eternity. In this respect, it is incredible to recall this. When Moses told God that he needed his name so that he could tell his people who it was that sent him to them, God told him his name. It was:

> I AM THAT I AM.

Put in another way. God as he named himself to Moses was:

> BE or BEING.

Of course, this is a difficult thing to swallow what we have said here about your “I”; it means you are to give up so much – such as the pleasure of heaping on it SELF-IMPORTANCE, SELF-ESTEEM, EGOISMS and loads of self-flatteries, the most delicious of which is “I am RIGHT”. Alternatively, we can diminish and demean our “I” to the point at which we do not believe in ourselves. The penalty for this stupid false belief is failing to function well in life.
