The other way is the process known as JUSTIFICATION. This is a process to attempt to prove or justify something. As an example, it can involve a debate that leads to a vote by those who are recognized to have the imprimatur to cast the vote; or it can be achieved by a body of delegates who arrive at a consensus. Whether Jesus was God or not was a very contentious issue. It was resolved at the Council of Nicaea, AD 325, in which a creed was proposed and all but two of some 300 bishops signed their agreement. And those outside who did not agree had their arms severely twisted until they agreed or they were banished.

However, as we have already pointed out, RIGHT and WRONG do not possess inherent qualities and properties by which they exist and endure forever. Things, people or situations change and in the end, are as ephemeral as a breeze. What we do know is that people are always wanting the JUSTIFICATION for anything they perceive to be WRONG or SHOULD NOT be. Therefore they will ask “Why?”

Of course there is a distinction between situations that call forth being questioned such as “What credentials do we have to write this book.” versus a situation that de novo unfolds itself. For example without a nursing licence to practice as a RN, you cannot unfold, de novo, to be one. With it, one can. This is true of all the professions and the trades.
