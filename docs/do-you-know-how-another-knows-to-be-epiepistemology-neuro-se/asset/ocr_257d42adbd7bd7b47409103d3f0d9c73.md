In our ORTHODOXY is our faculty of the meta function. From it we generate all our ABOUTS in the categories of our opinions, interpretations, conclusions, judgments and evaluations. We would like to point out that we are NOT generating these products of thinking. It is our ORTHODOXY that is doing it for us. The interesting thing is that our ORTHODOXY seems to be able to con us into believing that we are producing these things. It flatters us that what “we have produced” is always RIGHT. When we come up with an opinion of another and it is WRONG, and, there are seriously disastrous consequences, we foolishly take the BLAME, FAULT and GUILT of it.

When any of the products of thinking by one’s ORTHODOXY are “WRONG”, NSP commends you to chastise your ORTHODOXY. This is not a daft thing to do because your ORTHODOXY is an autopoietic sentient entity. It is aware. As its owner, it will silently accept its deserved chastisement and hopefully use the non-Aristotelian system instead. Whether it will bend to your will is another matter. How to bend it to your will is a matter that is not for this work as it will take us into the field of how to devise an algorithm to achieve such a thing. You will find it discussed in our forthcoming book, NSP and Human Sphexishness.

Interpretations and opinions are mental inventions; they are idiosyncratic and products of our Orthodoxy. The odd thing is that all interpretations and opinions are personal and unique. However, all of them share a common property. Each is RIGHT! When it is proven to be wrong, its generative source is never BLAMED for it. It is you!
