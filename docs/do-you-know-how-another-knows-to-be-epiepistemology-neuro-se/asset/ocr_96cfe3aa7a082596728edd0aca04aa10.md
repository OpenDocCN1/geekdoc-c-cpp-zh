Despite all the changes the room is essentially still the same. For it to have the status of Second Order Change one might convert it into a sauna or hot tub.

First Order Change, IOC is to do what Guy Fieri of Diners, Drive Ins and Dives does. He eats different preparations of food from one location to a different location. Second Order Change, IIOC, is if he were to become a Trappist monk and dine in an invariant humble and simple way. This is extremely unlikely as Guy is a dedicated gourmand.

IIOC, is a change to an altogether different state. A couple having marital problems and staying in the relationship attempting and wanting to hold it together is first order change. Ending the relationship and moving on is second order change. From the book, Change, you will find this quote:

> "The one way out of a dream involves a change from dreaming to waking. Waking, obviously, is no longer a part of the dream, but a change to an altogether different state. It is a change of change."

In the end, life is a balance between the reality that: is ever changing but is still the same versus changes entirely.
