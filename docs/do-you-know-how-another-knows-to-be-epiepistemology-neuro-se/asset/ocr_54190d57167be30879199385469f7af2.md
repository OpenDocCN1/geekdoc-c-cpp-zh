# (ii) quality of a feeling/emotion:

The quality of any feeling/emotion refers to which classification it would belong to:

+   (a). Primary
+   (b). Mixed
+   (c). Dissociated
+   (d). Meta-meta-meta . .
+   (e). Hypnotic
+   (f). Good, virtuous, saintly
+   (g). Dark Side

(a). Primary feelings/emotions are pure physiologies as expressed in these examples:

+   I like you.
+   I detest you.
+   I abhor you.
+   I love you.
+   I despise you.
+   I hate you.

(b). Mixed feelings/emotions are feelings of one Primary kind mixed with that of another as exampled below:

+   I am so happy to go to Jamaica on holiday, but I am nervous of flying.
+   I feel so proud for my promotion, but I am afraid of my new boss.
+   He is so fun to be with, but I sometimes feel anxious as he can be cutting.
+   I like to go with him to visit his mother, but not when she starts to guilt him.
+   My strength was mixed with concern when he started to hit the bottle.
