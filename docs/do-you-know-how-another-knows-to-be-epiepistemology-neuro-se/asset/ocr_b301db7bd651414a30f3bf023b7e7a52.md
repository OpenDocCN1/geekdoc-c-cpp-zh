All items in relationships in life are connected because of the verbs between the elements. For this all relationships are conjugated relationships via their verbs. We agree with HM and FV that, if and when, the relationship is well conjugated then it will be a well-formed relationship. Formally, a conjugated relationship can be expressed in this way:

```
y = f(x).
```

The above equation is expressed in an English sentence thus:

Y is a function of X.

However, in Formal Logic, a conjugated relationship is also known as a:

*modus ponens.*

What is a modus ponens? It is a relationship that “If A is true, then B is true. Examples of these possible sentence variations are:

+   - If x, then y.
+   - When x, then y.
+   - As x, then y.
+   - Whatever x, then y
+   - If you visit your mother, then she will be so happy.
+   - When you study hard, then you may have high marks
+   - As you grow older, then you will be wiser.
