If your ontology is one of being angry and frustrated, your words will be rapid, raised in volume, harsh in tone and your breathing will be high in the chest and tight. If your ontological disposition is one of being calm and serious, your words will be moderate in pace, soft spoken and your breathing will be low in your chest, smooth and regular.

In Applied Linguistics, all linguistic transforms 6 , namely the sentences that are spoken, are normally fitting to context. As the context changes, the ontology of the speaker will change in accord with the new semantics. In turn, the values of the sentences with respect to their semantics, intensity, urgency, focus and gravitas will also change. If, for example, you approach the topic of a context with perfunctory dismissiveness, your language will be very superficial and unfocussed. However, if you speak with seriousness and determination, your ontology will be one of grave earnestness.
