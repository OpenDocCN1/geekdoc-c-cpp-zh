Once the space shuttle: The Challenger took off, it seemed another NASA success and then disaster came upon it. If ever anyone needed the evidence that in life there are no guarantees, sureties or certainties, what we cite here is the ultimate proof. And it is one that is buttressed by the near pandemic numbers of divorces, lethal road traffic accidents and killings.

Such is the fear of this world’s uncertainties, improbabilities and unknowns, that it will be no surprise that many a rich person can and has opted to live a life of a recluse. They can do so in their estates, mansions and gated communities. The billionaire, Howard Hughes was one example. We believe that one of the Vanderbilt’s and one of the Rothschild’s were in a similar vein.

Certainty is the product of the structural thinking pattern of C→E. The corollary of any event’s certainty is that you can anticipate it. Out of this certainty is born either our SHOULD BE or SHOULD NOT BE. In my naïve youth, I thought everyone was like me: Punctuality is a hall mark of character. In my SHOULD without realizing it, was my EXPECTATION of the other to be ON TIME for any appointment. Now, if you just think on it for one moment: Are you not like me too, full right up to your attic with EXPECTATIONS. But, and this is a huge BUT, how on earth can you have even one teeny weeny EXPECTATION when the reality we live in has no guarantees, sureties or certainties. NSP is sure that to have even one EXPECTATION is a mark of insanity. Do you think this physical phenomenon is included in the Diagnostics of DSM-5? Of course not!
