## Chapter I: Thinking by Mind/Brains

To think or thinking are seemingly two simple words; but, they are disguises for a very complex subject matter. It has compelled us to redo this chapter four times.

This is a chapter is composed of a hierarchy of sub-sections. We propose to enumerate them as we do them.

- 1. To begin with, you think. However, do you know how you do it? If you do know how you do it, we CONGRATULATE you. Our surmise is that you do not because:

  - (a). the nature of thinking is unconscious. So, normally one has no cognitive awareness of it. What we are aware of are the end products of our thinking, to wit, the words we say or the actions we do.

  - (b). thinking has an incredible speed with the result that you cannot stop it, at a given instant, just to trap what is its next step.

The only normal time you might be stalled in your thinking is when you are faced with the choices of a menu at a fine restaurant. Then you cannot decide. Once you find that you can decide, PRESTO, the choice is done. That is how fast your thinking can be. So, now the question becomes:

How did you think to decide your choice from the menu at the speed of PRESTO?

Each category, e.g. an opinion, is ABOUT some person, thing or idea. Whatever is the ABOUT of another is by definition, meta to it. This is to say it is above it. It is for this that NSP concluded that the faculty of thinking is by the power of the meta function, mf.

What is the meta function? In Medicine, it is known as the ability to abstract. In Psychology, it is known as the Global Function. It is the faculty by which we can go above a topic of consideration and get a sight of it by which we can derive an abstraction, opinion or interpretation ABOUT it. At this logical level of consideration, the ability to think is about the ability to arrive at a transderivation about self, other and whatever is before us. The power of the meta function exists in our mind/brains and in our being/body.
