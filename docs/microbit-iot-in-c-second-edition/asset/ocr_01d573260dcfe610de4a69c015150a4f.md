While the V2 model is superficially similar, there are some important differences. From the user's point of view the changes are the addition of a microphone and speaker. From the point of view of the programmer the biggest change is the use of a faster processor – four times faster than the original – with a hardware FPU. The RAM has also increased to 128KB, which is eight times bigger. Clearly the new micro:bit is far more capable than the old.
