Notice the following in Figure 4.6:

+   - The 45° tilted square shows the frame of the scatterplot of the original columns.
+   - The mean difference is shown as the horizontal line, with the 95% confidence interval above and below. If the confidence region includes the horizontal line at zero, then the means are not significantly different at the 0.05 level. In the example shown in Figure 4.6, the difference is significant.
+   - The mean of the mean of pairs is shown by the vertical line.
