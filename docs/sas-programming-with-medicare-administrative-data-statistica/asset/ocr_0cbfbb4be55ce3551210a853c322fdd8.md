# Algorithms: Measuring Utilization of SNF, Home Health Agency, and Hospice Care

Claims for SNF and hospice services are billed in 30-day increments; claims for home health services are billed in 60-day increments. If a beneficiary is discharged (or expires) prior to the completion of a 30- or 60-day period, the last claim in a set may be for that 30 or 60 days, respectively. Therefore, when sorting claims from FROM_DT and THRU_DT for a beneficiary's home health care, we expect to see claims with a length of stay of 60 days. We may also see claims with a length of stay of more than 60 days for the last claim in the stay. In order to determine length of stay, we must collapse claims that comprise a single stay.
