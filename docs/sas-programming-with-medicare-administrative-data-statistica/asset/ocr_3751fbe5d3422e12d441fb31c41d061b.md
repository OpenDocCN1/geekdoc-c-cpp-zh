# Data Destruction

Most UAS require the destruction of all Personally Identifiable Information (PII) data and additional materials derived from PII data, even if those materials do not contain PII data themselves. Therefore, the first step in destroying the data is to become clear on exactly what data must be destroyed. The must destroy all files containing PII. For our example programming project, we will destroy all SAS data sets. The reader should check the details of his or her DUA. Common methods of destruction include shredding CDs or DVDs, erasure via degaussing of magnetic storage such as hard drives, and deleting data from hard drives, followed by overwriting the deleted files many times to ensure they cannot be recovered.
