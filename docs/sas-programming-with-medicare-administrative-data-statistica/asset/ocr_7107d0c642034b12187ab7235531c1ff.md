# Chapter 8: Measuring Costs to Medicare 99

Occasionally, the reader may note the presence of negative payments in the inpatient claims.4 Recall that our work in Step 7.5 in Chapter 7 excluded negative payment amounts from the inpatient claims data set.

```
* STEP 8.5: CALCULATE TOTAL INPATIENT COSTS BY BENEFICIARY FOR
BENEFICIARIES WITH AT LEAST ONE INPATIENT CLAIM */
proc sql;
    create table cst_ip_cost_bene_as
        select bene_id, sum(pmt_amt) as ip_tocost_bene format=dollar15.2
    label='TOTAL COST FOR THE BENE'
    from util.ip_2010_fnl_ss
    group by bene_id;
quit;
```
