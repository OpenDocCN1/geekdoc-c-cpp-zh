As discussed in Chapter 3, Healthcare Common Procedure Coding System (HCPCS) Level I codes, also known as Current Procedural Terminology (CPT) codes, are five digit numbers describing medical services, and are a copyrighted coding schema of the American Medical Association (AMA). HCPCS Level II codes are five alphanumeric codes defining services not described by the Level I codes.
