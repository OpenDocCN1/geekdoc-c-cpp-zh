What are some distinguishing characteristics of Listing 3.4 (variable.cpp)?

+   - Each statement appears on its own line. A statement is necessarily split between two lines of text. Visually one line texts one action (statement) to perform.
- The close brace aligns vertically with the line above that contains the corresponding curly brace. This makes it easier to determine if the curly braces match and nest properly. It better portrays the logical structure of the program. The ability to communicate the logical structure of a program becomes much important as more complex programs frequently use nested curly braces (for example, see Listing 5.1 (trouble.cpp)). Without a consistent, organized arrangement of curly braces it can difficult to determine which opening brace belongs which particular closing brace.
- The statements that constitute the body of main are indented spaces. This visually emphasizes the fact that the elements are logically enclosed. As with brace alignment, indentation to emphasize logical enclosure becomes more important as more complex programs are considered.
