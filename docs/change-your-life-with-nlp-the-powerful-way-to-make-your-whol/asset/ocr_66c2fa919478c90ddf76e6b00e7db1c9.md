# Deletions

Let's have some fun for a moment. Read to yourself the text you see in the triangle below.

![](bbox=[0.206, 0.313, 0.843, 0.563])

More than likely you said to yourself 'a bird in the bush'. If this is what you said, then you didn't notice that the word 'the' is repeated twice. Look again! Your brain does not expect to see the second 'the' and so it deletes it.
