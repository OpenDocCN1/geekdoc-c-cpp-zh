# Exercise 3

What would I do if I knew I couldn't fail?
I want you to dream for a moment. Think about what you would change in your life if I guaranteed that you couldn't fail. Anything in your life at all. Write your answers below:

+   - Bigger house beautifully day out
- New car
- Take Mom + Dad in paradise
- Take family trip to Australia
- Upgrade school for I
- Beautiful diamond earrings
- Buy beautiful paintings
- Do more work for young entrepreneurs

Now take another look at your list and make sure that you have really stretched yourself. This is the moment that you can dream.

Really enjoy the anticipation of achieving these things in future as you move on to the next chapter.

Tip: If you're still reluctant to move out of the Grey Zone, imagine turning the clock forward 10 years and you still haven't got what you really want and you're 10 years older. How frustrating would that be? Take the first step. You know you want to.
