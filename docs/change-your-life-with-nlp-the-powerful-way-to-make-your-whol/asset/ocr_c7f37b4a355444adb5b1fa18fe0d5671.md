# Matthew – focusing on dangerous things

I had a client recently came to see me after he had lost a successful business. The business had gone bankrupt after initially being very profitable. As he was telling me the story of what had happened I noticed in his language how he started to worry about losing the company after it had taken off so quickly and so well. It was almost as if it was too good to be true for him and he became obsessed with losing it all. It didn't take long before he stopped noticing the successes of his company and focused on those things that didn't go to plan. He lost the business.
