# Results v Reasons (excuses) = Empowerment

The Kybalion, published back in 1908, contains the seven Hermetic principles of success. These principles were adapted from ancient Egyptian and Greek wisdom. The principle of cause and effect embodies the fact that there is a cause for every effect that happens. This implies that there is no such thing as chance and nothing merely happens.

In modern-day terms, being at cause means that you decide in what you want in life and you take responsibility for whatever happens – good or bad. You see the world as a place of opportunity and you move towards achieving what you want. If things are not happening as you would like, you take action and explore other possibilities. Above all, you know you have choice in what you do and how you react to people and events.
