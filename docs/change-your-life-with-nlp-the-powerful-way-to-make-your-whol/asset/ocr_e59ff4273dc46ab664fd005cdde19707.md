Step 4: test

You may feel that your values are in the right order. However, your exercise partner should check them as follows. Your partner starts with value number 1 (the most important) and asks you (using our example): “Assuming you could have inspiration (value number 1) but not legacy (value number 2), would that be OK with you?

They need to force a choice from you. If you say it is OK, then those values are in the right order. If you say it’s not OK, the values need to be reversed – in other words, number 2 becomes number 1 and vice versa. Your partner then checks value number 2 against number 3 until you get to the end of the eight values. You can then be sure that they are in the right order.
