# CHANGE YOUR LIFE WITH NLP

+   11. If something isn't working, do something different.

If you always do what you've always done, you always get what you've got!

If your strategy isn't working, ask-yourself what you can do differently next time. Keep changing your approach until you reach your goal.

+   12. Anything less than 100 per cent is sabotage.

If you go for something in a half-hearted way, you are stopping yourself from succeeding. Better to do nothing!

Jedi master Yoda in Star Wars said: 'Do, or not try.' If you want to change your life, then go for it. If you go for your goals in a half-hearted way, you are likely to sabotage yourself.

I suppose for a moment that you held these 12 beliefs to be true for you. What difference would they make in your life and to your potential results? I'd like to suggest that you 'trial' them in the context of your journey of change. By trialling them and discovering the ones that have the biggest impact for you, you can, over time, turn them into your own beliefs and thoughts.
