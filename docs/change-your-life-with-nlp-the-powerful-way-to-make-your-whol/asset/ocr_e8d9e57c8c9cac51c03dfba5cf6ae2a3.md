# Example 1

Belief

+   - I can't get fit.

Outcome

I don't want to be fat

+   - I prepare my exercise schedule

I join the gym and start to exercise regularly

After a couple of weeks I begin to make excuses to myself to avoid exercising

I begin to exercise less often

I get demotivated when I don't see any improvement in my fitness

I get increasingly fed up with myself

When I'm fed up I eat more

I even begin to put on weight and I'm less fit than when I started

I prove my belief to myself
