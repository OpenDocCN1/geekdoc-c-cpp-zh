# Meta programmes

A simple way of thinking about meta programmes is to think of them as your patterns of behaviour or – even more simply – as your personality. They are largely unconscious in so far as you normally react without consciously thinking about it. They are deep-rooted mental programmes which automatically filter our experiences and guide and direct your thought processes. Different combinations of meta programmes lead to significant differences in behaviour from one person to the next. Learning about your meta programmes will help you decide which elements of your personality are helpful in your personal development journey and which you would like to change in some way. Maybe you've had feedback in the past and have not known what to do about that. The first step to changing your behaviour is to be aware of how you react. Then you can start to make different choices about what to do in future.
