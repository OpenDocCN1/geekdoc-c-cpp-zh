## Rachel – taking a risk to develop an amazing relationship

Rachel and Eddie had been together for five years and their relationship was on the rocks. They had little in common and spent the majority of their spare time with their friends. Rachel learnt about feedback on one of our programmes and decided to use it as a last-ditch attempt to save her marriage as she still loved her husband. They did a deal with each other that they would both put 100 per cent commitment to understanding each other's needs for the first time in their relationship. This involved being totally honest with each other about their feelings and about what was good and what needed to change in their relationship. It took courage to give the level of feedback both ways that was required. Yet, in those most open moments they learnt about each other and what made them both happy ever before. They agreed to focus on meeting each other's needs in an atmosphere of deep-felt understanding. Their relationship moved to an entirely new level as they saw each other for the first time and enjoyed developing and changing as their levels of understanding grew.

## What if you were to integrate feedback in your everyday life?

Incorporating feedback will help you to fast-track your development process. In terms of your goals, if you were to get feedback on your actions, you will eventually find a way through. guarantee that. You will notice that the relationships with people in all areas of your life will improve. They will become deeper and more meaningful with congruence, openness and honesty become the focus instead of avoidance. Some relationships may feel uncomfortable and this is not because enough rapport has been built at the start of the process. If you find that some of your colleagues, friends and family do not want to play the feedback game then consider the long-term value of these relationships – at best they can only be described as shallow. Most importantly, you will become even better at what you do and your ability both to give and receive feedback will be noticed and admired.

Tip: Giving feedback once a year is like dieting only on your birthday and wondering why you're not losing weight.

## Your moment of truth

You have the power to control what you think, you have the power to control how you feel and you have the power to control what you do. Now it is your moment of truth because it's time to take action. You decided that you want more from your life. You've worked on your goals and I've assisted you with the mindset and beliefs you need for success. Now it's your time to go for it. Some of you may be starting to feel a little unresourceful as we get down to your personal moment of truth and that's normal. Recognise where you are and where those feelings are coming from. If you need to work more on your mindset, then do it now because we're about to rock and roll!

And, I have to let you into a secret that successful people already know – one of the biggest myths is that it takes a superhuman act to achieve our goals. It's not just the case. It's the small decisions that we make that affect our destiny as much as the big ones. If we make enough small decisions then we will get there. I once asked my teacher:

> ‘What's the secret of achieving your goals?’

I still think about his answer at least once a day. He said:

> ‘Keep going until you do!’

It is a remarkably simple and very wise statement that has guided me through big life challenges. Stick with it and you will always achieve your goal. Last Christmas, there was a joke that one of my children read out of their cracker at the dinner table. It said:

‘How do you eat an elephant?’

The answer was:

‘In small chunks.’

It's the same for your goals. Each time we make a decision begin to create our future in advance and each time we take a decision there are consequences. It's important to think these through and know that you cannot plan for every eventuality. It's impossible. You jump off the edge of the cliff, have faith and learn as you fly.

## Get Started

Our society is used to instant gratification through the web, emails, text messages. Yet, we need to have a long-term focus if we are really to succeed. For example, if I had taken a short-term view after I postponed my first programme, Change Corporation may have ceased to exist. Take, simple steps and when things don't go to plan work out what you need to do next time.

If you take no action, then nothing will change. I'm reminded of a trip to MFI that I made two years ago because I wanted some bookshelves for my study at home. I had seen the ones I wanted and was delighted after Christmas were in the sale. I bought two bookshelves and discovered when I went to pay for what they needed to be together at home. In other words, they were flat-packed. I was rather disappointed to find out that and, of course, explained why they were cheaper than some of the other styles. Anyway, I didn't have enough cash flow at the time so I bought them and proceeded to take them in the back of my car. Even hauling the two large boxes in and out was a real effort. I put these boxes in the back of my garage where they were out of the way. Two years later I hadn't opened the boxes and in the end I took them to a car-boot sale and sold to a DIY enthusiast.

That's what it's like if you take no action after reading this book. It's like having all the secrets to success in a flat-packed furniture box waiting for the day you will have the motivation to get started.
