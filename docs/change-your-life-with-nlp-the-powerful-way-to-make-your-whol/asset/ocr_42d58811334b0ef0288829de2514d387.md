You will want the time to be able to maximise every experience. I'm going to introduce you to a different understanding of time that means you can create the time you take it. I call this time distortion.

Anybody who has ever had a long-distance relationship will know all about time distortion. When you are together, the time literally seems to fly past. The time apart until the next meeting feels like an eternity. If the long-distance relationship ends, time 'settles down' and every day feels the same length again.

Deepak Chopra writes that Einstein's brilliant contribution to modern physics was the idea that linear time is superficial. It is not a straight-line process. Time seems to flow and move, seconds, minutes, hours, days, months and years pass by. Yet, this is all relative - it has absolute value. Einstein replaced the idea of time with something more fluid. With fluid time, it can speed up or slow down depending upon the context. He noted that spending a minute on a hot stove seems like an hour, whereas spending an hour with someone you loves like a minute.
