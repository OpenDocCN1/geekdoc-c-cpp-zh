I had complete belief that my business would be a success because I believed myself to be good at what I do as a trainer, consultant and coach. I believed that I would be able to grow a successful company on these competencies. I planned everything I could think of – website, brochures, networking, offices and venues.

So, when I had to postpone my first course I was bitterly disappointed. Why? Because I soon discovered that not only did I need to be a world-class trainer and coach, I also needed to know how to grow a business and be a successful businesswoman. While I was great at training, I was rubbish at marketing, sales and financial management. All important qualities when you run a business. That came as a shock to me and I had to buy in or develop these competencies very fast. I decided that I would get part-time assistance to set up and run my financial systems and marketing and I knew I needed to get much better at selling myself.
