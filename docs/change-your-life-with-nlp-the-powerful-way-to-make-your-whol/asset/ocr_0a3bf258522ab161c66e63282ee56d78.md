# The power of values

I spoke in Chapter 3 of the importance of our values in the context of my career change. We're going to explore them in more detail now. Our values determine how we spend our time. They determine what we do. They motivate us to take action and they are the means by which we evaluate ourselves after the event, and decide if we did a good job – or, indeed, the right thing. Our values also provide our moral code, so if we feel uncomfortable about anything we've done it's likely to be due a conflict of values. Yet, they are largely unconscious. As a result, we may unknowingly choose goals that are not aligned to our values. When this happens we often experience an inner conflict between what is going around us and how we feel on the inside. This can manifest as unhappiness or an illness. So it's really important to find out now how far your goals are aligned to your values.
