# Acting ‘as if’

I wondered how I would ever know enough to run my own business. Through my NLP training I came across 'Heisenberg's principle'. This states that 100 per cent certainty doesn't exist. Successful people take risks because they accept that they will never know everything they need to know. But they act 'as if' they do know and take the plunge. The journey is exciting – that's the thing. That's what it's going to be like for you too as you move towards the Brilliant Zone.
