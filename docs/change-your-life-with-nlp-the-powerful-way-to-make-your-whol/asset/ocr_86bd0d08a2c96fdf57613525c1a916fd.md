# Exercise 16

Improve your focus, improve your results

![](bbox=[0.198, 0.185, 0.843, 0.493])

You'll need to stand up to do this exercise. Find a space where you can move around with your arm outstretched freely. Look forwards in front of you and stand with your feet apart. Stand rooted to the floor. Keep your legs still, raise your right arm horizon-tally out until arm and gently twist around keeping your arm horizontal until you cannot twist any further. Note carefully to yourself where your arm is pointing – maybe there's something in the room you can take as a marker. Or if you have someone else with you, ask them to stand as a marker for where your arm got to. Now focus and find a spot beyond your arm reached the first time. Fix that spot in your mind as you turn back around to face the front again. Twist again moving your arm as far as it will go. Hold steady, turn around and see how much further you have moved your arm this time. You will find that you have moved your arm much further the second time around. You may have twisted to way beyond the point even you visualised.
