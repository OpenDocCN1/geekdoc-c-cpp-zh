# CHANGE YOUR LIFE WITH NLP

Learnings are always:

+   - for you personally
- positive
- for the future.

Fundamentally, learnings are for yourself because you cannot control someone else's behaviour. They are what you will do differently in future, not what you shouldn't or won't do again - as we know from Chapter 4 this will focus your attention in the wrong direction. They are always for the future as the past has already happened. So, a learning at the end of a relationship might be, 'I will learn to enjoy life, spending time by myself as well as with others in future', 'I'm miserable unless I'm spending time with my partner'. Got it?

I confess that living life at cause can sometimes feel more of a challenge than living life at effect. Sometimes it feels this way because you are placing new demands on yourself – because your action is to do something differently – view this is a good thing. In fact, life becomes far stressful because you always know there is a choice and you are freed from relying on excuses. And, if you want to move out of the Grey Zone, this is the way to run your life. With this mindset you cannot achieve the goals that you set for yourself, because you are flexible and when things do not go to plan you get the learnings, move on quickly and take a different approach next time.

You now have an opportunity to live your life at cause for one month. At first, if you're not sure what to do, just pretend – act 'as if’. Enjoy the experience and reflect on your learnings.
