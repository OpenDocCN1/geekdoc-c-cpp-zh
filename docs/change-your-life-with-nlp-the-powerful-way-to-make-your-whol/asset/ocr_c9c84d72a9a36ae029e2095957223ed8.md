Our society is used to instant gratification through the web, emails, text messages and TV. Yet, we need to have a long-term focus if we are really to succeed. For example, if I had taken a short-term view after I postponed my first training programme, The Change Corporation may have ceased to exist and then never. Take, simple steps and when things don't go to plan the learnings and work out what you need to do differently next time.

If you take no action, then nothing will change. I'm reminded of a trip to MFI that I made two years ago because I wanted some new bookshelves for my study at home. I had seen the ones I wanted and was delighted after Christmas were in the sale. I bought two small bookshelves and discovered when I went to pay for what they needed to be put together at home. In other words, they were flat-packed. I was rather disappointed to find out that and and, of course, explained why they were cheaper than some of the other styles. Anyway, I didn't have enough cash flow at the time so I bought them and proceeded to take them in the back of my car. Even hauling the two large boxes in and out was a real effort. I put these boxes in the back of my garage where they were out of the way. Two years later I hadn't opened the boxes and in the end I took them to a car-boot sale and sold to a DIY enthusiast.

That's what it's like if you take no action after reading this book. It's like having all the secrets to success in a flat-packed furniture box waiting for the day you will have the motivation to get started.
