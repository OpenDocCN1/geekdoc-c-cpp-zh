So, let's use small kernels. If we convolve twice, using Gaussians of width \(\sigma_1\) and \(\sigma_2\), this is equivalent to convolving with a kernel of \(\sigma = \sqrt{\sigma_1^2 + \sigma_2^2}\). Therefore, smoothing can be accomplished by repeated application of convolution with a small kernel. The use of small kernels allows the blurring to approach the boundaries of the image much more closely.
