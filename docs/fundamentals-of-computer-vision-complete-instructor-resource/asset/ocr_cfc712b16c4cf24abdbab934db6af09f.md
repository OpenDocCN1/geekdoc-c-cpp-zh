Figure 12.10 (ABOVE) Automatic stitching of the two images in Figure 12.9. The right-hand half of the stitching may appear slightly lighter than the left. This is due to automatic gain control (AGC) on the camera. The camera sets its gain to the average brightness in the image. For this reason, we recommend that anyone doing Computer Vision work use a camera that allows the user to disable AGC.
