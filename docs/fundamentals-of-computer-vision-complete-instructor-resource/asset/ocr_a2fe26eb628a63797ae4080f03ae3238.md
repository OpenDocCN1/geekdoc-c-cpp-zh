The neuron may be modeled mathematically with a sum-of-products followed by a nonlinearity, as illustrated in Figure 1.4. Therefore, the equation of the neuron can be written as

y_q = S_q \left( \sum_i x_i w_{iq} \right) (1.1)

where i and q are the indices for inputs and neurons, respectively, and S_q is the nonlinear function. The nonlinearity S_q has the form of a sigmoid (having the shape of the letter “S”). A common sigmoid is the logistic function S(x) = 1 / (1 + e^-x), where a controls the slope of the sigmoid in its center.
