Now, what does one do with these concepts of transformations? One can correct transformations and align objects together through inverse transformations to assist in shape analysis. For example, one can correct for translation by shifting so the centroid is at the origin. Correcting for rotation involves rotating the image until the principal axes of the object are in alignment with the coordinates axes.
