If the equations are not satisfied by the time the while-loop ends, then there will be at least one change to an OUT (in the forward case) or IN (in the backward case), and we must go around the loop again.

To prove the second property, we first show that the values taken on by IN[B] and OUT[B] for any B can only decrease (in the sense of the ≤ relationship for lattices) as the algorithm iterates. This claim can be proven by induction.
