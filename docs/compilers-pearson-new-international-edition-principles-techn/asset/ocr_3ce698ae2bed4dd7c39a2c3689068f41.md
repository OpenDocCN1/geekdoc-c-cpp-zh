# ! Exercise 4.2.4: There is an extended grammar notation in common use. In this notation, square and curly braces in production bodies are metasymbols (like → or |) with the following meanings:

+   i) Square braces around a grammar symbol or symbols denotes that these constructs are optional. Thus, production \(A \to X\ [Y]\ Z\) has the same effect as the two productions \(A \to X\ Y\ Z\) and \(A \to X\ Z\).

+   ii) Curly braces around a grammar symbol or symbols says that these symbols may be repeated any number of times, including zero times. Thus, \(A \to X\ \{Y\ Z\}\) has the same effect as the infinite sequence of productions \(A \to X\), \(A \to X\ Y\ Z\), \(A \to X\ Y\ Z\ Y\ Z\), \(A \to X\ Y\ Z\ Y\ Z\ Y\ Z\), and so on.
