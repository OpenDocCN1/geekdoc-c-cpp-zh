# 4.7. MORE POWERFUL LR PARSERS

**METHOD:** The procedures CLOSURE and GOTO and the main routine *items* for constructing the sets of items were shown in Fig. 4.40.

**Example 4.54:** Consider the following augmented grammar.

$$\begin{aligned}
S' &\to S\\
S &\to CC\\
C &\to cC\mid d
\end{aligned}\tag{4.55}$$

We begin by computing the closure of {$[S' \to \cdot S,\ $]$}. To close, we match the item $[S' \to \cdot S,\ $]$ with the item $[A \to \alpha\cdot B\beta,\ a]$ in the procedure CLOSURE. That is, $A = S'$, $\alpha = \epsilon$, $B = S$, $\beta = \epsilon$, and $a = \$$. Function CLOSURE tells us to add $[B \to \gamma,\ b]$ for each production $B \to \gamma$ and terminal $b$ in FIRST($\beta a$). In terms of the present grammar, $B \to \gamma$ must be $\to CC$, and since $\beta$ is $\epsilon$ and $a$ is $\$$, $b$ may only be $\$$. Thus we add $[S \to \cdot CC,\ $]$.

We continue to compute the closure by adding all items $[C \to \cdot\gamma,\ b]$ for $b$ in FIRST($C(\$)$). That is, matching $[S \to \cdot CC,\ $]$ against $[A \to \alpha\cdot B\beta,\ a]$, we have $A = S$, $\alpha = \epsilon$, $B = C$, $\beta = C$, and $a = \$$. Since $C$ does not derive the empty string, FIRST($C(\$)$) = FIRST($C$). Since FIRST($C$) contains terminals $c$ and $d$, we add items $[C \to \cdot cC,\ c]$, $[C \to \cdot cC,\ d]$, $[C \to \cdot d,\ c]$ and $[C \to \cdot d,\ d]$. None of the new items has a nonterminal immediately to the right of the dot, so we have completed our first set of LR(1) items. The initial set of items is

$$\begin{aligned}
I_0:\quad &S' \to \cdot S,\ $\\
&S \to \cdot CC,\ $\\
&C \to \cdot cC,\ c/d\\
&C \to \cdot d,\ c/d
\end{aligned}$$

The brackets have been omitted for notational convenience, and we use the notation $[C \to \cdot cC,\ c/d]$ as a shorthand for the two items $[C \to \cdot cC,\ c]$ and $[C \to \cdot cC,\ d]$.

Now we compute GOTO($I_0, X$) for the various values of $X$. For $X = S$ we must close the item $[S' \to \cdot S,\ $]$. No additional closure is possible, since the dot is at the right end. Thus we have the next set of items

$$I_1:\quad S' \to S\cdot,\ $$$$

For $X = C$ we close $[S \to C\cdot C,\ $]$. We add the $C$-productions with second component $\$$ and then can add no more, yielding

$$\begin{aligned}
I_2:\quad &S \to C\cdot C,\ $\\
&C \to \cdot cC,\ $\\
&C \to \cdot d,\ $
\end{aligned}$$
