In a postorder traversal, we first perform all the actions in the leftmost subtree of the root, for the left operand, also labeled *expr* like the root. We then visit the leaf + at which there is no action. Next we perform the actions in the subtree for the right operand *term* and, finally, the semantic action `{print('+')}` at the extra node.
