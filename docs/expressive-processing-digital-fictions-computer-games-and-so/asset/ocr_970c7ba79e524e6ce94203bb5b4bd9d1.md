A sequencer might play itself for some time after being given instructions, but a guitar demands interaction for each note sounded. Similarly, Pax is structured for near-continual interaction. The larger area of the piece, on the left, shows characters floating up (in the first half of the piece’s duration) or falling down (in the second). Unless the reader interacts with these characters, almost no text appears. Readers interact by “catching” floating characters with the mouse pointer (figure 9.6). Characters can be released by moving the mouse away, or clicked (either by active clicking or holding them caught for twenty seconds).
