# N-gram Play

These two projects, of course, are quite different from those described earlier in this chapter—particularly Pax, riverIsland, and Screen. The most obvious difference is that the project authors did not write the text for Regime Change and News Reader, and for the latter authors did not even select the text (only the method by which text is gathered at each reading). In part this is connected to the nature of the experiment. A textual instrument meant to play many compositions (make many texts playable) is most convincing if it can work with texts that are not presecreened by their author(s). But there are also other reasons.
