Winning players will be those who can (a) build a large coalition by engaging a number of people in dialogue; (b) promote a desired set of themes of discussion that are taken up by others in their posts; and, (c) articulate an influential opinion about the themes of discussion.

For Sack, Agonistics is an attempt to make a playable game that operates in a manner inspired by those philosopher linguists (he cites Chantal Mouffe, Gilles Deleuze, Bruno Latour, and others) who imagine democratic debate in a gamelike rather than warlike terms. Obviously, such a game can never satisfy demands for a “quantifiable outcome,” though it is certainly playable.
