A simpler Mood display shows the overall state of the Sim, presented as a set of green or red bars. Other possible displays reveal the iconic topics in which the Sim is most interested (a Sim will gain the most from discussions with those who have similar interests), the current state of the Sim's interpersonal relationships, five measures of the Sim's basic personality (Neat, Outgoing, Active, Playful, and Nice), and so on.

In other words, when one begins playing The Sims, the game moves immediately and decisively toward the SimCity effect.
