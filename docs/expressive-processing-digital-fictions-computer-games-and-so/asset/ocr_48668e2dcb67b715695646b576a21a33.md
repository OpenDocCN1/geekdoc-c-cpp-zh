# AI, Neat and Scruffy

A name that does appear in Weizenbaum’s book, however, is that of Roger Schank, Abelson’s most famous collaborator. When Schank arrived from Stanford University to join Abelson at Yale University, together they represented the most identifiable center for a particular approach to AI what would later (in the early 1980s) come to be known as the “scruffy” approach. Meanwhile, perhaps the most identifiable proponent of what would later be called the “neat” approach, John McCarthy, remained at Stanford.
