# Notes

+   1. The uncanny valley—a term coined by Japanese roboticist Masahiro Mori—indicates a phenomenon in representations of human characters. As representations approach reality they appear increasingly familiar up to a certain point, after which (but before full realism) there is a tendency for humans to find it more difficult to identify with the representations the more realistic they become. They look almost right, but disturbingly wrong.
