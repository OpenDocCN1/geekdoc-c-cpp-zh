# A View of Digital Media

Let me return to my first conception of expressive processing: the possibility of creating new simulated machines, of defining new computational behaviors, as the great authoring opportunity that digital media offers. Seizing this opportunity requires a bit of a shift. It is common to think of the work of authoring, the work of creating media, as the work of writing text, composing images, arranging sound, and so on. But now one must think of authoring new processes as an important element of media creation.
