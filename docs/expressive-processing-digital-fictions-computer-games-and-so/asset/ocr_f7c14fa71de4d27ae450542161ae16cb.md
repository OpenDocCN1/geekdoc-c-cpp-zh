I think there is a hunger, on both sides, to connect the kinds of inquiry and expertise that exist inside universities and outside of them. Blogs are one of our most promising connection points—and blog-based peer review offers one simple way for the two groups to contribute to common work. If my experience is any guide, this can elicit remarkably generous responses. Especially given that I am a public employee (I work for the University of California at Santa Cruz), I look forward to pursuing this type of public connection further in the future.
