**N**owadays, computers are capable of solving numerous problems. The problems can be as simple as adding two numbers or as complex as launching a missile. People who use computer most of the time assume that a computer does all the work on its own; however, it is not true. Any problem whose solution is not known cannot be solved using a computer. A computer only works on the set of instructions given to it by a programmer. If it does not understand any of the given instructions, the solution may go haywire. Hence, the sole responsibility of devising a solution to the problem lies in the hands of the programmer. This chapter discusses a general approach of solving a problem and various techniques used to reach to a solution of any given problem.

# 1.1 Steps for Solving Problem

As mentioned earlier, it is humans who solved the problems, not the computers. A computer simply assists us in solving the problems faster. Irrespective of the type and complexity of problems, we need to follow the steps mentioned below to solve them:

+   Define the problem
Identify the input, output, and constraints
Find various alternative solutions
Select the best possible alternative
Prepare detailed stepwise result for the identified alternative
Compute the required result using the identified set of instructions
Check the correctness of the answer obtained

These steps, though look simple, require a good amount of efforts from the user while devising. The steps 1 through 5 are all performed by the user. The work of a computer comes into picture only in the 6$^{th}$ and 7$^{th}$ steps. This simply suggests that the critical part of achieving the solution to a problem is done by the user.

Let's take an example of a problem. Assume that we need to know whether Mr. Mohit Arolkar is eligible to vote in assembly elections for India or not. To get an answer to the preceding problem, knowledge of two things is important: the current age and citizenship status of Mr. Mohit Arolkar. Therefore, two main inputs to solve the given problem would be current age and citizenship status. If the current age and citizenship status are provided to the computer through instruction, the solution is applicable for any person
