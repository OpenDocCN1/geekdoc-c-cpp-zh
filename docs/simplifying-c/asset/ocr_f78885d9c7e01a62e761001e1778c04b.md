visible in the output. We may wonder at this point as to what happened to character t entered at the time of input. It is still in the stream; it will be either allocated to new variable or dropped from consideration, depending on the data type of the new variable. In case the next variable to be read is of character data type, t will be allocated to it; in all other cases, it will be simply dropped from the stream.
