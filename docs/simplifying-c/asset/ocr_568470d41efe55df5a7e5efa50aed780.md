The major role of precedence and associativity is to help the compiler evaluate an expression in C language. The next section discusses how expressions are evaluated in C language.

# 5.3 Expression Evaluation in C

Till now our focus of discussion was on different operators available in C and their usage in expression. Evaluation of expression is a major concern if the statements are not properly formed. Consider the expression: result = first * second + third / fourth – fifth. In the preceding expression, the multiplication of identifiers first and second will be done initially, and its value will be stored in a temporary location. Then third will be divided by fourth and its value will be stored in another temporary location. The values in both the temporary locations will then be added, and finally fifth will be subtracted.
