We'll talk about the important settings in the next section, but I'd like to point out that for my project, I set the Sky Light Color to a light blue. This was a stylistic decision as I often find a good artistic technique is to use a warm color for highlights such as sunlight, and then balance this with a cool tone for the shadows and indirect lighting as can be seen in Fig. 8.14.

The Maps tab allows you to choose the lightmap used for your scene and to toggle the option to compress the map. Once you've adjusted the lightmap settings to your liking, you'll then need to press the Bake button as shown in Fig. 8.15.
