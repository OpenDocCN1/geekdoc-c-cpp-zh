However, Apple's App Store is not without its controversy, and as a game developer, it's best to always look at every opportunity. Many have stated that the market is way over saturated and tough for the independent game developers to get noticed. Of course, the amazing development team behind Unity was able to weather this storm and as it turned out, Apple amended the TOS to allow third-party development tools.

I really like Apple's development environment and solely choose to develop for the iPhone and iPad. A standardized development environment, backed with powerful hardware capable of running high-end games and a massive marketplace, makes using Unity iOS and Apple's iDevices a premiere solution for independent game developers. With that said, as a game developer, it's always best to not put all of your eggs in one basket so to speak. Diversifying your platforms can be a good thing, and although Apple's platform is great, it's not the only game in town. There's Android and Windows Mobile to name a few other key players in the mobile market.
