# RAM

There is a difference in RAM among the iDevices. The iPhone 4 contains twice the amount of RAM of the iPad and 3GS at 512 MB while both the 3GS and iPad contain only 256 MB. It's important to understand the RAM available and what you have to work with. The entire amount of RAM is available to your application, as some of it must be saved for running the OS and other apps with multitasking. Your textures are usually the main culprit when it comes eating up RAM in your game. That's why, it's very important to use optimized compressed textures to minimize the RAM usage in your game. In Unity iOS, you can use the Statistics window to check the video RAM (VRAM) usage of your scene as shown in Fig. 1.9.
