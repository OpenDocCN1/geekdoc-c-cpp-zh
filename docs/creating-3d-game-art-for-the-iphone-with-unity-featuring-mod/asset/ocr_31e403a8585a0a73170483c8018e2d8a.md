# ## Meet “Tater”

I had a concept for Tater and contacted Warner to help flesh out and add his artistic expertise to Tater's design. Not being an illustrator myself, I handed Warner some very basic sketches of the design and he worked his magic from there, producing the version of Tater showcased in this book.
