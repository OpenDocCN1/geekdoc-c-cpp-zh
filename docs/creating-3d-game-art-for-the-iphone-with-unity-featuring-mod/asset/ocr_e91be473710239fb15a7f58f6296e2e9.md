Taking a look at Tater's torso, you can also see how terminating edge loops with triangles as shown in Fig. 2.19 controlled resolution differences from the pants to the torso. Adding edge loops to your mesh can be vital to adding detail. For instance, as shown in Fig. 2.19, Tater's pants needed an extra edge loop to keep the roundist form, however, I would have wasted vertices if I would have continued that loop up the torso and through the head.

Building on the concept of terminating edge loops, in Fig. 2.20, you can see how I handled a five-point polygon on Thumper's barrel. Instead of continuing the edge loop down the barrel of the gun, which is represented by the dashed blue line, I carried it up over the top and back around as shown by the yellow line. By doing this, I split the five-point polygon into a triangle and a quad polygon.
