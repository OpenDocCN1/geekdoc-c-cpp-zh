Creating visually stunning games for the iPhone and iPad is easier and more exciting than ever when you combine the cutting-edge game engine Unity iOS with artist-friendly modo, and also Blender, the free open-source 3D app. Get up to speed quickly with *Creating 3D Game Art for the iPhone with Unity*, the only book that takes you through creating optimized game art for the iPhone and iPad with a real-world approach.

Author Wes McDermott delivers production-proven techniques, captivating 3D imagery, and valuable tips and tricks you need to model, animate, and texture your characters and environment assets, optimized for Apple iDevices—all in a clear, motivating, and entertaining style.

+   - Learn how to create optimized game assets for the iPhone and iPad using Unity iOS
+   - Improve your game's performance while avoiding common development pitfalls with detailed information on batching, building models on the grid, and lightmapping with Beast
+   - Gain access to video walkthroughs and information on the FREE iPhone/iPad app, *Tater’s Training Trash Yard*, which demonstrates core concepts and techniques covered in the book at www.wesmcdermott.com

**Wes McDermott** has been a professional 3D artist for 11 years and is currently a Multimedia Developer for the Art Institute at the Flight Training Center where he develops 3D elements for video and interactive online training. The author of *Real World modo: In the Trenches with modo*, Wes has also been published in HDRI 3D magazine and is a featured artist at Luxology.com. He is a beta tester for Luxology, Autodesk, and Unity Technologies.
