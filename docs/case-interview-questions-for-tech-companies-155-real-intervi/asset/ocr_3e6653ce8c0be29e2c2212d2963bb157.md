# Selling X on Amazon.com

Imagine you are a supplier considering an opportunity to sell your flagship X on the Amazon.com marketplace.

What challenges would you face? And how would you overcome those challenges?

Things to Consider

+   * What's the expected demand forecast?
* What's the best way to fulfill and ship products?
* How would you promote the product on Amazon's merchandising pages?
* What additional promotion will you do outside of the Amazon.com marketplace?
* How would you determine the price?

Common Mistakes

+   * Forget about setting price or assuming that determining price is easy
