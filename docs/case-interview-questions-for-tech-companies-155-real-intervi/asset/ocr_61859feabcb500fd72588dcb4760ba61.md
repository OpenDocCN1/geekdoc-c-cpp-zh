# Bēance Metrics

Imagine you are the product manager for Bēance, a social network where designers display and discover creative work. What's the most important metric for your product?

Things to Consider

+   - AARM framework
- Popular metrics for user-generated content sites, featured in PM Interview Questions

Common Mistakes

+   - Forgetting about timeframe for a particular metric
- Choosing a lagging metric as Bēance likes vs. a leading metric such as projects uploaded

Bēance users are looking for visual inspiration. Users won't feel inspired if projects aren't fresh and new. Therefore, I would say the most important metric is the growth in new projects. That metric will drive growth for downstream metrics including:

+   - Visitors
- Returning visitors
- Views
