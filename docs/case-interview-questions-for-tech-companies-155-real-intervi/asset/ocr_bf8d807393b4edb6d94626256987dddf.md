INTERVIEWER: Wouldn't users be annoyed by being bombarded by ads when they are trying to enjoy their dinner?

CANDIDATE: We really need to approach this from another angle. Why do people think ads are intrusive? It's only bad when the ads aren't relevant to you. For example, while you are in line, you have nothing to do so seeing interesting news and ads is a good experience. While you are ordering food, getting an ad for a price off is of value to the user. We can even promote things like local events (e.g. theaters, shows, clubs) to the user. If they are out for dinner, they are probably going to stay out that too. This would be perfect as the ads are contextual.
