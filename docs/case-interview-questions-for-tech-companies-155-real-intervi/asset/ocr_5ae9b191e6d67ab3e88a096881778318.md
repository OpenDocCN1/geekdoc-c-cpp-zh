# Chapter 9 Product Design: Brainstorm Exercises

# Netflix for Kids

Brainstorm at least 10 solutions to flag age inappropriate content for kids less than 10 years old.

# Answer

Flag content based on:

+   1. Motion Picture Association of America (MPAA) ratings.
2. Known list of directors, with age inappropriate material.
3. Known list of actors, with age inappropriate material.
4. Known list of movie and TV studios, with age inappropriate material.
5. Content descriptions.
6. Visual recognition of inappropriate material, using human screeners.
7. Visual recognition of inappropriate material, using artificial intelligence.
8. Audio recognition of inappropriate language.
9. Text recognition of inappropriate language in transcripts.
10. Crowdsourced user reports.
