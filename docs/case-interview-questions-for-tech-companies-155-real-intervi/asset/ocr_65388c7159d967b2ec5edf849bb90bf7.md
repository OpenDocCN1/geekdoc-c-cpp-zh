# Prioritize Sales Metrics

The sales team's goal is to achieve $5M in new revenue each quarter. What's the one metric we should focus on?

Things to Consider

+   - What are the sales drivers? Hint: Outlining the sales funnel will help.
- Which goals are measurable, actionable and relevant to end goal?

Common Mistakes

+   - Choosing a metric without justification
- Not brainstorming a list of potential metrics first
