# Non-Answering Supplier

What happened if the supplier does not answer your call or reply to your email? What will you do?

Things to Consider

This is largely a brainstorming exercise on different influence tactics. Here are some ideas to start:

+   - Reminders
- Using urgency or deadlines
- Finding another contact such as your primary contact's boss
- Activating the reciprocity principle (e.g. “I'll get your child a Michael Jordan autograph if you make you email a high priority.")

Common Mistakes

+   - Being too passive about the situation. That is, assuming everything is okay when it's not.
- Being too aggressive about the situation. For instance, yelling at the supplier or being unnecessarily needy.

# Answer

CANDIDATE: I would first see if I could reach them through other methods, like texting, other instant messaging apps or faxing. If it's not time-sensitive, I could try calling. I would then try to see if I reach someone else besides my primary contact in the supplier's office. My contact could be busy right now. If not or the supplier is a one-man supplier, I could drive to the office if they are close enough or I can ask someone from the local Amazon office to do so if they are close enough. Obviously, this depends on how important this supplier is. It's unlikely I am responsible for only one supplier, so I might ignore this supplier up if this isn't a very big supplier. Likewise, if this supplier's pipeline were minimal, I would ignore them too.

Obviously, I would be able to keep track of everyone and not ignore any of them in case of problems. If I have to prioritize, I will prioritize.

If nothing works by this point, I can confirm this supplier cannot be reached any time soon. It's time to think of alternatives. I will first see if I can find another supplier, preferably one we are already working with, who can provide us with replacements. I'll try to find one that provides replacements that are as high quality and around the same price and amount. I'll also see if I can find alternatives (e.g. different brand). I'll also see if I need to limit the amount of purchases by account on these products so we don't run out of stock that fast.

INTERVIEWER: What if this is needed for a major holiday sale or some other sort of deadline?

CANDIDATE: Besides the methods above, I would also see if we could push the deadline back if this is centerpiece for. If not, take it out of the centerpiece and probably the whole altogether.
