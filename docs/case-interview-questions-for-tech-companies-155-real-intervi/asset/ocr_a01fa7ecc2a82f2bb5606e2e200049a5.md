# Lower Revenue in Canada

In the US and UK, Google receives X dollars of revenue from the US per Y searches.
However, in Canada, Google only gets 0.5X revenue per Y searches.

What financial analyses would you conduct to explain the revenue differential?

Things to Consider

+   - Consider search revenue drivers including CPC and CTR.
- The search query mix may be different. For example, a smaller portion of Canada’s searches are for commercial intent. "Buy flowers" is an example of a commercial search query whereas "What is the longest river in the world" is an example of an informational search query.

# Answer

CANDIDATE: Here are some analyses that I would conduct, based on the following hypotheses:

+   - **Dashboard data errors.** I would start by checking if the dashboard is correct. Sometimes there are errors in the data warehouse’s structure, transform and load processes, leading to false reports.
- **Differences in user population.** Canadians are less likely to buy online vs. the US and the UK. Given the lower ROI, online retailers are less likely to bid as much for advertising. Maybe Canadians are more aggressively using ad-blocking technologies like Adblock.
- **Differences in advertiser position.** Even if consumer purchase behavior was similar, Canadian advertisers may differ. For instance, perhaps certain Canadian verticals may not be as competitive as the US or UK. This could result less competition for the number of ad slots, leading to lower prices.
- **Differences in mobile vs. PC search share.** Typically, revenue per mobile is less than revenue per PC. If Canada’s mobile is different from the US or UK, it could explain country differences in revenue search.
- **Differences in operating system or browser search.** Similar argument to the previous, revenue per search can differ based on operating system (Windows vs. Mac) or browser (Chrome vs. Firefox. Internet Explorer). If Canada’s is different from the US or UK, it could explain the differences.
- **Differences in the product.** Perhaps the Canadian version is different from the rest of the world. Perhaps the Canadian version has a different UI. Or maybe it has a different algorithm for determining ad placement.
