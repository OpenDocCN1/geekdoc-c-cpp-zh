# Repricing Amazon Prime

How would you reprice Amazon Prime if your goal was to increase profitability?

Things to Consider

+   - Profit = revenue – cost.
- Revenue = price * quantity.
- Cost = fixed cost + (variable cost * quantity)

Common Mistakes

+   - Fixating on price changes only.
- Not considering how costs can be reduced.
- Factoring only impact on new Prime customers and not existing Prime customers.
