# Interview Evaluation Sheet: Metrics

Understanding Metrics
Did the candidate have an understanding of product metrics? Did the candidate provide a comprehensive and relevant list?

Evaluating Metrics
Did the candidate articulate which metrics are better than others, backed with sound logic and evidence? Did the candidate provide an issue tree depicting drivers that affect specific metric?

Diagnosis Metrics
How was the candidate's diagnosis? Did the candidate offer a plan on how to positively influence a metric, through product changes but perhaps through other levers, including marketing and business development initiatives?
