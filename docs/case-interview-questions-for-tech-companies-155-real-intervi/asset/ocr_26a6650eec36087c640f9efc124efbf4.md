# Yahoo’s Net Income Percentage

Estimate Yahoo’s net income percentage.

Things to Consider

+   - Ask or verify the net income percentage definition so that you are on the same page as the interviewer.
- Think about all the different revenue drivers of Yahoo’s business including ad and enterprise sales.
- Don’t forget about operational costs.

Common Mistakes

+   - Not wanting to answer because one is not familiar with the net income definition.
- Think that the interviewer wants to understand the process for finding this information vs. giving an estimate.
- Blurting out an unsubstantiated guess without rationale or calculations.
