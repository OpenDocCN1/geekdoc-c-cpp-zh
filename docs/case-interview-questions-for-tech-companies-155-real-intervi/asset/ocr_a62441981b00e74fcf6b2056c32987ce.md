# Solving Supply Constraints

How would you solve supply constraints and expand the supplier base in the face of an imminent product release?

Things to Consider

+   - There's nothing more discouraging than strong customer demand and not having inventory to fulfill.
- This question tests your ability to come up with creative ways to get more inventory to fill demand.

Common Mistakes

+   - Raising issues that will not address the supply constraint (e.g. lowering cost of critical inputs).
