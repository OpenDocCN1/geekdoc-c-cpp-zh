# Crowdfunding Website Pain Points

Rant about the customer, not creator, experience with Kickstarter.

# Answer

+   1. Unclear ship dates.
2. Late ship dates
3. Some products never ship at all.
4. Don't know who to invite.
5. Products are overwhelming.
6. There's no verification of a project's claims.
7. Popular pledge categories are limited and sold out already.
8. No or limited updates.
9. Don't like the peer pressure when friends ask to to back a project.
10. Limited information about the creator.
