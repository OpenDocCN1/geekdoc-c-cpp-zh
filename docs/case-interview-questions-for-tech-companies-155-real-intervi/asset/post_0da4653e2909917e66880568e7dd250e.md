CANDATE: Okay, I would make some assumptions:
- Rush hour is morning (7-9AM), lunch (12-1PM), and dinner (5-7PM) during the weekdays.
- Weekend rush hour would include late-night (12-2AM).
- Rush hour would probably see a 100% increase in traffic.
- Weekend would probably see a 50% increase in traffic.
Let's plug all of this into formula:

## Calculating cars for the week

Weekday Cars = Normal Hours * People per Hour * (1 + Rush Hours/Hours Day)
Weekday Cars = 19 * 1000 * (1 + 5/24)
Weekday Cars = ~23,000

## Calculating cars for the weekend

Weekend Cars = Normal Hours * People per Hour * (1 + Rush Hours/Hours Day)
Weekend Cars = 17 * 1000 * (2 + 7/24)
Weekend Cars = ~44,000

## Calculating cars for the whole week

Weekly Cars = Weekdays * Weekday Cars + Weekend Days * Weekend Cars
Weekly Cars = 5 * 23,000 + 2 * 44,000
Weekly Cars = 203,000

## Average cars per hour

Average Cars per Hour = Weekly Cars / (Days per Week * Hours per Day)
Average Cars per Hour = 203,000 / (7 * 24)
Average Cars per Hour = ~1,200
CANDATE: I say we need about 1200 cars per hour on average.
