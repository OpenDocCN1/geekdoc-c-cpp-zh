+   * All types of body lotion, including special varieties for babies or older people.

* 300 million people in the US.
* 75% of people use lotion once per day.
* 15% of people use lotion once per two days.
* 5% of people use lotion once per week.
* 5% of people don't use lotion.
* A typical bottle is about 1 liter.
* It takes about 6 months, so 60 x 3 = 120 uses to use the whole thing. That means it must be every 120 uses.
