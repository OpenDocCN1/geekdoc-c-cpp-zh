# Google and Teleporter

Google just invented the teleporter. Give me your go-to-market plan. Also add three features to this teleporter.

Things to Consider

+   - Use the Big Picture marketing framework to build your marketing plan.
- Trial usage will be a critical marketing goal.

Common Mistakes

+   - Providing marketing tactics only and neglecting marketing strategy, especially how the tactics tie into the strategy.
- Suggesting marketing ideas that are bland.

# Answer

CANDIDATE: Can I ask some clarifying questions?

INTERVIEWER: Sure.

CANDIDATE: I am assuming the teleporter transports people yes?

INTERVIEWER: Yes, it does.

CANDIDATE: It is more for transporting one or two people at a time or are we talking about 5-10? How big of a group are we talking about? Also, is it practical (cost-effective) to transport packages? Is there any limit to size, weight or number for those?

INTERVIEWER: It can transport 50 people at once. It can transport objects as well, around the same size and as 50 people. Number is not a limit, just the size. It's cost-effective for both cases.

CANDIDATE: Is there some sort of cool-down period for this machine? Do we also have hard requirement (e.g., lots of power, lots of space, government licenses) for this technology?

INTERVIEWER: There is around a 10-minute cool-down period for the machine. There is no hard requirement for this.

CANDIDATE: Can this teleports things into an empty space or does it need another teleporter on the other side?

INTERVIEWER: It needs another teleporter on the other side.
