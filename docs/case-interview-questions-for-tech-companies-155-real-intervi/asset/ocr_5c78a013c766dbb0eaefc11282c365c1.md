Sales Revenue = 1 billion * 0.15 * $200

Total Revenue = Ad Revenue + Enterprise Revenue + Sales Revenue

Total Revenue = $4.38 billion + $12 billion + $30 billion

# Calculating Operating Cost

Operating Cost = # Employees * Average Salary * Office Cost

Operating Cost = 60,000 * $100,000 * 1.1

Net Profit = Total Revenue * Profit Margin – Operation Cost

Net Profit = $46.38 billion * 0.5 – $6.6 billion

# Calculating Net Income Percentage

Net Income Percentage = 100 * Net Profit / Total Revenue

Net Income Percentage = 100 * $16.6 billion / $46.38 billion
