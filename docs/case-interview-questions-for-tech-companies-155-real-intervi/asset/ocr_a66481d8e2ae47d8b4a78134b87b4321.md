# Design a Windshield Wiper

Innovate a new windshield wiper design.

Things to Consider

+   - Windshield wipers is not something of us think about often. Take a moment to understand the purpose and pain points of a windshield wiper before proceeding.
- Decrease your probability of suggesting mediocre solutions by deeply exploring pain points.

Common Mistakes

+   - Suggesting a final recommendation that a combination of all your brainstorm ideas.
- Better candidates focus on brainstorm solutions for a single problem. It increases the chances of getting an impressive solution. Similarly, there's not enough time to come up with impressive solutions that are more than one problem to tackle.

# Answer

CANDIDATE: Is this a windshield wiper on a regular car or truck or something else altogether?

INTERVIEWER: This is a windshield wiper on a regular car.

CANDIDATE: Is there a cost restriction?

INTERVIEWER: Yes, it shouldn't cost over $50. Regular windshield wipers cost around $10.

CANDIDATE: Let me list out some problems with regular windshield wipers:

+   - Doesn't wipe the windshield.
- Hard time cleaning things like bird poop.
- Doesn't clean 100%.
- During heavy rain, it's not enough.
- Definitely can't power through snow.
- Can't ice on the windshield.

I have some ideas to address these problems:

+   - The windshield wiper needs to be redesigned in a way to not able to wipe most, if not all of the windshield. Not able to wipe the thing is not a big deal at first until you realize the dirt and things get stuck on your windshield because that's as far as the wiper goes.
- It needs have two separate modes. One for regular stuff (light dirt, rain) and one for stuff (bird poop). The blade needs to be designed a bit differently for the heavy mode.
- It should have two things it can disperse, water and soap. This needs to be hooked up to the car.
- Remember the wiper has two functions: clean and prevent rain. For the rain, during heavy rain it really isn't enough. It needs a smarter design. Maybe it can go to the top of the windshield and cover it, causing rain to drip over to the side of the car.
