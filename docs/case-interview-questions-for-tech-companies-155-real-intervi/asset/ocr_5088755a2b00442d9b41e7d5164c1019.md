# ## Financial Knowledge

Does the candidate demonstrate understanding and mastery of financial concepts? Can the candidate explain concepts to others?

# ## Analytical Ability

Is the candidate comfortable making calculations?

# ## Communication Skills

Can the candidate explain complex financial concepts in an easy-to-understand manner?

# ## Strategic Insight

Can the candidate connect their financial analysis with business strategy? Does the analysis recommend a specific course or course of action?
