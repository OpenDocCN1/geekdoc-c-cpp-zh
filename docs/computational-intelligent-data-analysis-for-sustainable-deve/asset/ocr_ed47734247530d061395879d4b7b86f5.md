The model that originates from biophysics (known in the literature as the FitzHugh-Nagumo system, [17, 25]) performs very well for power market modeling; see Lucheroni [26].

This model (written here in its discretized form) was developed by Lucheroni [26]:

$$f_{i+1}=f_i+z_i\Delta t \tag{8.1}$$

$$z_{i+1}=z_i+\left[\kappa(z_i+f_i)-\lambda(3f_i^2z_i+f_i^3)-\varepsilon z_i-\gamma f_i-b\right]\frac{\Delta t}{\varepsilon} \tag{8.2}$$

Here, $\omega_t$, and $a_t$ are noise terms, and $\Delta t$ is the time step. Equation (8.2) aims to exploit the fact that the current value of $z_t$ is useful to predict the future value $R_{t+1}$. The parameters $\kappa$, $\lambda$, $\varepsilon$, $\gamma$, and $b$ can be estimated using the method of ordinary least squares (OLS). For our deseasoned data, estimated values for the parameters are $\kappa=-2.1$, $\lambda=-6\times10^{-8}$, $\varepsilon=0.09$, $\gamma=0.5$, and $b=2$. $\lambda$ is virtually zero, which indicates that the deseasoned residuals $R_t$ behave linearly. Further to this, a negative value of $\kappa$ assures the stability of the inherent damped oscillator in Equation (8.2).
