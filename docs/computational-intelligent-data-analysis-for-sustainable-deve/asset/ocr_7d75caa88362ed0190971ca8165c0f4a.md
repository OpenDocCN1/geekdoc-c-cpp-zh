# 7.4.1 Data Management

Before addressing the actual data-analysis challenges, we present some considerations regarding data management. As motivated before; the rise of the *smart grid* leads to many large and new data sources. The most prominent sources of such data are *smart meters* (see Scenario 7.1). However, there are many more data sources, ranging from dynamic prices to data describing demand-response measures, to the use of energy storages and events in smart homes. In the following, we focus on smart-meter data. In Section 7.4.6.1 we deal with further data-management aspects in the context of the exploration and comparison of energy datasets.
