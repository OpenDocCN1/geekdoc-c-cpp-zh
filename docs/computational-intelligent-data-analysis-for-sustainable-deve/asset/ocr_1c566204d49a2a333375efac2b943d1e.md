$\hat{y}=\arg\max_{y\in Y} P(y\mid x)$ (10.21)

Note that the term in the denominator of Equation (10.20) remains constant; hence, for the purposes of finding the optimum, we can ignore its evaluation. We simply evaluate this conditional for the entire range of Y in discrete steps and pick the value of y∈Y that maximizes the conditional.
