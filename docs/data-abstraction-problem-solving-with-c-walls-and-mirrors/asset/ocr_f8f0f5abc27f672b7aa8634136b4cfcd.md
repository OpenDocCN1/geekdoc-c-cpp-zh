# Organization

His book's organization, sequencing, and pace of topic coverage makes learning and teaching easier by focusing your attention on one concept at a time, providing flexibility in the order in which you can cover topics, by clearly distinguishing between the specification and implementation of abstract data types, or ADTs. To accomplish these goals, we have organized the material into 21 chapters. Most chapters focus on the specification and use of an ADT or its various implementations. You can choose to cover the specification of an ADT followed by its implementations, or you treat the specification and use of several ADTs before you consider any implementation issues. The book's organization makes it easy for you to choose the topic that you need.
