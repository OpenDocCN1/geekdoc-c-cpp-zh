Notice that the algorithm does not specify the order of selection for the unvisited cities. It really does not matter what selection criteria the algorithm uses, because the choice will not affect the final outcome: Either a sequence of flights exists or does not. The choice, however, will affect the specific flights that the algorithm considers. For example, suppose the algorithm always follows the alphabetically unvisited city from the city to the top of the stack. Under this assumption, Figure 6-9 contains a trace of the algorithm's action, given the map in Figure 6-6, with P as the origin city and Z as the destination. The algorithm terminates successfully.
