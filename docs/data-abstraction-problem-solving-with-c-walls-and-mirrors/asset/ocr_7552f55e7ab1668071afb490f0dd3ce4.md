# 6.1.2 Specifications for the ADT Stack

We have identified the following operations for the ADT stack:

+   - See whether a is empty.
- Add a new item to the stack.
- Remove the item from the stack that was added most recently.
- Get the item that was added to the stack most recently.

The term "stack" is intended to conjure visions of things encountered in daily life, such as a stack of plates in the school cafeteria, a stack of books on your desk, or a stack of assignments that you need to work on. In common usage, "stack of" and "pile of" are synonymous. To computer scientists, however, a stack is not just any pile. The property that the last added to the stack will be the first one removed is commonly referred to as **last, first out** or simply LIFO.

LIFO: The last item
inserted onto a
stack is the first one
out

A stack of plates in a cafeteria makes a very good analogy of the abstract data type stack, Figure 6-1 illustrates. As new plates are added, the old ones drop further into the well beneath the surface. At any particular time, only the last plate placed on the stack is above the surface and visible. This plate is at the **top of the stack** and the one that must be removed. In general, the plates are placed in the opposite order from that they were added.

The LIFO property of stacks seems inherently unfair. Think of the poor person who finally gets the last on the cafeteria's stack, one that has been there for six years ago. Or would you like to be the first person to arrive on the stack for a movie—as opposed to the line for a movie. You would be the last person alone! These examples demonstrate the reason that stacks are not especially prevalent in everyday life. The property that we usually design in our daily lives is first, first out, or FIFO. A queue, which you will learn about in Chapter 13, is the ADT with the FIFO property. Most people would prefer to wait in a movie queue—as a line is called in Britain—than in a movie stack. However, while the LIFO queue of stacks is inappropriate for many everyday situations, it is precisely what is needed for a large number of problems in computer science.
