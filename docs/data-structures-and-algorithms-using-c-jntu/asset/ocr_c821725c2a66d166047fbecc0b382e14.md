Consider the graphs \( G_{17} \) and \( G_{18} \) shown in Figure 10.14. Both the graphs have 6 vertices and 6 edges. Also there exists one to one correspondences among them, which can be expressed as
Vertices a–3, b–1, c–2, d–4, e–5, f–6
Edges \( e_1–f_1, e_2–f_1, e_3–f_4, e_4–f_3, e_5–f_5, e_6–f_6 \)
Degree of all the vertices in \( G_{17} \) in a, b, c, d, e, f, is 2 and
Degree of all the vertices in \( G_{18} \) 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 is also 2
