# Iterating Collections: Iterators

In rest of this chapter, you see three different approaches to the general problem of iterating a collection. In this section, I continue discussing the most traditional approach (at least C# programmers), the iterator class, or enumerator, which implements the IEnumerator interface. For example, I take deeper into the iterator for the linked list, presented in the previous section.

The terms iterator and enumerator are synonymous. The term iterator is more common than the name of the interface, but enumerator has been popular at Microsoft. Verb forms of these two are also available: You iterate or enumerate through a collection or container. Note that the index and new blocks discussed in this chapter are other approaches to the same problem.
