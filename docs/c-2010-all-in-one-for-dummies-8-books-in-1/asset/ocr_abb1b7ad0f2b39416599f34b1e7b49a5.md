NET dictionaries are based on the idea of a hash table. Imagine a group of
bbuckets spread around the floor. When you compute a hash, using a hash
function get a value that specifies one of the buckets. That same
hash always points to the same bucket. If the hash is computed properly,
y should see a good, fairly even distribution of items among the
buckets. Thus the hash is a key to one of the buckets. Provide the key to
retrieve the bucket — its value.
