# About the Authors

Hi, my name is Bill Sf, and I am a software architect. Although I used to hate the term *architect*, it's clearly the only thing out there that defines what I do. My breadth of experience includes business and technical analysis, software design, development, testing, server management and maintenance, and in my 17 years of professional experience, I have participated in the creation of over 200 applications for large and small companies, managed the software infrastructure of two Internet service providers, coded complex software happily in every environment imaginable, and made mainframes talk to cellphones. In short, make the technology products that people are using every day nicely together.

I started playing with computers in 1979 and haven't looked back since. In 1985 I was helping my father (also named Bill) manage Apple II systems at the local library. Since then I have built applications for the likes of Bank Lucent, Technologies, Nationwide Insurance, and Sears, Roebuck and Co. I am the author of *Visual Basic 2008 For Dummies* and *Visual Basic 2005 For Dummies*; a coauthor of *Effective Visual Studio. ASP.NET Services, Professional VB.NET*; a frequent contributor to MSD, Builder.com, Hardcore Web Services, Cloud Computing Journal, Inside Web Development Journal, and Intranet Journal; and have been invited speaker for the ACM and IEE, DevEssentials, the International XML Web Services Expo, and the Association of Information Technology Professionals. I am a graduate of The Ohio State University with a bachelor's science degree in business administration, a Microsoft Certified Professional, Certified Internet Business Strategist, a credited Webmaster.
