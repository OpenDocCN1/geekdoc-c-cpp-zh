# Working without Primary Interop Assemblies

Speaking of PIAs (excuse the similarity to another well-known, three-letter acronym), they are handled by a lot better in .NET 4.0.

PIAs are large .NET assemblies generated from COM interfaces to facilitate strangely interopered. They provide great support at design time, where your experience of the Interop is as good as if the types were defined in .NET. However, at runtime these large assemblies can easily blast your program and also cause versioning issues because they are distributed independently of your application.
