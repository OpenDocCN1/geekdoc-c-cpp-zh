The BankAccount class is not unlike some in other chapters of this book. It begins for accounts that start out with an initial balance and another for which an initial balance of zero will have to suffer. Notice that this version of BankAccount doesn't take advantage of the little and greatest constructor advantages. If you read this entire chapter and then you can see why we chose to "drop back" a little later.

The balance property allows others to read the bank account without modifying them directly. The Deposit() method accepts any positive deposit. Withdraw() lets you take out as much as you want, as long as you have enough money in your account. (My bank's nice, but it's not that nice.)
BankAccountString() creates a string that describes the account.

The SavingsAccount class inherits all good stuff from BankAccount. It also adds an interest rate and the ability to accumulate interest at regular intervals.
