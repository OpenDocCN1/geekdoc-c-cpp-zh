# 5.2.7.2 Changing the Size of the Underlying Contiguous Range

When you change the size of the underlying contiguous range, the contiguous range may be reallocated, and the `std::span` refers to stale data. Only a `std::span` with a dynamic extent can have a resizable underlying contiguous range and can, therefore, be a victim of this subtle issue.
