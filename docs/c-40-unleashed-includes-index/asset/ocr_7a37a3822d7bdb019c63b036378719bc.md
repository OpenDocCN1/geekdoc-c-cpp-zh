usually realized by giving data records a unique identifier, which is referred to as a primary key. To refer to a data record from another table, a column is designated as a foreign key referring to the related table's primary key.

Figure 19.31 shows such a relationship, sometimes also referred to as an association, in the context of LINQ to SQL. In this particular case, one category can have many products associated with it, hence the OneToMany cardinality. To go from a Product to its Category, the Parent Property is set to Category. In the opposite direction, you can get the products in a given category through the Child Property named Products. At the bottom of the Properties window, the underlying relationship is shown:

```
Category.CategoryID -> Product.CategoryID
```
