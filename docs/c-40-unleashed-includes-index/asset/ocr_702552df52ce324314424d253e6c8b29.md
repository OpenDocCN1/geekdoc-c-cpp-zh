First of all, observe how the Memory window displays the floating-point number as 0.299... rather than 0.300..., illustrating the imprecision we're talking about. In the hexadecimal window, take a look at the byte pattern. Actually, it's shown in reverse due to the "endianness" of the platform. It really says 3FD3333333333333. Based on the floating-point formula shown earlier, you can reconstruct the decimal value as a little exercise. (Notice we're in the 64-bit "double-precision" situation now.)
