# 4.3. Classification of CA rules according to the non-robust periodic orbits

Generally, in addition to the robust periodic orbits, which are generated starting from a randomly chosen initial state, CA rules have also non-robust periodic orbits, which can be reached only from some specific initial states. When they exist, the density of non-robust periodic orbits tends to 0 as the size of the state space grows. We recall that the most complex rules — ie, those of groups C5 and C6 in Chua’s classification — do not even have robust periodic orbits.
