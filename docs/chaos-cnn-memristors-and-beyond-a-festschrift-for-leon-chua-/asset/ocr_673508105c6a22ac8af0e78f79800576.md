# 5. Numerical Analysis of Spatio-temporal Dynamics of the Network

Let us consider the network where the first neuron is subjected to time-varied external (applied) current in the form of a sequence of two square pulses (I1ext(t) = 0.13 + Itsp(t)) with the same amplitude (Ast), the same duration (Tst = 10) and interpulse interval τ. Results of numerics show that depending on the characteristics of external stimulation there are two nontrivial spatio-temporal activity regimes of the network.

The first regime is shown on Fig. 9. Here Figure 9(a) depicts corresponding spatio-temporal diagram of synaptic activation and Fig. 9(b) — waveforms of synaptic conductances. The spatio-temporal diagram of synaptic activation is constructed as following. We considered the rectangles Di(a,b), i = 1,2,3,4 on the plane with the horizontal axis corresponding to the time and vertical axis corresponding to the synapse number. The height of Di(a,b)

is 1, the width is (b − a), the lower-left corner has coordinates (a, i), and it is black if the synapse i is active at the instant t (s′i(t) > 0), a < t < b. It easy to see that the spatio-temporal order of synaptic activation obtained for the regime (see Fig. 9(b) as well) is in good correspondence with that for the attractor A2 of the CA. Note, however, that it is not fully periodic. This is related to the fact that the resonance condition T2 = 2T1 is satisfied only with some (nonzero) accuracy. Nevertheless, first of all, the spacial order of activation of synapses is exactly the same as for the CA, and, secondly, temporal intervals of activation are approximately the same as for the CA up to the scaling factor T1. On Fig. 9(c) waveforms of membrane potentials for different neurons are presented. We see that the temporal intervals of firing and the rest of neurons occur in the order prescribed by the order of synaptic activation.
