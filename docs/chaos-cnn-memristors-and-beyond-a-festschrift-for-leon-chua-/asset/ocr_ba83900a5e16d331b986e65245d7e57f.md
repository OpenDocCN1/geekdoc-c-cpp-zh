# 7. Conclusion

We browsed many papers published by Chua and co-workers over two decades. We selected different types of attractors and presented them more or less in a chronological order. Chua is mostly known for the Chua circuit and the memristor but we showed that he contributed to chaos theory by providing many electronic circuits which can serve as specific experimental realizations producing the different types of chaotic attractors identified in the general framework of the nonlinear dynamical systems theory. Driven by his rigorous analytical approach, he can be considered as making the bridge between mathematical contributions by Levinson or Cartwright, for instance, and a general theory for electronic circuits.
