For example, managers and politicians must be obliged to personal accountability of risky investments, decisions, and actions. With respect to local responsibility, we can no longer accept that profits are individual, but wastages socialized. During a long evolution, cellular self-organization of organisms was embedded in a hierarchy of control processors, emerging in a learning process with changing challenges of environments. In engineering science, we should aim at self-organizing systems with controlled emergence of new appropriate features. By detecting global trends of complex dynamics, we have the chance of implementing favourite tendencies. By cooperation of locally active agents in complex systems we can make much more progress in choosing our next steps. Cooperation in complex systems supports deciding and acting for a sustainable future of a complex world.
