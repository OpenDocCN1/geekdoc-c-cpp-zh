characteristic function $\chi_N^\tau$ and the union of the time-$\tau$ return plots, for all possible $x^0$, is equivalent to the time-1 characteristic function.

An alternative way to represent the dynamics of an elementary CA rule $\mathcal{N}$ is the Lameray diagram, also called cobweb plot [29]. It is similar to time-1 return plot, because it follows the evolution of a single binary array $x^0$ throughout time, but with two main differences: first, we discard the transient and hence the circuit depicted will always be closed; second, we draw not only the points but also the lines connecting them (see Fig. 3(b)).

# 6. Final Comments on Chua’s Original Contributions to the Study of Cellular Automata

The contribution given by Chua goes much beyond what has been presented in this paper, which has been rather focused on the fundamental notions of the ‘Nonlinear Dynamics Perspective of Cellular Automata’ in order to provide a synthetic, though comprehensive, overview of Chua’s works. It is fair to say that some of the results included in Chua’s papers had already been proved by other authors. Of course, Chua was aware of it, and this is why his papers have been presented in the ‘tutorial’ section of the journals in which he published. Nevertheless, this circumstance is sometimes used to deny the originality of his approach. For this reason, we think that is useful to conclude this chapter by discussing what ideas among the many given by Chua represent a genuine breakthrough in this area.

The starting point of Chua’s discourse is the formalization of the main characteristics of elementary CA rules, which we discussed in Sec. 2. This model of Cellular Automata is the most studied in the scientific literature, thus the majority of the concepts contained in that section, from the numbering of rules through Eq. (2) to the existence of globally-equivalent rules, are well-known. However, to the best of our knowledge, the definition of quasi-globally equivalent rules was introduced for the first time by Chua and we can expect that it will become part of the classical theory of Cellular Automata in the near future.

The study of the local dynamics of the single cell as a continuous-time nonlinear dynamical system, described in Sec. 3.1 is definitely among the most outstanding and original contributions by Chua. In fact, only by taking that approach it is possible to introduce rigorously the notion of index

of complexity of an elementary CA rule and link it to the concept of Boolean cube. The discussion about the rotation classes of Boolean cubes, as displayed in Table 8, is also novel and not fully studied yet, for which we may expect that soon it will lead to new results.

Conversely, the global dynamics of Cellular Automata (see Sec. 4) has been the object of study of innumerable papers, and hence it is more difficult to realize what can be really considered original. We feel confident saying that even though other authors have analyzed the periodic orbits of elementary CA rules, probably nobody before Chua has proposed the rigorous classification in six classes shown in Table 11 as well as the analysis of non-robust periodic orbits in order to perform a more accurate study of the elementary CA rules.

Finally, most of the ideas of Sec. 5 are very similar to those of other authors, including Wolfram. Probably, the main merit by Chua has been the emphasis that has put on the importance of these tools to draw general conclusion about CA rules. For this purpose, he included a systematic study of the time-$\tau$ characteristic functions, the time-$\tau$ return plots, and the cobweb plots for *all* 256 elementary CA rules in his papers, which can be easily used as a reference even by unexperienced readers.

In conclusion, Chua’s contribution to Cellular Automata has been fundamental and it definitely deserves the same credit as those by Ulam, von Neumann, Conway, and Wolfram. We can expect that an increasing number of researchers in the near future will be inspired by his works and possibly use them to provide further breakthroughs in this area.
