The great creative possibilities linked to the circuit have persuaded us to start an international experimental program together with the international association of art, science and technology Leonardo (http://www.leonardo.info/). At the beginning, the project is only addressed at associated members but later it will be extended to all interested people. Its aim is the divulgation of Chua’s attractors as tools for artistic, musical, and 3D creation.
