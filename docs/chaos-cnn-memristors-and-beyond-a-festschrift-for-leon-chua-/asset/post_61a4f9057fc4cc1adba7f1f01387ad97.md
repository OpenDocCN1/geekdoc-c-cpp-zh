The CA has three attractors: A0, A1 and A2. They correspond to various scenarios of activation of synapses of the network and so define different firing patterns of neurons.

The attractor A0 is the fixed point (0000). Here we denote the states of synapses by numbers: P0 → 0, P11 → 1, P21 → 2, P22 → 3. The attractor A0 is trivial — if all elements of the network are activated then all of them come to the rest state P0 next episode.

The attractor A1 is the periodic trajectory (1000) → (0200) → (0300) → (0011) → (1000). A part of the basin of A1 is shown on Fig. 7(a). Note that the periodic points in the attractor are marked by the grey color. Figure 7(b) depicts spatio-temporal order of synaptic activation determined by periodic attractor A1. It is constructed as follows. If i-th synapse in k-th episode is active, i.e. it is in one of the states P11, P21 or P22, corresponding rectangle is marked by the black color. On the other hand, if i-th synapse in k-th episode is silent, i.e. it is in the state P0, corresponding rectangle is marked by the white color. From the symbolic sequence (and spatio-temporal diagram of synaptic activation as well) corresponding to A1 it is easy to obtain the following order of firings of neurons: 1 → 2 → 3,4 and so on. Indeed, it is well known that a neuron fires the spikes when corresponding synaptic conductance overcomes some threshold value close to maximal conductance. In our case it corresponds to narrow time windows in the vicinity of transitions of synapses from the state P11 (or P22) to P0 (or similarly to the narrow regions in the vicinity of transitions from black to white rectangles on spatio-temporal diagram of synaptic activation).

The attractor A2 is the periodic trajectory (2000) → (3000) → (0100) → (0021) → (0030) → (2000). A part of the basin of A2 is shown on Fig. 8(a). Figure 8(b) depicts spatio-temporal order of synaptic activation determined by periodic attractor A2. From the symbolic sequence corresponding to A2 one can obtain the following order of firings of neurons: 1 → 2 → 4 → 3 and so on.
