## 6. Conclusions

We have presented a model of chaotic neural networks and its several extensions. Although we explained only memory related dynamics as an application, the models of chaotic neural networks have many other applications [40]. In particular, combinatorial optimization is one important application. Optimization dynamics of the chaotic neural networks is clarified both numerically [41, 42] and mathematically [43–45], and practical algorithms are also developed [46–49] (see Ikeguchi et al. [50] for a recent review). The formulation of the chaotic neural networks is a natural method to generate transitive dynamics among fixed point pseudo-attractors. Such transitive dynamics is realized by destabilization of fixed point attractors due to refractory effects, or equivalently self-recurrent inhibitory connections peculiar to chaotic neurons. It should be noted that transitive dynamics may be related to higher-brain functions in the prefrontal cortex [51].

Moreover, models of chaotic neural network have been implemented on electronic circuits [52–54]. Our future aim is to analyse spatio-temporal dynamics of extended chaotic neural networks in detail, consider their relations with the brain dynamics, and implement their electronic circuits for practical applications.
