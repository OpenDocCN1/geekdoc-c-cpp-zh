The first step is to determine the approximate linear operating region of the LDR as a relationship of the voltage across the LED, through a 1 kΩ resistor, and the conductance of the LDR. These data are used to set the offset voltage of the MMC to the center of the linear region.

Figure 9 shows the approximate linear region is between 4 V and 9 V, giving a fitted linear expression of

```
G(v) = 0.3v + 0.11. (8)
```

The MMC is examined and defined using (8) through (14). For input v1(t),

v2(t) = - R3/R2 v1(t). (9)

```
v4(t) = -1/τ ∫ v2(t) dt
= -1/(R4 C1) ∫ v2(t) dt = vφ. (10)
```
