# 3.5. Inductor

The inductor in Chua’s circuit can be realized by a physical inductor. Real inductors are wirewound components and have parasitic resistance associated with the windings of the coil. If this resistance is taken into account, then a physical realization of Chua’s circuit using a real inductor has a resistor R0 in series with the inductor. A problem with the early realizations of Chua’s circuit was that the inductors
