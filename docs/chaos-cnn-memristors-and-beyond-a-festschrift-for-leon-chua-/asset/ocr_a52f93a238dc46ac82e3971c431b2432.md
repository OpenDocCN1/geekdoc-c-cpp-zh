# 3.6. *Integrated circuit realization*

Cruz and Chua realized a Chua’s circuit in integrated circuit form using a gyrator-based implementation of the inductor [9]. Figure 28 shows the circuit schematic. Operational Transconductance Amplifiers OTA A and OTA B form the nonlinear resistor $N_R$. The gyrator comprising OTA C and OTA D terminated in capacitor $C_3$ emulates an inductance. Resistor $R$ is connected externally between pins 1 and 2. The bias block between pins 4 and 5 provides the required bias voltage. Figure 29 shows a measured double scroll attractor in this circuit.
