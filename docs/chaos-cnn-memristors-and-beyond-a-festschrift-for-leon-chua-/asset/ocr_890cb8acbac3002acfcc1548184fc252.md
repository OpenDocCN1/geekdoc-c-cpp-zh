A new impact on this area of research is linked with the real implementation of the memristor concept. Last three years have brought a wide-spread interest in applications of circuits containing memristors.

This is due to the fact that after a long period since this kind of elements have been proposed by L.O. Chua [12] there has been a breakthrough in technology permitting the fabrication of microelectronic memristive devices [21,22]. In this paper we study and compare bifurcation phenomena observed in the original Chua's circuit, its variant called Chua's oscillator (with a series resistor added in series with the inductor) and three types of memristor oscillators derived from the original Chua's circuit by replacement of some elements by a suitable memristive element proposed by Itoh and Chua [15]. The circuit configurations considered are shown in Fig. 1.

The dimension-less equations governing the prototype Chua's circuit read

```
dx/dt = α(y − f(x)),
 dy/dt = x − y + z,
 dz/dt = −βy,
```

while for the Chua's oscillator we add one term in the RHS of the first equation

```
dx/dt = α(y − x − f(x)),
 dy/dt = x − y + z,
 dz/dt = −βy,
```
