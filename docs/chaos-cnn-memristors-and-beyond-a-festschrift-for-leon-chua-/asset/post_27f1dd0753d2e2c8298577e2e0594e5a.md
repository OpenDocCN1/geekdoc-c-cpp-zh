### NONLINEAR DYNAMICS OF A CLASS OF PIECEWISE LINEAR SYSTEMS

M. LAKSHMANAN

Centre for Nonlinear Dynamics, School of Physics,

K. MURALI

Department of Physics, Anna University, Chennai 600025, India
kmurali@annauniv.edu

Piecewise linear dynamical systems constitute an interesting class of nonlinear dynamical systems exhibiting many kinds of bifurcations and chaos, synchronizations and spatiotemporal patterns. They can be relatively easily designed through electronic circuits and the corresponding dynamical equations can be numerically analysed and theoretically investigated. The ubiquitous Chua’s circuit, Murali-Lakshmanan-Chua (MLC) circuit, memristor based circuits, and negative conductance forced LCR circuits, as individual and coupled oscillators, are some of the well studied piecewise linear dynamical systems. More interestingly time-delay can be introduced in such circuits to study plethora of novel effects in complex systems, including networks. In this article, recent progress in understanding the nonlinear dynamics of such systems, especially of MLC circuits and its variants, will be briefly reviewed.
