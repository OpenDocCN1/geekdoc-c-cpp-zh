We can summarize the ESG's Science & Art route as follows:

+   - a series of theoretical research projects has led some researchers in the group to identify a variety of strange attractors, derived from Chua's circuit;
+   - these attractors have been represented as images, sounds and music by computational algorithms, embodied into software applications;
+   - from these applications, art forms such as 2D and 3D images, sound and musical compositions have been produced.

The group has also created a series of research products such as design elements, logos and other visual communication tools, new musical instruments, software environments for sound and music production and prototypes of video games. Another representation of the attractors, in interactive contexts, has allowed the construction of virtual imaginary environments and multimedia theatre performances [17–21].
