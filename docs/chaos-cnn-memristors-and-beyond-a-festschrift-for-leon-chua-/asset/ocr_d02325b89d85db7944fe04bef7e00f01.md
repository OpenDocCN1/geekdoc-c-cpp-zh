window undergoing period doubling sequence etc. In Fig. 12 we notice standard period-3 window and also a sequence of high-period windows starting with period-26 one.

One can notice also comparing the bifurcation trees obtained for the original Chua’s circuit and Chua’s oscillator with those for memristor-based oscillators that the extra degree of freedom introduced by the memristive element for the bifurcation trees become more developed and complicated. The period doubling sequences persist but within the chaotic range we have a very rich variety of periodic windows and changes of attractor size (jumps).

# 4. Conclusions

In this paper we reported a study of bifurcation phenomena observed in the family of circuits based on classic Chua’s circuit using memristors. Finding the ranges of existence of various dynamic behaviors such as fixed points, periodic orbits, chaos ranges has not been reported before and is clearly important for understanding of circuit dynamics and finding possible applications. Memristor circuits have been already confirmed to have a variety of applications e.g. in memory circuits [3,14,20,23].
