The mathematical model of the Chua’s circuit (5) has been numerically integrated for $T=300\,s$ with a fixed integration step $\Delta t=10\,ms$, thus obtaining $N=30000$ points. For each point $\bar{x}_i$ in the trajectory the first recurrence time $\tau_1(r)$ has been calculated according to Eq. (3). To explicit the dependence (index $i$) on the starting point $\bar{x}_i$ of the trajectory, let us now rewrite it as $\tau_1(r)=\tau_1(r,\bar{x}_i)$.

We first studied the first recurrence times for the Chua’s double scroll attractor at $\alpha=9$. The values of $\tau_1(r,\bar{x}_i)$ have been then reported in a color-coded graph, where the position represents the initial starting point $\bar{x}_i$ and the color codes the value of $\tau_1(r,\bar{x}_i)$. Different plots corresponding to different values of $r$ are shown in Fig. 2.

When $r=0.02$ (Fig. 2) the points in the periphery of the attractor recur after a long time while the inner points have lower recurrence times. Recurrence times are long and distributed along the attractor without particular correlation to their position in the phase plane. Increasing $r$, the values of $\tau_1(r,\bar{x}_i)$ sensibly reduce. Increasing the value of the threshold $r$ to $r=0.1$ (Fig. 2) and $r=0.2$ (Fig. 2), it is more evident the tendency of points in the outer region of the attractor to recur with longer times. Finally, for $r=0.4$ (Fig. 2) the points far from the axis origin are those with higher recurrence times, even if these values are sensibly decreased with respect to smaller $r$.
