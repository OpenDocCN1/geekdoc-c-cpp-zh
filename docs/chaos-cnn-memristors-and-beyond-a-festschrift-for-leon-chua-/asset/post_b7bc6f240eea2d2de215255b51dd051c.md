- a series of theoretical research projects has led some researchers in the group to identify a variety of strange attractors, derived from Chua's circuit;

## 5. Chaos is Simple and Everybody can Understand It

Chua’s circuit is important not only in the field of science and for the artistic products it inspires, but also because it is an astonishing tool able to bring the topics of chaos and complexity (and science in general) nearer to the younger generation [23]. In [21], experiments with secondary school students are presented to show this. In ad-hoc laboratories, students have physically built the circuit, simulated it by computers, realized 3D attractors and produced sounds and music. In this way they have approached scientific topics outside the regular school curriculum and usually studied only by chaos specialists. In this way, they have understood that science is not difficult or boring and can be understood through entertainment and studied in a very interesting way.

![](img/039e7d3c23390584a66737067db8b75b_133_0.png)

Fig. 13. Collection of jewels realized starting from the shape of Chua's attractors.

Experiments have been organized in theoretical lessons and laboratories. Students approached the chaotic phenomenon by creating 3D attractors, manipulating the control parameters of the simulated circuit and realizing images, sounds, and music (Fig. 14). Students have learned about many aspects of the contents of the experiment: the building and manipulation of circuit components, computer simulation, and the production of 3D attractors and music. All the students completed a learning questionnaire, providing positive results to the first empirical experiment. The materials used in this experimentation are available at the above mentioned web site.

## 6. Conclusions

The invention of Chua's circuit, and all Chua's work involves important aspects of contemporary science. In this work, we want to make a small contribution to the divulgation of the story of the genesis of Chua's circuit which sends a powerful message to the younger generation: science is beautiful because it allows the understanding of nature.

![](img/039e7d3c23390584a66737067db8b75b_134_0.png)

If science is also enjoyable and engaging, connecting us with our innate artistic side, we can appreciate the beauty of nature. Through non-conventional ways of study and research, the younger generation can better understand science. Our scientific path tries to connect two worlds, above all for promoting this message to future generations.
