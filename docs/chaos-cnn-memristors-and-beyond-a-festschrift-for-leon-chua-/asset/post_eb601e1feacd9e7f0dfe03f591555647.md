## 4. Conclusions

In this paper we reported a study of bifurcation phenomena observed in the family of circuits based on classic Chua’s circuit using memristors. Finding the ranges of existence of various dynamic behaviors such as fixed points, periodic orbits, chaos ranges has not been reported before and is clearly important for understanding of circuit dynamics and finding possible applications. Memristor circuits have been already confirmed to have a variety of applications e.g. in memory circuits [3,14,20,23].
