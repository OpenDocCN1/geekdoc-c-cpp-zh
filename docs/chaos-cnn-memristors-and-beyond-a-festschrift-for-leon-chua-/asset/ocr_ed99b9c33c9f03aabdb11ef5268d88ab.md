During the last years, many technical realizations of memristors have been conceptually and practically realized. The theory behind the memristor has been extended as well but mostly for memristors and less for electrical circuits with memristors which are often difficult to analyze due to the nonlinearity and the dynamics of the memristor. The theory of single memristor networks is the very aim of this paper. Based on the Multilinear Theory of Nonlinear Networks and on Volterra Series representation, a general description of single memristorcircuits is obtained.

# 1.  Introduction

The theoretical concept of the memristor, a special kind of resistor with memory, was published first in 1971 by Leon O. Chua [4]. But the scientific interest in memristors remained limited. This changed abruptly by a letter to “Nature” in 2008. Strukov *et al.* [13] observed that certain nanoscale devices with thin semiconductor layers can be described as memristors. Since then, the memristor has a wide attention in the international

research community of electrical engineers, physicists and biologists. Recent investigations are mainly focused on the realization of ultra dense VLSI resistive random access memories (ReRAM). The current main areas of research with respect to memristors are depicted in Fig. 1, but it can be assumed that more interesting fields will be opened up in future.
