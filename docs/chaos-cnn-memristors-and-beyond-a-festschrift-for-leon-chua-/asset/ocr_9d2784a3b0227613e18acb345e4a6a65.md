# 7. Summary and Conclusions

In this chapter, we have demonstrated the existence of CPS in prototype time-delay systems such as a piecewise-linear time-delay system, the Mackey-Glass and experimentally in a piecewise-linear time-delay with threshold nonlinearity possessing highly non-phase-coherent chaotic attractors. We have shown that there is a typical transition from a non-synchronized state to CPS and then to generalized synchronization using different frameworks. Further, we have demonstrated the phenomenon of noise-enhanced PS in both unidirectionally and bidirectionally coupled time-delay systems. It is to be noted that the time scales of the drive system remain unaffected, while most of the time scales of the response system are entrained to that of the drive system in unidirectionally coupled time-delay systems, whereas both systems adjust their rhythms to their principal time scale for mutual coupling. In particular, the return time clearly indicates the multiple time scales of the delay system. We have also demonstrated the phenomenon of noise-induced synchronization in time-delay systems driven by a common Gaussian noise.
