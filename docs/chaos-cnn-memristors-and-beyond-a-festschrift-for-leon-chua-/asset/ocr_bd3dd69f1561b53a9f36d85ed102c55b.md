and has in fact anticipated the era of neural networks launched by John Hopfield in 1982 [22, 23].

Yet another research subject that had predated and anticipated the recent active area of “synchronization of chaos” launched by Pecora and Carroll in [24] is a not-widely known yet fundamental and deep seminal paper entitled “Synchronization and Chaos” [26].
