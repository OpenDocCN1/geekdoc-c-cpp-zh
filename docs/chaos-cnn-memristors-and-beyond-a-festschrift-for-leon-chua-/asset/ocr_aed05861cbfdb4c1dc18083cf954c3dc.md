of them have to be eliminated to obtain global state equations. We have given a simple example of them, linked to different descriptions of a memristor. Otherwise, we did not pursue here the reduction process, which is a typical and rather involved problem of differential-algebraic systems. Furthermore, there is the danger of impasse points, i.e. special values of circuit variables that fail to determine a solution even if for all other values they determine a solution. We also have given a simple example of a memristor circuit with this singular behavior. Non-detected impasse points can be lead to wrong conclusions of numerical simulations. Impasse points can be eliminated by completing the circuit model with suitable parasitic capacitors. Knowing their presence not only leads to better circuit models, but also indicates singular behavior, such as jumps in the solutions [5].
