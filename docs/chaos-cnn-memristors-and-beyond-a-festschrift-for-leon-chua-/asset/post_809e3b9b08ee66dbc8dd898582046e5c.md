## 1. Introduction

The theoretical concept of the memristor, a special kind of resistor with memory, was published first in 1971 by Leon O. Chua [4]. But the scientific interest in memristors remained limited. This changed abruptly by a letter to “Nature” in 2008. Strukov *et al.* [13] observed that certain nanoscale devices with thin semiconductor layers can be described as memristors. Since then, the memristor has a wide attention in the international

research community of electrical engineers, physicists and biologists. Recent investigations are mainly focused on the realization of ultra dense VLSI resistive random access memories (ReRAM).
