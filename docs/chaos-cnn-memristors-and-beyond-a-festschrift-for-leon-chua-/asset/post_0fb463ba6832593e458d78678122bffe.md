## 7. Bifurcation and Chaos in Scalar Piecewise Linear Time-delay Systems

Nonlinear time-delay systems are fascinating dynamical systems. Even scalar piecewise linear time-delay systems can exhibit very complex dynamical behaviour, essentially because they are effectively infinite dimensional in nature [23,25,30,47]. For example, typically they can admit hyperchaotic attractors. In this section, we consider a simple delay differential equation (DDE) describing a time delayed feedback oscillator in the form

\[
\frac{dx}{dt} = -a x(t) + b F[x(t - \tau)], \tag{31}
\]

where a and b are positive scalar parameters, \(x(t)\) is a dynamical variable, \(F(x)\) is a nonlinear activation function and \(\tau\) is the time delay. The activation function \(F(x)\) in our study takes a symmetric piecewise linear form [39,40],

\[
F(x) = A F^*(x) - B x. \tag{32a}
\]

Here

\[
F^*(x) = \begin{cases} -x^*, & x < -x^*, \\ x, & -x^* \le x \le x^*, \\ x^*, & x > x^*. \end{cases} \tag{32b}
\]

where A and B are positive parameters and \(x^*\) is the controllable threshold value. In our analysis, we

Another piecewise system recently well studied by our group [33] is the five segment piecewise linear system, which is again of the form (1), where now

\[
F(x) = \begin{cases} 0, & x < -1.33, \\ -1.5x - 2, & -1.33 < x < -0.8, \\ x, & -0.8 < x < 0.8, \\ 1.5x + 2, & 0.8 < x < 1.33, \\ 0, & x > 1.33. \end{cases} \tag{33}
\]

The piecewise nature of the function \(F(x)\) is shown in Fig. 19(a). The scalar time delay equation admits a highly complex set of dynamical phenomena, including intricate bifurcations. It also admits higher dimensional hyperchaotic attractors with several positive Lyapunov exponents. In Fig. 19(c), we present the first ten maximal Lyapunov exponents for a range of time delays \(\tau \in (2,29)\), while the other parameters are fixed at \(a = 1.0\), \(b = 1.2\). It is clear that piecewise linear time delay systems have very rich dynamical behaviour and can be taken as paradigmatic models to understand complex nonlinear systems.
