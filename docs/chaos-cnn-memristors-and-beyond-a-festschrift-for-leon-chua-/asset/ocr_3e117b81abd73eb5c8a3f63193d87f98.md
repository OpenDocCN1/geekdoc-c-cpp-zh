In another paper with Alistair Mees, Chua investigated chaos as a lack of synchronization [47] where chaos was defined by its properties: (i) sensitivity to initial conditions, (ii) non periodicity, (iii) broad band Fourier spectrum, and (iv) complicated recurrences. With Tang and Mees, Chua then investigated an astable mutivibrator consisting of a capacitor connected across a resistive (memoryless) nonlinear circuit [48]. They investigated how the loss of synchronization implies chaos. In their circuit, they proved their model has the following features:

+   (1) There are no stable periodic solutions of any order.
+   (2) There is no almost periodic solution that is not periodic.
+   (3) There are unstable periodic solutions. If α is an integer, these exist for all possible periods.
+   (4) Any two trajectories either coincide exactly after a finite time, or are separated by order 1 infinitely often.
