The following questions are addressed in the remainder of this chapter: given the Huygens system (two pendulum clocks coupled through an elastic support), is it possible to replace the pendulum clocks by another type of second

Since synchronization of the chaotic behaviour will not occur naturally, is it possible to induce the synchronized behaviour by means of a control action? The first question is addressed in the next section, whereas questions 2 and 3 are considered in Sec. 4 and Sec. 5.
