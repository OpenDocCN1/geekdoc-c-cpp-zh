Now, we will demonstrate the results for unidirectional coupling. For the drive system A_x = 5.2 and for the response system A_y = 5.0, while B is fixed as B = 3.5. It is to be noted that the results presented here remain invariant irrespective of the values of the parameters A_x,y, B and b, which are chosen such that the individual systems exhibit chaotic behavior. We use the Poincaré section, chosen at x(t - τ) = 0.8 and x(t) < 0.8 on the attractor, technique to estimate phase and phase difference. In the absence of the coupling, both systems evolve independently with different frequencies due to the frequency mismatch ω_x ≠ ω_y.
