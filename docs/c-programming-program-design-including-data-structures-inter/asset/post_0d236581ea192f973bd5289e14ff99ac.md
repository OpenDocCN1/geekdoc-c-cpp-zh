- 34. Suppose that amount is a double variable, which denotes the amount you need to pay to the department store. If you pay the minimum amount, you get $10.00 or 1% of this billing amount, whichever is smaller, as a credit on your next bill; if you pay at least 50% of the billing amount, the penalty is 8% of your balance; if you pay at least 20% of the billing amount and less than 50% of the billing amount, the penalty is 10% of the balance; otherwise, the penalty is 20% of the balance. Design an algorithm that prompts the user to enter the billing amount and the desired payment. The algorithm then calculates and outputs the credit or the remaining balance. If the amount is not paid in full, the algorithm should also output the penalty amount.
