
In the figure can be seen that the true autocorrelations have the appearance of a damped exponential for $k > 1$, while the sample autocorrelations approximate this behavior. The true and sample partial autocorrelations exhibit a consistent behavior from the beginning of the plot. Based on these behaviors we conclude that $q-p=1$, and due to the simple damped exponential behavior of the autocorrelations, a reasonable conclusion is that $p=1$ and thus $q=2$.
