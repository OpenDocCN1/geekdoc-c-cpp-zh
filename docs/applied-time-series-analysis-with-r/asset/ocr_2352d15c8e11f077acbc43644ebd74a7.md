It can be seen from the figure that LG wave for the earthquake and the explosion have similar time-varying behavior with which the frequency is decreasing with time. For each of the 10 earthquakes and 10 explosions used in the study, Woodward (2007) transformed the corresponding LG wave into a stationary dual. The duals for the MM earthquake and the Starwort explosion are shown in Figure 13.13c and d, respectively. Each of the dual processes was modeled as an AR(10) model. The associated G(0; 0.8) spectral densities for MM and Starwort are shown in Figure 13.13e and f, respectively. Inspection of de-no spectral densities shows that the one associated with the Starwort explosion has a much sharper peak, and thus, indicates a stronger cyclic dual behavior than seen in the earthquake spectral density.
