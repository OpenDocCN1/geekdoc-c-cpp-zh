Note also that, for large lags, $k$, there seems to be less correlation between $X_t$ and $X_{t+k}$ to the extent that there is very little correlation between $X_t$ and $X_{t+40}$ We see this behavior manifested in [Figure 1.4a], which displays the true autocorrelations associated with the model from which realization 1 was generated. $ho_1 \approx 0.95$ while the lag increases, the autocorrelations decrease, and by lag 40 the autocorrelation has decreased to $ho_{40} = 0.1$.

Realization 2 in [Figure 1.3b] shows an absence of pattern. That is, there appears to be no relationship between $X_t$ and $X_{t+1}$ or between $X$ and $X_{t+k}$ for any $k \neq 0$ to matter. In fact, this is a realization from a white noise model, and for this model, $\rho_k = 0$ over $k \neq 0$ as be seen in [Figure 1.4b]. Note that in all autocorrelation plots, $\rho_0 = 1$.

Realization 3 in [Figure 1.3c] is characterized by pseudo-periodic behavior with a cycle-length of about 14 time points. The corresponding autocorrelations in [Figure 1.4c] showed a damped sinusoidal wave. Note that, not necessarily, there is a positive correlation between $X_t$ and $X_{t+14}$. Also, there is a negative correlation at lag 7, since within the sinusoidal cycle, if $x_i$ is above the average, $x_{i-7}$ would be expected to be below average and vice versa.
