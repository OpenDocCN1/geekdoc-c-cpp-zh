
## Forecasting

$E[(X_{t_0+\ell}-Z_{t_0}^*)X_j]=0, \quad j=1,2,\dots,t_0.$

$( )$ Let $Z_{t_0}^*$ be such that $E[(X_{t_0+\ell}-Z_{t_0}^*)X_j]=0, \ j=1,\dots,t_0,$ and let $Z_t$ be an arbitrary linear combination of $X_t, \ t=1,2,\dots,t_0.$ Then it follows that

$E[(X_{t_0+\ell}-Z_{t_0}^*)^2]=E[(X_{t_0+\ell}-Z_{t_0}^*+Z_{t_0}-Z_{t_0}^*)^2]$
$=E[(X_{t_0+\ell}-Z_{t_0}^*)^2]+E[(Z_{t_0}-Z_{t_0}^*)^2]$
$+2E[(X_{t_0+\ell}-Z_{t_0}^*)(Z_{t_0}-Z_{t_0}^*)]$
$\ge E[(X_{t_0+\ell}-Z_{t_0}^*)^2], \,{}$

since the cross-product term $E[(X_{t_0+\ell}-Z_{t_0}^*)(Z_{t_0}-Z_{t_0}^*)]\ge 0.$ The fact that the cross-product term is zero can be shown using properties of the conditional expectation (see Billingsley 1995).

$(\Rightarrow)$ To show that $A\Rightarrow B,$ we will use the contrapositive, that is, $\sim B\Rightarrow\sim A.$ We assume that $E[(X_{t_0}-Z_t^*)X_j]=a\neq 0$ for some $j,$ and will show that $Z_t^*$ do not have minimum MSE by producing a linear combination, $Z_{it}$ of $X_t, \ t=1,2,\dots,t_0$ with smaller MSE than $Z_t^*.$ Suppose $E[(X_{t_0+\ell}-Z_{t_0}^*)X_j]=a\neq 0$ and let $E[(X_{t_0+\ell}-Z_{t_0}^*)^2]\ge E\dots$

$Z_t=Z_{t_0}^*+\frac{aX_j}{E[X_j]^2},$

Then

$E[(X_{t_0+\ell}-Z_{t_0}^*)^2]=E[(X_{t_0}-Z_{t_0}^*+Z_{t_0}-Z_{t_0}^*)^2]$
$=E[(X_{t_0}-Z_{t_0}^*-(Z_{t_0}-Z_{t_0}^*)^2]$
$=E[(X_{t_0+\ell}-Z_{t_0}^*)^2]+E[(Z_{t_0}-Z_{t_0}^*)^2]-2E[(X_{t_0+\ell}-Z_{t_0}^*)(Z_{t_0}-Z_{t_0}^*)]$
$=E[(X_{t_0+\ell}-Z_{t_0}^*)^2]+E\left(\frac{aX_j}{E[X_j]^2}\right)^2-2E\left[(X_{t_0+\ell}-Z_{t_0}^*)\frac{aX_j}{E[X_j]^2}\right]$
$=E\left[(X_{t_0+\ell}-Z_{t_0}^*)^2\right]+\frac{a^2}{E[X_j]^2}-\frac{2a^2}{E[X_j]^2}$
$=E\left[(X_{t_0+\ell}-Z_{t_0}^*)^2\right]-\frac{a^2}{E[X_j]^2},$

Since $a \neq 0$, it follows that

$E\left[(X_{t_0+\ell}-Z_{t})^2\right] < E\left[(X_{t_0+\ell}-Z_{t_0}^*)^2\right]$

and the result follows.
