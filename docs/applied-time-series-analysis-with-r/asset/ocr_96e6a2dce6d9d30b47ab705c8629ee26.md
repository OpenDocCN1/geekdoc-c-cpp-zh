In Table 7.6 it is seen that the YW and ML estimates are quite good while the YW estimates are poor.
Table 7.7 shows the factor tables associated with the fitted models, where it is seen that all three models have a positive real root close to the unit circle and complex conjugate roots associated with a system frequency of about f=0.13. The poor performance of the YW estimates can be traced to the fact that the second-order factor is estimated to be much further (removed from the unit circle) than it actually is. Again, this can be attributed to the fact that the dominant real root tends to diminish the influence of the second-order factor in the sample autocorrelations. In Chapter 9, we will discuss the differing realizations as part of the model building procedure.
