Wavelet packet functions are a more flexible family of functions that include special cases as the wavelet and scaling functions. Briefly, the wavelet packet functions, are defined by $w_j^m(x) = \phi(x)$, $w_j^1(x) = \psi(x)$, and defining $w_j^m(x) = 2^{-j}\psi^m(2^j x - k)$, where $m=0,1,\dots$ is the modulation frequency. Wavelet packet functions with $m > 1$ are defined recursively. See, for example, Ogden (1997).
