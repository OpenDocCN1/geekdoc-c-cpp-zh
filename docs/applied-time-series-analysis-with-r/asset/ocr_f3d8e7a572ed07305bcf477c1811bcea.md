# 7.3 ML Estimation of ARMA(p,q) Parameters

The use of ML estimation necessarily involves making distributional assumptions. For purposes of calculating the ML estimates of the parameters, we assume that the $a_i$ are normally distributed and hence that $X=(X_1, ..., X_n)'$ has a multivariate normal distribution with mean $\mu$ and covariance matrix $\Sigma_n$ where

$$\mu = (\mu, \mu, \dots \mu)'$$

and

$$\Sigma_n = \begin{pmatrix} \gamma_0 & \gamma_1 & \dots & \gamma_{n-1} \ \gamma_1 & \gamma_0 & \dots & \gamma_{n-2} \ \vdots & \vdots & \dots & \vdots \ \gamma_{n-1} & \gamma_{n-2} & \dots & \gamma_0 \end{pmatrix}$$

As with previous estimation procedures, we analyze $X_t - X$ and assume $\mu = 0$.

# 7.3.1 Conditional and Unconditional ML Estimation

Let $\sigma_a^2 M_n^1 = \Sigma_n$, the joint probability density function of $X=(X_1, ..., X_n)'$ is given by

$$f(x_1, \dots, x_n) = \frac{1}{(2\pi\sigma_a^2)^{n/2}} |M_n|^{1/2} \exp\left\{-\frac{x^T M_n x}{2\sigma_a^2}\right\$$

The joint density function in Equation 7.29 can be written

$$f(x_1, \dots, x_n) = f(x_{p+1}, \dots, x_n | x_1, \dots, x_p)f(x_1, \dots, x_p),$$

where $f(|cdot|)$ is the conditional probability density function. Since the $a_i$ are uncorrelated $N(0, \sigma_a^2)$ random variables, it follows that

$$f(a_{p+1}, \dots, a_n) = \frac{1}{(2\pi\sigma_a^2)^{(n-p)/2}} \exp\left\{-\frac{1}{2\sigma_a^2} \sum_{t=p+1}^n a_t^2}\right\$$
