AR(1) and AR(2) models, we know that an AR(1) model based on the first-order factor 1 + 0.95 has: (a) realizations that display a rapidly oscillating behavior as seen in Figure 3.5b, (b) autocorrelations that are damped exponentials as in Figure 3.4b, and (c) a peak in the spectral density at f=0.5 as seen in Figure 3.6b. As mentioned in the Discussion of 3.17, the AR(2) model based on the order factor 1 + 0.9B will have: (a) realizations that are pseudo-periodic with period about 11, (b) autocorrelations that are damped sinusoidal with a period of about 11 lags, and (c) a spectral density with a peak near f=0.09. Examination of Figure 3.17 shows that the AR(3) realization based on Equation 3.75 has pseudo-periodic behavior with a period of about 11 along with a high-frequency oscillation behavior associated with the negative root. The autocorrelations show the damped sinusoidal behavior with mild evidence of the oscillating behavior. The spectral density clearly shows peaks at f=0.09 and f=0.5. The behavior related to the two frequencies in the data is much more visible in the spectral density than in the autocorrelations. This illustrates the point made in Example 1.11 that the spectral density is a better tool than the autocorrelations for detecting and measuring cyclic behavior in data.
