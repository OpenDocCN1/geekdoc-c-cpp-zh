# 10.5.1 Testing for Independence of Two Stationary Time Series

We consider the setting in which $X_{i1}$ and $X_{i2}$ are stationary univariate time series that each have a general linear representation for which the $\psi$-weights are absolutely convergent. Let $\hat{ho}_{12}(k)$ be the estimated cross-correlation at lag $k$ as defined in Equation 10.12. It can be shown that $\hat{ho}_{12}(k)$ is asymptotically normal with mean 0 and variance ($AV_{12}$) given by

> $AV_{12} = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{j=-\infty}^{\infty} \rho_{11}(j)\rho_{22}(j). \quad (10.45)$

If it is to test $H_0: \rho_{12}(k) = 0$ versus $ho_{12}(k) \neq 0$, it can be seen that, the asymptotic variance in Equation 10.45 will not in general be known. Note that if either of $X_{i1}$ and $X_{i2}$ is white noise, the asymptotic variance $AV_{12} = 1/n$. Haugh (1976) recommended a procedure for testing $H_0: \rho_{12}(k) = 0$ that follows the following steps:

+   1. Fit an ARMA($p,q$) model to $X_{i1}$ and $X_{i2}$ (these two models may be of different orders $p_1, q_1$ and $p_2, q_2$)

+   2. Calculate the residual series $\hat{a}_{it1}$ and $\hat{a}_{it2}, t=1,...,n$ based on each model

+   3. Calculate the estimated cross-correlations, $\hat{ho}_{12}(k), k=1, ..., K$ between the two residual series

+   4. For each $k$, conclude that $\hat{ho}_{12}(k)$ is significantly different from zero at the $\alpha=0.05$ level if $|\hat{ho}_{12}(k)| > 2(1/\sqrt{n})$
