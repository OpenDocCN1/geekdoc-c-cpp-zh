# 5.2.2 Cyclical Models

In Example 5.7, it is seen that for the ARUMA($p,d,q$) process, the limiting autocorrelations, $ho_k$, do not decay to zero. In some cases they stay fixed at 1, and in others they are periodic. In particular, if all of the $d$ roots of $\lambda(z)=0$ in an ARUMA($p,d,q$) process are equal to 1, the process is noncyclic. On the other hand, if at least one of the $d$ roots of highest multiplicity is –1 or real, then we will refer to the process as a cyclical ARUMA($p,d,q$) process. The simplest ARUMA process is $(1+B)X_t=a_t$. In this case, $ho_k=(-1)^k$ so that the autocorrelation is periodic with system frequency $f_0=0.5$.
