# **EXAMPLE 1.12**: WHITE NOISE

Figure 1.22a shows a realization from the white noise series $X_i=a_i$, where $a_i$, is, with zero mean and unit variance, and Figure 1.22b, show the true autocorrelations. This is the same realization and theoretical autocorrelations that were shown in Figures 1.3b and 1.4b. As before, notice that the data appear completely random with no discernible cycle or trending pattern.

The true autocorrelations, $ho_k$, are zero except for $k=0$. In Figure 1.22c, we show the true spectral density. For discrete white noise, Equation 1.30 becomes $S(f)=1$, $|f|\leq 0.5$. Thus, discrete white noise has a constant spectral density, and a plot of the spectral density is simply that of a straight horizontal line. Since we plot dB in Figure 1.22c, the spectral density plot is a horizontal line at dB=0.
