# EXAMPLE 12.10: WAVELET ANALYSIS OF WHALE CHIRP DATA

Figure 12.20a (data set whale in tswge) shows a plot of 256 points of a whale echolocation signal sampled at 4.8 kHz (see Robertson et al. 2010). From the figure it can see initially that the data are high frequency, towards the end of the series data become more low frequency, towards the end of the realization, the data become high frequency again. Figure 12.20b the Gabor spectrum which clearly illustrates that the frequencies start out high, decrease (linearly) and then at around t=140 begin to increase again (linearly). Figure 12.20c shows the DWT for the data in Figure 12.20a. It can be seen that the behavior at the beginning and end of the realization is higher frequency than that towards the middle time values. This behavior can also be seen in the data in Figure 12.20a.
