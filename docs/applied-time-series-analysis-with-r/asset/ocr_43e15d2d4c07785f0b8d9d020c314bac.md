The result in Equation 7.61 provides large sample confidence intervals for the model parameters of an ARMA(p,q). A confidence ellipsoid corresponding to Equation 7.57 and individual confidence intervals corresponding to Equation 7.58 are given

The estimation of the elements of $\mathbf{\Gamma}$ for more complex ARMA(p,q) models, however, is not straightforward as it was in the AR case, since the processes $U_t$ and $V_t$ are not observed. while the components of $\mathbf{\Gamma}_{pq}$ can be estimated from the autocovariances for the AR(q) model

```
$V_t-\hat{V}_1V_{t-1}-\dots-\hat{V}_qV_{t-q} = a_t,$
```
