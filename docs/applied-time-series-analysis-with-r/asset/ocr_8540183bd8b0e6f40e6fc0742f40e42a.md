# EXAMPLE 2.2: SUM AND MOVING AVERAGE FILTERS

Consider again a time series $x_t$, $t = 1, \dots, n$, and suppose we
compute the sum $y_t = x_t + x_{t-1}$, which is also a linear filter where in
this case $h_0 = 1$, $h_1 = 1$, and $h_j = 0$, $j \neq 0$ or $1$. Calculations show that

> $H(e^{-2\pi if}) = 1 + \cos 2\pi f - i \sin 2\pi f$

and

> $|H(e^{-2\pi if})|^2| = 2(1 + \cos 2\pi f)$

it is a mirror image of Figure 2.2b for the difference filter, and as a conse-\sequence, low frequencies are passed and high frequencies tend to be
removed. Again, this is not a sharp filter and can be improved.
