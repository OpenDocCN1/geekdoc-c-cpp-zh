# 4.2.3 AR Processes with ARCH or GARCH Noise

We briefly consider the effect of ARCH and GARCH noise on AR processes. For the discussion here, we consider the AR(2) process

> $X_t - 1.95X_{t-1} + 0.98X_{t-2} = a_t$.

Figure 4.14a shows a Gaussian noise realization and Figure 4.14b shows a realization from Equation 4.32 where $a_t$ is the noise sequence in Figure 4.14a. While there is no volatility in the noise sequence, the realization in Figure 4.14b shows oscillations in amplitude, which is consistent with realizations from AR(2) models with complex roots near the unit circle. Figure 4.14c shows a realization from an ARCH(1) model with $\alpha_0 = 0.1$ and $a_t$. Figure 4.14d shows a realization from the AR(2) model in Equation 4.32, where $a_t$ is the ARCH(1) noise realization in Figure 4.14c.
