From the table, it is seen that the three estimators have quite similar asymptotic variances for small values of $	heta_1$. However, although the asymptotic variance of the innovations estimator does not depend on $	heta_1$, as $|	heta_1|$ increases, the MM estimators deteriorate while the ML estimators become less variable. Thus, there are substantial differences among the estimators when $|	heta_1|$ is near one. Because of these results coupled with the fact that the moment equations often do not yield real solutions, in practice, we do not recommend the use of MM estimates for MA parameters.
