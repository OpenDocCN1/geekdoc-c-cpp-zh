values of $a_j^2 + b_j^2$ indicate the presence of frequency behavior at or near $f_j = j/n$ in the data. The proof of the equality of Equation 1A.4 with the other two forms is left as Problem 1.20.

When $n=2m$ is even, the formulas derived earlier vary slightly. Specifically, the formulas for $a_j$ and $b_j$ hold for $j=1,\dots,m-1$. However,

```
$a_m = \sum_{t=1}^n (-1)^t x_t, b_m = 0,$ and $l(f_m) = na_m^2$.
```
