# 5.4.2 Random Walk with Drift

An extension of the model in Equation 5.20 is the random walk with drift model given by

> $X_t = \alpha + X_{t-1} + a_t,$  (5.25)

where $\alpha$ is a nonzero constant called the *drift*, and $X_0=0$. Based on model in Equation 5.25, at time $t-1$, the movement to $t$ is $\alpha$ plus the random $a_t$. It follows that $E(X_t)=\alpha t$, and thus, the process increases or decreases in time depending on whether $\alpha$ is positive or negative, respectively. Figure 5.6b displays a realization of length $n=200$ for a random walk with drift $\alpha=0.1$ and $\sigma_{\epsilon}^2=1$ using the same random sequence $a_1, a_2, \dots, a_{200}$ that was used in Figure 5.6a. The upward drift is evident.
