# 3.2.11 Factor Tables

It is useful to summarize information regarding the first and second-order factors of an AR(p) model, in a factor table. For each first-order or irreducible second-order factor of the model factor table displays

+   1. Roots of the roots to the characteristic equation
2. Proximity of the roots to the unit circle (by tabling the absolute reciprocal of the roots)
3. System frequencies $f_{ij}$

Factors in the factor table are listed in decreasing order of the proximity of the associated roots to the unit circle the closer to the unit circle, the stronger the autocorrelation effect. See Woodward et al. (2009).

# EXAMPLE 3.11: FACTOR TABLES FOR EXAMPLES 3.8 AND 3.9

The information discussed regarding the AR(3) model in Example 3.8 (Equation 3.73) is summarized in the factor table in Table 3.1. Similarly, Table 3.2 is a factor table describing the factors of model (3.75) in Example 3.9.

Examination of these tables shows

+   1. The processes are stationary AR(3) models, the roots are outside the unit circle (i.e., the absolute reciprocals of the roots are all less than 1)
2. In each model, the three roots are equidistant from the unit circle (i.e., $|r^{-1}|=0.95)
3. The system frequencies will be used throughout this book. We note that

for higher-order models, the factorization must be done numerically.
