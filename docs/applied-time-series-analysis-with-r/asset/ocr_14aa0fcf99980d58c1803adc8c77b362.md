In Definition 11.4, we extend the idea of a FARMA process that can have both short and long-memory behavior.

# Definition 11.4

If $X$ is a causal, stationary, and invertible process satisfying

> $$\phi(B)(1-B)^d(X_t-\mu) = 	heta(B)a_t, \quad (11.6)$

where $a_t$ is white noise, and $\phi(z)$ and $	heta(z)$ are $pth$ and $qth$-order polynomials, respectively, that share no common factors, $X$ is a fractional ARMA (FARMA($p,d,q$)) process.
