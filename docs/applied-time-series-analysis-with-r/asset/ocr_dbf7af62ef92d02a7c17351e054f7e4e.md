g(x) = \sum_{k=-\infty} s_{j,k}\phi_{j,k}(x) + \sum_{j=-\infty} \sum_{k=-\infty} d_{j,k}\psi_{j,k}(x), \quad (12.17)

where $\phi_{j,k}$ dilates and translates of a father wavelet or scaling function, $\varphi(x)$, corresponding to the mother wavelet, $\psi(x)$. Scaling functions (father wavelets) are that have the property

\int \varphi(x)dx = 1, \quad (12.18)

and their dilates and translates are defined analogously to Equation 12.9, that is,

\varphi_{j,k}(x) = 2^{-j/2}\varphi(2^{-j}x-k), \quad (12.19)

for integer $j$ and $k$. Consequently, it follows that for all integer $j$ and $k,

\int \varphi_{j,k}(x)dx = 1. \quad (12.20)

As an example, the Haar wavelet is

\varphi(x) = 1, \quad x \in[0, 1] \n= 0, otherwise.

g(x) = S_j(x) + \sum_{j=-\infty} D_j(x), \quad (12.21)
