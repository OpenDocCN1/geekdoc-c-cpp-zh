A final comment should be made about root tests. While these tests give the investigator a "yes-or-no" answer regarding a unit root, there are areas of concern regarding their use. The decision to retain a unit root is based on failure to reject a null hypothesis, and depending on the power of the test, the conclusion that the model has a unit root may not be the one that with much confidence. Unit root tests can be useful tools to assist in the decision, the final decision concerning the inclusion of nonstationary factors rests with the investigator.
