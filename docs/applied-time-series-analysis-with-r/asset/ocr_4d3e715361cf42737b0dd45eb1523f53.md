(a) Realization, (b) autocorrelations, and (c) spectral densities of the AR(4) model $X_{t}-1.95X_{t-1}+0.19X_{t-2}+0.64X_{t-3}=(1-0.95B)(1-B+0.8B^2)X_{t}=a_{t}$, in Example 3.12.

fig318a in tswge. The autocorrelations have a sinusoidal appearance (with period around 6) along a damped exponential path with very little evidence of the 0.5 frequency behavior associated with the factor (1+0.8B). The realization shows pseudo-cyclic behavior of period produced by the factor $(1-B+0.8B^2)$ along a wandering path produced by the 1. The realization shows very slight evidence of the high-frequency behavior associated with the factor $1+0.8B$. The spectral density peaks at $f=0, 0.16,$ and 5 as would be expected based on the factor.

NOTE: It is very important to realize that the behavior associated with roots closest to the unit circle tends to be dominant. In this example, the root 1.05 associated with the factor $(1-0.95B)$ is closest to the unit circle while the root -1.25 associated with $(1+0.8B)$ is the furthest removed from the unit circle. We have already noted that the behavior associated with the factor $1-95$ is clearly evident in the realization and the autocorrelations while that associated with $1+0.8B$ is not apparent. In the spectral density, we see peaks close to all three system frequencies, the peak at $f=0$ is much higher than the peak at $f=0.5$ and $f=0.16$. We will explore the domination of roots close to the unit circle further in Example 3.14 and in Chapter 5.
