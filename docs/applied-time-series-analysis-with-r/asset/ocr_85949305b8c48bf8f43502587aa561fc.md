# 1.6 Estimating the Power Spectrum and Spectral Density for Discrete Time Series

Estimation of the power spectrum and spectral density from data can be a challenging problem, and there are a variety of estimation techniques in the literature. We will briefly introduce the topic here but come back to it in later chapters. Given the spectrum in Equation 1.29 for a discrete parameter time series, it seems that a natural estimator for the spectrum could be obtained by simply substituting the sample autocovances discussed in Section 1.4 into Equation 1.29. The resulting estimator is called the sample spectrum and is defined in Definition 1.14.
