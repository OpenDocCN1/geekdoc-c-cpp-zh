# 7.5 Asymptotic Properties of Estimators

In this section, we state some asymptotic results concerning the estimators discussed in this chapter.

# 7.5.1 AR Case

First, note that for an AR($p$) process, the YW, Burg, and ML estimates (either conditional or unconditional) have the same asymptotic distribution. Specifically, if $\phi$ denotes the vector of AR parameter estimators obtained using any of these techniques, then

```
$n^{1/2}(\hat{\phi}-\phi) \Rightarrow N(0,\sigma_{\gamma}^2\Gamma^{-1})$
```

where $\Rightarrow$ denotes convergence in distribution, and $\Gamma$ is the $p \times p$ matrix whose $ij$-element is $\gamma_{ij}$ that, is,

$$\Gamma = \begin{pmatrix} \gamma_0 & \gamma_1 & \dots & \gamma_{p-1} \ \gamma_1 & \gamma_0 & \dots & \gamma_{p-2} \ \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \ \gamma_{p-1} & \gamma_{p-2} & \dots & \gamma_0 \end{pmatrix}$

(Brockwell and Davis, 1991) Even though the estimators are asymptotically equivalent in the AR($p$) case, it is worth noting that the YW estimates can be quite poor in practice when some roots of the characteristic equation are very near the unit circle or when they are repeated roots. Consider, for the AR(3) model

```
$(1-0.995B)(1-1.2B+0.8B^2)X_t = a_t$
```
