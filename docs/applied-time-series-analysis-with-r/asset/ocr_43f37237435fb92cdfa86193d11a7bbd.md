In Figure 3.32a, a realization of length $n = 100$ is given shown from the AR(1) process, $X_i$, given in Equation 3.131. This realization was previously shown in Figure 3.5a. In Figure 3.32b, we show the corresponding realization of length $n = 99$ for $Y_i$. The realization for $X_i$ displays the behavior discussed in Section 3.2 for an AR(1) process whose characteristic equation has a positive root. The realization for $Y_i$ in Figure 3.32b has the appearance of white noise. More generally for the ARMA$(p,q)$ model

> $\phi(B)X_t = \theta(B)a_t,$  (3.132)

we consider the problem of determining the model satisfied by $Y_
u$ where

> $Y_t = \alpha(B)X_t$

$\alpha(B)$ is of order $k$, that is, $\alpha(B) = 1 - \alpha_1B - \dots - \alpha_kB^k$. Thus, $X_i = \alpha^{-1}(B)Y_
u$ and Equation 3.132 can be rewritten as

> $\phi(B)\alpha^{-1}(B)Y_t = \theta(B)a_t$.

There are two cases of primary interest.

Case (1) $\alpha(B)$ is a factor of $\phi(B)$. In this case, $k \leq p$ and $\phi(B)$ can be factored as $\phi_t(B)\alpha(B)$, so that formally

> $\phi(B)\alpha^{-1}(B) = \phi_t(B)\alpha(B)\alpha^{-1}(B)$
$= \phi_t(B),$

that is $\phi_t(B)Y_t = \theta(B)a_t$, and $Y_t$ satisfies an ARMA$(p - k, q)$ model.
