# EXAMPLE 4.4: DOW DAILY RATE OF RETURN DATA

Figure 4.9a shows the DOW daily rate of return for the first 1000 days of data in Figure 4.7 (data set down1000 in sweg), and Figure 4.9b shows the sample autocorrelations of the data in Figure 4.9a. Sample autocorrelations in Figure 4.9b suggest that the rate of return are uncorrelated and the sample autocorrelations are small and tend to stay within the limit lines. Figure 4.9c shows the squares of the rate of return in Figure 4.9a and have the same general appearance as the squared data in Figure 4.8c. However, the sample autocorrelations in Figure 4.9d are not uncorrelated, and the exponential damping behavior of the sample autocorrelations suggests an AR(1) model for the squared data. Based on the discussion preceding example, it appears reasonable to suggest that the DOW daily rate of return could be reasonably modeled as an ARCH(1) process.
