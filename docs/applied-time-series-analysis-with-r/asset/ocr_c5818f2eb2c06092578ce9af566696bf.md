Thus, the beginning of the "low-frequency" component is higher frequency than the "high-frequency" toward the end of the realization. Consequently no cutoff point will produce a low-pass filter that can recover $c_\gamma$. For example, Figure 13.18d in Figure 13.18a with cutoff 0.05. There can be seen that the low-frequency component, $c_\gamma$, passes through the filter for $t > 300$ or so, the early part of $c_\gamma$ was "filtered out." In order to adjust the filter so that the early part of the low-frequency component can pass through, we changed the cutoff point to 0.12. The result of this is shown in Figure 13.18e where it can be seen that the $c_\gamma$ signal passed through the filter but by $t=150$ or so, the filtered series looks like a noiseless version of the original series, that is, with both components.

Xu et al. (2012) proposed a method for filtering TVF data using the following strategy:

+   1. Transform the time axis to obtain a stationary dual realization.
2. Filter the dual process using standard, for example, Butterworth, techniques.
3. Transform back to the original timescale.

In Figure 13.19a we show the data on Figure 13.18a while in Figure 13.19 we show the dual process obtained using $\lambda=0$ and offset $=175$. Figure 13.19c shows the Parzen spectral estimate of the original data displaying the typical spectral density for TVF data. Figure 13.19d shows the Parzen spectral density estimate for the dual where two peaks are visible, and it appears that one at $f=0.05$ and another at $f=0.15$. Figure 13.19e we show the results of applying a fourth order Butterworth filter with cutoff at $f=0.1$. There we see that the lower component is obtained. Figure 13.19f shows the data in Figure 13.19e transformed back to the original timescale. Comparing Figure 13.19e with the actual low-frequency component in Figure 13.18b shows that the procedure did a good job of retrieving the low-frequency TVF component, $c_\gamma$, and in particular note the dramatic improvement over the low-pass filter results shown in Figure 13.18d and e in which the TVF data were filtered directly. For more details about this procedure see Xu et al. (2012). Also Xu et al. (2013, 2014).
