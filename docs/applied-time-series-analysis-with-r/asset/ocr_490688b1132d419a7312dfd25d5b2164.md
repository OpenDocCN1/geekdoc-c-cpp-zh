# EXAMPLE 5.6: NEAR-NONSTATIONARY MODEL WITH COMPLEX RO

Consider the AR(3) model

```
$(1-0.8B)(1-1.34B+0.995B^2)X_t = a_t, \quadquad (5.13)
```

whose factor table is given in Table 5.3. Model 5.13 is similar to Equation 5.12 in that the real root corresponds to $\alpha_1=0.8$, and the coefficients of the second-order behavior are designed so that the associated frequency is $f=0.13$. However, the difference is that the pair of complex roots is much closer to the unit circle in Equation 5.13 than for are in Equation 5.12. Specifically, for these roots $|r_i^{-1}|=0.9975$ while for realization of length $n=200$ for the model in Equation 5.13. In this case, the autocorrelations and realization are almost entirely dominated by the second-order behavior associated with the pair of complex roots close to the unit circle. This behavior was previously observed in Example 3.14, but the point to be made here is that the limiting case where the complex roots are on the unit circle will exhibit second-order behavior that "ignores" the first-order behavior induced by the positive real root, $0.8^{-1}$.
