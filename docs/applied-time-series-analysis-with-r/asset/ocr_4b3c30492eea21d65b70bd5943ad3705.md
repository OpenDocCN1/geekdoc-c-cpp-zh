It was noted in Section 8.3 that there are tests for a unit root (i.e., the factor $1 - B$ in model). However, as noted, the decision to retain a unit root is the result of failure to reject a null hypothesis, and as such the unit root tests do not sufficient power in general to distinguish between models near unit roots and with unit roots. The final decision whether to include a factor $1 - B$ should be based on such considerations as whether there is any physical reason to believe there is an attraction to the mean. Another important issue is that realizations from the model

```
$(1 - \delta_B)\phi(X_t - \mu) = \theta(B)a_t,$
```

where $\delta$ is close to equal to 1 (but not greater than 1) may show a piece-wise trending behavior.
