# Applied Time Series Analysis with R

Our interest is in annual data, and in Figure 1.23a (data set ss08_tswage) we plot these yearly averages for the years 1749–2008. The problem of modeling this data has been studied by many investigators in the past century. In particular, Yule (1927) proposed the AR model as indicating the disturbed periodic nature of the data.

The sunspot data in Figure 1.23a reveal a pseudo-periodic nature, where there is worth noting that the sunspot data are between 9 and 11 years. It is, in fact, that the sunspot is not sinusoidal in nature, and there is much more variability in the peaks than there is in the troughs (see Tong, 1990). In Figure 1.23b, we show the sample autocorrelations for the sunspot data. Figure 1.23c displays the spectral density where we estimated S(f) using the Parzen window with M=2\sqrt{n}=32. In Figure 1.23c, we notice a reasonably sharp peak at approximately $f=0.09$ indicating a predominant period of about 11 years in the data. It is also interesting to note that there is also a peak at $f=0$ indicating possibly longer period or aperiodic behavior.
