# **EXAMPLE 1.11: AN AR(4) PROCESS WITH MORE THAN ONE DOMINANT FREQUENCY COMPONENT**

In Figure 1.21, we show plots based on the model

```
$X_i = 0.85X_{i-1} + 0.03X_{i-2} + 0.745X_{i-3} - 0.828X_{i-4} + a_i,$ (1.43)
```

where $a_i$ is normal zero mean, unit variance noise. In Figure 1.21a and c, we display the realization and true spectral density, which were previously shown in Figure 1.16a (data set fig1.16a in tsew) and b, respectively. We notice a low frequency component associated with a period of about 30, while there is also indication of a higher frequency component in the data. The spectral density plot in Figure 1.21c clearly indicates the presence of two frequencies in the data, one at about $f=0.04$
