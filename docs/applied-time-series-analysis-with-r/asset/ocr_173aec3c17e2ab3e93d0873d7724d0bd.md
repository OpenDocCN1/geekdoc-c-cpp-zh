$Var(\hat{ho}_k) = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{u=-\infty}^{\infty} {\rho_u^2 + \rho_{u+k}\rho_{u-k} - 4\rho_k\rho_{u-k} + 2\rho_u^2}. (1.20)

When the time series has the property that $\hat{ho}_k \to 0$ as $k \to \infty$, we have for “large” $k$

$Cov(\hat{ho}_k, \hat{ho}_j) = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{u=-\infty}^{\infty} \rho_u\rho_{u+j-k}. (1.21)

and

$Var(\hat{ho}_k) = \frac{1}{n} \sum_{u=-\infty}^{\infty} \rho_u^2. (1.22)

Bartlett’s approximations will turn out to be quite important in our analysis of time series data. For approximating the variance of $\hat{ho}_k$ in determining when sample autocorrelations are significantly different from zero, Equation 1.21 shows that there can be correlation between neighboring sample autocorrelations. This is an important result to keep in mind when examining plots of $\hat{ho}_k$, as we are doing in our analysis of time series.

In the discussion of Section 1.3 relating to Figures 1.3 and 1.4, we described how patterns in the autocorrelations provide information about the nature of time series models. In Figure 1.9, we show sample autocorrelations, estimated using $\hat{ho}_v$ associated with the realizations in Figure 1.3. These sample autocorrelations are estimates of the true autocorrelations, shown in Figure 1.4. It can be seen that these sample autocorrelations approximate the behavior of the true autocorrelations, but the correlation among $\hat{ho}_k$ values should be noted. For example, notice that a value of $\hat{ho}_k$ tends to underestimate $ho_k$ for “near” $k$ also tends to underestimate the true value. This can produce a cyclic behavior in plots of $\hat{ho}_k$ when such behavior is present in the plot of $\hat{ho}_u$. This is seen in Figure 1.9a and d. It should also be noted that sample autocorrelations for a white noise realization will not be exactly zero. In Section 8.1, we will discuss tests for white noise that will help you decide whether sample autocorrelations such as those shown in Figure 1.9b are larger in magnitude than what would be expected for a realization from a white noise.
