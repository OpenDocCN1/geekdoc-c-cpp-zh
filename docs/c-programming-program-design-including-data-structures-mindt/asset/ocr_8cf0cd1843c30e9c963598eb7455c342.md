# Multimodal Retrieval-Augmented Generation: A Survey

Songping Yu, Yifan Feng, Hongmei Zhu, and Yue Gao

School of Informatics, Xiamen University, Xiamen, China

Abstract

Multimodal retrieval-augmented generation (Multimodal RAG) has emerged as a promising approach to enhance the quality and accuracy of generated content by leveraging external multimodal knowledge. This survey aims to provide a comprehensive overview of the recent advances in Multimodal RAG, covering its key components, methods, and applications.
