As already mentioned in Section 18.7, Common Cause Failures (CCF) are one of the reasons why the reliability and availability models of a system may dangerously underestimate the probability of failure. CCFs occur when multiple components fail due to common causes, such as impact, vibration, temperature, power failure, and improper maintenance.
