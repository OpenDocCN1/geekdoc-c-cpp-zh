The sub-net represented by places SUP and SD, transitions Fs, Rss and Res, and their respective arcs and tokens represent the eight servers, states (operational or in failure), faults, and their respective repairs, where #SUP=#NS=8 is the number of servers available when the system started. Similarly, the sub-net depicted by places SWUP and SWD, transitions Fsw, Rssw and Res represent the two switches, their states, their faults, and their respective repairs. #SWUP=#NSW=4 is the number of switches available in the initial configuration. Likewise, the aggregation routers of the SAN are similarly represented by specific sub-nets. The sub-net composed of places RUP and RD, transitions Fr, Rr and R, their respective arcs, states, their faults, and repairs, where #RUP=#NR=2 is the number of aggregation routers available when the system starts. The last sub-net composed of places SU and SUD, transitions Fsu, Rssu and Res, their arcs and tokens represent the two storage units, their states, faults, and repairs. #SU=#SUD=2 is the number of aggregation storage units available in the initial state. The server semantics of every transition that represents a fault (Fs, Fsw, Fr, and Fss) is iss for each of these device classes, there is more than one. The timed transition adopted to represent the repair (Res, Rss, Res, and Res) also has server semantics (iss). However, the enabling credit is initially stored in place RT; their actual server semantics are kss, where k is their enabling degree (see Section 13.1). The immediate transitions represents the team allocation to a repair activity (repairing a system's component). Their weights define the probability of a team repairing a system component (probability choice in Section 13.1). The probability of firing the immediate transitions will depend on the respective weights, the number of tokens in SD, SWD, RD, SUD, and if place RT is marked. The immediate transition weights may be marked to consider the number of devices of a specific class failure. In this study, their weights were defined as constant and equal to one. The priorities assigned to the immediate transitions are also one. The repairing activities for each device class are represented by an immediate transition (Rsi, i ∈ {s,sw,r,su}), a place (iUR, i ∈ {S,SW,R,SU})
