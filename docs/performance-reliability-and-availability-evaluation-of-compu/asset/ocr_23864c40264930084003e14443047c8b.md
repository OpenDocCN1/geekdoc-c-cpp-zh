**Example 22.8.2.** Consider the system depicted in the previous example. However, the system is considered operational as at least six ($K = 6$) out of the eight ($N = 8$). Hence, the reliability is computed using.

```
$R_{000}(t) = (1 - P\#DW \geq (N - (K - 1)))(t)\n(1 - P\#DW \geq (8 - (6 - 1)))(t) = (1 - P\#DW \geq 3))(t)$.
```

The reliability at $t = 1000h$ is $R_{000}(1000h) = 0.84332$. Figure 22.23 shows the reliability in the time interval $(0, 20, 200h)$. The mean time to failure is obtained from $MTTF = \int_{0}^{\infty} R(t) dt = 5673.01h$.
