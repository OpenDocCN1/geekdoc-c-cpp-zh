Chapter 18 and Chapter 19 introduce the Reliability Block Diagrams (RBD) and Fault Trees (FT) and their applications. Their evaluation methods are discussed in Chapter 20, Chapter 21 studies more complex systems than those studied in the previous chapter. Markov chains are applied to model systems, which are not well characterized by combinatorial methods. Chapter 22 adopts SPN systems that are not precisely described by combinatorial methods and whose state spaces are large enough to be challenging to represent directly by Markov chains. In these two chapters, multiple formalisms (RBD + CTMC, RBD + SPN, FT + FT + SPN, for instance) are also employed to model even more convoluted systems through hierarchy and refinement.

Computer system performance measures are founded on monitoring the system while being exposed to a workload. A workload could be observed during the system's typical operation or a workload test. For, obtaining meaningful measures, the workload should be carefully characterized. The analyst may also ponder different workloads, how the system is monitored, and how the measured data are summarized and presented.

Contasting performance, reliability, and availability-related measures are tougher to assess system behavior depends on failure incidents. Nevertheless, the time between failures (MTBF) in a dependable system is typically in the order of years, the fault occurrences have to be analytically sped up and injected into the system to analyze faults effects on the system behavior. Reliability data analysis concerns the analysis of observed product lifetimes. This lifetime data can be of products in the market or associated with the system in operation. The data analysis and prediction are described as lifetime data analysis or reliability data analysis.

Part IV is divided into four chapters. Chapter 23 presents the basic performance measuring mechanism applied in computer systems. Chapter 24 discusses workload generation methods. Chapter 26 examines fault monitoring and fault injection procedures. Finally, Chapter 25 presents a set of methods for reliability and maintainability data analysis.
