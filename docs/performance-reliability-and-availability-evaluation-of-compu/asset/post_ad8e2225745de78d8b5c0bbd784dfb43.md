# Composing Parallel and Series Structures

Series and parallel structures can be combined to form more complex configurations. In practice, a system may be decomposed into subsystems, and each subsystem may also be decomposed into smaller subsystems or components. Decomposition is very convenient when defining the structure, reliability, and availability functions of systems.
