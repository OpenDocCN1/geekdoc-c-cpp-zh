# 22.2 MODELING TTF AND TTR WITH PHASE-TYPE DISTRIBUTION

This section presents a single-component system whose time to failure and to repair are not exponentially distributed. Here, phase-type distributions were adopted to represent the TTF and TTR (see Section 13.3.8). In this specific case, the TTF is modeled by an Erlang distribution with $K$ phases and with phase rate $\lambda = 1/ttfph$, where $ttfph$ is the mean value of each exponential phase of the distribution. A Cox-1 distribution represents the TTR. The Cox-1 distribution has three parameters: $\omega_0, \omega_1, KC$ and $\mu = 1/ttprph$, where $KC$ is the number of phases, $ttprph$ is the mean value of each exponential phase of the Cox-1 distribution (see Section 13.3.8).
