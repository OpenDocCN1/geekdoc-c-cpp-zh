R1, F2, OSF, HWF, and STF. In the previous marking, none of these transitions are immediate; then the delay of each enabled timed transition is generated. Firing R2 removes one from place VM1D and stores one in place VM1U. When R2 is fired, the virtual machine is repaired. The repair is conducted by restarting the enabled transitions and calling the respective functions that emulate faults and confidence intervals of system availability, mean time to failure, mean time to restore, and other related measures are provided.
