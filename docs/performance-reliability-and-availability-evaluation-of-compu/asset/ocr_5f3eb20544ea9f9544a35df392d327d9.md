**Example 18.9.5.** The RBD depicted in Figure 18.29.c is a parallel composition of two-blocks series structures. The system is composed of six blocks $\{b_{11}, b_{12}, b_{21}, b_{22}, b_{31}, b_{32}\}$. The system reliability is

```
$R_s(t) = 1 - ((1 - R_{11}(t)R_{12}(t)) \times (1 - R_{21}(t)R_{22}(t)) \times (1 - R_{31}(t)R_{32}(t)))$.
```
