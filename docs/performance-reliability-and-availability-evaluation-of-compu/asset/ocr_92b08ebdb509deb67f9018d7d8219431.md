The block $b_4$ depicts a subsystem configured in $2oo_3$, so its reliability is

$r_4 = 3r^2 - 2r^3$,

where $r$ is the reliability of each subsystem components at $t$. The RBD of Figure 20.11.b may now be transformed by applying the parallel reduction on blocks $b_4$ and $b_{13}$. Figure 20.11.c shows the resulting RBD, in which $b_{14}$ is the resulting block. The reliability of the block $b_{14}$ is depicted by

$r_{14} = 1 - (1 - r_4)(1 - r_{13})$.
