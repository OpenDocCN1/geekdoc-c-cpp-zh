The hardware time to failure was already calculated. Let us assume the hardware time to repair is 24h. The other components MTTF and MTTR are presented in Table 19.2.

Using $A_i = MTTF_i/(MTTF_i + MTTR_i), i \in \{HW,OS,VMM,VM1,VM2,A1,A2,B1,B2\}$, we get $A_{as} = 0.994956958$.

The steady-state availability of the database-server can be calculated similarly. In the application server, the hardware MTTF was already calculated. The hardware MTTR is assumed as 24h. The operational system and database management system time to failure are $MTTF_{OS}=2000h$ and $MTTF_{DBMS}=6000h$, respectively. Their mean time to repair are $MTTR_{OS}=4h$ and $MTTR_{DBMS}=12h$.

> $A_{abs} = A_{hw} 	imes A_{os} 	imes A_{db} = 0.99455954$,

where $A_i = MTTF_i/(MTTF_i + MTTR_i), i \in \{HW_{db},OS_{db},DBMS\}$.
The NAS subsystem availability is expressed as

> $A_{NAS} = A_{NAS} 	imes A_{Storage} = A_{NAS} 	imes (3A_D^2 - 2A_D^3)$,

where $A_{Storage} = 3A_D^2 - 2A_D^3$ is the storage availability and $A_D$ is the availability of a single disk. $A_{NAS}$ is the availability of the NAS controller. As $A_i = MTTF_i/(MTTF_i + MTTR_i), i \in \{NAS,D\}$, and $MTTF_{NAS}=50000h, MTTF_{DB}=35000h$, and $MTTR_{NAS}=24h$, we obtain $A_{NAS}=0.99952023$.

Thus, the system availability is $A = A_{as} 	imes A_{db} 	imes A_{NAS} = 0.98979523$. The yearly downtime in hours is $DT = (1-A) 	imes 8760h = 89.7653h$.
