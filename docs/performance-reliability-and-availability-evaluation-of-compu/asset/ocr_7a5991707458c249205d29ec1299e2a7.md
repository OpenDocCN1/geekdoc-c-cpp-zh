$MC_1^c \Rightarrow \overline{P_1} \vee \overline{P_2} = P_1 \vee P_2$,

$MC_1^c \cap MC_2 \Rightarrow (P_1 \vee P_2) \wedge (\overline{P_1} \wedge M_2 \wedge M_3) = \overline{P_1} \vee P_2 \wedge M_2 \wedge M_3$,
