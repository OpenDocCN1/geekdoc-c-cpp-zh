# Lifetime Data Analysis or Reliability and Availability Data Analysis

covers the background, fundamentals of dependability, reliability, availability and related metrics, lifetime data analysis and fault injection subjects. In a general sense, there are two broad approaches for dealing with reliability and repairing data: the parametric and nonparametric approaches. This course covers many methods of general approaches.
