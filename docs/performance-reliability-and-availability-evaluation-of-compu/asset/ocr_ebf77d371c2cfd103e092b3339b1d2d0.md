$I_{RAW}(t) = \frac{1 - R_t(0,X(t))}{1 - R_s(t)} \quad$ (18.9.14)

where $R_s(t)$ is the system reliability at $t$, and $R_i(0,X(t))$ is the system reliability given component $i$ has failed (or is not present). It is worth mentioning that the RAW importance measure has also been adapted to consider steady-state availability [352].
