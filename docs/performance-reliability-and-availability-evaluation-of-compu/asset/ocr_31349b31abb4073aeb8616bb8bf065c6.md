**Example 26.1.3.** Assume a mechanical system whose time to failure is dependent on electrical tension, $V$, provided by the power supplier, and obeys the IPL

$TTF = BV^{-2}$,

where $B$ is the dimensional constant equal to $10^8 hV^2$. If the mean time to failure, $MTTF_2$, for $V_2 = 120V$ is $7000h$, the mean time to failure, $MTTF_1$ (and the respective-failure rate, assuming it is constant at that respective voltage) taking into account the electrical tension at $V_1 = 140V$ may be estimated using

$MTTF_1 = BV_1^{-2}$,

and

$MTTF_2 = BV_2^{-2}$.

Hence,

$\frac{MTTF_1}{MTTF_2} = \frac{BV_1^{-2}}{BV_2^{-2}}$,

$MTTF_1 = MTTF_2V_1^{-2}V_2^2$, (26.1.10)

$MTTF_1 = 120h 	imes 140^{-2}V^{-2} 	imes 120^2V^2 = 5142.86h$,
