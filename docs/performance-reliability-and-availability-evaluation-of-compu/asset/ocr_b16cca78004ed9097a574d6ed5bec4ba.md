A generative workload approach may be adopted to exercise real operating systems, system prototypes, or system simulators or be adopted as part of a numerical or analytic model. Depending on the context, a suitable abstraction should be adopted to specify the workload. However, in any circumstance, a generative workload representation’ fundamental characteristic resembles the temporal processes’ execution that produces the workloads.
