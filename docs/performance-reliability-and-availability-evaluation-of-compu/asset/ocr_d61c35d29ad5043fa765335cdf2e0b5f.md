The probability of the system $S$ fails during the interval $[t, t + \Delta t]$ if it has survived to the time $t$ (conditional probability of failure) is

```
$$P(t \leq T \leq t + \Delta t | T > t) = \frac{R(t) - R(t + \Delta t)}{R(t)}$$
```

$$P(t \leq T \leq t + \Delta t | T > t)/\Delta t$ is the conditional probability of failure per time unit. When $\Delta t \to 0$, then

```
$$\lim_{\Delta t \to 0} \frac{R(t) - R(t + \Delta t)}{R(t) \times \Delta t} = \lim_{\Delta t \to 0} \frac{[R(t + \Delta t) - R(t)]}{\Delta t} \times \frac{1}{R(t)} =$$
```

```
$$\frac{dR(t)}{dt} \times \frac{1}{R(t)} = \frac{dF_T(t)}{dt} \times \frac{f_T(t)}{R(t)} = \lambda(t), \quad (16.2.6)$
```

where $\lambda(t)$ is named the **hazard function (failure rate function)**. Hazard rates may decrease as decreasing failure rate (DFR), constant failure rate (CFR), or increasing failure rate (IFR).
