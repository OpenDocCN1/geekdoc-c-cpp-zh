$P(T > t_i|T > t_{i-1}) = R(\Delta t_i)$, which denotes the probability of surviving $\Delta t_i$, given it survived $t_{i-1}$, then

> $R(\Delta t_i) = \frac{R(t_i)}{R(t_{i-1})}$,

thus

> $R(t_i) = R(\Delta t_i) \times R(t_{i-1}). \quad (25.2.22)$

Assume

> $$\hat{F}(t_i) = \frac{i}{n+1}$

and

> $$\hat{R}(t_i) = 1 - \hat{F}(t_i)$,

then

> $$\hat{R}(t_i) = 1 - \frac{n+1-i}{n+1}$

and

> $$\hat{R}(t_{i-1}) = \frac{n+1-(i-1)}{n+1} = \frac{n+2-i}{n+1}$
