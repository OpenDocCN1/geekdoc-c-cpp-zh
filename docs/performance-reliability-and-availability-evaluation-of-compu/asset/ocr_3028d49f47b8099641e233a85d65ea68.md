Now assume an experiment in $n$ devices are observed for a period of $T$. Consider during the observation, $r$ devices failed; hence $(n-r)$ were right-censored because they survived the observation period. In such a case, we are dealing with the right-censored of type $I$. Equation 25.3.35 and Equation 25.3.36 may be adapted to estimate the distribution parameter:

```
$$\hat{\lambda} = \frac{r}{\sum_{i=1}^{r} t_i + (n-r)T}. \ (25.3.37)$
```

Thus, the estimate of the mean time to failure is

```
$MTTF = \frac{\sum_{i=1}^{r} t_i + (n-r)T}{r}. \ (25.3.38)$
```
