# 214 Performance, Reliability, and Availability Evaluation of Computational Systems

The instantaneous probability of being in state $U$ and $D$ are respectively¹

$$\pi_U(t) = \frac{\mu}{\lambda + \mu} + \frac{\lambda}{\lambda + \mu} e^{-(\lambda+\mu)t} \quad (21.1.1)$

and

$$\pi_D(t) = \frac{\lambda}{\lambda + \mu} - \frac{\lambda}{\lambda + \mu} e^{-(\lambda+\mu)t} \quad (21.1.2)$

such that $\pi_U(t) + \pi_D(t) = 1$. The instantaneous availability is equal to $A(t) = \pi_U(t)$, and the instantaneous unavailability is equal to $UA(t) = \pi_D(t)$. Figure 21.2 shows $\pi_U(t)$ and $\pi_D(t)$ for $\lambda = 1 fptu$ and $\mu = 3 rptu$ in the interval $t \in (0,2)$.²
