# Performance Evaluation: Measuring and Modeling

This course aims at studying computer systems' performance through modeling and measurements. Such study supports modeling computational systems through stochastic models to tackle complexities related to conflicts, resource sharing, uncertainties, failures, discard, etc. Performance evaluation through methods can be applied during the initial design stages of the system development activities to safeguard the product meets the performance needs. Even when systems are already available and in operation, it may be too costly or unfeasible to stop the system to evaluate scenarios. The modeling covers operational analysis, Markov chains, Markovian queues, stochastic Petri Nets, and simulation. In such cases, the performance may be assessed through measuring, but it represents the operational conditions of the system. It is impossible to create specific scenarios and test the system performance in new scenarios. Having an accurate system model allows the analyst to evaluate such performance cases, better tuning system parameters, and planning system capacity. Besides, this course also covers the measuring process. This part covers the topics already in the previous plan.
