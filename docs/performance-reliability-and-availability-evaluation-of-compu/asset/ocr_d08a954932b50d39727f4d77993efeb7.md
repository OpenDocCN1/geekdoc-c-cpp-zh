The simulator generates three types of transaction requests, that are $tr_1, tr_2$, and $tr_3$. The workload has three modulating states with event arrival follows the rates $\lambda$, $\mu$, and $v$. Each of these states has the sojourn time defined by $Exp(\alpha)$, $Exp(\beta)$, and $Exp(\delta)$, respectively. An event arrival is represented by the firings of transitions $t_l, t_m$, and $t_n$, whose rates are $\lambda, \mu$, and $v$. The sojourn time of each modulating state is specified by assigning the rates $\alpha, \mu$, and $v$ to $ta_1, tb_2$, and $tc_1$ and $tc_2$, respectively.
