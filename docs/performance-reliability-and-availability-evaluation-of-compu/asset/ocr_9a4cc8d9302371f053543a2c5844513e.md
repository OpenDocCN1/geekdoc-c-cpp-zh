Using Equation 25.2.22, we obtain

$\hat{R}(t_i) = \hat{R}(\Delta t_i) \times \hat{R}(t_{i-1}),$

hence

$\hat{R}(\Delta t_i) = \frac{\frac{n+1-i}{n+1}}{\frac{n+1-(i-1)}{n+1}} = \frac{n+2-i}{n+1}},$

$\hat{R}(\Delta t_i) = \frac{n+1-i}{n-2-i} \cdot i. \quad (25.2.23)$

As two sorts of event occur at $t_i$, that is a failure or a censoring, let

$\delta_i = \begin{cases} 1 & \text{if a failure occurs at } t_i \ 0 & \text{if a censoring occurs at } t_i. \end{cases} \quad (25.2.24)$

Therefore,

$\hat{R}(t_i) = \hat{R}(\Delta t_i) \times \hat{R}(t_{i-1}), \quad (25.2.25)$

thus

$\hat{R}(t_i) = (\frac{n+1-i}{n+2-i})\delta_i} \times \hat{R}(t_{i-1})., \quad (25.2.26)$

Hence

$\hat{F}(t_i) = (\frac{i}{i-1})\delta_i} \times \hat{F}(t_{i-1})., \quad (25.2.27)$
