If you're ever been caught in a downpour without a raincoat or umbrella — and who hasn't? — you'll wish you had sprung for this $3.99 app.

You get some basic weather info into, including a detailed 24-hour forecast, a 7-day forecast, and details on wind, humidity, and the UV Index. Dark Sky will also let you know when the sun will rise and when it will set. Through some simulation, you can map the path of upcoming precipitation or even go back in a few days. But for us, the real draw is the down-to–the–minute prediction feature, which in our experience has been right more than wrong.
