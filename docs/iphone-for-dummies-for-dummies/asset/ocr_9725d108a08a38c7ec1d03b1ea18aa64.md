To clear your history — nobody else can trace your steps — and just is it you're hiding? — tap Clear at the bottom right of the screen. You can clear your history for the last hour, for the day you're looking at, for the day you're looking at plus the day before, or all time.

Alternatively, on the Home page, tap Settings > Safari > Clear History and Website-Data. In both instances, per usual, you may have to check out without wiping the slate clean.

Folks browsing the web on their iPhones using their employer's Wi-Fi network shouldn't have a false sense of security. Your bosses will still have a history of every website you have visited, even if you clear your history.
