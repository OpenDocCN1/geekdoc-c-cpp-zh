# Shazam

Ever heard a song on the radio or television, in a store, or at a club, and wondered what it was called or who was singing? With the Shazam app, you may never wonder again. Launch Shazam, tap Touch to Shazam, and point your iPhone's microphone at the source of the music.

If you have an iPhone with 3D Touch, you can press the Shazam icon and choose the Shazam Now quick action shortcut (thus skipping the whole “tap Touch to step).

In a few seconds, the song title and artist's name magically appear on your iPhone screen, as shown in Figure 17–1.

Shazam isn't great at identifying classical music, jazz, big bands, show tunes, or opera, nor is it adept at identifying really obscure indie bands. But if you use it primarily to identify popular music or classic rock, it rocks (pun intended).

As of iOS 10, Siri has Shazam technology built in, which means you don't even need the app to identify a song. Just ask Siri what song is playing, as shown in Figure 17–2, and she'll usually be able to tell you.
