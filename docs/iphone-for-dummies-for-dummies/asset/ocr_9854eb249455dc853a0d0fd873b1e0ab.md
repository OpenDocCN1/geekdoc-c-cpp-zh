# Sharing video

Unlike other video on your iPhone, you can play back what you've just shot in portrait or landscape mode. And if the video is any good (and why wouldn't it be?), you're likely going to want to share it with others. To do so, display the playback controls by tapping the screen, and tap the share icon, which appears all the way to the bottom left of the screen (in the margin). You have several options: Make the video part of a slideshow (see Chapter 9), email it, include it in a message or in your Notes app, or send it to iCloud Photo Sharing, Facebook, Vimeo, or YouTube. (You have additional options if you've enabled a Chinese keyboard.) You can also add the video to the Notes app, or send it via AirDrop to a person who is nearby and carrying his or own AirDrop-compatible device.

To help you remember a video that's worth sharing, tap the heart icon that appears with the playback controls to designate it a favorite. As noted in Chapter 9, you can also tap the heart for still images.

# Watching video on TV

The Retina display on the iPhone is worth boasting, but sometimes you want to watch videos on your iPhone on a larger television. Apple affords you several ways to do:

+   **Add a cable:** Apple is all too happy to sell you a cable to connect the iPhone to a TV. The cable you choose depends on your TV setup, the available ports, and the iPhone model you use. Wired connections include an A/V adapter and HDMI cable (iPhone 4); a composite A/V adapter and a VGA adapter and VGA cable. Apple sells its Digital AV adapter for $49 and Lightning-to-VGA adapter for the same price. Some older cables no longer be available directly from Apple, but you can typically find them online for less money.
**Use wireless streaming:** You've heard us mention AirPlay, a wonderful feature for people who have iPhones and Apple TV boxes. If you're watching video, tap the AirPlay icon or summon it from Control Center, and choose an Apple TV unit from the list of AirPlay devices that shows up. If all goes well, the video you're watching now appears like magic on your TV set. If it doesn't go well, make sure the Apple TV is tapping into the same wireless network as your phone.
