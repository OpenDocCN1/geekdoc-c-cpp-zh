# Heads Up!

The heads-up on Heads Up! shouldn't come as a surprise: This simple Charades-like party game created by the Ellen DeGeneres Show is a blast to play. Ellen turns in a brief video to show you how to play.

But the fact is this 99-cent gem couldn't be easier. You pick a category of card decks — Europas, Blockbuster Movies, and so on — and have one person hold the phone to his or her forehead with the screen facing away. The other player or players tell out clues (or in some instances act out or mime scenes) to get the person holding the phone to correctly guess the names and actions on each card, within a one-minute time limit.
