# List view

List view isn't complicated. As you would expect, your calendar appointments are listed chronologically, as shown in Figure 7-3. If you have a lengthy list, drag up or down with your finger or flick to rapidly scroll through your appointments. If you're looking for a specific calendar entry, you can search for appointments by typing the names, locations, or notes in the search box above your list of entries (or Spotlight to help you search). The search box becomes visible after you tap the search icon.

Tap any of listings to get meeting or appointment details for that entry. If you tap a person's birthday, you see his or her contact information. Sorry, but you just ran out of excuses for not sending a card at least. Tap the calendar + in the upper-right corner to add a new entry, described in detail in the next section.

If you have an iPhone 6s, 6 Plus, 7, or 7 Plus, you can take advantage of the 3D Touch feature. Gently tap a calendar listing to peek at the entry. Press harder to pop into the Event Details page for that listing.
