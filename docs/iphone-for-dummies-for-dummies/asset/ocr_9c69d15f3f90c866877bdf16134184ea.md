Sharp-eyed readers may notice that we aren't covering syncing iPhone apps in this chapter. Apps are so darn cool that we've given them an entire chapter, namely Chapter 15. In that chapter, you discover how to find, sync, rearrange, review, and delete apps, and much, more.

Podcasts and videos from your computer are synced only one way: from your computer to your iPhone. If you delete a podcast or a video that went into your iPhone via syncing, the podcast or video will not be deleted from your computer when you sync. That, if you buy or download any of the following items on your iPhone, the item will be copied back to your computer automatically when you sync:

And if you save pictures from email messages, from the iPhone camera, web pages (by pressing and holding down on an image and tapping the Save Image button), or from screen shots (by pressing the Home and sleep/wake buttons simultaneously), these too can be synced.

# Ringtones, music, movies, and TV shows

You use the Tones, Music, Movies, TV Shows, Podcasts, and iTunes U to specify the media you want to copy from your computer to your iPhone. If you don't see these panes, make sure that your iPhone is selected in the iPhone section of the Summary pane (refer to Figure 3-1).
