# Bill Atkinson PhotoCard

Who is Bill Atkinson? He had a hand (or both hands) in the first Macintosh computer as the MacPaint and HyperCard Mac applications. Today, he's a world-renowned nature photographer, which brings us to his app.

Bill Atkinson PhotoCard is a free app that lets you create high-resolution postcards and send them by email or by the US Postal Service. Email is always free. And though sending by USPS costs between $1.50 and $2.00 per card, depending on how many print-and-mail credits you purchase, the 8½-x5½-inch postcards are stunning. Printed on heavy, glossy stock on a state-of-the-art HP Indigo Digital Press and laminated for protection against damage in mail, they're as beautiful as any postcard you've ever seen.
