You can tell if you even have voicemail in a few ways:

+   A red circular badge showing the number of pending messages awaiting your attention appears above the Phone icon on the Home screen or over the Voicemail icon in the Phone app. The tally, if it appears over the Phone icon on the Home screen, as well as include missed calls as well as voicemail messages.

+   You may also see a message alert on the iPhone display that shows the name of the caller and the word voicemail. You can tap to listen, or close the alert and listen later. You'll also be notified in Notification Center, where you can tap against a notification to return the call.

Whatever draws you, tap that Voicemail icon to display the list of voicemails. You will see the caller's number (assuming this info is known through Caller ID) and, in some cases, his or her name. Or you see the words Unknown or Private Caller.

The beauty of all of this is that you can ignore (or at not listening to) certain messages. We are not in the advice-giving business on what calls you can safely avoid; disregard messages from the IRS or your parole officer at your own risk.

A blue dot to a name or number signifies that you haven't heard the message yet.

To play back a voicemail, tap the name or number in question. Tap the tiny icon that shows up to the left. To pause the message; tap again to resume. Tap Speaker if you want to hear the message through the iPhone's speakerphone. Tap Call Back to immediately dial the person's number, or tap Delete to remove the voicemail from the list.

Tap the i-in-a-circle next to a caller's name or number to bring up any contact info on the phone or add the caller to your contacts.
