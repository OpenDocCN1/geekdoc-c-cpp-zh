# # A Brief iCloud Primer

Apple's iCloud service is more than just a wireless hard drive in the sky. iCloud is a complete data synchronization and wireless storage solution. In a nutshell, iCloud can store and manage your digital stuff — your music, photos, contacts, events, and more — and make it available to all your computers and devices automatically.

iCloud pushes information such as email, calendars, contacts, reminders, and bookmarks to from your computer and to your iPhone and other iDevices, then keeps those items updated on all devices wirelessly and with- any effort on your part. iCloud, iCloud Photo Library, and iCloud Photo Sharing (see Chapter 9), and email (see Chapter 12).
