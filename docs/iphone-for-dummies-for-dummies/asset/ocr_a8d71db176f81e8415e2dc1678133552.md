# Music, music videos, and voice memos

To transfer music to your iPhone, select the Music pane and then select the Sync music box. You can then select the option for Entire Music Library or Selected Playlists, Artists, Albums, and Genres, as shown in Figure 3–3. If you choose the latter, click the check boxes next to the particular playlists, artists, albums, and genres you want to transfer. You can also choose to include music videos or voice memos or by selecting the appropriate check boxes at the top of the pane.
