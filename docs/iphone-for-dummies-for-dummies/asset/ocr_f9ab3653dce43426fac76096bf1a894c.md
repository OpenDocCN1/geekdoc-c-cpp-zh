After tapping Send with picture and accompanying message on your, you have the option to change the image size (small, medium, or large) or keep the actual size. Consider the trade-offs: A smallersized image may get through any limits imposed by your or the recipient's Internet provider or company. +   > iCloud Photo Sharing: You can post pics to a shared album.

+   > Add to Notes: Sometimes you take pictures as a reference point, maybe a shot of where you parked, for example. You can stash images in the Notes App.

+   > Twitter: Lots of people send pictures with these tweets they tweet. The iPhone makes it breeze. Tap Twitter and your picture is embedded in an outgoing tweet. Add your words, sticking to Twitter's character limit of 140, and Post.

+   > Facebook: And lots of people share photos on the world's largest social network. After your Facebook account is configured, you too can post there from your iPhone.

+   > Sina Weibo and Tencent Weibo: If you've enabled a Chinese keyboard, you'll see options for China's own social networks.

+   > Flickr: The Yahoo-owned service is another popular photo-sharing destination.

+   > Save PDF to iBooks: You can turn the photo into a PDF that resides in the iBooks on your phone.

+   > Copy: To copy the image and paste it into an email or elsewhere.

+   > Duplicate: When two copies of the same image are better than one.

+   > Slideshow: As mentioned, this is your starting point for a slideshow, with an optional musical soundtrack.

+   > AirPlay: Own an Apple TV set-top box? You can use AirPlay to stream photos from the TV.

+   > Hide: You can hide an image so that it is hidden from your moments, collections, and years views but still visible in Albums.
