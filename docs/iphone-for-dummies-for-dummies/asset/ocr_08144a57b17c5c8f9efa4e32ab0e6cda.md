# # Waze

Lots of excellent GPS mapping apps can help you navigate unfamiliar territory or keep up with traffic. The iPhone's built-in Maps app has turn-by-turn navigation, of course. And we're fans of Google Maps (which used to be native to the iPhone).

Waze, which is now owned by Google, takes a different route. It relies on the community—other drivers who have the Waze app to report (by tapping one of the icons shown in Figure 17-13) accidents, speed traps, hidden cameras, and bottlenecks.

And Waze is useful even when individual drivers don't report their or other conditions because the app exploits crowdsourcing, passively leveraging in real time from you and everyone else with the app to determine traffic and how long it will take to get from here to here.
