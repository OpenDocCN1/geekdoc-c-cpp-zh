# VPN

A virtual private network, or VPN, is typically a way to securely access your company’s network behind the firewall — using an encrypted Internet connection that acts as a “tunnel” for data. The iPhone software supports the protocols IKEv2 (Internet Key Exchange), L2TP (Layer 2 Tunneling Protocol), and IPSec, which anyway provides the kind of security that satisfies network administrators.

![](bbox=[0.173, 0.126, 0.231, 0.203])

You can configure a VPN on the iphone by tapping VPN under General, tapping Add VPN Configuration, and tapping one of the aforementioned protocols. Then, using configuration settings provided by your company, fill in the private server information, account, password, encryption level (if appropriate), and other information. Better yet, lend your iPhone to the techies where you work and let them fill in the blanks for you. After you configure your iPhone for VPN usage, you can turn that capability on or off by tapping (yep) the VPN on or off switch under Settings.

# iTunes Wi-Fi sync

Through the magic of wireless, you no longer have to connect a cable to a PC or Mac to sync your iPhone with your iTunes account. If you’ve set up Wi-Fi syncing with iTunes, you can click Sync Now here to commence a Wi-Fi syncing session. For more syncing, consult Chapter 3. To get started, you have to connect to iTunes on a computer using a cable, and then click Sync with This iPhone over Wi-Fi.

# Spotlight search

You can designate which apps on your phone are searchable when you advantage Spotlight. (As a reminder, you initiate a Spotlight search by dragging down any screen, or if you have to the Home screen, swiping from left to right to summon the Today screen.) By default, all the options on the list shown in Settings will be part of a search. Tap the switch to remove any app that you don’t want the iPhone to include in its search mission.

Meantime, if Siri Suggestions is on, Siri can suggest apps, people, locations, and more, before you type. You see Siri Suggestions on the Today screen. Siri is iOS 10 to make your phone work for you proactively.

Spotlight shines its search beyond your phone. You can get Spotlight suggestions from the Internet, iTunes, and the App Store. Among other suggestions, you might receive timeouts for movies and info on nearby attractions.
