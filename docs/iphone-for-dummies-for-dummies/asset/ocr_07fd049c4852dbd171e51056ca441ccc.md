# Tunity

Tunity lets you listen to the audio from any TV through your iPhone or the speaker (though it's decidedly more useful with headphones). It's useful at the gym, in a sports bar, in a waiting room, and at home when your other is sleeping.

Tunity is cool, but the way it identifies what you're watching on TV is even cooler: Just point your iPhone at the TV, as shown in Figure 17-10, and tap to scan. In a few seconds, you'll hear through your iPhone whatever is playing on that TV.

That's the good news. The bad news is that it doesn't work with the majority of channels. It's good with sports but not so good for the cable channels we like.

Still, you can't beat Tunity when it works and it costs nothing to have it on your iPhone until you need it. Feel free to thank us then.
