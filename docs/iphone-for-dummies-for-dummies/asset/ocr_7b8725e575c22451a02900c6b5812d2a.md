If you select a video, it will be compressed and an icon for the movie will be inserted into the body of the message, as shown in Figure 12-18. (The recipient would want to tap to play the movie.)

If the video is too long, you won't be able to send it by email. (You may want to consider other choices for sharing the video, such as the online service Dropbox.) But than merely telling you that you can't send the video — nah, nah, nana nah — Apple provides an editing tool for shortening the sequence into a clip that you will be able to send along.
