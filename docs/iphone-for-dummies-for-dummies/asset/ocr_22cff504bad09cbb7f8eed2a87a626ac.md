# Happy haptics (iPhone 7 and 7 Plus)

The iPhone 7 and 7 Plus have an especially large haptic engine, the Apple technology that produces the vibrations that lend tactile feedback when using your phone. Here's where you can control what to receive feedback. On each device, tap Settings > Sounds & Haptics and scroll down to System Haptics. When this is enabled, the phone will play the various haptics for system controls and other interactions. Flip it off if you'd prefer otherwise.

# Happy Home button (iPhone 7 and 7 Plus)

The Home button on the iPhone 7 and 7 Plus is not a mechanical button at all rather a customizable solid-state-sensitive button that can deliver feedback via the aforementioned taptic engine. The button doesn't physically move, but it makes you think that it does. And the Home button also course invokes Siri, multitasking, Touch ID, Apple Pay, and other functions.

In Settings General on the 7 or 7 Plus, you'll find a Home Button listing where you can sample one of three touch options. Tap one of the three circular buttons (labeled 1, 2, or 3) and then tap the actual Home button to see how the Home button feels under that option. Tap Done to apply the setting with which you are most comfortable.
