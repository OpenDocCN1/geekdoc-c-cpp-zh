# Reading reviews from your computer

If you click Ratings and Reviews (above the screenshots of the app), you find a series of reviews by the folks who own the app. Each review includes a star rating, from zero to five; if an app is rated four or higher, it's probably liked by people who own it. The star rating and number of reviews also appear on the left side of the details screen (four and a half stars; 336 reviews for Real Racing 3).

# Downloading an app to your computer

Downloading an app is simple. When you find an app you want to try, click the Get button if it's free or the Buy button if it's not. At that point, you have to log to your iTunes Store account if you're not already logged in, even if the app is free.

After you log in, the app begins downloading. When the app has finished downloading, click the Library tab, as shown in Figure 15-5.
