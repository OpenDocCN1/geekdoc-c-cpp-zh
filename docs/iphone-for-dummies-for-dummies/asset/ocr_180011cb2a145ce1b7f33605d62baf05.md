# Details, details, details

To see the details for an item, tap it and the lower portion of the screen will offer additional information. Note the three dots above the world Marketed near the center of the screen. These dots tell you that three screens of information are available, all shown in Figure 13-11. To switch between these screens, swipe wisely to the left or the right on the lower part of the screen.

# Charting a course

Referring to Figure 13-11, note the chart at the bottom of the middle image. At the top of the chart, see a bunch of numbers and letters, 1D, 1W, 3M, 6M, 1Y, and 2Y. They stand for 1 day, 1 week, 3 months, 6 months, 1 year, and 2 years. Tap one of them and the chart is updated to reflect that period of time (In Figure 13-11, 6 is selected).
