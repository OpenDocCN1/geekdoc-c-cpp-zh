# Play it again

Tap Playlists on the main library screen to view a list of your playlists. If you don't
have any playlists on your iPhone, or don't know what a playlist is, don't sweat it. If
you had any, you'd find them here; if you don't know what they are, let us tell you.

Playlists let you organize and organize collections of songs around a particular
or mood: opera operas, romantic ballads, British invasion, or whatever. You can
make playlists of your favorite albums, songs of all times, or greatest
hits collections. (Younger folks sometimes call playlists mixes; older folks think of
them the digital equivalent of a mix tape.)

Tap a playlist and a list of the songs it contains. If the list is longer than
one screen, flick upward to scroll down. Tap a song in the list and it plays. Or tap
Shuffle All (above the list of songs) to hear a song at random from the playlist; all
ubsequent songs will then play at random.

That's all there is to selecting and playing songs from your.

It's easier to create playlists in iTunes on your computer than in the Music app
on your iPhone.

As you see in Chapter 3, you can sync playlists with iTunes on your computer. Or
if you're an Apple Music subscriber, all your playlists are always available on
devices.

Although it may be easier to create playlists in iTunes on your computer, this is
an iPhone book, so we show you how to create playlists in the Music app on your
iphone:

+   1. Tap the Music Icon.
2. Tap the Library icon at the bottom of the screen.
3. On the Library screen, tap Playlists.
4. Tap New Playlist at the top of the Playlists list.
5. Tap the Playlist Name field and type a name for your new playlist.
6. (Optional) Tap the Description field and type a description of your new
playlist.
