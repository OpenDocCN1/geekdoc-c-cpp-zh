# iPhone 7 Plus only — two rear cameras are better than one

As mentioned, the 7 Plus has not one but two cameras, and they work as a team. With this dual-camera system, you tap a button on the iPhone display to switch from a 16 wide angle (28mm-equivalent focal length) to a 2x telephoto (56mm lens) shot or back (see Figure 9-3).

You are taking advantage of the optical zoom on the iPhone 7 Plus, as we explained is more of a big-deal benefit compared to digital zoom.

Of course, you can put the digital zoom to work here as well, to a max of 10x. You can employ the pinch to zoom gesture (which you now know). Or, after tapping to get 2x with the optical zoom, slide your finger in direction to zoom left or right.

A free software update promises another benefit for the 7 Plus, but it's avail- yet when we wrote this book. It's a depth-of-field portrait mode, which lets you keep your main subject sharp and in focus while the background remains blurry but not in an artsy way. Photographers refer to this concept as bokeh, and when the feature comes to the 7 Plus, you'll see another shooting option on the Camera screen (next to Video, Photo, Square, and so on) named Portrait.
