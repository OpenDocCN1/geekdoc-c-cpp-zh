# Chapter 8

# Get in Tune(s): Audio
on Your iPhone

As we mention elsewhere in this book, your iPhone is one of the best
iPods ever — especially for working with audio and video. In this chapter,
content; in Chapter 10, we show you how to use your iPhone to listen to music and other audio

We start with a quick tour of the iPhone’s Music app. Then we look at how to listen
music on your iPhone. After you’re nice and comfy with using this way, we
show you how to customize your listening experience so it’s just the way you
like it. Then we offer a few tips to help you get the most out of using your iPhone
as an audio player. Finally, we show you how to use the iTunes to buy music,
audiobooks, videos, and more, and how to download free content.

We assume that you already synced your iPhone with your computer or with
iCloud and that your iPhone contains audio content — songs, podcasts, or audio-
books. If you don’t have any audio on your iPhone, we highly suggest that
you get some (flip to Chapter 3 and follow the instructions for syncing, or launch
the iTunes Store app and buy a song or download a free podcast) before you read
the rest of this chapter.

Okay, now that you have some audio content on your iPhone to play, are you
ready to rock?
