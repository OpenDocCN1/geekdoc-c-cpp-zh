If you tap another share icon option instead of Add Bookmark (refer to Figure 11–6, left), you can then tap

+   ** AirDrop to share the page with other people who have compatible devices and AirDrop turned on. You'll need to turn on AirDrop yourself in Control Center. You can then choose to make your iPhone discoverable to everyone or people in your contacts.

+   ** Message and send a link to the web page in a text or an iMessage.

+   ** Mail and the Mail program opens, with a link for the page and the name of the site or page in the Subject line.

+   ** Reminders to add the page to the Reminders app. By Tapping Options, you can be reminded on a specific day and at a specific location as well as assign a priority to the reminder.

+   ** Add to Notes and the web page reveals a spot in the Notes app.

+   ** Twitter to add the web page to an outgoing tweet. Of course, you must include in the rest of the actual page, remembering that Twitter limits you to 140 characters max. Tap Location if you want to identify your location at the time you posted your tweet. If this is your first time attempting to use Twitter on the iPhone, you will be prompted to go to Settings to enter your username and password.

+   ** Facebook to post the page — and whatever you choose to add — to the social network. Again, you can tap Location to include your location. Tap Audience to determine whether you can see your post.

+   ** Sina Weibo, if available on your phone, to post via this Chinese microblogging service.

+   ** Tencent Weibo, if available on your phone, if you'd rather use this Chinese service.

+   ** Save PDF to eBooks to do just that.

+   ** Add to Favorites makes your designated sites get marquee treatment in Safari.

+   ** Add to Reading List and create a bookmark to save pages that you want to read later. When you want to access your reading list, tap the bookmarks icon to summon your list of bookmarks. The reading list is represented by an eyeglasses icon. Through iCloud, you can keep this reading list across all your iOS devices, as well as your PC or Mac.

+   ** Add to Home Screen to add the site's icon to your Home screen so you can quickly access the site. Note that the Add to Home screen already shows the logo for the website you're visiting or a small image of the page itself. You can label the Home screen icon before tapping Add to complete the process.
