Setting up Favorites is a breeze. When looking at one of your contacts, you may have noticed the Add to Favorites option. When you tap this, all the numbers you have for that pop up. Tap the number you want to make into favorite, and it turns up on the list.

You can get to your contacts also by tapping + in the upper-left corner of the screen.

If any of your chosen folks happen to fall out of favor, you can easily kick them off the Favorites roster. Here's how:

+   1. Tap the Edit button in the upper-right corner of the screen.
   Note that a red circle with a horizontal line appears to the left of each name in the list.
2. Tap the circle next to the name getting the leave-ho. Swipe from right to left on the person's name.
   The swiping gesture is common throughout your iPhone experience. A red Delete button appears to the right of the name, as shown in Figure 4-2.
3. Tap Delete.
   The person (or one of his given phone numbers) is no longer afforded the privilege of being in your iPhone inner circle.

Booming off the Favorites list does not remove that person from the main Contacts.

You can rearrange the order in which your favorites are displayed. Tap Edit, and to the right of the person you want to move, press your finger against the symbol that looks like three stacked horizontal lines. Drag that symbol to the place on the list where you want your favorite contact to appear. Tap Done when the listings are as you like them.
