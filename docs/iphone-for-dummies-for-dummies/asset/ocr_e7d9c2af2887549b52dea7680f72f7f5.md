# THE GLOBETROTTER IPHONE

Different countries employ different radios and frequencies, and Apple has managed to cram a bunch of radios into the latest iPhones.

Among its networks, the iPhone supports GSM/EDGE radios (850, 900, 1,800, and 1,900 MHz), UMTS/HSPA+/DC-HSDPA (850, 900, 1700/2100, 1,900, and 2,100 MHz), plus CDMA EV-DO Rev. A and Rev B radios (800, 1700/2100, 1,900, and 2,100 MHz and more). Oh, and radios for Bluetooth, Wi-Fi, and GPS, Wi-Fi Calling now available in more global markets, can be a godsend if you have a nonexistent or poor cellular signal.

The phones also support LTE (Long Term Evolution) networks. LTE, a 4G (fourth-generation) communications standard, handles data rather than voice calls, and Verizon is the early LTE leader, though the other carriers are all in the game.

The iPhone 6, 6 Plus, 6s, 6s Plus, SE, and 7 Plus can tap into faster LTE networks and Support over LTE (VoLTE) technology, for high-quality broadband bands. VoLTE is available in select markets with carriers; T-Mobile is the first to support this capability in the U.S. Wi-Fi Calling is also possible on the newest phones, T-Mobile is the first to exploit this capability as well.

The iPhone 7 and 7 Plus support up to 25 LTE bands and technology known as LTE Advanced. The promise: exceptionally fast data speeds.

When you're cruising at LTE speeds, you'll see the little LTE indicator in the upperleft corner of the status bar. But just to confuse you, AT&T displays a 4G indicator instead of LTE when you're cruising a fast but slightly slower cellular network called HSDPA. We are obliged to point out that sometimes your carrier lets you down, with dropped or less pristine quality calls. More than not, we've been happy with the quality of voice calls on the iPhone.

When you're abroad, your carrier must run on something called international roaming (unless, you live in a foreign land and have a local carrier). Consult your carrier for the latest international rates. If you're calling the United States overseas, you can take advantage of Dial Assist. This feature automatically adds the proper prefix to U.S. numbers dialed from abroad. Tap Settings > Phone > Dial Assist.
