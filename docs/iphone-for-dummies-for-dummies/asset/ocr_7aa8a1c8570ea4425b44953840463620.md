>> Slide your finger against the filmstrip at the bottom of the Photos app: This lets you quickly pore through the pics in an album in either direction.

>> Switch from landscape or portrait mode: The iPhone's cool sensors are at work. When you turn the iPhone sideways, the picture automatically reorients itself from portrait to landscape mode. Rotate the device back to portrait mode and the picture adjusts accordingly.

>> Zoom: Double-tap to zoom in an image and make it larger. Do so again to zoom out and make it smaller. Alternatively, on the phone, spread your thumb and index finger to zoom in and pinch to zoom out.

>> Pan and scroll: This cool little feature was once practically guaranteed to make you the life of the party. Now it's commonplace if no less cool. After you zoom in a picture, drag it around the screen with your finger. Besides impressing your friends, you can bring front and center the part of the image you most care about. That lets you zoom in Fido's adorable face as opposed to say, the unflattering picture of the person holding the dog in his lap.

>> Swipe up: The gesture shows the people and related views that we introduce you in the "Making memories" section.

# Launching slideshows

Those of us store lot of photos on computers are familiar with slideshows of those images. It's a breeze to replicate the experience on the iPhone:

+   1. Choose an album in the albums list.

To so, tap the Photos icon from the Home screen or tap the Camera icon in the Camera app.

+   2. Do one of the following:

+   * In the Photos app: Select a picture from an album, tap it to summon the picture controls (as shown in Figure 9-13, left), and then tap the share icon, in the bottom left. At the top of the screen, the image you selected as the basis of your slideshow has a check mark, as shown in Figure 9-13, right. Scroll to the left or right to view other pictures in the slideshow and tap any or of those adjacent images to select the slideshow as well. Check marks appear as you do so. Tap any picture second time to deselect and remove the check mark.

+   * In the Camera app: Tap the image in the lower-corner of the screen to display the most recent image you've shot, and a picture to include in your slideshow. From there, follow the instructions for the Photos app to tap and choose other images for the slideshow.
