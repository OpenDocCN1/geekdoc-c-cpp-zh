+   6. In the Backups section of the Summary pane, click iCloud or This Computer.

Your iPhone creates a backup of its contents automatically every time you sync, regardless of whether you sync using a USB cable or wirelessly.

If you choose this Computer, you can encrypt and password-protect your backups by selecting the Encrypt iPhone backup check box.

You want to create an encrypted backup even if you don't care about privacy. Backups to iCloud are encrypted automatically, if you choose to do that, you're golden. But backups to your hard drive are not encrypted unless you select the box. The problem: if you don't encrypt it (by either selecting the box for backups to your computer or by backing up to iCloud), your passwords, health data, and other sensitive information are not backed up. And trust us: you want that backed up.
