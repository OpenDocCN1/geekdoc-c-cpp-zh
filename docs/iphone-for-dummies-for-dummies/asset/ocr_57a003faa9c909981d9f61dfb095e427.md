If you'd rather keep Siri silent — you may be in a public setting and don't want other people to know who is calling you — choose the Never option so that none of your incoming calls are announced.

# # Rejecting the call

We're going to assume that you're not a cold-hearted person out to break a caller's heart. Rather, we assume that you're a busy person who will call back at a more convenient time.

Keeping that positive spin in mind, there are a few ways to reject a call on the spot and send the call to voicemail:

+   * Tap the red Decline button. Couldn't be easier than that.
* Do nothing and let the call go to voicemail.
* Press the sleep/wake button twice in rapid succession. (The button is on the top or side of the device).
* Using the supplied headset, press and hold the microphone button on the headset for a couple of seconds, and let go. Two beeps let you know that the call was indeed rejected. Since Bluetooth earbuds, piercing and holding down other buttons does the trick.

# # Replying with a text message

Sometimes you can't take a call because, well, you can't take a call — you're under the gun at work or the kids demand your attention. You want to be polite or give callers a sense that you do care about them. Consider replying with a text message.
