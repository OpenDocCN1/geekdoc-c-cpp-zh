The interactive notification feature has a major role in Mail. For, if the Mail program recognizes a restaurant or a flight status that arrives in an incoming mail, you'll receive a notification to that, giving you a chance to add the information to your calendar. If a phone number is recognized in a mail message, a notification will ask you to add the number to your contacts.

Moreover, you can act upon Mail notifications that arrive in Notification Center via 3D Touch. Gently press against the notification and then select Trash or Mark as Read. We have more to say about 3D Touch's role in Mail later in this chapter.
