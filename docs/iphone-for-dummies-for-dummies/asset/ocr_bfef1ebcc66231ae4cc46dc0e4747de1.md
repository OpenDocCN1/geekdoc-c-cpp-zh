Although it's useful to keep tabs on all this stuff, you don't want to be hit over the head with information, distracting you from whatever else you're doing on the iPhone. Apple delivers notifications unobobtrively by displaying banners at the top of the screen that disappear until you actively choose to view them. And when you want to do that, you can summon Notification Center, shown in Figure 2-15, by swiping down from the top of the screen.

You can also choose which notifications you will see. Tap Notifications and then tap the notifications you want to see. More than that, you can customize the way those notifications appear. For example, in Settings, you can Reminders under the list of apps and widgets that you can include in Notification Center. Figure 2-16
