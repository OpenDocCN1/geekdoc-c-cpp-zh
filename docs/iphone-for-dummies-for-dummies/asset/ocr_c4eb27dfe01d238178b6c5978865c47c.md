To use an old cable with these new devices requires a Lightning-30-pin adapter, which Apple sells for $19 or $29. (For that price, you should probably buy a new USB-to-Lightning cable instead — unless you have old devices that you still need to connect and sync.)

+   3. Restart your iPhone and try to sync again.

We describe restarting your iPhone in full and loving detail in the "Restart iPhone" section, earlier in this chapter.

+   4. Restart your computer.

We found that restarting your computer often fixes non-iPhone issues as well. It's a good idea to reboot your computer before you do any kind of troubleshooting, be it with your iPhone or your computer.

+   5. Install iTunes.

Even if you have an iTunes installer handler, you would probably visit the Apple website and download the latest-and-greatest version, in just case. You'll always find the latest version of iTunes at www.apple.com/itunes/download.

# # More Help on the Apple Website

If you try everything we suggest earlier in this chapter and still have problems, don't give up just yet. This section describes a few places you may find helpful. We recommend that you check out some or all of them before you threw in the towel and smash your iPhone into tiny little pieces (or ship it back to Apple for repairs, as described in the next section).

First, Apple offers an excellent set of support resources on our website at www.apple.com/support/iphone. You can browse support issues by category, search for a problem by keyword, or even get personalized help by phone, as shown in Figure 16-1.

While you're visiting the Apple support pages, check the discussion communities at http://discussions.apple.com. They're check-full of questions and answers from other iPhone users. Our experience shows that if you can't find an answer to a support question, you can also find something helpful in these forums. You can browse by community — Using iPhone, iPhone Hardware, iPhone in Business and Education, or iPhone Accessories; by subcategory, as shown in Figure 16–2 for the Using iPhone community; or search by keyword.
