Tap the slow-motion video you want to watch. The video starts playing at normal speed, and then slows at a point determined by the phone. You can adjust this slow-motion section by sliding the vertical bars above the frame viewer (see Figure 10-5). When the vertical lines are close, the video is playing back at a normal speed. The video plays back slowly when the lines are spread apart. When you're playing back a segment in motion, any accompanying audio is slowed down.

# Going fast

The Time-Lapse camera feature has the opposite effect of Slo-Mo, enabling you to capture a scene and play it back at a warped speed. Time-Lapse is the same way that you select other shooting modes and tap the record icon. The app captures photos at dynamically selected intervals. As of this writing, there was no way to adjust the interval manually.

# Editing what you shot

We assume that you captured some really great stuff — as well as some footage that belongs to the cutting room floor. That's not a problem because you can perform simple edits on your iPhone. Just do the following:

+   1. Tap a video recording to display the on-screen controls, and then tap the Edit icon (shown in the margin).

+   2. Drag the start and end points along the timeline to select only the video you want to keep, as shown in Figure 10-6.
