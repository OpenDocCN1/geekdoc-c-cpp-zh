# Listening to recordings

After you capture your thoughts or musings, how do you play them back?

You can start playback in a couple of ways, and both involve the same icon. Immediately after recording the memo and tapping Done, you'll get a chance to name your recording. When you do, a list of all your recordings pops up in chronological order, as shown in Figure 7-1, with the most recent on top. That memo is the one you just recorded, of course. When you launch the app, you'll also see a list of the few recent memos you've made. To make a more complete list, tap Done. From the list, tap the recording in question and then tap the play icon to start listening.

You can drag the playback along the scrub bar to move to any point in the membro.

If you don't hear anything after tapping play, tap the speaker icon in the upper-right corner of the screen. Sound will pump through the built-in iPhone speaker.

> TIP

# Trimming recordings

Maybe the person who left the recording rambled on and on. You just want to trim the chase, for goodness' sake. Fortunately, you can trim the audio directly on the iPhone.

To do so, tap a recording and tap Edit. Then tap the trim icon (shown in the margin) and drag the red start and end markers to choose the audio segment you want to keep. To conclude the process, tap Trim — but before doing so, recommend that you preview your edit by tapping the play icon.

Your edits are permanent. Make sure that you're completely satisfied with your cuts before tapping Trim — or that you've synced the original back to iTunes on your computer so that you're working with a copy.
