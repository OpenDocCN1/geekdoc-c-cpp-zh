> Note that a music file doesn't have to be stored on your iPhone to be available on the Library tab. However, you must have Internet access to play songs that aren't on your phone. ## Play it again

Tap Playlists on the main library screen to view a list of your playlists. If you don't have any playlists on your iPhone, or don't know what a playlist is, don't sweat it. If you had any, you'd find them here; if you don't know what they are, let us tell you.

Playlists let you organize and organize collections of songs around a particular theme or mood: operas, romantic ballads, British invasion, or whatever. You can make playlists of your favorite albums, songs of all times, or greatest hits collections. (Younger folks sometimes call playlists mixes; older folks think of them the digital equivalent of a mix tape.)

Tap a playlist and a list of the songs it contains. If the list is longer than one screen, flick upward to scroll down. Tap a song in the list and it plays. Or tap Shuffle All (above the list of songs) to hear a song at random from the playlist; all subsequent songs will then play at random.

That's all there is to selecting and playing songs from your playlists.

It's easier to create playlists in iTunes on your computer than in the Music app on your iPhone.

As you see in Chapter 3, you can sync playlists with iTunes on your computer. Or if you're an Apple Music subscriber, all your playlists are always available on devices.

Although it may be easier to create playlists in iTunes on your computer, this is an iPhone book, so we show you how to create playlists in the Music app on your iPhone:

- 1. Tap the Music Icon.
- 2. Tap the Library icon at the bottom of the screen.
- 3. On the Library screen, tap Playlists.
- 4. Tap New Playlist at the top of the Playlists list.
- 5. Tap the Playlist Name field and type a name for your new playlist.
- 6. (Optional) Tap the Description field and type a description of your new playlist.

![](img/8361dd8dda9818e97bf48313b13b8d6b_167_0.png)

- 7. Tap Add Music (below the description field).
- 8. Tap Playlists, Artists, Albums, Songs, Video, Genres, Composers, Compilations, or Downloaded Music to choose songs for this playlist.

If you choose Songs, an alphabetical list of songs appears.

If you choose any of the other options, a list of whatever you selected — Genres, Composers, Albums, and such — appears. You need to tap an Album, Genre, or Composer before you see a list of songs.

Finally, note the little + that appears to the right of each song.

- 9. Tap the + to a song name to add the song to your playlist.

Repeat this process until you've added all the songs you want on this playlist.

- 10. Tap the Done button in the upper-right corner.

If you create a playlist on your iPhone and sync with your computer, the playlist remains on the iPhone and will also appear in iTunes on your computer, or if you're an Apple Music subscriber, the playlist will appear on all devices within a few minutes.

The playlists remain until you delete them in iTunes or on your iPhone. To remove a playlist in iTunes, select the playlist's name in the source list and then press Delete or Backspace. To remove a playlist on your iPhone, tap the Edit button (at the top of the list) (left), tap the red minus sign to the left of its name, and then tap the red Delete - that appears to the right of its name.

You can also edit playlists on your iPhone. To do so, tap the playlist you want to edit and then tap the Edit button near the top of the screen on the right to do any (or all) of the following:

- **Move a song up or down in the playlist:** An icon with three gray bars appears to the right of each song, as shown in Figure 8-3. Drag the icon up to move the song higher in the list or drag to move the song lower in the list.

- **Add more songs to the playlist:** Tap the + Add Songs button above the list.
