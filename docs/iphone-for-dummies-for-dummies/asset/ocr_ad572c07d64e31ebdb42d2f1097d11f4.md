# Restart your iPhone

If you recharge your iPhone and it still misbehaves, the next thing to try is restart-it. Just as restarting a computer often fixes problems, restarting your iPhone sometimes works wonders.

Here's how to restart:

+   1. Press and hold down the sleep/wake button
2. Slide the red slider to turn off the iPhone, and then wait a few seconds.
3. Press and hold down the sleep/wake button until the Apple logo appears on the screen.

If your iPhone is still frozen, misbehaves, or doesn't start up, it's time to try the third R, resetting your iPhone.

# Reset your iPhone

To reset any iPhone a 7 or 7 Plus, merely press and hold down the sleep/wake button while pressing and holding the Home button on the front at the same time. After about ten seconds, the screen will go black; when the Apple logo reappears, you can release buttons.

> Because iPhone 7 and 7 Plus have solid-state Home buttons, which may not respond if your iPhone isn't running properly, the reset procedure is slightly different. Instead of pressing and holding down sleep/wake and Home, press hold down the sleep/wake and volume buttons.

Resetting your iPhone is like forcing your computer to restart after a crash. Your data shouldn't be affected by a reset. So don't be shy about using this technique a. In many cases, your iPhone goes back to normal after you reset it.

> Remember to press and hold down the sleep/wake button and the Home button (or the volume down button for the iPhone 7 or 7 Plus). If you press both and then release them, you create a screen shot — a picture of yours at the time — rather than resetting your iPhone. This type of picture, by the way, is stored in the Photos app's Camera Roll album as well as the Screen-shots.)
