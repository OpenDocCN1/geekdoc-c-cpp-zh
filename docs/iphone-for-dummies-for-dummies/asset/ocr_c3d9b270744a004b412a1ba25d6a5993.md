# Using Your iPhone to Find Apps

Finding apps with your iPhone is almost as easy as finding them using iTunes. To browse, search, download, and install apps, the only requirement is that you have an Internet connectionction of some sort — Wi-Fi or wireless data network.

To get started, tap the App Store icon on your iPhone's Home screen. After you launch the App Store, you see five icons at the bottom of the screen, representing five ways to interact with the store, as shown in Figure 15-6.

# Looking for apps from your iPhone

The first four icons at the bottom of the screen — Featured, Categories, Top Charts, and Search offer two ways to browse the virtual shelves of the App Store.

Tap the Featured icon and you'll see a mini-version of the iTunes App Store (shown in Figure 15-1) when you search on your computer, with similar departments, namely New Apps We Love, New Games We Love, and What're Playing.

Top Charts, Categories, and Search offer different ways to view the App Store's shelves. The Top Charts section works the same as the Featured section. The three departments represent the most popular apps that either cost money (and top gross) or (don't).

> Top Paid, Free, and Top Grossing

Each page displays dozens upon dozens of apps, but you only see a handful at a time on the screen. Remember to flick up and down and left to see all the others.
