To change or delete the ringtone for a specific person, go back into Contacts and tap Edit. Tap Ringtone and then choose a different ringtone for the person or revert to the default. Tap Done.

In the ringtone section Settings, you can choose whether the phone will vibrate when you get a call (on a ring or silent). And you can choose various vibration styles (as a heartbeat or an SOS) and even create your own vibration patterns, which you can name. Ringtones also apply to FaceTime, and a variant, text tones, works with Messages.

# While on a Call

You can do lots of things while talking on an iPhone, such as consulting your calendar, taking notes, or checking the weather. Press the Home button to get the apps that let you perform these tasks.

If you're using Wi-Fi, 3G, or 4G and on an AT&T or a T-Mobile iPhone, you can also surf the web (through Safari) while talking on the phone. You can surf while you talk if your only outlet to cyberspace is, say, the pokier AT&T EDGE network or CDMA network which uses the ones
that Sprint relies on.

Here are other things you can do while on a call:

+   **Mute call:** From the main screen (shown in Figure 4-8), tap Mute. Now you need not mutter under your breath when the caller ticks you off. Tap Mute again to unmute the sound.

+   **Tap to display the Contacts list:** This option is useful if you want to look up a friend's name while you're talking to another pal or add someone to a conference call.

+   **Place call on hold:** Touch and hold the aforementioned Mute icon. Tap Mute again to take the person off hold. You might put a caller on hold to answer another incoming call or to make a second call yourself. The next section shows you how to deal with more than one call at a time.
