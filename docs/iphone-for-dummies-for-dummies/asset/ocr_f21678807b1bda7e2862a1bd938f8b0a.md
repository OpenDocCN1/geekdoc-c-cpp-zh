# Making memories

Hidden in almost everyone's photo collection are the crown jewels, those images that trigger the most precious memories. As of the Photos app in iOS 10, Apple added a Memories feature that can help trigger such memories automatically. Tap the Memories tab to take a photographic trip down memory lane.

How does it work? The Photos app smartly scans your picture library and collects images around vacations, birthday parties, or what the app deems are the best pictures you took during the year or a shorter time frame.
