Right. Here’s how to address situations when Do Not Disturb is enabled. From the Home screen, tap Settings > Do Not Disturb. Tap the Calls From option (Figure 4-6) and choose whether to allow calls from your Favorites, Everyone (kind of defeats the Do Not Disturb, eh?), No One, All Contacts, or a particular Group Name from Contacts.

You can also permit calls from individual contacts. Tap to open the contact listing for the person and tap Ringtone (of which we'll have more to say to) or Text Tone, and tap the Emergency Bypass switch so that it is enabled (green is showing). Now you'll hear sounds and detect vibrations from the person when Do Not Disturb is.

You can also take advantage of the Repeated Calls option. When Repeated Calls is turned on, a second call from the same person three minutes will not be silenced. The rationale is if someone is trying to reach you badly, which can be important.

You can also schedule when Do Not Disturb is on. Tap Settings Do Not Disturb > Scheduled, and flip the switch on. Then tap the From To time field and choose a quiet time.

From the Do Not Disturb settings, you can also silence the iPhone when you receive incoming calls and notifications, or choose to do so only when the phone is locked.
