+   >> Allow Notifications: This setting is the master/off switch for Messages notifications in Notification Center. Turn it on if you want to see them; turn it off if you don't.

+   >> Show in Notification Center: Decide whether you want to set 0, 1, 5, or 10 recent items in Notification Center.

Swipe down from the top of screen to see Notification Center. Note, too, that on-screen alerts are not affected by turning this setting on or off. Rather, the Notification Center on/off switch determines whether or not notifications from the Messages app appear in Notification Center. The upshot is that if you choose banners or the Alert Style, you'll see a banner or an alert on the screen when a new message arrives, but you won't see it in Notification Center.

+   >> Sounds: Tap this setting to change the sound you hear when a new message arrives. (Or set to None).

+   >> Badge Icon: This on/off determines whether you see a badge with the number of unread messages on the Messages icon.

+   >> Show on Lock Screen: Enable this setting to see new alerts when your iPhone is locked (refer to Figure 6-2, left).

+   >> Alert Style: Tap this setting to choose the appearance of on-screen notifications when your iPhone is unlocked.

Both banners and the Alert option display the notification at the top of the screen, as shown in Figure 6-3. If you select Banners, after a few seconds the notification disappears with a slick animation. however, the notification remains on the screen until you dismiss it by flicking it up or reply to it by swiping the little gray bar at the bottom of a banner downward toward the bottom of the screen. The keyboard appears and you can type your response immediately, without opening the Messages app.

Finally, the None option means neither a banner nor an alert appears on the screen when a new message arrives.
