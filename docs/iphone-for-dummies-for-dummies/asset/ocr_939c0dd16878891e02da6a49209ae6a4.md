To use the App Store on your iPhone, it must be connected to the Internet. And, if you obtain an app using iTunes on your computer, it won't be available on your iPhone until you sync the iPhone with your computer — unless you've turned on automatic downloads, as described.

But before you use the App Store on your iPhone or computer, you have to have an iTunes Store account. If you don't already have one, we suggest that you launch iTunes on your computer, click Sign in the upper-right corner of the iTunes window, click Create Apple ID, and then follow the on-screen instructions. If you prefer to create your account on your iPhone rather than your computer, follow the instructions near the end of Chapter 8.

Let's put it this: If you don't have an iTunes Store account, you can't download a single cool app for your iPhone. Enough said.

# Using Your Computer to Find Apps

Okay, start by finding cool iPhone apps using iTunes on your computer. Follow these steps:

+   1. Launch iTunes on your Mac or PC.

+   2. Choose Apps in the Categories drop-down menu in the upper-left corner of the iTunes window.

+   3. If you don't see Apps in the menu, choose Edit Menu and enable the check box for Apps.

+   4. Click the App Store tab near the top-center of the iTunes window.

The iTunes Store appears.

+   5. Click the iPhone tab (not the iPad tab) below the App Store tab.
