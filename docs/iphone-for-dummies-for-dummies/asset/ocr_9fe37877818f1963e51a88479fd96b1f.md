# Saving web pictures

You can capture most pictures you come across a website — but be mindful of any potential copyright violations, depending on what you plan to do with the image. To copy an image from a website, hold your finger against the image and tap the Save Image button that slides up, as shown in Figure 11-9. Saved images end up in your Recently Added album in the Photos app, from which they can be synced back to a computer. If you tap copy instead, you can paste the image into an email or as a link in a program such as Notes. On 3D Touch-capable iPhones you'll have to apply the right amount of pressure when you hold your finger across an image. It may take practice. We have on Touch ID a later in this chapter.
