The big deal is that while automatic backups protect most of the files on your iPhone, you can't manipulate them. They're backed up and restored, but heaven help you if you want to extract one or more iMessages, SMS, and MMS messages, songs, videos, notes, or other types of data from your iPhone. The bottom line is that there's no easier way to manage files on your iPhone than using SearchView.

In nutshell, here's how SearchView works. After downloading the software to your Mac, double-click the program's icon to start it. Then do one of the following:

+   To transfer files and folders to the iPhone (assuming you have room on the device), click the Copy to iPhone button on the toolbar and select the files you want to copy. The files are copied into the appropriate folder on the iPhone. Alternatively, you can drag files and folders from the Mac Desktop or a folder into the SearchView browser.
