# # Syncing Your Data

Now that your data is backed up and you've completed the prep work, you're
ready to sync. The next order of business is to tell iTunes what data you want to
synchronize between your iPhone and your computer. You start this process by
clicking Info in the sidebar on the left side of the window.

If you're using iTunes 11 or 12.0, do yourself a favor and upgrade to iTunes 12.5 or
hier. To see which version of iTunes you have, choose iTunes  About iTunes.
