Chances are you haven't heard of Wolfram | Alpha. But if you want to know the gross domestic product of France or find events that happened on the day you were born, Wolfram | Alpha can deliver such facts. You don't search the web per on Wolfram | Alpha as you would using a service such as Google, Wolfram | Alpha describes itself as a “new way to get knowledge ... by doing dynamic computations based on a vast collection of built-in data, algorithms, and methods.” It taps into knowledge curated by human “experts.” So you can get nutritional information for peanut M&Ms or com-pute growth for your 4-foot, 7-inch, 10-year-old daughter.

There's a reason Siri relies on this “computational knowledge engine,” which was driven over a period of nearly 30 years by a really smart guy: Stephen Wolfram. We also recommend checking out the $2.99 Wolfram | Alpha app for your iPhone.

# Making Siri smarter

You can tell Siri in language which you want to convert by going to Settings General > Siri. Siri is available in English (United States, United Kingdom, New Zealand, India, Ireland, Singapore, South Africa, or Australia), Arabic, Danish, Dutch, Finnish, French, German, Chinese, Italian, Hebrew, Korean, Malay, Norwegian, Portuguese, Russian, Spanish, Swedish, and Turkish.

You can also receive feedback from Siri all the time or when you control by a ring switch.

In the Info field in Settings, you can tell Siri who you are. When you type My Info, your Contacts list appears. Tap your own name in Contacts.

And as we mean earlier, you can call Siri's voice from male to female or versa. You can also give an English-speaking Siri an accent: Australian or British.

As noted, you can call Siri even from the Lock Screen. The default setting away. Consider this feature a mighty blessing. Not having to type a passcode to activate Siri is convenient. On the other hand, if your phone ends up in the wrong person, she would be able to use Siri to make a call, send an email, or send a message in your, bypassing passcode security you thought was place. If you find this potential scary scenario, tap Settings Touch ID & Passcode. Enter your passcode — a fingerprint won't cut it — and under Access When Locked, change the Siri option to off (white rather than green is showing). For more on Settings, read Chapter 14.
