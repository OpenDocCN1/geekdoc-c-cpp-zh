# Finger-typing on the virtual keyboards

Apple's multitouch interface just might be considered a stroke of genius. And it just might as easily you drive it, at least initially.

If you're patient and trusting, you'll get the hang of finger-typing in a week or so. You have to use the virtual keyboard that appears when you tap a text field to enter notes, text messages, the names of new contacts, and so forth.

![](bbox=[0.168, 0.663, 0.228, 0.737])

Apple's recommendation on the early iPhones — with which we concur is to start typing with your index finger before graduating to two thumbs. We think the advice is sound on newer models too.

As we've noted, Apple has built a lot of intelligence into its virtual keyboard, so it can correct typing mistakes on the fly and take a stab at predicting what're about to type. The keyboard isn't exactly Nostradamus, but it does a pretty good job in coming up with the words you have in mind.
