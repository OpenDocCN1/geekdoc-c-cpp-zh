Know exactly what you're looking for? Instead of simply browsing, you can tap the Search icon and type a word or phrase. Or use the Categories icon, which works a little differently because it has no apps. Instead, it offers a list of categories as Books, Business, Catalogs, and Education, to name a few. Tap a category to see either a page full of apps of that type or a list of subcategories for that type. For example, the Games category offers subcategories as Action, Arcade, Kids, Music, and Puzzle. Other categories have no subcategories — you'll go directly to the page full of apps when you tap them.

If you're wondering about Updates, the fifth icon at the bottom of the screen, we discuss it a little later in this chapter in the imaginatively named “Updating an app from your iPhone” section.
