# Browsing the iPhone App Store screen

Most retail stores, the iPhone App Store rearranges things in windows (ha!) all the time. Names of departments and the layout can change from week to week. For the most part, the main departments are down from the middle of the screen (interspersed with some ads), with Quick Links to ancillary departments and the Top Paid, Free, and Grossing apps appearing to the right of the main departments.

We start with the middle:

> The New Apps We Love department has eight and a half visible icons (from Alto to Spark Pro in Figure 15-1), representing that the store's curators feel are well, new apps store's curators love.
