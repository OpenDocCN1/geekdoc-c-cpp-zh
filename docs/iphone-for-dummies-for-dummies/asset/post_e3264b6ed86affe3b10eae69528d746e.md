Weather is a simple app that provides you with the current forecast for the city or cities. By default, you see the daily forecast at the bottom of the screen, as shown in Figure 13-13.

To add a city, first tap the info icon in the bottom-right corner. Next, tap the + icon in the lower-right corner; type a city, a zip code, or an airport location; and then tap the Search button in the bottom-right corner. Finally, tap the name of the found city. Add as many cities as you want.

If you've added one or more cities, you can switch between them by flicking your finger across the screen to the left or right. delete a city, tap the info icon. Tap the icon to the left of the city's name, and then tap the Delete button that appears to the right of its name.
