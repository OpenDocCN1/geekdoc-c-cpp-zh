# # Keeping in Good Health

Apple's Health app, in conjunction with a tool designed for developers called HealthKit, provides a repository for your health data, with places to add blood sugar levels and other lab results, medications, nutritional intake, calories and burned.

The app, as shown in Figure 7-17, covers all aspects of your health, including sleep and mindfulness. Inside, you'll be able to show such parameters as the number of steps you've taken, and how far you've run or walked.

Indeed, you can collect data from third-party health and fitness apps or devices; third parties can request permission to supply data to the Health app. The iPhone supplies its own data. A barometer in the iPhone models can factor in elevation to figure out how many stairs you've climbed. Selected data can be viewed in the Health app dashboard.

You can sign up throughout the day to donate organs to help save lives. And the Medical ID inside the Health app may help save yours own.
