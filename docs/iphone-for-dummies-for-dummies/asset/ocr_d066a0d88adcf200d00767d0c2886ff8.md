You can delete email messages without opening them in a few ways:

+   Swipe left halfway across the message, and then tap the Red Trash button that appears to the right of the message, adjacent to the yellow Flag and gray More buttons. If you swipe all the way to the left by keeping your finger pressed against the screen, you'll trash the message immediately. Be careful if that was not your intention. Or, if you swipe in the other direction (to the right), you can mark a message as unread. As well in some email accounts such as Google's Gmail, an Archive button may appear inside Trash, depending on whether you turned on Archive Mailboxes in Settings. In this case, tap the Archive button to archive the message; it is not deleted. We get to that later in the chapter.

+   Tap the Edit button (in the upper-right corner of the Inboxes or a mail sender selection), and then tap the circle to the left of each message you want to remove, as shown in Figure 12-10. Tapping that circle puts a check in it and changes the Trash All button at the bottom of the screen to a Trash button. Tap that Trash button to erase all messages you selected.

+   Trash the message (or mark it as read) inside an interactive notification that appears regardless of the screen you are viewing. The advantage of this method is you don't have to leave the screen to delete the message. Just tap the Trash button in the notification.

+   When you've opened a message to read it, drag the entire edge of the screen toward the right. Upon doing so, you can sneak a peek at the messages in your inbox, as revealed in Figure 12-11. You haven't trashed the message you just dragged to the right — it remains in the inbox and subfolder where it was — but you did dismiss it for the moment.
