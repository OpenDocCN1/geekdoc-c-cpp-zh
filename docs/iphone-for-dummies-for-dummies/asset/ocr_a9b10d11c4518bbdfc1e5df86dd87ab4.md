# Chapter 13
Tracking with Maps, Compass, Stocks, and Weather

In this chapter, we look at four of the iPhone's Internet-enabled apps: Maps, Compass, Stocks, and Weather. We call them Internet-enabled because they display information collected over your Internet connection — whether Wi-Fi or cellular data network — in real time (or in the case of Stocks, near-real time).
