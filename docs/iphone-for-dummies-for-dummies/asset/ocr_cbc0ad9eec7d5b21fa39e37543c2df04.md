+   **Wi-Fi Calling:** Tap the switch here to make and receive calls over Wi-Fi. Consult your carrier to make sure this option is available.

+   **Allow calls on other devices:** Through what Apple refers to Continuity (of which the Handoff setting you met earlier in this chapter is a part of), you can share your iPhone's cellular connection to make and receive calls with other Apple devices on the same Wi-Fi network. Select your Macs, other phones, and iPads here.

+   **Respond with text:** If you're tired and can't take a call but want the wondering whether you're avoiding him or her, you can respond with canned responses as "on my way" and "Can I call you later?”

+   **Call forwarding:** If you expect to spend time in an area with poor or no coverage, you may want to temporarily forward calls to a landline or other portable handset. Simply do the following:

+   1. On the Settings screen, tap *Call Forwarding*.

+   2. Tap the switch to turn on *Call Forwarding*.

+   3. Use the virtual keypad to enter the number where you want incoming calls to ring.

+   4. Tap Phone to return to the main Phone settings screen.

To change the forwarding number, tap the circle with the X in the Phone Number to get rid of the old number, and then enter a new one.

Remember to turn off Call Forwarding to receive calls directly on your iPhone again. You must have cellular coverage while setting the Call Forwarding feature.

+   **Call waiting:** Tap the Call Waiting button to turn on the feature or off. If Call Waiting is off and you're speaking on the phone, the call is automatically dispatched to voicemail.

+   **Show My Caller ID:** Don't want your name or number displayed on the phone when you're calling? Make sure to tap My Caller ID and turn off the setting. If privacy isn't a concern, you can leave this on.

+   **Blocked:** By adding people from your contacts to the Blocked list, you'll longer receive phone calls, messages, or FaceTime from them.

+   **TTY:** Folks who are hearing impaired sometimes rely on a teletype, or TTY, to hold conversations. You can use the iPhone with standard TTY devices by plugging in a cable from the TTY device into an optional $19 iPhone
