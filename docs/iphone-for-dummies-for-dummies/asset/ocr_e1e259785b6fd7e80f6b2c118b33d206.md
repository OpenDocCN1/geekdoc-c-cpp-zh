# Taking Control of Your Tunes

Now that you have the basics down, take a look at some other things you can do with the Music app, starting with the controls you see when a song is playing.

![](bbox=[0.163, 0.178, 0.233, 0.241])

If you don't see the controls when a song is playing, tap the song name near the bottom of the screen.

Now, here's how to use those controls:

+   **Back icon**: Tap to return to whichever list you used last — Playlists, Artists, Songs, and so on.

+   **Scrubber bar**: Drag the little gray dot (the playhead) along the scrubber bar to skip to any point within the song. Note that the playhead is roughly one-third of the way into it in Figure 8-4, which means the person has heard roughly one-third of the song so far.

+   **Time elapsed and remaining**: Just below the scrubber bar on the left is the amount of time this song has already played (0:43 in Figure 8-4) and how much of this song remains to be played (1:54 in the figure).

+   **Restart/previous track/rewind**: Tap once to go to the beginning of the track. Tap twice to go to the start of the preceding track in the list. Touch and hold down to rewind the song at double speed.

+   **Play/pause icon**: Tap to play or pause the song.

+   **Next track/forward icon**: Tap to skip to the next track in the list. Touch and hold down it to fast-forward through the song at double speed.

+   **Volume control**: Drag the little dot left or right to reduce or increase, respectively, the volume level.

+   **AirPlay selector**: You may not see the AirPlay selector icon (shown in the margin) in Center on your iPhone (visible in Figure 8-4). AirPlay lets you stream content wirelessly over Wi-Fi from your iPhone to AirPlay-enabled device. Check out the “Playing with AirPlay” sidebar for details.

+   **Share button**: Tap to share this song with others via AirDrop, Message, Mail, Twitter, Facebook, or any of the other options in your iOS 10 Share sheet.

+   **Shuffle function**: Tap once to shuffle songs and play them in random order. The button turns dark gray when shuffling is enabled. Tap again to play songs in order again. The button goes back to its original color — light gray.

You can also shuffle tracks in any list of songs — such as playlists or albums — by tapping Shuffle, which appears at the top of the list.
