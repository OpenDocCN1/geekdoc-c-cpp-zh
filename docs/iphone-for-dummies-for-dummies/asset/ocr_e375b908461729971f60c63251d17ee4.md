# iCloud

The iCloud settings are where you let iCloud know of your apps, Pl Photo Stream, ought to be turned on or off. You can also check on your storage and iCloud Backup options, and if goes worse to worst, delete your iCloud account.

In iCloud settings, you can turn on the iCloud Drive feature as a place to upload and store a gaggle of documents, spreadsheets, pictures, and more with all the items coming from Apple's built-in apps or third-party apps. You can access and work on any of these Drive-based files from your iPhone, iPad, iPod touch, Mac, or PC. Make changes from one device and these alterations are reflected on all the devices you use to visit iCloud Drive.

> Consider carefully before deleting your iCloud account. If you do to delete your account, all your Photo Stream photos and documents stored in iCloud be removed from your phone.

# Promoting harmony through / Family Sharing

While in iCloud settings, consider setting up Family Sharing. Up to six members of your family — adults and kids — can partake. One-up must take the role of family organizer, and we figure it might well be you, the person reading this book. This means you'll be the person presenting your iCloud Apple ID username and password, and the one on the hook to pay for iTunes, iBooks, and App Store purchases. As the family organizer, you can turn on Ask Buy so that you can approve (or deny) any purchase or download requests from other members of your clan.
