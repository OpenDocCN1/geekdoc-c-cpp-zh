TripCase includes an action view with flight alerts, reminders, and other messages. Any way you look at it, TripCase does most of the work for you. For example, if you need to tell a hotel or an airline, just tap the convenient icon to make the call.

TripCase can help you locate an alternate flight based on your original reservation should it be cancelled or delayed. And it reminds you in card and printing booking 24 hours before each flight.

TripCase's motto is “stress-free travel,” and while it may not make travel stress free, it definitely makes it stressful.
