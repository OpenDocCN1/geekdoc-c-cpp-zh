# Summoning Siri

When you first set up the iPhone, you have the option of turning on Siri during which you can train Siri to your voice by repeating "Hey Siri" and a few other phrases. If you did set up Siri, you're good to go. If you didn't, tap Settings General and flip the switch to on, meaning greener than white is shining. (If you turn off Siri, you effectively turn on Voice Control.)

Having Siri is a prerequisite for using the Dictation functionality on your phone, discussed later in this chapter. Siri assumes a vital role in search.
