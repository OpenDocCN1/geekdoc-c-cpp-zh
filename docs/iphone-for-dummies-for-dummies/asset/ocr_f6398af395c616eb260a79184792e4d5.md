# # Sharing your photos

Apple in its infinite world recognizes that you might want to share your best images with friends and family and have those pictures automatically appear on those's devices.

An impressive and aptly named solution called Shared Photo Streams arrived on the iPad, iPod, and iPhone back in iOS 6 (and a bit earlier on Macs running OS Lion). It was modified in iOS 7 and now referred to as iCloud Photo Sharing. The feature enables you to share pictures and videos with other folks and lets you receive photo streams that they make available to you. Here's how:

+   1. On the Home screen, tap Settings.
2. Tap Photos & Camera.
3. If the iCloud Photo Sharing isn't on, tap it to turn it on.
4. Open the Photos app, and then tap the Shared tab.
5. Tap the + at the upper-left corner of the screen, and then type a name for your stream in the iCloud dialog that appears.
6. Tap Next and choose who will receive your shared photos, as shown in Figure 9-11.
7. Above the To field, tap Create.

The name is yours, but we recommend something descriptive, like the lines of My Trip to Tahiti (and you should be so lucky).

You can type a phone number, a text address, or an email address, or choose one of your contacts by tapping the + in a circle in the To field.
