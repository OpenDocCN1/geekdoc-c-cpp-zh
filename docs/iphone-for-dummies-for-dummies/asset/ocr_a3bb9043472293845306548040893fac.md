When a player makes a correct guess he or flips the phone in a downward motion to get a new card, continuing the process until the time is up. A player can also flip the phone upward to pass on a card.

Figure 18-14 shows some of the available card deck categories, a bunch of which are included to get you started.

Additional card decks, on topics ranging from Baseball to Oldies but Goodies are also available for 99 cents each. And one add-on-deck lets you design your own cards.

You can capture instant replays, by enabling the phone’s camera and microphone during gameplay. Ellen encourages you to share the funniest videos.
