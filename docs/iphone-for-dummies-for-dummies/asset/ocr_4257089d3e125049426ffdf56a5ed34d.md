REMEMBER If you have just a single mail account configured on your iPhone, you'll see only that one in the Mailboxes screen.

When you tap a mailbox to open it, Mail fetches the most recent messages. Tap a message to read it. When a message is on the screen, icons for managing messages appear below it. These controls are addressed in the next section.

Meanwhile, swipe down to refresh a mailbox. You will momentarily see a spinning gear until the mail is satisfied that all the emails that can be delivered have been delivered. (Obviously, you'll need an Internet connection to receive new mail.)

On the iPhone 6 Plus, 6 Plus, and 7 Plus in landscape mode, you get a two-panel view that takes advantage of spacious 5.5-inch screens on devices. In the left pane, you see the header and first line from messages in your inbox. In the right pane, you see the contents of the highlighted mail.
