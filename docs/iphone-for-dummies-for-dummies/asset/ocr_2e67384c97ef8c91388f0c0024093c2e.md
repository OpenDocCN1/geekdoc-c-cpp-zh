To add bubble or screen effects: Prepare your message as usual but rather tapping the little up-arrow-in-a-blue-circle to send it, press and hold on it for several seconds and the Bubble and Screen effects appear. The Bubble tab at the top to select Slam, Loud, Gentle, or Invisible Ink as the bubble effect for your message. Or tap the Screen tab and swipe left to select Balloons, Confetti, Lasers, Fireworks, or Shooting Star as the screen effect for this message.

If you change your mind and don't want to add an effect, tap the x-in-a-circle to dismiss the Bubble and Screen effects.

To send a handwritten message: Sometimes nothing but a handwritten note will do. To send, first rotate your iPhone 90 degrees so the long edge is parallel with the ground (landscape mode). If the handwriting interface shown in Figure 6-7 doesn't appear when you rotate the iPhone, tap the handwriting icon in the lower-right corner of the keyboard (and shown in the margin).
