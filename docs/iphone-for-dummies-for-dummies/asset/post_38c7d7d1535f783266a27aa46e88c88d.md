In our experience, iPhones are usually quite reliable; most users we've talked to report mostly trouble-free operation. Notice our use of the words most and mostly. What kinds of bad things are we talking about? Well, we're referring to problems involving

- >> The phone itself
- >> Making or receiving calls
- >> Wireless networks
- >> Syncing with your Mac or PC

After all the troubleshooting, we tell you how to get even more help if nothing we suggest does the trick. Finally, if your iPhone is so badly hoped that it needs to go back to the motherhood for repairs, we offer ways to survive the experience with a minimum of stress or fuss.

## iPhone Issues

Our first category of troubleshooting techniques applies to an iPhone that's frozen or otherwise acting up. The recommended procedure when this happens is to perform the seven Rs in sequence:

- Recharge your iPhone.
- Restart your iPhone.
- Reset your iPhone.
- Remove content from your iPhone.
- Reset your iPhone's settings and content.
- Restore your iPhone.
- Renew your iPhone in Recovery mode.

If the first technique doesn't do the trick, go on to the second. If the second doesn't work, try the third. And so on.

## But first...

But before you even start those procedures, Apple recommends you take these steps:

1. Verify that you have the current version of iTunes installed on your Mac or PC.

2. Verify that you're connecting your iPhone to your computer using a USB 2.0 or 3.0 port.

## 3. Make sure your iPhone software is up-to-date.

- To check your iPhone software from iTunes on your Mac or PC: Connect your iPhone to your computer, launch iTunes (if necessary), and click the iPhone icon to the right of the drop-down menus near the top-left of the iTunes window. Click the Summary tab, and then click the Check for Update button.
- To check your iPhone software from your iPhone: On the Home screen, tap Settings ⚙️: General ⚙️ Software Update.

If your iPhone requires an update, you receive instructions for do so. Otherwise, please continue.

If those three easy steps don't get you back up and running and your iPhone is still acting up — it freezes, doesn't wake up from sleep, doesn't do something it is used to, or in any other way acts improperly — don't panic. The following sections describe the things you should try, in the order that we (and Apple) recommend.

## Recharge your iPhone

If your iPhone acts in any way, shape, or form, the first thing you should try is to give its battery a full recharge.

> Don't plug the iPhone's Lightning connector cable into a USB port on a keyboard, a monitor, or an unpowered USB hub. Need to plug the cable into one of the USB ports on the computer itself or a powered hub (one that requires an AC source) because the USB ports on your computer supply more power than ports on keyboards, monitors, or unpowered hubs.

> Most USB hubs, the kind you plug into an AC outlet, will charge your iPhone just fine. But powered, or unpowered, hubs — ones that don't plug into the wall for power — won't cut it when it comes to charging your phone.

Note that you use the included USB power adapter to recharge your iPhone from an AC outlet rather than a computer. So if your iPhone isn't charging when you connect it to your computer, try it from a wall outlet instead. In addition, power strips by Belkin and other vendors include one or more USB ports that will work for charging.

If you're in a hurry, charge your iPhone for a minimum of 20 minutes. We think a full charge is a better idea, but a 20–minute charge is better than no at all. For faster charging in any circumstances, turn your iPhone off while it charges.

## Restart your iPhone

If you recharge your iPhone and it still misbehaves, the next thing to try is restart-it. Just as restarting a computer often fixes problems, restarting your iPhone sometimes works wonders.

Here's how to restart:

1. Press and hold down the sleep/wake button
2. Slide the red slider to turn off the iPhone, and then wait a few seconds.
3. Press and hold down the sleep/wake button until the Apple logo appears on the screen.

If your iPhone is still frozen, misbehaves, or doesn't start up, it's time to try the third R, resetting your iPhone.

## Reset your iPhone

To reset any iPhone a 7 or 7 Plus, merely press and hold down the sleep/wake button while pressing and holding the Home button on the front at the same time. After about ten seconds, the screen will go black; when the Apple logo reappears, you can release buttons.

> Because iPhone 7 and 7 Plus have solid-state Home buttons, which may not respond if your iPhone isn't running properly, the reset procedure is slightly different. Instead of pressing and holding down sleep/wake and Home, press hold down the sleep/wake and volume buttons.
