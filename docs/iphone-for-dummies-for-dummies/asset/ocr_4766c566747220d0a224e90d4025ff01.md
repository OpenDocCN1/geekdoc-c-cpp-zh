> We can’t think of any easier way to add a calendar entry than by using Siri. You can instruct Siri along the lines of “Set a lunch appointment for tomorrow at noon with the Smiths.” Siri is pleased to comply.

Meanwhile, if you have the 6s or 7 models, you have another easy way to add an entry: Use 3D Tool. Just press against the Calendar icon and, from the Quick Actions menu, tap Add Event.

Choose a default calendar by tapping Settings > Calendars, and then flipping the screen until the Calendar section appears. Tap Default Calendar and select the calendar that you want to show regularly.

If you travel long distances for your job, you can make events according to whichever time zone selected for your calendars. In the Calendar settings, tap Time Zone Override to turn it on, and tap Time Zone. The type zone location using the keyboard appears. When Time Zone Override is turned off, events are displayed according to the time zone of your current location.

Tap Alternate Calendars in Settings if you want to display a Chinese, a Hebrew, or an Islamic calendar alongside your English calendar.

> To turn off an audible calendar alert, tap Settings > Sounds, and then make sure the selected alert tone is set to None. (You see only the last calendar alert you selected. To see your other choices, tap Calendar Alerts.) You can also default alert times for birthdays, all-day events, when it’s time to leave, and certain other events. Tap Settings > Calendar, and scroll down to Default Times.

Back in the Calendar app, if you want to modify an existing calendar entry, tap the entry, tap Edit, and make whichever changes need to be made. To wipe out a calendar entry, tap Edit > Delete Event. Then tap Delete Event (again).

Calendar entries you create on your iPhone are synchronized with the calendar you specified in the ITunes pane.

> Current and future calendar events can be synchronized automatically through Apple’s iCloud service. In Settings, you can also sync events. Your options are to sync events 6 months back, 3 months back, 1 month back, or 2 weeks back. Or you can sync all your events.

You can use iCloud to keep your calendars updated and in sync across all your iOS devices and computers. Tap Settings > iCloud and ensure the Calendar–option is turned on.
