# Special Mail options on 3D Touch models

The Mail app is among those to exploit the 3D Touch feature added exclusively (as of this writing) to the 6s, 6s Plus, and 7 models.

For starters, there are quick actions. Press your finger with a little bit of force against the Mail icon on any Home screen to summon a Quick Actions menu. As you can see in Figure 12-23, doing so you quickly access your inbox or your VIP messages, search for other messages, or begin composing a new message. You can also display a widget with circles that represent your VIP contacts.
