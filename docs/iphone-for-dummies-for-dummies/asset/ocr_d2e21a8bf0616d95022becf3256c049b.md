+   **Show Preview:** This setting determines whether or not you see the first line of the message and a picture from the message in banners or alerts.
**Repeat Alerts:** Tap this to select the number of times an alert is repeated. Your choices are Never, Once, Twice, 3 Times, 5 Times, 10 Times, and at-two minute intervals.

Notifications on the Lock screen look like the one in Figure 6-2, left, regardless of whether you've selected banners or alerts. The notifications remain on the Lock screen when you swipe the Slide to Unlock at the bottom of the screen or swipe a notification icon to reply. If you've enabled your iPhone's passcode feature (which we recommend highly), you will need to enter your passcode after you swipe.
