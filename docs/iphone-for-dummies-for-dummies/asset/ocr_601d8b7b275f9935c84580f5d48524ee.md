Meanwhile, if you press and hold the period key on the Safari or Mail keyboard, you get your choice of .com, .net, .edu, .org, and .us. Pressing and holding down the period key in certain situations, such as in Messages, Notes, or Pages, gives you a choice of a period or an ellipsis.

If you enabled any international keyboards, you'll choose when you hold down the period key. For example, if you enabled a French keyboard, pressing and holding down the period will also give you options for .eu and .fr.
