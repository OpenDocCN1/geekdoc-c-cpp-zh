# iTeleport

At $24.99, iTeleport isn't cheap and we admit that it is more than a little geeky, but it's so cool and potentially useful that we would have been remiss had not included it.

iTeleport is technically a VNC (Virtual Network Computing, also known as remote screen control) client. Put another way, it's an iPhone app for controlling your Mac, Windows, or Linux computer “from a few feet away or halfway around the world.”

Yes, you can actually see your screen and control its keyboard and mouse from anywhere (as long as your iPhone connect to the Internet through Wi-Fi, 3G, 4G, EDGE, or whatever).

Figure 18-9 shows an iPhone running iTeleport, which is controlling a Mac in another room (the Mac could just as easily in a different city, state, or country). iTeleport is an iPhone app, so you pinch and unpinch to zoom in and out. In Figure 18-9, we zoomed in the upper-left corner of the Mac screen, where a character is being edited in Microsoft Word.
