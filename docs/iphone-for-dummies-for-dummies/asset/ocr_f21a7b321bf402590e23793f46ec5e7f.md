What’s more, thanks to interactive notifications you can accept or reject incoming calendar invitations without ever entering the Calendar app. When you enter the app, you can display five main calendar views: by year, by month, by day, by week, and by a searchable list view that shows current and future appointments.

You must hold the phone in portrait mode to check the calendar in yearly, monthly, or daily view. In these portrait views, the current date on your calendar is circled in red. A Today button at the bottom of the screen returns you to the current date. Next to the Today button are Calendars button and an Inbox button, which we get to shortly.

Get to list view, orient the phone in portrait mode and tap a particular day. You will see either the daily view or the list view, depending on how you last viewed the calendar. To toggle from one to the other, tap the icon at the top of the screen shown in the margin. To peek at a weekly calendar instead, rotate the iPhone to landscape mode.
