If you have an iPhone with 3D Touch, you can save a few steps by pressing the Music icon and choosing Play Beats 1 from the Quick Actions list that appears.

Beats 1 isn't your cup of tea, scroll down the page for additional radio stations.

Did mention that some radio stations including Beats 1, are available on your iPhone, iPad, iPod touch, Mac, and Apple TV for free? You'll hear the occasional ad, or you can listen without ads if you subscribe to iTunes Match. Finally, if you subscribe to Apple Music, you receive dozens of additional radio stations not available to non-subscribers.

So that's how you find a station and start listening to it.

When a station is playing, the name of the current song appears near the bottom of the screen; tap it for additional options, as shown in Figure 8–1.
