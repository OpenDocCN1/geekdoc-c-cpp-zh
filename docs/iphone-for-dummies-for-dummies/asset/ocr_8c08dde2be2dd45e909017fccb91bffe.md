# Chapter 10
Starring in a Blockbuster:
Video on Your iPhone

Picture this scene: The smell of popcorn permeates the room as you and your family congregate to watch the latest Hollywood blockbuster. A motion picture soundtrack swells up. The images on the screen are stunning. All eyes are fixed on the iPhone.

Okay, here's the reality check. The iPhone is not going to replace a wall-sized high-definition television as the centerpiece of your home theater, even on the 4.7-inch screen of the 6, or 7, or on the comparatively massive 5.5-inch screen of the 6 Plus or 7 Plus. But we want to emphasize that on glorious widescreen display—and even on the smallest model in the current lineup—watching movies and other videos on the iPhone can be a cinematic delight. And if you're bordering on the larger screen, you can always use your iPhone to pump video to an Apple TV via AirPlay.

As you discover later in this chapter, you can shoot your own blockbuster footage and shoot in stunning 4K on the iPhone 6s, 6 Plus, SE, 7, and 7 Plus. As a measure of sharpness, 4K boasts more than 8 million pixels and four times the resolution of what was the top-of-line 1080p high-definition video standard. If you have an older device and are shooting in 1080p, don't fret; the quality is still excellent. Let's get with the show!
