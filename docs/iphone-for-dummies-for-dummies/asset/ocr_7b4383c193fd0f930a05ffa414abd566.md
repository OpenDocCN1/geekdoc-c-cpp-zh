To turn threading, go to the Home screen and tap Settings > Mail > Organize Thread. Finally, tap the switch so that it is visible, as shown in Figure 12-13. You may have to scroll down to see the Organize Thread setting. While in Settings, you can choose to place the most recent message in a thread on top by enabling that. Still another threading option lets you display all the messages that make up a conversation, even if some have been moved to other mailboxes. If that is your preference, tap the Complete Threads switch.

When you look at a message that's part of a thread, the numbers at the top of the screen tell you the message's location in the conversation. For example, in Figure 12-14, the message is 6 of 8 in this thread.

# Searching emails

You can easily search through a bunch of messages to find the one you want to read away—such as that can't miss stock tip from your broker. Tap status bar to scroll through the top of the inbox or manually scroll to search. You can type or whichever search term seems relevant. Matching emails that have already been downloaded surface. Scroll to the top of the screen to see your current mailbox. If you have multiple accounts on your phone, you'll also see an option at the top to search all your mailboxes.

What's more, Apple groups search results by people, subjects, unread, flagged messages, messages with VIPs, and attachments

In the stock example, if you have an email to from a person named Stockman, he or she will show up in the People search results. And if you have a subject line that reads "Your stock portfolio performance," it will appear in the Subjects search results.
