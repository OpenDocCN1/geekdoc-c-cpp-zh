# Voicing Memos

Consider all the times you'd find it useful to have a voice recorder in your pocket — perhaps when you're attending a lecture or interviewing an important source
(that's a big for us journalist types). Or maybe you just want to leave yourself
a quickie reminder about something (“Pick up milk after work”). Well, you're in
luck. Apple has a built-in digital voice recorder.

A bunch of third-party apps add voice recording to the iPhone. Ed sometimes uses
Recorder from Retronyms and Dictation from Nuance, and Bob uses iTalk
Recorder from Griffin.
