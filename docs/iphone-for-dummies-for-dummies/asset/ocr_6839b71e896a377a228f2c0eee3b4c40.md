Want stations to play more songs you'll like and fewer songs you dislike? When a song you love or hate is playing, tap the ellipses (...) and then tap the heart (love) or broken heart (dislike) icon. The more you do this, the better the suggestions in the For tab will be.

And that, friends, is pretty much all you need to know to use and enjoy stations in the Music app.

# # My Music Is Your Music

Moving right along, let's look at how to listen to music you already know, which is to say the music in your iTunes library. If you haven't already launched the Music app, do so now, and then tap Library at the bottom of the screen.

# ## A library without library cards

Music in the Library tab becomes available by syncing with iTunes (as described in Chapter 3), subscribing to iTunes Match, subscribing to Apple Music, or any combination of the three.

> Note that a music file doesn't have to be stored on your iPhone to be available on the Library tab. However, you must have Internet access to play songs that aren't on your phone. If you want to ensure that you'll be able to listen to a song, an album, or a playlist anytime you like, even without an Internet connection, you will have to tap the ellipses (...) and then tap the Download button (shown in the margin).

In your you'll find the words Playlists, Artists, Albums, Songs, and Home Sharing in red. These are filters for how your music is displayed. Tap Playlists to see a list of playlists (about playlists shortly); or Artists, Albums, or Songs to see the music in your library arranged by artist, album, or song, respectively. Tap Edit to add Genres, Composers, and more to the list.

Finally, at the bottom of the screen is a section of recently added songs and albums; to see more, scroll downward.
