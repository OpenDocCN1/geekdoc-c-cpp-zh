# Private Browsing

Don't leave any tracks while you surf? Don't worry, we won't and we won't tell. Turn on private browsing for a “what happens in Safari stays in Safari” tool. Truly bent on staying private will also want to tap Clear History and Website Data, as we mention earlier in this chapter.

To go incognito, tap Pages so you see a tabbed view of open pages, and tap the Private button, in the bottom-left corner. You're given the option to close your existing tabs before turning on private browsing.
