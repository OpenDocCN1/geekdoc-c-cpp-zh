You might not let the kids remove old apps, install new apps, or make purchases in the apps you do. You can try to avoid exposing them to explicit language. When restrictions are in place, icons for off-limit functions can no longer be seen (including the Camera icon in Control Center).

# Date & Time

In our neck of the woods, the time is reported as 11:32 PM (or whatever time happens to be). But in some circles, it's reported as 23:32. If you prefer the format on the iPhone's status bar, the 24-Hour Time setting (under Date & Time) to turn on the setting.

This setting is just one that you can adjust Date & Time. You can also have the iPhone set the time automatically, using the time reported by the cellular network (adjusted for your time zone).

If you've turned off the option to set the time automatically, you're asked to select the time zone and then set the date and time manually. Here's:

- 1. Tap Set Automatically so that it's off.

You see fields for setting the time zone and the date and time.

- 2. Tap the Time Zone.

The current time zone and virtual keyboard are shown.

- 3. Tap out the letters of the city or country time zone you want to enter until the one you have in mind appears. Then tap the name of the city or country.

The Time Zone field is automatically filled in for that city.

- 4. Tap the Set Date & Time field so that the time is shown. Roll the carousel controls until the proper time is displayed.

- 5. Tap the date shown so that the carousel controls pop up for the date. Then roll the wheels for the month, day, and year until the correct date appears.

- 6. Tap General to return to the General settings.

# Keyboard

Under Keyboard settings, you can turn on or off auto-capitalization and turn or Enable Caps Lock. Autocapitalization, which the iPhone turns by default, means that the first letter of the first word you type after ending the preceding sentence with a question mark, or an exclamation point is capitalized. If Caps Locks is enabled, all letters are uppercase LIKE THIS if you double-tap the shift key (The shift key is the one with the arrow pointing up.)

You can also tap on a keyboard that inserts a period when you double-tap the space key. Additionally, you can choose to use an international keyboard (as discussed in Chapter 2), which you select by tapping Keyboards under the Keyboard setting and choosing New Keyboard.

Apple gives you the power to substitute the keyboard on your phone for a custom alternative. Among the choices you might consider — some free and for most fees — are Swype, SwiftKey, Flexapt, Advext, and Minuum. For more on adding a third-party keyboard, see Chapter 2.
