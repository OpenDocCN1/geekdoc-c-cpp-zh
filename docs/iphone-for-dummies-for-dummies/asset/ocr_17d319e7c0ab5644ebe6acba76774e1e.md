Your iPhone contacts reflect any groups you belong to, such as different departments in your company, friends from work, and friends from school. When you're looking at the Contacts for an individual person, a Contacts button appears in the upper-left corner. Tap Contacts to display the roster of all your contacts, and the button at the upper-left corner changes to Groups. Tap that button to access contacts for a given group.

Among the Group listings you see is one for all your iCloud contacts and one for all your friends on Facebook. In some cases, your contacts will be linked — that is, information supplied by Facebook, Microsoft Exchange, or some other source will be combined into a single entry. The obvious benefit of this linking is that you can cut down on duplicate contact lists. If the iPhone doesn't automatically link appropriate entries, you can do the job manually from the given contact. Tap Edit, scroll to the bottom, and tap the green circle with the white + inside to link contacts from your entire list of contacts.

# Favorites

Favorites is where you can keep a list of the people and numbers you dial most often. Consider Favorites the iPhone equivalent of speed dialing. Merely tap the person's name in Favorites, and your iPhone calls the person.

![](bbox=[0.8, 0.163, 0.855, 0.222])

You can set as many favorites as you need for a person. So, for example, you create separate favorite listings for your spouse's office phone number, cell number, and FaceTime entry.
