# A KEYBOARD FOR ALL BORDERS

Apple continues to expand the iPhone's global reach by supplying international keyboard layouts for more than 50 languages. To access a keyboard that isn't customized for English American, tap Settings ⚙ General ⚙ Keyboards ⚙ Add New. Pop up a list with custom keyboards for Russian, Slovak, Swedish, and other languages. Apple even supplies two versions of Portuguese to accommodate customers in Brazil and Portugal, and four versions of French (including keyboards geared to users in Belgium, Canada, and Switzerland, as well as France). Here's a version of English for Australian English, for Indian English, and one for Singapore English. Here,s an English for Japan. (Go. figure) Keyboards for "right-to-left" languages are also on the list.

Have a multilingual household? You can select, in turn, as many international keyboards as you want. When you're working in an app that summons a keyboard, tap the international keyboard key, with the toggle and dictation keys, you see the keyboard you want. Note that the space momentarily displays the name of the keyboard language and then translates the word itself to the language of the keyboard in use. Tap again to select the next keyboard in the list of international keyboards (well as the Emoji keyboard) that you turned on Settings. If you keep cycling, you eventually come back to your original English layout. Here's an alternative method for summoning a keyboard you've enabled: Press your finger against the international keyboard key to summon a pop-up window that displays all the keyboards that are ready for action. Slide your finger from the list until it lands on the keyboard you want to use, and then release to select that.

You can use handwriting character recognition for simplified and traditional variations of Chinese. Just drag your finger in the box provided. Some Chinese keyboards don't rely on handwriting.

And here's a neat trick with iOS 10: You can type in two languages without switching from one to the other, just type the appropriate word or in whichever language makes sense and the iPhone takes a stab at what it thinks you mean to type next — in the appropriate language. You can type with any pair of the following languages: English, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, and Spanish. And this multilingual typing feature is also supported for English and Chinese.
