# Dude, Where's My Stuff?

If you performed a restore or had your iPhone replaced or repaired, you still have one more task to accomplish. Your iPhone may work flawlessly but some or all of your stuff — your music, contacts, iMessages, or whatever is missing. You're not stuck, at least not yet. We still have a few tricks up your sleeve:

+   Trick 1: Sync your iPhone with iTunes, and sync again. That right — sync and sync again. Why? Because sometimes stuff doesn't get synced properly on the first try. Just do it. If that doesn't seem right, try Trick 2.
