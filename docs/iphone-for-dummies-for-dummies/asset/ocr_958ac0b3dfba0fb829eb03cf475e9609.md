# The Way-Cool Big Red Audio Scrubber Tip

Here's the situation: You're listening to a podcast or an audiobook and trying to find the beginning of a specific segment by moving the scrubber (that little gray dot on the scrubber bar) left and right. The only problem is that the scrubber isn't very precise and your fat keeps moving it too far away or the other.

Never fear — your iPhone has a wonderful fix (albeit hidden by your finger). Just press your finger on the scrubber, but instead of sliding your finger to the left or right, by sliding it up or down, as shown in Figure 19–2.

Tap the playhead and drag up or down...

Figure 19–2: Press on the scrubber and slide your finger or down to see the big red scrubber.

The scrubber gets big and red, as shown on the right in Figure 19–2. Now, lifting your finger, slide to the left or right to scrub forward or backward.

While you're sliding, keep an eye on the elapsed time and remaining time indicators because they provide useful feedback.

This scrub trick is easier to do than to explain, so give it a try.
