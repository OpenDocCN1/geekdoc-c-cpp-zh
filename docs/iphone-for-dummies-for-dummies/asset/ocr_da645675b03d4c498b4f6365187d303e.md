When someone requests FaceTime with you, you'll appreciate being able to politely decline a FaceTime call. Cool as it can be to see and be, ask if you really want to be seen, say, when you just get out of bed or before your morning coffee.

Search for any FaceTime calls previously made by tapping an entry for call in Recents. The iPhone knows to take the call straight to video, though of course the person you're talking- has to accept the invitation each time.

You can decline FaceTime by also tapping a pal's listings in Contacts.

So what is a FaceTime call like? In our experience, first-time reactions were gleeful. You are only seeing the other person, but also the quality of the video is typically good. You also see your own mug (and maybe else is in the room with you) in a small picture-in-picture (PIP) window (as shown in Figure 4–12), which you can drag to a corner of the screen. The PIP image represents what the other person sees, so it's a good way of knowing, short of the other person dropped out of the frame.
