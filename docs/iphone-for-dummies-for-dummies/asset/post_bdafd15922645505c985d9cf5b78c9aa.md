## Prep Work: Setting Up Your Accounts

First things first. To use Mail, you need an email address. If you have broadband Internet access (that is, a cable modem, FIOS, or DSL), you probably received one or more addresses when you signed up. If you're one of the handful readers who doesn't already have an email account, you can get one free from Yahoo! (http://mail.yahoo.com), Google (http://mail.google.com), Microsoft (www.outlook.com), AOL (www.aol.com), or one of many service providers.

You might also pay a few dollars for a premium email account that doesn't track advertising (or anything else) onto your messages, though that is rarely necessary anymore. As mentioned, you can get a free email account as part of Apple's iCloud service.

## Setting up your account the less way

If you don't want to sync the emails on your computer, you can set up an email account on your iPhone manually. It's not quite as easy as clicking and syncing your iPhone, but it's not rocket science.

If you have no email accounts on your iPhone, the first time you launch Mail, you're walked through the following procedure. If you have one or more email accounts on your iPhone already and want to add a new account manually, start on the Home screen by tapping Settings > Mail > Accounts > Add Account.

Either way, you should be staring at the Add Account screen, shown in Figure 12-1. Proceed to one of the next two sections, depending on your email account.

### Setting up an email account with the Big Guys

If your account with Apple's iCloud service, Google's Gmail, Yahoo!, AOL, or Microsoft-Outlook.com, tap the appropriate button on the Add Account screen now. If you're setting up company email through Microsoft Exchange, skip to the "Setting corporate email" section. If your account is with a provider other than the listed, tap the Other button and skip to the next section.

Enter your name (requested), email address, and password, as shown in Figure 12-2. The description field is usually filled in automatically with the content you have in the address field, but you can replace that with your own description (such as Work or Personal).

### Setting up an account with another provider

If your email account is with a provider other than iCloud, Exchange, Gmail, Yahoo!, AOL, or Outlook, you have a bit more work ahead of you. You're going to need a bunch of information about your email account that you may not know or have handy.

We suggest that you scan the following instructions, note the items you know, and find the answers before you continue. To find the answers, look at the documentation you received when you signed up for your email account or visit the provider's website and search for.

Here's how you set up your account:

1. On the Add Account screen, tap the Other button.
2. Tap Add Mail Account.
3. Fill in the name, address, password, and description in the appropriate fields, if you were setting up an account for iCloud, Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo!, or AOL. Tap Next.
With any luck, that's all you'll have to do, although you may have to endure a spinning cursor for a while as the iPhone attempts to retrieve information and validate your account with your provider. Otherwise, continue with:
4. Tap the button at the top of the screen that denotes the type of email this uses: IMAP or POP, as shown in Figure 12-3.
5. Fill in the Internet name for your incoming server, which should look like mail.providername.com.
6. Fill in your username and password.
7. Fill in the Internet name for your outgoing server, which should look like smtp.providername.com.
8. Scroll down to the bottom of the screen to see the fields.
9. Tap the Next button in the upper-right corner of the screen.
