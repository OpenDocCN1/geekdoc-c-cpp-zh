# # Using digital zoom

When you spread your fingers or chin closer together on the screen, the zoom slider appears. Continuing to pinch or unpinch has the same effect of dragging the slider to the right or left. The zoom features works shooting video too.

> You may not love the results you get zooming in. The iPhone has a digital zoom, not an optical zoom — the 7 Plus is an exception and the quality distinction is enormous. Digital zoom in Photos enables you to get closer to your subject zooming up to 5x on models preceding the 7 and up to 10x on those latter two models. What digital zoom is really is cropping and blowing up part of image, which can cause fuzziness or blurring. Not to say harsh, but a subject's imperfections — and any inadequacies of the photographer's part — may come to light. Despite that, we find that even when using digital zoom, the camera renders some pretty strong pictures.
