# About the Authors

Edward C. Baig writes the Personal Technology column in USA TODAY and makes regular video appearances on the web and TV. Ed is also the author of Mac For Dummies, 10th Edition, and cowriter (with Bob LeVitus) of iPad For Dummies. Before joining USA TODAY as a columnist and reporter in 1999, Ed was on the editorial staff at Business Week, U.S. News & World Report, and Fortune magazine.

Bob LeVitus, often referred to as “Dr. Mac,” has written or rewritten nearly 80 popular books, selling millions of copies worldwide. His most recent includes iPad For Dummies and macOS Sierra For Dummies for John Wiley & Sons, Inc. In addition to books, Bob has penned the popular “Dr. Mac” column in the Houston Chronicle for 20 years and has written for pretty every magazine that has ever used the word Mac in its title.
