# Sorting and displaying contacts

Do you think of us as Ed and Bob or Baig and Levit? The answer to that question will probably determine whether you choose to sort your Contacts list by last name or first name.

Tap Contacts; scroll down to the Contacts section; and peek at Sort Order. Then tap Last, First or First, Last. You can determine whether you want to display a first name or last name by tapping Display Order and then choosing First, Last or Last. You can also go with a Short Name setting that lets you fit more names on the screen. (Chinese, Japanese, and Korean names are not affected.)You can choose a first name and last initial, first initial and last name, first name only, or last name only. If you prefer nicknames, you can choose those.

You'll also find a Contact Found in Mail switch. If you turn it off, any unconfirmed contact suggestions are deleted. You won't see suggestions when you try to complete a mail address, on the incoming call screen, and in the Contacts app.

In My Info, make sure your own name is chosen so that Siri knows where you live, among other reasons.

You can also import contacts that may be on a new SIM card that you inserted into the phone.
