## Using Your iPhone to Find Apps

Finding apps with your iPhone is almost as easy as finding them using iTunes. To browse, search, download, and install apps, the only requirement is that you have an Internet connection of some sort — Wi-Fi or wireless data network.

To get started, tap the App Store icon on your Home screen. After you launch the App Store, you see five icons at the bottom, representing five ways to interact with the store.

## Looking for apps from your iPhone

Know exactly what you're looking for? Instead of simply browsing, you can tap the Search icon and type a word or phrase. Or use the Categories icon, which works a little differently because it has no apps. Instead, it offers a list of categories as Books, Business, Catalogs, and Education, to name a few. Tap a category to see either a page full of apps or a list of subcategories. For example, the Games category offers subcategories as Action, Kids, Music, and Puzzle. Other categories have no subcategories — you'll go directly to the page full of apps when you tap them.

If you're wondering about Updates, the fifth icon at the bottom of the screen, we discuss it a little later in this chapter in the imaginatively named “Updating an app from your iPhone” section.
