The iPhone plays videos from the Apps or those you buy or rent from the store only in landscape, widescreen, mode. Video you shoot yourself in portrait mode using the iPhone camera, however, plays back in portrait mode. Of course, you can shoot and play back iPhone camera videos in landscape mode well.

# 8. Tap the controls that follow, as needed:

+   * To play or pause video, tap the play/pause icon.
* Drag the volume slider to raise the volume and to the left to lower it. Alternatively, use the physical volume buttons to control the audio levels. If the video is oriented properly, the buttons will be on the bottom left of the iPhone.
* Tap the restart/rewind icon to restart the video or tap and hold down the same icon to rewind.
