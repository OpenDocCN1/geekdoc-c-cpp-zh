# Threading messages

Apple lets you thread messages, or have automatically group related misses. The beauty of this arrangement is that you can easily trace an email conversation.

When you organize messages by thread or conversation, the related messages show up as a single entry in the mailbox, with a box-right arrow pointing you that the message is part of a larger ongoing exchange. If a message is not part of a thread, you see a single arrow. Figure 12–12 (left) shows that Bob and Susan are part of the same conversation in Ed—the listing reveals pretty the eight outlying messages that make up the conversation. Only two portions of them are visible in Figure 12–12 (right); you’d have to scroll to see the rest of the messages.
