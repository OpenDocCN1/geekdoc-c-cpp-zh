# # Keypad

From time to time, of course, you have to dial the number of a person or company who hasn't earned a spot in your Contacts. Or you need to access a keypad to navigate a voicemail system. In those situations, you'll want to tap the iPhone icon to bring up the large keys of the virtual touchscreen keypad you see in Figure 4-3. Just tap the appropriate keys and tap the Green button to dial. If you're dialing across in your Contacts list, you'll see the name of the caller just below the digits you've dialed.

If you dial an unfamiliar number, you can add it to your Contacts by tapping the + sign to the number you just dialed. After doing so, tap New Contact, Add to Existing Contact, or Cancel.

You can use the iPhone's keypad to remotely check your voicemail at work or home. Come to think of it, what a perfect segue into the next section. It's on one of our favorite iPhone features, visual voicemail.
