On earlier models, you can bring the phone up to your ear, wait for a tone, and then talk. Siri responds also when you press a button on a Bluetooth headset. For this work, the Raise to Speak feature must be enabled inside Siri's settings.

What happens next is up to you. You can ask a wide range of questions or issue voice commands. If you didn't get your words out enough or you were misunderstood, tap the microphone icon and try again.

Siri relies on voice recognition, artificial intelligence, and, of course, an Internet connection. She (or he) will respond in a conversational (if still slightly robotic) manner. Using Siri isn't entirely a hands-free experience. Spoken words are supplemented by information on the iPhone (so you can see in the next section).

And where does Siri get that information? By tapping into Location Services and seeking answers from the web using such as Bing, Wikipedia, Yelp, Yahoo!, OpenTable, Twitter, and Wolfram|Alpha, which you can learn more about in the coming sidebar.

And Siri can open apps, Apple's own and third-party apps. Indeed, from your contacts, Siri might be able to determine your suppose, coworkers, friends, as well as where you live. You might ask, "How do I get home from here?” and Siri will fire up Maps to show you your way. Or you can ask, "Find a Italian restaurant near Barbara's house," and Siri will serve up a list, sorted by Yelp rating. Siri can even let you make a reservation (via OpenTable).

If you ask about a favorite sports team, Siri will retrieve the score of last night's game and if you're rummaging through a longish email that you can't get through at the moment, you can have Siri set a reminder to follow up later in the evening.

We also think it's pretty cool that Siri teams up with one of our favorite apps, Shazam (see Chapter 17), to identify the name and artist of the musical track that's playing. Just ask Siri what song is playing and she'll tell you (assuming that you can identify the song). When the song is properly identified, you can choose to buy it.
