Hexadecimal floating-point literals were first proposed by Thomas Köppe in http://wg21.link/p0245r0. The finally accepted wording was formulated by Thomas Köppe in http://wg21.link/p0245r1.

The prefix for UTF-8 character literals was first proposed by Richard Smith in http://wg21.link/n4197. The finally accepted wording was formulated by Richard Smith in http://wg21.link/n4267.

Making exception specifications part of the function type was first proposed by Jens Maurer in http://wg21.link/n4320. The finally accepted wording was formulated by Jens Maurer in http://wg21.link/p0012r1.

Single-argument static_assert was accepted as proposed by Walter E. Brown in http://wg21.link/n3928.

The preprocessor clause __has_include() was first proposed by Clark Nelson and Richard Smith as part of http://wg21.link/p0061r0. The finally accepted wording was formulated by Clark Nelson and Richard Smith in http://wg21.link/p0061r1.
