The problem of getting access to the weak pointer type of shared pointers was first raised by Arthur O’Dwyer in http://wg21.link/n4537. The finally accepted wording for this feature was formulated by Arthur O’Dwyer in http://wg21.link/p0163r0.

weak_from_this was first proposed by Jonathan Wakely and Peter Dimov in http://wg21.link/p0033r0. The finally accepted wording for this feature was formulated by Jonathan Wakely and Peter Dimov in http://wg21.link/p0033r1.

The numeric GCD and LCM functions were first proposed by Walter E. Brown in http://wg21.link/n3845. They became part of the first Library Fundamentals TS. For C++17, they were then adopted as proposed by Walter E. Brown in http://wg21.link/p0295r0.

The three-argument overloads of std::hypot() were first proposed by Benson Ma in http://wg21.link/p0030r0. The finally accepted wording for this feature was formulated by Benson Ma in http://wg21.link/p0030r1.

Adding special mathematical functions was first proposed in 2003 by Walter E. Brown in http://wg21.link/n1422. Making them mandatory in C++17 by adopting the international standard IS 29124:2010 was proposed by Walter E. Brown, Axel Naumann, and Edward Smith-Rowland in http://wg21.link/p0226r1.

The chrono extensions were first proposed by Howard Hinnant in http://wg21.link/p0092r0. The finally accepted wording for this feature was formulated by Howard Hinnant in http://wg21.link/p0092r1.

Adding constexpr for chrono was first proposed by Howard Hinnant in http://wg21.link/p0505r0. and accepted. The finally accepted wording for this feature was formulated by Howard Hinnant in http://wg21.link/p0092R1.

Adding constexpr at various other places was accepted as proposed by Antony Polukhin in http://wg21.link/p0031r0 and in http://wg21.link/p0426r1.

The fixes for noexcept were first proposed by Nicolai Josuttis in http://wg21.link/4002. The finally accepted wording for this modification was formulated by Nicolai Josuttis in http://wg21.link/n4258.
