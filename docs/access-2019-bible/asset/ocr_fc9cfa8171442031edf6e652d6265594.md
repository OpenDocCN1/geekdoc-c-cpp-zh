# CAUTION

Be aware that Access interprets dates based on the region and language settings in the Windows Control Panel. Those settings could tell Access to interpret short dates as mm/dd/yyyy or dd/mm/yyyy, depending on the region. Make sure that you account for these regional differences when working with dates. Also, keep in mind that Access, by default, interprets two-digit years from 00 to 30 as 2000 to 2030, while all two-digit years between 31 and 99 are taken to be 1931 to 1999. This is one reason why consistently using four-digit years during data entry is always a good idea.
