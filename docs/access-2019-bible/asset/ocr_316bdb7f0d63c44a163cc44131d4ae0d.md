By default, the form’s Property Sheet appears docked to the right side of the Access window. Because the Property Sheet is a window, it can be undocked, moved, and resized. In Figure 18.10, the Property Sheet has been undocked and dragged to a position overlying frmProducts. Notice that the Property Sheet window doesn’t have Maximize or Minimize buttons or searching capabilities. There is a sort icon that toggles between the default order of the properties and an ascending alphabetical order.

# Changing the title bar text with the Caption property

Normally, a form’s title bar (or its tab if you’re using tabbed documents instead of overlapping windows) shows the name of the form after it’s saved. The form’s Caption property specifies the text displayed in the title bar when the form is in Form view. Follow these steps to change the title bar text:

+   1. Click the form selector to make sure the form itself is selected.
+   2. Click the Property Sheet button in the Design tab’s Tools group, or press F4 to open the Property Sheet.
+   3. Click the Caption property in the Property Sheet and enter Products in the property’s text box, as shown in Figure 18.11.
