# Adding emphasis at run time

You can show or hide a control for a particular record by setting its Visible property. This can be useful if you only want to show a field in certain conditions and hide it otherwise. You can even use the value of another control as your condition. You hide a control, of course, by setting its Visible property to False (or No) at design time. Only when the information contained in the control is needed do you reset the Visible property to True.

An example might be a message to the Northwind Traders customers that a certain item has been discontinued and inventory is shrinking. If you were to show this message for every item in the Northwind catalog, the report would be very cluttered and you would lose the effect of highlighting discontinued items. But by only showing the stock count message on discontinued items, you might encourage buyers to stock up on the item.

Notice that the Guaraná Fantástica beverage product appears in italics, the price is bold italics, and the Only 20 units in stock! message appears to the right of the product information.
