# Aggregating Data

Access datasheets support a Total row at the bottom of datasheets. The Total row is opened by clicking the Totals button in the Records group on the Home tab of the Ribbon (the Totals button is marked with a Greek sigma character, much like the AutoSum button in Excel). Each column in the Total row can be set to a different aggregate calculation (Sum, Average, Minimum, Maximum, Count, Standard Deviation, or Variance).

To use the Total row, open a table or form in Datasheet view and click the Totals button in the Records group (see Figure 5.23) on the Home tab of the Ribbon. Access adds a Total row at the bottom of the datasheet, just below the New row.
