The My Form tab, with its associated controls, is available while the form is open. Closing the form causes the new tab to disappear. The list of Ribbons in the Property Sheet acts like the list in the Access Options dialog box. That is, it doesn't update whenever you add a new Ribbon to USysRibbons. You have to close and reopen your application to update the list.

> Tip
> Choosing Compact & Repair Database from File > Info automatically closes and reopens your database. It's a quick option for updating the loaded Ribbons list. It has the added benefit of keeping your database compacted.

# Removing the Ribbon Completely

Assume, for a moment, that there are perfectly legitimate reasons why you don't want to use the Ribbon in your applications. Perhaps you've developed a set of effective switchboard forms, or you've mimicked the old-style toolbars and menus with borderless forms. Or, your applications are entirely forms-driven and don't need the flexibility provided by toolbars and Ribbons.
