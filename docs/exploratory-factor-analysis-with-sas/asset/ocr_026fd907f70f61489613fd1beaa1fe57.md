Next, we examine the pattern and structure coefficients, presented in Table 10.1 on page 175. The structure coefficients show relatively strong loadings across factors, and the factors are moderately correlated (r = 0.30 to 0.50). The moderate correlations among the factors could indicate the presence of a second-order factor or it could suggest the factors are not unique, and fewer factors should have been extracted. Note that while very low correlations between factors can indicate that there is no higher-order factor, moderate to strong correlations between factors demonstrate only the possibility of a higher-order factor.
