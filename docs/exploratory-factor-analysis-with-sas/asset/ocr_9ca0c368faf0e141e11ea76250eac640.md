The scree plots produced by the SCREE option and ODS are produced in Figure 3.2 on page 39 and Figure 3.3 on page 40, respectively. They show mostly identical results, the plotted initial eigenvalue estimates, just in slightly different formats. The SCREE option produces a simplified text-based figure (that can actually be copied and pasted as text), but ODS produces a figure in a graphical format. ODS also includes a plot that combines a graphical representation of the eigenvalues with the proportion of variance explained.

Based on these scree plots, we can see an “elbow” or inflection point at factor 3, suggesting that two factors should be retained. There is also an additional “elbow” that appears at factor 4. This is much less pronounced than the previous inflection point but, based on these results, we can consider exploring a three-factor solution in addition to a two-factor solution.
