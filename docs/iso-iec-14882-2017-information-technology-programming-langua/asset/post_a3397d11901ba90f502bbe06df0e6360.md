9 A default-template-argument is a template-argument (17.3) specified after = a template-parameter. A default-template-argument may be specified for any kind of template-parameter (non-type, type) that is not a template parameter pack. A default-template-argument may be specified in a template declaration. A default-template-argument shall not be specified in the template-parameter-lists of the definition of a member function. If a function template declaration specifies a default-template-argument, then that declaration shall be a definition and shall be the only declaration of the function template in the translation unit.
