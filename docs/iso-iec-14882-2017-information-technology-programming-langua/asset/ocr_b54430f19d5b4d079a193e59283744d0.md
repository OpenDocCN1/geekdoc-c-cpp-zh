basic_ios& copyfmt(const basic_ios& rhs); does nothing. Otherwise it assigns the member objects of *this the corresponding member objects of rhs as unchanged:

+   1. calls each registered callback (fn, index) as (*fn)(erase_event, *this, index);
2. assigns to the member objects of *this the corresponding member objects of rhs, except that
   - rstate(), and exceptions() are left unchanged;
   - the contents of arrays pointed by pword and iword are copied, not the pointers;
   - if newly pointed values points outside the object rhs and those objects are destroyed when rhs is destroyed, the newly pointed values are altered to point at newly constructed objects of the this;
3. calls each callback that was copied from rhs as (copyfmt_event, *this, index);
4. calls exceptions (rhs.exceptions()).

Note: The second pass through the callback pairs a copied pword value to be zero, or to have its referent copy or referenced, or to have other special action. — end note

Returns: *this.
