1 A string-literal is a sequence of characters (as defined in 5.13.3) surrounded by double quotes, optionally prefixed by R, u8, u8R, u, U, UR, L, or LR as in "...R(...)", u8"...", u8R*(...", u"..., uR*(...)", U"...", UR(...).", L"...", or LR\(...\), respectively.
