The template <valarray> is a one-dimensional smart array, with elements numbered sequentially from zero. It is a representation of the mathematical concept of an ordered set of values. For convenience, an object of type <valarray> is referred to as an "array" throughout the remainder of 29.7. The illusion of higher dimensionality may be provided by the familiar idiom of subscript indices, together with the powerful subsetting capabilities provided by the generalized subscript operators.276
