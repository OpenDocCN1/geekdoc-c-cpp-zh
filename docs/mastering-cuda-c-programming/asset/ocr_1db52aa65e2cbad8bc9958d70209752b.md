Perception and Environment Modeling

At the heart of any autonomous system is its ability to perceive and understand the world around it. It involves processing data from a variety of sensors, including cameras, lidar, radar, and ultrasonic sensors. The challenge lies in the sheer volume of data and the necessity for real-time processing to navigate and interact with dynamic environments safely.

CUDA addresses these challenges by leveraging the parallel processing capabilities of GPUs. Image and signal processing tasks, which are inherently parallel, can be distributed across thousands of GPU cores, resulting in significant performance gains. For instance, processing lidar point clouds to detect obstacles involves vast numbers of calculations that can be efficiently parallelized with CUDA.
