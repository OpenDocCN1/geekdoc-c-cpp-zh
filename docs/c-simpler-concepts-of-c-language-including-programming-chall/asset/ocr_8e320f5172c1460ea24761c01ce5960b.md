# 4.2.4. Bitwise operators

As everybody is aware of the fact that the computer treats everything as 0s and 1s. They are called bits (short form of Binary digits). ‘C’, being a high level program is no exception with the above fact. It has operators to deal with bits. There are 6 operators in this category.

- Bitwise OR (|)
- Bitwise XOR (^)
- Left Shift (<<)
- Right Shift (>>)
