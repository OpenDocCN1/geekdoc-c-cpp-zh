# PREFACE TO THE FIRST EDITION

C- Simpler, the title of the book itself talks about the nature of the contents of this book. This book explains of C language in a form that is well suited for the beginners. Each and every, though very simple, has been programmatically explained. After going through the programs, the reader will be mentally prepared to face programming challenges. This book will also serve as a guide for those who are heading towards the campus interviews, since this book covers the minutes about the constructs of C language. The readers will be able to visualize tricky concepts through the diagrams used and then in this book. This book was developed with the notion that, after reading every chapter, the reader should be able to apply the constructs that they studied.

Here goes the brief discussion of chapters. First chapter covers all the elementary things. Chapter 2 talks on learning constructs followed by the discussion of arrays in chapter 3. Chapter 4 takes the reader through different kinds of operators available in 'C'. From chapter 5, reader will be taken through the rough and touches of the language. Chapter 5 discusses the most important function concepts, followed by storage qualifiers in chapter 6. Chapter 7, deals with the most significant concept in 'C', the 'Pointers', that are explained in the simplest way. Structures and unions are discussed in chapter 8. Chapter 9 deals with the so easy-to-learn concepts, the 'strings'. File handling concepts have been covered in chapter 10, followed by the very useful data structures which actually show the applicability of 'C', in chapter 11. Chapter 12 is meant to deal with command line arguments. This chapter is written in such a way that, the reader will voluntarily start answering questions on command line arguments. Chapter 13 is unique and quite helpful for those who work on Linux/Unix platform. It discusses the utilization of 'C' constructs in Linux/Unix platform. Chapter 14 discusses the most interesting pre-directors. But not the last, chapter 15 is meant to empower the programmers with some basic, yet powerful guidelines.

At the end of every chapter, the brain squeezing quizzes are given to check the level of understanding. The critics are the most valuable gift to you. It is also for us.
