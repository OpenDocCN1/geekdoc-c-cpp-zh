The *CellularAutomaton* class, which is derived from the *Network* class of the simulation engine, contains an array of *Cell* objects. Output from a cell is sent to its two neighbors: output from the cell at position *i* goes to the cells at positions *i + 1* (right) and *i − 1* (left). Output from the two cells at the ends of the array can be discarded or sent to the cell at the opposite end.
