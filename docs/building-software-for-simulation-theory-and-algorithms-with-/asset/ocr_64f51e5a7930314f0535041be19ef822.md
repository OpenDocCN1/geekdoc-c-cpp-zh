((τ, c) ▷ (ε1, 0)) ▷ (ε2, 0) = (τ + ε1, 0) ▷ (ε2, 0) = (τ + ε1 + ε2, 0)

and so the lookahead of the series is ε1 + ε2.

The combined lookahead of models in parallel is just as easily deduced from the parallel configuration shown in Figure B.4; the lookahead of the resultant is equal to the smaller of the lookahead of its components. This is a consequence of the resultant's output at any time t being the bag union of the output of each component at t.

Feedback connections within a network do not contribute to its lookahead because the feedforward connections are the quickest means for input to affect output. This observation and the two rules above imply that the lookahead of a network is the shortest path by which an input to the network can influence its output.
