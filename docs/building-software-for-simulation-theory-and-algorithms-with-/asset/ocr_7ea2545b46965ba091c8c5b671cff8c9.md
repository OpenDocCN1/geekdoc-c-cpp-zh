The frequency and mechanical power trajectories for the generators are shown in Figures 6.18 and 6.19. The correspondence between the control signal and frequency excursion is readily apparent. The contingency ends with the generators able to supply sufficient voltage to the majority of the loads, but voltages near the disconnected generator have noticeably sagged: Figure 6.20 shows bus voltages initially and at the end of the scenario. If this model were extended to include voltage regulating transformers at the loads (see, e.g., Ref. 58), these would initiate a second round of corrective actions by creating a greater demand for power (reactive power, in this case) from the generators. This, in turn, could cause nearby generators to reach their E_f limits, instigating a voltage collapse and additional loss of generation. This scenario would make an interesting study.
