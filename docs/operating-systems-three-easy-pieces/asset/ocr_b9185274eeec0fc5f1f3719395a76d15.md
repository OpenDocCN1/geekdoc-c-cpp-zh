However, this textbook by Bryant and O'Hallanan dives into the details of x86, which is at least an early system that used such structures. It's just a great book to have.

[M98] "Virtual Memory: Issues of Implementation" by Bruce Jacob, Trevor Wood. IEEE Computer, June 1998. An excellent survey of a number of systems and their approach to virtualizing memory. PowerPC, MIPs, and others Plenty of details on x86, and other architectures.

[L82] "Virtual Management in the VAX/VMS System" by Hank Levy, P. Lipman, IEEE Computer, Vol. 15, No. 7, 1982. A terrific paper about a virtual memory manager in a classic operating system, VMS. So terrific, in fact, that we used everything we learned about virtual memory thus far a few chapters from now.
