# References

[BC91] "Performance for Architecture: A RISC and a CISC with Similar Hardware Organization" by D. Bhandarkar and Douglas W. Clark. Communications of the ACM, September 1991. A great and fair comparison between RISC and CISC. The bottom line: on similar hardware, RISC was a factor of three better performance.

[CM01] "The evolution of RISC technology at IBM" by John Cocke, IBM Journal of Research and Development, 441:2. A summary of the ideas and work behind the IBM 801, which many consider the first true RISC microprocessor.

[C95] "The Core of the Black Canyon Computer Corporation" by John Coeur. IEEE Annals of History of Computing 17, 1995. In this fascinating historical note, Coeur talks about how he invented the TL in 1984 and the fortuitous collaboration that ensued with the Project MAC folks at MIT.

[CS68] "Shared-access Data Processing System" by John E. Coeur, Edward L. Gather. Patent 3412382, November 1968. The patent contains the ideas for an associative memory to store translations. Morgan-Hill, Ca, in 1968.

[CP78] "The Architecture of the IBM System/370" by R.P. Case, A. Padegs. Communications of the ACM. 21:73-96, June 1978. The first person to use the term lookaside buffer. The name is the Russian nickname for a cache, which was a lookahead buffer called by those developing the Atlas System at the University of Manchester; a cache of address translations because of translation. Even the term lookahead buffer felt out of favor, TLB seems to have fault, whatever reason.

[IH93] "MIPS R4000 Processor User Manual" by John Heinrich. Available: http://www.csmit.edu/docs/R4000.Uman.Book.Ed2.pdf A manual that is quite surprising or not.

[HP06] "Computer Architecture: A Quantitative Approach" by John Hennessy and David Patterson. MorganKaufman, 2006. A great book computer architecture. We have a particular attachment to the classic edition.

[PS81] "IQA-1 Reduced Instruction Set VLSI Computer" by D.A. Patterson and C.H. Quisco. ISA 81, Minneapolis, May 1981. The paper that introduced the term RISC, started the avalanche of research in scholarly computing systems for performance.

[SB92] "CPU Performance and Execution Time Prediction Using Narrow Spectrum Benchmarking" by Rudolph H. King, Computer Department, University of California, Berkeley. Technical Report No. UCB/CS-92-684, February 1992. A great discussion about how to predict execution time by breaking down into into pieces and knowing the cost of each. Probably the most interesting part that comes of this work is the tool to measure the details of the cache hit (described in Chapter 5). Make sure to check out the wonderful dreams therein.

[W03] "A Survey on the Interaction Between Caching, Translation and Protection" by Adam Wiggins. University of New South Wales at TRNSW-CSE-TR-0321, 2003. An excellent survey of how TLBs interact with other parts of the CPU pipeline, namely hardware caches.

[WG00] "The SPARC Architecture Manual: Version 9" by David L. Weaver and Tom Green. SPARC International, San Jose, California, September 2000. Available: http://www.sparc.org/standards/SPARC9.pdf. Another manual: I bet you were hoping for a more fun citation to end this chapter.
