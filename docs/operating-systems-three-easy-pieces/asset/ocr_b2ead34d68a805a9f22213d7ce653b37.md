# References

[ADR03] "More Than an Interface: SCSI vs." by Dave Anderson, Jim Dykes, Erik Riedel. FAST '03, 2003. One of the best recent-ish references on how modern disk drives really work; a must read for anyone interested in knowing more.

[CKR72] "Analysis of Scheduling Policies for Disk Seek Times" E.G. Coffman, L.A. Klimko, B. Ryan SIAM Journal of Computing, September 1972, Vol. 1, No. 3. Some of the early work in the field of disk scheduling.

[HK17] "The Unwritten Contract of Solid State Drives" by Jun, Sudarsan Kannan, Andrea C. Arcpaci-Dusseau, Remi H. Arcpaci-Dusseau, Euroys '17, Belgrade, Serbia, April 2017. We take advantage of the uncertain control, and it extends to SSDs. Using SSDs wells seems as complicated than hard drives, and sometimes with even more.

[IW01] "Anticipatory Scheduling: To Overcome Deceptive Idle-In Synchronous I/O" by Sitaran, Peter Dusselj. SOSP '01, October 2001. A cool paper showing how anticipatory scheduling; better requests may be on their way!

[JW91] "Disk Scheduling Algorithms Based On Rotational Position" by D. Jacobson, J. Wilkes Technical Report HP-CSP-22(1), Hewlett-Packard, J. Wilkes. 1991. A modern take on disk scheduling. It remains a technical report (and not a published paper) because the authors were quoted by Scalter et al. [50].

[RW92] "Introduction to Disk Drive Modeling" by C. Ruecmeller, J. IEEE Computer, 273, March 1994. A terrific introduction to the basics of disk operation. Some pieces are out of date, but the basic mechanics remain.

[SCO90] "Disk Scheduling Revised" by Marno Saltzer, Peter Chen, John Ousterhout. USENIX 1990. A paper that talks how head rotation moves in the world of disk scheduling.

[G04] "MEMS-based storage devices and solid disk interfaces: A square peg in a round hole?" Steven W. Schlosser, Gregory K. Ranger '04, pp. 77-100, 2004. While the MEMS aspect of this paper has made an impact, the discussion of the connection between file systems and disks is helpful and a little lacking. We later on this work to study the "Unwritten Contract of Solid State Drives" [HK17].

[S09a] "Barracuda ES2 data sheet" by Seagate, Inc. Available at this website, at least it was: http://www.seagate.com/docs/pdf/datasheet/disc/ds_baracuda_es.pdf. A data sheet; read at your own risk. Risk of what? Boredom.

[S09b] "Cheetah 15K-5" by Seagate, Inc. Available at this website, we're pretty sure it is: http://www.seagate.com/docs/pdf/datasheet/disc/cheetah-15k-5-us.pdf.
