# References

[A+8] "Design Tradeoffs for SSD Performance" by N. Agrawal, V. Prabhakaran, T. Wobber, J. D. Davis, M. Manasse, R. Panigraahy, USENIK '08, San Diego California, June 2008. An excellent overview of what goes into SSD design.

[A14] "Operating Systems: Three Easy Pieces" by Chapters: Crash Consistency: FSC and journaling-Log-Structured File Systems. Remzi Arpac-Dusseau and Andrea Arpac-Dusseau. A lot more detail here about how logging can be used in file systems; how many of the ideas can be applied inside devices too to be be.

[A15] "Pricing Strategy" by Remzi Arpac-Dusseau, February 2015. This is not an actual paper, but one of the authors to Amazon and looking at current prices of hard drives and SSDs. You can read this paper online and see what the current costs are today.

[B12] "CORE Log Design for Flash Controllers" by M. Balakrishnan, D. Dalkhi, V. Prabhakaran, T. Wobber, M. Wei, J. D. Davis. NSD '12, San Jose, California, April 2012. A new way to think about designing remapped log for flash controllers.

[D10] "Understanding Intrinsic Characteristics" by Simona Boboila, Peter Denoyers. FT '10, San Jose, California, February 2010. A paper that reviews endurance flash-lifters. Endurance somections for manufacturers predictor, by up to 100x.

[E07] "The Last Word in File Systems" by Jeff Bonnwick and Bill Moore. Available here: http://www.step.org/citations/fs_last.pdf. What is the last word in file systems? No, but maybe it's close.

[CG09] "Characterizing Flash Memory: Anomalies, Observations, and Applications" by L. M. Grupp, A. M. Caulfield, J. Cowan, S. Steven. ASPLOS '09, Washington, DC, March 2009. Early research on flash memory into larger-scale controllers; definitely worth a read.

[CK09] "Understanding Intrinsic Characteristics and System Implications of Flash Memory based Solid State Drives" by Feng Chen, David A. Kaoudy, and Xiaodong Zhang. SIGMETRICS/'09, Seattle, Washington, June 2009. An excellent overview of SSD performance problems circa 2009 (though now a little dated).

[G14] "The SSD Endurance Experiment" by Geof Geiger. The Tech Report, September 19, 2014. Available: http://techreport.com/review/27062. A nice set of simple experiments measuring performance of SSDs over time. There are many other similar studies; use google to find more.

[HK17] "The Constraint of Solid State Drives" by Jun He, Sudarsan Kannan, Andrea C. Arpac-Dusseau. EuroSys '17, Berlin, Serbia, April 2017. Our paper lays out five rules clients should follow in order to get the best performance out of modern SSDs. The rules require locality, aligned sequentiality, grouping by death time, and uniform lifetime. Read the paper for details.

[H+14] "An Aggressive Worn-out Flash Block Management Scheme: To Alleviate Performance Degradation" by Ping Huang, Guanying Wu, Weijun Xiao. EuroSys '14, 2014. Recent work showing how to get the most out of worn-out flash blocks; neat!
