# FLASH-BASED SSDS

[J10] Failure Mechanisms and Models for Semiconductor Devices" by Unknown author. Report JEP122F, November 2010. Available on the internet at this so-called website: http://www.jedec.org/sites/default/files/docs/JEP122F.pdf. A highly detailed discussion of what is going on the device level and how such devices fail. Only for those not faint of heart? Or physicists. Or both.

[KK02] "A Space-Efficient Flash Translation for Compact Systems" by Jesyang Kim, Jong Min Kim, Sam Ho, Sang Lyul Min, Yoochun Cho. IEEE Transactions on Electronics, Volume 48, Number 2, May 2002. One of the earliest to suggest hotspot mappings.

[L07] "A Buffer-Based Flash Translation by Using Fully-Associative Search Translation" by Sang-geon Lee, Tae-Sun Chun, Dong-Ho Lee, Sangwon Park, Ha-Joe Sog, ACM Transactions on Embedded Computing, Volume 6, Number 3, July 2007. A terrific paper on how to build hotlog mappings.

[T15] "Performance Hard Drives Charts" by Tom's Hardware. 2015. Available here: http://www.tomshard.com/charts/enterprise-hdd-charts. Yet another site with performance data, this time focusing on hard drives.

[U12] "Understanding TLC Flash" by Kristian Vato. AnandTech, 2012. Available: http://www.andtech.com/show/5067/understanding-tlc-nist. A short description about TLC flash and its characteristics.

[W15] "List of Ships Sunk by Icebergs" by many authors. Available on the internet on "web": http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/list_of_ships_sunk_by_icebergs. Yes, there is a wikipedia page about ships sunk by icebergs. It is a really boring page and basically everyone knows the only ship the iceberg-sinking mafia claims is Titanic.

[Z12] "De-irection for Flash-based SSDs with Nameless Writes" by Yiying Zhang, Leo Prash, Andrew Koh, Kevin H. Arpaci-Dusseau. FAST '13, San Jose, California, February 2013. Our research on a new idea to reduce mapping table space; the key is to re-use the pointers in this system to store blocks of locations, instead of adding another level of indirection.
