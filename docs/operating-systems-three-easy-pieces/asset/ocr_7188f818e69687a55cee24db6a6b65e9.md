# 48.1 Communication Basics

The central tenant of modern networking is that communication is fundamentally unreliable. In the wide-area Internet, or a local-area high-speed network such as Infiniband, packets are regularly lost, corrupted, or do not reach their destination.

There are a multitude of causes for packet loss or corruption. During transmission, some bits get flipped due to electrical or other similar problems. Sometimes, an element in the system, such as a network link or router may not working correctly; network cables could accidentally get severed, at least sometimes.

More however is packet loss due to lack of buffering within a network switch, router, or endpoint.
