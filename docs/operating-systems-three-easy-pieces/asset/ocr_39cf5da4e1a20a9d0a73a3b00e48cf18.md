# References

[B19) "A fork() in the road" by Andrew Baumann, Jonathan Appavao, Oran Krier, Timothy Roscoe. HotOS '19, Bertinorno, Italy. A full paper of fork (ing rage, Read it to get an viewpoint on the UNIX process API. Presented at the always HotOS workshop, where systems researchers go to present意见 in the hopes of pushing the community in new directions.

[C63] "Multiprocessor System Design" by Melvin E. Conway. AFIPS '63 Fair Joint Computer Conference, New York, USA 1963. An early paper on how design multiprocessing systems; may be the first term fork() was used in the discussion of spawning new processes.

[DV66] "Programming Semantics for Multiprocessor Computations" by Jack B. Dennis and Earl C. Van Horn. Communications of the ACM, Volume 9, Number 3, March 1966. A classic paper that outlines the basics of multiprogramming on multiprocessor systems. Undoubtedly had great influence on future systems.

[I16] "They could be twins?" by Phoebe Jason-Edwards. The Daily Mail, March 1, 2016. This hard-hitting piece of journalism touches on weirdly similar child parents and is frankly kind of memorizing. Go ahead, waste minutes of life and check it out. Don't forget to come back here! This, in a microcost, is the danger of surfing the web.

[I83] "Hints for Computer Systems Design" by Butler Lampson. ACM Operating Systems Review, November 15:1983. Lampson's famous hints on how to design computer systems. You should read it at some point in your life, and probably at points in your life.

[QI15] "With Great Power Come Responsibility" by The Quote Investigator. Available: https://quoteinvestigator.com/2015/07/23/great-power. The quote investigator concludes the earliest mention of this concept is 1783, in a collection of decrees made at the French National Convention. The quote is: "Un événement naissant qu'une grande responsabilité est la suite indispensable d'un pouvoir qui exige." They must consider that great responsibility follows inseparably from great power." Only in 1962 did the following words appear in Spider-Man: "...with great power there must also come-great responsibility!" So looks like the French Revolution gets credit for this one, not Stan Lee. Sorry.

[SR05] "Advanced Programming in the UNIX Environment" by W. Richard Stevens, Stephen A. Rago. Addison-Wesley, 2005. All nuances and subtleties of UNIX APIs are found here. Buy this book! Read it! And most importantly, live it.
