SCHEDULING: INTRODUCTION

# References

+   [B79] "The Convoy Phenomenon" by M. Blasen, J. Gray, M. Mitoma, T. Price. ACM Operating Systems Review, 13:2, April 1979. Perhaps the first reference to convoys, which occurs in databases as well as the OS.

+   [C54] "Priority Assignment in Waiting Line Problems" by A. Cobham. Journal of Operations Research, 2/70, pages 70–76, 1954. The pioneering paper using an SJF scheduling in the repair of machines.

+   [K64] "The Art of Time-Shared Processor" by Leonard Kleinnrock. Naval Research Logistics algorithm; 11/1, pages 59–73, March 1964. May be the first reference to robin-robin algorithm alling to scheduling a time-shared system.

+   [CS68] "Computer Scheduling Methods and Countermeasures" by Edward G. Coffman and Leonard Kleinnrock. AISF '68 (Spring), April 1968. An excellent early introduction to and analysis of a number of basic scheduling deicisions.

+   [W91] "The Art of Systems Performance Analysis: Techniques for Experimental Design, Measurement, Simulation, and Modeling" by R. Jain. Interscience, New York, April 1991.

+   [O45] "Animal Farm" by George Orwell. Secker and Warwick (London), 1945. A great depressing alleg book about power and its corruptions. Some say it is a critique of Stalin and the pre-WWII Stalin in the U.S.R. we say it is a critique of pigs.

+   [MB91] "The effect of context-switches on cache performance" by Jeffrey C. Morgan, Anita Antorg, ASPLOS, 1991. A nice study on how cache performance can be affected by context switching; less of an issue in today's systems where processors issue billions of instructions per second but context-switches remain in the millisecond time range.
