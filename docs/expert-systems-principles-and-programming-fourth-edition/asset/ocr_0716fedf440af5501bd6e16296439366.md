CLIPs brings everything up to the important topic of Truth Maintenance in expert systems. CLIPs has a facility for Truth Maintenance where if a fact is proven incorrect, all succeeding facts or rules which were asserted, modified or deleted are retracted and the system restored to the state it was in before the incorrect fact was asserted. This allows the conclusions of CLIPS to be validated, which is more than most people can say about their own beliefs and conclusions.
