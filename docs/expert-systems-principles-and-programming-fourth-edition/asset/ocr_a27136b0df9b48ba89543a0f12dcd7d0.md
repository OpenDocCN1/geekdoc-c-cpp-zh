Another powerful method of inferences, analogy, was discussed. Although not widely used in expert systems because of the difficulty in implementing it, analogy is the normal method of doctors and lawyers, and should be considered in the design of expert systems. Generate-and-test was also discussed with an example of its use in MYCIN. The application of metaknowledge in TEIRESA S was described and its relationship to verification and validation of expert systems.
