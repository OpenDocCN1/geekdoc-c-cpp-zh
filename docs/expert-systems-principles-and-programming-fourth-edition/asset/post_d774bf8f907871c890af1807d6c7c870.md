Neural networks are not just for research but are in practical use to save millions of dollars in detecting debt/credit card fraud for credit service organizations (CUSO). The particular tool used by PSCU Financial Services is the Falcon neural net tool which detects credit card fraud in real-time, http://www.pscufs.com/falcon.htm.
