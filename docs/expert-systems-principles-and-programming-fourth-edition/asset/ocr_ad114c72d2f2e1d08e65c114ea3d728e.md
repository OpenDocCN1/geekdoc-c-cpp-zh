\(\mu_{A|\neg B}(x) = \vee(0, (\mu_A(x) - \mu_B(x)))\)

where the "-" separating \(\mu_A\) and \(\mu_B\) is the arithmetic minus operator. A | ¬ B represents those elements which are more in A than B. The complement can be written in terms of the universe set, \(\mathcal{U}\), and bounded difference as:

\(A^c = \mathcal{U} / | - | A\)

\(\mu_{CON(A)}(x) = (\mu_A(x))^2\)

The CON operation concentrates fuzzy elements by reducing the membership grades more of elements that have smaller membership grades. Figure 5.13 illustrates the CON operation.
