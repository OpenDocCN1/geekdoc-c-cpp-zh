# # EXPERT SYSTEMS
PRINCIPLES AND PROGRAMMING
FOURTH EDITION

The new edition of this market-leading text builds upon the blend of expert systems theory and application established in earlier editions. The first half of the book concentrates on the theoretical base of expert systems, and offers a broad overview of Artificial Intelligence and its relation to expert systems. The second half of the text focuses on application, with the introduction of the CLIPS expert systems tool, and its new object-oriented language, COOL. Students will learn to use COOL to develop entire expert systems using rules and objects. All chapters end with an extensive problem set designed to reinforce knowledge.

# ## Features

+   - Provides thorough coverage of expert systems theory and rule-based applications

# ## About the Authors

Joseph Giarratano is a Professor of Computer Science at the University of Houston Clear Lake. As a consultant to NASA, he participated in the development of the expert system tool CLIPS that is included with this book. In addition, he is the author of more than ten texts and thirty research papers.

Gary Riley graduated from Texas A&M University in 1984 with a B.S. in Computer Science. He worked at NASA for eleven years where he developed the rule-based features of the CLIPS expert system language. He currently resides in Texas and works for PeopleSoft, Inc.
