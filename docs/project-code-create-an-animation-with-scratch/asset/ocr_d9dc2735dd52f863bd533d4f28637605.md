# MAKE FUN BACKDROPS

Characters and action are important in animation, but the background plays a big part in bringing it all to life. As with the sprites (see pages 12-13) you can get creative making your own backgrounds. Either choose a background (called "Backdrops" in Scratch) from the Backdrop Library, paint your own background using the creative tools, select a background that you already have on file on your computer, or click on the camera and see what your computer is seeing.

# It's behind you!

Different settings really change the mood of your animation. Think about what kind of background will fit with the story of your animation. An underwater background won't fit with a story set in a forest. A really crazy animation might need you to make a funky background to go with it if there's nothing suitable in the library.
