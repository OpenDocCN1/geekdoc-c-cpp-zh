In a similar manner, **Ricardo Miranda Zúñiga's Vagamundo: A Migrant's Tale** highlights the experiences of illegal Mexican immigrants in New York City through a transportable arcade-style interaction. The artist describes the game as a “mobile public art” project. Using a battery-powered ice cream cart containing a computer, joystick, and monitor, he takes the immigration game into the urban neighborhoods.

In ***Vagamundo***, players stand alongside the cart (figure 6.14), looking down at the computer screen as they control and play the role of the central game character called “Cantinflas.” Cantinflas, a character from classic Mexican cinema, engages in slapstick humor while attempting to overcome obstacles to get ahead in the game. After sneaking across the border in a standard arcade style, the player must avoid flying liquor bottles in order to get a job.
