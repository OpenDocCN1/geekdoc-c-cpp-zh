Along these lines, Souzis designed and implemented a TAZ where participants would agree to not use their cell phones, in order to liberate participants from what she saw as a demanding, and at times oppressive, technology (figure 6.11). In this game, also conducted in New York City, Souzis required participants in a specific space and time to follow a set of rules—which, ideally, they determine themselves.

Participants wear “cell-phone free” badges to mark themselves as nodes in the zone, and move through space wearing them.
