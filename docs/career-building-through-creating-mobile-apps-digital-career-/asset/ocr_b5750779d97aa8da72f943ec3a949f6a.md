# APPRENEURS MAKE MOBILE APP RICHES

Compared to other professions, mobile app development is relatively new. This means that top developers may not have formal developer backgrounds. Some come with a long list of credentials and achievements in related fields. As an ATM software designer, Steve Demeter took to designing apps in his spare time. He had been inspired by attending an iPhone conference in the summer of 2007. He believed Apple was opening a world of opportunity to developers when it released the SDK. This allowed third-party apps to be downloaded onto mobile devices, and Demeter knew this experience could open the door for him into the budding industry.
