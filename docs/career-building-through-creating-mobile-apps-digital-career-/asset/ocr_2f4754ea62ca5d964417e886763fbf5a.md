# CHAPTER FIVE

# THE APP WORLD
AWAITS

Twenty years ago, the most established form of communication was the home phone. It was a landline, and its only features consisted of call waiting or voicemail. Today, millions of people rely on their mobile phones to not only communicate with others, but also to access information from online consulting as part of their job to ordering a large pepperoni pizza with extra cheese. Entertainment, business, pop culture, and retail are just some of the options available, thanks to an ever-changing mobile app market. Because of this, highly qualified and passionate developers are needed more than ever before.
