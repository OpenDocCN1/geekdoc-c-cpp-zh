### Starting a Career

Teen app developers face many options once they graduate from high school. Some decide to use their developing experiences as building blocks for college applications. They may want to develop skills with university studies in computer science or software engineering. Because public and private universities are looking for well-rounded students, admissions staff look favorably on a developer's ability to be well balanced, take initiative, and participate in extracurricular activities. You want to stand out, and learning how to develop mobile apps is a good way to do that. If you have an interest and talent in development, then jump in and start developing.
