Monetization companies use in-app advertising to maximize your mobile earnings. While they offer low cost-per-thousand impressions (CPM) rates, monetization companies bring recognition to new apps. Besides AdMob, there are a number of other mobile ad networks in the industry, each with its own packages and payment structures. These include AdWhirl, iAd, InMobi, Jumptap, Millennial Media, Mojiva, StartApp, and Tapjoy.
