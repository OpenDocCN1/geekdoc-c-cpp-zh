# Notable Algae

| Division | Representative Genera | Ecological Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Chlorophyta | Chlamydomonas, Chlorella, Ulva | Primary producers in freshwater and marine ecosystems |
| Phaeophyta | Macrocystis, Laminaria, Fucus | Major components of kelp forests, provide habitat |
| Rhodophyta | Porphyra, Gelidium, Corallina | Red algae, important in coral reef ecosystems |
| Bacillariophyta | Diatoms (e.g., Navicula) | Major phytoplankton, produce oxygen |
| Dinoflagellata | Gonyaulax, Alexandrium | Some cause harmful algal blooms (red tides) |
