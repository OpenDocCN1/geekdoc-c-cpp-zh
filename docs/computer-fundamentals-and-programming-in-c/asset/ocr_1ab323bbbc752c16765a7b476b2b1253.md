4. \((X+Y)(\overline{X}+Y+Z)(\overline{X}+Y+\overline{Z})\)

**Solution:**

\((X+Y)(\overline{X}+Y+Z)(\overline{X}+Y+\overline{Z})\)
\(=(X+Y)((\overline{X}+Y)+Z)((\overline{X}+Y)+\overline{Z})\) (Associative)
\(=(X+Y)((\overline{X}+Y)+(Z\cdot\overline{Z}))\) (Distributive)
\(=(X+Y)(\overline{X}+Y+0)\) (Complement)
\(=(X+Y)(\overline{X}+Y)\) (Null)
\(=(X\cdot\overline{X})+Y\) (Distributive)
\(=0+Y\) (Complement)
\(=Y\) (Null)

There are two instances in this problem that require the use of the distributive property of ‘+’ to simplify the problem. Another way to solve this problem is to use the principle of duality:

\((X+Y)(\overline{X}+Y+Z)(\overline{X}+Y+\overline{Z})\)

The dual of the expression above is:

\(XY+\overline{X}YZ+\overline{X}Y\overline{Z}\)

After simplifying this expression and obtaining an answer, the dual of the answer has to be taken so that the simplified form of the original expression is obtained.

\(XY+\overline{X}YZ+\overline{X}Y\overline{Z}\) simplifies to \(Y\)

So,

Dual \((X+Y)(\overline{X}+Y+Z)(\overline{X}+Y+\overline{Z})\) = Dual \((Y)\)

The dual of \(Y\) is still \(Y\), hence,

\((X+Y)(\overline{X}+Y+Z)(\overline{X}+Y+\overline{Z})=Y\)

5. \(X+XYZ+\overline{X}YZ+\overline{X}Y+W+XW+X\)

**Solution:**

This example is long and uses many of the axioms and theorems discussed. A term has also been duplicated (see line 9).

\(X+XYZ+\overline{X}YZ+\overline{X}Y+W+XW\)
\(=X+(X+\overline{X})YZ+\overline{X}Y+(W+W)X\) (Distributive)
\(=X+(1)YZ+\overline{X}Y+(1)X\) (Complement)
\(=X+YZ+\overline{X}Y+X\) (Identity)
\(=(X+X)+YZ+\overline{X}Y\) (Associative)
\(=X+YZ+\overline{X}Y\) (Idempotent)
\(=X(1)+YZ+\overline{X}Y\) (Identity)
\(=X(Y+\overline{Y})+YZ+\overline{X}Y\) (Complement)
\(=XY+X\overline{Y}+YZ+\overline{X}Y\) (Distributive)
\(=XY+\overline{X}Y+YZ+\overline{X}Y\) (Idempotent)
\(=XY+\overline{X}Y+YZ+\overline{X}Y\) (Associative)
\(=X(Y+\overline{Y})+YZ+(X+\overline{X})Y\) (Distributive)
\(=X(1)+YZ+(1)Y\) (Complement)
\(=X+YZ+Y\) (Identity)
\(=X+Y(Z+1)\) (Distributive)
\(=X+Y(1)\) (Identity)
\(=X+Y\) (Identity)

6. \((X\cdot(\overline{X}Y))+(Y\cdot(\overline{X}Y))\)

**Solution:**

\((X\cdot(\overline{X}Y))+(Y\cdot(\overline{X}Y))\)

\(=(X\cdot(\overline{X}+Y))+(Y\cdot(\overline{X}+Y))\) (De Morgan’s Law)
\(=(XX+X\overline{Y})+(YX+Y\overline{Y})\) (Distributive)
\(=(0+X\overline{Y})+(YX+0)\) (Complement)
\(=(XY)+(YX)\) (Null)

7. \(XY+\overline{X}Z+YZ\)

**Solution:**

Here, the expression needs to be expanded to obtain a simpler solution.

\(XY+\overline{X}Z+YZ\cdot1\)
\(=XY+\overline{X}Z+YZ\cdot(X+\overline{X})\) (Identity)
\(=XY+\overline{X}Z+XYZ+\overline{X}YZ\) (Complement)
\(=XY+\overline{X}Z+XYZ+\overline{X}YZ\) (Distributive)
\(=XY+XYZ+\overline{X}Z+\overline{X}YZ\) (Commutative)
\(=XY+XYZ+\overline{X}Z+\overline{X}YZ\) (Commutative)
\(=XY(1+Z)+\overline{X}Z(1+Y)\) (Distributive)
\(=XY(1)+\overline{X}Z(1)\) (Identity)
\(=XY+\overline{X}Z\) (Identity)

8. \(X\cdot X\)

**Solution:**

This is the dual of the idempotent theorem that was proved; the equality is true because of the duality principle.

\(X\cdot X\)
\(=X\cdot X+0\) (Null)
\(=X\cdot X+(X\cdot\overline{X})\) (Complement)
\(=X\cdot(X+\overline{X})\) (Distributive)
\(=X\cdot(1)\) (Complement)
\(=X\) (Identity)
