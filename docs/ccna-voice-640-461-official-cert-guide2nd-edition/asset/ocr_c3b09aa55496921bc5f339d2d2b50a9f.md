Look at the configuration of ephone-dn 50 in Example 7-6. Designating a call park extension is as simple as using the park-slot command over ephone-dn configuration mode.

**Note:** When planning to configure call park, keep in mind that each parked call slot consumes an ephone-dn slot (regardless of single- or dual-line configurations). You may need to increase the number of ephone-dns (max-dn) that your CME deployment supports.
