Note: If you are synchronizing your router's clock via NTP, do not enter the time-webbed-command, to ensure that the time remains to the more accurate NTP server.

From the supported web browser, enter the http://<CME_IP_Address>/ccme.html to access the CME GUI. After authenticating your web admin account, the CME management console is displayed, as shown in Figure 7-24.
