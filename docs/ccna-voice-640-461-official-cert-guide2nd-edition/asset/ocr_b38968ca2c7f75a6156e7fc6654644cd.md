# ## Describe How to Provide End-User Support for Connectivity and Voice Quality Issues

Troubleshooting a complex application like CUCM can be challenging. The scope of CCNA Voice (ICOMM) requires us to keep it relatively simple, so by no means should be considered a comprehensive CUCM troubleshooting manual. In this chapter, you learn how to troubleshoot basic IP Phone registration issues and look at the extensive reporting capabilities of the CUCM system, which you monitor the health and well-being of the servers in your deployment.
