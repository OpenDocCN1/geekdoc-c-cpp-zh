# About the Authors

Jeremy D. Cioara, CCIE 11727, is a senior voice engineer consultant in Atlanta, GA. He has more than ten years of Cisco experience, with the last five years dedicated to VoIP; he has worked with all the VoIP products. He has been a Cisco instructor for 12 years, starting with basic courses and now teaching Unified Computing, Nexus switching and other data center technology. Jeremy lives in Florida with his wife and three kids.

Mike Valentine, CCNA, CCNP, CCVP, is the founder of Boson Software and training. Currently focused on cloud technologies using VBLOCK Infrastructure Platform, his company has published over 500 apps for the iPhone. John created the Cisco Press CCNA simulator, the Boson Netsim, and numerous practice tests. He is also focused on mobile technology.

Jeremy D. Cioara, CCIE 11727, holds a Bachelor of Arts degree from the University of Columbia. As an author, he has written many Cisco Press titles. As an instructor, he has demystified VoIP for hundreds of students. Jeremy also casually blogs about Cisco topics.

# About the Reviewers

Brion Washington, CCNA, is a senior voice engineer consultant in Atlanta, GA. He has more than ten years of Cisco experience, with the last five years dedicated to VoIP; he has done large projects involving VoIP, from complete network design, implementation, to the last level. He is currently finishing up his CCVP.

John Swartz, CCIE No. 4426, is the founder of Boson Software and training, He served as technical editor and contributor on several Cisco Press titles. He has worked with all the Cisco VoIP products.
