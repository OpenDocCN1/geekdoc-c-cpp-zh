The enterprise permission configuration page Inter-Presence Group Subscription policy defines the default setting for whether Presence Groups have permission to watch each other Presence status or not; this setting may be overridden in the Presence Group settings. Members of a Presence Group are always able to watch other members of the same group, unless the Subscribe CSS prevents the subscription. The Subscribe CSS and Presence Group settings be used independently of one another or together.
