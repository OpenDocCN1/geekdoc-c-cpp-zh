The signaling and voice bearer stream paths are the same as in the single-site deployment; the difference is simply that the branch phones send all signaling traffic over the WAN to the CUCM server, along with non-RTP voice streams to IP phones at other locations. However, if a branch phone calls another phone, both phones signal to the CUCM across the WAN, and the RTP voice streams stay local to the branch—direct from phone to one, always.
