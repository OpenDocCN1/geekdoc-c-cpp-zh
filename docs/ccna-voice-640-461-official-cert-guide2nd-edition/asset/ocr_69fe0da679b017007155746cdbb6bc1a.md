# Common CME Management and Troubleshooting Issues

If it worked right the first time every, none of us would have jobs! This chapter discusses how to handle questions and troubleshoot Cisco Unified Communication Manager Express (CME). The chapter is divided into the three major areas of troubleshooting typically encountered on production networks: IP phone registration issues, dial-plan issues, and Quality of Service (QoS) issues.
