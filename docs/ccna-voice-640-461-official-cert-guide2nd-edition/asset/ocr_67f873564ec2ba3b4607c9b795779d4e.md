# Managing Endpoints and End Users in CUCM

IP Phones and End Users are important parts of a Unified Communications deployment; after all, without phones or people to use them, what is the point of having the system? This chapter reviews the configuration of endpoints and users in CUCM, including up basic network services, registering phones, configuration, and bulk administration.
