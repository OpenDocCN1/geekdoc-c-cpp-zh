- **Other group pickup:** You can pick up a ringing phone in another group by pressing the GPickUp button and entering the other group number when you hear the second dial tone.

## Configuring Intercom

Intercom configurations are common in traditional phone systems. This feature allows an administrative assistant and executive to work closely together by having a speakerphone “tether” between them.

Technically, the intercom deploys is through a speed-dial and auto-answer speed-dial configuration. If the administrative assistant presses the button configured as an intercom, it dials the executive's phone, which auto-answers the call on muted speakerphone. To establish two-way communication, the executive deactivates mute (by pressing the M button). Understanding this helps the intercom configuration much clearer.

To configure intercom functionality, you must configure two ephone-dns, one for each side of the intercom connection. These intercom lines should be assigned a number, just like any other ephone-dns. However, to prevent others from accidentally (or purposely) dialing the intercom and ending up on muted speakerphone for a random IP phone, the number should be something users cannot dial from other IP phones. The configuration in Example 7-8 achieves this objective.

## Configuring Intercom

Intercom configurations are common in traditional phone systems. This feature allows an administrative assistant and executive to work closely together by having a speakerphone “tether” between them.

Technically, the intercom deploys is through a speed-dial and auto-answer speed-dial configuration. If the administrative assistant presses the button configured as an intercom, it dials the executive's phone, which auto-answers the call on muted speakerphone. To establish two-way communication, the executive deactivates mute (by pressing the M button). Understanding this helps the intercom configuration much clearer.

To configure intercom functionality, you must configure two ephone-dns, one for each side of the intercom connection. These intercom lines should be assigned a number, just like any other ephone-dns. However, to prevent others from accidentally (or purposely) dialing the intercom and ending up on muted speakerphone for a random IP phone, the number should be something users cannot dial from other IP phones. The configuration in Example 7-8 achieves this objective.
