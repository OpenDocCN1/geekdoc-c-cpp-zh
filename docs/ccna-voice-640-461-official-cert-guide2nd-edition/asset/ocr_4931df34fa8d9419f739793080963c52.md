# CCNA Voice 640-461
Official Cert Guide

The official study guide helps you master all the topics on the CCNA Voice exam, including

+   * Voice and data convergence
* IP phone connections to the LAN infrastructure
* Cisco Unified Communications Manager
* CUXM IP phone configuration and voice productivity features
* Gateways and trunks
* Cisco Unity features
* Smart Business Communications System
* UC500 series for voice

CCNA Voice 640-461 Official Cert Guide is a best-of-breed Cisco exam study guide that focuses specifically on the objectives for the CCNA Voice ICOMM exam. Senior voice consultants and trainers Chiara and Michael Valentine prepare preparation hints and test-tips, helping you identify areas of weakness and improve your knowledge and hands-on skills. Materials are presented in a concise manner, focusing on increasing your understanding and performance.

"Do I Know Already" quizzes open each chapter and to help you know how much time you need to spend on each section. Chapter-ending Exam Preparation Tasks help you on key concepts you must know thoroughly.
