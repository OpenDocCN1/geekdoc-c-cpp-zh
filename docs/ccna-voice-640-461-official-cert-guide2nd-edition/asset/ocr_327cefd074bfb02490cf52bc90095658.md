To understand the benefits of H.450.3, you must first understand what happens with typical
VoIP forwarding. When a call enters the network and hits a forwarded device, that de-
vice takes responsibility for the call and becomes a tandem hop in the call flow. How
means that the voice traffic now forwards through the IP phone that forwarded the call.
This can cause quality problems if the device that forwarded the call is a large geography-
distance away from the phone receiving the forwarding call. The H.450.3 standard rep-
sresents a method that allows the CME router to redirect the call directly to the final
destination instead of acting as a tandem hop. Figure 7-8 illustrates this concept.
