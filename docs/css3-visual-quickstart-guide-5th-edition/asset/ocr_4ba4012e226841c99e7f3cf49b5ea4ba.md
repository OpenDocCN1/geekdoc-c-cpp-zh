# Determining the Cascade Order

Within a single Web page, style sheets may be linked, imported, or embedded. Styles may also be declined in the HTML.

In addition, many browsers allow visitors to have their own style sheets that can override yours. It's guaranteed, of course, that simultaneous style sheets from two or more sources will have conflicting declarations. Who comes on top?

The cascade order refers to the way styles begin at the top of the page and, as they descend, collect and replace each other as they are inherited. The general rule of thumb is that the last style defined is the one that is used.

However, at times, two or more styles will conflict. Use the following procedure to determine which style will come out on top and be applied to a given element.
