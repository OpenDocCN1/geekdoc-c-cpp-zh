Suddenly she came upon a thrice three-legged table, all made of solid glass; there was nothing on it except a tiny golden key, and Alice's first thought was that it belonged to one of the doors of the hall; but alas, either the locks were too large, or the key too small, but at any rate it would not open any of them. However, on the second round, she came upon a curtain which had not noticed before, and behind it was a little door about fifteen inches high; she tried the golden key in the lock, and to her great delight it fitted!

Alice opened the door and found that it led into a small passage, not much larger than a rat-hole; she knelt down and looked along the passage to the loveliest garden you ever saw.
