# When Will HTML5 Be Ready?

It's important to note that many people are concerned that HTML5 is still a "Working Draft." Widespread adoption is not predicted until 2012, and the final "Proposed Recommendation" will not be finished until 2022. (Yes, that's over a decade from now.) The skeptics throw their hands and assume that it will be some time before HTML5 is relevant. That date is really misleading.

Many browsers currently support the important HTML5 properties, and the 2012 date is actually much faster than the adoption periods of some previous standards. The 2022 date is distant, but it is when all browsers will have adapted all the HTML5 properties.

Practically speaking, HTML5 is here now and will grow in over the next few years until it becomes the main game in town.

HTML5 is ready for prime time, if you are ready to use it.
