### Chapter 1. Down the Rabbit-Hole

Alice was beginning to get very tired of sitting by the bank, and of nothing to do or see until she saw the rabbit pull into his waistcoat-pocket, and then out a watch; 'How strange! I have no watch,' said Alice without pause for conversation;

She was so caught in her own world (as well as she could, for the best she had done all day) that the mere thought of making a daisy chain would be worth the getting up and picking daisies, while she wondered if Rabbit with eyes that could run.

Alice started to follow him, for curiosity had seized her so intently that she ran out of the house, down the garden, across the field, and she was just in time to see him pop down a large rabbit-hole under the hedge.

In another moment Alice was falling, never even considering how strange the world she was getting into.
The Rabbit-hole went down a tunnel for a long time, and then dropped so abruptly that Alice had not even a moment thinking about herself before she fell tumbling down a very deep well.
