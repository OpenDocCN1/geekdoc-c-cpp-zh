# TABLE 4.2
Parametric Resonance

| Excitation Frequency | First Harmonic | Natural Frequency | Submultiples of First Harmonic |
|---|---|---|--|
| | $\omega_e = 2\omega_0$ | $\omega_e = \omega_0$ | $\omega_e = \frac{2\omega_0}{n+1}$ |
| Subsection (s) | 4.3.13–4.3.14 | 4.3.15–4.3.17 | 4.3.19 |
| without damping | frequency range | $\omega_e - 2\omega_0 < \delta_0 = \omega_0 h \left(1 - \frac{h}{8}ight)$ | $\omega_0 - \delta_- < \omega_0 < \delta_+$, $\delta_- = \frac{5\omega_0 h}{6} > \omega_0 = \delta_+$ | $\omega_e - \frac{2\omega_0}{n+1} < \delta_n$, $\delta_+ - O(\omega_0 h^{n+1})$ |
| maximum of characteristic exponent | $b_0 = \frac{\omega_0 h}{2}$ | $2b_1 - O(\omega_0 h)$ | $2b_n - O(\omega_0 h^{n+1})$ |
| with damping | frequency range | $ar{\delta}_0 = \sqrt{\omega_0^2 h - 4\lambda^2}$ | $ar{\delta}_1 = \sqrt{\omega_0^2 h} - \lambda$ | $ar{\delta}_n = \sqrt{\omega_0^2 h^{2n+2}} - 4\lambda^2}$ |
| maximum of characteristic exponent | $b_0 = \frac{\omega_0 h}{2} - \lambda$ | $ar{b}_1 - O(\omega_0 h) - \lambda$ | $ar{b}_n - \omega_0 h^{n+1} - \lambda$ |
| threshold amplitude | $h_0 > \frac{2\lambda}{\omega_0}$ | $h_1 > \frac{2\lambda}{\omega_0}$ | $h_n > (\frac{2\lambda}{\omega_0})^{1/(n+1)}$ |

> Note: The parametric resonance occurs for a linear oscillator (damped or undamped) with oscillating support excited at the frequency of: (i) the first harmonic, that is, twice the natural frequency; (ii-iii) any submultiples, (ii) the natural frequency or (iii) lower, with reduced magnitude.
