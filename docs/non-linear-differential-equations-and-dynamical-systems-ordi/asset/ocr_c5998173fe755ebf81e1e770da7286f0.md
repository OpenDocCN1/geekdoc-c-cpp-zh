# 4.3 Variable Coefficients and Parametric Resonance

The linear second-order differential equation with constant coefficients (4.37a, b) has two generalizations: (i) coefficients depending on time for variable systems, where example periodic coefficients (section 9.3), leading to parametric resonance (section 4.3) and (ii) non-linear systems and equations (sections 9.1–9.2), leading to a variety of phenomena hysteresis and bifurcations (sections 4.4–4.9). The parametric resonance occurs in the absence of forcing for second-order mechanical system (electric circuit) whose the mass (induction), damping (resistance) or spring (capacity) depend on time.
