An example of limit cycle oscillation is the case of competing populations, for example: (i) herbaceous animals $x$ with an unlimited supply of food; (ii) carnivorous animals $y$ that can only feed on the herbaceous. The limit cycle occurs (a) because: when there are too many herbivores they are easy to find and the carnivorous population increases; (b) a large carnivorous population cannot be sustained by a declining herbivorous population. Thus the carnivorous and herbivorous population decrease and increase in opposition with some time lag between the extrema (b).
