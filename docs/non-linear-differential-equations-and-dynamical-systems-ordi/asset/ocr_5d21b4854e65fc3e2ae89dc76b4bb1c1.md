# Bibliography

The bibliography of “Non-linear Differential Equations and Dynamical Systems”, that is the second book of volume IV “Ordinary Differential Equations with Applications to Trajectories and Oscillations”, and sixth book of the series “Mathematics and Physics Applied to Science and Technology”, adds the subject of “Non-linear Differential and Integral Equations”. The books in the bibliography that have influenced the present volume the most are marked with one, two, or three asterisks.
