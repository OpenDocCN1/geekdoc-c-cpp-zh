The quadratic (cubic) term in the restoring force of a non-linear, undamped, unforced oscillator corresponds to a cubic (quartic) term in the potential [Figure 4.9a(b)] that: (i) has a flexion point (at a minimum) at the origin, so that the average position is shifted to the side of lower potential (at the origin); (ii) does not (does) contribute to the potential well at the origin created by the linear restoring force or quadratic potential, and thus does (not) change the oscillation frequency of the non-linear oscillator relative to the natural frequency of the harmonic oscillator, and (iii) creates harmonics stretching at the double (triple) of frequency, that is, the first (second) harmonic.

and (iv) the first harmonic is out of phase −π/4 relative to the fundamental:

```
cos(2ω_t) = sin[2ω_t + π/4].
```

The next second order of non-linearity O(e²) will be affected by the quadratic and cubic terms in the restoring force (subsections 4.5.3–4.5.4).
