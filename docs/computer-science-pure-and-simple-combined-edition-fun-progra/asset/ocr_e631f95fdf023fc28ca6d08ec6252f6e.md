What is happening the dog is walking along, changing shapes once and then jumping. In order to get it to walk more than it jumped we made thesize larger (100). But this ruined the animation because I am not calling forward very often. What I really want is for the dog to walk along many steps while changing, and to jump. We need to revise our walk procedure so that we can control both the step size and the quantity of steps. Let's add another parameter (variable) to our walk procedure.

```
to walk :stepsize :qsteps
  repeat :steps
    [forward :stepsize
      wait 1|
end
```

Qsteps stands for quantity of steps. Now we can easily control the size of each step and the quantity of steps with each call to walk. Change Main to make use of this new power and try your program.
