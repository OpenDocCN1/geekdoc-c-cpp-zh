# Who's on first?

In MicroWorlds, the most recently hatched turtle goes in front of any other turtles. Also the turtles are automatically named t1, t2 and so on. In our case, this is not the order we want. The only way I know of changing this is to just accept it and change turtles. In other words, we will make our dog t2 and our pond t1. To see what a particular turtle's name is, right-click on the turtle.
