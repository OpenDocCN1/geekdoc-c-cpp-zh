Make your list of commands in English on paper first. When you can pretend to be a turtle and follow them, it's time to translate into Logo and type them into the Command Center to see if they work. When they do, copy them and paste them onto the Procedures Page. Make procedures called **move.to.door** and **door**, and call them from **house**. When your program draws a house with a door, show your teacher. Save your work using **<ctrl>** s under the name you already gave it.
