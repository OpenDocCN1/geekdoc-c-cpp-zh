# Decide on the Goal

We want our turtle to wander aimlessly. Let's have the procedure handle just one step in the turtle's aimless wander, and be be called repeatedly. The procedure would slightly change the direction the turtle is going and then take one step in that direction. (Can you think of different plans for doing this?)

# Sandbox to Acquire Necessary Knowledge
