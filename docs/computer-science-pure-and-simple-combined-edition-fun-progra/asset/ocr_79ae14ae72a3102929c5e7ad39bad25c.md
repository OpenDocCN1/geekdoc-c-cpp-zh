Ninety? Enough times to go full circle? Experiment until your turtle is drawing a star. Remember repeat has two inputs: a number of times to repeat, and a list of commands to repeat. You have just learned that there are 360 degrees in a circle, and each degree is a tiny turn for the turtle.

Now, about going through the turns to draw a figure. Let’s suppose YOU are a turtle, and the floor of the room you are in the screen. If you are facing straight ahead and then you turn yourself to face the right, you have turned 90 degrees.

Choose three endpoints for a big equal-sided triangle on the floor (for example, a chair, the door jamb, and a table leg). Walk along one side of the triangle, and then turn yourself the required angle to get to the next side. Notice you have turned more than 90 degrees! In fact, you have turned 120 degrees. Go to the next corner. Turn 120 degrees. Walk to the next corner. Turn 120 degrees. You are now facing the same way you started, so you have turned a total of 360 degrees, or full circle. You have done all the turns that a turtle would do if it were drawing an equilateral, or equilateral, triangle.
