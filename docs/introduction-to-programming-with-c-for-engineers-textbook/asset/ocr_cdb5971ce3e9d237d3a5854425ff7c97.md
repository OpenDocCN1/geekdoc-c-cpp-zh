# ## Table 7.3 Shift operations explained.

\begin{tabular}{ccc}
Shift type & Description and examples & Diagram \\
Logical shift left & Bits are shifted left by a specified number. The leftmost bit(s) is lost. The rightmost position is filled with a 0 bit. & \\
Logical shift right & Bits are shifted right by a specified number. The rightmost bit(s) is lost. The leftmost position is filled with a 0 bit. & \\
Arithmetic shift left & Like the logical shift left. Note that the sign can be lost. & \\
Arithmetic shift right & The sign bit is copied to preserve the sign. & \\
Rotated shift left & Bits are shifted left, and the leftmost bit is inserted into the rightmost position. Usually available in the microprocessor's machine code. Sometimes additional bits are involved in the chain. & \\
\end{tabular}
