# What games are available?

As you might expect for an experience about involving you directly into the game, virtual reality games are – for the largest part – first-person. Now there is a broad variety here since first-person viewpoints can fit into a number of genres, from shooters, racing games, platformers, puzzle games and even rhythm action games where you hit notes to the beat of the music. That last one, for example, includes a game called Beat Saber, which is essentially a game about swinging lightsabers to a funky beat; as you might expect, it’s pretty fun. Alternatively, there are shooters like Hover Junker or the truly inventive Superhot VR, which has the novel gameplay mechanic of time only moving when you do, making for some cool bullet-dodging moments.
