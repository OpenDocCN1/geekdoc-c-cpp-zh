# PART 5 Monitors

Buying a monitor for gaming is even more arduous than buying a new TV; while you’re looking at all the same tech details – such as contrast ratio – you now also want to pay more attention to refresh rate and response time. What this amounts to is a lot of confusing numbers and no real certainty that the one you’ll get is the best; hopefully we can dispel some of those concerns. Further questions are derived from how you want your set-up to look: do you want to run multiple screens? A single larger one? A wider screen with an unusual aspect ratio? There are too many options, too many variables to consider, so we’ve picked out the key facets you need to know about buying the best PC gaming monitor.

# What to look for

Aspect ratio – Gaming monitors come in all shapes and sizes, and aspect ratio – or the width of the screen versus the height – is a large reason for this. But as gaming dimensions have become a little more standardised over

the years, 16:9 is the standard to aim for. This will be the same as your TV, most likely, and if you’re not too fussy about how much of your games you can see then it’s easier just to stick with this.
