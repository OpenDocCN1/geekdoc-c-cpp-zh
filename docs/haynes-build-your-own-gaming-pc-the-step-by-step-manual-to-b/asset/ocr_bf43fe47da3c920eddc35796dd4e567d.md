# PART 3 Installing the CPU

# 1
Prepare the case

The worst first move would be to screw your motherboard into the case without first installing the most important – not to mention delicate – components of the entire system. Start by taking the panels off the case of the PC, typically through two thumbscrews at the back of the case on either side. Then remove the motherboard from its packaging and rest it on a sturdy, well-lit table.

# 2
Unlock the CPU socket

There are either one or two levers on either side of the CPU socket; push down and away from the socket on these one by one to release them. Pull them up and clear of the socket. This will release the load plate, which in turn can be lifted to free the CPU socket's protective cover. Note the four alignment keys on the naked socket in all four corners and the triangular mark in one of the corners.
