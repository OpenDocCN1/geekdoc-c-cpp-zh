# PART 5 Headsets and microphones

To get the best audio from your PC gaming it might seem counter-intuitive to use a headset, but the personal audio space is a much more immersive experience than even the best PC-compatible stereo systems. Buying a headset isn’t quite as littered with jargon as most other accessories, but there are some features you’ll want to keep an eye out for. As ever it’s about asking the right questions. Do you want the most detailed sound quality? Do you want the clearest microphone and chat options? Are you planning on streaming? It’s a minefield of options, but the first step is to know what you’re after.
