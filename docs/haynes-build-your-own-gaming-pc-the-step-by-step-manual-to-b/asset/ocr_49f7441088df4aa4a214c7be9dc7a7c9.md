Acceleration – Most mice will have some form of acceleration, a feature whereby the cursor will move faster the faster you move the mouse itself. It can be beneficial in some circumstances, such as if you have a smaller mouse mat or desk space to enable smaller movements of the mouse itself. However, acceleration is generally disliked by many gamers since it means you’re not getting the exact one-for-one control over the cursor and there’s an element of unpredictability about cursor movement. It’s unlikely it’ll be too heavy-handed in gaming mice, and can usually be disabled.
