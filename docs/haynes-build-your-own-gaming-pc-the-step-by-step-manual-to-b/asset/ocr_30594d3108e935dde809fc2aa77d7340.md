# Go through defragmentation

Hard drives – by their very nature – can become a little confused. With so much data writing and rewriting going on in even a single session on your PC, months of use can cause your files to become fragmented, slowing your PC down as it has to search for information in a wider number of places. It's a healthy process to go through defragmentation – called disk optimisation on Windows 10 – to analyse your hard drives and recalibrate the data so it can be more easily found.
