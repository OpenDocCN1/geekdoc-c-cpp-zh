5. In an ancient land, the beautiful Eve had many suitors. She decided on the following procedure to determine which suitor she marry. First, all of the suitors were lined up one after the other and assigned numbers. The first suitor be number 1, the second number 2, and so on up to the last suitor, number n. Starting at the first suitor, she would then count three suitors down the line (because of the three letters in her name) and the third suitor would be eliminated from winning her hand and removed from it. Eve would then continue, counting three more, and eliminating every third suitor. For example, if there were six suitors, the elimination process would proceed as follows:

+   123456 Initial list of suitors, counting from 1
12456 Suitor 3 eliminated, continue counting from 4
1245 Suitor 6 eliminated, continue counting from 1
125 Suitor 4 eliminated, continue counting from 5
15 Suitor 2 eliminated, continue counting from 5
1 Suitor 5 eliminated, 1 is the lucky winner
