# Introduction

File I/O makes a small but essential use of inheritance, which is not covered until
Chapter 14. However, we have placed this chapter before the inheritance chapter
 programmers often want to start using file I/O early. Therefore this chapter\includes a brief introduction to what fine inheritance details are needed for file I/O.

You may cover this chapter anytime after covering the material of Chapters 1 to 4
and 6 to 9; in other words, you may cover this chapter before Chapters 5, 10, and 11.

continuity. The basic elements of file I/O, may be covered anytime after reading Chapters 1 to 4, Chapter 6, and the subsection of Chapter 9 entitled "The Member Functions get and put." That subsection is selfcontained does not require reading any other parts of Chapter 9. All of Section 12.2,
except for the subsection entitled "File Names as Input" may also be read after reading

Functions get and put."
