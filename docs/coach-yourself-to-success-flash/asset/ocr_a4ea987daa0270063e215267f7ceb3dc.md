such as learning some new skills or getting exposure to people that can help your career. Always consider the true advantages of helping out – advantages for you as well as for the person requesting the help.

+   * How much time do I want to spend on this project?

If you decide you want to help out, and you've considered how much work could be involved, measure this against how much time you are willing to spend on the project. Think about clever solutions to achieve the advantages of the situation within the time you are willing to allocate.

When you've addressed these issues, you can say yes or no to any request knowing that you are still in charge of your own time and still catering for your own needs.
