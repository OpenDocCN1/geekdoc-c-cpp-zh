The first part of the answer to the question “Why did I never do this before?” is that in many cases people simply don’t take the time to look closely enough at what needs to be done and what the first steps on the way to implementing change need to be.

The second, and perhaps more crucial, part of the answer is that possibly there haven’t been compelling enough reasons to take the time to plan and make the necessary changes. If the idea of making changes to your life doesn’t excite you enough, chances are that the upside of making these changes isn’t yet big enough. Now that you know the areas to address and the actions to take, all you need is a good enough reason to put the changes into practice. Change for the sake of change is difficult to sustain, and making changes that someone else thinks would be good for you is an uphill struggle because your heart simply won’t be in the task. To make successful changes and guarantee great results, you need to know what matters to you and why these things really matter. To help you clarify your thoughts in this area, take a look at the following questions:

# Uncovering personal motivation 1 – what do you really want?

+   * If you could have anything in life, what would you have?
+   * If you were living your ideal life, what would you be doing
+     * every day?
+     * every week?
+     * every month?
+     * every year?
