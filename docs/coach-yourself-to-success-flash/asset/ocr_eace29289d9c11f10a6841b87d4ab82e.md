# ## How to make sure you live by your mission statement every day

Keep your mission statement close to hand at all times. Either commit it to memory and repeat it regularly, or write it down and make sure that you read it as often as you can. You can put as much detail as you like into your mission statement, and it will evolve over time. Keep it concise because living by a mission statement that is short and to the point will simplify every decision you make.

# ## The values of a mission statement

A mission statement works because it is a short summary of what you value in life and what matters to you. Examples of core values that you may hold dear are honesty, integrity, generosity and kindness. Everyone has a value system, and being aware of your core values is a sure-fire way to guarantee that you act in accordance with them at all times.
