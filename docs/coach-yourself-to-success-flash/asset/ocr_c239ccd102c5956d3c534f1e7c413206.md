Time is a precious commodity and we're all striving to be as efficient as we possibly can be every single day. Achieving consistently great results requires an ongoing engagement with how you choose to allocate your time, how you prioritize, and how you set yourself up with the resources and assistance you require to run your life effectively. This chapter explores how to achieve the right mindset for success and examines how to develop a sense of purpose that will ensure you're in the best position to mobilize all the help at your disposal to keep your life moving forwards at a pace that suits you. The techniques outlined in Chapter 5 ensure you never waste time on non-urgent activities that distract you from attending to what really matters in life. This chapter also describes how to get everything you need done without having to do it all yourself.
