# A sense of purpose

![](bbox=[0.04, 0.09, 0.97, 0.34])

When you have achieved the desirable situation of being in full control of your life and your time, you will set about everything you do with a sense of purpose. You will experience renewed vigour and a new-found effectiveness. This purpose will be decided by your mission statement and what's important for you to achieve. With it comes a liberating feeling that you are in charge, making the decisions that matter and pushing life along in the right direction.
