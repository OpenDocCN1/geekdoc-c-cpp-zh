What’s magical about the process is that, while it enables you to draw positives from the higher scores, you can also see the close relationship between some of the areas that you’ve scored low in. There will be common themes in these areas that can be addressed simultaneously with new thoughts, behaviours and actions – when you’re thinking about what changes you’d like to make to your life, focus on the areas in which you’ve scored lowest to begin with. Improving your rating from four to eight in a selection of areas will bring you a greater feeling of success than improving any single score from nine to ten.

Take a look at where you’ve scored lowest and consider the following questions for each area in turn.

# ### Instant progress questions

+   1. What would have to happen in this area of my life for me to be able to score myself higher?
+   2. What would I like my rating out of ten to be in this area?
+   3. By what date could I improve my rating in this area?
+   4. What’s the first action I need to take to begin raising my score in this area of my life?
+   5. When can I take this first action?
