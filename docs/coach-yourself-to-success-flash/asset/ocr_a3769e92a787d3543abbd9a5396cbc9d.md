# # Setting your objectives

Everyone has a different take on what constitutes short-, medium- and long-term planning, so for the following exercise you may want to adjust the time deadlines a little to suit the planning schedule that works best for you. Generally, short term should mean two to eight weeks, medium term six to twelve months and long term eighteen months and beyond.
