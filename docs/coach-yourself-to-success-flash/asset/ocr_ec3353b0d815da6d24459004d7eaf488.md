+   - What does success mean to you? Not to society or to your friends, family or those around you, but what does it mean to you? What would be your greatest success in life?
+   - What single thing would make you truly happy?
+   - If you were to live your life again, knowing what you know now, what would you do differently?

Too many times people get tied up doing what they think they ought to do or what their family or friends think they should be doing. They often get drawn into doing what they think society expects of them rather than focusing on what they would really like to be doing. The important thing for you to pay attention to right now is what you want and why you want these things.

# What drives you?

Now you have some ideas of what you really want in life, it is important to pinpoint why you want these things and how you can best motivate yourself to achieve them. To help you do this, you must establish what currently motivates you, what drives you to success, and what gets you out of bed each day. What are your guiding lights, your mantras, the thoughts and dreams that flow through your head creating your personal choices and sustaining your mission in life?
