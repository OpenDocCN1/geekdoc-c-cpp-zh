# Interpreting Contracts

Interpreting a cloud computing contract may involve a new area of expertise for IT staff. As always, consulting legal counsel, collections management, or administrative staff can be helpful. Some key areas to focus on in SLAs and terms of service include these:

+   - Duration of contract and termination procedures: What constitutes subscription and termination? How are renewals handled?
+   - Payment methods: What payment methods are supported, and what payment terms are extended? What is the procedure for termination if payment lapses?
+   - Legal jurisdiction: What jurisdiction and laws apply to the contract, and what limits are placed on issues of arbitration and liability?
+   - Indemnification, warranty, and service assurance: What warranties does the provider agree to and how do these assurances relate to the SLA? What liabilities does the provider recognize?
+   - Privacy policy: Under what conditions are customer data made available to external organizations?
+   - Data ownership and retention upon service discontinuation: Who owns data located on the cloud service, and what happens to that data when the service agreement is discontinued?
+   - Service integrity: What does the provider define as a complete service, and how does it assure that its service remains operational?
+   - Data disclosure and service monitoring: Does the provider regularly disclose data regarding customers? Does it monitor traffic, stored data, or processes?
+   - Data and service location: Where are the data housed by the cloud provider physically located? What implications does this have for your organization’s adherence to organizational policy and legal regulations?

A thorough exploration of these topics would easily fill a chapter, but good resources include policy papers by Jaeger et al. (2008, 2009) and Bradshaw et al. (2010).
