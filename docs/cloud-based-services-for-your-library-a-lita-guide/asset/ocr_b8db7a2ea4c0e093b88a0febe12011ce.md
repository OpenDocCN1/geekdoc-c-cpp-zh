Specifically, the TAM model provides a structure for understanding the individual, social, and technological context surrounding technology adoption. In contrast, the TOE model provides a framework for understanding the interaction of organizational, technological, and external (to the organization) factors.

Previous studies have also used these models to explore cloud adoption in educational and technology environments. For example, the TAM model has been used to examine the factors that influence cloud computing adoption in community college systems (Behrend et al. 2011), and the TOE model has been used to explore the organizational factors that influence the adoption of technology in different contexts (Daylami et al. 2005). The inclusion of both models helps frame the research in terms of the participant’s perceptions of cloud computing and situates their use of cloud computing within the organization. The analysis of survey responses looked for elements of each model, including personal views of ease of use/value (i.e., TAM) and personal views of environmental, organizational, and technical factors (i.e., TOE) in relation to four computing platforms.
