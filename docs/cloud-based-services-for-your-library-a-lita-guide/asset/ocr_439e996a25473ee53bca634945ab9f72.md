# Perceptions of Cloud Computing and Virtualization

In addition to exploring the landscape of cloud computing adoption, the survey also explored the participants’ view of the potential impact of cloud computing and virtualization. Participants were asked to indicate their agreement with two statements about both cloud computing and virtualization: “Cloud computing/virtualization is a current priority of the administration,” and “Cloud computing/virtualization has the potential to transform library IT in the short term.” Figures 2.2 through 2.5 show participant responses for these questions.

The data from these questions show a largely positive view of the impact of both cloud computing and virtualization. Likewise, survey participants largely viewed cloud computing and virtualization as priorities of the administration. There was some variation in participant views of the priority of cloud computing and virtualization, indicating that participants are bullish on cloud computing and virtualization but more positive about the latter.

# Motivating and Inhibiting Factors of Cloud Computing and Virtualization

The survey also collected qualitative data on the motivating and inhibiting factors related to the use of cloud computing and virtualization resources. For each series of qualitative responses, codes were generated from responses and then mapped onto the survey analysis framework. Statements were coded into TAM areas (value and ease of use) when the participant stated a perception (e.g., nonvirtual is easier) as opposed to a situation (e.g., current IT policy inhibits cloud migration).
