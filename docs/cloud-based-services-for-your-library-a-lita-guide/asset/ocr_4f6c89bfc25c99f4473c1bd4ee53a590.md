An important strength of Amazon’s service is its seven different cloud regions, which make it possible for customers to implement a geographically separated cloud infrastructure. In fact, multiregion replication is a recommended practice for Amazon customers and is often offered as a component of Amazon’s managed services including Elastic Beanstalk and RDS. On the other hand, a potential limitation of Amazon is that its services are designed to be implemented in a self-service environment. Amazon does not provide the individualized service that librarians are used to receiving from SaaS providers, and they may find it difficult to get answers to questions and feel confident about adopting a service that lacks a personal level of customer service. In response, Amazon has implemented basic support levels that come bundled with cloud services in addition to premium service levels that operate on a monthly subscription model. The four levels of support include an enhanced SLA that defines support request response time, types of support activities, and scope of systems supported. The Amazon premium support costs, ranging from $49 per month to over $15,000 per month, illustrate why IaaS services tend to be cheaper than PaaS or SaaS services.

# Linode

Linode provides an IaaS service similar in scope to many of Amazon’s services (figure 5.2). Like Amazon, Linode offers different server levels, from 512 MB to 20 GB RAM. Unlike Amazon, which charges for resources by the hour, Linode charges on a monthly basis. Linode nodes (or servers) come with a specified level of included storage and CPU resources and can be deployed in one of six zones in the United States, Great Britain, or Japan. Linode uses a customized language to deploy and manage servers using the Xen virtualization platform. All Linode nodes include premium support services. Unlike some other IaaS providers discussed in this chapter, Linode offers automated backup and restoration services as well as node-balancing services. By facilitating the process of backup and balancing, Linode helps users capitalize on cloud features with little required knowledge or effort. In addition to backups, Linode offers add-on services that include extra computing resources (e.g., RAM, hard-disk space, bandwidth, or IP addresses). It supports internal cloning of servers and features configuration profiles to help manage server builds.
