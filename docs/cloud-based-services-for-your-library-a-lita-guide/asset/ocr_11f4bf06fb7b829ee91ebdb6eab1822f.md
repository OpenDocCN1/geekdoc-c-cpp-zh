digital preservation systems require a consideration of digital object preservation features, network bandwidth, and long-term platform viability. In selecting both a platform and a provider, libraries must be aware of the balance of responsibility that comes with different solutions and be prepared to migrate to new systems in the future. In turning to cloud providers, libraries must also focus on the creation of an efficient and economical cloud infrastructure. In a highly outsourced or SaaS environment, for example, libraries may find that they cannot avoid duplicating resources or overbuying capacity (e.g., paying for extra disk space on multiple platforms or paying support for multiple web service front-end systems). In the coming chapters we explore these issues and see specifically how libraries can both benefit from the advantages and mitigate the risks that come with cloud computing adoption.
