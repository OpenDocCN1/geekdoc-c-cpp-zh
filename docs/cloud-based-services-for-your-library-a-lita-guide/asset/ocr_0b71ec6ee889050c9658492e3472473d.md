Participants were also given the option of including additional system classes. Based on their selections of one of the four virtualization categories for system types in general, participants were asked to comment on enabling and inhibiting factors that influenced their selection. Participants were not asked specifically about systems but rather generally (e.g., “You indicated that some services operate in an internally virtualized environment…”).

# RESULTS

In total, the survey received 111 responses. All questions in the survey were optional, so response totals for each question may differ. The survey data were grouped into three main areas: demographic information, quantitative results on cloud computing adoption, and qualitative results on cloud computing adoption.

Survey data were coded with guidance from the framework represented in figure 2.1 but were open to addition and interpretation based on participant-generated themes.
