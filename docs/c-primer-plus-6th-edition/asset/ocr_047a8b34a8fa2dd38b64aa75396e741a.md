# Abstraction and Classes

Life is full of complexities, and one way to cope with complexity is through simplifying abstractions. You are a collection of more than an octillion atoms. Students of the world would say that your mind is a collection of several semiaautonomous agents. But it's much simpler to think of yourself as a single entity. In computing, abstraction is the crucial step of representing information in terms of its interface with the user. That is, in terms of the softball statistics example, the interface describes how the user initializes, updates, and displays the data. From abstraction, is a short step to the user-defined type, which in C++ is a class design that implements the abstract interface.
