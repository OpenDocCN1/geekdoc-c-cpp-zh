**Infinitely Many Solutions.** Row reduction of $A$ gives one of three possibilities. First,

$$\begin{bmatrix}1 & b_1/a_1 & c_1/a_1\\0 & 0 & 0\end{bmatrix}$$

which yields solutions $(x,y)=(a_1t,c_1-b_1t)$ with arbitrary $t$. Second, if $a_1=0$ and $b_1\ne0$, then row reduction gives

$$\begin{bmatrix}0 & 1 & d\\0 & 0 & 0\end{bmatrix}$$

and the solution is $(t,d)$ for arbitrary $t$. Third, if $a_1\ne0$ and $b_1=0$, then row reduction gives

$$\begin{bmatrix}1 & 0 & d\\0 & 0 & 0\end{bmatrix}$$

and the solution is $(d,t)$ for arbitrary $t$.

**No Solution.** Row reduction gives

$$\begin{bmatrix}1 & h & d\\0 & 0 & e\end{bmatrix}$$

with $e\ne0$. Then there is no solution as $0x+0y=e\ne0$ is impossible.
