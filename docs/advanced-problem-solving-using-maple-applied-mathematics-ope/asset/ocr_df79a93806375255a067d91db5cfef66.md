# 5.1 Introduction

In the mid-1800s, a large number of ironwork bridges were constructed as railways crossed the continents. One of the popular designs for trusses, rigid triangular support structures, was the Warren truss with vertical supports.¹ The center span of the 1926 Bridge of the Gods (Figure 5.1) over the Columbia River is a nice example.
