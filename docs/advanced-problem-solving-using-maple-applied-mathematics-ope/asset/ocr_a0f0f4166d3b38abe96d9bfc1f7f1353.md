Let \(I\) be the identity matrix. We can solve for \(\mathbf{x}\) as follows:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\mathbf{x} &= C\mathbf{x}+\mathbf{d}\\
\mathbf{x}-C\mathbf{x} &= \mathbf{d}\\
(I-C)\mathbf{x} &= \mathbf{d}\\
\mathbf{x} &= (I-C)^{-1}\mathbf{d}
\end{aligned}
\]

If the economy produces 900 million dollars of petroleum, 300 million dollars of textiles, 850 million dollars of transportation, 800 million dollars of chemicals, and 750 million dollars of manufacturing, how much of this production is internally consumed by the economy?

Use Maple to enter the augmented matrix \(L=\langle C\mid\mathbf{d}\rangle\) and row reduce it. Remember to load *LinearAlgebra* first.

```
```text

We interpret the unique solution as the amounts the sectors need to produce to meet the total demand:

+   - petroleum: \(x_1=6944.2\)
+   - textiles: \(x_2=1070.0\)
+   - transportation: \(x_3=5620.6\)
+   - chemicals: \(x_4=6629.8\)
+   - manufacturing: \(x_5=2234.3\)
