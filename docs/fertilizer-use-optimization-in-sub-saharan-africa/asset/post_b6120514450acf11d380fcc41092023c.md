Soil test P is considered with the assumption that most fields have sufficiently low soil test P that the probability of response is high but if soil test results indicate adequate P, then the P application should be withheld from that land parcel and applied elsewhere or the money reallocated. The soil test K assumes that generally soil K availability is adequate and the FOT recommendation is followed, but if soil test results find very low K availability, some application of KCl is advised.

In the highland AEZ, with the exception of bean, crops had good responses to applied N and P (Table 9.5a). Maize responded well to applied K and S. In the mid-elevation and upland plateau AEZ and in the lakeshore and the Shire River Valley AEZ, maize again responded well to N, P and S but not to K (Table 9.5b,c). Bean was more responsive to N than in the highlands.
