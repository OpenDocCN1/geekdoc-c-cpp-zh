# Table 7.3: An example of a paper tool
Western Kenya Upper (>1400 m) Fertilizer Use Optimizer

The below assumes: Calibration measurement is with i) a 5 ml water bottle lid (lid) that holds about 3.5 g urea and 5.5 g DAP and MOP, ii) a 500 ml water bottle of 5 cm diameter cut to height of 4 cm has approx. 80 ml to hold 56 g urea, and 88 g DAP and MOP. Row spacing: maize, Irish potatoes, sweet potatoes and climbing bean have 75 cm: bean at 50 cm; wheat at 25cm. Grain values per kg (Ksh): 25 maize, 30 Irish potatoes, 30 sweet potatoes, 50 rice, 60 bean, 30 wheat. Cost of using 50 kg fertilizer (Ksh): 2850 urea; 3600 DAP; 3600 MOP. Application rates are in kg/ac. Minimum rates are 10 kg/ac.

### Level 1 financial ability.

| Crop | Details |
|---|---|
| Maize HP (>3t) | at planting band 10 kg DAP (1 lid for 2.2 m) |
| Irish potato | at planting band 60 kg DAP (1 lid for 0.4 m) and 20 kg MOP (1 lid for 1 m) |
| Sweet potato | at planting band 39 kg DAP (1 lid for 0.6 m) |
| Maize-bean intercropping | at planting band 19 kg DAP (1 lid for 1.1 m) and side dress by banding 15 kg urea at 6 WAP (1 lid for 0.9 m) |
| Climbing bean | at planting band 28 kg DAP (1 lid for 0.8 m) |

The recommendations for each crop in this level have somewhat similar potential for profit from fertilizer use.
