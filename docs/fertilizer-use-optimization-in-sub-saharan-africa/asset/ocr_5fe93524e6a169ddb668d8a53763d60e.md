The y-axis shows net returns to investment for the nutrient applied. For each curve, the profit potential of the nutrient applied to a crop is displayed. The steeper the curve, the higher the net rate of return to investment. The slope decreases with higher investments, but profit is increasing if the slope is still upward. At the peak of a curve, the point of maximum profit per hectare is reached; this rate is often referred to as the economic optimum rate (EOR).
