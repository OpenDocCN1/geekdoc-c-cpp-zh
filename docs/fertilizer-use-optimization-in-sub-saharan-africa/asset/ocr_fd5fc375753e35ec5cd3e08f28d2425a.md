There are several mathematical formulas that are used to model fertilizer response. Creating an equation to represent crop response to a fertilizer input allows economic analysis to determine the return on an investment over a range of prices and costs. In this case the curvilinear to plateau yield response to applied nutrient is represented by the equation Y = a – bc^x where Y is yield, a is yield at the plateau, b is the yield increase due to application of the concerned nutrient, c is a determinant of the shape of the response curve, and r is the nutrient application rate.
