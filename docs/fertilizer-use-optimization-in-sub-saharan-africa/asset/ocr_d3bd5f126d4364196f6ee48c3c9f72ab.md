Therefore, farmers need access to single nutrient fertilizers to maximize profit from fertilizer use. An NPK blended fertilizer may supply one or two nutrients that have an economical response, but paying for the unneeded nutrients reduces the farmers' financial ability to use more fertilizer nutrients that have high profit potential.
