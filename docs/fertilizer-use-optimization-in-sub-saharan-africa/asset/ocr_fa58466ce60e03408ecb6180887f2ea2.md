# 11.7 Adjusting fertilizer rates in consideration of other practices and soil test information

The Excel and paper versions of the FOTs gives optimized recommendations for fertilizer use for within the farmer’s context. However, these recommendations need to consider other practices that affect nutrient availability (Table 11.4). When soil test information is available, fertilizer rates may be adjusted. For example, the application of farmyard manure or compost at 2.5 t/ha is expected to result in increased yield but also increased response to fertilizer on upland soils. Therefore, fertilizer rate should not be reduced. However, application of high quality dairy or poultry manure justifies a fertilizer rate decrease and reallocation to another field. If Bray-1 P is above 12 ppm, recommended fertilizer P should not be applied to that field.
