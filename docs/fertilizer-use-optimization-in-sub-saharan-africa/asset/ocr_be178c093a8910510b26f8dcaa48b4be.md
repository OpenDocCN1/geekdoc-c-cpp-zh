The below assumes:
Calibration measurement is with: a FARO water bottle lid (FARO) that holds about 6.3 g urea, 10 g SSP and KCl, 9 g NPK 15:15:15, 11 g NPK 20:10:10, and 14 g ZnSO₄; and with a GINO tomato can (GINO) of 70 ml to hold 49 urea, 77 g SSP and KCl; 165 g ZnSO₄, 70 g NPK 15:15:15, and 84 g NPK 20:10:10.
Planting: Maize, sorghum pulses are planted at 75 cm x 25 cm; pearl millet 1 x 1 m; cassava 1 x 1 m.
Crop values: Naira/kg 50 maize, 60 sorghum, 67 rice, 56 pearl millet; 120 unshelled groundnut; 165 cowpea, 120 soybean and 20 cassava.
Fertilizer use costs for Naira for 50 kg: 5500 Urea; 4500 SSP; 7000 MOP; 6000 for NPK 15-15-15 and 20-10-10; and 2000/kg for ZnSO₄,.
Broadcast width: 2.5 m; WAP = weeks after planting, WAT = weeks after transplanting. Application rate is in kg/ha.
