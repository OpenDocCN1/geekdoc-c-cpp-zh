The below assumes:
Measurement for calibration is with a: Oriba bottle cap (Oriba) of 7 ml: holds about 4.9 g urea, and 7.7 g DAP, TSP or KCl.
Row spacing: 80 x 40 cm for maize and sorghum; 100 x 100 cm for pearl millet; 20 x 20 cm for rice; 80 x 50 cm for cowpea; and 40 x 20 cm for groundnut.
Grain prices per kg (FCFA): pearl millet 160; sorghum 170; maize 180; rice 280; groundnut unshelled 180; cowpea 200.
Fertilizer use costs per 50 kg bag (FCFA): urea 13,500; TSP 18,000; DAP 20,000; KCl 15,000; SSP 12,500 and NPK (15-15-15) 13,500.
Bandwidth width: 2.0 m; DAS=Days After Sowing; DAT= Days After Transplanting.
