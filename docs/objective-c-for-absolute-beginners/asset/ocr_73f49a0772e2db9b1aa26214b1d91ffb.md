# Downloads

> Read Me Before Downloading
- iOS 5 beta can be installed using the over-the-air update from devices that have iOS 5 beta or later. Ensure that the software is installed by checking Software Update.
- Be sure to back up your device using iTunes (10.5 beta or through) or iCloud prior to installation. If you want to leave iOS 5 beta, you must restore from your iTunes/iCloud backup.
- When using iTunes or iPad to generate backups from iOS 5 beta (7), you must follow the first instructions in the Release Notes.
- This version of iOS is only available for devices registered with iOS 5. Attempting to install this version of iOS 5 on an incompatible device might cause your device to become unstable and could lead to data loss.
- Devices on iOS 5 beta may not be upgraded to later versions of iOS 5. They can be upgraded to future beta releases and the final public release.
