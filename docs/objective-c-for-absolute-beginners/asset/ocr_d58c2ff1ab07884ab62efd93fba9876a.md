# The Formula for Success

Learning to program is an interactive process between your program and you. Just like learning to play an instrument, you have to practice. You must work through the examples and exercises in this book.
Understanding the concept doesn't mean you know how to apply and use it.

You will learn a lot from this book. You will learn a lot working through the exercises in this book. However, you will really learn when you debug your programs. Spending time walking through your code and trying to find out why it is not working the way you will. an unparalleled learning process. The downside of debugging is a new developer can find it especially frustrating. If you have never thrown your code out the window, you will question why you are failing to debug and whether you are smart enough to solve the problem. Programming is very humbling, even for the most experienced developer.

Like a musician, the more you practice the better you get. By practicing, you can do some amazing things as a programmer. The world is your oyster. Seeing your app in the iTunes App Store is one of the the most satisfying accomplishments. However, there is a price, and that price is time spent coding and learning.

Having taught more than a thousand students iOS development, we have put together a formula for what makes students successful. Here is our formula for success:

+   Believe you can do it. You will be the only one who says you can't do this. So don't tell yourself.
Work through all the examples and exercises in this book.
Code, and keeping code. The more you code, the better you'll get.
Be patient with yourself. If you're fortunate enough to have been a 4.0 student who can memorize material just by reading it, this will not happen with Objective-C coding. You are going to spend time coding.
You learn by reading this book. You really learn by debugging your code.
Use the free xcelMe com webinars and YouTube videos explained at the end of this chapter.
Don't give up!
