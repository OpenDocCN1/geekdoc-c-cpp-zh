# 11.8 Participant becomes agitated by a product's usability issues (cont.)

# How to avoid

+   At the beginning of the session, tell the participant that if she finds anything confusing or frustrating, you want to hear about it the product can be improved so others don't experience the same issues. Making sure the participant feels that she has permission to share her frustrations may reduce any agitation she feels later in the session.
