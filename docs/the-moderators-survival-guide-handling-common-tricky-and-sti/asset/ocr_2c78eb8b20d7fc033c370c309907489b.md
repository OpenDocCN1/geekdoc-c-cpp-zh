# 13.4 Participant flirts with you (cont.)

# What to say

If complimented in a way that you feel compelled to look at this area....

+   - “Thanks. Why don’t you go back to...<task/question>.”
- “Okay. Let’s go back to...<task/question>.”

If you need to redirect a flirtatious participant:

+   - “If it’s okay, I want to stick to the agenda for the session because we have a lot to get through. Let me bring your attention back to...."

If you’re having a colleague take over moderating duties for the session:

+   - “Okay <participant>, for the next part what I’m going to do is have my colleague come in and join you. She’ll be right in"

If the participant asks you before or after the session:

+   - “It’s very nice to meet you. But I have an obligation to keep our interaction on a professional level.\"

# What not to do or say

+   - Don’t yield to flirting or give the perception that you welcome that behavior! It’s important to draw the line and keep the session professional, especially if you’re on a site visit at someone’s home or if the participant is at your facility after hours when few people are around. Over friendly or flirtatious behavior can lead to bad things—physical danger or legal liability.
- Don’t embarrass the participant!

# How to avoid

+   - There’s no easy way to avoid a flirtaty participant, but if you notice a participant becoming more friendly or starting to flirt, adapt your style immediately to a more disengaged (less friendly) demeanor before it escalates to a more awkward situation.
