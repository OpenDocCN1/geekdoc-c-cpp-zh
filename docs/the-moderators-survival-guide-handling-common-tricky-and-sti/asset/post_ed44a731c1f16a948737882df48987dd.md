## Listen to stories from other moderators

The great thing about listening to stories from other moderators is that you can learn without needing to go through the experience yourself. It's not as powerful as experiencing it yourself, even in a practice setting, but learning vicariously gives you a framework for?

- During the session, the participant seemed upset about how poorly he was doing. I've seen other scenarios where the moderator offered to let the participant take a break when the participant gets upset. What do you think about that—is that ever appropriate?

## THE MODERATOR'S SURVIVAL GUIDE

If you feel that you have a strong relationship with the person you'd like to give feedback to and aren't worried about his reaction, you can try a more direct approach. But don't just giving him feedback; start by asking if he'd like to hear it:

- I had a chance to watch one of your sessions, and thought that you did a good job. There were a couple of things that I noticed while you were moderating. You have a lot going on, would you be interested in hearing my feedback?
