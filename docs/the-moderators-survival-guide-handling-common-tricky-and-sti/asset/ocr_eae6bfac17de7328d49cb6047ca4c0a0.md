# 9.6 Session interrupted accidentally by an observer or someone else (cont.)

+   If your session has remote observers, take advantage of options within your screen-sharing application or conference call system to automatically mute all attendees from the start (except for the participant, course; you may need to call her separately if she is remote). Also, tell observers ahead of time that they should mute their phones when they join the call—you can't be too careful
