# 4.3 Participant has an unexpected physical feature (cont.)

# How to avoid

+   Familiarize yourself with the adjustments that your facility and equipment can support. For example, most chairs and desks can be lowered or raised, other types of chairs are usually available from somewhere, and a mouse (and its control panel settings) can be moved from the right side to the left side.
