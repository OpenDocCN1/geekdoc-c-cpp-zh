# 7.1 Technical issues arise with your setup and/or equipment (cont.)

# What to say

+   - “Excuse me for a moment, but I just noticed that there is something wrong with our equipment. Let's take a short break so I can get it working.
- “Please bear with me as I try to get this working again. Sorry about this!"
- “We’re having a technical problem and I need to get one of our technical experts to come in and fix this. She'll need to use *the product*, and it may take a few minutes, so let's take a break. <Technical expert>, please come on in."

# What not to do or say

+   - Don’t freak out or panic!
- Try not to badmouth the makers of the equipment or software that you’re using. While you’re probably frustrated by this situation, maintain professional demeanor around the participant.
- Don’t blame the participant for anything that happened, even if the technical issue was caused by someone she did.

# How to avoid

+   - Test the technical setup beforehand. If the setup is complex, use a checklist of everything that needs to happen. That, if you need to restart or change something in the session, you can reference the checklist to make sure you've forgotten anything. This checklist should also include any URLs, user names, or passwords necessary. For more tips and discussion, refer to section 15.3.
- Verify the status of all your equipment before the session. Make sure any devices and rechargeable batteries are charged. Also, be sure you have additional batteries or battery packs.
- In cases where you have equipment or software problems that take time to resolve, it helps to have a generic questionnaire prepared to give to participants. For example, you could put together a survey asking the participant additional questions about her usage of the product and her experiences.
- If you use a screen-sharing application for observers to watch the session, consider having a backup account with a different service so if you have technical problems with one, you can quickly set up a new meeting using your backup service. Be sure to include information about this backup service on your checklist.
