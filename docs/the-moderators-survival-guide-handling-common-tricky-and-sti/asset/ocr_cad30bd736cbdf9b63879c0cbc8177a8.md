# 7.4 Remote participant drops off the call (cont.)

# What not to do or say

+   Don't say anything negative about the participant, as she may not really have been dropped from the call (e.g., she just be having audio problems) or may be dialing back in. You may not want to allow observers to speak on the call for the same reason.

# How to avoid

+   Provide the participant with instructions ahead of time on what to do if she gets dropped. For example, "I will call you again at this number."
