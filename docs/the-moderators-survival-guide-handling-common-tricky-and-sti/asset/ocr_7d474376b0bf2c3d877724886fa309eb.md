# 10.1 Participant starts going on a tangent (cont.)

# How to avoid

+   - Warn participants at the beginning of the session that you may be moving them along if you need to keep on task or make it through all of your questions. In a usability study, you may also set the expectation that you may not get through every task, depending on time.
