# 10.5 Participant gives vague responses to questions (cont.)

“Have you ever tried to do this before? Tell me about your experience.”

# What not to do or say

+   Try not to get frustrated with the participant, as it may lead him to become closed down and resentful.

# How to avoid

+   Craft your questions to provoke a thoughtful, detailed response. A poorly worded question or a question that can be answered with a simple “yes/no” may generate a vague and disinterested answer. For example, a participant may give a general response to “What do you think about your local bus service?” but will be more specific when asked “Describe the last time you used your local bus service.”
