Part 3 offers tips on how to minimize the chance of certain types of situations occurring and how to sharpen your moderating skills, no matter your experience level!

The appendices include a handy aggregated checklist of the most useful phrases a moderator can use during a session and a list of things that you can do before a session to minimize the chances of something unexpected or sticky happening. These checklists are also available on our website (http://www.modssurvivalguide.org).

Here is a detailed breakdown of what each part of our book covers:

# Part 1: Your Moderation Toolkit

Chapter 1: Moderation Matters: Power, Responsibility, and Style. This discusses the qualities that are most helpful for a moderator during a research session and the different moderating styles that you can choose depending on the needs of the session.

Chapter 2: In the Trenches: Six Steps for Handling Situations. This chapter provides a high-level view of the steps you should take to understand what type of situation the participant is dealing with and respond appropriately.

Chapter 3: Mix and Match: Your Moderation Patterns Toolbox. This chapter defines specific steps that you can take to change the course of a session and when each pattern works best.

# Part 2: Your Survival Mishaps

Chapter 4: Recruiting Participants You W't Expecting. This chapter covers situations where the participant does not fit your recruiting criteria or has some other feature that makes proceeding with a session challenge.

Chapter 5: Participant Misconceptions: Not What the Participant Was Expecting. This chapter covers situations where there is a mismatch between the participant's expectations for the sessions and you've planned.

Chapter 6: Some Guidance Required: Participants in Need of Sherding. This chapter covers situations where the participant or your observers are not fully engaged with the session or require some other type of guidance to ensure that you get the feedback you need.
