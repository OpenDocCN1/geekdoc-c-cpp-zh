* “You’re going to be giving feedback on a live <product> today. Because it's live

# What not to do or say

+   * Don't point out if the participant hasn't noticed it on her own.
* Avoid bothering the designers/developers for making the change.

# How to avoid

+   * Discuss your rules ahead of time with designers/developers. We typically ask our teams not to make any changes to the prototype or product without consulting ahead of time (and even then, we may not agree that the change should be made). It's also important to insist that no changes be made to the prototype during a session. Often a “quick” has unexpected consequences consequences. See section 15.3 for more on this topic.
* If using a website for the study, find out any A/B testing is done on the website and, if so, see if there is a way for you to have that functionality turned off during your research or to consistently see the same version of pages (e.g., always the A design).
* As part of your briefing, let the participant know she'll be using a live product so she might notice changes as she proceeds with the session. This warning can be especially useful if you’re unable to guarantee a design freeze.
