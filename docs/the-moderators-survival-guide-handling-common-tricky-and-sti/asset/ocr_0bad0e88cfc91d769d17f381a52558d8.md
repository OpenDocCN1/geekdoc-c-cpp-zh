# 13.5 Participant does something awkward or uncomfortable (cont.)

# What not to do or say

+   - Don't shield the participant or do anything to make her feel inadequate, or that would make her more nervous about participating.
- Don't laugh or make fun of the participant, even if her request or actions seem ridiculous to you. She may just be trying to be friendly, or is making herself comfortable.

# How to avoid

+   - It's hard to see these situations coming! Approach each session with a sense of humor so you can roll with the unexpected.
