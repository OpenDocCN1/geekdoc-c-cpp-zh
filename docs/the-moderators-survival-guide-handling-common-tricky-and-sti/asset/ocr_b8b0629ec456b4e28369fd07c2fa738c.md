# 11.1 Remote participant is obviously distracted (cont.)

# What to say

+   > "<Participant>, is now there's something a good time for you?"
> "It sounds like there's something else going on. Do need to take a quick break before we continue with <task/question>?"\n> "There seem to be a number of things competing for your attention now. Are you able to continue with this study? If not, that's fine, and we can reschedule."
> "I know you're very busy, I appreciate you taking the time to give us feedback."

# What not to do or say

+   - Hide your frustration or annoyance with the participant. Be polite and respectful.

# How to avoid

+   - Be upfront about your expectations for the session so the participant can reschedule any competing priorities and plan accordingly. But, recognize that this may not be enough and that interruptions could still come up.
- During your recruiting process, ask the participant for the “best” time to conduct the session, and schedule her session for that time. Check with her day before to ensure that the scheduled time still works.
