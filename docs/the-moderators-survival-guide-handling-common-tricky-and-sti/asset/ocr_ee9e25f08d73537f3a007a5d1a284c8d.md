# 10.7 You don’t have time to complete everything (cont.)

+   - “I’m interested in hearing more about this. I want to make sure we cover everything we have planned, so if there’s time, let’s come back to this at the end of the session"

If you know that you need to move quickly:

+   - “I just did a time check, and we’re running a bit low on time. We have a lot of <questions/tasks> that I’d like to get your feedback on, so I may just push you along at times so that we can cover as much as possible."

+   - “For the remaining tasks, we’re going to move faster than we have before. For each of the following tasks, show where you would click first"

If the participant feels bad about not getting through at the end of the session:

+   - “Don’t worry about that; I didn’t expect us to get through everything. Your feedback was very important and helpful. I’m glad we spent the time that we did."

# What not to do or say

+   - Don’t rush participants through tasks or questions for the sake of getting everything done. Adjust and prioritize what you need to do based on the time available so they don’t leave the session feeling like they were rushed.

+   - Don’t panic. If you’re concerned about time, try not to let it show. This, of course, comes with practice but it’s good to be mindful of it.

# How to avoid

+   - Run a session with a participant to make sure that you have an accurate sense of timing. Of course, you’ll always get circumstances that result in a time crunch, but at least you can adjust the things that are under your control.

+   - As you put together your study plan, identify which questions/tasks are the highest priority. This will help you adapt the session if necessary as discussed in section 15.2.
