# 12.4 Participant tells you something personal (cont.)

# How to avoid

+   - For research involving sensitive topics, such as using a website about cancer, consider whether you need to recruit participants who have firsthand experience with the topic. In some cases it may make sense to do so, but in others you may want to actively screen out participants who are likely to experience distress because of the subject matter (e.g., family members of someone who died of cancer).
