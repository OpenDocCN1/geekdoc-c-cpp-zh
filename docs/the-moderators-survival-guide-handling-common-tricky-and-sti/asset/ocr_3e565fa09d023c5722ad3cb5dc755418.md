# 14.5 You notice a bad smell or have an allergic reaction (cont.)

+   - “I know you warned me about this ahead of time, I’m having trouble handling the smell in. Here is another location we can move to for a little bit?”

# What not to do or say

+   - Try not point out a smell unless it's something that's expected from the kind of location you're in (e.g., a factory or manufacturing facility). Doing so would be rude and potentially insulting.

# How to avoid

+   - We wish there was a way to avoid this situation, especially when it comes to contextual inquiry, there’s only so much you can do. For site visits, you can ask ahead of time what kind of environment to expect, but these kinds of olfactory issues may come up. There’s no way (that we know of) to avoid participants with poor personal hygiene.

+   - If you have known allergies (e.g., to cats or dogs), be prepared and have medication with you at all times.
