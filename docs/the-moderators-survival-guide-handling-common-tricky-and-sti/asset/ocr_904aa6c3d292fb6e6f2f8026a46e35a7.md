# THE MODERATOR'S SURVIVAL GUIDE

![](bbox=[0.295, 0.093, 0.805, 0.345])

**Figure 1.1** As the moderator, you're balancing a lot of competing needs during the session.

During a session, your reactions as a moderator must balance the following competing needs (Figure 1.1):

+   * The participant's emotional and physical states. Is she nervous or uncomfortable? Is she looking like she is unwell or experiencing physical discomfort?
* Your emotional and physical states. Are you uncomfortable with anything happening in the session? Are you reacting to an allergen in the environment? Is there an external circumstance occurring that might threaten your physical safety or of the participant's?
* The study's research goals. Are you measuring an experience, looking for issues that need to be fixed, or trying to understand the participant's process?
* General session management needs. Are your remote session technology and equipment working correctly? Are your observers behaving well, especially if they're in the same room as you?
* Protection of your organization. Are you ensuring the participant leaves with a positive experience so she doesn't say things about your organization on social media? Are you doing your best to get feedback from participants that will help improve the experience for future users of your organization's products or services?

Throughout a session, this balance may shift you, as the moderator, have to adjust accordingly. The way you handle a situation at the beginning of a session can be very different than how you handle the same situation later on, even with the same participant.
