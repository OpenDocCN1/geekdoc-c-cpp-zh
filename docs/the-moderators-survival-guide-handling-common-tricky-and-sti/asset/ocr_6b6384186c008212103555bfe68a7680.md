# 9.6 Session interrupted accidentally by an observer or someone else

> At some point during the session, someone accidentally intrudes the study space. The intrusion may be in person (e.g., someone coming into your research space without realizing it's occupied) or remote (e.g., an observer doesn't realize that her phone is not muted, or puts you and the other observers on hold and now everyone hears music).

Method[s]: Any
Frequency: Occasional
Pattern(s) to apply: Take responsibility, Reassure the participant

# What to do

For an in-person interruption:

+   - Get the interrup out of the room as quickly as possible. If the interrup is lost or looking for something in particular, take a moment to help her or direct her to someone who can.
- Apologize to the participant for the interruption, and let her know that you don't anticipate any more interruptions going forward (hope for the best).

If the interruption is during a remote session:

+   - Remind all observers to put their phones on mute.
- If you're using screen-sharing software (e.g., in WebEx, by right-clicking/contextual-clicking on the observer's name and choosing Mute). If you can't find this functionality, you should be able to eject the observer from the meeting, which will disconnect her audio. Conference calling systems have a similar ability to mute everyone or except certain attendees.
- If you can still hear the observer (e.g., if she has stepped away from her phone and you can hear her background conversation), as a colleague to send an email reminding all observers to mute their phones. You may also need to pause the session for a few minutes, especially if the noise is very loud. This pause may give the disruptive observer a chance to get back to her desk and realize what is happening.
- If you have access to another conference call number, you can ask anyone to join a new call instead. If the participant is remote, be sure to give the new information to her, or let her know that you'll call her directly to conference her in (depending on your setup).
