Finding the right balance of these needs can be difficult and stressful, especially if you’re new to moderating. There are times when you’ll feel pressure to help the participant—it can be painful to watch their struggle—but you must resist that and maintain neutrality so that the data you’re collecting are unaffected. On the flip side, you’ll feel pressure from the stakeholders to stick to your plan but you’ll need to deviate from plan to comfort an upset participant.

# 1.3 The session ringmaster

Empathy. Flexibility. Creativity. Sense of humor. Aura of authority. To successfully handle tricky and sticky situations while moderating, you must embody and balance all these qualities. You may recognize these skills as being similar to that of a circus ringmaster. The ringmaster actively listens to monitors everything that is going on around her, directs the audience’s attention, and controls the overall pacing and flow of the show.
