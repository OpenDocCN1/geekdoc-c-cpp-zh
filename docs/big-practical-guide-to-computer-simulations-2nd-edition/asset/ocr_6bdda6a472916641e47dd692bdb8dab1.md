labels, you can also choose a Precision. This and other fields of this tab you can leave at their standard values here. Nevertheless, you should also adjust the Char size of the axis labels (tab Axis label & bar) and of the tick labels (tab Tick labels). For publications, character sizes above 150% are usually well readable. Note that in the Axis label & bar tab, there is a field Axis transform where you can enter formulas to transform the axis more or less arbitrarily, see the manual for details. All tabs have many other fields, which are useful as well, but here we stay with the standard choices. Note that sometimes the Special tab is useful, where you can enter all major and minor ticks individually.
