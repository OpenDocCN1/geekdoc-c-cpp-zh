Alexander Karl Hartmann is Professor of Computational Physics of the Department of Science at the University of Oldenburg (Germany). He obtained a M.S. in Computer Science at the University of Hagen and a M.S. in Physics at the University of Duisburg. After receiving his Ph.D. in Theoretical Physics at the University of Heidelberg, he worked at the University of Göttingen, the University of California in Santa Cruz and the Ecole Normale Supérieure in Paris. In 2007, he moved to Oldenburg. His research interests comprise computer simulations, disordered systems, combinatorial optimization, large deviations and Bioinformatics. Professor Hartmann is author or co-author of 8 books and more than 120 scientific publications.
