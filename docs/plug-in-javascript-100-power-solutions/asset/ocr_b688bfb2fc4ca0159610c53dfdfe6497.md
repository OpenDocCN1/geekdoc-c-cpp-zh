# Reflate()

This is the companion plug-in to Deflate(). With it, you can expand a deflated object back to its original dimensions over a specified period of time, with a choice of three different animation types. In Figure 6-4, a div has been added to the example in the Deflate() plug-in with which you can deflate or replate the objects.
