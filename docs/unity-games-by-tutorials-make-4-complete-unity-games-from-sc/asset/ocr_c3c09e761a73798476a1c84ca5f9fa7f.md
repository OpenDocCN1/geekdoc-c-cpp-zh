# # Chapter 20: Virtual Reality
By Eric Van de Kerckhove

For centuries, humans have been fascinated by the idea of virtual worlds taht they could step into: An escape from reality where anything is possible. From panoramic paintings that were so large they gave the illusion of actually stepping into them, to stereoscopic viewers, primitive head mounted displays and Nintendo’s Virtual Boy, people have been searching for a portal to the land of endless imagination for a long time.

![](bbox=[0.358, 0.483, 0.639, 0.638])

Virtual reality has become an accessible technology in the last few years. Individuals can be transported into virtual worlds and interact with its inhabitants from the comfort of their homes using modern state-of-the-art head-mounted displays (HMD) and unique techniques to accept user input. Two especially popular devices are Oculus Rift and HTC Vive, both offering experiences never seen before.

In this chapter, you’ll learn how to use this bleeding edge tech in Runestife, which will get you ready to use it in your own projects. You’ll transform Runestife into a VR game, and learn how to use Oculus Rift and HTC Vive with Unity.
