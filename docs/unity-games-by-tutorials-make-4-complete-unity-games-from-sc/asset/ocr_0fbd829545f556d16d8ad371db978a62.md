# # Chapter 18: Making A Tower Defense Game
By Eric Van de Kerckhofove

In a tower defense game, you build up defenses to protect yourself from advancing waves of enemies. To do this, you build and upgrade towers or other defensive elements that either damage or slow down the enemies as they pass by.

![](bbox=[0.313, 0.423, 0.686, 0.596])

There are early examples of these kinds of games. A 1990 called Rampart, wherein you placed cannons that would automatically attack incoming enemies, for example. In the Fort Condor minigame from the 1997 classic Final Fantasy VII, the player had to put down units to defend the fort. With the release of the popular custom maps for Warcraft III, the genre became extremely popular with lots of modern titles in all kinds of flavors coming out across all platforms.

In this chapter you'll learn to:

+   - Use manager classes to build all sorts of stuff such managing the state of the game, the amount of enemies to produce, and the amount of waves to send at a player.
- Use utility scripts to make your life easier so you can prepackage various types of animations to use in other games.
- Get enemies to spawn and move around on a path while your hero reacts accordingly
