Time to fill up these fields! Expand ViveRig and Controller (right) to expose the ControllerCamera. Drag this to the ControllerCamera field. Also drag Controller (right) to the Right Controller field.

| Controller (right) | Vive Controller Look Module (Script) |
|---|---|n| Model | ViveControllerLookModule |
| ControllerCamera | None (Camera) |
| Camera (head) | Right Controller None (Steam VR_Tracked Oje) |

Now drag the Reticle and Laser to the Reticle and Laser fields, respectively.

| Reticle | None (Game Object) |
|---||
| Laser | None (Transform) |

Select World Canvas and drag the ControllerCamera onto the Event Camera field of the Canvas component:

| ControllerCamera | Canvas |
|---|---|n| Camera (head) | World Space |
| Reticle | Event Camera None (Camera) |

This ensures the controller can access the UI elements in the World Canvas.

Now, save the scene, prepare the HMD and controllers and press the Play button. Try pointing the right controller at the tower slot. It should turn yellow and the laser and reticle should be clearly visible:

![](bbox=[0.4, 0.711, 0.599, 0.816])

Press the Trigger button on your controller to open up the AddTowerWindow — and, once again, it's underneath the board.
