# Rendering your model

Your model is really starting to shine. To get an even better look at it, change the Viewport Shading pop-up to Rendered.

![](bbox=[0.423, 0.187, 0.567, 0.335])

![](bbox=[0.23, 0.347, 0.769, 0.597])

> Tip: Press Shift-Z to toggle Viewport Shading to Rendered; press it again and the shading will revert to Solid. Use the pop-up to set it back to Material, if you still want to see your colors.

# Ambient occlusion

Your render looks nice, but there's a quick way to make it even better. The Screen Layouts pop-up at the top, make sure you are in the Default layout.

+   1. While in Rendered view, go to the Properties panel and click the World tab.

+   2. Scroll down and check the Ambient Occlusion checkbox. Much nicer!
