Now save the scene and press the Play button to test the new input module and reticle. Put on your HMD, take the remote in your hand and look around.

You should be able to see the reticle when you look at the tower slots.

![](bbox=[0.364, 0.276, 0.636, 0.452])

Press the Select button on your remote to open up the AddTowerWindow. But it won't be where you'd expect. It'll appear underneath the board where it shows your gold and the wave number!

![](bbox=[0.311, 0.537, 0.689, 0.814])
