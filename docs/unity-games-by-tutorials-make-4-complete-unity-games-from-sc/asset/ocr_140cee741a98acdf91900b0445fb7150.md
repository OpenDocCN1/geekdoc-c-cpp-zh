# # Chapter 10: Adding Enemies
By Anthony Uccello

In Chapter 9, “The Player and Environment,” you added the first-person controls, let the player switch guns, and added visual and audio effects.

In this chapter, you'll add some robots to the mix, but to make things interesting, there will be evil robots. Evil robots that will try to defeat you. You'll add lots of them and give them the ability to fire — but to make things fair, you'll also add some pickups as well. In short, by the end of this chapter, you'll have yourself an FPS where enemies pose a real threat.

# ## Creating the robots

Open the current project in progress, or you can start fresh by opening the starter project for this chapter included with the Resources folder. Open the Battle scene to pick up where you left off.

Click on the **Resources** folder and drag **RedRobot**, **YellowRobot** and **BlueRobot** onto the scene in front of the player, somewhere above ground level:
