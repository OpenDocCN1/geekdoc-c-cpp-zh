# Unity Games by Tutorials

First, you start increaseScoreEachSecond, then initialize all the variables to your starting values, before starting updateWaveTimer.

In SpawnRobots(), add the following before the closing brace:

```
gameUI.SetText(enemiesLeft);
```

This sets the enemy count to the last value after spawning new robots. Save your work and open Robot.cs in your code editor.

Here, you need to call the RemoveEnemy() method you just created. Add the following immediately after the call to StartCoroutine("DestroyRobot");

```
Game.RemoveEnemy();
```

This will reduce the enemy count by one and update the UI. Save your change and switch back to Unity.

Great job getting here! Now, you can reap the rewards of your hard labor and click Play. Run around, kill robots and watch the enemies decrease. Notice that your ammunition decreases when you fire and updates when you change guns or get a pickup. Also, check out the text that notifies the player of the pickup he grabbed. Your health and armor also goes down when you get hit by robots.

You'll also see the new robot-wave text alert, and the bonus you get from killing all the enemies before the wave timer ends. The wave timer will click down every second and spawn new enemies (and reset) when it hits 0.
