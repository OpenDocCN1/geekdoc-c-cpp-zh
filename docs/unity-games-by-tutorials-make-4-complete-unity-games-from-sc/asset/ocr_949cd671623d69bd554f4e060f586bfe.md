Now to make your build. Open File > Build Settings and click Build. You will be prompted for a location to save the build. Select your Desktop, call it Runestrifle, and click Save.

Unity will take a bit of time to generate the Xcode project, which contains your iOS port of your game. All that's left is to submit it to the App Store.

The Xcode project contains everything necessary to build and test your game on a device, and publish the game to the App Store. Apple will permit you to test the game on a device without a paid developer account, but you must have a paid developer account to submit to the App Store.

To learn more about creating a developer account, running your app on a device and submitting your game to the App Store, check out our tutorial How to Submit an App to Apple: From No Account to App Store.
