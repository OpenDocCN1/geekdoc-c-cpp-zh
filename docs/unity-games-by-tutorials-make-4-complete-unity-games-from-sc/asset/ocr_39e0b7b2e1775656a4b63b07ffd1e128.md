# Adding audio effects

Another cool thing the Sound Manager can do is add special effects to audio files. **Click the Gun group to see the effect properties in the Inspector. Click the Add Effect Effect button at the bottom.**

![](bbox=[0.413, 0.226, 0.566, 0.477])

Then choose **Chorus**. Set the **Depth to 0.93** and fire that gun.

![](bbox=[0.316, 0.526, 0.683, 0.786])

The chorus makes it sounds like rapid fire. Feel free to play around with more effects — the mixer comes with plenty of them.
