# Enemies

Enemies, often called minions or creeps in tower defense games, are the cannon fodder for the towers as they try to reach the exit of the map.

![](bbox=[0.212, 0.353, 0.781, 0.613])

In Runestife, there are three kinds of enemies: A normal one (Knight), a fast one (Airship) and a sturdy one (Ram). Each of these have different speeds and amount of health but, in essence, they are the same; they all try to reach the exit of the player kills them.

These enemies are located in the Prefabs Enemy folder. Go to the folder and drag the Fast Enemy to the Hierarchy and set its Position to (2, 0, -5). Press the Play button to see what happens.

He's just standing there right now. That's no good! To make him move, you'll have to edit the Enemy script so it uses the WaypointManager that you made earlier.
