## Introducing the Audio Mixer

While playing your game, you might notice that the sounds are a little out of whack. The music is a bit too loud, the death sounds are a tad too soft and the bullets are lacking pizzazz!

You could tweak each sound individually, but you have better things to do, such as killing aliens and designing an epic game.

Think about it; you'd have to play the game and keep track of the individual sound levels then make adjustments. While you could copy some of the changes, most of the work would be done by hand.

Before Unity 5, the painful one-at-a-time process was replaced by the Audio Mixer, which allows you to control groups of sounds at once.

To create a mixer, go to Window > Audio > Audio Mixer. Click New and name it Master. This creates a Mixer asset with a Master group.

In the Mixer window, you'll see the Master group. Click the + icon to create a new group and name it Music. Now you can assign your music tracks to this group.

To assign a track, select the music AudioSource in the Hierarchy. In the Inspector, find the Output property and drag the Music group from the Audio Mixer into it. Now you can control the volume of all music tracks simultaneously.
