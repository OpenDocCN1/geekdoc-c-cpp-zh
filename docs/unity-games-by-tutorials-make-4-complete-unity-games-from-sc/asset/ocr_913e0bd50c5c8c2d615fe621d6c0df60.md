# Unity Games by Tutorials

Congratulations! You've created your first sprite. It's that simple.

Unity automatically created a GameObject, with an attached SpriteRenderer, and assigned sprite texture. Look at the Inspector to confirm that the GameObject was created with a Transform and a SpriteRenderer component.

![](bbox=[0.135, 0.196, 0.864, 0.366])

You might wonder what's up with the white sprites in the Sprites sub-folder. The answer is simple: You can easily color them dynamically in-game, or in the editor with your own color choice, without having to create sprites for each color.

The single-block sprite will serve as the basic block for the ground — a.k.a. platform that Super Boy boy runs along. With that in mind, you'll color it brown to represent terrain.

In the Hierarchy, select the single-block GameObject and click Color in the Inspector to bring up a color picker.

![](bbox=[0.135, 0.586, 0.864, 0.637])

Enter the hex value: 59443CFF. Close the color picker.
