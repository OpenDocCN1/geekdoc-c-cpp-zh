# Chapter 3: Components

![](bbox=[0.207, 0.093, 0.792, 0.269])

In the Hierarchy, hold the **Alt** key on PC or **Option** on Mac and click the **disclosure triangle** next to **BobbleMarine-Body**. You'll see a list of child GameObjects. Look for **Gun**.

![](bbox=[0.393, 0.355, 0.606, 0.532])

**Select** that **GameObject** and click the **Create** button. Choose **Create Empty Child** and rename it to **Launcher**. This new GameObject lives in the center of the gun's barrel and represents where bullets will spawn — feel free to move it around in the scene if you'd like to tweak the spawn position.

![](bbox=[0.393, 0.646, 0.606, 0.735])

Keep all the GameObjects expanded and select **BobbleMarine-Body** so that the Inspector shows all the components. Drag the new **Launcher** GameObject into the Gun component's **Launch Position** field.

Notice that when you add the GameObject to a transform field, Unity finds and references the attached transform.

It's official! The marine's gun is locked and loaded. All that's the firing code.
