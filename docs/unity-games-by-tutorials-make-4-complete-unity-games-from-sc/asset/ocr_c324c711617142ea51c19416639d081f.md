Chapter 4: Physics

Select BobbleMarine-Head in the Hierarchy. In the Inspector, assign it the Head layer and select the Yes, change child option in the confirmation dialog.

Finally, click Edit\Project Settings\Physics, and in the physics matrix, uncheck the Player column in the Head row.
