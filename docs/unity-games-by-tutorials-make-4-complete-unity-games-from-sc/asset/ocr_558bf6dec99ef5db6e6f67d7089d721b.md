Imagine the mesh getting squashed flat. The flattened mesh is projected onto the image in the UV ► Image Editor panel on the left.

Move your cursor over the UV/Image Editor panel and use the same shortcuts for moving and scaling as the 3D View: G to move and S to scale. Scale down so that the entire thing fits into one of the colored squares, then move it onto the darkest square.

You've got some color on your mesh. But it's a little plain. You can use some of the shaded color variations you made to liven up a bit.

With your cursor in the 3D View, press 7 to switch to Top view. To make the separate segments a slightly different color, you need to select a loop of connected faces, encircle the body. To do this, hold the Alt key and click the edge shown directly under the 3D Cursor, here.

Alt-click performs a Loop Select operation. When in Face Select mode, it selects the quads on either side of the edge, and all connected faces all the way around the model (as long as they are quads). Orbit around your model to verify which faces are selected.

Press 7 to return to Top view. Shift-Alt-click the segment just forward of the one you have selected, to add it to the selection.
