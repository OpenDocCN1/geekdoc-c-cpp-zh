Thankfully, Visual Studio is amiable. It won't scold you for inserting tabs nor will it scoff at you for tapping the space bar. To customize formatting, selecting **Tools ▸ Options**. When the window opens, you'll see a ton of things to configure. Frankly, the options contain a search bar to make it easier to find the setting(s) you seek.

To change your indent style, you can find the options under **Text Editor ▸ C# ▸ Tabs** the following:

![](bbox=[0.186, 0.23, 0.802, 0.503])

To change your brace style, you can find the options under **Text Editor ▸ C# ▸ Formatting ▸ New Lines** like so:

![](bbox=[0.186, 0.571, 0.802, 0.846])

You can set up your braces by reviewing the options and checking the conditions for when the brace should be on a new line.
