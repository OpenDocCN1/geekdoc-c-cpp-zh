# # Chapter 8: Finishing Touches
By Brian Moakley

It's funny to think that only seven chapters ago, you might have fired up Unity for the first time, and now you're about to put final touches on your first game!

At this point, gameplay is pretty much set, sounds are properly mixed and the assets are pretty much launched.

What's left to do? Ship it!

![](bbox=[0.378, 0.487, 0.615, 0.663])

Actually, no. This game isn't early access! We're going to ship this completed. (Followed by a massive Day One patch; ;)

Currently, BobbleHeadWars doesn't provide a winning or losing condition. After a bit of time, the aliens stop spawning, leaving the space marine to ponder existence with nothing more than a loaded weapon in an empty arena.

In this chapter, you'll provide a winning and losing condition and add some realism. In the process, you'll learn about UnityEvent, the Unity particle system, and some additional animation features.
