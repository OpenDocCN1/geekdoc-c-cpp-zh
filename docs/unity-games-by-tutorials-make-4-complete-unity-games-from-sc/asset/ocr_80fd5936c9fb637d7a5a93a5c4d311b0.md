Now, select BobbleMarine-Head in the Hierarchy, and look at the hinge joint in the
Inspector. You'll see many options!

![](bbox=[0.292, 0.146, 0.707, 0.439])

The first option is the Connected Body, and in this case, it's the other GameObject
that the head will be "hinged" to.

In the Hierarchy, drag the BobbleMarine-Body to the Connected Body property. Now,
the Game are connected by physics.

![](bbox=[0.219, 0.552, 0.78, 0.725])

The Anchor indicates where the end point of the joint attaches to the GameObject
sified in the object's local coordinate space. (0, 0, 0) within the head's
coordinate space represents the neck area, leaving the value at (0, 0, 0) is fine.

The Axis determines what direction the body will swing. i.e. up and down. You want to move this on the
z-axis, i.e., by setting the Axis to (0, 0, 1).
