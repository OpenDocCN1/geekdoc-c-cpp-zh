(14.26)
I_{11} = \rho \int_{-h/4}^{3h/4} \int_{-h(z)}^{h(z)} \int_{-a(z)}^{a(z)} (y^2 + z^2) dx dy dz = \rho a_0 b_0 h \left( \frac{4}{15} b_0^2 + \frac{1}{20} h^2 \right),
I_{22} = \rho \int_{-h/4}^{3h/4} \int_{-h(z)}^{h(z)} \int_{-a(z)}^{a(z)} (x^2 + z^2) dx dy dz = \rho a_0 b_0 h \left( \frac{4}{15} a_0^2 + \frac{1}{20} h^2 \right),
I_{33} = \rho \int_{-h/4}^{3h/4} \int_{-h(z)}^{h(z)} \int_{-a(z)}^{a(z)} (x^2 + y^2) dx dy dz = \frac{4}{15} \rho a_0 b_0 h (a_0^2 + b_0^2).

Substituting the mass m, this gives us the inertia tensor

(14.27) \(\mathbf{I}_{\text{pyramid}} = \begin{bmatrix} \frac{1}{5} m b_0^2 + \frac{1}{30} m h^2 & 0 & 0 \\ 0 & \frac{1}{5} m a_0^2 + \frac{1}{30} m h^2 & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & \frac{1}{5} m (a_0^2 + b_0^2) \end{bmatrix}.\)
