You can try to prevent such situations by removing decals after a certain amount of time or by restricting the number of decals on screen at once. The latter approach allows us to keep many decals in the world as long as they are not visible at the same time. Most games simply clean up the scene by removing decals after a fixed amount of time, but lose a lot of immersive quality in the process. The game Max Payne places a limit on the number of decals created in a single area, which has the great effect that if the player comes back to a room where he was previously, all the blood stains and bullet holes are still there.
