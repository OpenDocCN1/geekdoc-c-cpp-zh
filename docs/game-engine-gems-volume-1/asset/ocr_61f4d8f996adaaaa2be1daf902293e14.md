If you measure relative to a centroid of faces, you get partitions that are very densely packed, but may not share vertices well (imagine three parallel planes intersecting a sphere, where triangles inside the sphere are close together, but have a lot of boundary vertices that are only used once). Just after I'd solved this problem, I read a paper [3] that expressed the same solution only a couple of years earlier. As with most discoveries, in retrospect, the solution is obvious: minimize the distance between related triangle centers.
