A single experiment or run would consist of selecting the line, the pole, and the lure, then making a cast on a grass field and measuring the distance from the fisherman's feet to the spot where the lure landed. The experimental unit consisted of the conditions such as the wind speed and direction and the caster's ability at the time a certain trial or combination of treatment factors was employed.

This was a 3 x 2^2 factorial experiment. The experimenter could easily clip his fishing reel onto one fishing pole or the other between casts, and he could easily change the lure between casts, but to change the line weight he had to take all the line off his reel, wind it up on a spindle, and then fill the reel with a different weight line. Thus, the line weight factor was hard to vary.
