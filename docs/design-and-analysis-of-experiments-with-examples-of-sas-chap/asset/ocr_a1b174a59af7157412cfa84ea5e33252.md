The design shown in Table 11.8 is balanced, in that the whole simplex-centroid design in mixture components is repeated for both levels of one process variable when the other is ignored, and it has center points at \(x_i = \frac{1}{3}\) and \(z_j = 0\) for \(i = 1, 3\) and \(j = 1, 2\), which allow for testing the goodness of fit of model 11.28.
