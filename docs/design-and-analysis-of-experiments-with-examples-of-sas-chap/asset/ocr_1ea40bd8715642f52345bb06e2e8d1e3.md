since each factor has only two levels, the model can be rewritten in the form shown in Section 3.7.2 as:

\( y = \beta_0 + \beta_A A + \beta_B B + \beta_C C + \beta_{AB} AB + \beta_{AC} AC \\
+ \beta_{BC} BC + \beta_{ABC} ABC + \beta_P P + \beta_Q Q + \beta_{AP} AP \\
+ \beta_{AQ} AQ + \beta_{BP} BP + \beta_{BQ} BQ + \beta_{CP} CP + \beta_{PQ} PQ \\
+ \beta_{CQ} CQ + \epsilon \) (8.9)

where the \(\beta\)s represent the regression coefficients or half of the effects, and the \(X\)s represent the coded factor levels.
