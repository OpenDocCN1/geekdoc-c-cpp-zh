Box-Behnken designs can also be easily modified to create EESPRS designs. Simply sort the Box-Behnken design by the whole-plot factors and determine the number of subplots in each whole plot to match the block with the maximum number. Add center points with respect to the subplot factors in whole plots where the whole-plot factor levels are not at the center value, and add at least two whole plots consisting of entirely center points.
