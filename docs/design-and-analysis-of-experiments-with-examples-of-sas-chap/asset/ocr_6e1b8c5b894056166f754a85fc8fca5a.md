The least squares means for the two main effects produced by proc mixed are shown on the next page. Here it can be seen that main effect A had the most influential effect on the bursting strength. However, the presence of the three-factor interaction means that the effect of factor A depends upon the levels of both factors B and C. This can be visualized in the interaction plot shown in Figure 8.3. There it can be seen that increasing factor A from its low to high level has caused the greatest decrease in bursting strength when factor B is at its low level and C is at its high level. If the linear relation between the bursting strength and the factor levels is reasonable, there appears to be many combinations of factor levels that would result in bursting strengths greater than the threshold value of 2.0.
