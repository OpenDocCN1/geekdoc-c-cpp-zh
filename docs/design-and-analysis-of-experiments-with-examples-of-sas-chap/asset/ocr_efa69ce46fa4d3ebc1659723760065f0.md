# Figure 11.9 Translation and Rotation of Axis To Form Independent Factors

![](bbox=[0.15, 0.42, 0.85, 0.72])

Cornell (2002) shows the translation and rotation from the k dimensional mixture component space to k - 1 dimensional space for the independent factor space can be written as k - 1 contrasts of the mixture components. For example, in three-mixture components ($x_1, x_2, x_3$),

```
w_1 = \sqrt{6}(2x_1 - x_2 - x_3) \qquad (11.11)
```

```
w_2 = \sqrt{18}(x_2 - x_3), \qquad (11.12)
```
