The SAS automated design of experiments tool ADX also has many tools for the design and analysis of mixture experiments. Once a button is selected (as shown in Figure 11.10), clicking the Design Details button gives you the option of looking at a description of the design, a listing of the runs in the design, or a two-dimensional graph showing the runs in the simplex design space.
