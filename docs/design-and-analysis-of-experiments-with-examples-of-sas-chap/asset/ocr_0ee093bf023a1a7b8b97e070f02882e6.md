The estimable effects (shown above) are split into the two columns for the following reason. A full 2^4 factorial is conducted with factors A, B, C, and D in the 16 whole plots, therefore the factorial effects A, B, C, D, AB, AC, AD, BC, BD, CD, ABC, ABD, ACD, BCD, ABCD and all their aliases are whole-plot effects and should be tested with the whole-plot error term. All the other estimable effects are sub-plot effects and are tested with the sub-plot error term.
