# 9.6.2 Adjusted P-values

When the Huynh-Feldt condition does not hold, there is less information in the estimate of the variance of the within plot experimental error due to the correlation in responses. Greenhouse and Geisser (1959) and Huynh and Feldt (1976) proposed adjustments to the degrees of freedom for the standard subplot F-tests in repeated measures designs. These adjustments were developed for designs where there is only one within subjects treatment factor. Both of these adjustments consist of calculating an \(\epsilon\) value that is a function of the elements in the covariance matrix of the repeated measurements. Once the \(\epsilon\) value is computed, the degrees of freedom for the within subplot F-tests are modified by multiplying by \(\epsilon\). Huynh and Feldt’s adjustment is slightly less conservative than Greenhouse and Geisser’s.
