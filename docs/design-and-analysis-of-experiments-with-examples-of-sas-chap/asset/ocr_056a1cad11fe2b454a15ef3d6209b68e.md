in each sealed bag. A factorial experiment was to be planned to find out which factors affected variability in intermix weights. In order to determine which factors to include in a factorial experiment, a reasonable first step would be to partition the variability in intermix weight into the variability from soup batch to soup batch and the variability within a batch caused by the process to mix and add intermix to the dried soup. If there was little variability from batch to batch, the experiment would only need to consider factors involved in the mixing step.

In order to partition the variability in package weights into batch-to-batch and within batch, a sampling experiment was performed. A random sample of four batches was selected over a month of production runs. From each batch, three 1-lb. samples of the finished soup mix were taken from the mixing tank as it was being packaged. The weight of the intermix was determined for each sample. The results of the sampling experiment are shown in Table 5.4.
