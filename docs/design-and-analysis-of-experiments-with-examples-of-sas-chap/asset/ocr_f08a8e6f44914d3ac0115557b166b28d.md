Figure 10.3 shows Myers et al. (1992)'s variance dispersion graph for this design, from SAS ADX. The variance dispersion graph plots the maximum, minimum and average scaled variance of a predicted value ($NVar(\hat{y}(\mathbf{x}))/\sigma^2$) as a function of the distance from the origin of the design. When the design is not rotatable, there will be three distinct lines on the graph. The closer together the lines, the closer the design is to being rotatable. In Figure 10.3 the lines overlap showing that the design is rotatable. It can also be seen that the value of the scaled variance of a predicted value at the origin is nearly equal to the variance at radius 1.0, indicating the design has uniform precision.
