# 3.9 Review of Important Concepts

When experimenting with more than one treatment factor, factorial designs with all possible treatment combinations randomized to experimental units are much more efficient than separate one factor designs. When there are homogeneously experimental units and multiple factors under study the CRFD or completely randomized factorial design should be used.
