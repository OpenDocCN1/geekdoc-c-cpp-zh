Assembly language programming, on the other hand, is essential for understanding the internal architecture of a microcontroller. Furthermore, familiarization with assembly language may be helpful in understanding how to write microcontroller interrupt programs using C. This topic will be discussed later. Finally, assembly language may be useful for debugging a C-program.
