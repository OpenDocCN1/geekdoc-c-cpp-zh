# BZ Branch if Zero

**Syntax:** BZ n

**Operands:** -128 $\le$ n $\le$ 127

**Operation:** if Zero bit is '1' (PC) + 2 + 2n $\rightarrow$ PC

**Status Affected:** None

**Encoding:**

\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|c|} \hline 1110 & 0000 & nnnn & nnnn \\ \hline \end{tabular}

**Description:** If the Zero bit is '1', then the program will branch. The 2's complement number '2n' is added to the PC. Since the PC will have incremented to fetch the next instruction, the new address will be PC + 2 + 2n. This instruction is then a two-cycle instruction.

**Words:** 1

**Cycles:** 1(2)

**Q Cycle Activity:**

\begin{tabular}{|l|l|l|l|} \hline & Q1 & Q2 & Q3 \\ \hline If Jump: & Decode & Read literal & Process \\ \hline & & & Q4 \\ \hline & & & Write to PC \\ \hline\end{tabular}

**If No Jump:**

\begin{tabular}{|l|l|l|l|} \hline & Q1 & Q2 & Q3 \\ \hline & Decode & Read literal & Process \\ \hline & & & Q4 \\ \hline & & & No \\ \hline \end{tabular}

**Example:** HERE BZ Jump
Before Instruction
PC = address (HERE)
After Instruction
If Zero
PC = 1;
PC = address (Jump)
If Zero
PC = 0;
PC = address (HERE + 2)

# CALL Subroutine Call

**Syntax:** CALL k(s)

**Operands:** 0 $\le$ s $\le$ 1048575
s $\in$ [0,1]

**Operation:** (PC) + 4 $\rightarrow$ TOS, k $\rightarrow$ PC<20:1>, if s = 1
(W) $\rightarrow$ WS, (STATUS) $\rightarrow$ STATUSS, (BSR) $\rightarrow$ BSRS

**Status Affected:** None

**Encoding:**
1st word (k<7:0>)
2nd word(k<19:8>)

\begin{tabular}{|c|c|c|} \hline 1110 & 110s & k<7:0> \\ \hline 1111 & k<19:8> & kkkk \\ \hline \end{tabular}

**Description:** Subroutine call of entire 2-Mbyte memory range. First, return address (PC + 4) is pushed onto the return stack. If 's' = 1, the W, STATUS and BSR registers are also pushed into their respective shadow registers, WS, STATUSS and BSRS. If 's' = 0, no update occurs (default). Then, the 20-bit value 'k' is loaded into PC<20:1>. CALL is a two-cycle instruction.

**Words:** 2

**Cycles:** 2

**Q Cycle Activity:**

\begin{tabular}{|l|l|l|l|} \hline & Q1 & Q2 & Q3 \\ \hline & Decode & Read literal & PUSH PC to \\ \hline & & & stack \\ \hline & & & Q4 \\ \hline & & & Read literal \\ \hline & & & 'k<19:8>' \\ \hline & & & Write to PC \\ \hline \end{tabular}

**Example:** HERE CALL THERE, 1
Before Instruction
PC = address (HERE)
After Instruction
PC = address (THERE)
TOS = address (HERE + 4)
WS = W
BSRS = BSR
STATUSS = STATUS
