As an example, initializing master SSPSTAT with 40H will select the SPI master mode, data transmission from HIGH to LOW clock (CKE = 1), and SSPBUF empty (BF = 0). During data transmission, the BF bit in the SSPSTAT register (master or slave) can then be polled by waiting in a loop until BF = 1.
