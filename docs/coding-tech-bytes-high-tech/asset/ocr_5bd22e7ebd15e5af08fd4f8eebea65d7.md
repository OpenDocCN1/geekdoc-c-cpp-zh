# Building More  
Complex Machines

Since Lovelace’s day, computers and coding have come a long way. In the 1940s, German inventor Konrad Zuse created what is considered the first modern computer. Called the Z3, it could remember and carry out instructions.

US programming pioneer Grace Hopper worked with early computers during World War II. She later helped in the development of the UNIVAC I computer and a programming language called COBOL. Hopper served in the US Naval Reserve, helping the US Navy develop and use early computers. She eventually rose to the rank of rear admiral.
