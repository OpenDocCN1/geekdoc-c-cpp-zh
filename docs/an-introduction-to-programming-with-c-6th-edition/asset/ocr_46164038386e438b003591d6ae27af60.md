# More on the Selection Structure

Robin is holding a bag of trash or a bag of recyclables in her right hand. She is directly in front of two containers: one marked Trash and the other marked Recycle. Robin needs to lift the lid from the appropriate container (necessary), drop the bag in the container, and then put the lid on the back.

```
nested selection structure if (the bag contains trash)
    if (the lid is on the Trash container)
        lift the Trash container's lid using your left hand
    else
        drop the bag of trash on the Trash container
        put the lid back on the Trash container using your left hand
else
    if (the lid is on the Recycle container)
        lift the Recycle container's lid using your left hand
    else
        drop the bag of recyclables in the Recycle container
        put the lid back on the Recycle container using your left hand
```
