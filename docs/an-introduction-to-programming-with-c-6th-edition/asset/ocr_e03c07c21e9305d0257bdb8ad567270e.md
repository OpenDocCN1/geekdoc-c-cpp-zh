Robin is at a store's checkout counter. She'd like to use one of her credit cards—either Discovery card or her Visa card, but preferably her Discovery card—to pay for the items she is purchasing. Robin is not sure whether the store accepts either card. If the store does not accept either card, Robin will need to pay for the items.

+   1. ask the store clerk whether the store accepts the Discovery card
2. if (the store accepts the Discovery card)
   pay for your items using your Discovery card
else
   ask the store clerk whether the store accepts the Visa card
   if (the store accepts the Visa card)
     pay for your items using your Visa card
   else
     pay for your items using cash
   end
end

> Notice that the nested selection structure is contained in the outer selection structure's false path.
