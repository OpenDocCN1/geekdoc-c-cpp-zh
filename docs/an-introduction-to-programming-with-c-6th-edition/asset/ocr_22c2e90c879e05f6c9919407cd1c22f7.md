# CHAPTER 5 The Selection Structure

Figure 5-4 shows a modified version of the previous problem specification. In the modified version, the contents of the bag that Robin is holding are not certain: the bag might contain trash or it might contain recyclables. You can solve this using either of the solutions shown in Figure 5-4. The condition in Solution 1's selection determines whether Robin is holding a bag of trash. If she is, follow the three instructions contained in the original algorithm from Figure 5-3. If she's not, it means the bag contains recyclables. In that case, Robin should lift the Recycle lid before dropping the bag into the container and then replacing the lid. The condition in Solution 2's selection structure, on the other hand, determines whether Robin is holding a bag of either. If she is, she belongs in the Recycle container; unlike the selection structure in Figure 5-2, which provides a special action for Robin to take only when the selection condition is true, the selection in Figure 5-4 require Robin to perform one or the other, but a different set of instructions when the condition is false. The instructions are to follow when the condition evaluates to the true path. The path begins with the if and ends with either the else (if it is one) or the end if. The instructions to follow when the condition evaluates to false are called the false path. The path begins with the else and ends with the end if. For clarity, the instructions in each path should be denoted as in Figure 5-4. Selection structures that contain instructions in both paths, like the ones in Figure 5-4, are referred to as dual-alternative selection structures.

Robin holding either a bag of trash or a bag of recyclables in her right hand. She is directly in front of two containers: one marked Trash and the other marked Recycle. Each is on each container. Robin needs to lift the lid from the appropriate container, then drop the bag in the container, and put the lid back on the container.

# Solution 1

```
if (the bag contains trash)
    lift the Trash container's lid with your left hand
    drop the bag of trash in the Trash container
else
    put the lid back on the Trash container with your left hand
if
    lift the Recycle container's lid with your left hand
    drop the bag of recyclables in the Recycle container
else
    put the lid back on the Recycle container with your left hand
end if
```
