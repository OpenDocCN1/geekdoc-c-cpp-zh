A recursive declaration of the factorial method is arrived at by observing the following relationship:

n! = n · (n - 1)!

For example, 5! is clearly equal to 5 · 4!, as is shown by the following equations:

5! = 5 · 4 · 3 · 2 · 1
5! = 5 · (4 · 3 · 2 · 1)
5! = 5 · (4!)
