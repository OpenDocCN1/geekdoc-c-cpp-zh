+   - **Command** — These are the actual commands that instruct Nmap how to scan. They follow (by default) the Profile. You can change those to fit your needs.
+   - **Hosts and Services** — These are buttons that will show the results of the post-scan findings.
+   - **Nmap Output** — The results are shown here in detail.
+   - **Ports / Hosts** — This is a summary of the ports found, and their status, as well as a listing of the Hosts.
+   - **Topology** — This is a visual representation of the hops to the target.
+   - **Host Details** — This gives you a wealth of information about the host operating system.
+   - **Scans** — This shows the historical scans you have run.
