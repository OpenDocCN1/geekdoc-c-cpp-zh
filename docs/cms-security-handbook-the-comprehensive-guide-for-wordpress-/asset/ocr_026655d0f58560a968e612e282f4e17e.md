# Step Five: Control the Damage

The next step is to review the SENT directory to confirm whether mail has gone out. If so, then the damage is done. Clean out the current and pending-to-send queues, and scan for viruses.

If your situation was that the spam was going out for a very long time, then you might be blacklisted as a sender by several organizations. One major place to check is SpamHaus.org.
