# Appendix A • External Programmer Setup

Hobbyist programmers, such as the USBasp and USBtinyISP, can be used to program some of the ATtiny range of microcontrollers. These hobby programmers are cheaper alternatives to the Atmel-ICE programmer/debugger. They do have limitations in the ATtiny parts that they can program, which depends on which programming interface the specific ATtiny microcontroller parts have available. The USBasp and USBtinyISP are both “program only” USB devices that do not have any debugging capabilities. Both of these programmers are open-source designs and widely available from various suppliers. They are also available as slightly different designs, where the manufacturers may supply their own version of these programmers that are slightly different from the originally released open-source design.
