On-chip peripherals, such as I/O ports and timers, on AVR microcontrollers are controlled through the I/O registers that are grouped together as 64 8-bit registers which can be found in the Register Summary section of the datasheet. A detailed description of these registers is found in the relevant sections of the datasheet, such as the I/O Ports section for controlling pins on an AVR, and 8-bit Timer/Counter0 with PWM section for details on controlling the timer/counter and its PWM features.

This chapter covers I/O ports as an introduction to accessing AVR peripherals on the ATtiny13(A) and ATtiny25/45/85 range of AVRs. Other peripherals are covered in later chapters of this book, and also where they are used in code or projects.
