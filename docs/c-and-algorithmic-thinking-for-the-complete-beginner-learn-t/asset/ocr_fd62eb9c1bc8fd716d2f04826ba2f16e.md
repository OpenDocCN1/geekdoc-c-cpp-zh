There is a myth about the origin of the term "debugging." In 1940, while Grace Hopper¹ was working on a Mark II Computer at Harvard University, her associates discovered a bug (a moth) stuck in a relay (an electrically operated switch). This bug was blocking the proper operation of the Mark II computer. So, while her associates were trying to remove the bug, Grace Hopper remarked that they were "debugging" the system!
