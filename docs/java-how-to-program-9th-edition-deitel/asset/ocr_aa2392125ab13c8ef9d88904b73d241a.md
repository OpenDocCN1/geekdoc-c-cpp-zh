# Build the GUI

Drag three Sliders (objects of class JS1ider) from the Palette onto the JFrame (you may need to scroll through the Palette). As you drag components near the edges of the client area or near other components, NetBeans displays guide lines (Fig. I.5) that show you the recommended distances and alignments between the component you're dragging, the edges of the client area and other components. As you follow the steps to build the GUI, use the guide lines to arrange the components into three rows and three columns, as in Fig. I.6.
