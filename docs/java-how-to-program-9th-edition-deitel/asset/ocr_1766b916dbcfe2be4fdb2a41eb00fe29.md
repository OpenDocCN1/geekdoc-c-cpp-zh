# K.6 Mashup Performance and Reliability Issues

There are several challenges when creating mashup applications. Your applications become susceptible to traffic and reliability problems on the Internet—circumstances generally beyond your control. Companies might suddenly change APIs that your applications use. Your application is dependent on hardware and software capabilities of other companies. Also, companies could impose fee structures on previously free web services or could increase existing fees.
