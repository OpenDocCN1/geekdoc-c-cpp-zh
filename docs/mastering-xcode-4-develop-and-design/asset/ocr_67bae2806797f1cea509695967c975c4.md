# THE ORGANIZER WINDOW

![](bbox=[0.135, 0.172, 0.718, 0.481])

The Organizer window (Figure 3.20) stands apart from workspace windows as a collection point for project and device management, including repositories, archives, snapshots, and more. The various aspects of the Organizer will be explored throughout the book. To open the Organizer, choose Window > Organizer from the main menu.
