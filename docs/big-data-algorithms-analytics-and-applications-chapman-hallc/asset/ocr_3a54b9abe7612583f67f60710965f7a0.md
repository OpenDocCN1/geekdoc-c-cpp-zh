# ABSTRACT

This chapter describes a unified framework for dynamically integrating and meaningfully organizing personal and social Big Data. With the rapid development of emerging computing paradigms, we have been continuously experiencing a change in work, life, playing, and learning in the highly developed information society, which is a kind of seamless integration of the real physical world and cyber digital space. More and more people have been accustomed to sharing their personal contents across the social networks due to the high accessibility of social media along with the increasingly widespread adoption of wireless mobile computing devices.
