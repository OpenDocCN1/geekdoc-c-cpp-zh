+   9. (Numerical) Write, compile, and execute a program to calculate and display the fourth root of a user-entered number. Recall from elementary algebra that you find the fourth root of a number by raising the number to the 1/4 power. (Hint: Don't use integer division—can you see why?) Verify your program by calculating the fourth roots of your test data: 81, 16, 1, and 0. When you're finished, use your program to determine the fourth roots of 42, 121, 256, 587, 1240, and 16,256.
