+   1. (General Math) The volume of a sphere can be determined by using this formula:
V = 4/3\pi r^3

V is the volume of the sphere.
\pi is the dimensionless number (no units) having the value 3.1416, accurate to four decimal places.
r is the radius in centimeters (cm) of the sphere.
 a. Determine the volume of V by calculating the units resulting from the right side of the formula. Check that your answer corresponds to the units for work listed in Table 1.1.
 b. Determine the volume of a sphere with a radius of 4 cm.
 c. If you were required to write a computer program to determine the volume of a sphere, what inputs, outputs, and algorithm would you use?
