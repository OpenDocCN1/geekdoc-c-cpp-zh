If the str.replace(12, 6, "to") is executed next, the existing characters in indices 12 through 17 are deleted, and the two characters contained in to are inserted at index 12. Figure 9.10 shows the net effect of this replacement. The number of replacement characters (in this case, two) can be fewer, equal to, or greater than the number of characters being replaced, which in this case is six.
