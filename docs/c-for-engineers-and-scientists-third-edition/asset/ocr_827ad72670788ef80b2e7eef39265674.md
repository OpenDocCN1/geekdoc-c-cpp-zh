# Engineering and Scientific Units

Two unit systems are commonly used in engineering and scientific work: the English Engineering system and the International System (SI). Both are used in this book. Table 1.1 lists the units used in these two systems.

| Quantity | Symbol | International System (SI) Units | English Engineering Units | Conversion Equalities |
|---|---|---|---|--|
| Time Length | t | seconds (s) | feet (ft) | 1 m = 3.2808 ft |
| Area | A | meters (m) sq. meters (m²) | sq. feet (ft²) | 1 m² = 10.76 ft² |
| Volume | V | cubic meters (m³) | cubic feet (ft³) | 1 ft³ = 35.31 ft³ |
| Mass Force | m F | kilograms (kg) Newton (1 N = 1 kg-m/s²) | pounds-mass (lbm) pounds-force (lbf-ft/s²) | 1 kg = 2.19 lbf = 4.448 N |
