The general quadratic equation, Equation 14.1, can be solved easily and exactly by using this quadratic formula:

```
$r = \frac{-b \pm \sqrt{b^2 - 4ac}}{2a}$
```

Equation 14.2 can be solved for x exactly by factoring the polynomial. The roots are then clearly 1, -1, 2, and 3/2. However, most polynomials cannot be factored easily, and other general techniques are required. There are formulas for the exact solution of general cubic or quartic equations, but they are cumbersome and, therefore, seldom used. No exact formula is possible for a polynomial such as Equation 143, in which the highest power of x is greater than 4. For these polynomials, numerical means must generally be used to determine the roots.
