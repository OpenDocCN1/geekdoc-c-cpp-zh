go go further and calculate the average of items. Where do you calculate the average—
inside the loop or outside it?

In the case of hand, calculating an average requires that both final sum and the number
of items in that sum. The average is computed by dividing the final sum
of number of items. At this point, you must ask, "At what point in the program is the
correct sum available, and at what point is the number of items available?

In reviewing Program 5.6, you can see that the correct sum is needed for calculating the
average after the while loop is finished. In fact the whole purpose of the while
loop is to ensure that the sum is calculated correctly to produce a correct sum.
After the loop is finished, you also have a count of the items used in the sum.
However, because of the way the while loop was constructed, the number in count (5)
when the loop is finished is one more than the number of items (four) to obtain the
total. Knowing this, you subtract one from count before using it to determine the
average. With this information as background, take a look at Program 5.7.
