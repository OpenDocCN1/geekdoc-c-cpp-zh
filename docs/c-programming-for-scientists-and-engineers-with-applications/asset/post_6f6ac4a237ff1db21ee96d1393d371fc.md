
Both of these are accurate because (using 5! as the example) (((1) × 2) × 3) × 4) × 5 is equal to 5 × (4 × (2 × (1))).

### 5.6.1 Rocket Motor Thrust

Method

funcforce(): function to compute the total force.

Compute the total force from the formula

$f = \frac{wv}{gt}$

where w is the weight of the rocket in pounds,
v is the average velocity of the rocket in feet/second,
t is the time interval in seconds, and
g is the gravitational acceleration in feet/sec².

functrust(): function to compute the total thrust.

$t = w + f$

- time

```
return t
```

```c
float funcforce(float weight1, float velocity1, float timel)
{
  const float G = 32.2;
  float force;

}
```
