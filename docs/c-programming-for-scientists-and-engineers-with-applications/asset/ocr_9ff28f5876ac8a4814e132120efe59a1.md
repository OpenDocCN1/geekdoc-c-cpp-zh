# 3.5.3 Convection Heat Transfer

Problem Calculate the heat transfer through a metal plate of length $l$ and width $w$ (given in centimeters). Air is blowing at temperature $t_1^{\circ}C$ over a metal plate maintained at temperature $t_2^{\circ}C$. The convection heat transfer coefficient is 28 $W/m^2^{\circ}C$.

Method From Newton's cooling, the heat transfer is given by the formula

$q = hA(t_w - t_\infty)$

Where $q$ is the heat transfer in kilowatts or Btu/hour,

$h$ is the convection heat transfer coefficient in watts/meter$^{25^{\circ}C$,

$a$ is the area of the plate in meters$^2$,

$t_{plate}$ is the plate temperature, and

$t_{air}$ is the air temperature.

Data Five real numbers accurate to two decimal places representing $l, w, t_{air}^*$, $t_{plate}^*$ and heat transfer coefficient.
