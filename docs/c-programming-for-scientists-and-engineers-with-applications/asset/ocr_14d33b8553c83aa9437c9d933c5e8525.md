# 8.6 Higher-Dimensional Arrays

In science and engineering, mathematical modeling of physical problems for simulation and design is very common. Often problems involve three dimensions and sometimes even four dimensions. Problems having three-dimensional geometry require three-dimensional arrays. Three-dimensional problems such as airflow over an airfoil, wave propagation in solid and liquid media, vibration of three-dimensional objects, and stress analysis in three-dimensional structures dealing with three space variables and, in some cases, a time variable requires four dimensions.
