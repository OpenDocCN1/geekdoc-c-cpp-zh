```
\omega = \frac{2\pi s}{60} \quad v = \frac{\omega d}{2}
```

$d$ is the propeller diameter in ft.

+   - $s$ is the speed in revolutions per minute
- $\omega$ is the radial velocity in radians per minute
- $v$ is the velocity per minute
