# 8.7.4 Geometric Transformations

In computer graphics, points, lines, and polygons are translated from one position to another, and lines and polygons are rotated and scaled. These types of operations are sufficient to change the position, the orientation, and the size of the objects that are displayed. These are called geometric transformations. Transformations in two-dimensions are carried out by multiplying three-by-three square matrices. Transformations in three-dimensions are carried out by multiplying four-by-four square matrices.
