funcforce(): function to compute the total force.

Compute the total force from the formula

$f = \frac{wv}{gt}$

where w is the weight of the rocket in pounds,
v is the average velocity of the rocket in feet/second,
t is the time interval in seconds, and
g is the gravitational acceleration in feet/sec2

functchrust(): function to compute the total thrust.

$t = w + f$

where t is the total thrust,
w is the weight of the rocket, and
f is the total force.

Data Input data

+   - weight of the rocket
- velocity of the rocket
- time
