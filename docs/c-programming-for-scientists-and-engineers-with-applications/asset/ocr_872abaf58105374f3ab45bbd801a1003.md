Problem Construct a table showing how the drag force on the end of a circular cylinder varies with the diameter of the cylinder and the velocity of the air. Let the diameter vary from one to five feet in increments of one foot, and the velocity of air vary from 60 ft/sec to 100 ft/sec in increments of 10 ft/sec. This example shows the initialization of a two-dimensional array by computation, and the output of the array.

# Data

The density of air is $2.33 \times 10^{-3}$ slug/ft³

The drag coefficient is assumed to be 0.3

Method The magnitude of the drag force is determined by the following equation:

$F_D = \frac{1}{2} \rho A v^2 C_D$

Where $\rho$ is the density of air in slug/ft³

+   - $A (ft^2)$ is the projected area of the cylinder facing the air flow,
- $v$ is the velocity of air flow on the cylinder in ft/sec, and
- $C_D$ is the drag coefficient.

The drag coefficient $C_D$ is assumed to be 0.3.
