Method Input the translation matrix

```
$T(t_x, t_y) = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 0 & t_x \\ 0 & 1 & t_y \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
```

Where $t_x$ is the translation in the $x$-coordinate direction, and $t_y$ is the translation in the $y$-coordinate direction.

Input the coordinates of the line.

```
$L(x_1, y_1) L(x_2, y_2)$
```

```
$L = \begin{pmatrix} x_1 & x_2 \\ y_1 & y_2 \\ 1 & 1 \end{pmatrix}$
```

Multiply the translation matrix and the coordinates of the line.

# $L' = T(t_x, t_y) * L$

```
$$\begin{bmatrix} x'_1 & x'_2 \\ y'_1 & y'_2 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 & t_x \\ 0 & 1 & t_y \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix} * \begin{bmatrix} x_1 & x_2 \\ y_1 & y_2 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$$
```

Where $L'$ is the matrix for the translated line segment between points $(x'_1, y'_1)$ and $(x'_2, y'_2)$.

 * *********************************************************************************/

 * Input parameters:
 * transmat[IMAX] - transformation matrix
 * oldline[IMAX] - the coordinates of old line
 * Computed Variables:

void funcmultiply(float transmat[][IMAX], float oldline[][JMAX], float newline[][JMAX])
{
    int i, j, k;
    for(i = 0; i < IMAX; i++) {
        for(j = 0; j < JMAX; j++) {

        }

# Rotation of a line

Problem Rotate a line with the coordinate point $L(x_1, y_1)$ and $L(x_2, y_2)$ around the origin by an angle $\theta$ to $L'(x_1, y_1)$ and $L'(x_2, y_2)$.

![](img/8356e6c3bfe3b10354682c60b1e74b1d_546_0.png)

# Method

Input the rotation angle.
Calculate the rotation matrix.

```
$R(\theta) = \begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta & -\sin\theta & 0 \\ \sin\theta & \cos\theta & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$
```

Where $R(\theta)$ is the rotation matrix and $\theta$ is the counterclockwise rotation angle of the line.

Input the coordinates of the line.

$L = (x_1, y_1) (x_2, y_2)$

Set up the coordinate matrix.

```
$L = \begin{bmatrix} x_1 & x_2 \\ y_1 & y_2 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$
```

Multiply the translation matrix and the coordinate matrix.

```
$L' = R(\theta) * L$
```

```
$$\begin{bmatrix} x'_1 & x'_2 \\ y'_1 & y'_2 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta & -\sin\theta & 0 \\ \sin\theta & \cos\theta & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix} * \begin{bmatrix} x_1 & x_2 \\ y_1 & y_2 \end{bmatrix}$$
```

Data

```
$R(\theta) = \begin{bmatrix} \cos\theta & -\sin\theta & 0 \\ \sin\theta & \cos\theta & 0 \\ 0 & 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$
```

Coordinate matrix:

```
$L = \begin{bmatrix} x_1 & x_2 \\ y_1 & y_2 \\ 1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}$
```
