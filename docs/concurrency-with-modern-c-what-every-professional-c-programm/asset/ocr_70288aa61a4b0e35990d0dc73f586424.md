# The Challenge of Understanding Coroutines

It was quite a challenge for me to understand coroutines. I strongly suggest that you should not read the sections in the chapter in sequence. Skip in your first iteration the sections “The Framework”, and “The Workflow”. Furthermore, read the case studies “Variations of Futures”, “Modification and Generalization of a Generator”, and “Various Job Workflows”. Reading, studying, and playing with the provided examples should give you an initial intuition needed for you to actually dive into details and the workflow of coroutines.

What I present in this section as a new idea in C++20 is quite old. The term coroutine was coined by Melvin Conway¹. He used it in his publication on compiler construction in 1963. Donald Knuth² called procedures a special case of coroutines. Sometimes, it just takes a while to get your ideas accepted.
