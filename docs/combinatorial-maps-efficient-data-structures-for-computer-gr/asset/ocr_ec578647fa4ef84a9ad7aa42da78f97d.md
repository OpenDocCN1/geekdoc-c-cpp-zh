An example of 0-removal in a 2-map is illustrated in Fig. 6.20, showing that the modifications are slightly different for the dimension 0. Vertex {1,8} is removed from the initial 2-map in Fig. 6.20(a). β₁' is computed for the two darts 7 and 10, by following the path of darts β₁ ∘ β₁. However, this is not sufficient, since darts 7 and 10 are linked by β₂ with darts 8 and 1. It is thus necessary to define β₂'(7) = 10 and β₂'(10) = 7. This example shows that the 0-removal operation is a special case for n-maps: all β have to be modified, not only βᵢ.
