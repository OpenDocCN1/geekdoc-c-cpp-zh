Let $H=(D,P_1,\ldots,P_k)$ and $H'=(D',P'_1,\ldots,P'_k)$ be two hypermaps. $\phi:D\to D'$ is an *isomorphism* between $H$ and $H'$ if $\phi$ is a bijection between $D$ and $D'$, such that: $\forall d\in D,\ \forall i\in\{1,\ldots,k\},\ \phi(P_i(d))=P'_i(\phi(d))$. $H$ and $H'$ are isomorphic (by $\phi$). The corresponding graphs are isomorphic, and for each $i$, edges corresponding to permutation $P_i$ are associated with edges corresponding to permutation $P'_i$.
