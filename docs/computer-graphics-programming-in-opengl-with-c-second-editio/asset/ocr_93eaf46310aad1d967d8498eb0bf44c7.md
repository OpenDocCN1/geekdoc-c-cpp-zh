We achieve this effect by using a matrix transform that converts parallel lines into appropriate non-parallel lines. Such a matrix is called a *perspective matrix* or *perspective transform*, and is built by defining the four parameters of a *view volume*. Those parameters are (a) *aspect ratio*, (b) *field of view*, (c) *projection plane* or *near clipping plane*, and (d) *far clipping plane*.

Only objects between the *near* and *far* clipping planes are rendered. The near clipping plane also serves as the plane on which objects are *projected*, and is generally positioned close to the eye or camera (shown on the left in Figure 3.15). Selection of an appropriate value for the far clipping plane is discussed in Chapter 4.

The *field of view* is the vertical angle of viewable space. The *aspect ratio* is the ratio width/height of the near and far clipping planes. The shape formed by these elements and shown in Figure 3.15 is called a *frustum*.
