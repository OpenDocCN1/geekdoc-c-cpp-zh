The rendered examples shown in Figure 10.14 and Figure 10.15 work acceptably because the two models (grid and sphere) have a sufficient number of vertices to sample the height map values. That is, they each have a fairly large number of vertices, and the height map is relatively coarse and adequately sampled at a low resolution. However, closer inspection still reveals the presence of resolution artifacts, such as along the bottom left edge of the raised box at the right of the terrain in Figure 10.14. The reason that the sides of the raised box don't appear perfectly square, and include gradations in color, is because the 100×100 resolution of the underlying grid cannot adequately align perfectly with the white box in the height map, and the resulting rasterization of texture coordinates produces artifacts along the sides.
