# How Can I Live (Mostly) in Learner?

How would your life be different if you lived most of the time in Learner? At the beginning of this workbook, I asked you to focus on a person who says or does something that regularly triggers your Judger Mindset. When you chose that triggering situation to work on, I asked you how your life would be better if you could change your reaction. We will return there in a moment, but first let’s take time to reflect on how living predominantly in Learner would improve your life in general. I encourage you to think big about all the joy, love, and success you can envision for yourself!

*What would be possible if I could spend most of my time in Learner Mindset at work?* How would my relationships at work be better if I were in Learner Mindset most of the time?
