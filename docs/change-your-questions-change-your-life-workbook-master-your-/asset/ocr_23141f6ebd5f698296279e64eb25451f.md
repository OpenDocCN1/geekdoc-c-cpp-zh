# About Inquiry Institute

Our mission is to help create a world of new thinkers inspired by open-minded, open-hearted questioning.

Leadership, engagement, communication, collaboration, coaching, and innovation are uniquely empowered by the Question Thinking™ (QT) system of tools and practices pioneered by Dr. Marilee Adams and the Inquiry Institute. Our team of coaches, facilitators, and consultants are experts at providing QT approaches and solutions so that individuals, teams, and organizations become more successful and satisfied in realizing their desired results.

We invite you to visit our website to explore free resources, including the Choice Map, a Change Your Questions Book Club, and information about joining our active Learner Community. On the website and on the following pages you can also learn about other Learner Resources (www.InquiryInstitute.com/LearnerResources) that can make a substantial, and often life-changing difference, wherever they are applied.
