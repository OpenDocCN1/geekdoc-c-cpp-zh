**Learner Living:** A commitment to create better experiences, relationships, and results in our daily lives so that we spend more time in Learner and decrease the frequency and impact of Judger.

**Learner Listening and Judger Listening:** The mindset through which we listen impacts how we interpret what we hear. Learner Mindset listens with Learner questions such as, “What's valuable in what they're saying?” Judger ears listen with Judger questions such as, “What's wrong with what they're saying?” At any moment we can choose to listen with Learner ears or with Judger ears.
