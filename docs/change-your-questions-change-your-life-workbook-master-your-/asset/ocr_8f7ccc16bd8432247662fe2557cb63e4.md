# Your Mindset Challenge:  
Q-Prep

*What are my goals for this conversation (for myself, the other person, and the situation)?*

*What questions am I asking myself about the situation, the other person, myself, and/or my goals? (Include both your Judge and Learner questions.)*

*What **assumptions** am I making about myself, the situation, the other person, and my goals?*

*What questions might **the other person** be asking themselves with regard to the situation, me, their concerns, and/or goals? (Include possible Judge and Learner questions.)*
