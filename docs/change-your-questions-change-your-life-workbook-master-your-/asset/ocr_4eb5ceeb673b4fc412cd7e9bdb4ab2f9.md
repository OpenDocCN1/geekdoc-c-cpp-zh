# # About Andrea F. Lipton

![](bbox=[0.496, 0.076, 0.762, 0.36])

Andrea F. Lipton is an adult learn-ing, instructional design, and leadership development expert with more than 25 years of experience creating and facilitat-ing learning and development, mentor-ing, and coaching initiatives for Fortune 500 companies across an array of indus-tries. At the Inquiry Institute, Andrea serves as the Senior Director of Learning where she partners with Dr. Marilee Adams to design and develop Question Thinking tools including the workbook you are now holding. Her thought leadership has also been featured in *Training Journal*, *Training Industry*, *People + Strategy*, and in the Harvard Business Publishing blog “Leading the Way.”

In addition to her partnership with the Inquiry Institute, Andrea is the creator of the “Ways of Being That Work Toolkit” that helps indi-viduals and organizations define the leader attributes that matter most to them. She publishes a bi-monthly video blog of the same title. Visit Andrea’s LinkedIn profile at https://www.linkedin.com/in/aflipton/ to view her latest posts.

Andrea has held an array of leadership roles in learning and devel-opment for organizations including Harvard Business Publishing; the City of Los Angeles’ Department of Transportation, where she served as a FUSE Corp Executive Fellow; and Ferrazzi Greenlight, a boutique firm that specializes in executive coaching and leadership development. She relaunched the corporate university for CBRE in the Americas and has led learning and development teams for companies including Arden Realty, a subsidiary of General Electric, and JPMorgan Chase.

Andrea earned a BA and an MA from Columbia University.

Andrea is available for executive coaching, consulting, and workshops (virtual and onsite). You can contact her at Andrea@ InquiryInstitute.com.
