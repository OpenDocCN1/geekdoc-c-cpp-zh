# What Is It Like to Switch?

Look around you. Notice all the things in your space. Maybe you are at home and through a window you see a beautiful tree you planted thriving. Or maybe you see a picture hanging on the wall and notice that it is crooked. Perhaps you are on a plane and someone around you is talking loudly; or instead, perhaps you look outside and see the sun setting behind the mountains in the distance.

As you look around, pause to observe each mindset as it is triggered by your surroundings.
