# Ben’s Mindset Challenge:
Ben’s List of Annoying People and Situations

| Person<br><em>(list their name and your relationship)</em> | Behavior I find annoying<br><em>(describe what they say/do that bothers you)</em> | How I react<br><em>(describe what you think, feel, say, and do)</em> |
|---|---|---|
| **Charles (my employee)** | Charles asks so many questions in meetings it feels like he challenges everything and slows down our progress. | I know I complain about Charles in my head. I think sometimes when he asks a question I sigh out loud. Once I rolled my eyes. |
| **Grace (my wife)** | Grace calls me when I’m at the office and complains about her workday as if I am not busy with mine. | I get a sharp tone of voice with Grace and sometimes tell her I am too busy to talk. (Which I am!) |
| **Drivers who don’t use their turn signals** | These people act like they are the only ones on the road and cut people off, which could cause an accident. | I honk. I curse. When I am alone in the car sometimes I yell at them as if they can hear me. |
| **Me (Ben)** | I am so frustrated with myself that I can’t seem to figure out why the team is stuck. I usually am the “Answer Man” but I don’t have the answers at this new job. | When I am alone, I shake my head in frustration and exasperation. I tell myself it was a mistake to leave my old job. |
