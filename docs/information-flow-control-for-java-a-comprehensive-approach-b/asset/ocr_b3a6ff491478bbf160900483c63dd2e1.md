# 4.8.2 Computation of $R(d)$ for Summary Declassification Nodes

> Algorithm 8 Computation of $R(d)$ for Summary Declassification (backward propagation)

```
1 **procedure** SummaryDeclassification($G, L, R$)
2 **input** $G$: a system dependence graph
3 L: a security lattice
4 R: the required annotations
5 **output**: the set of summary declassification nodes (included in $G$)
6
7 pathEdges $=\ \emptyset$ // set of transitive dependencies already seen
8 **foreach** formal-$w$ out $o$ in $G$
9 pathEdges $\cup=$ (o, $o \in D$, T)
10 od
11 workList := pathEdges
12 **while** workList not empty
13 **remove** ($x, y, f$) from workList
14 **if** $x$ is an formal-node in $n$
15 addSummaries($x, y, f$)
16 **else**
17 **foreach** $w \to x$ in $G$
18 addToPathEdges(extendPathEdge($x, y, f, w$))
19 od
20 fi
21 od
22 end
24 **procedure** addToPathEdges($x, y, f, l$)
25 **input**: $(x, y, f, l)$: a path edge tuple
26 begin
27 **if** $(x, y', f', l') \in$ pathEdges$ where $f' \neq f$ or $l' \neq l$ **then**
28 **remove** ($x, y', f', l'$) from pathEdges
29 fi
30 **if** $(x, y, f, l) \notin$ pathEdges **then**
31 pathEdges $\cup=$ $(x, y, f, l)$
32 workList $\cup=$ $(x, y, f, l)$
33 fi
34 end
```

As summary declassification nodes represent the effect of declassifications on paths in the procedure body, and these can in turn call (recursively), a simple transitive closure over formal parameters does not yield a correct solution for summary declassification nodes [HRB90, RHSR94].
