In the fragment shader for the water’s top surface, the mixture of reflection and refraction depends on whether the camera is above or below the water surface. If the camera is above the surface, then both textures are included. If the camera is underwater, then the refraction texture is mixed with the blue water color. The output, both for the camera above and below the water surface, is shown immediately after the code, in Figure 15.5.
