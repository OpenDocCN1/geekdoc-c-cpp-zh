**Normalize (change to length=1; note the `^` notation above the `A`):**

Â = A/|A| = A/sqrt(u²+v²+w²), where |A| ≡ length of vector A  
JOML: Vector3f.normalize()  
GLSL: normalize(vec3) or normalize(vec4)

**Dot Product:**

A • B = ux + vy + wz  
JOML: Vector3f.dot(Vector3f)  
GLSL: dot(vec3,vec3) or dot(vec4,vec4)

**Cross Product:**

A × B = (vz-wy, wx-uz, uy-vx)  
JOML: Vector3f.cross(Vector3f)  
GLSL: cross(vec3,vec3)

Other useful vector functions are *magnitude* (which is available in both JOML and GLSL as length()) as well as *reflection* and *refraction* (both are available in GLSL; JOML includes reflection only).

We shall now take a closer look at the functions *dot product* and *cross product*.
