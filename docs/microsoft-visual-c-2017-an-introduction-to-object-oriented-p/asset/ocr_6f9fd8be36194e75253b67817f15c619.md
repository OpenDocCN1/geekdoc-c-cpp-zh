**Figure 4-1** Flowchart steps and pseudocode of a sequence structure

Sometimes, logical steps do not follow in an unconditional sequence. Instead, some tasks might or not occur based on the outcome of some decisions. To represent a decision, flowchart creators use a diamond shape to hold a question, and they draw paths to alternative courses of action emerging from the corner of the diamond. Figure 4-2 shows a flowchart describing directions in which the execution of some depends on a decision. Figure 4-2 shows a **decision structure**—one that involves choosing between alternative courses of action based on a value.
