Theorem 4.6 suggests a strategy for generating numbers from the normal distribution. Generate two uniforms $U_1$ and $U_2$ from the interval $[-1, 1]$. If $U_1^2+U_2^2 \le 1$, accept the pair or use pseudo-random normal deviates (4.18)-(4.19). Otherwise, generate another pair and repeat the accept-reject evaluation. This approach discards $100(\frac{\pi}{4}-1)=27.32395\%$ of the uniform random number rejection (Exercise 4.35).
