# Analysis

The coord structure is defined on lines 15 through 18 with its two members, x and y. Lines 20 through 23 declare and define an instance, called mybox, of the rectangle structure. The two members of the rectangle structure are topleft and bottomrt, both of type coord.

Lines 29 through 39 fill the values in the mybox structure. At first, it might seem that there are only two values to fill because mybox has only two members.
