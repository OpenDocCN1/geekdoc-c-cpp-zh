A for statement is a looping statement. It can have an initialization, test condition, and increment as parts of its command. The for statement executes the initial expression first. It then checks the condition. If the condition is true, the statements executes. When the statements are completed, the increment expression is evaluated. The for statement rechecks the condition and continues to loop until the condition is false.
