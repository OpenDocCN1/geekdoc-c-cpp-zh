The thoracotomy has a greater chance of causing death than the alternative treatment of radiation. If we let \( T \) be the decision concerning which treatment to have, \( t1 \) be the choice to undergo a thoracotomy, \( t2 \) be the choice to undergo radiation, and \( ThorDeath \) be a variable whose values are \( tdie \) and \( tlive \) depending on whether the patient dies from the treatment, we have

\( P(ThorDeath = tdie | T = t1) = .037 \)
\( P(ThorDeath = tdie | T = t2) = .002 \).
