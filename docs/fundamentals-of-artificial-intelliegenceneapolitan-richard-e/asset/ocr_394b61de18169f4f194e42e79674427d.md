Pearl [1986] conjectured that uncertain knowledge is structured with causal edges between propositions, we can represent this knowledge with a causal network, and we can model uncertain reasoning by traversing links in this network. The model does not entail that the entire network exists at a cognitive level at any particular time. Rather, it says that a human develops individual causal links between pairs of propositions and recalls and reasons with these links as needed.
