# ## 9.4 Discussion and Further Reading

The remaining two areas of evolutionary computation are evolutionary programming and evolution strategies. Evolutionary programming is similar to genetic algorithms in that it uses a population of candidate solutions to evolve into an answer to a specific problem. Evolutionary programming differs in that the concentration is on developing behavioral models of the observable systems interaction with the environment. Fogel [1994] presents this approach. Evolution strategies models problem solutions as species. Rechenberg [1994] says that the field of evolution strategies is based on the evolution of evolution. See [Kennedy and Eberhart, 2001] for a complete introduction to all four areas of evolutionary computation.
