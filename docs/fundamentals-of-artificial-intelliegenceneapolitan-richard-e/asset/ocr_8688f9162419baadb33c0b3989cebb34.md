# 8.8.14 Vehicle Control and Malfunction Diagnosis

+   1. Horvitz et al. [1992] describe Vista, which is a decision-theoretic system used at NASA Mission Control Center in Houston. The system uses Bayesian networks to interpret live telemetry, and it provides advice on the likelihood of alternative failures of the space shuttle's propulsion systems. It also considers time criticality and recommends actions of the highest expected utility. Furthermore, the Vista system employs decision-theoretic methods for controlling the display of information to dynamically identify the most important information to highlight.
