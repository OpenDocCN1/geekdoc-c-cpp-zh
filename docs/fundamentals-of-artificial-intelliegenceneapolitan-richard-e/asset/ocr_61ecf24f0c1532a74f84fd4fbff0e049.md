There are two problems with the decision tree approach just introduced. First, experts systems are often developed by eliciting information or knowledge from an expert. It may be difficult for a botanist, for example, to identify the entire decision tree from the top down needed for a classification system. Second, even if the entire tree was developed, if a mistake were made and information needed to be added or delete it would be necessary to break edges in the tree and perhaps add new ones. So, the tree would need to be reconstructed each time it was necessary to change the knowledge in the system.
