# Large-Scale Systems

The method used to control our robot could be used in a more complex system. For example, an autonomous vehicle could use a vision-based, lane-position sensor to keep it in the center of its lane. The position sensor’s accuracy would be affected by rain and an uneven road surface. Also, both rain and a bumpy road could cause the position sensor to fail.
