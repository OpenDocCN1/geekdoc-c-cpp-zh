Definition 2.8 A proposition is in disjunctive normal form if it is the disjunction of conjunctive clauses.♦

Definition 2.9 A proposition is in conjunctive normal form if it is the conjunction of disjunctive clauses.♦

Example 2.21 The following propositions are in disjunctive normal form:

(P ∧ Q) ∨ (R ∧ ¬P)

(P ∧ Q ∧ ¬R) ∨ (S) ∨ (¬Q ∧ T)

The following proposition is not in disjunctive normal form because R∨S∧Q is not a conjunctive clause:

(P ∧ Q) ∨ (R ∨ S ∧ Q)

The following propositions are in conjunctive normal form:

(P ∨ Q) ∧ (R ∨ ¬P)

(P ∨ Q ∨ ¬R) ∧ (S) ∧ (¬Q ∨ T)

The following proposition is not in conjunctive normal form because R∨S∧Q is not a disjunctive clause:

(P ∨ Q) ∧ (R ∨ S ∧ Q)
