# Space Exploration

The moon landing on July 21, 1969, was perhaps the most complex and ambitious engineering project in the United States' history. Apollo XI was launched on July 16, 1969 and was the first manned lunar mission. Apollo XI astronaut Neil A. Armstrong took his first step on the moon on July 21, and his footprint, in the dust of the lunar surface, was photographed.

The exploration in space has motivated recent engineering achievements in the collection of scientific data. The Mars Exploration Rovers are designed to move across the surface of the planet and examine the soil and rocks in fine detail. The Mars Exploration Rover mission is part of NASA's Mars Exploration Program, a long-term effort of robotic exploration of the red planet. Primary among the mission's scientific goals is to search for and characterize a wide range of rocks and soils that hold clues to past water activity on Mars. The spacecraft is targeted to sites on opposite sides of Mars that appear to have been affected by liquid water in the past.
