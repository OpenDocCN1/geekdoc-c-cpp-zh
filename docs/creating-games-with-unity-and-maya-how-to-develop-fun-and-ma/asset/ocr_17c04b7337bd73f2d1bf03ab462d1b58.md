**Step 9:** Adjust the mib_amb_occlusion node. In the Hypershade, double-click the mib_amb_occlusion1 node. This will open its attributes in the Attribute Editor. Change the samples to 256 (the rest of the settings should be fine).

# Why?

A low sample rate will render quickly but will be very grainy. In some cases this may be the desired effect, but not in this one. By turning up the sample rate, the AO will be smoother and more refined.

**Step 10:** Change the render settings to render with Mental Ray. Go to Window>Rendering Editors>Render Settings. Change the Render Using drop-down to mental ray. Click the Quality tab and change the Quality Presets to Production.

# Why?

The ambient occlusion node we are using is a mental ray only node.

**Step 11:** Apply the surface shader to AegisChung. You can do this by middle-mouse-dragging the surface shader from the Hypershade onto the geometry in the view panel. Or, in the view panel, right-click-hold Aegis and select Assign Existing Material>surfaceShader1 from the hotbox menu.
