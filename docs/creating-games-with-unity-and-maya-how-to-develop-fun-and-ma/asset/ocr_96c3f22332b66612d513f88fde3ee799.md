**Step 4:** Fix errant normals. If your model has normals facing the wrong way, switch to face mode and double-click one of the incorrectly oriented faces. This will select outward until it hits polys that have normals facing in another direction. Select Polygons>Normals>Reverse.

**Step 5:** Double-check one more time that all normals are correct.

# ## Why?

Reversed normals are just a general pain. It's always a major annoyance to discover a see-through polygon in Unity when you've moved on to other Unity-specific issues. With a bit of careful inspection, a lot of bothersome stops in procedure can be prevented.

**Step 6:** Hunt and destroy five-sided polygons. In object mode, select AC_AegisChung. Choose Mesh>Cleanup... In the Cleanup Effect section change the Operation radio button to Select Matching Polygons. In the Fix by Tesselation section check the Faces With More Than 4 Sides check box. Click the Apply button (Figure 9.2). This will highlight all the faces with more than four sides. Use the Split Polygon tool to cut these bad boys into three- and four-sided polygons.
