# ### Mandible

One of the things Jake's models are always very good at is appropriate topology. The organization of polygons (and particularly, loops of polygons) is very important for face models because the face is a very complex form, and if the polygons are oriented incorrectly, and there just aren't the places to bend, it's impossible to get the right form. Picking where to extrude what polygons becomes a very important process, and is a big reason why I prefer this additive process to others that might be built from a cube.
