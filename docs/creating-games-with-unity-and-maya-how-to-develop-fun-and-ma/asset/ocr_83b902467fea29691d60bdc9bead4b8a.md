Shadows are drawn as maps laid over the top of existing texture. In 3D-speak these are referred to as shadow maps. When using Maya or other 3D applications, drawing the shadow map can indeed be a slow process as the software figures out where to draw these dark blobs of pixels. A shadow map is essentially an image with a finite resolution (and, like any raster image, can look pixelated when viewed at a close range).
