# Conclusion

There is almost always a bit of massaging that can be done with lighting and baking, even after you have to move on to other parts of the game. Lighting can be tricky because a scene may look too bright on one monitor and too dark on another. There are some predictable differences as a game moves from PC to Mac (Mac will be brighter), and it is hard to know when a scene will look its best on most platforms. I find that a baked solution that finally looks good on my machine needs to be run (as a build) on other machines before I'm confident with the solution.

The baked solutions that Unity produces can be further adjusted in Photoshop.
