# Why?

Virtually simply changes and forces the direction of the drawn circle. Although the circles could remain parallel to the ground this sometimes makes the handle difficult to see. By rotating them up on their side they are easy to see sticking out of Aegis's back and help define a little easier what the back rotation will usually be doing.
