> > **Why?**
> >

However, if this is imported into Maya at too high a resolution, Maya can be dragged to a crawl, but too low a resolution can mean that any detail (like the face) is just a patchwork of blotchy pixels. By working with a high-resolution head, most of the critical information will be available for that detailed modeling; then later, when it's time to do the body, this image can be swapped out for a lower-resolution body.
