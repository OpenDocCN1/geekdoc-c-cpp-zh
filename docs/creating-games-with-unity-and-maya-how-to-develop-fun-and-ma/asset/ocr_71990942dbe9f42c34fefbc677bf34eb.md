# Tips and Tricks

In the style sheets (the drawings) there sometimes are some areas where the front and side don't line up perfectly. This mandible area is one of those. Be sure to be looking at the perspective view to get the right look and anatomy. While good style sheets are really close in the side and front drawings, it's very difficult to have them exactly right in every place.
