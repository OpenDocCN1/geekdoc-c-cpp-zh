# Tips and Tricks

Notice that in Figure 9.28, projection has moved around a lot to get into that place. The manipulator handles can be really tough to see, especially with a strong texture pattern like the checkerboard. With lots of quick switches between textured or not (pressing 5 or 6), it can be easier to see and manipulate the handles, see the results, then back to seeing the handles.
