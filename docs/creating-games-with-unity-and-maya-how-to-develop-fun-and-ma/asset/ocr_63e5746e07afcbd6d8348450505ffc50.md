# ### Why?

Figure 6.21 seems to show a pretty sparse placement of trees and bushes. And it is. The point of the trees is to help shake up the profile of the land a bit, but since this game calls for a foggy outside, we only want to catch a hint of those trees. No need to have trees in areas of the Terrain that will never be seen, so careful placement will continue to keep the data set small and the game snappy.

# ### Tips and Tricks

Remember that in this game, the scene will never be seen from way up in the God-mode that the trees are painted in. When painting in God-mode, the trees will be continually popping in and out of billboards and sometimes look really strange on the Terrain. Be sure to get down close to see how the trees really lie across the Terrain. Further, be sure to Play the game and see which of the trees you can see and which are just invisible. Get rid of the stuff not needed and be sure to understand really how those trees look and feel in the game.
