# Build Settings

Builds are actually what Unity outputs. People play the builds that Unity produces. Part of Unity's strength is the ability to author once and then output builds for multiple platforms. Incursion is undoubtedly too heavy for mobile devices, although with a bit of poly paring down and the appropriate licenses it could ultimately be published to Android or iOS.

The Build Settings are where the target platform is defined, and the levels to be included are indicated. To access the Build Settings go to File>Build Settings (Figure 12.6).
