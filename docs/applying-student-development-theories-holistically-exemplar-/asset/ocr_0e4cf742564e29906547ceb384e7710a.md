As a reminder, involving student staff members in every part of the IAP process is critical. If supervisors are hesitant to relinquish some power in the working relationship to share the responsibility of creating an IAP, student staff can have a difficult time understanding how to take charge of their own experiences within their role. Some student staff members may expect to be told what to do and how to do it at all times.

# Table 7.2 Individualized Accountability Plan Sample Questions for Student Staff

| | |
|---|---|
| REFLECT: | What went well with my IAP last semester?
What would I like to do better?
What will my goals be for myself this semester? |
| YOUR IAP SHOULD INCLUDE: | Personal goals for the RA job.
Programming requirements, including your individualized dates.
Professional development & cultural competency facilitation.
Organizational system: How will you keep track of dates/times/email info/verbal info? |
| HOW WILL I MANAGE MY TIME? | How will you let your supervisor know that you have completed your work?
What happens when you do not complete a task?
How do we remedy this?
Caveat: When there is a departmental expectation, sometimes your supervisor cannot be flexible.
How will you be aware of your task load and verbalize when you need help? If you need extra time on a task, how do you communicate that to Liz or the staff (where appropriate)? How far in advance will you communicate this need?
When you read an email with lots of information and directions, how do you ensure that you have understood all of the information? |
