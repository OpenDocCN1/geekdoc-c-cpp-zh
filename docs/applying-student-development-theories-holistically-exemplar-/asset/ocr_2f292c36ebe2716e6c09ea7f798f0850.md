On the far extreme are scholars who thrive by developing theory, models of practice, and doing original research, making recommendations to practice but not engaging in the design or applications of practice. All readers should locate their own professional identity on that continuum. I sincerely hope that identity includes being a scholar-practitioner.

Student development applications are not magic. They are grounded in theory and relevant research that ensure they will promote the desired outcomes in the college experience. Keep sharing your own good practices as well as lessons from practices that were not as successful to advance this professional work. Students deserve it.
