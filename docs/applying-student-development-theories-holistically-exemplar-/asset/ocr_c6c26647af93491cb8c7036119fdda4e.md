# ***Student Employee Hiring and Training in University Program Board***

The hiring and training processes of the campus University Program Board include a focus on ethical reasoning. During an executive council group interview process, students are asked to use the 8KQ in a circle process to decide what to do in a scenario based on an incident the board faced regarding selection of a controversial movie to show on campus. Interviewers (staff and students currently in the role) observe which of the 8KQ interviewees reference in their answers, how well they interrogate their instincts, and how they work together to make decisions.

Once students are hired, they engage in longer training using the 8KQ to analyze and make decisions about other ethical scenarios they may face in their roles, including organization membership issues and inappropriate performers. Throughout the year, the students use the 8KQ to debate a course of action when actual ethical scenarios arise.

# ***Student Conduct Educational Workshops***

As part of educational programming for minor student conduct offenses, the Office of Student Accountability and Restorative Practices staff members utilize the 8KQ to help students examine their values and behaviors. In the *Values in Action* program, they discuss scenarios where two or more values were in conflict and use the 8KQ to make sense of that situation. Students are also asked to reflect on a personal ethical decision and how it might be different had they used the 8KQ. Both activities support the overarching goal of helping students consider their own values before acting. In the *Calling the Shots* program, after students participate in discussions to establish the benefits and drawbacks of alcohol use, they ask the 8KQ to help them identify and consider the broader complexities in the decision whether to drink alcohol.

The office also conducts both small- and large-scale restorative circles surrounding various issues of harm to the community. In reflection papers and group circle processes, students are asked to use the questions to consider the harms inflicted on others and what ways those may be effectively repaired.
