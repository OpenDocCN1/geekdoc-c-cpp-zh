# RECOMMENDATIONS AND RESOURCES

It is important to instill a nuanced understanding of the 8KQ and to ensure that students understand the larger concepts of each question, not just the key words or phrases as they are stated in the programs. To achieve these goals, we make the following recommendations.

# Allot Significant Time for Development

Thorough and non-confirmatory usage of the questions also requires significant time. When students analyze a scenario, allow plenty of time for self-interrogation; stretching their minds is a process. We encourage students to acknowledge an immediate reaction, set it aside, and ask questions in each of the ethical areas to develop this practice.

# Offer Repeated 8KQ Application Opportunities

Multiple interactions with the 8KQ allow students to develop the habit of asking questions as part of their decision-making and to recognize when ethical dilemmas arise. Using the 8KQ in groups is also an important developmental tool to expose students to diverse viewpoints and stimulate dissonance.

# Explore Resources About the Program

Resources available at the program’s website include the Eight Key Question Handbook (The Madison Collaborative, 2013) that explains each of the 8KQ, the JMU Ethical Reasoning Rubric© (2014) used to rate student assessment essays and understand what is considered proficient in ethical reasoning at JMU, and a link to request 8KQ educational materials.

As partners in planning and delivering It’s Complicated to incoming students, the office of Orientation hosts the website for students to access their pre-move-in assignment related to ethical reasoning. On this site, you may read the written version and view the video of the current case study used for the program. The detailed guide or script designed yearly for It’s Complicated facilitators is available upon request.
