Students indicated that having a friend to turn to meant opportunities to reflect on the differences that made them unique and provided the support and encouragement necessary to see that these unique differences should be celebrated and embraced rather than ignored or viewed in a negative context.

# *Moving Beyond Difference to Reflection on Ethnic Identity*

While our survey data overall suggests students have meaningful exchanges with students from another culture, we have asked, “Does Tippie Buddies provide a platform for students to reflect on their own ethnic identities?” To answer our question, we sought open-ended feedback from participants with at least one semester completed in the program, and had at least one semester to reflect on their experiences. We found many students believed that participation in the program led to a greater understanding of their own ethnic identities:

+   - “The program was helpful in giving me a better understanding of my own ethnic identity by my buddy pointing out the ‘different’ and ‘strange’ things we do here. Our dialogue usually consisted of discussing similarities and differences among our cultures. Having my buddy bring certain norms and beliefs that I hold up as being unique to our culture gave me a better understanding of the ethnic identity I hold.”
+   - “It definitely made me aware of who I am and where I belong. It made me compare myself with other people around me and also made me realize that I’m somewhat different from other people in the way of my thinking and behavior. I got to talk to many people and sometimes I thought ‘no, that’s not a way of thinking or to behave I can identify myself with,’ which doesn’t mean there is right or wrong. It just made me realize how accustomed I am to my home country and the way things are regulated there.”
+   - “Every time I experience the cultural differences, I realize a unique part in my own ethnic identity. The more cultural differences I come across, I know my own ethnic identity better.”
