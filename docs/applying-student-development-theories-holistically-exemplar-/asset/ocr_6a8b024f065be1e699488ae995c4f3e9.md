For example, several students remembered how issues of personal space, and the cultural context of what that means when interacting with others, prompted more critical examination of how to be sensitive to others’ preferences.

# *International Students Experience Difference Within a Welcoming Support Structure*

For international students in particular, the program appears beneficial because, despite the university’s efforts, it can be difficult to build meaningful friendships with students of different cultural backgrounds, particularly at a PWI. For many international students, the idea of difference is inherent from the time they enroll at the university, not the least of which stems from the strict regulations that govern their stay. However, like many domestic students, this is still a crucial time in their development, and we see many students with identities still in flux.
