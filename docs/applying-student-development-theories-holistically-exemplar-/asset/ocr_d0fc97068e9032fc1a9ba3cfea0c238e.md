# LESSONS FROM APPLYING THEORY ARTFULLY

The incorporations of the LPM and the concept of intersectionality have truly transformed the work we do in the Pride Center. Since implementation in 2015, the Pride Center has seen markedly increased engagement in programming. This increased engagement can be attributed in large part to the deep learning and community building (and, therefore, increased investment and productivity) that has occurred within the student staff team. Admittedly, the development and implementation of this model have not been easy. Often at our institution, student staff members are given task-oriented roles to fill a need within a department; work-study or wage positions are rarely seen as developmental opportunities. Because of this, the office has faced the challenge of creating a unique, impactful experience in an institutional context where little precedent exists.

Though our model intentionally creates and advances developmental outcomes, we also aspire to maintain a balance between these outcomes and the core functions of our space. This balance includes planning large- and small-scale programming for our campus community. As a result, we have been challenged to be intentional in the ways in which we structure our limited time with student staff members. For example, during the weeks leading up to the larger-scale, signature events, many perfunctory tasks need to be discussed and executed during the student staff meetings. However, we ensure time and space for self-exploration and self-care. During periods of the semester where programming is less demanding, we dedicate extended time to professional development, discussions about personal identities, and community building. This investment throughout the student staff experience ultimately makes the implementation of programming more impactful, as the ideas that are generated by the student staff are grounded in intersectional thinking, and the execution of their ideas is made more meaningful by the synergistic community of our team. Most important, the environment we have created allows for intentional reflection after implementation of each program, both individually as well as collectively.
