# COLLEGE OUTCOMES

Many years ago my dear friend Alexander 'Sandy' Astin addressed what he called "the black box" between student inputs (i.e., who they are and what they bring) and the college outcomes they achieve. Astin's college impact model, known as inputs-environment-outputs, or I-E-O (1993; see Figure 0.2), addresses the impact of environmental experiences, including pedagogical practices that are intentionally designed to interact with salient dimensions of student inputs leading to desired college outcomes.
