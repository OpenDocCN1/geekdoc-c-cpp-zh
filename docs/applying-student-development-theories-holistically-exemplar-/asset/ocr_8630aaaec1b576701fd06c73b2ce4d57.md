# Shape the Environment to Specifically Meet the Needs of FGS at Your Institution

Feedback and involvement from FGS are essential to shaping the organizational, and hopefully, institutional, environments to meet their needs. It is difficult to predict the needs and demographics of FGS at any particular institution. The WIGS student organization exists at a midsize, four-year, regional state institution—thus, more research regarding the experience of FGS at other institutions is needed. Creating FGS student support systems at various institutions can help develop more well-rounded research about the FGS student experience and, subsequently, how to better support FGS.

# Consider How Best to Support FGS at Your Institution

Practitioners seeking to create student organizations similar to WIGS may want to consult with the office of student involvement about how to start a student organization and to learn the requirements for getting an organization recognized.

There are many ways to support FGS in addition to creating student organizations. Initiatives such as *I’m First* (Center for Student Opportunity, 2017), created by and for FGS, provides on-line multimedia resources for institutions and FGS. This resource allows others to listen to student stories and learn about initiatives throughout the country to assist in creating FGS spaces.
