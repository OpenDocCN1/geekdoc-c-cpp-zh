# Implementation Specifics

Initially, the advising team developed ideas for recruitment and promotion, and identified the organization’s values. WIGS would be open to all self-identified FGS. We agreed that, if the organization attracted no students, it would not be pursued further; we wanted WIGS to be an organization created by the students, for the students. Advertising involved a table display in the middle of campus, where we recruited the initial group of student members. On the first day, more than 60 students signed up to join the organization.

Throughout the initial semester, we worked with student members to create a name, logo, and motto. "Western 1st Generation Society" became our name and "First in Line" became the motto. We also considered what the core values (i.e., dedication, grit, curiosity, community, and integrity) meant for the organization as a whole. During this process, members were able to build a general sense of community that was further enhanced by having different group members and advisors share personal FGS stories at each meeting. Our approach to creating this organization served as a way for everyone to connect through common experiences, which is why we decided to use this process each semester.

The second semester, W1GS members and advisors worked to create governing documents for the organization. The students held elections for President and Treasurer and registered WIGS as an official student organization. One major task in this process was drafting a constitution, which includes the developmental outcomes. The constitution also defines a nontraditional meeting structure; weekly meetings are run as a collaborative effort among the members, rather than a positional effort. W1GS alternates weekly between business meetings and social meetings. Social meetings allow members to continue building connections with one another, their campus, and their community. Business meetings provide members time to discuss ideas and make organizational and logistical decisions.

Today, W1GS claims more than 165 members. Approximately 10 to 15 G S consistently attend weekly meetings. All members of the organization receive weekly emails containing resources for FGS and the upcoming WIGS meeting agenda. Members are also invited to join the WIGS Facebook group and to communicate via the GroupMe® texting application.
