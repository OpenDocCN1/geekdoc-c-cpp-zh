**Gordon S. Louie** earned both a bachelor’s degree and a master’s degree in History and a Graduate Certificate in Higher Education from the University of Nevada, Las Vegas. While in graduate school, he began working in higher education through a grant for program development/assessment, and has since gained experience in academic support services, admissions/financial aid, academic advising, and international programs. Alongside his role as the Assistant Director, Global Community Engagement, in the Tippie College of Business at the University of Iowa, he is currently a PhD student in the Educational Policy and Leadership Studies program at Iowa. His current research interests and projects include ethnic identity exploration for international students, and the points of intersection/divergence in policies regarding campus diversity and internationalization.

**Marcus Mayes** graduated from the University of Missouri with his BS in Business Administration and a master’s degree in Education Leadership and Policy Analysis. He co-founded the Mizzou Black Men’s Initiative in 2009 and served as the program coordinator until 2013, providing guidance, support, and mentorship to program participants as they transitioned from high school to college. In his current role as the Coordinator of Student Activities at the University of Texas, Austin, Marcus coordinates development and recognition programs for registered student organizations on the 40 Acres, and co-advises Student Government, Senate of College Councils, and the Graduate Student Assembly.

**Jonathan A. McElderry** serves as the Assistant Dean of Students and Director of the Intercultural Center at Wake Forest University. His experience in higher education has focused on enhancing diversity, equity, and inclusion on college campuses. Additionally, his research has sought to raise awareness of the experiences of underrepresented students at Predominantly White Institutions and provide strategies to increase their academic and social success. He has presented both nationally and internationally, has held leadership positions within the American College Personnel Association, and has received several awards for his work in higher education. Jonathan earned a PhD in Educational Leadership and Policy Analysis with an emphasis in Higher Education from the University of Missouri and an MEd in College Student Personnel from Ohio University.
