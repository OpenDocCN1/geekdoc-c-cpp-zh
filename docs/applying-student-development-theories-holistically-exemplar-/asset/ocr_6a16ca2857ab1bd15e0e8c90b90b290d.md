One third of Randolph-Macon students are athletes, and nearly a third (29%) are in Greek Letter organizations. Seventy-eight percent of the students identify as White, 8% as Black, 4% as Hispanic/Latino, 4% as multiracial, 2% as Asian American, and 4% as other or unknown. More than half of the students (55%) receive need-based financial aid. The six-year graduation rate is 58% (Office of Institutional Research, 2017; SCHEV, 2017).

In 2010, the RMC historical average freshman-to-sophomore retention rate was approximately 75%, and the two years prior to the 2015 *Launch* retreat, it had risen to 77% (2013 cohort) and 78% (2014 cohort) (SCHEV, 2017). In 2015, RMC faculty, staff, and administrators turned their attention to addressing new students’ developmental considerations, and *Launch* was conceived as an effort to continue to improve retention.

# ## DESCRIPTION OF LAUNCH

During the summers of 2015 and 2016, incoming students were invited to attend a four-day, pre-move-in retreat called *Launch*. Sixty-eight students participated (28 in 2015, 6% of the incoming class; 42 in 2016, 10% of the class). The initial invitation targeted students of color, community college transfer students, and first-generation students—students who typically have lower retention rates in colleges and universities (National Student Clearinghouse Research, 2017; National Student Clearinghouse Research, 2012; Engle & Tinto, 2008).

The Director of Student Transitions in the Higgins Academic Center administered *Launch*. The Academic Center assists students through their academic and social transition by offering free tutoring, mentoring, and disability support services. The Academic Center has a robust upper-level student staff with 37 tutors and 36 mentors, several of whom hold both positions. Both the tutoring and mentoring programs are certified by the College Reading and Learning Association; the staff members meet rigorous national selection and training requirements. *Launch* student mentors were selected from the tutor and mentor staff, and received additional training specific to *Launch*’s developmental goals.
