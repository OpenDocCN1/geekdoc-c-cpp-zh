As is evident, student staff members are not left to create what the expectations of their role should be; rather, they do have the opportunity to shape what their particular experience looks like and feels like on a day-to-day basis within the context of larger parameters.

To create an IAP, student staff members take the parameters set forth by their supervisors and examine the best way to go about meeting those expectations. In several cases at Miami University, RAs were given a list of questions they needed to answer in order to formulate what their IAPs would look like. These questions can guide the creation of IAPs with any student staff, or are modifiable for the user’s needs.
