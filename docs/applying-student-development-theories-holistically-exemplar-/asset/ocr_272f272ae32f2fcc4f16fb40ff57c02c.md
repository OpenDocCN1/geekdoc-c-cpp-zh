**Sharee L. Myricks** is the Assistant Director of the Indiana University-Purdue University Indianapolis (IUPUI) and Ivy Tech Coordinated Programs (Passport) Office. She completed her MEd at Texas A&M University within the Student Affairs Administration in Higher Education program. Within higher education, Sharee utilizes her diverse experiences to successfully collaborate with students, faculty, staff, and community partners. Sharee has authored several publications and presents regularly at National Association of Student Personnel Administrators conferences on topics focused to enhance the career development and organizational leadership of new professionals within higher education. Additionally, her research focuses on resiliency of women within higher education.

**Christine Nguyen** is originally from Iowa and earned her undergraduate degree in Psychology from Cornell College. She completed her master’s degree at Western Illinois University in College Student Personnel, where she gained paraprofessional experiences in career development, leadership development, and residence life. In her first professional position, she worked as Director of Health and Wellness Services at Trine University in Angola, Indiana. She currently works as an Academic Advisor for the College of Arts & Sciences Honors Program at The Ohio State University.

**Vanessa Perez** has been a higher education professional since 2012. She earned a Master of Public Policy degree from Rutgers and has served in student affairs roles at New York University (NYU), Abu Dhabi, and NYU, New York. She currently focuses on international initiatives and continuing education with senior leadership at the NYU College of Dentistry. Vanessa’s diverse experiences have afforded her the opportunity to apply theories to practice in a variety of professional settings, thus inspiring reflection, assessment, and innovation in her work. Vanessa is a New Jersey native who is passionate about equity and inclusion and plans to pursue a doctorate in the future.
