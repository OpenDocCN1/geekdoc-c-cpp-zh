# RECOMMENDATIONS AND RESOURCES

The *Passion to Action* program was created to respond to a perceived gap in comprehension and implementation of diversity and social justice efforts among the undergraduate paraprofessional staff members. To ensure that the program—or similar programs—are sustainable, we recommend the following:

# Align with Departmental Objectives

New York University’s RLHS provided five departmental program learning goals: Life Skills, Multiple Perspectives, Community, Diversity, and Wellness. Each hall’s leadership team defined how these goals would be achieved within their communities. Prior to *Passion to Action*, in Lafayette Hall, undergraduate paraprofessional staff members were submitting program proposals heavily representing Life Skills, Multiple Perspectives, Community, and Wellness, but few for the Diversity goal. One of the goals of *Passion to Action* was to enhance the alignment between the expectations of the department and the offerings in the building, and to facilitated programming that supported the Diversity goal.
