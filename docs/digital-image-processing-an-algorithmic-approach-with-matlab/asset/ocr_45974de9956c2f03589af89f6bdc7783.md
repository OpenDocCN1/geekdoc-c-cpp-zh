+   • Some form of variable-length coding technique is then used to encode the final coefficients.
+   • For decompression, the reverse procedure is utilized.
+   • The most commonly used transforms are the discrete wavelet transform and discrete cosine transform.
+   • The most commonly used coding techniques are Huffman coding and arithmetic coding.
+   • The JPEG compression standard utilizes all of the aforementioned techniques.
+   • If information is heavily lost in the quantization step, the decompression will not produce the original image, and the compression is termed lossy compression.
