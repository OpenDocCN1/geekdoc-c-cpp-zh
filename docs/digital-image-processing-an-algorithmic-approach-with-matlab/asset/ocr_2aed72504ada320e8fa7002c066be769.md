Because JPEG trades accuracy for compression, the more the file is compressed, the more quality is lost. Three factors affect the quality of the JPEG image:

+   - The amount of data removed during subsampling (one-third or one-half of the file).
+   - How aggressively the data is rounded during quantization.
+   - The accuracy of the JPEG viewer in reversing the compression process while expanding the image for display. There are a lot of calculations involved, and some viewers trade accuracy for speed.
