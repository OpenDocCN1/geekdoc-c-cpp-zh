| Operation | Operators |

| **Translation:** Pixel movement by \( b_1 \) in x and \( b_2 \) in y direction. The treatment of elements in near image edges varies with implementation. Translation is used to improve visualization of an image, but also has a role as a preprocessor in applications where registration of two or more images is required. | \( A = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix} \), \( B = \begin{bmatrix} b_1 \\ b_2 \end{bmatrix} \) |
| **Rotation:** Rotating all pixels by an angle of \( \theta \) degrees (counterclockwise for positive angle).
