# ## ABSTRACT

Early-life diets may have a long-lasting impact on metabolic health. This study tested the hypothesis that an early-life diet with large, phospholipid-coated lipid droplets (Concept) induces sustained improvements of hepatic mitochondrial function and metabolism. Young C57BL/6 mice were fed Concept or control (CTRL) diet from postnatal day 15 (PN15) to PN42, followed by western style (WSD) or standard rodent diet (AIN) until PN98. Measurements comprised body composition, insulin resistance (HOMA-IR), tricarboxylic acid (TCA) cycle- and β-oxidation-related hepatic oxidative capacity, high-resolution respirometry, mitochondrial DNA content, markers of mitochondrial biogenesis and dynamics (PGC1α), markers of endoplasmic reticulum stress as well as systemic oxidative stress.
