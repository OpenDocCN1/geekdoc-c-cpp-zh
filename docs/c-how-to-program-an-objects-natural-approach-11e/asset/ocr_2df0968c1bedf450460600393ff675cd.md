**9.35**  *(An RSA Algorithm Improvement)* In 1998, an improvement was made to the RSA algorithm replacing Φ(n) with λ(n) (pronounced “lambda of n”).⁶⁷,⁶⁸

λ(n) = lcm((p − 1), (q − 1))

where lcm represents the least common multiple.⁶⁹ We used Φ(n) in the previous RSA exercise with p = 13 and q = 17. The corresponding new λ(n) calculation would be

λ(n) = lcm(12, 16)

where the least common multiple of 12 and 16 is 48, as you can see in the multiples below:

| 12 | 24 | 36 | **48** | … |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 16 | 32 | **48** | … | |
