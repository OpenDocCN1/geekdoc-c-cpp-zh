# # Artificial Intelligence: Evasion

The previous section on artificial intelligence explained the basic concepts of tracking and random motions, but we left out another concept commonly known as evasion. Evasion, in coding terms, is simply the reverse of tracking. Here, rather than moving closer to an object, our opponent's characters are told to avoid it, thus creating the illusion of intelligently driven tactics.
