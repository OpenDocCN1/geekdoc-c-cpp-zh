# Including Obstacles: The Sun

Another excellent way to increase the enjoyment of a game is in the insertion of field obstacles. The first obstacle we'll create is a small sun in the middle of our screen, which is like a little gravity well, slowly pulling in its prey until Bang! The ships make contact with its center. We'll set up the force of gravity to pull once every 20 cycles, displacing the ships with increasing increments (see Examples 3.65 and 3.66).
