# The Pawn

The Pawn can only move forward, straight, and diagonally to capture an opponent's piece. The Pawn is only allowed one step per move, except on his first move, when he has the option of moving up to two steps (see Figures H.10 and H.11).

Pawns also hold a special right to capture other Pawns. This special case is known as the "en Passant," but two conditions must be upheld: 1) The capturing Pawn must be on the fifth rank (ranks counted from their players side); and 2) The opponents Pawn must exercise his initial two-step option. The adjacent Pawn can then step diagonally onto the unoccupied square and take the opponent's Pawn. This special right can only be executed immediately after the two-step advance.
