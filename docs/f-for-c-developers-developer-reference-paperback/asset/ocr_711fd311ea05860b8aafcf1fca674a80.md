The crossover is considered to not introduce diversity, which means there is no new number introduced by the crossover.. The building block in the individual is just moving from one individual to another. The mutation, on the other hand, is considered to be a way to introduce diversity into the population. Depending on evolutionary strategy settings, the crossover rate is usually set to a higher number, such as 90 percent, and the mutation rate is as low as 2 percent. This agrees with the real-world number. To maintain existence, species usually do not have a high mutation rate, but the crossover mate (mating) is high.

# Elitism

Mother Nature does not necessarily give favor to a good individual. The recombination operators, in most cases, generate new individuals by destroying their parents. Even though the best individual is selected every time, it can still be destroyed and the next generation will be downgraded. Most of the GA implementations add the concept of elitism to preserve the best individuals and allow them to go into the next generation without interruption from the recombination operation. This section uses elitism by default.
