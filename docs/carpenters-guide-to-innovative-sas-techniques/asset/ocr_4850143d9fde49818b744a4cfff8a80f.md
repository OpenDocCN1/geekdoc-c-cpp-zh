We can further refine the sequencing by using keyword qualifiers.

The CASE_FIRST=UPPER ❸ qualifier causes uppercase to take priority over lowercase. Notice, however, that the order is still not the same as ASCII which also gives priority to uppercase letters. Here the sensitivity is within the word not across the list of words. The CASE_FIRST keyword qualifier can also take on the value of LOWER.
