+   1. 3.15 **_Target-Heart-Rate Calculator_** While exercising, you can use a heart-rate monitor to see that your heart rate stays within a safe range suggested by your trainers and doctors. According to the American Heart Association (AHA) ([http://bit.ly/AHAtargetHeartRates](http://bit.ly/AHAtargetHeartRates)), the formula for calculating your *maximum heart rate* in beats per minute is 220 minus your age in years. Your *target heart rate* is a range that’s 50–85% of your maximum heart rate. [*Note:* These formulas are estimates provided by the AHA.
