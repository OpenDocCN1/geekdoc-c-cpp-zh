# Ingredients

For the dough:

+   - 2 cups all-purpose flour
+   - 1 teaspoon salt
+   - 1 tablespoon olive oil
+   - 3/4 cup warm water

For the topping:

+   - 1/2 cup tomato sauce
+   - 1 cup shredded mozzarella
+   - Fresh basil leaves

# Instructions

+   1. Mix flour and salt in a bowl.
+   2. Add olive oil and warm water; knead until smooth.
+   3. Let the dough rest for 30 minutes.
+   4. Roll out the dough and spread tomato sauce.
+   5. Top with cheese and basil.
+   6. Bake at 220°C for 15 minutes.
