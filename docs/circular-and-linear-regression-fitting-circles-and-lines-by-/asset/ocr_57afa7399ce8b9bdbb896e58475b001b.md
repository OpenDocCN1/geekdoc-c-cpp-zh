The top portion of Fig. 2.4 is for $n=10$ and the bottom for $n=100$. We see that, as $n$ increases, the accuracy of the approximation (2.24) remains about the same, but that of (2.25) noticeably improves, i.e., the ratio $r_{2}$ gets closer to one as $n$ grows. No doubt, the factor $n-1$ in the second order term is the big helper here.

Also note that the ratios $r_{1}$ and $r_{2}$ are always below 1, i.e., both approximations (2.24) and (2.25) underestimate the true values of Var(\hat{\theta}). Perhap s the third order expansion would do even better, but no formula for it is published in the literature.
