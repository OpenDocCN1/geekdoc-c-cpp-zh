ALTHOUGH THE JUMP in version number by only 0.1 seems to suggest that there's not much new in ASP.NET 4.5.1 or Visual Studio 2013, you'd be surprised by the number of changes — small and large — that made they into these products. I spent the past couple of months working on updating this book for the .NET 4.5 release to the .NET 4.5.1 release. I discovered new features and functionality every day. Some of those changes are really small, but could mean a boost in productivity on a day-to-day basis. Others are much bigger and affect the way you build or deploy your websites.
