# How It Works

The ability to see trace information for pages that have been requested is extremely valuable. The information can help you understand the flow of information from and to a web page. For example, the trace information for the Contact.aspx page also shows the information that users have entered in the text box controls on the page. To see what this information looks like, click the View Details link for an item you want to zoom in on.

Although the information that ASP.NET traces for you automatically is extremely useful, you’re not limited to just this information. You can also add your own information to the trace log.
