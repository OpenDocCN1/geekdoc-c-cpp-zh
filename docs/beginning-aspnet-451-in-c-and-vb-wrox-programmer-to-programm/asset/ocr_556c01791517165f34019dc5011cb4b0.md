The forward slashes (/) in the address have been encoded to their URL-safe counterpart — %2f — automatically. Log in with your Manager account and you should see the Management section appear again. Next, log out again, log in with one of the other accounts, and then try to access the Management folder again. Because that account doesn’t have permissions to access that folder, you’re redirected to the Login page again.
