There are three types of queues: circular queues, deques, and priority queues. Let's learn about these three in the next sections.

# Circular Queues

Consider a scenario where the REAR pointer of a queue has reached the MAXSIZE, but we still want to insert some more elements into the queue. As the REAR pointer has reached the MAXSIZE, this will give rise to the Queue Overflow situation. However, it may happen that plenty of space is still left in the queue, as FRONT might have moved from its initial position after deleting some elements. In such situation, we should not consider that the queue is full. A smarter solution to this problem is to point REAR back to the initial empty positions so that further insertions in the queue can take place. This mechanism is called circular queue. In a circular queue, the queue[1] element comes just after the queue[n] element, where n is the MAXSIZE of the queue. When REAR reaches n, we simply reset REAR to 1.
