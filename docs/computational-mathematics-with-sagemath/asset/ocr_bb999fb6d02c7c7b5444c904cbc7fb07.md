**Algebraic Extensions.** An important special case is the quotient of K[x] by an irreducible polynomial to build an algebraic extension of K. The number fields, finite extensions of Q, are represented by the objects `NumberField`, distinct from the quotients of `QQ['x']`. # ## 7.3 Factorisation and Roots

A third level after the elementary operations and the Euclidean arithmetic concerns the decomposition of a polynomial into a product of irreducible factors, or factorisation. It is maybe where computer algebra is the most useful!
