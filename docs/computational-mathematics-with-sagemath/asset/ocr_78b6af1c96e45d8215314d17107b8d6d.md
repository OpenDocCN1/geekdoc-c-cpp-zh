Here we have presented the simplest variant of the Chinese Remainder Theorem. One can also consider the case of several moduli $m_1, m_2, \ldots, m_k$. The Sage command for finding $x_0$, given $a, b, m, n$, is `crt(a,b,m,n)`:

