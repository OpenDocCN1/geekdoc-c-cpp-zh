This lemma consists of two statements, where substitution is performed in (i) terms, and (ii) expressions.

# Lemma 11 (Substitution).

+   i. If \(\Gamma \vdash^{\text{exp}} v : T'\) and \(\Delta \cdot (\Gamma, x:T') \vdash^w S \vdash t : T <: S'\), then \(\Delta \cdot \Gamma \vdash^{w[x'/x]} S \vdash t[\gamma/x] : T <: S'\)
+   ii. If \(\Gamma \vdash^{\text{exp}} v : T'\) and \(\Gamma, x:T' \vdash^{\text{exp}} e : T\), then \(\Gamma \vdash^{\text{exp}} e[\gamma/x] : T\)

Proof. By induction on the derivation of \(\Delta \cdot (\Gamma, x:T') \vdash^w S \vdash t : T <: S'\) for Item i, and by induction on the derivation of \(\Gamma, x:T' \vdash^{\text{exp}} e : T\) for Item ii. We show the main cases for Item i:

# [TLET]

From the rule, we know that \(t = (x = t_1; t_2)\), and

\(x' \neq w\) (B.9a)

\(\Gamma \vdash^{\text{exp}} v : T'\) (B.9b)

\(\Delta \cdot (\Gamma, x:T') \vdash^w S \vdash t_1 : T'' <: S'\) (B.9c)

\(\Delta \cdot (\Gamma, x:T', x':T'') \vdash^w S' \vdash t_2 : T <: S'\) (B.9d)

The variable binding environment of eq. (B.9d) can be reordered to

\(\Delta \cdot (\Gamma, x':T'', x:T') \vdash^w S' \vdash t_2 : T <: S'\) (B.9e)

We need to show that \(\Delta \cdot \Gamma \vdash^{w[x'/x]} S \vdash x = (t_1; t_2)[\gamma/x] : T <: S'\), which by the Variable Substitution Definition, is equivalent to

\(\Delta \cdot \Gamma \vdash^{w[x'/x]} S \vdash x' = t_1[\gamma/x] : t_2[\gamma/x] : T <: S'\) (B.9f)

for \(x \neq x'\) and \(x' \neq v\). To obtain eq. (B.9f), we need some preliminary results. Applying the inductive hypothesis to eqs. (B.9b) and (B.9c), and similarly to eqs. (B.9b) and (B.9e), results in

\(\Delta \cdot \Gamma \vdash^{w[x'/x]} S \vdash t_1[\gamma/x] : T'' <: S'\) (B.9g)

\(\Delta \cdot (\Gamma, x':T'') \vdash^{w[x'/x]} S'' \vdash t_2[\gamma/x] : T <: S'\) (B.9h)

From eq. (B.9a) and the Variable Substitution Definition we know that \(x \neq w[\gamma/x]\). Applying this information, along with eqs. (B.9g) and (B.9h) to the premise of [rLet] results in eq. (B.9f), as required.

From the rule, we know that for some \(n \in \mathbb{N}\) and

\(\Gamma \vdash^{\text{exp}} v : T'\) (B.10a)

\(t = \text{receive do } (\{ ; l_i, p_i^1, \dots, p_i^n \} \to t_i)_{i \in I} \text{ end}\) (B.10b)

From the premise, we also know that, for all \(i \in I\) and \(j \in 1..n\):

simpeltpat(\(p_i^j, t_i^j\))

\(\Delta \cdot (\Gamma, x:T', \Gamma_i^1, \dots, \Gamma_i^n) \vdash^w S_i \vdash t_i : T <: S'\) (B.10e)

This case holds if the following statement is obtained:

\(\Delta \cdot \Gamma \vdash^{w[x'/x]} S \vdash t[\gamma/x] : T <: S'\) (B.10f)

where \(t[\gamma/x] = \text{receive do } (\{ ; l_i, p_i^1, \dots, p_i^n \} \to t_i)_{i \in I} \text{ end}\). To obtain eq. (B.10f) we need to use the [tBranch] rule which requires multiple premises. Applying the inductive hypothesis to eqs. (B.10a) and (B.10e) results in

\(\Delta \cdot (\Gamma, \Gamma_i^1, \dots, \Gamma_i^n) \vdash^{w[x'/x]} S_i \vdash t_i[\gamma/x] : T <: S'\) for all \(i \in I\) (B.10g)
