**Definition A.2 (Bound Variables).**

\(\mathbf{bv}(t) \stackrel{\text{def}}{=} \begin{cases} \emptyset & t = e \text{ or } t = \text{send}(w, \{..l, \tilde{e}..\}) \text{ or } t = f(\tilde{e}) \\ \{ x \mid x \in \mathbf{bv}(t_1) \cup \mathbf{bv}(t_2) \} & t = (x = t_1; t_2) \\ \bigcup_{i \in I} \mathbf{bv}(t_i) \cup \mathbf{vars}(\tilde{p}_j) & t = \text{receive do } (\{l_i, \tilde{p}_i \rightarrow t_i\})_{i \in I} \text{end} \\ \bigcup_{i \in I} \mathbf{bv}(t_i) \cup \mathbf{vars}(p_i) & t = \text{case } e \text{ do } (p_i \rightarrow t_i)_{i \in I} \text{end} \end{cases}\)

**Definition A.3 (Variable Substitution).**

\( e \lfloor e' / a \rfloor \stackrel{\text{def}}{=} \begin{cases} v & e = x \\ y & e = y, y \neq x \\ b & e = b \\ e_1 \lfloor e' / a \rfloor \diamond e_2 \lfloor e' / a \rfloor & e = e_1 \diamond e_2 \\ \text{not } (e' \lfloor / a \rfloor) & e = \text{not } e' \\ \{ e_1 \lfloor e' / a \rfloor \diamond e_2 \lfloor e' / a \rfloor \} & e = \{ e_1 \diamond e_2 \} \\ \{ e_1 \lfloor y / a \rfloor \dots e_n \lfloor y / a \rfloor \} & e = \{ e_1, \dots, e_n \} \end{cases}\)

\( t \lfloor e' / a \rfloor \stackrel{\text{def}}{=} \begin{cases} \text{send}(w \lfloor e' / a \rfloor, \{..l, e_1 \lfloor e' / a \rfloor, \dots, e_n \lfloor e' / a \rfloor \}) & t = \text{send}(w, \{..l, e_1, \dots, e_n\}) \\ \text{receive do } (\{l_i, \tilde{p}_i \rightarrow t_i \lfloor e' / a \rfloor \})_{i \in I} \text{end} & t = \text{receive do } (\{l_i, \tilde{p}_i \rightarrow t_i\})_{i \in I} \text{end} \\ f (e_1 \lfloor e' / a \rfloor, \dots, e_n \lfloor e' / a \rfloor) & t = f (e_1, \dots, e_n) \\ \text{case } e \lfloor e' / a \rfloor \text{ do } (p_i \rightarrow t_i \lfloor e' / a \rfloor)_{i \in I} \text{end} & t = \text{case } e \text{ do } (p_i \rightarrow t_i)_{i \in I} \text{end} \\ y = t_1 \lfloor e' / a \rfloor; t_2 \lfloor e' / a \rfloor & t = (y = t_1; t_2), x \neq y, y \neq v \end{cases}\)

**Definition A.5 (Variable Patterns).** Computes an ordered set of variables from a given pattern \(p\).

\(\mathbf{vars}(\tilde{p}) \stackrel{\text{def}}{=} \mathbf{vars}(p_1, \dots, p_n) \stackrel{\text{def}}{=} \mathbf{vars}(p_1) \cup \dots \cup \mathbf{vars}(p_n)\)

\(\mathbf{vars}(p) \stackrel{\text{def}}{=} \begin{cases} \emptyset & p = b \\ \{ x \} & p = x \\ \mathbf{vars}(w_1) \cup \mathbf{vars}(w_2) & p = [ w_1 \mid w_2 ] \\ \bigcup_{i \in 1..n} \mathbf{vars}(w_i) & p = [ w_1, \dots, w_n ] \end{cases}\)
