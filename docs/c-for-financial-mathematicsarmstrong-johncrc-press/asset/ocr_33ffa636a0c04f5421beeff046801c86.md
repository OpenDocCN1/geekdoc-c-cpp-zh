The trader’s strategy is to delta hedge this liability. They delta hedge at N discrete time steps. So each time step has length δt = T/N.

We label the times at which the trader hedges with integers i ∈ {0, N − 1}. So time point i takes place at time iδt. At time point i, the trader will ensure that they hold precisely Δ_i units of stock where Δ_i is the Black–Scholes delta of the call option at time point i. Any remaining money is invested in a risk-free account which grows at a fixed interest rate of r.
