# 1.4 What did we want to do with bioinformatics?

Because the origins of bioinformatics were rooted in sequence analysis, the earliest years aimed to understand what biomolecular sequences could tell us about the functions of genes and their encoded proteins. Ultimately, scientists wanted to discover how amino acid sequences identified 3D protein folds, and what their sequences and structures could tell us about their evolutionary histories. Perhaps more importantly, researchers wanted to know how sequence and structure information could be used to elucidate the roles of particular genes and proteins in pathogenic processes, and how the assembled data could be used to design better, more efficacious drugs.
