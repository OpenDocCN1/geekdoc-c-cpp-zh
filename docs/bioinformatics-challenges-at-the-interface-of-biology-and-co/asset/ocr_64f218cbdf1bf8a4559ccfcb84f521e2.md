identifier (ID) – see ID.

identity matrix – see unitary matrix.

IEEE – Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers, a world-wide professional asso-ciation that, amongst other things, acts as a standards for many technology-related industries.

Ile – IUPAC three-letter abbreviation for isoleucine.

imidazole – an aromatic, heterocyclic compound (1,3-diaza-2,3-cyclopentadiene), weakly in solution; its ring-structure is the base of histidine.

imperative programming – the programming paradigm that consists of a series of com-mands to be executed in sequence; this is the most common paradigm in use today.

InChL – IUPAC International Chemical Identifier, an encoding of the structure of small molecules into a compact text string

indel – a term denoting an insertion/deletion ambiguity; between two sequences, say, from different species, it is impossible to infer which part of the sequence or the other species gained the extra part – i.e., the phylogenetic depth of the sequence change cannot be inferred.

informatics – a broad discipline that encompasses aspects of computer science, infor-mation science, information technology, mathematics, social sciences, and so on, informatics concerns the application of computational and statistical techniques to the management, and retrieval of information.

information technology – a field of engineering that concerns the design, development and management of computer-based systems.

INSDC – the International Nucleotide Sequence Database Collaboration, an interna-tional consortium of the nucleotide sequence-data providers; its mission is to synchronize the production of nucleotide sequence data and to present them according to common guidelines that regulate the content and syntax of entries (a common feature definition); its partners are EMBL-Bank, GenBank and DDBJ.

in silico – ‘literally, in ‘silicon’; performed by means of computer.

Institute of Electrical and Electronics Engineers (IEEE) – see IEEE.

insulin – a peptide hormone, responsible for the regulation of carbohydrate and fat metabolism; it mediates the uptake of glucose from the blood and its storage as glycogen in liver, muscle and fat cells; it inhibits the release of glucagon, and ensures the use of it as an energy source.

integer – a natural or rational whole number.

Interface Definition Language (IDL) – see ID.

International Nucleotide Sequence Database Collaboration (INSDC) – see INSDC. Internet – the global network of networks that connects government, ac-ademic and business industries; it grew into a world-wide network of networks that use the Internet Protocol Suite (TCP/IP) was standardised in 1982.

Internet Assigned Names Authority (IANA) – see IANA.

InterPro – an integrated database of protein sequences that serves to automatically annotate proteins and genomes – it classifies sequences at superfamily, family and sub-levels, and predicts functional and structural domains, repeats and functional units, using GO to format these annotations; InterPro’s finding partners were PROSITE, PRINTS, Pfam and ProDom, but it now contains around a dozen partners.

intron – a region of a nucleotide sequence that is not expressed; in eukaryotic DNA, introns are the fragments of sequence between the exons that must be excised from the mRNA coding sequence to be generated.
