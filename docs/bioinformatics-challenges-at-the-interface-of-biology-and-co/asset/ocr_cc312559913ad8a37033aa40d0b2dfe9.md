Mosaic – one of the first Web browsers, with a user-friendly graphical user interface – the first to display images inline with text – and credited with making the Web accessible to the general public.

motif – in the context of protein alignment, a contiguous, conserved region, typically 10–20 amino acids in length, often denoting a key structural or functional feature of its parent proteins; motifs are sometimes exploited by pattern-recognition techniques to decode sequences that share the same features.

MRC – Medical Research Council, the government agency responsible for coordinating and funding medical research in the UK

mRNA – messenger RNA, a class of RNA molecules that carry the genetic information within genes to ribosomes, the sites of protein synthesis, where the chemical message embedded in the mRNAs is translated into a polypeptide by means of tRNA.

Muirhead, Hilary – a crystallographer, known for her work with Max Perutz in determining the 3D structure of hemoglobin; she was jointly responsible for establishing crystallography at Bristol, where she went to determine the structures of a glycolysis enzymes.

Multain – a multiple sequence alignment tool based on dynamic programming and clustering.
multiple alignment – the process of, or the product of the process of, comparing (aligning) more than two sequences of proteins or nucleic acids, necessitating the insertion of gaps to bring equivalent parts of the sequences into vertical register; alignments help to illustrate which sequences are most highly conserved, often scoring functions to help quantify their degree of relatedness.

MUSCLE – Multiple Sequence Comparison by Log-Expectation, a multiple sequence alignment tool based on a progressive alignment algorithm.

mutation – the process by which genetic material undergoes a detectable, heritable structural change, such as a base change in a DNA sequence, a structural change in a chromosome, addition or loss of a chromosome, and so on.

Mycoplasma genitalium – a small parasitic bacterium that lives on the ciliated cells of respiratory and genital tracts; it is the smallest genome that can constitute a cell, and is hence used as a model organism.

myoglobin – iron-containing, oxygen-carrying protein of the muscle tissue of vertebrates, a homologue of haem.
