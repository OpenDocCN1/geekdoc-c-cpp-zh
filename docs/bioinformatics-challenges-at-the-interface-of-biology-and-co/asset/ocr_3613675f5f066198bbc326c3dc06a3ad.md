# Glossary

wireless network – a network that connects computers without the use of cables to make physical connections between them; communication is mediated via radio waves.
wobble – the name given to the tendency of the anticodon of tRNA to bind with less specificity than dictated by Watson-Crick base-pairing.
world-wide Protein Data Bank (wwPDB) – seePDB.
World-Wide Web (WWW) – see WWW.
WormBase – a database of Caenorhabditis genes and genomes, and the primary repository for genetic and molecular data for the model organism C. elegans.
WSDL – Web Service Description Language, an Interface Definition Language (IDL) for Web services.
WTSI – Welcome Trust Sanger Institute, a genomics and genetics research institute, established as a large-scale-DNA-sequencing centre to participate in the Human Genome Project, based on the World Trust Genome Campus, Hinton, UK.
wwPDB – the world-wide Protein Data Bank, an international collaboration of macromolecular-structure providers whose mission is to maintain a single archive of macromolecular structure for storage; its partners are the RCSB PDB, PDBe, PDBj and the Biological Magnetic Resonance Data Bank (BMRB).
WWW – the system of interconnected documents, data, images, videos and so on, accessed via the Internet with browser software.

# X

xenology – relatedness that arises as a result of gene transfer between two organisms.
XML – eensible Markup Language, a textual format for representing arbitrary data structures for storage or transmission.
Xray crystallography – a method for deducing atomic arrangements within crystals using X-ray diffraction; commonly used to determine the structures of small molecules, proteins and nucleic acids.
X-ray diffraction – a technique that exploits the diffraction patterns formed when X-rays passes through crystals, or other regular molecular arrays, to measure inter-atomic distances and to determine the 3D arrangement of atoms (or molecules) in a material; used extensively in the determination of the structures of proteins and nucleic acids.

# Y

Y – the IUPAC single-letter abbreviation for tyrosine.

# Z

Zuckerkandi, Emile – a biologist considered to be one of the pioneers of molecular evolution, and best known for his work with Linus Pauling introducing the concept of the molecular clock.
