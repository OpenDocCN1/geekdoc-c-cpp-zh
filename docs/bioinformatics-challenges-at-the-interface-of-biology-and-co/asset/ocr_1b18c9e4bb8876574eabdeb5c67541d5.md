β-turn (beta-turn) – a basic element of protein secondary structure resembling a hairpin, the polypeptide chain turns back on itself, reversing its direction; often, there is a hydrogen bond between the first and four residues of turn.

Babbage, Charles – mathematician and engineer who conceived the idea of a programmable computer, and is credited with inventing the first mechanical computer.

Babylonians – inhabitants of Babylon, an ancient nation in central-southern Mesopotamia (Iraq); the Babylonians are known for developing methods to predict the motions of the planets, and for using a sexagesimal system of mathematics (a modified form of which provides the basis of our systems for measuring angles, geographic coordinates and time).

back-up – a physical storage device for copying data, particularly to allow restoration of a computer system (data, files, etc.) following a crash, data loss or data corruption.

bacteria – ubiquitous source of prokaryotic microorganisms as single cells or in clusters of single cells; bacteria consist of three kingdoms of life.

bacteriophage – a bacterial virus whose genetic material (circular or single-double-stranded RNA or DNA) is enclosed in a protein capsid.

bacteriophage ϕ174 – a small, single-stranded DNA bacteriophage, the first DNA-genome to be sequenced, by Fred Sanger in 1977.

bacteriophage λ – a small, double-stranded DNA bacteriophage that infects Escherichia coli.

bacterioropsin – a light-driven proton pump used by archaea to traffic protons across membranes, creating chemical energy from the resulting proton gradient.

Bairoch, Amos – a biochemist and person of bioinformatics, responsible for developing and co-developing a range of databases, using Swiss-Prot, PROSITE, ENZYME, SeqAlRef, InterProt, UniProt and nextProt, and analysis software, PC/Gene; he is the co-founder of the Swiss Institute of Bioinformatics, and of the GeneBio and GeneProt products.

base – nucleotide base.

base-calling – see Phred base-calling.
base pair (bp) – pairing between bases in opposing strands of nucleotide sequences (DNA or RNA): adenine with thymine in DNA, or uracil in RNA; guanine with cytosine.

Basic Alignment Search Tool – see BLAST.
basic pancreatic trypsin inhibitor – an inhibitor that reduces the availability of biologically active trypsin in a range of animals, including humans; it weakly inhibits chotry.

baud – in telecommunications and electronics, the number of symbols or pulses per second.

BBN – Bolt, Baranek and Newman, a high-technology company, best known for its work contributing to the development of ARPANET and the Internet.

BCE – Before Common Era, a neutral designation for the calendar era traditionally identified as Before Christ (BC).
