The concept of a 'family' is fluid and can have different meanings depending on
whether the particular grouping has been derived from sequence- structure-
based analysis methods; merging families in resources like InterPro is therefore
problematic;

13 The functions of biological molecules are evolutionarily plastic – highly similar
sequences may have different functions (proteins may moonlight), different
sequences may have similar functions; deriving functional annotations from
quence evidence alone may sometimes be misleading;

14 The functions of multi-domain or modular proteins are multi-faceted and
depend on the nature and choice of their constituent elements – it’s important
to understand which component of a multi-domain protein function
annotation within a database refers

15 The concept of 'function' has been ill-defined; ontologies have been introduced
to try formal functional descriptions in database entries.
