Retinitogenesis (RP) is a neurodegenerative, misfolding disease caused by mutations in rod ops – as EDEM1 is able to promote correct folding and enhanced degradation of mutant proteins in the ER, this suggests that it may be able to combat protein-misfolding diseases. It has been suggested that EDEM1 may clear terribly misfolded P23H rod ops, allowing others to interact with the folding-competent mutant rod ops and assist in productive folding; the other hand, enhanced degradation of mutant ops could reduce aggregation, and hence allow productive folding to occur in the absence of aggregation seeds and proteome inhibition.
