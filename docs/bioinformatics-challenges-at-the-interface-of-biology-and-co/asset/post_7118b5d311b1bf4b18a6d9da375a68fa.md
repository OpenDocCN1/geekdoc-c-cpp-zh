
It turns out, then, that while it's possible for us to make a reasonable attempt to understand what the text means, provided we mentally rearrange the sentence, the task is much harder for a computer, suggesting that the machine's interpretation may not be entirely safe. As hinted above, the authors, editors and/or publishers could have made this more machine accessible had they supplied database identifiers for the main concepts (the drug, the gene, etc.).
