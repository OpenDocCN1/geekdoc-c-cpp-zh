As with Shotton’s adventure into semantic publication, adding this additional narrative on top of existing traditional publications, making precise links to define database entries required some non-trivial manual curation to make sure that the story was precisely the one we wanted to tell; neither the scholarly articles nor databases yet contained enough semantic information for this to be completely automatically. As tantalising as the vision of completely and seamlessly linking research studies with their cognate data may seem, there’s still some way to go before dream becomes a reality.

## 8.11 Summary

This chapter explored some of the problems that arise when trying to bridge the gaps between scientific narratives that make sense to humans and those that are comprehensible by machines, and the challenges we face when we try to join the two together to add meaning to biological data.
