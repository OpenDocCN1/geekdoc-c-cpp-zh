Halobacterium salinity – an extreme halophilic marine archaeon, a Gram-negative obligate anaerobe; formerly referred to as Halobacterium heliumum.

halting problem – the problem of being unable to know whether or not some algorithms ever complete.

HAMAP – a system that identifies and semi-automatically annotates proteins that are part of well-conserved families or subfamilies; HAMAP is based on manually formed rules – it is applied to bacterial, archaeal and plastid-encoded proteins.

helix – a spiral structure in which successive turns are made at a constant angle to the base and constant distance from the axis.

hexadecimal – the base-16 number system used to represent values in binary form, with a single hexadecimal digit representing four bits of information; the tenth to the fifteenth digits are represented with the letters A-F; the decimal number 13810, which in binary would be 11010110101010, in hexadecimal, is

HGF – Human Genome Project, the 13-year-old, international project, coordinated by the US Department of Energy and the NIH, whose mission was to completely map and sequence the three billion bases of the human genome; the project began formally in 1990, the first draft of the genome was released in 2000, and the project was completed in 2003.

Hidden Markov Model (HMM) – see HMM.

hieroglyphics – the formal and numerical system of the ancient Egyptians.
high-throughput biology – scaled-up laboratory techniques made possible by increased levels of automation, using robots.

high-throughput DNA sequencing – a parallelised, automated process, capable of producing thousands or millions of sequences with sufficient rapidity to allow complete genomes to be sequenced.

hippopotamus – a large, semi-aquatic, herbivorous mammal from sub-Saharan Africa belonging to the family Hippopotamidae.

His – the IUPAC tri-letter abbreviation for histidine.
hidine-2-amino-3-(1 H-midimidazol-4-yl)propanoic acid, a chiral amino acid found in proteins.

HMM – Hidden Markov Model, a probabilistic model consisting of an inter connecting states to match, delete or insert status at each position in a sequence alignment (a match state denotes a conserved alignment column; an insert state denotes relation to a match state; a delete state denotes a deleted match position); in the context of protein sequence analysis, HMMs are used in a similar way to profiles to encode full domains.

Hogew, Paulien – a theoretical biologist, and progenitor of the term bioinformatics as the study of inform processes in biotic systems.
hostasis – the physiological and biochemical maintenance of a relatively constant internal environment in the bodies of animals.
homology – relatedness that arises as a result of the evolutionary process of divergence from a common ancestor; homology isn’t a synonym for similarity – it is an infer about evolutionary relationship; in the CATH database, the homology level is equivalent to the superfamily level of SCOP.
