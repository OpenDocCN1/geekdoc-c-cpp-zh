Looking at the entry in more detail, we see that the curators have also added information about diseases with which rhodopsin is associated. Specifically, we learn that,

Retinitis pigmentosa 4 (RP4). Disease description: a retinal dystrophy belonging to the group of pigmentary retinitis. Retinitis pigmentosa is characterized by retinal pigment deposits visible on fundus examination and primary loss of rod photoreceptors followed by secondary loss of cone photoreceptors. Patients typically have night blindness and loss of midperipheral field. As their condition progresses, they lose their peripheral field and eventually central vision. See also OMIM:617331⁵.

These statements are supported by 24 citations (Figure 8.2, bottom panel, E). To verify the statements and understand the process that led the curator to create this particular worth of information, we are now faced with 24 articles to which to discover the contexts of the original claims!
