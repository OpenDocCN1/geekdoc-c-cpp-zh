led us to believe. The first-frame start codon is encountered around a third of the way through the sequence, and the first-frame stop codon appears towards the end, as shown by the highlighted region.

Now we have made progress: we've identified what we believe to be a likely message region, or Open Reading Frame (ORF) – the longest piece of sequence between an infame and an in-frame stop codon. We can now translate this sequence using the genetic code to discover the putative protein (Figure 4.3(b)) – see Box 4.1 for more details.
