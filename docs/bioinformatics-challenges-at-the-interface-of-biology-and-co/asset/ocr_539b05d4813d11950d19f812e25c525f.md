# Figure 5.2

Excerpt from the Swiss-Prot entry for bovine rhodopsin during the 1980s. Compare the highlighted lines with those in Figure 5.3.

Compare the highlighted lines in Figures 5.2 and 5.3. Note that the Swiss-Prot ID and AC# are the same, but the underlying sequence is different (one has 315* amino acids, the other 348*) because the two versions were based on different PIR entries, each of which used the same identifier (OOSH) but different ACR (A03155 and A30407). This challenges our understanding of the role of primary database IDs and ACs—if they chart database entry versions, what do they mean? Here, OPSDSSHEEP/P02700 points us to pinpoint where the sequence in UniProt's history file changed -i.e., between versions 6 and 7. Clearly, with effort, we can piece together the history of the current sequence of bovine rhodopsin

```
http://www.uniprot.org/uniprot/P02700.txt?version=6
http://www.uniprot.org/uniprot/P02700.txt?version=7
http://www.uniprot.org/uniprot/P02700
```
