$$\psi_{n,m}(x)=\begin{cases}\sqrt{\left(m+\frac{1}{2}\right)2^{k/2}P_m(2^k x-\hat n)}, & \text{for }\frac{\hat n-1}{2^k}\le x<\frac{\hat n+1}{2^k},\\0, & \text{otherwise}\end{cases}$$

where $\hat n=2n-1$, $n=1,2,\ldots,2^{k-1}$; $k\ge 1$. The polynomials $P_0(x)$ and $P_1(x)$ are given as

$$P_0(x)=1$$

$$P_1(x)=x$$

and all higher degree Legendre polynomials are obtained using recursive formula

$$P_{m+1}(x)=\left(\frac{2m+1}{m+1}\right)xP_m(x)-\left(\frac{m}{m+1}\right)P_{m-1}(x),\quad\text{for }m=1,2,3,\ldots$$

Legendre wavelets have compact support and form a complete orthonormal basis for $L^2[0,1]$ [3, 12].

# 24.2.1 Approximation of Function

Any square integrable function $f(x)$ defined over $[0,1]$ can be written as the sum of Legendre wavelets as

$$f(x)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}c_{n,m}\psi_{n,m}(x),$$

where $\psi_{n,m}(x)$ are Legendre wavelets and $c_{n,m}$ are Legendre wavelet coefficients given by

$$c_{n,m}=\langle f(x),\psi_{n,m}(x)\rangle=\int_0^1 f(x)\psi_{n,m}(x)\,dx.$$

After truncating the series at $m=0,1,2,\ldots,M-1$ and $n=1,2,\ldots,2^{k-1}$, Equation (24.4) can be written as the sum of series of $2^{k-1}M$ terms.

$$f(x)\simeq\sum_{n=1}^{2^{k-1}}\sum_{m=0}^{M-1}c_{n,m}\psi_{n,m}(x),$$

Writing it in matrix notation,

$$f(x)=C\psi^T(x),$$

where $C$ and $\psi(x)$ are $1\times 2^{k-1}M$ matrices and are given by

$$C=[c_{1,0},c_{1,1},\ldots,c_{1,M-1},c_{2,0},\ldots,c_{2,M-1},\ldots,c_{2^{k-1},0},\ldots,c_{2^{k-1},M-1}],$$

$$\psi(x)=[\psi_{1,0}(x),\psi_{1,1}(x),\ldots,\psi_{1,M-1}(x),\psi_{2,0}(x),\ldots,\psi_{2,M-1}(x),\ldots,$$ $$\ldots,\psi_{2^{k-1},0}(x),\ldots,\psi_{2^{k-1},M-1}(x)].$$
