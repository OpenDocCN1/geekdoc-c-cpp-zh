So that $D^{\beta}y=\sum_{r=0}^{\infty}\beta r c_r x^{(r-1)\beta}$ and $D^{2\beta}y=\sum_{r=0}^{\infty}\beta r(r-1)c_r x^{(r-2)\beta}$ using the definition of conformable derivative.

$\Rightarrow \sum_{r=2}^{\infty}\beta^2r(r-1)c_rx^{(r-2)\beta}-\sum_{r=2}^{\infty}\beta^2r(r-1)c_rx^{r\beta}-\sum_{r=1}^{\infty}\beta^2rc_rx^{r\beta}+p^2\sum_{r=0}^{\infty}\beta^2c_rx^{r\beta}=0$

$\Rightarrow \sum_{r=2}^{\infty}r(r-1)c_rx^{(r-2)\beta}-\sum_{r=2}^{\infty}r(r-1)c_rx^{r\beta}-\sum_{r=1}^{\infty}rc_rx^{r\beta}+p^2\sum_{r=0}^{\infty}c_rx^{r\beta}=0$

$\Rightarrow 2c_2+p^2c_0+(6c_3-c_1+p^2c_0)x^\beta+\sum_{r=0}^{\infty}\left[(r+2)(r+1)c_{r+2}-\{r(r-1)+r-p^2\}c_r\right]x^{r\beta}=0$

putting the constant and coefficient of $x^{\beta r}$ equal to zero, we get

$2c_2+p^2c_0=0\Rightarrow c_2=-\frac{p^2}{2}c_0,\ldots,c_{r+2}=\frac{r^2-p^2}{(r+1)(r+2)}c_r$

and putting $r=1,2,3...$ we get

$c_3=\frac{1-p^2}{3!}c_1,\quad c_4=\frac{(2^2-p^2)(-p^2)}{4!}c_1,\quad c_5=\frac{(3^2-p^2)(1-p^2)}{5!}c_1,\quad c_6=\frac{(4^2-p^2)(2^2-p^2)(-p^2)}{6!}c_0$

in general

$c_{2r}=\frac{[(2r-2)^2-p^2][(2r-4)^2-p^2]\cdots[2^2-p^2](-p^2)}{(2r)!}c_0$

$c_{2r+1}=\frac{[(2r-1)^2-p^2][(2r-3)^2-p^2]\cdots(3^2-p^2)(1^2-p^2)}{(2r+1)!}c_1$

Hence the complete solution is given by

$y=A\left(1+\sum_{r=1}^{\infty}\frac{[(2r-2)^2-p^2][(2r-4)^2-p^2]\cdots[2^2-p^2](-p^2)}{(2n)!}x^{2r\beta}\right)+B\left(x+\sum_{r=1}^{\infty}\frac{[(2r-1)^2-p^2][(2r-3)^2-p^2]\cdots(3^2-p^2)(1^2-p^2)}{(2n+1)!}x^{(2r+1)\beta}\right)$

where $A,B$ are constants.
