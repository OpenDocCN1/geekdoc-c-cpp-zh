# 7.3 Simulation of Conformable Fractional Heat Equation

The general form for a one-dimensional heat equation is

$\frac{\partial u}{\partial t} = k \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}$.

A heat equation has many fractional forms ,see for example Hammed and Khalil [28, 29] and AVCT et al. [9]. In this section we investigate the solution of a time fractional heat differential equation:

$\frac{\partial^{\alpha} u}{\partial t^{\alpha}} = k \frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial x^2}, \quad 0 < x < L, \ t > 0. \quad (7.4)$

with conditions

$u(0,t)=0, \ t \ge 0 \quad (7.5)$

$u(L,t)=0, \ t \ge 0 \quad (7.6)$

$u(x,0)=f(x), \ 0 \le x \le L, \quad (7.7)$

where the derivative is conformable fractional derivative and $0 < \alpha < 1$.

$\frac{\partial^{\alpha} y}{\partial t^{\alpha}} \pm \mu^2 y = 0. \quad (7.8)$
