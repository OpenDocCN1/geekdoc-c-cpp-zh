Steps for calculations are:

+   2. Then, find the ratio of number of clean words (C) and number of total raw words (T).
+   3. Given the ratio (r) of number of clean words (C) to number of total raw words (T), compute the reduction score, R = 1 − (C/T) = 1 − r.

Now, 0 < r < 1 ⇒ 1 − 0 > 1 − r > 1 − 1 ⇒ 0 < R < 1. When 0<r<0.5, the polarity of opinions is toward the negative perspective and 0.5<r<1, polarity moves toward the positive perspective.
