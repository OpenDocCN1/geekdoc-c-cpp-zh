To determine the radical concentration (on a relative scale) in a given test sample, the peak-to-peak height (hpp) of an absorption line (as was shown in Figure 4.4) is used. For the case of a multi-line or a composite spectrum, double-integration of the entire absorption spectrum is used. For an absolute measure (number of radicals per gram), however, the double-integration of an absorption spectrum of the test sample is compared with that of a standard material whose radical concentration is known. Our laboratory has been using a standard NIST (National Institute of Standard and Testing) reference material (Electron Paramagnetic Resonance Intensity Standard SRM 2601).
