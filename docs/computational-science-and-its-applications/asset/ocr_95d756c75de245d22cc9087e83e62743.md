# 5.1 Introduction

The field of inverse problem is a fairly mature area of research and was initially motivated by industrial problems [1]. The growth of this research area has been tremendous in the last two decades. It is predicted that the use of inverse problems in applications in developing countries will grow due to significant demand from the industrial sector. Therefore, inverse problems relevant to developing countries should be getting a lot more attention.
