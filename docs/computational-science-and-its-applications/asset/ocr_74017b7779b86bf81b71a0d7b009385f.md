From Figure 10.9, it is observed that the stress values are comparatively high at the tip of the ship. This can be explained further from the constitutive relation in Equation (10.6), the relation between fluid velocity and stress, which states that the stress tensor is proportional to the deformation of the velocity. At the tip portion of the ship, the velocity gradients will be high in that region since the flow is being obstructed, which would in turn have caused higher stress values.
