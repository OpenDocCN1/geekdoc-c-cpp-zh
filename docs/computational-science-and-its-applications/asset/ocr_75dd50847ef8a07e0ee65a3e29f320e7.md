# 22.3.3 Generating Function for $U_n(x^\beta)$

The Chebyshev fractional polynomials of the first kind can be developed by the following:

$$\frac{1 - t x^\beta}{1 - 2 t x^\beta + t^2} = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} U_n(x^\beta) t^n$$

# 22.4 Conclusion

We have discussed the fractional power series solution as well as corresponding functions of conformable fractional Laguerre and Chebyshev differential equations. The work reflects that at $\beta=1$ all the solutions and functions behave like classical functions. In addition, we present some properties of the special fractional functions. The findings of the whole work indicate that the results of the fractional case conform with the results of the ordinary case.
