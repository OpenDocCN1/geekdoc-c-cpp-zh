# 23.1 Introduction

Computational linguistics is the scientific discipline concerned with understanding written and spoken language from a computational perspective, and building devices that usefully process and produce language in various settings. It is basically a branch of linguistics which deals with applications of techniques of computer science to the analysis and synthesis of language and speech. It can also be understood as a conglomerate of mathematics and linguistics with computer science.
