# 16.3 Results and Discussion

A crop yield forecast model for district-level rice production was developed over Gorakhpur region of Eastern Uttar Pradesh with the help of statistical technique, by using five weather variables, i.e. maximum and minimum temperatures, relative humidity of morning and evening time and 24-hourly rainfall, of the crop growing season, i.e. the period from sowing to harvesting of crop, and the observed yield of past years and results were discussed.
