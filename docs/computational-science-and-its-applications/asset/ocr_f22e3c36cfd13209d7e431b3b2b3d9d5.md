We now define the functional spaces for the velocity and pressure in Ω(t) as

V(Ω(t)) := { v ∈ H¹(Ω(t))³ : v = v̂ ∘ 𝒜ₜ⁻¹ , v̂ ∈ H₀¹(Ω̂)³ },

Q(Ω(t)) := { q ∈ L²(Ω(t)) : q = q̂ ∘ 𝒜ₜ⁻¹ , q̂ ∈ L₂(Ω̂) }.

In order to derive the variational form of the Navier–Stokes equations, we multiply the ALE form of momentum equation in Equation (10.8) by a test function v ∈ V, integrate over the computational domain Ω(t) and get

∫_{Ω(t)} ∂u/∂t · v dx + ∫_{Ω(t)} ((u − w)·∇)u · v dx − ∫_{Ω(t)} ∇·𝕋(u,p)·v dx = 1/Fr ∫_{Ω(t)} e·v dx.

Applying integration by parts to the third term gives

∫_{Ω(t)} ∇·𝕋(u,p)·v dx = ∫_{∂Ω(t)} v·𝕋·v ds − ∫_{Ω(t)} 𝕋(u,p):∇v dx = ∫_{∂Ω(t)} v·𝕋·v ds − ∫_{Ω(t)} 1/2 𝕔(u):∇v dx − ∫_{Ω(t)} 1/2 𝕔(u):∇vᵀ dx + ∫_{Ω(t)} p∇·v dx = ∫_{Γ_fs} v·𝕋·v ds + ∫_{Γ_wall} v·𝕋·v ds + ∫_{Γ_s} v·𝕋·v ds − ∫_{Ω(t)} 𝕔(u):𝕔(v) dx + ∫_{Ω(t)} p∇·v dx.
