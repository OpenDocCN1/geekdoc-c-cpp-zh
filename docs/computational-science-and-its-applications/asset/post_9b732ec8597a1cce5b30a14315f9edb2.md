## 6.5 Conclusion

In this chapter, we have presented a new family of two-step iterative methods with memory for solving nonlinear equations. Since our aim is to construct the method of higher-order convergence without additional calculation, we have used two approximations of selfcorrecting parameters, designed by Newton interpolating polynomials in the fourth-order methods to achieve higher-order convergence without any additional calculation. The R-order of convergence of the new with-memory iterative methods is increased from 4 to 6, and 7. The numerical results have been given to confirm the validity of theoretical results.
