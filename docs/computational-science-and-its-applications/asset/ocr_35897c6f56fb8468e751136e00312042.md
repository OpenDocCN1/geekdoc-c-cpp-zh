After renaming $f_i := f_i^2 - \frac{1}{\alpha_i}\partial_x f_i^1$, $\mathcal{I} = \{i=1,\dots,m\}$ and introducing $\beta_i = \frac{c_i^2}{a_i\alpha_i}$ we have $p_i = -\beta_i\partial_x q_i + p_i$. We consider the semi-linear elliptic problem on the graph $G$ with Neumann controls at simple nodes.

$$\begin{aligned}

\end{aligned}$$

In Equation (2.8), we have arrived at a sequence of mixed-integer optimal control problems for elliptic semi-linear systems on a metric graph. We have deliberately discretized with respect to time the nonlinear part in such a way that the denominator of the expression contains $p_i$ at the previous time level. This choice leads to monotone nonlinearity. Therefore, for given controls $(u,s)$, we can apply the celebrated Brezis theorem for the surjectivity of nonlinear operators [10]. In this spirit, we can extend a wellposedeness result in [7, 11] to this situation.

Let $(u,s)\in\mathbb{R}^{|\mathcal{J}_N^\delta|}\times\{0,1\}^{|\mathcal{J}_v\cup\mathcal{J}_c|}$ be given controls and $f\in\Pi_{i=1}^N L^2(0,\ell_i)$ given right-hand sides. Then there is a unique solution $q\in\Pi_{i=1}^N H^1(0,\ell_i)$ of Equation (2.12). Moreover, the optimal control problem in Equation (2.8) admits a unique optimal solution $(u,s)$.
