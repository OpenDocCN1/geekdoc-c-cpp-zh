Considering the actual appearance of the plot in Figure 19-2, it does indeed appear that the mean number of warp breaks for wool type A is higher if tension is low, but the nature of the difference changes if you move to a medium tension, where B has a higher point estimate than A. Interactions are certainly not a concept unique to multiple-factor ANOVA; they form an important consideration in many different types of statistical models. For the moment, it's good just to gain a basic appreciation of interactions.
