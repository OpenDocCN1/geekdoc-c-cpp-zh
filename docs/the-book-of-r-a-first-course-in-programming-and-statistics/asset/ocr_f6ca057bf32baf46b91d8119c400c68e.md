# 16.2. Other Density Functions

There are a number of other common probability density functions used for a wide range of tasks involving continuous random variables. We summarize a few here:

+   * The chi-squared distribution models sums of squared normal variates and is often related to operations concerning sample variances of normally distributed data. Its functions are $\chi^2, p\chi^2, q\chi^2$ and $chis$, and like the $t$-distribution (Section 16.2,3), it's dependent on degrees of freedom provided as the argument df.
