The help file ?persp details a host of arguments specific to controlling the presentation of any given perspective. As a few examples, you could shade the surface in grayscale to emphasize the 3D depth of the image, you could change the color or suppress the plotting of the grid lines making up the surface itself, or you could change the relative length of the z-axis.
