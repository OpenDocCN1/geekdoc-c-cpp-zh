If you don't explicitly specify the bins, 30 intervals will be used to span the range of the data. Inspecting the relevant geom documentation given with a call to `geom_histogram` tells you the following:

By default, `stat_bin` uses 30 bins. This is not a good default, but the idea is to have you experimenting with different binwidths. You may need to look at a few to uncover the full story behind your data.

So, rather than defaulting to a data-driven algorithm such as hist, ggplot2 encourages you to be aware of the issue and actively set their own binwidths. You can see that 30 bins yields inappropriately narrow intervals for this example—there are many gaps where no observations have fallen. There are a number of ways to choose histogram in ggplot, one of which is to use breaks earlier, supplying it with an appropriate numeric value of interval endpoints. Starting with a "blank" geom, geom_histogram completes the most of work, with color governing the bar outline color and fill the internal color of the bars. The argument closed="right" determines that each interval is "closed" (in other words, exclusive) on the right and "open" (on the left, as is the default noted in ?7). The `geom_vline` function is used to add the vertical mean and median lines; here, the mapping must be instructed to change aes and the locations of these lines. To ensure a correctly labeled legend is created for the mean and median, you must also insert linetype to be mapped to the desired values. In this case, it is a simple factor composed of the two desired "levels".

Since you're manually adding these lines and the associated mapping to the ggplot2 code, the legend must be instructed to appear with show.legend=TRUE. By default, the two lines are drawn linetype=1 (solid) and linetype=2 (dashed), but to match the earlier plot, you want linetype=2 and linetype=3 (dotted). You can add the `scale_linetype_manual` modifier to make this change; the desired type numbers are passed as a vector to values. Finally, to suppress the automatic inclusion of a title for your manually added legend, the labs(linetype="") addition instructs the scale associated with the variable mapped to linetype in the aes aes to be displayed without this title.
