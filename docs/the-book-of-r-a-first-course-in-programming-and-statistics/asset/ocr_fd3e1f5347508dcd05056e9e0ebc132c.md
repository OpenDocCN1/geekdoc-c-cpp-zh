# B.2.3 Support for Debugging

Another nice feature of RStudio is its built-in tools for code debugging. Debugging strategies usually involve being able to "pause" your code at a specific point to inspect your objects and function values in a "live" state. Specific techniques are best left to more advanced texts as *The Art of Debugging* (Mattloff and Salman, 2008) and *The Art of R Programming* (Mattloff, 2011); see also Chapter 9 of *Advanced R* (Wickham, 2015). But I mention here the tools available in RStudio provide more convenient, higher-level support for debugging than base R commands.

Once you're at the stage where you're starting to write programs of multiple interlinked R functions, you might like to learn more. With respect to R and RStudio in particular, there is a good introductory article by Jonathan McPherson on the support website at *https://support.rstudio.com/en-us/articles/205612627-Debugging-with-RStudio/.*
