# About the Author

Tilman M. Davies is a lecturer at the University of Otago in New Zealand, where he teaches statistics at all university levels. He has been programming in R for 10 years and teaches R programming in all his courses. His research on spatial point pattern modeling has been recognized by the New Zealand Statistical Association's Woolsey Award, and he has been awarded a prestigious Marsden grant by the Royal Society of New Zealand to work on related problems. He organizes an annual three-day Introduction to R workshop, which inspired him to write this book for beginners.

# About the Reviewer

Debbie has been an R user for many years. She is passionate about teaching students the fundamentals. In particular she enjoys guiding students to develop expertise with R so they may appreciate it as a valuable component in their toolbox. Debbie joined Massey University as a Senior Tutor in Statistics on completion of her PhD from the University of Auckland in 2010.
