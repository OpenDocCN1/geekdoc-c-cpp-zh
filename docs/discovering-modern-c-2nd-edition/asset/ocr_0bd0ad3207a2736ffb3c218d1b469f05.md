Do not get scared to the point that you decide not to use templates for the rest of your life. In most cases, the problem is much simpler than the never-ending error message makes us believe. In our experience, most errors in template functions can be found more quickly than run-time errors—with some training.

Expressing a function’s requirements with C++20 concepts (to be introduced in Section 3.10) can lead to much shorter though more comprehensible error messages.
