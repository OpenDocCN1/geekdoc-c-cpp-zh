# Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction

Richard S. Sutton and Andrew G. Barto

Second Edition, in progress

MIT Press, Cambridge, MA, 2017

This is the preprint version of the book that is available online. The book is the second edition of the classic 1998 edition. The main changes are the addition of a new chapter on deep reinforcement learning, and a new chapter on the relationship between reinforcement learning and psychology and neuroscience. Several other chapters have been substantially revised.
