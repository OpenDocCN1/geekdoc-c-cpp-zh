Pong has four major game objects, a ball, two paddles and a rectangular box (Fig. 27.3). The ball will bounce across the screen inside the box while the players control the paddles to keep the ball from hitting the left or right sides. If the ball hits the left or right side of the box, the player “attacking” that side is awarded a point. The score is displayed at the top of the screen.

Play the game for a while, using the **A** and **Z** keys to control the left paddle and the up and down arrow keys to control the right paddle. Notice the colors of the objects, the ball interacting with the other objects and the score displayed at the top of the screen. Hit the **Esc** key to quit—closing the window won’t stop the program.
