# Stereotyping

Most people accept that stereotyping is a bad thing, but in UML it's actually a good thing. In UML, you can take an existing symbol, such as the component symbol, and slightly modify it to make your own, customized version of the symbol. This modification process is called *stereotyping*. When you do so, you add a word inside the top of the component symbol. You put the word in double angle brackets, like this: << and >>. (Guillemets again — one for everybody!) In fact, in UML, anytime you see a word enclosed in guillemets, you're seeing a stereotype. Just think of a stereotype as a modified form of the symbol, or as your own version of the symbol.
