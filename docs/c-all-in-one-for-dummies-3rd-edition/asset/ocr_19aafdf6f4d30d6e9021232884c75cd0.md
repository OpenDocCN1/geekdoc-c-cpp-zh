In the example, when there's a hill, the road angle either increases or decreases, depending on the side of the hill you're on (uphill or downhill). The road does not need to know about all the other car parts. Instead, it just informs the mediator of the change. The mediator then informs the necessary car parts.

This may seem like overkill: Why can't the road just talk directly to the car parts? The idea is that if you enhance this application later, you may want to add more car parts. Rather than have to go through and hook up connections to all the necessary car parts, you just make a connection with the mediator object. Suppose that you add a new part called an automatic transmission. When the car begins to climb a hill, the automatic transmission might detect this and automatically shift to a lower gear, resulting in an increase to the engine speed. To add this class, you only need to define its behavior and how it responds to various events, and then hook it up to the mediator. You will also modify the mediator so that it knows something about the automatic transmission's behavior. Thus, you don't need to hook it up to all the other instances.
