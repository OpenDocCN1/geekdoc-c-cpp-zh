# ## Performing Numeric Conversion

Numeric conversion isn’t hard to perform — it’s accurate numeric conversion that’s hard to perform. Getting the right result as you move from one type of number to another is essential. Sure, you probably won’t notice too much if your game score is off by a point or two, but you’ll definitely notice the missing dollars from your savings account. Worse yet, when you finally get to take that rocket into space, a rounding error can definitely ruin your day as you head off toward the sun, rather than Planet Earth.

The Boost library includes the converter template, which makes converting from one kind of number to another relatively easy. The converter template includes all kinds of flexibility.
