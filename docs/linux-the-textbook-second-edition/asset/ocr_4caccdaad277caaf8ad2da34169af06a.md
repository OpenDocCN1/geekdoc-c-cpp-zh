You can also replace a word (or phrase) with another by pressing <Ctrl+\>. When you press this key combination, nano prompts you for the word (or phrase) that you want to replace. After typing in the word (or phrase), press <Enter>. You will be prompted for the replacement word (or phrase). After this, you will be prompted for various ways of doing the replacement, and then are asked to confirm the changes. Once confirmed, the replacement is done.
