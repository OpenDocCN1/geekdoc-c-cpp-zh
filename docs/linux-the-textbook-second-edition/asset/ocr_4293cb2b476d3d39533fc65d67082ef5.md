# 18.2.3 systemd-Controlled Shutdown

As introduced in Chapter 17, Section 2.3.2, systemd-controlled shutdown can follow several paths, depending upon which target units are “pulled in” to achieve the desired form of powering off or rebooting of the system. Referring to Figure 18.3, these paths flow from shutdown.target and umount.target to the poweroff.target. The specific systemctl commands that initiate procedures along these these paths were shown in Table 17.1.

Figure 18.3 shows both the common targets and services involved in a shutdown, reboot, and power off. In the case of kexec, which enables you to load and boot into another kernel from the currently running kernel, the reboot is done very quickly without waiting for the whole BIOS boot process to finish.

We give additional information on shutdown targets in Chapter W27, Sections 4 and 5, at the book website.
