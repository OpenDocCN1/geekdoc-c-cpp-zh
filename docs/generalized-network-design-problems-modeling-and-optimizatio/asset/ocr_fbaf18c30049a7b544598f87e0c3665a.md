+   2. 3-opt. The three-opt procedure extends the 2-opt and involves deleting 3 arcs in a route and reconnecting the network in all other possible ways, and then evaluating each reconnection method to find the optimum one.

+   3. Exchange operator: two strings of at most k vertices are exchanged between two routes. A new solution is obtained from the current one by selecting k vertices from one route and exchanging them with k vertices belonging to another route. The exchange is acceptable only if it produces a feasible solution. This is pictured in Figure 5.9.
