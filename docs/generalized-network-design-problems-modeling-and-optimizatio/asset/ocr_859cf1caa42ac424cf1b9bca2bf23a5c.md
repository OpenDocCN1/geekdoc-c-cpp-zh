# 3.6.1 Stigmergy and autonomous robots

The proposed metaheuristic combines the concepts of stigmergic communication and autonomous robotic search. Stigmergy occurs when an action of an insect is determined or influenced by the consequences of previous actions of another insect [12]. Stigmergy provides a general mechanism to relate individual and colony-level behaviors. Individual behavior modifies the environment, which in turn modifies the behavior of other individuals. Self-organization of social insects [15] can emerge, due to stigmergic interactions among individuals.
