# 5.7 Notes

The generalized vehicle routing problem (GVRP) is an extension of the vehicle routing problem, and consists of designing optimal delivery or collection routes from a given depot to a number of predefined, mutually exclusive and exhaustive clusters, subject to capacity restrictions. The GVRP was introduced by Ghiani and Improta [48] and reduces to the generalized traveling salesman problem if there is no restriction on the capacity of the vehicle.
