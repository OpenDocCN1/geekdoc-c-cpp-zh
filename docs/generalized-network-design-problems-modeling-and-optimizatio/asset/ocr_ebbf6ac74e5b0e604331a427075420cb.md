This new formulation of the GMSTP is obtained by incorporating the constraints characterizing \( P_{MST} \), with \( y \in \{0, 1\} \), into \( P_{local}(y) \). The local-global formulation of the GMSTP uses three types of variables to define the connections between clusters in the graph. The first group of variables, which we have already used in the previous formulations, are indicator edge variables, \( x_{ij} \). These variables define the use of specific edges between nodes in the graph. On the other hand, the other two groups of variables, \( y_{lr} \) and \( \lambda_{klr} \), define connections between clusters in the graph.
