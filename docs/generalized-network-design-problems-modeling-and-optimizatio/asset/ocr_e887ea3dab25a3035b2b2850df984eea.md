By taking into account the demands on clusters at the endpoints of the arc $(i, j) \in A$, the constraints (5.22) can be strengthen as follows:

```
$q_P \le y_{Pr} \le (Q - q_P)x(V_p, V_r) \quad \forall p, r \in C. \quad (5.23)$
```

In model $F_1$ we will use constraints (5.23) instead of the constraints (5.22).
