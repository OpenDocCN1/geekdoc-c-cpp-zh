Now, we can formally model the reliability of series parallel structures using the above definitions in HOL4 as:

```
Theorem 12.13
|- ∀ p L x. (prob_space p) ∧
(events p = POW (p_space p)) ∧
(0 < x) ∧
(∀z. MEM z (List_rel_event_list p L x)
=> NULL z) ∧ (mutual_indep p
(FLAT (List_rel_event_list p L x))) ∧ (∀x'.
MEM x' (FLAT (List_rel_event_list p L x)) => x' ∈ events p) =>
(prob p (series_parallel_struct p
(List_rel_event_list p L x)) =
list_prod (one_minus_list
(list_ser_par_rel_list_prob p L x)))
```

The assumptions of Theorem 12.13 are similar to the ones used in Theorem 12.12. The conclusion models the right-hand-side of the reliability of a series-parallel system. The proof of the above theorem uses the results of the previously describes theorems and some basic probability theory axioms.

The formalization reported in this chapter so far took about 300 man-hours and about 6000 lines of HOL4 script. The most challenging part in the reasoning process was to verify that given the mutual independence of individual events the event corresponding to a sub-configuration (series, parallel, parallel-series or a parallel-series) is also mutually independent from other sub-configurations and individual sub-modules. The rest of the verification process was primarily based on probabilistic, set-theoretic and arithmetic simplification and some parts of the proofs were also handled automatically using the various built-in automatic provers and simplifiers in HOL4.

The formal verifications of the above mentioned theorems, which are available in reliability textbooks, guarantee the correctness of our formal definitions. Moreover, the formal verification of these properties is expected to facilitate the process of formal reasoning about RBD-based analysis of real world systems as will be demonstrated in the next two sections of this chapter.
