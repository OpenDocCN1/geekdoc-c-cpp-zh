# # Acknowledgment

The authors would like to acknowledge the contributions of their research students, without whom all this reported research could not have been possible. Dr. Tarek Mhamdi formalized the foundational theories of measure, Lebesgue integration, probability, and information in HOL4. Dr. Liya Liu formalized Discrete Markov chains in HOL4 and utilized them to analyze many interesting case studies. Dr. Naeem Abbasi formalized some reliability theory foundations and provided reasoning support for using the Lebesgue integral to analyze the expectations of continuous random variables. Dr. Maissa Elleuch used the foundational probability theories to analyze the scheduling algorithms, coverage and detection properties of Wireless Sensor Networks. PhD Candidate Ghassen Helali analyzed the Heavy Hitter problem using the probability theories and also worked on the Min-Entropy and Belief-Min-Entropy related formalization. PhD Candidate Waqar Ahmad formalized the reliability theory foundations and reliability block diagrams in HOL. Moreover, we would also like to acknowledge our research assistants, Saad Hameed and Usman Pervez, for their help in formatting the text of this book and converting our latex source files to MS Word format. Finally, we would like to thank our families for being so supportive and bearing with our busy schedules during all the years when the research reported in this manuscript was being conducted.
