# # Chapter 8 Formalization of Hidden Markov Model

# ## ABSTRACT

In this chapter, the authors provide the formalization of extended DTMC models, namely Hidden Markov Models (HMMs), which are the core concept for formally evaluating the probability of the occurrence of a particular observed sequence and finding the best state sequence to generate given observation (Mantyla & Tuukimuskeskus, 2001; Rabiner, 1990). In order to present the usefulness of the formalization of HMM and the formal verification of HMM properties, the authors illustrate the formal analysis of a DNA (Deoxyribron Nucleic Acid) sequence at the end of the chapter.
