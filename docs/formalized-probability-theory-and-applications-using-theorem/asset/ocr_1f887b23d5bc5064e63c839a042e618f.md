# KEY TERMS AND DEFINITIONS

+   - **Extended Real Numbers**: Real numbers including ±∞ are usually referred to as the extended real numbers.
+   - **Lebesgue Integration**: A Lebesgue integral can be considered as a generalization of the regular integral. It is general since it allows integrating over a larger class of functions and domains.
+   - **Lebesgue Measure**: Lebesgue measure the notion of measure, such as length and area, to sets.
+   - **Measure Theory**: Measure theory allows us to study measures, such as length, area and volume.
