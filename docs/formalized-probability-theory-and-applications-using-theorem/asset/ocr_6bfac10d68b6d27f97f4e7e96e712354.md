This kind of quality measurement of solutions is based on Radio-frequency identification (RFID), which is a technology that identifies and tracks objects, such as a product, an animal or a person by using radio waves to transfer data from an electronic tag, called the RFID tag. In the last decade, a large volume of research was conducted on complying RFID systems with the international standard ISO/IEC 18000-7. The AMQM system exhibits some features of the ISO/ IEC 18000-7 standard and hence its formal analysis is quite important.

In an AMQM system, tags are intended for identifying the objects that are to be managed. The interrogator communicates with the tag in its RF (Radio Frequency) communication range and controls the protocol, reads information from the tag, directs the tag to store data in some cases, and makes sure that messages are delivered and are also valid. An interrogator controls the messages that are transmitted during their allotted time periods called slots and an acknowledge received when each message has been received successfully.
