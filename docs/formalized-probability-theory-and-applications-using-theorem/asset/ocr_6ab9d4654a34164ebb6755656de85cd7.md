extended-real numbers in the formalization, as is the case with (Mhamdi, 2013), has many advantages. It allows us to define sigma-finite and other infinite measures as well as signed measures. It also allows defining extended-real valued functions. Similarly the formalization of probability theory, reported in (Hölzl, 2011; Hölzl, 2013), lacks the important properties of the Radon Nikodym derivative and the Kullback-Leibler divergence and the different measures of information. It has not been used to formalize any notion of reliability or wireless sensor networks. Moreover, it also does not handle signed measures or functions taking negative valued as only positive extended-real numbers are supported. Thus, the normalization presented by Mhamdi (2013) is more general and has been selected as the main focus of this manuscript.
