You register for a Windows Phone Developer Account at the Windows Phone Developer Center (http://dev.windowsphone.com). Once you have a Windows Phone Developer Account, there is still one more step. You have to register your phone or device as a developer device. As a student, you can only register or unlock one device for free. In order to register the phone, the phone must be physically connected to a Windows 8 PC and you have to have a current developer account and know your Microsoft ID and password associated with your developer account. You connect your phone to your computer using a USB cable. Launch the **Windows Phone Developer Registration** app.

> **NOTE** Find the **Windows Phone Developer Registration** app from the Start screen by right-clicking near the bottom-right corner and selecting All apps. Windows Phone Developer Registration is listed under the **Windows Phone SDK 8.0**.

Once the phone is registered, you can then deploy applications to it. Instead of selecting one of the emulator options, you select **Device** from the dropdown list tied to the **Start** icon, as illustrated in Figure 15-43.
