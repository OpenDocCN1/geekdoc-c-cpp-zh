# 2 OPEN A NEW TRINKET

Click on Trinket and you will see the following drop-down menu. Click on Blocks.

You will see the following window. Click on Turtle.

You will drop the blocks for your program in this white canvas.

![](bbox=[0.377, 0.319, 0.969, 0.58])

The following menu will open up on the right when you click on Turtle. Drag the `shape turtle` block to the white canvas. You are now ready to code.

# Did you know?

Both tortoises and turtles are reptiles. Both lay eggs and have hard, bony shells. Tortoises have flatter backs than turtles and they live on land in deserts, grasslands, and forests. Turtles have webbed feet for swimming and live in rivers, lakes, or oceans.
