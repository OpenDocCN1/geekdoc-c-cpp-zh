Empowering questions on the other hand lead your mind to focus on what you have, what’s right about you and come from a place of power.

Read these questions out loud:

+   - Why am I so successful?
+   - Why do I have enough?
+   - Why am I loved so much?

Did you notice the intriguing pause and how quickly the mind jumped from confusion to questioning to seeking clarity and confirmation? Did you notice the, ‘Mmm that’s interesting’, thought coming into play? Are you thinking more positively? Feeling good about yourself?

Now reverse the negative questions you wrote above to positive ones.

Remember to ask these questions of yourself on a regular basis, you may want to write them out on card and put them in places where you can regularly look at them.
