# Exercise - Getting Clear On and Clear of Tolerations

+   - For each of the following categories write down a list of at least ten tolerations:
+     - **Car.**
+     - **Equipment.**
+     - **Home.**
+     - **Office.**
+     - **Work.**
+     - **Relationships** - family, friends, significant other, colleagues. Consider their behaviour, habits, attitude and communication style.
+     - **You** - Your habits, behaviour, attitudes and communication style, health and well-being.

+   - Beside the tolerations write down how these are serving you. What benefits are you perceiving from putting up with the situation?

+   - What are these tolerations costing you? These can be measured in terms of time, resources and money or the opportunity cost, level of attraction e.g. respect, recognition. Remember these are likely to impact more on your confidence.

+   - Commit to eliminating the tolerations from your list.
