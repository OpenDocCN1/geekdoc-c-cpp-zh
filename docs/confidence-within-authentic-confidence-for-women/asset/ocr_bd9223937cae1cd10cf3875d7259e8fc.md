# **Ask and you shall receive**

Noah St. John, Success Mentor and author of *The Great Little Book of Afformations* suggests rather than making a statement you don't believe, ask yourself a question. By doing so your mind automatically begins to search for an answer without your conscious volition. The trick is to ask empowering questions rather than disempowering questions.

Disempowering questions come from a place of fear, focus on what you lack and what's wrong with you. They speak from your pain making you feel yucky and stuck because as the Law of Attraction states what you continually focus on will grow and bear fruit.

Read the following questions out loud:

+   - Why am I so unhappy?
+   - Why don't I have enough?
+   - Why am I so lonely?'

Feeling good? Didn't think so!

Now for the next few hours I want you to listen to what you say to yourself when you talk to yourself. Sit with this for a bit, you might even want to leave completing the following exercise for a day or two, during that time just be mindful of 'the voice' what it's saying and how it's saying it. When you are ready answer the following question on the next page:

When you talk to yourself what are three disempowering questions you ask?
