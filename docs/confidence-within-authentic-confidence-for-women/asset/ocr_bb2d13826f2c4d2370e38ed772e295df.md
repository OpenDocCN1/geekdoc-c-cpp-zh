# ### Self Esteem

+   - When I believe in myself, so do others.
+   - I express my needs and feelings.
+   - I am my own unique self - special, creative and wonderful.
+   - By loving myself, I allow others to love me as well.

# ### Trust

+   - I release the past and now embrace a trusting attitude.
+   - I trust myself and I trust in the process of life.
+   - Life supplies all my needs in abundance, I trust in life.
+   - I trust my vibes.

# ### Uniqueness

+   - I accept my uniqueness.
+   - I am perfect exactly as I am, I celebrate and honour my uniqueness.
+   - Freedom is mine when I express my individuality.
