## 4.2 Experimental evaluation

The performance of the evolved strategies is evaluated based on three measurements. Firstly, the average fitness is the fitness of the population at each generation over 50 generations. The average fitness shows how well the strategy converges over time to find the best solution.

Secondly, success rate is the percentage of time that an agent succeeds in acquiring the item by the given time at any price less than or equal to its private valuation. This measure will determine the efficiency of the agent in terms of guaranteeing the delivery of the requested item. Individuals will be selected from each of the data sets to compete in the simulated marketplace for 200 times. The success is calculated based on the number of times the agent is able to win the item over 200 runs. The formula below is used to calculate the success rate.

Success Rate = (Number of winning) × 100 / 200 (9)

Finally, the third measurement is the average payoff which is defined as

$$\frac{\sum_{i=1}^{200} \left(\frac{p_i - v_i}{p_i}\right)}{n}$$ (10)

where p_i is the agent's private valuation, n is the number of runs, v_i is the winning bid value for auction i. This value is then divided by the agent's private valuation, summed and averaged over the number of runs. The agent's payoff is 0 if it is not successful in obtaining the item.

A series of experiments was conducted using the set of crossover and mutation rates described in Table 2. It was found that 0.4 crossover rate and 0.02 mutation rate performed better than the other combinations (Gan et al, 2008a, Gan et al, 2008b). An experiment was conducted with the newly discovered crossover rate p_c = 0.4 and mutation rate p_m = 0.02. The result was then compared with the original combination of the genetic operators (p_c = 0.6 and p_m = 0.02). The new strategies evolved from the combination of the crossover rate of 0.4 and mutation rate of 0.02 produced better results in terms of the average fitness, the success rate and the average payoff. It can be observed that the mutation rate of 0.02 evolved better strategies when compared to other mutation rates as well (0.2 and 0.002). This rate is similar to the research outcome by Cervantes (Cervantes & Stephen, 2006) in which a mutation rate below the 1/N and error threshold is recommended. ### 0 引言

碎石桩复合地基是高速公路软基处理中常见的一种地基处理方式，其以施工速度快、造价低等优点在工程中得到广泛应用。目前，针对碎石桩复合地基的设计与计算方法尚存在不少问题有待进一步研究。本文结合某实际工程，对碎石桩复合地基的承载力特性进行综合分析。

### 1 碎石桩复合地基的受力机理与承载力计算方法

碎石桩复合地基的承载力受桩体材料、桩长、桩径、桩土模量比以及桩间土性质等多种因素的影响。在竖向荷载作用下，桩与桩间土共同承担上部荷载，并通过桩体将荷载传递至较深土层，从而提高了地基的整体承载力。

目前常用的承载力计算方法有：应力比法、稳定分析法及经验公式法等。其中，应力比法在工程设计中应用最为广泛，其核心在于合理确定桩土应力比 n。

### 2 工程概况

依托工程位于某沿海地区，地基为深厚的淤泥质软土，具有含水率高、压缩性大、强度低等特点。设计采用振动沉管碎石桩对软基进行处理，桩径为 0.5 m，桩长 11.5 m，桩间距 1.4 m，按正三角形布置。
