The second line `num2=5` means that variable `num2` (our shortened way of writing `number2`, second number) has been assigned 5 before the program runs.

The computer interprets these instructions and stores the numbers given.

The third line `sum=num1+num2` tells the computer to take whatever `num1` has been given and add it to whatever `num2` has been given. In other terms, sum the values of `num1` and `num2`.

The fourth line `print(sum)` means that it displays whatever `sum` has. If we put double quotes around `sum`, the computer will simply display the word `sum` and not the sum of the two numbers! Remember that cliché that computers are garbage in and garbage out. They follow what you give them!
