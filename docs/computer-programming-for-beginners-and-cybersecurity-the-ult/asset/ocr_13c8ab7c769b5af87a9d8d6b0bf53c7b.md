Top 15 signals relate to cyber attackers’ behavior. They are timely signals, before the cyberattack is executed, occurring at the intrusion, lateral movement, or command and control steps of the Cyber Attack Chain, and are, therefore, of greatest value. This is not an exhaustive list, and there will probably be other signals relevant to an organization based on its risk profile and its Crown Jewels that may indicate the cyber attackers at work. As such, each organization should tailor its list of Cyber Attack Signals for monitoring.

# Patch window
