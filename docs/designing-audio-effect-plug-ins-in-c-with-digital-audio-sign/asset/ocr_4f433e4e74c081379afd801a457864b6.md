By sweeping around the field, you can generate many different combinations. You could use these combinations as mix ratios for something in your plug-in. Or, you could use them to control any parameter or other aspect of the plug-in that you like. You are responsible for mapping the 0.0 to 1.0 values to your plug-in's parameters. The vector joystick does not connect to any variable in your plug-in; like the assignable buttons, these passive controls simply tell you when they've moved. When the joystick moves, a function called joystickControlChange() is called.
