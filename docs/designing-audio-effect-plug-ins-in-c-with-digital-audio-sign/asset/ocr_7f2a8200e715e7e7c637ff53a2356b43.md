small enough to not cause clicks or pops in the output. It sounds just as smooth as the direct form oscillator when the frequency is adjusted slowly.

The two outputs are labeled y(n) and yq(n) where the “q” stands for quadrature. Therefore, there are two difference equations. The difference equation for yq(n) must be solved first because y(n) is dependent on it. A Gordon–Smith oscillator block diagram is shown in Figure 9.8.

The difference equations are as follows:

```
yq(n) = yq(n - 1) - εy(n - 1)
y(n) = εyq + y(n - 1)
```

Specify:

+   - f_o, desired oscillation frequency

The design equations are as follows:

```
θ = 2πf_o / fs
ε = 2sin(θ/2)
```

Initial conditions:
y(n - 1) = sin(-10)
yq(n - 1) = cos(-10)
