# 1.15 Gain, Attenuation, and Phase Inversion

As shown in Figure 1.25, a simple coefficient multiplier will handle the three basic audio processing functions of gain, attenuation, and inversion. If the coefficient is a negative number, phase inversion will be the result. If the coefficient has a magnitude less than 1.0, attenuation will occur, while amplification occurs if the magnitude is greater than 1.0.
