For the sampling theorem to hold true, the audio data must be arriving and leaving on a strictly timed interval, although it may be asynchronous with the DSP's internal clock. This means that when the DSP does receive an audio INTR it must do all of the audio processing and handle any UI interrupts before the next audio INTR arrives in one sample interval. The interrupt handling scheme is prioritized such that the audio data interrupt is the most important. Thus, while servicing the audio data interrupt, the DSP might be set up to ignore all other interrupts (except the reset interrupt) until the audio data is finished processing.
