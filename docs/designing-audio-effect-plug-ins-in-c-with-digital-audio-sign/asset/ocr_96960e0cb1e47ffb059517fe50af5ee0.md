> The topology of the filter determines its difference equation. The coefficients ($a_N$) of a filter determine its filter frequency and phase response and therefore its type (HPF, LPF, etc.) and its sonic qualities. Your plug-in implements the difference equation in processAudioFrame(). Your plug-in calculates the coefficients in userInterfaceChange() in response to the user making changes to the control surface.
