Two different FDNs were used; the normal four-delay line and the delay and LPF in series (Figure 11.35, but without the correction filter on the output). An impulse response was taken for the target listening environment. The GA fitness function used the time domain envelope of the target impulse response for the matching criteria on the output of the system. During each iteration, the top 35% of offspring candidates were kept and inter-mated. Mutation rates of 10% for the delay coefficients and 5% for the LPF cutoff frequencies were used.
