Rather than defining a separate method, Listing 16.5 uses a statement lambda that counts the number of times the method is called. First, after selection is assigned, DelegateInvocations remains at Zero.

Two things in the output are remarkable. At the time of assignment to selection, no iteration over keywords is performed. If Keywords were a property, the property call would run—in other words, the from clause executes at the time of assignment. However, neither the projection, nor the filtering, nor anything after the from clause execute until the code iterates over the values within selection. It is as though at the time of assignment, selection would more appropriately be called "query."
