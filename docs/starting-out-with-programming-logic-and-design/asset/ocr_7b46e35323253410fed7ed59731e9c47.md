# Chapter 2 Input, Processing, and Output

The disadvantage of drawing flowcharts by hand is that mistakes have to be manually erased, and in many cases, require that the entire page be redrawn. A more efficient and professional way to create flowcharts is to use software. There are several specialized software packages available that allow you to create flowcharts.

# Flowchart Connector Symbols

Often, a flowchart is too long to fit on a page. Sometimes you can remedy this by breaking the flowchart into two or smaller flowcharts, and placing them sideby-side on the page. When you do this, you use a connector symbol to connect the pieces of the flowchart. A connector symbol is a small circle with a letter or number written inside it. In Figure 2-3, the (A) connector symbol indicates that the second flowchart segment begins where the first flowchart segment ends.

When a flowchart is too large to fit on a single page, you can break the flowchart into parts, and place the parts on separate pages. You then use the off-page connector symbol to connect the pieces of the flowchart. The off-page connector shape is the "home plate", with a number shown inside it. If the connector is at an exit point in the flowchart, the number indicates where the next part of the flowchart is located. If the connector is at an entry point, it indicates the page where the previous part of the flowchart is located. Figure 2-4 shows an example. In example, the flowchart on the left is on page 1.
