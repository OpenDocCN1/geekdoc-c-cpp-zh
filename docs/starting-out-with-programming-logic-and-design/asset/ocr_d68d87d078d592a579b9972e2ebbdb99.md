Notice that the first line of the FinalExam declaration uses the Extends key word, which indicates that this class extends another class (a superclass). Notice that the GradedActivity's score field is not listed among the members of the FinalExam class. That is because the score field is private. private members of the superclass cannot be accessed by the subclass, so technically speaking, they are not inherited. When an object of the subclass exists in memory, the private members of the superclass exist, but only methods in the superclass can access them. They are truly private to the subclass.
