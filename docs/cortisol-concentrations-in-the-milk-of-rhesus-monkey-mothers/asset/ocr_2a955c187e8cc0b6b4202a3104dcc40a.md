Patients with the minor allele were less successful losing weight after adjusting for baseline BMI (*P* = 0.008) (Table 4). The difficulty in losing weight was particularly evident after 12 weeks of treatment as evidenced by the analysis of repeated measurements after adjusting for baseline BMI (*P* = 0.038). No statistically significant differences were found for the remaining *CLOCK* SNPs analysed (*P* > 0.05) (Figure 3 a–c). However, significant associations were found between *CLOCK* polymorphisms and changes in the total

plasma cholesterol, even for those SNPs that showed no statistical associations with weight loss, such as rs3749474 and rs4580704 (Table 4).
