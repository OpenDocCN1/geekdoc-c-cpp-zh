```
Kleisli f <<< identity      = Kleisli f -- Left Identity
Kleisli f <<< Kleisli pure  = Kleisli f -- Substituted using 2
Kleisli $ pure >>= f        = Kleisli f -- Substituted using 1
Kleisli $ \x -> pure x >>= f = Kleisli f -- Substitute using 3
Kleisli \x -> f x          = Kleisli f -- Right Identity (Bind)
Kleisli f                  = Kleisli f -- ETA reduction
```

So we’ve proven that our identity for Kleisli works just like it does for Function, i.e. it’s law abiding.

This barely scratches the surface of the Kleisli Category and we were fast and loose without our "proofs", but hopefully this has given you a tiny glimpse as to where the names and concepts come from when you encounter them in the PureScript or Haskell.
