The fact that they all return Rights tells us that we’ve successfully parsed in each case.

The Tuple has, as its first element, what’s left over after the Parser completes. In the first result, we can see that it consumed 1 character and returned 2 characters in the remaining String, i.e. the second element of the Tuple.

The second result consumed 2 characters and left 1 character behind in the String. And the final case consumed all 3 characters leaving us with an empty String.
