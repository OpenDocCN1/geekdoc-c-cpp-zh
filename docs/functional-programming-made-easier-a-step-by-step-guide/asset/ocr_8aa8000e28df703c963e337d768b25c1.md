And finally, if mustChangePassword is true then we want to navigate to ChangePassword otherwise we’ll navigate to Users. But we want to decouple the Logon Page from knowing anything about our Routes.

That’s why we made Capability.LogonRoute. With the idea that maybe our Logon Page would exist in a library.
