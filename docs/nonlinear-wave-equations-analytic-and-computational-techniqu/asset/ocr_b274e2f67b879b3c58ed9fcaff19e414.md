Proof. Substituting the asymptotic relation (3.25) into (3.23), we may compute, modulo terms of order $z^{-2}$,

together with the fact that $u$ defined by $\bar{\partial}u=\partial q$ satisfies $u=\mathcal{O}\left(|z|^{-2}\right)$. The identities (3.26) and (3.27) are immediate.
