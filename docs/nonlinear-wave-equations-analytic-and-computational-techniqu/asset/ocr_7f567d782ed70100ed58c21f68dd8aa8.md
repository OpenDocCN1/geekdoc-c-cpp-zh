The key observation is that the derivatives on F1n and F2n in (2.11), and on un at the end of the procedure (to obtain un,x and un,y), are balanced by the inverse Laplacian in (2.11). In other words, at the point in the algorithm where large numbers enter the computation due to applying derivatives in Fourier space, we divide by even larger numbers by applying the inverse Laplacian.
