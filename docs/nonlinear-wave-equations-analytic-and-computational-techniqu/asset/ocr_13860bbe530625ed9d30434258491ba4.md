Instead of deriving the perturbation expansion for $G(\eta)$ using the ill-conditioned basis functions $e^{ikx} e^{\pm ky}$, as was done in (2.1) above, we perform a boundary-flattening change of variables:

$u(x,y)=\phi\bigl(x,(1+h^{-1}\eta)(y+\eta)\bigr),\qquad 0\le x<L,\qquad -h<y<0.$

A straightforward calculation reveals that

$u_{xx}=\phi_{xx}+(1+h^{-1}y)(1+h^{-1}\eta)^{-1}\eta_x\partial_y[u_x-\circledast]+\partial_x[\circledast],$

$u_{yy}=\phi_{yy}+\Bigl[1-(1+h^{-1}\eta)^{-2}\Bigr]u_{yy},$

where $\circledast=(1+h^{-1}y)(1+h^{-1}\eta)^{-1}\eta_x u_y$. Since $\Delta\phi=0$, we find that

$\Delta u=\partial_x(F_1)+\partial_y(F_2)+F_3,$

where

$F_1=(1+h^{-1}y)E\eta_x u_y,\qquad E=(1+h^{-1}\eta)^{-1},$

$F_2=(1+h^{-1}y)E\eta_x u_x-(1+h^{-1}y)^2E^2\eta_x^2 u_y+(1-E^2)u_y,$

$F_3=-h^{-1}E\eta_x u_x+h^{-1}(1+h^{-1}y)E^2\eta_x^2 u_y.$

Next, we write $\eta(x)=\varepsilon f(x)$ and expand $E$ and $E^2$ in powers of $\varepsilon$ to conclude that the terms of the series $u(x,y)=\sum_{n=0}^\infty \varepsilon^n u_n(x,y)$ satisfy

(2.10) $\Delta u_0=0,\qquad u_0(x,0)=\mathscr{D}(x),$ 

(2.11) $\Delta u_n=\partial_x(F_1^n)+\partial_y(F_2^n)+F_3^n,\qquad u_n(x,0)=0,$

as well as $u_{n,y}(x,-h)=0$ and $u_n(x+L,y)=u_n(x,y)$. Here

$F_1^n=(1+h^{-1}y)f_x\sum_{m=0}^{n-1}(-h^{-1}f)^m u_{n-1-m,y},$

$F_2^n=(1+h^{-1}y)(F_4-F_5)+\frac{f}{h}\sum_{m=0}^{n-1}(m+2)(-h^{-1}f)^m u_{n-1-m,y},$

$F_3^n=h^{-1}(F_5-F_4),\qquad F_4^n=f_x\sum_{m=0}^{n-1}(-h^{-1}f)^m u_{n-1-m,x},$

$F_5^n=(1+h^{-1}y)f_x^2\sum_{m=0}^{n-2}(l+1)(-h^{-1}f)^m u_{n-2-m,y}.$

Finally, we use

$G(\eta)\mathscr{D}=\mathbf{n}\cdot[\nabla\phi]_{y=0}=\Bigl[-\eta_x u_x+(1+\eta_x^2)(1+h^{-1}\eta)^{-1}u_y\Bigr]_{y=0},$

$\mathbf{n}=(-\eta_x,1),\qquad \nabla\phi=\bigl(u_x-(1+h^{-1}y)(1+h^{-1}\eta)^{-1}\eta_x u_y\,,\,(1+h^{-1}\eta)^{-1}u_y\bigr)$

to conclude that $G(\varepsilon f)=\sum_{n=0}^\infty \varepsilon^n G_n(f)$ with

$G_n(f)\mathscr{D}=-f_x u_{n-1,x}+\sum_{m=0}^n(-h^{-1}f)^m u_{n-m,y}+f_x^2\sum_{m=0}^{n-2}(-h^{-1}f)^m u_{n-2-m,y},$

where empty sums (with upper index smaller than lower index) are zero.
