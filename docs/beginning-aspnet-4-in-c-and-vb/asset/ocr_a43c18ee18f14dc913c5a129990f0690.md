# # Video Generation Models: A Comprehensive Review
## A Comprehensive Review of Diffusion, Autoregressive, and Generative Frameworks

Yihan Cao, Li Ma, Dangguo Chen, Yichen Jia, Zhiwei Xu, Ling Chen, Xiaolong Jiang, Yan Gao

Institute of High Performance Computing, A*STAR, Singapore 138632

**Keywords:** Video Generation · Diffusion Models · Autoregressive Models · Generative AI · Video Synthesis

**Abstract.** Significant progress in the field of video generation has been made due to the emergence of deep generative models. Given the scale of progress, a comprehensive and up-to-date analysis is essential to guide future developments and applications. In this work, we summarize the current state-of-the-art and categorize video generation models into three main frameworks: (i) diffusion-based, (ii) autoregressive-based, and (iii) other generative frameworks. We analyze their methodologies, architectural innovations, and the challenges they address. We also review benchmark datasets, evaluation metrics, and key applications to provide a holistic view of the field. Finally, we discuss open challenges and future research directions, including computational efficiency, temporal coherence, and multimodal integration.

# ## 1 Introduction

Video generation, the task of synthesizing realistic and temporally coherent sequences of frames, is a longstanding challenge in computer vision and artificial intelligence. Early approaches primarily relied on autoregressive models in pixel space or Generative Adversarial Networks (GANs) [1], but these methods often struggled to capture the complex spatiotemporal dependencies inherent in video data. The recent rise of diffusion models [2, 3, 4] and large-scale Transformers [5, 6] has significantly shifted the landscape, enabling unprecedented realism and controllability in generated videos.

This review addresses three central questions. First, what are the principal methodologies and architectural innovations that define the current generation of video models? Second, how are these models evaluated, and what benchmarks have become standard? Third, what are the prominent applications and open challenges that shape the future of this domain? By systematically investigating these aspects, we aim to provide a comprehensive reference for researchers and practitioners entering the field.
