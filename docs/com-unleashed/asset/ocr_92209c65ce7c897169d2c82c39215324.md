# # About the Author

**Rick Leinecker** is a veteran software developer and author with more than 15 years of experience. Among his current book titles are *The Visual C++ 6 Bible* and *Sams Teach Yourself Visual J++ in 21 Days*. He's written hundreds of magazine articles, and now writes for developer Web sites such as www.developer.com and www.sourceDNA.com.

You might find some of his software such as *Perfect Checkmate* and *Bicycle Bridge* when you visit retail stores. He's written dozens of entertainment titles over the years including *Trump Castle* and *Miami Vice*.

He's held a number of lead positions in major companies including Senior Software Engineer at MCI's digital imaging division and Senior Software Engineer at Landmark Communications.

You can find Rick's hot-off-the-press work by visiting www.developer.com and www.sourceDNA.com, where he writes weekly articles on DNA technology. He also moderates the Windows 2000 forums on www.CodeGuru.com. You can find his well-developed imaging library named *ImageObject* at www.infinetvision.net.

When he's not writing books and software, Rick can be found participating in theater guild productions such as *Bye Bye Birdie* and *Camelot*. He also sings, plays the guitar and French horn, and writes music.
