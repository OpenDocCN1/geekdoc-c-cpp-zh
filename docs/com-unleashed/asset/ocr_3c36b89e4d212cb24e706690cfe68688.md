# ## Frameworks

Visual Basic and Visual C++ (MFC and ATL) provide built-in facilities that automatically register both in-process and out-of-process servers.

If you look at the MFC source code, you’ll notice two functions called AfxOleRegisterServerClass() and AfxOleUnregisterServerClass() that provide some of the necessary functionality of registering and unregistering your COM servers. In ATL, you have two similar functions called AtlModuleRegisterServer() and AtlModuleUnregisterServer().

MFC has two functions to register type libraries, AfxOleRegisterTypeLib() and AfxOleUnregisterTypeLib(). ATL has a function, AtlModuleRegisterTypeLib(), that also helps in registering type libraries.
