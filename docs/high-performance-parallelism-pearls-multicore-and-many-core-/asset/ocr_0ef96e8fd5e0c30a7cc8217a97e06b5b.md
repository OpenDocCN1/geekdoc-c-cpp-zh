Since performance falls off as chunksize increases, we expected the “i-inside” version of WSM6 to perform better than the “k-inside” version. Figure 2.18 shows that our expectation was incorrect. Although the technique of pushing a horizontal index down into single-column (k-innermost) code has proven beneficial for other physics packages such as shortwave radiation (recent work by Michalakes), in the case of WSM6, the slowdown incurred by the need to introduce complex and sparse masks in the “i” dimension overwhelmed the benefit of matching chunksize to vector length.
