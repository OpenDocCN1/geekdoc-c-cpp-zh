## Foreword

I am very happy that a team of young teachers from the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, KLE Dr. M.S. Sheshgiri College of Engineering and Technology, Belgaum has produced this beautiful book “*C Programming Laboratory Handbook for Beginners*”. This collective effort, based on the long experience of the authors involved in teaching programming in “C”, succeeds eminently not only in explaining “C” but also in conveying the spirit of solving problems using “C”!

The authors use a set of team exercises to expose the readers to different features of “C” as well as problem-solving using “C”. These sections are structured well. The problem definition is followed by the objectives, prerequisites for coding the solution, related theory, the actual algorithm, the corresponding flowchart, the actual program, and then the sample outputs and outcomes. Where appropriate, this is followed by a set of related programs and practice programs. This structure allows the students to gain a deep understanding of the issues involved! An extremely useful section at the end of the book describes common programming errors and this section clearly demonstrates the deep familiarity of the authors with novice programmers and the types of mistakes they generally commit. Another interesting feature of the book is that the authors provide, wherever possible, alternative approaches to solving problems. This pedagogic technique will definitely help the readers in getting a proper perspective of the art of programming!

I congratulate the teachers for producing this beautiful book and wish them all the best.

I am confident that students will find this book very useful.

Dr. K. Rajinikanth  
Former Principal, M.S. Ramaiah Institute of Technology  
Bangalore

## Preface

This handbook is essentially embodying the art and science of “conversion of problem to logic” and aids the learner to develop computer program rather than learning only the semantics of the programming constructs. The programming exercises are sequenced in the handbook, in sync with the university-defined syllabus for First Year Engineering course. The supporting theory makes the content learner centric.

The depth of this handbook focuses on the kinds of problems that the beginners stumble across. Each program in this handbook has been successfully compiled and executed for various inputs. Sample programs and practice problem definitions related to each term exercise help the learner to strengthen the programming skills in an incremental fashion.

Special acknowledgment goes to all my junior colleagues who not only provided material for this handbook, but also successfully compiled and executed all the programs. I gratefully acknowledge the senior faculty members of my department who shared their knowledge, ideas, provided numerous tips and reshaped the entire manual all of which culminated in the completion of this handbook. I owe my sincere gratitude to KLE family. Special thanks to our beloved Principal Dr. S. C. Pilli and my research guide Dr. S. S. Manvi for their constant moral support. Any errors in the manual rest solely on my shoulders.

Dr. Nandini Sidnal
