
## Interaction between PS and SDTTD
Actual data from the `PS` (e.g., production schedule, working states of machines, inventories of materials) are injected into the `SDTTD` continuously for data cleaning, transformation, and fusion. The processed data in the `SDTTD` can be obtained by the constructed agent for each entity to make the entity aware of the states of itself and others. If necessary, the behaviors of the entities can be adjusted to better implement cooperation considering a global production target.

## Interaction between VS and SDTTD
Simulated data from the `VS` (e.g., predefined orders, simulated analysis results, predicted states) are transmitted to the `SDTTD` and fused with data from other sources. For the

## Interaction between SSS and SDTD
Services data from the `SSS` (e.g., market data, scheduling for resources, machine maintenance strategies) are transmitted to the `SDTD` for data processing and fusion. For the `SSS`, the `SDTD` provides the corresponding data, algorithms, and models that can be encapsulated into subservices to feed the service composition and the subsequent service process toward the specific demand.

## Interaction between PS and SSS
When the task is transmitted to the `DTS`, services in the `SSS` provide the initial production plan to allocate the production resources in the `PS` and start the production. During the production, real-time data collected from the `PS` are transmitted to the `SSS`. Based on the data, the `SSS` analyzes the current demands of the resources and invokes the corresponding services autonomously, such as the services for prognostics, maintenance, scheduling, and energy consumption optimization.

## Interaction between VS and SSS
Services for the `PS` predefine the production activities and can be transmitted to the `VS` for verification before execution. The `VS` will feed back the simulated data and optimization advice to the `SSS` to adjust the predefined production activities until they can fully pass the validation. According to the validated activities, a set of predefined production orders can be generated to guide the actual production. Whereas, for `VS`, the `SSS` provides services to ensure the high fidelity of the `VS` through calibrating the model parameters to sustain its performance with the `PS`, which supports more accurate validation.

## Interaction between PS and VS
During the actual production, the `PS` and the `VS` operate simultaneously. The `PS` generates real-time data, and transmits them to the `VS` to update the states of the corresponding models. The `VS` can adjust the predefined production orders according to the real-time data from the `PS`, and feeds back the adjusted orders in real-time to trim the actual production process to make it synchronize with the predefined one. Through the above process, the production process can be optimized continuously.

After the production, the products will be output, satisfying the requirements of delivery, cost, quality, etc. Then the production resources will be ready for the next production operation.

If the two parts are not consistent, meaning their difference exceeds the predefined threshold, the evaluation service driven by the fused data will be given. It judges whether the inconsistency is caused by the disturbances in the `PS` (e.g., equipment failure, material shortage, and emergency order) or by the inaccuracy of models in the `VS` (e.g., unreasonable setting on boundary and initial condition). If the disturbances exist in the `PS`, corresponding services, such as scheduling service, quality service, and congress service, will be invoked to eliminate them. These services are given to the `VS` first for verification, then transformed into control orders to regulate the `PS`. Otherwise, the inconsistency may be caused by the `VS`. In this case, services for model calibration are scheduled and implemented on the `VS`. The production plan verified by the previous models shall be regulated based on the calibrated ones, while the production process will be adjusted accordingly [6].

After production, the finished products are output and transported to the warehouses. At the same time, the history production data are achieved from the records in the models. Based on these data, a data mining service extracts new knowledge for model building and calibration. With the adjustable timeline, the `VS` can play back the historical situations, which is an effective way to identify the defects in previous productions as well as the corresponding solutions [6].

Thus, `DTS` involves a constant evolution process along with different rounds of production.
