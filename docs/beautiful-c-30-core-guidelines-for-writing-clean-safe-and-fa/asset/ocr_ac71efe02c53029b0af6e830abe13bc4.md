# ## Abstract

Thermal barrier coatings (TBCs) are widely used in gas turbine engines to protect metallic components from high temperatures. The porosity of TBCs is a critical parameter affecting their thermal insulation performance and lifetime. This paper presents a non-destructive approach for measuring the porosity of TBCs using terahertz frequency-modulated continuous-wave (FMCW) reflectometry. The method is based on the correlation between the effective refractive index of the coating and its porosity. Terahertz FMCW reflectometry provides depth-resolved information, allowing the measurement of the refractive index and thickness of the TBC. The porosity of the TBC is then determined from the effective refractive index using effective medium approximations. The proposed method was validated on plasma-sprayed YSZ coatings with varying porosity levels. The results show that terahertz FMCW reflectometry can accurately measure the porosity of TBCs in a non-destructive and non-contact manner. The method has the potential to be used for quality control and lifetime assessment of TBCs in industrial applications.

**Keywords:** Terahertz, FMCW, thermal barrier coating, porosity, non-destructive testing
