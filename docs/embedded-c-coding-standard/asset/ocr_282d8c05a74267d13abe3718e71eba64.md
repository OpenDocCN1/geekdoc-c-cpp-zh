It is permissible for individual project teams, whole companies, and others to adopt all or a subset of the rules herein as their coding standard. Indeed, we are happy that many readers of earlier editions have done this and that many will. Adoption of the rules as presented herein may be done simply by identifying “Barr Group’s Embedded C Coding Standard” (alternatively, “BARR-C2018”) as the source of your rules.

To help develop teams customize this coding standard to meet their project-company-specific needs, an editable version of this document is available for license and download at barrgroup.com/coding-standard.
