**Dr. Neeraj Kumar Rathore** is an accomplished professional holding the position of Associate Professor and serving as the Head of the Department of Computer Science at Indira Gandhi National Tribal University (IGNTU-A Central University), located in Amarkantak, Madhya Pradesh, India. He assumed his role as an Associate Professor in March 2021 and subsequently took on the responsibility of Department Head in May 2022. Prior to joining IGNTU, Dr. Neeraj accumulated extensive teaching experience of more than thirteen years at prestigious institutions such as Sri G.S. Institute of Technology & Science in Indore, Madhya Pradesh, Jaypee University of Engineering & Technology in Guna, Madhya Pradesh, and Modi Institute of Technology in Rajasthan. Additionally, he gained valuable insights from the IT Industry, having worked as a Software Engineer at Computer Sciences Corporation (CSC). Dr. Neeraj holds a Ph.D. in Computer Science, specializing in Grid Computing, which he earned in 2014 from Thapar University, Punjab. He also holds an M.E. in Computer Engineering, obtained in 2008 from the same university, and a B.E. in Computer Science and Engineering, which he achieved in 2006 from Rajasthan University, Rajasthan. His areas of expertise and research interests encompass Parallel and Distributed Computing, Grid Computing, Cloud Computing, Big Data, IoT, DBMS, and Data Structures. Dr. Neeraj has contributed significantly to the field, with a remarkable portfolio of 72 publications, including 16 SCI Papers, 7 Patents, and 7 Books in prestigious International/SCI Journals and Conferences. As an esteemed academician, Dr. Neeraj has successfully supervised seven Master’s theses and two Ph.D., with several ongoing research projects under his guidance. His dedication to research and education has earned him a commendable reputation in the academic community.
