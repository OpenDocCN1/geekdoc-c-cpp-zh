# 7.9. SUMMARY

In this session you had a detailed look at strings and their characteristics along with an idea as how they are represented in the memory. Then the important standard string functions which are the strlen(), strcpy(), strcat() and the strcmp() were introduced and explained with examples. You then learnt how to implement strings into functions, pointers, and arrays. The session concluded with a regular dose of Do’s and Don’ts.
