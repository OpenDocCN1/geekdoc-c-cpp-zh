# 14.1 INTRODUCTION

Entertainment, gaming, digital content, and streaming services are increasingly provided by means of cloud computing technologies. The benefits of this deployment model are as numerous as the legal challenges it poses. In fact, cloud technology generally improves availability, usability, integration, and portability of digital media services while decreasing costs.
