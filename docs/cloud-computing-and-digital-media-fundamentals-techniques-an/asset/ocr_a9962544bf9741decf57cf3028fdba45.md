2. Add $e^j$ to $V$
3. for $k = 1$ to $n$ do

6. Make BPQx deterministic in $e_i \doteq e_j$
7. end if
8. $V \leftarrow V \cup \{e^j \doteq e^k\}$
9. $E \leftarrow E \cup \{e_i \doteq e_j, e_j \doteq e_k, e_i \doteq e_k\}$
