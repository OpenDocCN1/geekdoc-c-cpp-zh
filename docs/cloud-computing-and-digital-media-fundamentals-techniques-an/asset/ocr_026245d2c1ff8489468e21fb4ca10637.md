# 14.2 PERSONAL DATA PROTECTION ISSUES

Users provide their personal data to the right holder to create an account and enter into a relevant licence agreement in order to access the digital media. The right holder is the collector of the users’ data, which will be automatically shared with the other relevant subjects [i.e., CSP(s) and, possibly, with the third-party IPR owner(s)] in order to provide users with the requested service.¹ Legally speaking, the right holder acts as the data controller,² CSP(s) and third-party IPR owner(s), if any, who will typically be the data processors,³ and the users are the data subjects.³⁴ At the time of the collection of the personal data, the right holder must provide the user with information
