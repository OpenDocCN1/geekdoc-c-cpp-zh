## Cloud Computing and Digital Media

### 1.1 INTRODUCTION

Mobile multimedia cloud provides access to data-intensive services (multimedia services) and data stored in the cloud via power-constrained mobile devices. With the development of multimedia computing, mobile devices, multimedia services, and cloud computing, mobile multimedia cloud attracts growing attention from researchers and practitioners.

Mobile devices refer to miniaturized personal computers (PCs) in the form of pocket PCs, tablet PCs, and smartphones. They provide optional and portable ways for users to experience the computing world. Mobile devices are also becoming the most frequently used terminal. A mobile application (mobile app) is a software application designed to run on mobile devices. Mobile apps such as Apple App Store (https://store.apple.com/us), Google Play (https://play.google.com/store?hl=en), Windows Phone Store (http://www.windowsphone.com/en-us/), and BlackBerry App World (http://appworld.blackberry.com/webstore/) are usually operated by the owner of the mobile system. Original mobile apps were for general purposes, e-mail, calendars, contacts, stock market, and weather information. However, the number and variety of apps are quickly increasing to other categories, such as mobile games, factory automation, global positioning system (GPS) and location-based services, binding ticket purchases, and multimedia applications. Mobile multimedia applications are concerned with intelligent multimedia techniques to facilitate effort-free multimedia experiences on mobile devices, including media acquisition, editing, sharing, browsing, management, search, advertising, and related user interface.

Cloud computing creates a new way of designing, developing, deploying, and maintaining applications on the Internet. The cloud center distributes power, applications, and large systems among a group of machines. A cloud computing platform consists of a variety of services for developing, testing, deploying, and maintaining applications in the cloud.
