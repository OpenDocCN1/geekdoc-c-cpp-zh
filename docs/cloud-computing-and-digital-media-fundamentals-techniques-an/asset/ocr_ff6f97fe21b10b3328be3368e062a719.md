As regards the active role of right holders, they shall have to keep the pace of the swift developments in technological field to remain competitive and adapt their business offer in an effective way to meet users' expectations. The offer of digital content via cloud services to users shall have to be planned in due advance as to (1) identify ahead of time opportunities arising from technological developments in the short-mid term (e.g., a new unexplored platform or device for making available digital content, such as streaming or progressive download to set-top boxes and/ portable devices); (2) adapt existing business models or identify new ones in respect of the said technological opportunities arising (e.g., extending the offer of pay-TV on portable devices to current residential customers and/ new customers, whether on a subscription or free trial basis); and (3) assess ahead of whether current licensing agreements in place enable the right holders to make available to the public digital content according to their envisaged business models or otherwise require further investments. In a nutshell, the competitiveness of right holders shall require a continuous internal dialog and cohesion between internal departments (technology, legal, marketing, CRM, finance, etc.) to ensure an effective and timely business plan is adequately devised.
