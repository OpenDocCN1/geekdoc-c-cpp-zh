# Foreword

The contemporary necessity of a ubiquitous access requirement for increasing mobile device users has boosted the adoption of cloud computing for digital media enterprises and executives. Most cloud centers can be considered as geographically neutral because they can be accessed by mobile devices from locations worldwide. This is this characteristic of cloud services that enables digital media companies to develop new and better ways to quickly and efficiently deliver media content to fine-grained consumers.

Among various technical disciplines that have been vigorously impacted by cloud computing, digital media is probably the most beneficiary from the recent advances in cloud computing. One reason for this pronounced impact can be attributed to digital media in its enormous data volume and real-time requirement throughout the entire life cycle from generation, encoding, storage, processing, transmission, reception, and consumption. Cloud computing services, with their on-demand provision of nature, have been able to offer extremely flexible

Many digital media applications are indeed demanding computing at the data center for an efficient management of media contents so as to release the burden of computational requirements for media terminals. Such applications are very suited for the most acknowledged cloud computing service class known as infrastructure as service (IaaS). The demands for intensive computation typically involving metric data with parallel machines at cloud centers. More recently, two new types of cloud computing services, known as a service (SaaS) and platform as a service (PaaS), have also been recognized as having the potential to substantially change the way media content is accessed by consumers distributed over scattered geographical locations.

We have every reason to anticipate a profound penetration of cloud technology into all walks of life. Based on recent forecasts by leading market analysis, the compound growth rate for cloud services should remain at 20% or even higher for the next few years. The cloud-based strategy can also improve media companies' competitive advantage
