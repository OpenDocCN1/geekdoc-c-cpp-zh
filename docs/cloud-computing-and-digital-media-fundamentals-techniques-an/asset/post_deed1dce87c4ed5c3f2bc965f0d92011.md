- subscription/rental/pay per download (assisted by DRM measures, e.g., Rhapsody), relatively successful, many because of DRM-triggered issues (see above);

## 14.4 UPCOMING SCENARIOS AND RECOMMENDATIONS

The development of new technologies is significantly changing the existing business models and legal frameworks, and puts in conflict the fundamental rights that were not in conflict before (e.g., privacy and copyright). This is pushing toward finding new balances between the concurrent interests of the right holders, CSPs, and other service providers and intermediaries.
