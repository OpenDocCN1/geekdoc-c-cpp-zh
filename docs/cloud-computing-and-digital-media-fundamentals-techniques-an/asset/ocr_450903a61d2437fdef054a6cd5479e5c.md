11.2.3.4 Computing Fundamental Matrix Using Eight-Point Algorithm
In this section, the equations on $F$ generated by point correspondences between two images and their minimal solution are described. Then an algorithm is described for automatically obtaining point correspondences that $F$ may be estimated directly from an image pair.

11.2.3.4.1 Basic Equations In Section 11.2.3.3, the fundamental matrix is defined by the equation: $m^	op Fm=0$. Given sufficiently many point matches $m^	op$(at least seven), the equation can be used to compute the unknown matrix $F$. Specifically, the equation corresponds to a pair of points $(x, y, 1)$ and $(x', y', 1)$ is

```
$xf11+xf12+xf13+yf21+yf22\n+yf23+xf31+yf32+f33=0 \quad (11.9)$
```

The nine-vector made up of the entries of $F$ in row-order is denoted by $f$. From a set of $n$ point matches, we obtain a set of linear equations for the form:

```
$Af=\begin{bmatrix}\tilde{x}_1 & x_1 & y_1 & & x_1 & y_1 & \sim \& & & & & & & 1. \x_n & y_n & & x_n & y_n & 1^a\end{bmatrix}f=0 \quad (11.10)$
```
