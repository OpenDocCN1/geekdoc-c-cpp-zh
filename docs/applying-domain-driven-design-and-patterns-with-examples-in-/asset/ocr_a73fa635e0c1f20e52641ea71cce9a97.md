# PROPOSAL

Abstract

This proposal is submitted to request funding for the project titled *Environmental Impact Assessment of Urban Development*. The project aims to evaluate the potential environmental effects of planned infrastructure projects in the metropolitan area, focusing on air quality, water resources, and biodiversity.

Objectives

+   1. Assess the current environmental conditions in the target area.
+   2. Predict potential impacts of proposed urban development activities.
+   3. Recommend mitigation strategies to minimize negative impacts.

Methodology

The study will use a combination of field surveys, remote sensing data, and statistical modeling. Monitoring stations will be installed to collect baseline data over a six-month period.

| Phase | Duration | Tasks |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Phase 1 | Months 1-2 | Baseline data collection |
| Phase 2 | Months 3-4 | Impact modeling and analysis |
| Phase 3 | Months 5-6 | Reporting and recommendations |
