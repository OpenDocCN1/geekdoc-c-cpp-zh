# Construction Supply Chain Management Business Models

# A Road Map for Success

The construction industry has become increasingly more complex. There has been significant growth in construction projects in domestic markets and in emerging free market economies throughout the world. Horizontal and vertical construction projects are increasing at an impressive rate. The construction of bridges, highways, schools, housing developments, hospitals, apartment buildings, manufacturing facilities, and levies has led to an explosion in construction knowledge. What follows is a general categorization of two construction supply chain business models that encompass the various types of construction projects.

# Public and Private Sector Heavy Construction and Horizontal Supply Chain Models

Projects in heavy construction or horizontal segment are the most complicated and require the highest levels of technical, financial, and managerial expertise. This market sector also requires sophisticated materials handling and placement methods. The characteristics of the heavy construction supply chain market segment are:

+   - Dams, bridges, flood control highways, airports, interchanges, and the like
+   - Large projects—petroleum refineries, nuclear plants, and the like
