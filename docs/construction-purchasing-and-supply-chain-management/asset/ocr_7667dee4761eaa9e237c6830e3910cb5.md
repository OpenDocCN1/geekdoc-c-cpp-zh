In some respects, prequalification is akin to a performance bond and helps the state safeguard the taxpayers’ dollars. Annual prequalification is the key to regular bid lettings. Only the contractors who are prequalified in advance—that is, those who are capable of building a particular item and have the financial resources, equipment, and personnel—are permitted to bid.

Because of prequalification, when all the bids are accepted then, generally speaking, the only remaining task is to identify the low bidder.
