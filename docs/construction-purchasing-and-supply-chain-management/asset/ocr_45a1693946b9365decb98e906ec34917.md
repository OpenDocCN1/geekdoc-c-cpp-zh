# Indiana Department of Transportation (DOT) Project Development Process (PDP) for Major Projects Construction (Updated 12/06/05)

START

Accounting

Procurement Services & Traffic

+   STEP 31
+   PRECONSTRUCTION ACTIVITIES
+   - Pre-construction meeting
+   - Subcontractor & supplier listings
+   - After award prior to NTP
+   - 10 days after NTP

+   STEP 32
+   FIRST DAY OF WORK
+   - First physical work
+   - Project limits
+   - Typically when contract time starts
+   - 25 days maximum after NTP

+   STEP 33
+   FIRST DAY OF TRAFFIC
+   - May be same as first day of work
+   - 7-day notice required from contractor

+   STEP 34
+   CONSTRUCTION ADMINISTRATION
+   - Inspection & testing activities
+   - Inspector's daily reports
+   - Traffic control
+   - Material certifications
+   - Material investigations (as required)

+   STEP 35
+   PROGRESS ESTIMATE (payroll)
+   - Contractor requests monthly progress estimate
+   - Constructed & eroding work complete

+   STEP 36
+   TRAFFIC EVENTS
+   - May change (72 hr notice required)
+   - Compliance with contract
+   - Facility closures
+   - Approval required

+   STEP 37
+   CHANGE ORDERS
+   - Processed in quantities, added items, deleted items, time adjustments, specifications
+   - Loaded contract CL 5, ERL, etc.

+   STEP 38
+   MONTHLY REPORTS
+   - Processed by 5th of each month
+   - Percent complete
+   - Contract &
+   - Modified contract $
+   - Narrative of significant work items
+   - Status of DBE, EEO, etc.

+   STEP 39
+   INTERMEDIATE CONTRACT COMPLETION (S)
+   - Contract completed (unless otherwise noted)
+   - May be noted "inactive" when no work performed for a period of time
+   - Contractor has 2x contract time (inactive days excluded)

+   STEP 40
+   SUBSTANTIAL COMPLETION DATE
+   - Contractual date
+   - Open to unrestricted traffic
+   - Signage
+   - Essentially complete
+   - Ready for Pretrial & Final inspections
+   - 4 weeks after this date without LDs

+   STEP 41
+   FINAL ACCEPTANCE DATE
+   - Letter issued by D/O
+   - 90 days after inspection
+   - Contractor referred to surety
+   - Contractor referred to warranty
+   - Guarantee & warranty periods if specified

+   STEP 42
+   CONSTRUCTION COMPLETION
+   - Written acceptance by owner of completed work
+   - Last day of work

+   STEP 43
+   FINAL FIELD ADMINISTRATIVE ACTIVITIES
+   - Complete field activities by completion of final plan items
+   - Submission of final construction records
+   - Final & proposal estimate received from contractor
+   - Project site acceptance
+   - Loaded contract processed
+   - Final CRL issued
+   - PERF & required submitted to District
+   - PERF & required submitted to PECPS
+   - Final Field closed

+   STEP 44
+   CONTRACT CLOSEOUT
+   - CRL received by District
+   - CCR & QC complete certification by Contractor
+   - FIN-47 submitted by Contractor
+   - Final voucher 180 days after completion

+   STEP 45
+   CONTRACT COMPLETION DATE
+   - Final payment voucher processed

+   STEP 46
+   WARRANTY COMPLETION
+   - Warranty bond periods completed
