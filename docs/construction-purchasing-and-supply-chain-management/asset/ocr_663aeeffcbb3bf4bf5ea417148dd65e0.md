+   (a) Use the Joint Venture name or assets in any way, except for the transaction of the legitimate Joint Venture business, nor to do any act in contravention of the Joint Venture Agreement;

+   (b) Have any power or authority to do any act prohibited by law to be done by a single joint venturer, unless authorized specifically herein;

+   (c) Release or discharge any indebtedness owed the Joint Venture in excess of $1,000.00 for less than the full amount thereof without the express consent of the other Joint Venturer;
