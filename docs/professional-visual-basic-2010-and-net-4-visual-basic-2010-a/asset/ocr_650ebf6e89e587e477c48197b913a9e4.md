# Design Surface

Visual Studio and Expression Blend also share a similar design surface. The design surface is the area onto which you can drag your controls. For example, you can drag a Button control onto the design surface from the Toolbox or the Assets window. Just as in Visual Studio, you can drag any control to any location on the layout.

Before delving too deeply into the design surface, drag a few controls onto it. For this example, you'll build a simple login screen by dragging two TextBlocks, a TextBox, a PasswordBox, and a Button onto the design surface. Position your controls as shown in Figure 18-5.
