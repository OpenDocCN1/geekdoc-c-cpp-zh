# # R1 Rotation

Suppose right subtree of node A is Ar of height h, left and right subtree of node B are Bl and Br of height h and h-1 respectively. Now a node X from Ar is deleted, then the height of Ar becomes h-1 and A be the nearest ancestor node of X, with balance factor +2. The node B is the root of the left subtree of A with balance factor +1 and node X deleted from the right subtree of A, therefore R1 rotation is used.
