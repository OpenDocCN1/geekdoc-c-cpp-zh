# ## Acknowledgments for the First Edition

This book would not have been possible without the assistance from many wonderful people. We'd like to thank all of the people and companies who helped and supported us during the past few years.

First, we'd like to thank all of the reviewers and everyone else who gave us their opinion on early manuscripts. These people ensured the book is in a quality state we would never have without their input. The reviewers for this book were Kyle Reilly, Thomas DeVries, Patrick McNeil, Michael Kinn, and Jan Christiaan van Wijk. Special thanks to Dimitri Kustas, who was the technical editor and whose attention to the book was an immeasurable contribution to the quality of this book.

We'd also like to thank all of the people and companies who gave us the opportunity to test our examples on different platforms with different devices. Many thanks to the Nokia Design Group for their great support and their help during the standardization process and the writing of this book. Many thanks also to all of the developers in the W3C CSS Working Group for their specifications and the fruitful discussions we've had. For more information, see www.w3.org/Style/CSS/members. Our thanks also to the members of the Open Mobile Alliance (OMA), the Web Standards Project (WaSP), and the World Wide Web Consortium (W3C).

The Adobe, Apple, and Opera teams did another great job. We want to thank them for the donations in producing this book, especially the help we received from Adobe's support in the book. Thanks also to Typo3, Bruce Attridge, Maria Eklund, Joe D'Angelo, Charles Larsson, Christian Heilmann, and Molly Holzschlag. We're grateful to Molly, too, who reviewed the book with Adobe, wisely, helping us to make it better.
