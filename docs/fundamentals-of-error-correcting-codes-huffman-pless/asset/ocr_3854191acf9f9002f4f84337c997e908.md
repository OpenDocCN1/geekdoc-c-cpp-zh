In [263], a subgroup of Γ Aut(S(q)) = M Aut(S(q)) has been found. There is evidence that this subgroup may be the entire automorphism group of S(q) for q > 5, at least when q is a prime. The monomial automorphism group contains Z = ⟨I2q+2, -I2q+2⟩. The group Z is the center of M Aut(S(q)). If q ≡ 1 (mod 4), the automorphism (10.8) generates a subgroup J4 of M Aut(S(q)) of order 4 containing Z. If q ≡ -1 (mod 4), the automorphism (10.9) generates a subgroup J2 of M Aut(S(q)) of order 2 intersecting Z only in the identity I2q+2. Finally, M Aut(S(q)) contains a subgroup P such that P/Z is isomorphic to the projective general linear group PGL2(q), which is a group of order q(q2 - 1). If q ≡ 1 (mod 4), M Aut(S(q)) contains a subgroup generated by P and J4 of order 4q(q2 - 1); if q ≡ -1 (mod 4), M Aut(S(q)) contains a subgroup generated by P and J2 also of order 4q(q2 - 1).
