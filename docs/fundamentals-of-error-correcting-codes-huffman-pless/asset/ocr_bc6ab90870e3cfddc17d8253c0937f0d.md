# **Theorem 4.4.3** Let $C$ be an $[n, k]$ cyclic code over $\mathbb{F}_q$ with zeros $Z$ in a splitting field $\mathbb{F}_{q^t}$ of $x^n - 1$ over $\mathbb{F}_q$. Let $\alpha \in \mathbb{F}_{q^t}$ be a primitive $n$th root of unity in $\mathbb{F}_{q^t}$, and let $Z = \{\alpha^j \mid j \in C_{i_1} \cup \cdots \cup C_{i_w}\}$, where $C_{i_1}, \dots, C_{i_w}$ are distinct $q$-cyclotomic cosets modulo $n$. Let $L$ be the $w \times n$ matrix over $\mathbb{F}_q$ defined by

```
L = \left[ \begin{array}{ccccc}
1 & \alpha^{i_1} & \alpha^{2i_1} & \cdots & \alpha^{(n-1)i_1} \\
1 & \alpha^{i_2} & \alpha^{2i_2} & \cdots & \alpha^{(n-1)i_2} \\
\vdots & \vdots & \vdots & \ddots & \vdots \\
1 & \alpha^{i_w} & \alpha^{2i_w} & \cdots & \alpha^{(n-1)i_w}
\end{array} \right].
```

Then $c$ is in $C$ if and only if $Lc^T = 0$. Choosing a basis of $\mathbb{F}_{q^t}$ over $\mathbb{F}_q$, we may represent each element of $\mathbb{F}_{q^t}$ as a $t \times 1$ column vector over $\mathbb{F}_q$. Replacing each entry of $L$ by its corresponding column vector, we obtain a $tw \times n$ matrix $H$ over $\mathbb{F}_q$ which has the property that $c \in C$ if and only if $Hc^T = 0$. In particular, $k \ge n - tw$.

**Proof:** We have $c(x) \in C$ if and only if $c(\alpha^j) = 0$ for all $j \in C_{i_1} \cup \cdots \cup C_{i_w}$, which by Theorem 3.7.4 is equivalent to $c(\alpha^{j'}) = 0$ for $1 \le j \le w$. Clearly, this is equivalent to $Lc^T = 0$, which is a system of homogeneous linear equations with coefficients that are powers of $\alpha$. Expanding each of these powers of $\alpha$ in the chosen basis of $\mathbb{F}_{q^t}$ over $\mathbb{F}_q$ yields the equivalent system $Hc^T = 0$. As the rows of $H$ may be dependent, $k \ge n - tw$.

If $C'$ is the code over $\mathbb{F}_{q^t}$ with parity check matrix $L$ in this theorem, then the code $C$ is actually the subfield subcode $C'|_{\mathbb{F}_q}$.
