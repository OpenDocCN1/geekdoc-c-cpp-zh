# **Lemma 7.8.4** If $\mathcal{C} = \mathcal{P}_1^\perp \perp \dots \perp \mathcal{P}_k$ is an orthogonal sum where each $\mathcal{P}_i$ is either an $H$-plane or an $A$-plane, then the hull of $\mathcal{C}$ is $\{0\}$.

**Proof:** If $\mathbf{y} \in \mathcal{C} \subseteq \mathcal{C}^\perp$, where $\mathbf{x} = \mathbf{x}_1 + \dots + \mathbf{x}_k$ with $\mathbf{x}_i \in \mathcal{P}_i$, for $1 \le i \le k$, then $\mathbf{x} \cdot \mathbf{y} = 0$ for all $\mathbf{y} \in \mathcal{C}$. As $\mathcal{P}_i \cap \mathcal{P}_i^\perp = \{\mathbf{0}\}$, if $\mathbf{x}_i \neq \mathbf{0}$, there exists $\mathbf{y}_i \in \mathcal{P}_i$ with $\mathbf{x}_i \cdot \mathbf{y}_i \neq 0$. Thus $0 = \mathbf{x} \cdot \mathbf{y}_i$; but $\mathbf{x} \cdot \mathbf{y}_i = \mathbf{x}_i \cdot \mathbf{y}_i \neq 0$ as $\mathbf{x}_j \cdot \mathbf{y}_i = 0$ if $j \neq i$. This contradiction shows $\mathbf{x}_i = \mathbf{0}$. So $\mathcal{C} \cap \mathcal{C}^{\perp \perp} = \{0\}$.

We now state our main decomposition result, found in [3].

# **Theorem 7.8.5** Let $\mathcal{C}$ be an $[n, k]$ even binary code whose hull $\mathcal{H}$ has dimension $r$. Then $k = 2m + r$ for some nonnegative integer $m$ and $\mathcal{C}$ has one of the following forms:

+   (H) $\mathcal{C} = \mathcal{H} \perp \mathcal{H}_1 \perp \mathcal{H}_2 \perp \dots \perp \mathcal{H}_m$, where $\mathcal{H}_1, \mathcal{H}_2, \dots, \mathcal{H}_m$ are $H$-planes and $\mathcal{H}$ is doubly-even.
+   (O) $\mathcal{C} = \mathcal{H} \perp \mathcal{H}_1 \perp \mathcal{H}_2 \perp \dots \perp \mathcal{H}_m$, where $\mathcal{H}_1, \mathcal{H}_2, \dots, \mathcal{H}_m$ are $H$-planes and $\mathcal{H}$ is singly-even.
+   (A) $\mathcal{C} = \mathcal{H} \perp \mathcal{H}_1 \perp \mathcal{H}_2 \perp \dots \perp \mathcal{H}_{m-1} \perp \mathcal{A}$, where $\mathcal{H}_1, \mathcal{H}_2, \dots, \mathcal{H}_{m-1}$ are $H$-planes, $\mathcal{A}$ is an $A$-plane, and $\mathcal{H}$ is doubly-even.

**Proof:** If $\mathcal{C} = \mathcal{C}^\perp$, we have either form (H) or form (O) with $m=0$. Assume $\mathcal{C} \neq \mathcal{C}^\perp$. Let $\mathbf{x}_1, \mathbf{x}_2, \dots, \mathbf{x}_r$ be a basis of $\mathcal{H}$; extend this to a basis $\mathbf{x}_1, \mathbf{x}_2, \dots, \mathbf{x}_n$ of $\mathcal{C}$. Let $\mathcal{C}_1 = \text{span}\{\mathbf{x}_{r+1}, \mathbf{x}_{r+2}, \dots, \mathbf{x}_n\}$. Clearly, $\mathcal{C} = \mathcal{H} \perp \mathcal{C}_1$ as every vector in $\mathcal{H}$ is orthogonal to every vector in $\mathcal{H}$ is orthogonal to every vector in $\mathcal{C}_1$. If $\mathbf{z}$ is in the hull of $\mathcal{C}_1$, it is orthogonal to everything in $\mathcal{C}_1$ and in $\mathcal{H}$, and hence to everything in $\mathcal{C}$. Thus $\mathbf{z}$ is also in $\mathcal{H}$, implying that $\mathbf{z} = \mathbf{0}$. So the hull of $\mathcal{C}_1$ is $\{\mathbf{0}\}$. By Lemma 7.8.3, the result follows once we show that the decomposition given in (A) with $\mathcal{H}$ singly-even reduces to (O). In that case, let $\mathcal{A} = \text{span}\{\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{y}\}$ and let $\mathbf{z}$ be a singly-even vector in $\mathcal{H}$. By Lemma 7.8.1, $\mathbf{x}_1 = \mathbf{z} + \mathbf{x}$ and $\mathbf{y}_1 = \mathbf{z} + \mathbf{y}$ are both doubly-even. As $\mathbf{x} \perp \mathbf{y}$, $\mathbf{x}_1 \perp \mathbf{y}_1$; hence $\mathcal{H}_m = \text{span}\{\mathbf{x}_1, \mathbf{y}_1\}$ is an $H$-plane. Clearly, $\mathcal{H} \perp \mathcal{H}_1 \perp \dots \perp \mathcal{H}_{m-1} \perp \mathcal{A} = \mathcal{H} \perp \mathcal{H}_1 \perp \dots \perp \mathcal{H}_m$ and we have form (O).
