# Exercise 614

Show that the set of vectors in R^{24} whose components are multiples of 4 and whose component sum is a multiple of 16 are precisely the vectors in the lattice spanned by 4e_T for T \in T and 4e_i - 4e_j for i and j in \Omega.

The lattice \Gamma_1 is defined to be the lattice spanned by \Gamma_0 and the vector s = (-3, 1, 1, ..., 1). Notice that we obtain a vector with -3 in coordinate i and 1s elsewhere by adding 4e_i - 4e_j to s.

# Lemma 10.6.7

The vector v = v_1 v_2 \cdots v_{24} is in \Gamma_1 if and only if the following three conditions all hold:

+   - (i) The components v_i are all congruent to the same value m modulo 2.
+   - (ii) The set of i for which the components v_i are congruent to the same value modulo 4 form a G_{24}-set.
+   - (iii) \sum_{i=1}^{24} v_i \equiv 4m \pmod 8, where m is defined in (i).

Furthermore, if x and y are in \Gamma_1, then x - y is a multiple of 8 and x \cdot x is a multiple of 16.

# Proof:

Statements (i), (ii), and (iii) hold for the vectors spanning \Gamma_1 and hence for any vector in \Gamma_1. The final statement also holds for vectors in \Gamma_1 as it too holds for the vectors spanning \Gamma_1, noting that if x = \sum x_i . x_i, then x - x = \sum x_i . x_i + 2 \sum_{i<j} x_i . x_j.

Suppose that v satisfies (i), (ii), and (iii). Then we can subtract \alpha s from v where \alpha is one of 0, 1, 2, or 3, so that v - \alpha s has only even coordinates and its component sum is a multiple of 16. By (ii), the set of coordinates in v - \alpha s where the components are not divisible by 4 forms a G_{24}-set. Hence v - \alpha s is in \Gamma_0, by Lemma 10.6.6 and the proof is complete.

The lattice \Gamma_1 is not self-dual. We obtain \Lambda_{24} by multiplying all vectors in \Gamma_1 by 1/\sqrt{8}. Thus

\Lambda_{24} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{8}} \Gamma_1.

By Lemma 10.6.7, the inner product of any two vectors in \Lambda_{24} is an integer, making \Lambda_{24} integral. By the same lemma, the norm of a vector in \Lambda_{24} is an even integer. It can be shown that det \Lambda_{24} = \pm 1 implying that \Lambda_{24} is a Type II lattice. By Exercise 615, its minimum norm is 4 and its kissing number is 196 560.
