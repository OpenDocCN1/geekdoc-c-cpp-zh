The following corollaries are immediate consequences of Theorem 11.7.5.

**Corollary 11.7.7** Let $\mathcal{C}$ be a binary code and $\mathcal{C}'$ a coset of $\mathcal{C}$ with coset leader $\mathbf{x}$. Let $i \in \text{supp}(\mathbf{x})$ and let $\mathbf{u}$ be the vector with 1 in coordinate $i$ and 0 elsewhere. Then $\mathbf{x} - \mathbf{u}$ is a coset leader of a child of $\mathcal{C}'$, and no coset leader of $(\mathbf{x} - \mathbf{u}) + \mathcal{C}$ has $i$ in its support.

**Proof:** By Theorem 11.1.6(v), $\mathbf{x} - \mathbf{u}$ is a coset leader of a child of $\mathcal{C}'$. By Theorem 11.7.5, every coset leader of this child is of the form $\mathbf{y} - \mathbf{u}$, where $\mathbf{u} \le \mathbf{y}$; in particular, no coset leader of the child has $i$ in its support.

**Corollary 11.7.8** ([129]) Let $\mathcal{C}_1$ and $\mathcal{C}_2$ be two cosets of a binary linear code $\mathcal{C}$ and assume that $\mathcal{C}_1 < \mathcal{C}_2$. Then for each coset leader $\mathbf{x}$ of $\mathcal{C}_1$ there is a coset leader $\mathbf{y}$ of $\mathcal{C}_2$ such that $\mathbf{x} < \mathbf{y}$.

From that one coset leader $\mathbf{x}$ every coset leader of an ancestor has support containing $\text{supp}(\mathbf{x})$.
