# **Theorem 12.4.7** Let $\mathcal{D}_l$ be the $\mathbb{Z}_4$-quadratic residue codes of length \(p\). The following hold:

+   (i) If \(p \equiv -1 \pmod 8\), then $\widetilde{\mathcal{D}}_l$ and $\widetilde{\mathcal{D}}_l^\perp$ are self-dual. Furthermore, all codewords of $\widetilde{\mathcal{D}}_l$ and $\widetilde{\mathcal{D}}_l^\perp$ have Euclidean weights a multiple of 8.

+   (ii) If \(p \equiv 1 \pmod 8\), then $\widetilde{\mathcal{D}}_l^\perp = \widetilde{\mathcal{D}}_2$ and $\widetilde{\mathcal{D}}_l^{\perp\perp} = \widetilde{\mathcal{D}}_l$.

**Proof:** Let \(p \equiv -1 \pmod 8\). Then by Theorem 12.4.3(vi), $\mathcal{C}_l^{\perp\perp} = \mathcal{D}_l$. Hence as the extended coordinate of any vector in $\mathcal{C}_l$ is 0 by Exercise 733, the extended codewords arising from $\mathcal{C}_l$ are orthogonal to all codewords in either $\widetilde{\mathcal{D}}_l$ or $\widetilde{\mathcal{D}}_l^\perp$. Since the inner product of $\widetilde{j}(x)$ with itself (and $\widetilde{j}(x)$ with itself) is \(3^2(p+1) \equiv 0 \pmod 4\) (and \(1^2 + 3^2 p \equiv 0 \pmod 4\)), $\widetilde{\mathcal{D}}_l$ and $\widetilde{\mathcal{D}}_l^\perp$ are self-orthogonal using Theorem 12.4.3(v). By Theorem 12.4.3(iv), $\widetilde{\mathcal{D}}_l$ and $\widetilde{\mathcal{D}}_l^\perp$ have \(4^{(p+1)/2}\) codewords implying that $\widetilde{\mathcal{D}}_l$ and $\widetilde{\mathcal{D}}_l^\perp$ are self-dual. To verify that the Euclidean weight of a codeword in $\widetilde{\mathcal{D}}_l$ and $\widetilde{\mathcal{D}}_l^\perp$ is a multiple of 8, we only have to verify this for the rows of a generator matrix of $\widetilde{\mathcal{D}}_l$ and $\widetilde{\mathcal{D}}_l^\perp$ by Theorem 12.2.4. The rows of the generator matrix for $\widetilde{\mathcal{D}}_l$ and $\widetilde{\mathcal{D}}_l^\perp$ are the extensions of $\widetilde{j}(x)$ and cyclic shifts of the generating idempotent of $\mathcal{C}_l$; see Theorem 12.4.6. But the rows of the generator matrix for $\widetilde{\mathcal{D}}_l$ and $\widetilde{\mathcal{D}}_l^\perp$ have Euclidean weight a multiple of 8 from Exercise 733.
Suppose now that \(p \equiv 1 \pmod 8\). By Theorem 12.4.5(vi), $\mathcal{C}_l^{\perp\perp} = \mathcal{D}_2$ and $\mathcal{C}_2^{\perp\perp} = \mathcal{D}_1$. Hence as the extended coordinate of any vector in $\mathcal{C}_l$ is 0 by Exercise 733, the extended codewords arising from $\mathcal{C}_l$ are orthogonal to all codewords in either $\widetilde{\mathcal{D}}_l$ or $\widetilde{\mathcal{D}}_l^\perp$ where \(j \neq i\). Since the inner product of $\widetilde{j}(x)$ with $\widetilde{j}(x)$ is \(3 + p \equiv 0 \pmod 4\), $\widetilde{\mathcal{D}}_l^{\perp\perp} \subseteq \widetilde{\mathcal{D}}_l$ where \(j \neq i\). Part (ii) now follows from Theorem 12.4.5(iv).
