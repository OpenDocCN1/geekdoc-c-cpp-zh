**Lemma 6.2.5** Let \( p \) be an odd prime. Then 2 is a square modulo \( p \) if and only if \( p \equiv \pm 1 \pmod 8 \).

**Proof:** We prove only the case \( p = 8r + 1 \), where \( r \) is an integer, and leave the cases \( p = 8r - 1 \) and \( p = 8r \pm 3 \) as exercises. By Lemma 6.2.2, 2 is a square modulo \( p \). \(\square\)
