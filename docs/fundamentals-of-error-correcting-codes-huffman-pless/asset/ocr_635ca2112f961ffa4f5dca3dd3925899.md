and for this it suffices to show that \(\mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = 0\) for all \(\mathbf{b} \in C^\perp\). Let \(\mathbf{b} = (b_1, \dots, b_n)\) be a vector in \(C^\perp\). Then \(\beta \mathbf{b} \in C^\perp\) for all \(\beta \in \mathbb{F}_{q^r}\), and

\(0 = \mathbf{a} \cdot \text{Tr}_i(\beta \mathbf{b}) = \sum_{i=1}^n a_i \text{Tr}_i(\beta b_i) = \text{Tr}_i\left(\beta \sum_{i=1}^n a_i b_i\right) = \text{Tr}_i(\beta x),\)

where \(x = \mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b}\) by Lemma 3.8.5(ii) and (iii). If \(x \neq 0\), then we contradict (i) of Lemma 3.8.5. Hence \(x = 0\) and the theorem follows.

If \(C\) is a code of length \(n\) over \(\mathbb{F}_{q^r}\) which has a basis of vectors in \(\mathbb{F}_q^n\), the minimum weight vectors in \(C\) and \(C|_{\mathbb{F}_q}\) are the same up to scalar multiplication as we now see. In particular the minimum weight vectors in the two codes have the same set of supports, where the *support* of a vector \(\mathbf{c}\), denoted \(\text{supp}(\mathbf{c})\), is the set of coordinates where \(\mathbf{c}\) is nonzero.

# **Theorem 3.8.8** Let \(C\) be an \([n, k, d]\) code over \(\mathbb{F}_{q^r}\). Assume that \(C\) has a basis of codewords in \(\mathbb{F}_q^n\). Then every vector in \(C\) of weight \(d\) is a multiple of a vector of weight \(d\) in \(C|_{\mathbb{F}_q}\).

**Proof:** Let \(\{\mathbf{b}_1, \dots, \mathbf{b}_k\}\) be a basis of \(C\) with \(\mathbf{b}_i \in \mathbb{F}_q^n\) for \(1 \le i \le k\). Then \(\{\mathbf{b}_1, \dots, \mathbf{b}_k\}\) is also a basis of \(C|_{\mathbb{F}_q}\), by Theorem 3.8.4. Let \(\mathbf{c} = c_1 \cdots c_n = \sum_{i=1}^k \alpha_i \mathbf{b}_i\), with \(\alpha_i \in \mathbb{F}_{q^r}\). Thus \(\mathbf{c} \in C\), and by Lemma 3.8.5, \(\text{Tr}_i(\mathbf{c}) = \sum_{i=1}^k \text{Tr}_i(\alpha_i \mathbf{b}_i) \in C|_{\mathbb{F}_q} \subseteq C\). Also \(\text{wt}(\text{Tr}_i(\mathbf{c})) \le \text{wt}(\mathbf{c})\) as \(\text{Tr}_i(0) = 0\). Assume now that \(\mathbf{c}\) has weight \(d\). Then either \(\text{Tr}_i(\mathbf{c}) = \mathbf{0}\) or \(\text{Tr}_i(\mathbf{c})\) has weight \(d\) with \(\text{supp}(\mathbf{c}) = \text{supp}(\text{Tr}_i(\mathbf{c}))\). By replacing \(\mathbf{c}\) by \(\alpha \mathbf{c}\) for some \(\alpha \in \mathbb{F}_{q^r}\), we may assume that \(\text{Tr}_i(\mathbf{c}) \neq \mathbf{0}\) using Lemma 3.8.5(i). Choose \(i\) so that \(c_i \neq 0\). As \(\text{supp}(\mathbf{c}) = \text{supp}(\text{Tr}_i(\mathbf{c}))\), \(\text{Tr}_i(c_i) \neq 0\) and \(\text{wt}(c_i \text{Tr}_i(\mathbf{c}) - \text{Tr}_i(c_i) \mathbf{c}) < d\). Thus \(c_i \text{Tr}_i(\mathbf{c}) - \text{Tr}_i(c_i) \mathbf{c} = \mathbf{0}\) and \(\mathbf{c}\) is a multiple of \(\text{Tr}_i(\mathbf{c})\). ■
