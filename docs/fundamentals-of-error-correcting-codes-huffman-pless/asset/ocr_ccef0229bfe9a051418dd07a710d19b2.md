scalar multiples of the rows of $[I_6 \ S_5]$ and $[-S_5 \ I_6]$, all of which have weight 6. Hence $\mathcal{S}(5)$ has minimum weight 6 since its minimum weight is a multiple of 3. In a similar, but more complicated, fashion you can prove that the minimum weight of $\mathcal{S}(11)$ is 9; if it is not, then a minimum weight codeword must be a combination of at most three rows of $[I_{12} \ S_{11}]$ or $[S_{11} \ I_{12}]$. This requires knowing only the weights of combinations of at most three rows of $S_{11}$.
