**Proof.** By Theorem 8.6.4, $C_2(A)^\perp$ is the subcode of even weight vectors in $C_2(A)$. The result follows from Theorem 8.6.6.

We have settled the question of the dimension of $C_2(A)$ for a projective plane of order $\mu$ except when $4 \mid \mu$. Combinatorists have long been fascinated with the problem of finding values $\mu$ for which there is a projective plane of that order or proving that no such plane can exist. Since PG(2, $\mu$) is a projective plane of order $\mu$ when $\mu$ is a power of a prime, projective planes exist for all prime power orders. There are three projective planes of order 9 that are not equivalent to PG(2, 9) or each other; see [182]. However, at the present time, there is no known projective plane of any order other than a prime power. In fact it is conjectured that projective planes of nonprime power orders do not exist.
