Since \( e(x) = b^2(x) \), \( \mu(e(x)) = \mu(b^2(x)) \in \langle \mu(f(x)) \rangle \) in \( \mathcal{R}_n \). So \( e(x) = u(x)f(x) + 2v(x) \) in \( \mathbb{Z}_4[x] \). Squaring yields \( e^2(x) = u^2(x)f^2(x) \), implying that \( e^2(x) \in \langle f(x) \rangle \) in \( \mathfrak{R}_n \). As \( e(x) \) is an idempotent in \( \mathfrak{R}_n \), \( e(x) \in \langle f(x) \rangle \) or \( \langle e(x) \rangle \subseteq \langle f(x) \rangle \). Therefore \( \langle e(x) \rangle = \langle f(x) \rangle \) and \( e(x) = b^2(x) \) is the generating idempotent for \( \langle f(x) \rangle \).
