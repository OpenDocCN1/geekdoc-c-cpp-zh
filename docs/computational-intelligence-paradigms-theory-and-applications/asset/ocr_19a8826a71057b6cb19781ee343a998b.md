# 7.4.1 Mamdani's Fuzzy Inference Method

Among the above mentioned inference methods, the Mamdani model is the commonly used method due to its simple min-max structure. The model was proposed by Mamdani (1975) as an attempt to control a steam engine and boiler combination by synthesizing a set of linguistic control rules obtained from experienced human operators.
