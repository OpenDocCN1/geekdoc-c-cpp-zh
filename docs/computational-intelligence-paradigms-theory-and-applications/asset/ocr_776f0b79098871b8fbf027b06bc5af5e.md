# ## 10.2 Illustration 2: Fuzzy C-Means Clustering - Comparative Case Study

Two levels of fuzzy c-means clustering are illustrated in this program. FCM Clustering often works better than Otsu's method which outputs larger or smaller threshold on fluorescence images.

```
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
% Main Program
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
clear;close all;
im=imread('cell.png');
J = filter2(fspecial('sobel'), im);
fim=mat2gray(J);
level=graythresh(fim);
bwfm=im2bw(fim,level);
[bwfim0,level0]=fcmthresh(fim,0);
[bwfim1,level1]=fcmthresh(fim,1);
subplot(2,2,1);
imshow(fim);title('Original');
subplot(2,2,2);
imshow(bwfm);title(sprintf('Otsu,level=% f', ...
             level));
subplot(2,2,3);
imshow(bwfim0);title(sprintf('FCM0,level=% f', ...
             level0));
subplot(2,2,4);
imshow(bwfim1);title(sprintf('FCM1,level=% f', ...
             level1));
- - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - - -
```
