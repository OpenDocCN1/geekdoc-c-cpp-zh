# 6.11 Fuzzy Relations

Fuzzy relations are fuzzy subsets of X × Y, i.e., mapping from X ⟶ Y. Relations map elements of one universe, X to those of another universe, say Y, through the Cartesian product of the two universes. This relation is expressed as

R = { ((x,y), μ_R(x,y)) | (x,y) ∈ X × Y }
