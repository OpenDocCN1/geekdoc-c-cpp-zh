An “increasing generator” is a continuous and strictly increasing function from $[0,1]$ to $\mathbb{R}$ such that $g(0)=-\inf$. The pseudo-inverse of an increasing generator $g$, denoted $g^{-1}$, is a function from $\mathbb{R}$ to $[0,1]$ defined by

$g^{-1}(a)=\begin{cases}0, & a\in(-\inf,0)\\ g\sim(a), & a\in[0,g(1)]\; (g\sim\text{ is the inverse of }g)\\ 1, & a\in[g(1),\inf]\end{cases}$
