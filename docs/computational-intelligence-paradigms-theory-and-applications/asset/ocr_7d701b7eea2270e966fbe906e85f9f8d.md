**Min-Max Composition:** Let R̄₁ ⊆ X × Y and R̄₂ ⊆ Y × Z denote two fuzzy relations, the min-max composition on these relations is defined as R̄₁ ○ R̄₂ = max{min{μR₁(x, y), μR₂(y, z)}}ₓ∈X, y∈Y, z∈Z.

**Max Product Composition:** Let R̄₁ ⊆ X × Y and R̄₂ ⊆ Y × Z denote two fuzzy relations, the max product composition on these relations is defined as R̄₁R̄₂ = maxᵧ μR₁(x, y) × μR₂(y, z), x∈X, y∈Y, z∈Z.

Consider R̄₁ ⊆ X × Y and R̄₂ ⊆ Y × Z be defined as:

$$R̄₁ = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 0.4 & 0.5 \\ 0.3 & 0.7 & 0.7 \\ 0.2 & 0.6 & 0.9 \end{bmatrix}$$

$$R̄₂ = \begin{bmatrix} 0.1 & 0.4 \\ 0.9 & 0.4 \\ 0 & 0.8 \end{bmatrix}$$

The min-max composition can be computed as

$R̄₁ ○ R̄₂(x₁, z₁) = \max\{\min(1, 0.1), \min(0.4, 0.9), \min(0.5, 0)\}$  
$\qquad\qquad\quad = \max\{0.1, 0.4, 0\} = 0.4$

$R̄₁ ○ R̄₂(x₁, z₂) = \max\{\min(1, 0.5), \min(0.4, 0.4), \min(0.5, 0.8)\}$  
$\qquad\qquad\quad = \max\{0.5, 0.4, 0.5\} = 0.5$

$R̄₁ ○ R̄₂(x₂, z₁) = \max\{\min(0.3, 0.1), \min(0.7, 0.9), \min(0.7, 0)\}$  
$\qquad\qquad\quad = \max\{0.1, 0.7, 0\} = 0.1$

$R̄₁ ○ R̄₂(x₂, z₂) = \max\{\min(0.3, 0.5), \min(0.7, 0.4), \min(0.7, 0.8)\}$  
$\qquad\qquad\quad = \max\{0.1, 0.4, 0\} = 0.1$

$R̄₁ ○ R̄₂(x₃, z₁) = \max\{\min(0.2, 0.1), \min(0.6, 0.9), \min(0.9, 0)\}$  
$\qquad\qquad\quad = \max\{0.1, 0.6, 0\} = 0.6$

$R̄₁ ○ R̄₂(x₃, z₂) = \max\{\min(0.2, 0.5), \min(0.6, 0.4), \min(0.9, 0.8)\}$  
$\qquad\qquad\quad = \max\{0.2, 0.4, 0.8\} = 0.8$

Therefore the result obtained by min-max composition is

$$R̄₁ ○ R̄₂ = \begin{bmatrix} 0.4 & 0.5 \\ 0.1 & 0.1 \\ 0.6 & 0.8 \end{bmatrix}$$
