The operations of fuzzy relations are similar to those of fuzzy sets and are defined below.

Let R and S denote two fuzzy relations in the Cartesian space X × Y.

+   1. **Union operation:** The union of two fuzzy relations R and S in the Cartesian space X × Y is defined as $\mu_{R\cup S}(x,y)=\max\{\mu_R(x,y),\mu_S(x,y)\}$.

+   2. **Intersection operation:** The intersection of two fuzzy relations R and S in the Cartesian space X × Y is defined as $\mu_{R\cap S}(x,y)=\max\{\mu_R(x,y),\mu_S(x,y)\}$.

+   3. **Complement operation:** The complement operation of the fuzzy relation R is defined as $\mu_R(x,y)=1-\mu_R(x,y)$.
