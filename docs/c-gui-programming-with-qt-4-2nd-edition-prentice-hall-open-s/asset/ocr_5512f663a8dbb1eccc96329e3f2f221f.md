The first step is to create the child widgets and place them on the form. Create one label, one horizontal spacer, and two push buttons. For each item, drag its name or icon from Qt Designer's widget box and drop the item roughly where it sheould go on the form. The spacer item, which is invisible in the final form, is shown in Qt Designer as a blue spring.

Now drag the bottom of the form to make it shorter. This should produce a form that is similar to Figure 2.6. Don't spend too much time positioning the items on the form; Qt's layout managers will lay them out precisely later on.
