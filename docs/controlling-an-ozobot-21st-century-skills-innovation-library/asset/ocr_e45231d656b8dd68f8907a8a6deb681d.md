# # Starting with Ozobot

It’s a good idea to **calibrate** your Ozobot when you use it on a new kind of paper.

+   1. Draw a black circle on the paper you are going to use.
+   2. Hold down Ozobot’s power button. The lights will blink.
+   3. Put Ozobot on the circle.
+   4. Ozobot will drive to the edge of the circle. Its lights will flash green.
