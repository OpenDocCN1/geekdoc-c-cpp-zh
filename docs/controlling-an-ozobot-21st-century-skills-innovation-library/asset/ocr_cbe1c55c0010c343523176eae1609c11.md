# # Your Neighborhood

Show Ozobot around your neighborhood! Use black, red, blue, and green markers to draw a map of your neighborhood on white paper. Make sure the map has a path for Ozobot to follow. You can make your path more complicated by adding forks and turns.

# ## What Is in Your Neighborhood?

Mark important locations on your neighborhood map. Here are some places you might include:

+   - Your house
+   - Your school
+   - Friends’ houses
+   - Parks and playgrounds
+   - Stores you visit
+   - The library
