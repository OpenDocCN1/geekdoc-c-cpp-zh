# GETTING STARTED

Combining photography, art, and typography are a big part of what a graphic designer does. Before working on print or video games you can practice graphic design by working on small web pages and perhaps building a personal blog. Making choices about layout, color, and what elements go where will go a long way to helping you as your skills advance.
