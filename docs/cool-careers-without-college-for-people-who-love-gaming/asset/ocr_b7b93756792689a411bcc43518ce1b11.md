# # COMPANY BRANDING

One job that graphic designers do is to create company logos that communicate an idea in a simple, strong, and graphic way. A company logo helps brand a company and make people remember it when they shop for products. Some of the original video game companies such as Atari and Activision still use logos similar to their original, so consumers can quickly recognize and identify them.

One example of a simple graphic gaming company logo today is Rockstar, which simply shows an “R” and a star. Another is LucasArts, which shows a person with a head as the iris of an eye. When consumers see these logos they are visually reminded of the company’s reputation and previous products.
