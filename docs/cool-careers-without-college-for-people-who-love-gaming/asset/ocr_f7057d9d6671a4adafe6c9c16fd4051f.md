# BOOKS

Brathwaite, Brenda, and Ian Schreiber. *Challenges for Game Designers*. Newton Centre, MA: Charles River Media, 2008.

Brathwaite and Schreiber present exercises that readers can follow to create their own game designs.

Rogers, Scott. *Level Up! The Guide to Great Video Game Design*. New York, NY: Wiley, 2014.

Rogers describes video game design, how to market your video game ideas, and how to create games for both mobile and console systems.

Schell, Jesse. *The Art of Game Design: A Book of Lenses*. Oxfordshire, England: AK Peters/CRC Press, 2014.

Schell, a top game designer, explains game design through the many “lenses” of people who view it from outside the field.
