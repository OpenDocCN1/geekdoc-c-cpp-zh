# ## VIDEO GAME WRITING

Game writers have a complex job. They should have incredibly active imaginations to help conceptualize original game stories and adventures. No idea for a game should be ignored. Keep a journal of any idea that comes into your head. Some of the tasks done by a person who writes video game or board game stories include:

+   - Research the competition to know what other games exist in the field.
+   - Communicate with game designers and programmers to make sure the story concepts are able to be realistically carried out.
