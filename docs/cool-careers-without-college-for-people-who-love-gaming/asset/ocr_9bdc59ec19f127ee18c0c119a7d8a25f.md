# ## GOING IT ALONE

A great way to stay involved in the gaming industry is to attend the annual Game Developers Conference (GDC) and the Independent Games Summit that is held there. This is the biggest annual gathering for gaming professionals. Attending the GDC is a great way to see what is being created, as well as a great opportunity to make connections in the gaming industry.

Another great resource for indie game developers is Steam, a website where many games, small and large, can get distributed for sale. While you may not get the in-store visibility that larger gaming companies have for their games, Steam has had sixty-five million users worldwide since 2014. Games on the website have a good chance of being seen and played.
