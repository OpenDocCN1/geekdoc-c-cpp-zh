# # WRITING THE FUTURE OF GAMES

A good foundation of any narrative is a well-written story. Having a good story for your game will make it more compelling for the player. Writing careers in the gaming industry can be as varied as the many games and platforms that exist. Writing games is just one job that a writer can have in the gaming field. A good writer can get involved in journalism and blogging related to the gaming field. Writers are also needed for game guides, game instructions, and “art of” books, as well as any text that accompanies any book produced for the gaming industry.
