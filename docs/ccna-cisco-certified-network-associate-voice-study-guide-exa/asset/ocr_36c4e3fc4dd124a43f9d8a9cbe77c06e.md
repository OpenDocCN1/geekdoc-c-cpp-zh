# VoIP Defined

Voice over IP (VoIP), also called IP telephony, uses IP data networks to transfer audio- or sound-based communications. While traditional voice networks convert voice to electrical signals and then pass these signals along the wires, VoIP networks convert voice to data first. The data is still converted to electrical signals that are passed on either wired or wireless networks, but the added step of converting the voice to data (specifically, IP packets) is the new factor introduced by VoIP networks.
