# on the job

> Many would suggest that switches are not a part of the infrastructure. In the Cisco world, a switch is usually considered part of the infrastructure if endpoints do not connect with it directly. The switches that connect endpoints are at the access layer or at the edge of the network, much like a merge lane on a highway.
