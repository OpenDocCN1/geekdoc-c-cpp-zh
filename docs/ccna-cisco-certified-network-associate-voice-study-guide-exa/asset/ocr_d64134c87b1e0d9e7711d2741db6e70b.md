+   - The G.711 codec has an MOS of 4.1 or higher.
+   - The G.729 codec has an MOS of 3.92.

# 642-436: Choose the Appropriate Codec for a Given Situation

+   - The available network bandwidth must be considered when choosing a codec.
+   - The network latency must be considered when choosing a codec.
+   - You must ensure that all devices support the chosen codec; otherwise, you will be required to implement gateways for conversion between the differing devices.
