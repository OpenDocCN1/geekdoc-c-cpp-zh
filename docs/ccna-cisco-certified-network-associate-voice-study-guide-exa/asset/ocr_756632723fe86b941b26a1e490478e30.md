We'll use e-mail as a simple demonstration of the OSI model in this chapter. At this point, you may not be fully aware of VoIP technologies, but you should understand basic e-mail operations from your CCNA and other networking studies and work. Imagine that you are sending an e-mail using the Simple Mail Transport Protocol (SMTP), which is the most popular method of sending an e-mail message. Your e-mail application will connect to an SMTP server to send the e-mail message. Interestingly, from the e-mail application's perspective, it is connecting directly to the SMTP server and is completely unaware of all the other layers of operation that allow this connection to occur.
