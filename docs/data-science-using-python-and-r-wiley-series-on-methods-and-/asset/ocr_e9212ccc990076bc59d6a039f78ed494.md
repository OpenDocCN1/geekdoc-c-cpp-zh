+   Much can be learned from a contingency table. The baseline proportion of bad risk is 2/10 = 20%. However, the proportion of bad risk for applicants without a mortgage is 1/3 = 33%, which is higher than the baseline; and the proportion of bad risk for applicants with a mortgage is only 1/7 = 1%, which is lower than the baseline risk. Thus, whether or not the applicant has a mortgage is useful for predicting risk.
