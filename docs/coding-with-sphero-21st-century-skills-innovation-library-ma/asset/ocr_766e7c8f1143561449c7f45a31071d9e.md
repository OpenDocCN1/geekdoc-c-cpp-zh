# Glossary

**gyroscope** (JYE-ruh-skope) a device inside of Sphero and other objects that measures movement and direction

**LED** (EL EE DEE) a small light that uses very little energy and gives off very little heat; LED stands for “light-emitting diode”

**maker** (MAY-kur) a creative person who makes everything from artwork and useful objects to robots and computer programs

**pairing** (PARE-ing) connecting two devices using a wireless communication method such as Bluetooth

**sensors** (SEN-surz) instruments that can detect and measure changes and transmit the information to a controlling device
