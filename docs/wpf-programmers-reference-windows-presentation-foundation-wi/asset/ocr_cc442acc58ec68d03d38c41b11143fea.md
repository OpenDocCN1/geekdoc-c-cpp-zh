# TextureCoordinates

The TextureCoordinates property is a collection that defines how points are mapped to positions on a material's surface. You specify a point's position on the surface by giving the coordinates of the point on the brush used by the material to draw the surface.(The section “Materials” later in this chapter says more about materials.)

![](bbox=[0.513, 0.291, 0.891, 0.477])

The coordinates on brush begin with (0, 0) in the upper left with the first coordinate extending to the right and the second extending downwards. This is similar to the way you use X and Y coordinates in a bitmap (although to avoid confusion with 3D space, many graphics programmers call the axes U and V rather than X and Y).
