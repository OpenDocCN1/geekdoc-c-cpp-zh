## Templates

Properties and styles determine a control's. For example, a Slider's TickFrequency, TickPlacement, Background, and Width properties help determine its appearance, while Minimum, Maximum, LargeChange, and IsEnabled properties help determine its behavior.

In contrast, templates determine its structure. They determine what components make up the control and how these components interact to provide the control's features.

This chapter describes templates in general terms and shows how you can build templates of your own to change the way existing controls work.
