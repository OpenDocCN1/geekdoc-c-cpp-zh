# CHAPTER 5 CONTENT CONTROLS

In Scroll, the control works like a FlowDocumentScrollViewer. See the following section for information about that control.

In TwoPage, the control displays the two pages at a time side-by-side. As is the case with the FlowDocumentPageViewer, the size of a page is determined by the document’s PageHeight and PageWidth properties and the available space in the viewer. By default, the viewer divides its area in two, scales to the fit of the two pages, and displays as much of the document as will fit at each page.

Figure 5-6 shows a FlowDocumentReader displaying a FlowDocument in TwoPage. The arrows in the viewer’s center let you between pages. The icons to the right let you pick between Page, TwoPage, and Scroll. The plus and minus buttons on bottom right let you zoom in and out.

# FlowDocumentScrollViewer

The FlowDocumentScrollViewer displays a FlowDocument as a tall, continuously scrolling document much as a web browser displays the contents on a long page.
