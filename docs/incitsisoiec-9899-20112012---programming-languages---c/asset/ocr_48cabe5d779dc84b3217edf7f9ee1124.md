The conversion speculator includes all subsequent wide characters in the format string, up to and including the matching right bracket (). The wide characters between the brackets (the scanlist) compose the scanset, unless the wide character after the left bracket is a circumflex (^), in which case the scanset contains all wide characters that do not in the scanlist between the circumflex and the right bracket. If the conversion begins with [] or [^], the right wide character is the matching right bracket that ends the specification.
