To get a clear understanding of the concept of ‘associativity’, let us change the expression for evaluating the value of variable s as shown below:

s=p-q+r;

By applying substitution we have,

s=10-5+3

Again, we know that the operator - and + are of same priority. Hence, C/C++ determines order of evaluation using the associativity of arithmetic operators which is ‘Left to Right (L→R)’. Therefore, we will evaluate the complete expression in a direction from Left to Right, such that, the subtraction of 10 and 5 will be performed before evaluation of the result of addition. Hence, the final result of the expression will be evaluated as 8 as shown in Figure 3.4.
