This too is part of the overarching strategy to sell the console to what Nintendo constructs as "moms"—obviously a domesticated gender-specific idea of how to gain entrée to the vast untapped market of those who do not own a console of any kind and do not think of themselves as gamers but might be induced to purchase this one. The stated goal of Nintendo designers is for the slim and efficient console to become an unobtrusive fixture in the idealized family living room.
