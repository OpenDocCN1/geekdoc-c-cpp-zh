Substituting into the B-L formula:

E(r - r_f) = [(rΣ)^{-1} + P^T Ω^{-1} P]^{-1} [(rΣ)^{-1} Π + P^T Ω^{-1} Q]
= [0.02015^{-1} + 0.005^{-1} × 0.02]^{-1} × [0.02015^{-1} × 0.0 × [0.3674 + 4]/0.074 + 0.005^{-1} × 0.02]
= [49.63 + 200]^{-1} × [49.63 × 0.0074 + 200 × 0.02]
= [249.64]^{-1} × [0.3673 + 4]
= 4.3673/249.63
= 0.017495
= 1.749% per month
