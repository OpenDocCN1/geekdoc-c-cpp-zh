Braga and Natale and Tracking Error Volatility (TEV) Braga and Natale (2007) proposed the use of tracking error between the posterior and prior portfolios as a measure of distance from the prior. Tracking error is commonly used by investors to measure risk versus a benchmark, and may be used as an investment constraint. Since it is commonly used, most investors have an intuitive understanding and a level of comfort with TEV.

Tracking Error Volatility (TEV) is here defined as

TEV = √[(w_actv^T Σ w_actv)], where w_actv = w̅ - w_r,  (3.173)

and where

+   w_actv is the active weights or active portfolio,
+   w̅ is the weight in the investor's portfolio,
+   w_r is the weight in the reference portfolio, and
+   Σ is the covariance matrix of returns.
