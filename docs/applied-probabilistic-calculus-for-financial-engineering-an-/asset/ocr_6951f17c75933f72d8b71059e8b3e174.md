This efficient half-line is called the Capital Allocation Line (CAL), and its equation may be shown to be

$E(R_C) = R_F + (\sigma_C[(E(R_P) - R_F)/\sigma_P])$ (3.11)

In Equation (3.11),

P is the subportfolio of risky assets at the tangency with the Markowitz bullet.

F is the risk-free asset.

C is the combination of portfolios P and F.
