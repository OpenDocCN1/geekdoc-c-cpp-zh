# ## Papers

The AI scholar is a scientist — standing on the shoulders of giants, yet often isolated by the sheer volume of literature. This page collects papers that have shaped our understanding — and a few that challenge it.

# ### Foundations

+   - Attention Is All You Need (2017) — Vaswani et al. Introduces the Transformer architecture, the backbone of modern LLMs.
+   - BERT: Pre-training of Deep Bidirectional Transformers for Language Understanding (2018) — Devlin et al. Establishes bidirectional pre-training for NLP.
+   - GPT-2: Language Models are Unsupervised Multitask Learners (2018) — Radford et al. Demonstrates zero-shot task transfer at scale.

# ### Recent Highlights

+   - Chain-of-Thought Prompting Elicits Reasoning in Large Language Models (2022) — Wei et al. Shows intermediate reasoning steps improve performance.
+   - Tree of Thoughts: Deliberate Problem Solving with Large Language Models (2023) — Yao et al. Extends CoT with search over multiple reasoning paths.
+   - LIMA: Less Is More for Alignment (2023) — Zhou et al. Suggests that high-quality data, not scale, drives alignment.
