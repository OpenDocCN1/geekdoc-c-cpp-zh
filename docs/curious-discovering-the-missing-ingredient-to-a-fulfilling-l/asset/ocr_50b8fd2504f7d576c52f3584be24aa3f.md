* Statements that are worded in a negative way must be reverse-scored, so figuring out your score for this tool requires several steps:

STEP 1: Reverse-score statements 3, 4, 5, 7, 11, 15, 16, 18, 19, and 20. That means if you circled a 1 for any of those statements, change it to a 7. Change a 2 to a 6, a 3 to a 5, a 5 to a 3, a 6 to a 2, and a 7 to a 1. If you circled a 4, keep it as a 4.

STEP 2: Add the scores for statements 1, 4, 8, 11, 14, 17, and 20, and then divide the total by 7. That is your average “Autonomy” score. My Autonomy score is ___.

STEP 3: Add the scores for statements 3, 5, 10, 13, 15, and 19, and then divide by 6. That is your average “Competence” score. My Competence score is ___.

STEP 4: Add the scores for statements 2, 6, 7, 9, 12, 16, 18, and 21, and then divide by 8. That is your average “Relatedness” score. My Relatedness score is ___.
