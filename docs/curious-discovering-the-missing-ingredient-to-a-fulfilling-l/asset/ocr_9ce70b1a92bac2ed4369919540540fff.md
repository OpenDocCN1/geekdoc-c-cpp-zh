# Acceptance and Action Questionnaire-II (AAQ-2)

The AAQ–2 measures psychological flexibility or the process of being alive in the present moment, working with any unpleasant thoughts, feelings, and bodily sensations (instead of avoiding them), and moving in the direction of important values. To see where you score, circle the number next to each statement that best corresponds to how true you believe the statement is for you.
