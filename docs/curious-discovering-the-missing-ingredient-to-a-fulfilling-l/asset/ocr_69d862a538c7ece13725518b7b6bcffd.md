# OLYMPIC ATHLETES: Shepard, J. A. & McNulty, J. K. (2002). The affective consequences of expected and unexpected outcomes. Psychological Science, 13, 85-88.

In a study of Olympic athletes, those entering the competition expecting to win were happier winning a bronze medal than a silver medal. Why? Because the bronze medalists were comparing their win to the possibility of falling out of the medal ranks, whereas silver medalists were comparing themselves to the gold medal they “should” have won.
