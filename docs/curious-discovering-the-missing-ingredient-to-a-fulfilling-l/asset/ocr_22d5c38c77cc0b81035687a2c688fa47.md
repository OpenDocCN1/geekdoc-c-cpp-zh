# # Measurement Tools

In many of the studies that provided the scientific foundation for this book, psychologists often ask people to answer questionnaires about certain topics. The answers that people give, when they are being honest, often provide tremendous insight into what that person thinks, feels, and does.

The measurement tools that psychologists use are not something that someone just made up and printed. Instead, they have been given to hundreds, or even thousands, of people in a process called “validation.” Validation allows the researcher who developed the tool to know that it measures what it is supposed to measure and not something else. The process of validation occurs before the tool is used outside of a research laboratory.

To help you understand yourself better, I received permission from several well-known and highly regarded researchers to reprint their measurement tools, as well as reprinting one of my own. So work your way through them, and enjoy the process of getting to know yourself better. It is important to highlight that there are no right and wrong answers, and you are not competing with anyone. The more honest you are in responding, the more you gain in understanding yourself.
