# Making Sense of Your Curiosity and Well-Being Profile Scores

Now you have determined the degree to which you are a curious explorer (with the Curiosity and Exploration Inventory-II), your basic psychological needs are satisfied (with the Basic Psychological Needs Scale), you work with instead of avoid negative feelings such as anxiety and show signs of psychological flexibility (with the Acceptance and Action Questionnaire-II), and your life is meaningful (with the Meaning in Life Questionnaire).
