# Which direction?

The cat is marching left and right across the screen. It’s possible to change the cat’s direction, so it walks up and down, or even diagonally. The “Motion” blocks can be used to make a game of cat and mouse.

The direction -90° means "left"

![](bbox=[0.673, 0.068, 0.943, 0.243])

Click the block to make the cat change direction

Select or type in a new number to change the direction of the cat

# Heading the right way

Drag the "point in direction" block into the scripts area and opens its drop-down menu. There are four directions to choose from. Or, click the number in the window and type in a new direction.

+   point in direction 45
(90) right
(-90) left
(0) up
(180) down

Directions are measured in degrees, from 0° at the top. You can use any number between -179 and +180.

The cat will follow the mouse-pointer

# Cat and mouse

Remove the "move 10 steps" and "if on edge, bounce" blocks from the script. Now drag a "point" block into the "forever" block. Open the menu and choose "mouse-pointer".

The drop-down menu gives you four options

# Chase the mouse

Can the cat catch the mouse? Drag a "move 10 steps" block into the "forever" loop. Now the cat walks towards the mouse-pointer.

+   when clicked
forever
point towards mouse-pointer
move 10 steps

Sprites are objects in a Scratch program that you can move around (see pp.28–29). Every new project stars the cat sprite, but you can add cats, dinosaurs, dancers, and all sorts of sprites from the library. You can even go go at designing your own.
