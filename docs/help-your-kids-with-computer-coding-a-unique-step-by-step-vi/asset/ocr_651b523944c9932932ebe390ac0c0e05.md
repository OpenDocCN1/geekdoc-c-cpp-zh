# PROJECT 2

# Roll the dice

Simple programs can be both useful and fun. This program creates a dice that can be rolled. Play it to see who can get the highest number, or use it instead of a real dice when you play a board game.

# How to create a rolling dice

The dice in this program uses six costumes. Each costume shows a face of the dice with a different number on it—from one to six.

+   Select the paintbrush button under the stage to draw a new sprite.

![](bbox=[0.115, 0.521, 0.403, 0.613])

+   Click the rectangle button on the left of the painting area. To make your dice colorful, select a solid color from the palette (see box below). Then in the painting area hold the "shift" key, press the mouse button, and drag the mouse-pointer to make a square in the middle.

+   The rectangle button makes a square when the "shift" key is pressed

> EXPERT TIPS

# Changing colors

Under the painting area are the color controls. Click the rectangle to draw a block of solid color. Use the empty rectangle to draw an outline of a square or rectangle. Use the slider to change the thickness of the square's lines. To choose a color, simply click it.
