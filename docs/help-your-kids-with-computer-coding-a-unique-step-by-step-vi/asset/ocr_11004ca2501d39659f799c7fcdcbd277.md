# Supercomputers

Supercomputers—used by high-tech organizations such as NASA—combine the power of thousands of computer processors that share data and communicate quickly. The result is a computer that can perform millions of calculations per second.

# REAL WORLD Weather forecast

Weather patterns are unpredictable. Supercomputers crunch the huge amounts of data needed to accurately predict what will happen. Each processor in the supercomputer calculates the weather for a small part of the map. All the results are combined to produce the whole forecast.
