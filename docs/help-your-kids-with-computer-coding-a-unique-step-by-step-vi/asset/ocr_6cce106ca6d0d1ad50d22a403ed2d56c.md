# Making apps

Mobile phones have opened up a world of possibilities for coders. With a computer in everyone’s pocket, mobile apps can use new inputs, such as location-finding and motion-sensing, to give users a better experience.

# What is an app?

“App” (short for “application”) is a word that describes programs that run on mobile devices, smartphones, tablets, and even wearable technology such as washes. There are many different categories of apps that do different things.

People use apps to track their fitness when running or cycling, and can also keep up to date on the latest sports scores while on the go.

Educational apps are great for learning. Young children can learn to count and spell, and older people can learn a new language.
