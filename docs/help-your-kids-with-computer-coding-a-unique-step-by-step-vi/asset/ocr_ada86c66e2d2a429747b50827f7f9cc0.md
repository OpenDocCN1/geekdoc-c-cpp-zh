# Nested loops

Loops can also be “nested,” which means they can be inside each other. In this script, the dinosaur finishes his dance by walking right and left and then thinking for a moment. When he’s got his breath back, he dances again and stops only when you click the red stop button.

> Try giving me some looping music!

Loops in loops

This “forever” loop has several repeat loops inside it. Make sure the blocks are inside the right loops, otherwise the program won’t work properly.
