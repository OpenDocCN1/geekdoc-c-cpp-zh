# Things to try

Not sure what to do next with Scratch? Here are a few ideas. If you don't feel ready to write a whole program on your own yet, you can start with one that has already been written and change parts of it.

+   - Join a coding club: Is there a coding club in your school or local area? They're great places to meet other Scratch users and share ideas.

# Backpack

The backpack enables you to store useful scripts, sprites, sounds, and costumes and move them from project to project. It's found at the bottom of the Scratch screen.

+   - Drag and drop: You can drag sprites and scripts into your backpack, then add them to other projects.

Drag and drop to copy a script or sprite into the backpack
