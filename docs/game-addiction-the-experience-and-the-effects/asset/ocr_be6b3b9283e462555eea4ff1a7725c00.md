The power of any story has been its ability to show us how we might make a better reality. Fantasy helps us to see the world through reinvigorated eyes; imagination takes us beyond the status quo.³³ If playing in a game helps us to improve our real lives, then that’s all we can ask for. What media isn’t supposed to do is replace a life. The line between reality and illusion is complicated and subtle, but it’s one that many more of us will be walking soon. Understanding this line begins with what is arguably the most important puzzle piece: how our physical bodies see and hear what’s happening in the digital living room.
