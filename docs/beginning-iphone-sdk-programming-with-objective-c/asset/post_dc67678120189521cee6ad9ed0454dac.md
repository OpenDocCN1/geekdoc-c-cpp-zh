## ABOUT THE AUTHOR

**WEI-MENG LEE** is a technologist and founder of Developer Learning Solutions (www.learn2develop.net), a technology company specializing in hands-on training for the latest Microsoft and Mac OS X technologies. He is also an established author with Wrox and O'Reilly.

Wei-Meng first started the iPhone programming course in Singapore and it has since received much positive feedback. His hands-on approach to iPhone programming makes understanding the subject much easier than reading books, tutorials, and documentation from Apple.

Contact Wei-Meng at weimenglee@learn2develop.net.
