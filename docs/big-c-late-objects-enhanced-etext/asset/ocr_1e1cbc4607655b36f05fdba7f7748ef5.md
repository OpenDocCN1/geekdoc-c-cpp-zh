# Findings at a glance

> "Good health is the best asset. Without it, everything else falls apart."

In the aftermath of the COVID-19 pandemic, health has emerged as a top priority for people around the world. Nearly **7 in 10** global respondents rank health as their top concern, ahead of financial well-being and job security. Across geographies, this concern is universal, driven by rising costs and access to care.

![](bbox=[0.045, 0.113, 0.305, 0.411])

With this broad context in mind, this report explores how people from 10 countries think about health today. Specifically, it looks at:

+   1. Health system confidence
+   2. Health information and data sources
+   3. The health gap: challenges of today and tomorrow

Respondents were aged 18+ across 10 countries: Australia, Brazil, France, Germany, Japan, Saudi Arabia, South Africa, the UAE, the UK and the US. Quotas were set to be representative of each country's age, gender and ethnicity profile. Where data is weighted, the base population is the general public (18+).
