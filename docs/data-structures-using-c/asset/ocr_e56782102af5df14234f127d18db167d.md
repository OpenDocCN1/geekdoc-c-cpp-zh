Note that KMP finds every occurrence of a P in text T, and hence, KMP does not terminate; rather, it searches the remaining part of T for any more occurrences of P.

Let us consider text T and pattern P as follows:

Text T =

| b | a | c | b | a | b | a | b | a | b | a | c | a | a |

Pattern P =

| a | b | a | b | a | c | a |
