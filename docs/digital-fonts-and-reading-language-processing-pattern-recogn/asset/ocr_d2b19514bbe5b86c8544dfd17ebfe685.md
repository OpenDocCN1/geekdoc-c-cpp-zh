# 1.2.2.3 FangSong (仿宋)

FangSong is also called Imitation Song, which suggests that the former shares several main characteristics with the latter, particularly in terms of geometrical regularity in structure. However, FangSong, as shown in Figure 6 and Figure 7, has several distinctive differences from the Song style. The strokes in FangSong are relatively thin, whereas the stroke width variation between horizontal and vertical strokes is very low. Moreover, major horizontal strokes in a character are not absolutely horizontal but rather slant up slightly from left to right. Finally, the beginnings of strokes have slant serifs, and the intersections have sharp corners.

FangSong was created for book publishing and is currently being used for the typesetting of formal documents.
