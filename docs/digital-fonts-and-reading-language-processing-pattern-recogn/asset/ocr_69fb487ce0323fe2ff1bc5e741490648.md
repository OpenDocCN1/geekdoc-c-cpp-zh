# ### 1.3.3 Combining Experiments 1 and 2

Analyzing both experiments together, bilinguals (Hong Kong and UK participants) were more accurate than UK monolinguals, (F(1,56)=27.87, p<0.0001, η²=.33) and Chinese characters were less accurately discriminated (F(1,56)=45.99, p<0.0001, η²=.45). As expected, these results were primarily due to less accurate performance by monolinguals with Chinese characters, resulting in a significant interaction between writing system and reading expertise (F(1,56)=38.71, p<0.0001, η²=.41).

An unanticipated outcome, illustrated in Figure 10, was a significant interaction between writing system and typeface expertise (F(1,56)=5.70, p=0.025, η²=.09). There is a greater difference between the writing systems in non-designers.
