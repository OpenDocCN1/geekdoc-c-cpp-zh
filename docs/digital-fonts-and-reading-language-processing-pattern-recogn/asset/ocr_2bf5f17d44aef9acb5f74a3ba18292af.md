# 1.4.3.2 Design and procedure

General instructions were given at the beginning so that the participants had a trial sample to absorb the idea, then three samples were displayed sequentially in the fixation zone they were asked to focus on. Once the sample is triggered, it lasts for 1000 millisecond which is the same period used in the previous experiment. As done before, a mask of black dots is displayed after for the same duration to control the after-image impact. Next, participants were asked to fill six text boxes using a virtual touch keyboard with words they remember. Although abilities of short-term memory in participants may differ, it has been found that the average number of items people may remember after a short appearance is four [Machizawa, Goh and Driver, 2012]. By this way, we ensure that the most recognizable font among six words will catch the participant's attention first. So, there is no problem at all if all participants could not remember all the words. Although spelling mistakes could lead to incorrect results, the selected words had been chosen to be easy to spell and commonly used. Therefore, the possibility of a typo was expected to be low.
