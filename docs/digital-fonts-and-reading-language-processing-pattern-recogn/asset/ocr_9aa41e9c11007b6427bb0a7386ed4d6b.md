In the future, we can enhance the study to examine other types of tablets and smart phones. Moreover, we can improve the functionality of M-Short-Exposure method by: reducing the allowed directions in isolated letters matrix to one direction only (right-to-left), and by fixing the level of word complexity in connected letters, in part to keep the focus on fonts’ performance.
