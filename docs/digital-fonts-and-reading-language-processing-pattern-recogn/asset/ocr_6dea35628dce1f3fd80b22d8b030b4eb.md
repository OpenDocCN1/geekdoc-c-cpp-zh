# 1.4.1 Type size

Amongst the partially sighted, Times New Roman in its larger size (23 pts), was the typeface with the most read words in the entire experiment, having a difference of 126 words from the same typeface in its smaller size (14 pts). Participants with normal sight and the ones using the simulating spectacles performed better with larger typefaces. However, the difference between the two extreme reading sizes in both Arial (12 and 20 pts) and Times New Roman (14 and 23 pts) was less pronounced than for the partially sighted and differed only by a few words. Consequently, size enhances the reading rate of the visually impaired, as opposed to normal-sighted people.
