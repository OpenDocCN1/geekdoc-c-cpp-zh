# 1.3.2 Selection of participants

Thirty-three adults took part in this experiment. Twenty-one were partially sighted people aged from 70 to 94 years old. Of these, ten had macular degeneration and eleven had various eye deficiencies. The rest were divided into two categories: six with perfect vision and six with pronounced myopia. They constituted an element of comparison with the results of the partially sighted. Participants with normal visual acuity were tasked to simply read the paragraph.
