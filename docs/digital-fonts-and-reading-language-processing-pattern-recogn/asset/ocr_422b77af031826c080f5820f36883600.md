Although the type designer will never solve these problems, it is very important to understand the reasons behind them and to mitigate them to the extent that it’s possible.

All of us who took part in this project felt that Sika as it was completed and released was a significant improvement in legibility. We also felt that the design improved dramatically over the course of the development effort. Tantalizingly, we did not demonstrate statistically reliable reading speed benefits as a result of the improvements. We learned a great deal from this project, but it must be said that there is much about the legibility of type and its measurement that is not yet fully understood.
