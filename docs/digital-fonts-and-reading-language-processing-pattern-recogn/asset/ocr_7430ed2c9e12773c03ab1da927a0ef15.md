These choices suggest a search for appropriateness and authenticity within the cultural framework of each respective language, rather than a uniform, regularised aesthetic for all. Thus, the type used for the French translation makes no attempt to imitate the Arabic, and vice-versa, there is no apparent dilution of the characteristics of the Arabic script to conform to those of Latin. Indeed, as the reading of each text category is bound to differ, the question as to why they should be stylistically regularised begs to be asked.
