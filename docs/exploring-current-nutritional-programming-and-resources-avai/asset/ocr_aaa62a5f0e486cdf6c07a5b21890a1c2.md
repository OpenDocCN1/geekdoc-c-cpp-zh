This method relies on individuals considered to have knowledge on the topic (ie, people with lived experience). The expert group aims to reach a consensus on a complex and multifaceted topic. In this case, the DM provided a framework to guide consultation and subsequent decision-making (eg, how the review will inform subsequent surveying). Contributors to the consultation had the opportunity to highlight additional references and provide valuable insights about problems and ideas that the scoping review alone had not identified. A DM flow chart is provided in Appendix II, adapted from Green.

A list of 13 experts was generated from existing FoodNOW steering and advisory committees, as well as colleagues of the research team from relevant fields (ie, nutrition, health care providers), who were then invited to participate in the DM process. Eight experts agreed to participate as panelists, and the DM was employed by a trained facilitator to a group that included registered dietitians (n = 2), service providers (n = 4), a medical advisor (n = 1), and a dietitian candidate (n = 1). Panelists were invited to share feedback in the form of typed responses collected via email or prerecorded video. The scoping review manuscript was provided 2 weeks in advance of the first feedback submission deadline (round 1). The panelists were asked to offer feedback on the practical application of the findings and to identify any reservations about materials included and/or excluded. Informed by Hemmings et al. and Williams and Webb, the facilitator collated the feedback into an anonymous summary report and circulated it back to the DM panelists for review 1 week in advance of the round 2 feedback submission deadline. The facilitator then integrated all final additional feedback into the review.

# Data analysis and presentation

The results of this review are presented in tables and figures, and accompanied by a narrative summary that describes how the results relate to the review objective and question. The focus of this review was the broad concept of nutrition and food programming available to PLWH, and as such, we reported a broad range of programming types.

# Results

# Study inclusion

A search of the peer-reviewed literature conducted on July 14, 2021, yielded 696 results from 5 databases. Gray literature searches conducted on August 24, October 5, and October 15, 2021 (see Appendix I), yielded another 143 results (see Appendix IV). A total of 839 results were recorded. For purposes of this study, identified articles (peer-reviewed) and evidence (gray literature) are referred to as results, and individual programming and resources available to PLWH or AIDS are referred to as programming and resources.

After duplicates were removed from the published literature, 439 results remained for title and abstract screening. Of these, 366 results did not match the research inclusion criteria, and 2 results were unable to be retrieved, leaving 213 results for full-text-review (71 published and 143 gray literature results). After full-text screening, 64 results were included in the review (17 published and 47 gray literature results). See Figure 1 for the complete search results and study selection and inclusion process. The 6 reasons for exclusion after full-text screening were i) not nutrition and food programming (n = 83), ii) not Canadian context (n = 37), iii) duplicates (n = 22), iv) not focused on PLWH (n = 6), v) conference abstract (n = 1), and vi) not in English (n = 1). See Appendix II for the list of excluded results. Duplicates identified among the published literature during the full-text review were due to multiple records published on the same resource/service organization. As the objective of the review was to identify the number of resources available in Canada, only 1 record per resource/service organization was eligible to be included in the scoping review.

# Characteristics of included studies

Details of the 64 results included in the scoping review are provided in Appendix IV. Study characteristics were recorded as they relate to the research objectives and include full citations. The included results were published between 1993 and 2021. Four results had no date available.
