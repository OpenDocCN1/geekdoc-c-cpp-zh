Dissemination of the review results have been and will continue to be formative (eg, community meetings, consultation) and summative. Results were used to build the subsequent 3 FoodNOW project phases, which have been disseminated via traditional academic routes, including conferences, manuscripts, and reports. Innovative approaches, such as infographics and short videos, depending on audience (eg, funders, academics, government, PLWH, service providers), may also be employed. For instance, we have presented preliminary findings in the context of integrated knowledge translation (iKT) through a poster presentation at the KT Canada Scientific Meeting in spring 2022.⁵³ We have also presented a needs assessment summary of the findings in the form of a poster and oral presentation at the KT Scientific Meeting in spring 2023.⁵⁴ Sharing the learnings from this project and plans on how the data will be used/implemented with PLWH and other community members remains one of the key priorities towards our iKT objectives.
