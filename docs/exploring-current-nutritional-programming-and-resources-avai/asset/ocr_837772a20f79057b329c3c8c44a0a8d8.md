# **Population**

Most results for available nutrition and food programming targeted the general population of PLWH in Canada. Of the 64 results included in the review, some offered specific programming for more than 1 population, for a total of 69 targeted programming resources. Of these 69 results, 44 (63.8%) were broadly focused on PLWH, rather than breaking programming down to target specific diverse populations. Some programming identified in the review did target specific populations that would have distinct needs compared to the general population of PLWH (see Figure 2 for resources offering targeted programming per Canadian city). # **Context**

All results included in the review were based in Canada, per the research inclusion criteria. Of the 64 results identified, 18 (28.1%) were published in British Columbia; 14 (21.9%) in Ontario; 10 (15.6%) were Canada-wide non-provincial/territory specific; 7 (10.9%) in Nova Scotia; 3 (4.7%) in Alberta; 3 (4.7%) in Saskatchewan; 2 (3.1%) in Manitoba; and 1 (1.6%) each in Quebec, New Brunswick, Prince Edward Island, Newfoundland and Labrador, Yukon, Northwest Territories, and Nunavut. For context, the 2019 provincial and territorial diagnosis rates of HIV per 100,000 people were as follows: Saskatchewan, n = 16.9; Manitoba, n = 8.8; Quebec, n = 7.4; Alberta, n = 5.8; Ontario, n = 4.7; British Columbia, n = 3.5; the Atlantic provinces, n = 3.0; and the Northwest Territories, n = 1.7.²
