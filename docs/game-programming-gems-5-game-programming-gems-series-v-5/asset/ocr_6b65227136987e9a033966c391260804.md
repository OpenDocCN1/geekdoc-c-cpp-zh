# A Simple Method for Rendering Gemstones

Thorsten Scheuermann, ATI Research
thorsten@ati.com

Many games require players to find or earn treasure in order to advance in the game environment. This article discusses a technique for rendering gemstones that could be used to reward successful treasure-hunting players with neat eye candy.

The beautiful and complex appearance of gemstones is mainly due to their transparent material that has a high index of refraction. This causes chromatic dispersion and total internal reflection of light rays traveling through the gem.

The gem-rendering technique for this article was used in ATI's Radeon X800 launch demo Ruby: The Double Cross (see Figure 5.7.1).
