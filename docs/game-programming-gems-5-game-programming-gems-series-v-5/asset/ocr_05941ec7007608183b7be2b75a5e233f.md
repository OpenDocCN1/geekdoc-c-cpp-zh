\(\alpha . \beta = \alpha \beta\) scalars
\(\mathbf{a} . \beta = 0\) vector and scalar
\(\alpha . \mathbf{b} = \alpha \mathbf{b}\) scalar and vector
\(\mathbf{a} . \mathbf{b} = \mathbf{a} . \mathbf{b}\) usual dot product on vectors
\(\mathbf{a} . (\mathbf{b} \lrcorner \mathbf{C}) = (\mathbf{a} . \mathbf{b}) \lrcorner \mathbf{C} - \mathbf{C} . \mathbf{b} \lrcorner (\mathbf{a} . \mathbf{C})\) expansion formula
\((\mathbf{A} \wedge \mathbf{B}) . \mathbf{C} = \mathbf{A} . (\mathbf{B} . \mathbf{C})\) distribution formulas
\(\mathbf{A} \wedge (\mathbf{B} . \mathbf{C}) = (\mathbf{A} \wedge \mathbf{B}) . \mathbf{C}\)
(2.1.9)

Note the contraction product is *not* associative: \((\mathbf{A} . \mathbf{B}) . \mathbf{C} \neq \mathbf{A} . (\mathbf{B} . \mathbf{C})\). So parentheses are needed to avoid ambiguity. As a simplifying notation to avoid having to parenthesize every product combination, the precedence conventions shown in Equation 2.1.10 are used:

\((\mathbf{A} \wedge \mathbf{B}) \mathbf{C} = \mathbf{A} \wedge \mathbf{B} \mathbf{C} \neq \mathbf{A} \wedge (\mathbf{B} \mathbf{C})\)
\((\mathbf{A} . \mathbf{B}) \mathbf{C} = \mathbf{A} . \mathbf{B} \mathbf{C} \neq \mathbf{A} . (\mathbf{B} \mathbf{C})\)
\(\mathbf{A} . (\mathbf{B} \wedge \mathbf{C}) = \mathbf{A} . \mathbf{B} \wedge \mathbf{C} \neq (\mathbf{A} . \mathbf{B}) \wedge \mathbf{C}\).
(2.1.10)

This says that when there are ambiguous mixtures of types of products, outer products are performed first, then contraction products, and then geometric products.

We show an example computation before we continue. Being able to compute the following example covers most of what we have explained so far. \(\mathbf{A} \wedge \mathbf{B} = 2\mathbf{e}_2 + 7\mathbf{e}_1 \wedge \mathbf{e}_2 \wedge \mathbf{e}_3 + 4 \cdot 0 + 14 \cdot 0 - 6\mathbf{e}_1 \wedge \mathbf{e}_3 \wedge \mathbf{e}_2 - 21 \cdot 0\)
\(= 2\mathbf{e}_2 + 13\mathbf{e}_1 \wedge \mathbf{e}_2 \wedge \mathbf{e}_3\)
\(\mathbf{AB} = 2\mathbf{e}_2 + 7\mathbf{e}_{21} + 4\mathbf{e}_{22} + 14\mathbf{e}_{2123} - 6\mathbf{e}_1 - 2\mathbf{e}_1 \mathbf{e}_{13123}\)
\(= 2\mathbf{e}_2 + 7\mathbf{e}_{123} + 4 - 14\mathbf{e}_{123} + 6\mathbf{e}_{123} - 2\mathbf{e}_1 \mathbf{e}_{1233}\)
