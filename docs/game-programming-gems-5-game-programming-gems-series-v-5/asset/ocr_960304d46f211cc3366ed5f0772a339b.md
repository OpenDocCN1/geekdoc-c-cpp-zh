Ranking based solely on distance treats targets behind the observer equal to those in front of the observer. If we would like to favor targets in front of the observer, we can select based on the angle from the view axis (or local z-axis) of the observer. A contour plot of a potential field ranked by angle looks like Figure 3.2.2.

**FIGURE 3.2.2** A potential field based solely on angle. Points of equal potential form V shaped pairs of rays (or in 3D, cones) originating at the observer.
