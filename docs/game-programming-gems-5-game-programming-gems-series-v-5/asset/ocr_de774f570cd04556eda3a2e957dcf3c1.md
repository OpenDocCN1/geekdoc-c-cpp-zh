# OpenGL Implementation

The standard OpenGL perspective projection matrix M is given by

$$
\mathbf{M} = \begin{bmatrix}
\frac{2n}{r-l} & 0 & \frac{r+l}{r-l} & 0 \\
0 & \frac{2n}{t-b} & \frac{t+b}{t-b} & 0 \\
0 & 0 & -\frac{f+n}{f-n} & -\frac{2fn}{f-n} \\
0 & 0 & -1 & 0
\end{bmatrix}
$$

where n is the distance to the near plane, f is the distance to the far plane, and the values l, r, b, and t represent the left, right, bottom, and top edges of the rectangle carved out of the near plane by the four side planes of the view frustum. Since M_s = (0, 0, -1, 0), Equation 2.6.12 simplifies to

$$
\mathbf{M}'_s = \frac{-2Q}{\mathbf{C} \cdot Q} \mathbf{C} + (0,0,1,0).
$$

The point Q is given by

$$
Q = \mathbf{M}'^{-1} \langle \text{sgn}(C_x), \text{sgn}(C_y), 1, 1 \rangle,
$$

applying the inverse of M, we have

$$
Q = \begin{bmatrix}
\text{sgn}(C_x) \frac{r-l}{2n} + \frac{r+l}{2n} \\
\text{sgn}(C_y) \frac{t-b}{2n} + \frac{t+b}{2n} \\
-1 \\
1/f
\end{bmatrix}
$$
