# How Many Units Remain at Time t?

The number of units remaining at a particular time is given by these equations.

$$ B = R_0 - c_R t $$ $$ R = R_0 - \left( B_0 c_B t - \frac{1}{2} c_B c_R t^2 \right). $$ (3.3.36)

In this scenario, after five turns, the party's health (and thus the amount of damage it does) is down to almost half, but the boss's health is down to only 20%. Still, the battle is close because the party has lost much of its ability to do damage and the boss has not.

$$ B = 1000 - 90 \cdot 5 = 550 $$ $$ R = 5000 - \left( 1000 \cdot 1 \cdot 5 - \frac{1}{2} \cdot 1 \cdot 90 \cdot 5^2 \right) = 1125 $$ (3.3.37)

# How Much Health Does One Side Have When the Other Side Loses?

The health remaining when the battle is over is given by these equations. The result is valid only for the winning side:

$$ B_{R=0} = \sqrt{B_0^2 - 2 \frac{c_R}{c_B} R_0} $$ $$ R_{B=0} = R_0 - \frac{1}{2} \frac{c_R}{c_B} B_0^2. $$ (3.3.38)

In this scenario, the boss loses; however, the combined health of the party is now only 316 (of the original 1,000). The party is in such bad shape that perhaps it would be better to stop and recuperate:

$$ B_{R=0} = \sqrt{1000^2 - 2 \cdot \frac{90}{1} \cdot 5000} = 316. $$ (3.3.39)
