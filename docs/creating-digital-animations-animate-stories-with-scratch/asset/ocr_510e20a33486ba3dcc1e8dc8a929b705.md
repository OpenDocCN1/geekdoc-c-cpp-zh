You can make a realistic sky by using a *gradient*, which allows you to blend between two colors.

+   1. Click the Color a Shape tool.
+   2. Click the Horizontal Gradient option.
+   3. Click the white color swatch.
+   4. Click the Swap Colors button to swap the foreground and background color.
+   5. Click a sky blue color swatch.
+   6. Click inside the sky rectangle to replace the flat blue color with the gradient.
