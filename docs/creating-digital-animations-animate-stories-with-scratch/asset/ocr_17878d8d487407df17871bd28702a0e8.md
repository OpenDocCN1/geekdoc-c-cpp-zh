Generally, you will want the sky to be brightest near the horizon and darkest toward the top of the canvas. You can also experiment with gradients on the ground. (I chose a light and darker green.)

![](bbox=[0.071, 0.538, 0.111, 0.566])

Click the Rectangle tool and select a dark gray color swatch. Then click and drag from the horizon (the border between your sky and ground rectangles) down to the bottom of the canvas.
