You can add sound to a Scratch project in three ways:

- Choose a sound from the Sound Library.
- Import a sound file (.mp3 or .wav format).
- Record a sound directly in Scratch.

Although the Sound Library has a bunch of music and sound effects to choose from, it has no dialogue.
