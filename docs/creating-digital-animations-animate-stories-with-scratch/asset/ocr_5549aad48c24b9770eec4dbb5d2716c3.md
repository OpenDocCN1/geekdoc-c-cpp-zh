# ## BROADCAST SCRATCH ANIMATION ON ANY WEBSITE

You have seen how sharing a Scratch project will allow logged-in Scratch users to view and remix your projects. You can also display working Scratch projects on your own blog or on another website.

+   1. Go to [www.scratch.mit.edu](http://www.scratch.mit.edu) and log into your account.

+   2. Click the My Stuff button near the top-right part of the web page.
