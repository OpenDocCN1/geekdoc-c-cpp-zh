# MAKE SCENES MORE IMMERSIVE

What if you want viewers to see objects through a window, or have characters enter and exit through doors or hide behind trees? Instead of putting the walls, windows, floors, and doors all in one backdrop, you can put them in a sprite! Scratch sprites are far more flexible than backdrops; sprites can move behind and in front of each other, move from side-to-side, and get smaller and larger.
