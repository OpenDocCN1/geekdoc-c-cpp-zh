# ### ADD A SET OF TEETH

I am much happier with how the eyes and nose look now. But that mouth is kinda freakin' me out. It looks like somebody knocked out the boy's teeth out. Teeth!

+   1. Shift-click the costume and choose Duplicate.
+   2. Click the Color a Shape tool and then choose the white color swatch.
+   3. Click inside the mouth to fill it with white.
+   4. Click the Line tool, choose the black color swatch, and then draw a straight line across the middle of the mouth.
+   5. Click the Reshape tool, click the mouth, and then adjust the shape.
