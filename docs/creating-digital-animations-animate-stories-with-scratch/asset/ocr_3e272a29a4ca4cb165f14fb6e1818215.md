# ## SHARE YOUR SCRATCH PROJECTS IN THE SCRATCH ONLINE COMMUNITY

By default, you are the only person who can see your Scratch projects. To allow other Scratch users to view your project, remix it, or add it to their studio, you must enable sharing. Sharing a project makes it available to all users around the world, so you should delete any personal information or other elements (spare sprites, blocks, and sounds) that you do not want strangers to see. If your project is not yet complete, click the Draft check box before sharing it to inform people that it is a work in process.
