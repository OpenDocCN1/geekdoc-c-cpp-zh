# # FANGS MAKE THE BEAST

When drawing detailed shapes, such as sharp teeth, either zoom in or draw the shape large and then shrink it to size:

+   1. Click the Line tool.
+   2. Click the Outline option.
+   3. Click the black color swatch.
+   4. Click and drag to draw one side of fangs.
+   5. Click back on first point to close the shape.
+   6. Click the Color a Shape tool, click the white swatch, and then click inside the shape.

+   7. Click the Duplicate tool, click the first set of fangs, and then drag them to the other side of the snout.
+   8. Click the Flip Left-Right button to flip the fangs.
+   9. Use the Select tool to drag the fangs into position.
+   10. Use the Reshape tool to adjust the mouth to fit the new snappers.
