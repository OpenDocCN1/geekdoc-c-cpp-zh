# TRIM BEGINNING OF SOUND

Start by deleting the extra silence (where the line is completely flat) at the beginning of the sound wave:

+   1. **Select the sprite you recorded audio on, and then click the Sounds tab.**
+   2. **If you have more than one sound, click the one you want to edit.**
+   3. **Use the horizontal scroll bar to find the beginning of the sound you want to use.**
+   4. **Click the sound wave right at the point where the silence ends and drag left, all the way to the beginning of the sound wave.**
+   5. **Below the sound wave, click the Edit menu and choose Delete.**
