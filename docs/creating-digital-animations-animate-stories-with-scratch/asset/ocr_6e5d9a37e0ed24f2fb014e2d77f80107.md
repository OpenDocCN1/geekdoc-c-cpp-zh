# PROJECT 4 SOUNDS GOOD TO ME

**ARE YOU LIKE MY NIECE AND NEPHEW?** Half the time that you are supposedly watching television, your eyes are not even on the screen. You’re texting somebody with your phone or looking up something on your tablet or playing a game on your laptop or even doing homework. One of the easiest ways to catch your attention is to hit the Mute button on the remote, right? “Hey, I was watching that!” This should illustrate how important sound is to conveying a story.
