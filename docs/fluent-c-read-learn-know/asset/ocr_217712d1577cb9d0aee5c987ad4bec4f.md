# # BRUSHES

Painters these days don't necessarily use a brush to transfer paint from the palette to the canvas. (Come to think of it, they don't necessarily use a canvas, either.) But in .NET, if you're going to display a color, you need to lead it into a Brush. On the other hand, .NET lets you paint with more than a single color. You can lead a brush with multiple colors in a gradient, an image, or even video. At last, you can paint with stripes or a plaid!
