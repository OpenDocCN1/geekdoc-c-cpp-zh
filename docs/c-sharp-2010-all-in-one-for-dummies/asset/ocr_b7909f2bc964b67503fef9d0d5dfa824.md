The overall point here is that whoever called the method needs to know that its task wasn't completed. Throwing an exception is almost always better than using any error-return code.

What the caller does with the exception depends on the nature and severity of the problem. Some problems are worse than others. We use the rest of this chapter — plus the article “Responding to an exception” on csharp101.info — to explore the caller’s options when your method “throws.”
