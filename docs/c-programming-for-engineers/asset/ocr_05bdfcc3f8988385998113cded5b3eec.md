# 2.3.3 Flowcharts

Flowcharts are visual representations of algorithms, using geometric shapes and connecting lines to illustrate the steps and logic of a process. They are comprised of shapes like rectangles for process steps, diamonds for decisions, and ovals for start/end points, connected by lines to show the order of execution. Fig. 2.3 summarizes the symbols mentioned.

Key features of flowcharts:

+   1. **Graphical Representation**: Flowcharts provide a clear visual overview of a process, making it easy to understand the sequence of steps.
2. **Decision Points**: Diamonds indicate where conditions are evaluated, influencing the flow based on outcomes.
3. **Start and End Points**: Ovals signify the beginning and end of the algorithm, guiding the flow.

Benefits of flowcharts:

+   * **Clarity**: Enhances understanding and communication, especially during the planning phase.
* **Planning**: Useful for high-level planning before detailed coding.
* **Documentation**: Flowcharts serve as documentation for algorithms, helping to understand and maintain logic over time.

Drawbacks of flowcharts:

+   * **Complexity**: For very complex algorithms, flowcharts can become challenging to follow.
* **Specificity**: Lacks the precision of pseudocode for detailed language syntax.

Flowcharts complement pseudocode as a visual tool, offering a clear and organized representation of algorithmic processes. They are widely used in various fields, including programming, business process design, and system analysis.

Flowcharts and pseudocode provide systematic approaches to problem-solving, aiding programmers in creating well-organized, readable, and efficient code. They are essential tools for both beginners and experienced programmers to design and implement effective solutions to a wide range of problems.

# 2.4 Control Structures

Any algorithm can be described using only three control structures:

+   * **Sequence**: A series of statements executed one after another
* **Selection**: Different blocks of statements are executed based on a condition
* **Repetition**: A statement block is repeated until a condition is met
