
Welcome to "C Programming for Engineers," an innovative learning resource tailored specifically for engineering students and embedded systems designers.

This book presents a refreshing approach to mastering C programming by addressing the unique needs and challenges faced by engineers. Unlike conventional C programming books that often cater to computer science applications, this book focuses on the practical aspects of C programming that are directly applicable to engineering and embedded systems design.

In recognition of the changing landscape of embedded systems design, we have intentionally de-emphasized certain areas such as file I/O, printf, and scanf, which are less frequently utilized in engineering applications. Instead, we have strategically emphasized topics that are highly relevant to engineers; including bit manipulations, arithmetic with resource-constrained systems, and the integration of Arduino in the learning process.

The module balances theoretical concepts with practical examples to equip you with the knowledge and skills necessary to write efficient and robust code for engineering applications. Our ultimate goal is to empower you to design and implement your innovative solutions. Engage actively with the exercises, explore concepts further, and most importantly, enjoy the process of coding!

Remember, mastering programming requires practice and persistence. We are confident that "C Programming for Engineers" will serve as an invaluable resource in your journey to becoming a skilled engineer.
