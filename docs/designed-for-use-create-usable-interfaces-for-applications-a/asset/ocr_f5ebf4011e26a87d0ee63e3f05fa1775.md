# Takeaway Points

+   - People don’t know what they want, so you have to visit them and observe what they do.
+   - If you ask specific questions, you may get useful information, but do interview several people before coming to conclusions.

# Further Reading

Cennydd Bowles and James Box have a chapter on this kind of user research in the book *Undercover User Experience Design* [BB10]. Robert Hoekman covers shadowing and contextual interviews in *Designing the Obvious* [Hoe06].
