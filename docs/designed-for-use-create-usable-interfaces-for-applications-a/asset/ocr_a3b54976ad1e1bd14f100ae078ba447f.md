# 6.11 Display Legible Text

Making the content itself usable is important, but the way you present it also matters.

*Pick a large font size.* Although most people hold books quite close to their faces, computer screens tend to be further away. While sitting in front of your computer and looking at your site or application, hold up a book at typical reading distance, and compare the font sizes.¹⁴
