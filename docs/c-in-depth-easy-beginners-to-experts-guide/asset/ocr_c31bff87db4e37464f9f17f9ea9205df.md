# do while loop -

The do while loop in C is an “upside down” version of the while loop. Whereas while has the condition followed by the body, do while has the body followed by the condition.

This means the body must be executed before the condition is reached. Thus the body is guaranteed to be executed at least once.if the condition is false the loop body
