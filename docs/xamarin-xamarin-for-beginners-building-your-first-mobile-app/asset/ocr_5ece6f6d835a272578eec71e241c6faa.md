# -Rotation

Any visible element in Xamarin can be rotated by selecting the center of rotation and angle of rotation. The angle of rotation is determined by degrees, and the positive values of the angle of rotation indicate the clockwise rotation. The rotation center of any element is determined by its AnchorX and AnchorY properties. The rotation angle can also be determined by Rotation.
