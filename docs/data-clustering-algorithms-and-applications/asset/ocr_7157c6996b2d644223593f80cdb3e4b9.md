# ## Semisupervised Embedding Methods
We compare the six label propagation methods based on semisupervised manifold embedding (see Section 20.5). In particular we examine both of Laplacian Eigenmaps-based Label Propagation (LELP, LEPC), LLEPC, and Tangent Space Alignment-based Label Propagation (LTSALP, LTSCAP). We compare the methods on the UCI datasets and show representative plots in Figure 20.3.Based on our experiments, we observe that LELP and LTSALP performed well mostly, while LLEPC did only well on specific data sets like Wuhan. The performance of LLEPC seems to be more sensitive to the geometry of a data set.
