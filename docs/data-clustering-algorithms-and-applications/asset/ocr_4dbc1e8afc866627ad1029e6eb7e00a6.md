# 20.5 Semisuvised Embedding

In this section, we show SSC methods based on graph-supervised representations, as discussed in Sections 20.3 and 20.4, can be viewed as semisuvised embedding. The geometric perspective helps in identifying relationships between existing embedding and label propagation methods, e.g., between Laplacian Eigenmaps [8] and Gaussian Fields [46]. Further, we illustrate that it is possible to derive novel SSC methods based on existing methods, including Linear Embedding (LE), Local Tangent Alignment (LTSA) [44] and Laplacian Eigenmaps (LE). While all such methods can be seen as a special case of the GLP formulation, they differ in—the positive semidefinite matrix $L$ and nature of constraints. Our focus is on two clusters, the embedding will always be on $\mathbb{R}$, a one-dimensional space. For number clusters $k>2$, the embedding space may be of dimensionality $k$ or $\log k$, depending on the representation used.
