MULIC is motivated by clustering of categorical datasets that have a few multilayered structure. Instance, in networks a cluster (or module) may have a center of a few well-connected objects (nodes) surrounded by peripheries of sparse connectivity [27, 13, 8, 9, 11]. On data, MULIC outperforms other algorithms that create a flat clustering. MULIC produces layered (or nested) clusters, which is different in several ways from traditional hierarchical clustering. MULIC requires no-specified parameters and the resulting clusters are useful for bioinformatics for finding protein modules and complexes, and for visualization [7, 11, 13, 62, 68].
