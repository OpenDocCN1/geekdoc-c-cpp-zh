Some of the methods, like mixture models, may be more appropriate for quantitative data, whereas others, as topic models, PLSI, and LDA, are used more commonly for text data. The goals of learning algorithms for these probabilistic models are to find MLE or MAP estimations for parameters in these models. There are no closed form solutions for most of them. The EM algorithm provides a general and elegant framework to learn mixture models and topic models. For more complicated cases, some variations of the EM algorithm, such as generalized EM and variational EM, can be employed to solve these problems.
