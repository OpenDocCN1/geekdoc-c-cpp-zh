# 4.3.2 Group Averaged and Centroid Agglomerative Clustering

**Group Averaged Agglomerative Clustering (GAAC)** shows the similarity between all pairs of points present in this cluster and diminishes the drawbacks associated with single and complete link methods. We look at the formula let us introduce some terminology. Let $C_a$ and $C_b$ be merged such that the resulting cluster $C_{a,b} = C_a \cup C_b$. The new center for this cluster is $C_{a,b} = \frac{Na_a+Nb_b}{N_a+N_b}$, where $N_a$ and $N_b$ are the sizes of the clusters $C_a$ and $C_b$, respectively. The similarity measure for GAAC is as follows:

```
$S_{GAAC}(C_a, C_b) = \frac{1}{(N_a+N_b)(N_a+N_b-1)} \sum_{i\in C_a\cup C_b} \sum_{j\in C_a\cup C_b, i\neq j} d(i,j)$
```
