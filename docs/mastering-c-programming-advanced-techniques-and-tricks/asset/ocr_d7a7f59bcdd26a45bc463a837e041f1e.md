Minimize Work Inside Signal Signal handlers should do minimal work, ideally setting a flag checked by other parts of the program or using mechanisms communicate with other threads.

Careful Use of Synchronization Avoid locking mutexes that may already be locked by the thread interrupted by the signal, as this can lead to deadlock.

Consistent Signal Use control which signals are blocked or handled by specific threads to ensure predictable signal delivery.

Effectively handling signals in multithreaded environments requires a deep understanding of both the signal model of Unix-like systems and the concurrency mechanisms provided by pthreads. By adhering to best practices and carefully designing signal handling strategies, it’s possible to build robust, safe multithreaded applications.
