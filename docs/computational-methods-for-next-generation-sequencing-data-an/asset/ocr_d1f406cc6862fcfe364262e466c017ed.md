# 3.6 CONCLUSION

In this study, we present a novel framework for massive NGS of highly mutable RNA viruses, such as HCV and HIV. To the best of our knowledge, this is the first application of the pooling strategy to highly heterogeneous viral samples. The developed framework takes into account specific aspects of viral sequencing, such as the extensive heterogeneity of viral samples, the large number of distinct viral variants
