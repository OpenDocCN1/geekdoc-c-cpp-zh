To compare between different methods (with different parameter settings), we use the following two:

- average distance to clones ADC = $\sum d(c_i) \cdot f(c_i)$
- average prediction error APE = $\sum d(q_j) \cdot f(q_j)$

where $c_1, \dots, c_{10}$ are Sanger clones, $q_1, \dots, q_j$ are reconstructed variants, $f(c_i)$ and $f(q_j)$ are frequencies of ith and jth variant, and $d(c_i)$ and $d(q_j)$ are edit distance from ith clone to the closest reconstructed variant and edit distance from jth reconstructed variant to the closest clone.

Average Distance to Clones (ADC) and Average Prediction Error (APE) are analogous to sensitivity and pvy. The difference is that ADC and APE indicate better quality whenever they are close to 0, while sensitivity and pvy indicate better quality when they are close to 1.
