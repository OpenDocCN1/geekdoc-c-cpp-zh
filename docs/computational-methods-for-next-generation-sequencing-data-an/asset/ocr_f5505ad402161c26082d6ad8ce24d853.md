# 14.5.2.2 Effect of Gene Abundance

We studied the effect of gene abundance on the IsoDE, GFOLD, and edgeR prediction accuracy. We selected the subset of genes that are expressed in at least one of the two runs. We sorted these genes by the average of the gene's expression. We used the FPKM values predicted by IsoEM, the FPKM values predicted by GFOLD, and the number of uniquely mapped reads, for IsoDE, GFOLD, and edgeR, respectively. Genes were then divided into quantiles, for each method independently, where quantile 1 had the genes with the lowest expression levels, and quantile 5 had the genes with the highest expression levels. Sensitivity, PPV, and F-score were calculated for each quantile separately.
