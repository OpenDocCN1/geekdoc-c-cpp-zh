# 7.5.1.2 Internal Segment-Based Evidence

Internal segment-based evidence is provided by evaluating the empirical statistics on fragment length inherent to the library read stems. We develop this part in view of fragment length statistics being approximately Gaussian. However, this can be easily generalized to arbitrary statistics. Let $D$ be the deletion to be genotyped and $C(D)$ be its centerpoint. Let $R$ be the read that has alignment to A where $x_A < C(D) < y_A$ that is, the alignment interval of $A$ contains the centerpoint of the breakpoints of $D$. Note that a centerpoint-oriented selection leads to a balanced is in choosing equal amounts of reads to provide evidence for and against the deletion, as outlined in Reference 10. Let $\mu$ and $\sigma$ be mean and standard deviation of the internal segment size distribution of the library $R$ stems from. So, internal segment length, as a random variable $X$, is distributed as the normal distribution $X \sim N_{\mu, \sigma}$ for the library under consideration. There are two cases: first, alignments $A$ whose reads stem from a chromosomal copy that is not affected by $D$, and second alignments $B$ whose reads stem from a chromosomal copy that is affected by $D$. We obtain

```
$I(A) \sim N_{\mu, \sigma}^A$ and $I(B) \sim N_{\mu+L, \sigma}^B$,
```

where the case reflects that the alignment contains the deletion of length L. Refer to Figure 7.7 for an illustration. We compute that $P(A^+ | G_0) \sim N_{\mu, \sigma}^A(I(A))$ and $P(A^+ | G_2) \sim N_{\mu+L, \sigma}^B(I(A))$ as appropriate densities for the cases of no variant and a homozygous variant.

Let $Z := N_{\mu, \sigma}^A(I(A)) + N_{\mu+L, \sigma}^B(I(A))$. In analogy for the split-read case, we arrive at

```
$P(G_i | A^+) := \frac{1}{2} \cdot N_{\mu, \sigma}^A(I(A)) \quad i = 1 \\
\frac{1}{2} \cdot N_{\mu+L, \sigma}^B(I(A)) \quad i = 0$
```

as an appropriate probability distribution for reads whose alignments span the breakpoints of deletions by their internal segments.
