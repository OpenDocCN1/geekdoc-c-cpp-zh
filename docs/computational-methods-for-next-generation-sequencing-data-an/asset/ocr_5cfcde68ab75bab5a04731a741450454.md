# ## 7.6 RESULTS

# ### 7.6.1 Data Set

We downloaded all variant annotations for Craig Venter's genome from the HuRef database (16). We generated a diploid genome using these annotations, as per the procedure described in Reference 10 to generate a 'father' genome, which results in a genome that is realistic in terms of both variants and zygosity status of variants. Note that direct use of the annotations results in a genome with an unrealistic ratio of heterozygous to homozygous deletions (vastly overrated homozygous indels), which is likely due to the difficulties in determining the zygosity status of insertions and deletions during the original assembly stage. We have resolved these issues such that the ratio of heterozygous and homozygous deletions is realistic.

Subsequently, we simulated reads for each copy, using Simseq, the read simulator of the Assemblathon (17), at 15x coverage, which results in 30x coverage overall. We opted to simulate reads according to two prevalent and most recent Illumina protocols: HiSeq and MiSeq, where mean and standard deviation for the internal segment were 112 and 15 (HiSeq) and 250 and 15 (MiSeq), respectively.
