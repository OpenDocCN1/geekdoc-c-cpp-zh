The computational exploration of classes of genetic variants, such as single-nucleotide polymorphisms (SNPs) and small insertions and deletions (indels) have become standard. See, for example, the GATK (4) for best-practice workflows. Treating other classes of variants, however, such as much, inversions or nested combinations of simpler variants, is often nonstandard or requires computationally advanced techniques. See, for example, References 5, 6 for reviews on the discovery of structural variants.
