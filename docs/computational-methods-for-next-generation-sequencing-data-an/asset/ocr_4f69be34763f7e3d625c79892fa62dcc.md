**Figure 4.1** (See Reference 5) Workflow. (a) DNA material from a viral population is cleaved into sequence fragments using any suitable restriction enzyme. (b) Individual barcode sequences are attached to the fragments. Each tagged fragment is amplified by the polymerase chain reaction (PCR). (c) Amplified fragments are then sequenced. (d) Reads are grouped around the fragment of origin based on their individual barcode sequence. An error-correction protocol is applied for every read group, correcting the sequencing errors inside the group and producing corrected consensus reads. (e) Error-corrected reads are mapped to the population consensus. (f) SNVs are detected and assembled into individual viral genomes. The ordinary protocol lacks steps (b) and (d).
