RNA-seq is a relatively new field, approaches are constantly changing, while it is maturing in terms of its use in expression analyses, these challenges suggest that there is a lot of improvement in the field of *de novo* RNA-seq assembly. A similar state of the art also exists for *de novo* genome assembly and should not be surprising. This emphasizes the advantage of having -a well annotated genome to guide any assembly. The current value of a *de novo* approach to assembly remains in the potential to drive discovery. It will take further improvements, either in the algorithms or the data, before this approach can match that of assembly to reference methodology.
