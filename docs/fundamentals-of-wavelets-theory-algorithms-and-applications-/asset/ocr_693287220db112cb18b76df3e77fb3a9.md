```
\left\{\begin{array}{ll} A_{k+1} & = A_s + W_s; \\ A_r \cap A_m & = A_r; m \ge \ell \\ W_r \cap W_m & = \{0\}; \ell \neq m \\ A_r \cap W_m & = \{0\}; \ell \le m. \end{array}\right.
```

# MRA \(\{\tilde{A}_s\}\)
