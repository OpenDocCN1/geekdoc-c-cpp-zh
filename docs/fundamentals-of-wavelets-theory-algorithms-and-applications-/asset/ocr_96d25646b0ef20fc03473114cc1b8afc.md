(3.102)
\(\hat{f}(\omega) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a^n e^{-jn\omega}\)
\(= \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} (ae^{-j\omega})^n\)
\(= \frac{1}{1 - ae^{-j\omega}}.\)

We pointed out in Chapter 2 that the DTFT can be obtained from the z-transform by replacing the variable \(z\) with \(e^{j\omega}\). For this example, the z-transform is

\(F(z) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} a^n z^{-n}\)
\(= \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} (az^{-1})^n\)
\(= \frac{1}{1 - az^{-1}}.\)

Therefore, replacing the variable \(z\) with \(e^{j\omega}\) yields

\(F(z)|_{z=e^{j\omega}} = \frac{1}{1 - ae^{-j\omega}} = \hat{f}(\omega).\)

The z-transform \(F(z)\) and the DTFT, \([\hat{f}(\omega) = F(z)|_{z=e^{j\omega}}]\), will be used interchangeably in future derivations and discussions on wavelet construction.
