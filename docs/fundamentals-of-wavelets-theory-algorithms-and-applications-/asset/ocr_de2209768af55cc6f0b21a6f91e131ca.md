We use the derivation of the Daubechies [5] scaling function coefficients as an example. Let us recall the conditions on the half-band filter

$S(z) + S(-z) = 2$

The simplest form of $S(z)$ other than the Haar filter is

(7.102) $S(z) = (1+z)^2 (1+z^{-1})^2 R(z)$.

All even coefficients of $S(z)$ must be zero except at 0 where $s(0) = 1$. Let

$R(z) = az + b + az^{-1}$

be a noncausal symmetric filter so that $S(z)$ remains symmetric. By carrying out the algebra in (7.102) and using condition on $S(z)$, we have

+   \(\begin{cases} S(0)=1 & \Rightarrow 8a+6b=1 \\ S(2)=S(-2)=0 & \Rightarrow 4a+b=0 \end{cases}\)

giving $a = -1/16$ and $b = 1/4$. The symmetric filter $R(z)$ becomes

$R(z) = -\frac{1}{16}z + \frac{1}{4} - \frac{1}{16}z^{-1} = \left(\frac{1}{4\sqrt{2}}\right)^2 [1+\sqrt{3} + (1-\sqrt{3})z^{-1}][1+\sqrt{3} + (1-\sqrt{3})z]$
