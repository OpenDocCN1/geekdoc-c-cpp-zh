# 9.5.1 Pattern Recognition of Acoustic Signals

Acoustic signal recognition has gained much attention in recent years. It is applicable to the recognition of ships from sonar signatures, cardiopulmonary diagnostics from heart sounds, safety warnings and noise suppression in factories, and recognition of different types of bearing faults in the wheels of railroad cars. The basic goal of acoustic signal recognition is to identify an acoustic signal pattern from a library of acoustic statistics.
