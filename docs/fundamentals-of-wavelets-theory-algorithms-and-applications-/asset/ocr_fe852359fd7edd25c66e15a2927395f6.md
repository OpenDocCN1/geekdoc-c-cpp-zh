An IZ represents an insignificant DWC with at least one significant descendent. It is this symbol that reduces the compression since more symbols are needed for encoding its descendants. With these definitions, two bits per symbol on average are needed for each of the four symbols. Without a good follow-up entropy coder for those symbols, zerotree cannot get good compression results. Also, all of the encoded symbols must be reordered so that the entropy coder can achieve compression on them. Both of these procedures increase the computation overhead.
