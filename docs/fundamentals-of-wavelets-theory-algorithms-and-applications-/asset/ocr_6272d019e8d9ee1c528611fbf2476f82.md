(7.52) \(\tilde{A}(e^{j\omega}) := \sum_k \tilde{a}_k e^{jk\omega}, \quad A(e^{j\omega}) := \sum_k a_k e^{jk\omega}.\)

The dual scaling function \(\tilde{N}_m\) is given by

(7.53) \(\hat{\tilde{N}}_m(\omega) = \frac{\hat{\tilde{N}}_m(\omega)}{E_{N_m}(z^2)}, \quad z = e^{j\omega/2}\)

where \(E_{N_m}(z^2) = |\hat{\tilde{N}}_m(\omega + 2\pi k)|^2 \neq 0\) for almost all \(\omega\) since \(\{N_m(\cdot - k)\}\) is a stable or Riesz basis of \(A_0\). As discussed in Chapter 6, \(E_{N_m}(\omega)\) is the Euler-Frobenius Laurent series and is given by

(7.54) \(E_{N_m}(z^2) = |\hat{\tilde{N}}_m(\omega + 2\pi k)|^2 = \sum_{k=-m+1}^{m-1} N_{2m}(m+k) z^{2k}.\)

It is clear that by multiplying (7.54) by \(z^{m-1}\), we can get a polynomial of degree \(2m-1\) in \(z\). The last equality in (7.54) is a consequence of the relation

(7.55) \(\sum_{k=-\infty}^{\infty} |\hat{f}(\omega + 2\pi k)|^2 = \sum_{k=-\infty}^{\infty} \left\{ \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f(t+k) \overline{f(t)} dt \right\} e^{jk\omega}.\)

Proof for (7.55).
