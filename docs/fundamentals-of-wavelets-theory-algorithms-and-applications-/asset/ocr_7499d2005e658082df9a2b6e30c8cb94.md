The coefficients are merged (zipped) together to form the next higher resolution signal.

```
a_{2k, l+1}
a_{2k+1, l+1} \xrightarrow{merge} a_{k, l+1}
```

This process is repeated for every level of resolution until all the wavelet coefficients at each level have been used in the reconstruction process and the final sequence of samples will be the original signal x(k).
