## 7.6.2 Change of Basis for Spline Wavelets

Here our objective is to write

(7.66)
\[
r(t) = \sum_k \tilde{w}_k \tilde{\psi}_m(t-k) = \sum_k w_k \psi_m(t-k).
\]

Replacing $N_m$ by $\psi_m$ in (7.53) we get the relationship between $\psi_m$ and $\bar{\psi}_m$. Proceeding in the same way as before, we get

(7.67)
\[
w_k = (\{\bar{w}_{n_1}\} * q[n])(k)
\]

where

(7.68)
\[
\sum_k \frac{1}{|\hat{\psi}_m(\omega + 2\pi k)|^2} = \sum_k q[k] e^{-j\omega k}.
\]

Furthermore, we have

(7.69)
\[
\sum_k |\hat{\psi}_m(\omega + 2\pi k)|^2 = E_{N_m}(z^2) E_{N_m}(z) E_{N_m}(-z), |z| = 1.
\]

Proof for (7.69).

With the help of two-scale relation, we can write

(7.70)
\[
\sum_k |\hat{\psi}_m(\omega + 2\pi k)|^2 = \sum_k |G_1(\exp(j\frac{\omega+2\pi k}{2})) \hat{N}_m(\frac{\omega+2\pi k}{2})|^2
\]

with

(7.71)
\[
G_1(e^{j\omega/2}) = \frac{1}{2} \sum_k g_1[k] e^{jk\omega/2}.
\]

Now separating the right-hand side of (7.70) into parts with even \( k \) and odd \( k \) and making use of the relation (7.54), we can write

(7.72)
\[
\sum_k |\tilde{\psi}_m(\omega+2\pi k)|^2 = |G_1(z)|^2 E_{N_m}(z) + |G_1(-z)|^2 E_{N_m}(-z).
\]

From the relation $G_1(z) = G_0(-z) E_{N_m}(-z)$, with $G_0(z)$ defined in a similar way as in (7.71) with $g_1[k]$ replaced by $g_0[k]$, we can write

(7.73)
\[
\sum_k |\tilde{\psi}_m(\omega+2\pi k)|^2 = [|G_0(-z)|^2 E_{N_m}(-z) + |G_0(z)|^2 E_{N_m}(z)] E_{N_m}(z) E_{N_m}(-z).
\]

Following the steps used to arrive at (7.72), it can be shown that

(7.74)
\[
|G_0(-z)|^2 E_{N_m}(-z) + |G_0(z)|^2 E_{N_m}(z) = E_{N_m}(z^2).
\]

which, together with (7.72), gives the desired relation (7.69).

The expression for \(q[k]\) has the same form as that of \(p[k]\) with \(u_m = -(2m-1)!^3\), \(p_m = 2m-2\), and \(\lambda_i\) being the roots of (7.69). Observe from (7.68) and (7.69) that

(7.75)
\[
\sum_k q[k] = \frac{1}{E_{N_m}(-1)}
\]

since \( E_{N_m}(1)=1 \). Roots \( \lambda_i \) and \( \sum_k q[k] \) for linear and cubic splines are given below. The coefficients are given in Tables 7.3 and 7.4. The coefficients \( q[k] \) have better decay than \( \{h_1[k]\} \) in Figure 7.12.
