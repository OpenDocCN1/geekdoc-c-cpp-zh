# 4.2.4 Properties of STFT

Linearity:
Let \( f(t) = \alpha f_1(t) + \beta f_2(t) \) be a linear combination of two functions \( f_1(t) \) and \( f_2(t) \) with the weights \( \alpha \) and \( \beta \) independent of \( t \). Then the STFT of \( f(t) \),

(4.19) \( G_\phi f(b, \tilde{\xi}) = \alpha G_\phi f_1(b, \tilde{\xi}) + \beta G_\phi f_2(b, \tilde{\xi}) \),
is the linear sum of the STFT of the individual function.
Hence the STFT is a linear transformation.
