```
$$\mathbf{W}_f(t, \omega) = \frac{1}{2\pi} \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} f\left(t + \frac{\tau}{2}\right) \overline{f\left(t - \frac{\tau}{2}\right)} e^{-j\omega\tau} d\tau. \tag{4.64}$$
```

The constant \(1/2\pi\) is a normalization factor for simplicity of computation. We should note that the linearity property no longer holds for equation (4.64). The Wigner-Ville distribution is a nonlinear (or bilinear) time-frequency transform because the signal enters the integral more than once. One may also observe that the Wigner-Ville distribution at a given time \(t\) looks symmetrically to the left and right sides of the signal at a distance \(\tau/2\). Computation of \(\mathbf{W}_f(t, \omega)\) requires signal information at \(t \pm \tau/2\), and cannot be carried out in real-time.
