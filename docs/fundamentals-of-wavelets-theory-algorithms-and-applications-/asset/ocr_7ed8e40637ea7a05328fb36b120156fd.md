# 4.10 PROPERTIES OF WIGNER-VILLE DISTRIBUTION

There are several general properties of WVD that are important for signal representation in signal processing. Some of them are discussed in this section. It has been shown [5] that the Wigner-Ville distribution has the highest concentration of signal energy in the time-frequency plane. Any other distribution that has a higher energy concentration than WVD will be in violation of the uncertainty principle. Furthermore, it cannot satisfy the so-called marginal properties discussed in this section.
