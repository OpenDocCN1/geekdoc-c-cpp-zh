In addition, the developments on the discrete wavelet transform (DWT) and the wavelet series (WS) make the wavelet approach more suitable than the STFT for signal and image processing. To clarify our points, let us observe that the radii \(\Delta_\phi\) and \(\Delta_{\hat{\phi}}\) of the window function for STFT do not depend on the location in the \(t - \omega\) plane. For instance, if we choose \(\phi(t) = g_\alpha(t)\) as in the Gabor transform (4.28), once \(\alpha\) is fixed, then so are \(\Delta g_\alpha\) and \(\Delta_{\hat{g}_\alpha}\), regardless of the window location in the \(t - \omega\) plane. A typical STFT time-frequency window was shown in Figure 4.3. Once the window function is chosen, the time-frequency resolution is fixed throughout the processing.
