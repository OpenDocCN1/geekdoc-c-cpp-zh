```
\( F_1(x) = \sum_k g_0[k] f(x - k) \)

```

```
\( F_2(x) = \sum_k g_1[k] f(x - k), \)
(9.2)
```

then \(\{F_1(\cdot - 2k), F_2(\cdot - 2k); k \in \mathbb{Z}\}\) are orthonormal bases of \( E = \text{span}\{f(\cdot - n); n \in \mathbb{Z}\} \).
