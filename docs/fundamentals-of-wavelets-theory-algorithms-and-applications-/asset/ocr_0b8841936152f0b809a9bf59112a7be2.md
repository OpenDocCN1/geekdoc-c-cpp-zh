where the overbar indicates complex conjugation, we express the orthogonality by

```
(2.15) 
\(\frac{1}{T} \int_{-T/2}^{T/2} e^{jk\omega_0 t} e^{-jl\omega_0 t} dt = \delta_{k,l}, \forall k, l \in \mathbb{Z}.\)
```

We may normalize the basis functions (with respect to unit energy) by dividing them with \(\sqrt{T}\). Hence \(\{e^{jk\omega_0 t}/\sqrt{T}\}_{k \in \mathbb{Z}}\) forms the orthonormal basis of the discrete Fourier space.
