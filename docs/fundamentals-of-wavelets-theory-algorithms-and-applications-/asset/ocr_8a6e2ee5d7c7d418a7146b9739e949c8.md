Chapters 4–6 are devoted to discussing theoretical aspects of time-frequency analysis, multiresolution analysis, and the construction of various types of wavelets; Chapters 7 and 8 give several algorithms for computing wavelet transform and implement them through a filter bank approach. Part of Chapter 8 and Chapters 9 and 10 present many interesting application of wavelets to signal-processing and boundary value problems.
