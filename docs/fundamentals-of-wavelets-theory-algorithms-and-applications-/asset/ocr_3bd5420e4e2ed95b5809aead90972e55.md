We remark here that a signal may be represented by a selected set of wavelet packets without using every wavelet packet for a given level of resolution. An engineering practitioner may construct an algorithm to choose the packets for optimizing a certain measure (such as energy, entropy, and variance). Best-basis and best-level are two popular algorithms for signal representations. The reader can find these algorithms in Ref. 2.
