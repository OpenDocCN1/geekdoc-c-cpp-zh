(3.45) 
\(\hat{r}(\omega) = \int_{-\infty}^{\infty} r(t) e^{-i\omega t} dt = \int_{-T}^{T} e^{-i\omega t} dt = \frac{2T \sin \omega T}{\omega T}.\)

The function, \(\sin \omega T / \omega T\) , called the sinc function, is the Fourier transform of a rectangular pulse. We remark here that for \(T = 1/2\) , the function \(r(t - 1/2) = z_{(0,1)}(t)\) is called the characteristic function or the first-order B-spline. This is an important function to remember and will be recalled later in the development of wavelet theory.
