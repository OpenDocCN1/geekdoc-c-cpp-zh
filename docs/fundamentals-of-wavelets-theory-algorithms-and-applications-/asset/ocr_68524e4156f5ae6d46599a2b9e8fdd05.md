# 10.2 METHOD OF MOMENTS

Method of moments [7] is probably the most widely used technique for solving integral equations in electromagnetics. In conventional MoM, the boundary of integration is approximated by discretizing it into many segments. Then the unknown function is expanded in terms of known basis functions with unknown coefficients. These bases may be “global” (entire domain), extending the entire length [$a, b$], or they may be “local” (subdomain), covering only a small segment of the interval, or a combination of both. Finally, the resultant equation is tested with the same or different functions, resulting in a set of linear equations whose solution gives the unknown coefficients.
