# 8.1.4.4 Perturbed Sinusoidal Signal.

Figure 8.16 gives the time-frequency representation of a function that is composed of two sinusoids and two delta functions, represented as sharp changes in some data values. Observe that two sinusoids appear as two bands parallel to the time axis whereas the delta functions are indicated by two vertical bands parallel to the frequency axis. As discussed in Chapter 4, the frequency spread is due to finite window width of the wavelets.
