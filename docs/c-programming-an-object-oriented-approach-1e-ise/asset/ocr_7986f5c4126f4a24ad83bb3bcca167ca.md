# ### 17.3.2 Prime Numbers

Mathematicians divide the positive integers into three groups:

+   1. Integer 1
+   2. Primes
+   3. Composites

The integer 1 is neither prime nor composite. A prime number is divisible by 1 and itself. A composite number is divisible by 1, itself, and other integers. In other words, the integer 1 has only one divisor (itself), a prime has two divisors (integer 1 and itself), and a composite has more than two divisors (integer 1, itself, and other integers smaller than itself). We can see that the smallest prime is integer 2. The primes less than 10 are 2, 3, 5, and 7.
