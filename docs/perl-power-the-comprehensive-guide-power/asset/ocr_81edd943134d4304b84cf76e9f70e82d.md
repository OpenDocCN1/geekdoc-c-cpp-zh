This book originated in my efforts as a teacher at a trade college. I taught one course after another on Perl. How many such courses there are at the college level is hard to say, but whatever the case, over time I learned that the students in the class were delighted when they discovered programming through Perl. On thing I learned as a teacher was that the people in the classes (and they constituted a fairly diverse group) enjoyed the essentials most. They tended to express dissatisfaction when I approached Perl as a module-driven language.

It could have been the teacher, of course.
