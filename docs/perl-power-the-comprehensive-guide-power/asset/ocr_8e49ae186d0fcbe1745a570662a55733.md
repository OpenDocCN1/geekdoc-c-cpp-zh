# ## 1. Igneous Rocks

Rocks and minerals that originate
as magma are igneous. Magma is rock
that has been heated to the melting
point by tectonic pressures.

Magma is a common feature of volcanic
eruptions, but it exists only in the
upper crust of the Earth. Deeper in the
earth, pressures prevent rock from melting.

Examples: porphyry, granite, pegmatite,
gabbro. These rocks form the cores
of mountains and are exposed by weathering.
