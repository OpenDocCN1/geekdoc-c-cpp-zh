16.8 (Project: Cooking with Healthier Ingredients) Obesity in the United States is increasing at an alarming rate. Check the map from the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) at http://www.obesity.gov/adult-obesity/ which shows obesity trends in the United States over the last 20 years. As obesity increases, so occurrences of related problems (e.g., heart disease, high blood pressure, high cholesterol, type 2 diabetes). Write a program that helps users choose healthier ingredients when cooking a recipe, and helps those allergic to certain foods (e.g., nuts, gluten) find substitutes. The program should read the recipe from the user and suggest replacements for some of the ingredients. For simplicity, your program should assume the recipe has no abbreviations for measures as teaspoons, cups, and tablespoons, and uses numerical digits for quantities (e.g., 1 egg, 2 cups) rather than spelling them out (one egg, two cups). Some common substitutions are shown in 16.16. Your program should display a warning such as "Always consult your physician before making significant changes to your diet."

| Ingredient | Substitution |
|---|---|
| 1 cup cream | 1 cup yogurt |
| 1 cup milk | 1/2 cup evaporated milk and 1/2 cup water |
| 1 teaspoon lemon juice | 1/2 teaspoon vinegar |
| 1 cup sugar | 1/2 cup honey, 1 cup molasses or 1/4 cup agave nectar |
| 1 cup butter | 1 cup margarine or yogurt |
| 1 cup flour | 1 cup rye or rice flour |
| 1 cup mayonnaise | 1 cup cottage cheese or 7/8 cup Greek yogurt |
| 1 egg | 2 tablespoons cornstarch, arrowroot flour or potato starch or 2 egg whites or 1/2 of a large banana (mashed) |
| 1 cup milk | 1 cup soy milk |
| 1/4 cup oil | 1/4 cup applesauce |
| white bread | whole-grain bread |

**Fig. 16.16** Common ingredient substitutions.
