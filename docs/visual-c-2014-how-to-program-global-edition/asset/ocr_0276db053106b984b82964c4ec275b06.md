**5.17 (Interest on Term Deposit)** Investors are concerned with the investments earn in one month. One investor has kept track of the interest earned on several investments by recording the amount of each investment and the interest earned on each (both as integers) for each investment. Develop a C# app that will input the amount invested and the interest earned. The app should calculate and display the combined interests earned for each investment up to the current month. The results rounded off to the nearest hundredth. Use the Console’s ReadLine method and sentinel-controlled iteration to obtain the data from the user.

**5.18 (Inventory Level Calculator)** Develop a C# app that will determine the level of any of the items in a store's inventory has fallen below 1% of the initial number of units. For each inventory, the following factors are available:

+   a) the item's code;
b) the initial number of units
c) the number of units purchased
d) the number of units sold

The app should input all factors as integers, calculate the closing number of units (= opening number of units + number of units purchased - number of units sold) and determine whether the closing number of units has fallen below the lower limit. For items whose quantity has fallen below the lower limit, the app should display the message "Procure more immediately". Sentinel-controlled iteration to obtain the data for each item.

**5.19 (Sales-Commission Calculator)** A large company has its salespeople on a commission basis. The salesperson who sells $5,000 worth of merchandise per week plus 9% of their gross sales for week receives $200 per week (9% of $5,000, or a total of $650). You have been supplied with a list of items sold by each salesperson. The values of these items as follows:

| Item | Value |
| 1 | 239.99 |
| 2 | 129.75 |
| 3 | 99.95 |
| 4 | 350.89 |

Develop a C# app that inputs one salesperson's items sold for the last week, calculates and displays that salesperson's earnings.

**5.20 (Discount Calculator)** Develop a C# app that will determine the net amount payable for items purchased at a department store. The store offers a 10% discount if the total purchase is less than or equal to $5,000 and an additional 10% on purchase amounts exceeding $5,000. The app should input the price of each item purchased, calculate the total purchase value, the amount of discount applicable, and the net amount payable by the customer, and display the results. Use the Console’s ReadLine method to input the data.
