| Electronic health records | These might include a patient's medical history, prescriptions, immunizations, lab results, allergies, insurance information and more. Making these available to healthcare providers across a secure network improves patient care, reduces the probability of error and increases the health-system's efficiency, helping control costs. |
| Human Genome Project | The Human Genome Project was founded to identify and analyze the 20,000+ genes in DNA. The project used computers to analyze complex genetic data, determine the sequences of the billions of chemical base pairs that make up DNA and store the information in databases, which have been made available over the Internet to researchers in many fields. |
| AMBER Alert | The AMBER (America's Missing: Broadcast Emergency Response) Alert helps find abducted children. Law enforcement notifies TV and radio broadcasters and state officials, who then broadcast on TV, radio, computerized highways, the Internet and wireless devices. AMBER partners with Facebook, those who can "Like" AMBER pages to receive alerts in their feeds. |
| World Community Grid | People worldwide donate their unused computing power by installing a free software program that allows the World Community Grid (http://www.worldcommunitygrid.org/) to harness unused capacity. This computing power over the Internet is used to conduct scientific research projects that make a difference—providing clean water to third-world countries, fighting cancer, growing more nutritious rice for regions fighting hunger and more. |
| Cloud computing | Cloud computing allows you to use software, hardware and information stored in the "cloud"—i.e., accessed on computers via the Internet and available on demand—popular examples are Dropbox, Google Drive and OneDrive. You can increase or decrease resources incrementally to get what you need at any time, so cloud services can be more cost-effective than purchasing expensive hardware to ensure you have enough storage and processing power to meet peak levels. Cloud computing shifts the burden of managing these applications to the service provider, saving businesses time, effort and money. In an online, you use Microsoft Azure—a cloud-computing platform that allows you to develop, manage and distribute apps in the cloud. With Microsoft Azure, your apps store their data in the cloud so that it is available at all times from any of your desktop computers and mobile devices. For information on Microsoft Azure's free and paid visits https://azure.microsoft.com.
| Medical imaging | X-ray computed tomography (CT) scans, also called CAT (computerized axial tomography) scans, take X-rays of the body from hundreds of different angles. Computers are used to adjust the intensity of the X-rays, optimizing the scan for each type of tissue, to combine all of the information to create a 3D image. MRI scanners use a technique called magnetic resonance imaging to produce internal images noninvasively.|
