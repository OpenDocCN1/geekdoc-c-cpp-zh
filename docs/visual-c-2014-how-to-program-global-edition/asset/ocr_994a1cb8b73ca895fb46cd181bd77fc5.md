For example, SumOfDigits(234) = 2 + 3 + 4. Assume that the number entered is greater than or to less than 10 but equal to 99,999. The recursive step should use the relationship

```
((number%10) + SumOfDigits(number/10)).
```

The terminating condition occurs when the number is less than 10. Incorporate this method into an app that allows the user to enter the number.

7.36 (Towers of Hanoi) Every building computer scientist must grapple with that classic problems, and the Towers of Hanoi (Fig. 7.24) is one of the most famous. Legend has that in a tower in the Far East priests are attempting to move a stack of disks from one peg to another. The initial stack has 64 disks threaded from top to bottom by decreasing size. The priests are attempting to move this stack from peg to peg under the constraints that exactly one disk is moved at a time and at no time a larger disk be placed over a smaller disk. A third peg is available for temporarily holding disks. Supposedly, the world will end when priests complete their task, so there's little incentive for us to facilitate their efforts.
