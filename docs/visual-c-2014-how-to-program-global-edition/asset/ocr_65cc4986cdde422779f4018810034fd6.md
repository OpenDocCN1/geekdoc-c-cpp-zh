# Statements

The entire line 10, including Console.WriteLine, the parentheses, and "Welcome-to C# Programming" in the parentheses and the semicolon (;), is called a statement. Most statements end with a semicolon. When the semicolon in line 10 executes, it displays the message Welcome to C# Programming in the console window. A method is typically composed of one or more statements that perform the method's task.

# 3.2.7 Matching Left ({}) and Right ({}) Braces

You might find it difficult when reading or writing an app to match the left and right braces ({ }) that delimit a class's or method's body. Visual Studio can help you locate braces in your code. Simply place the cursor immediately in front of the left brace or after the right brace, and Visual Studio will highlight them.
