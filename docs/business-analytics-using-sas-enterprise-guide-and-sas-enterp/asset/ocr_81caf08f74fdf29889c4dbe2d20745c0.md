# ## Introduction

In today’s highly competitive global economy, organizations are increasingly dependent on their ability to leverage accurate, accessible, actionable data. This increased dependence on data requires a new kind of visionary leadership focused on a smart investment in an enterprise data management system, the right analytic talent to leverage it, and a culture designed to support “big data.”

A culture of big data includes powerful analytic tools. Advanced analytics and reporting software platforms, such as SAS Enterprise Guide and SAS Enterprise Miner, enable the user to extract deep insights from big data and its underlying patterns. This book is designed to guide the business analyst or manager as he or she seeks these insights by providing case studies and everyday examples.

In this chapter, you will learn the importance of clearly defining the objective of any data analysis project.
