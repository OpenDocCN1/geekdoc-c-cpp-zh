Policy is a general concept, applicable to any of the viewpoints. However, as these examples have shown, it is particularly heavily used in the enterprise language, where there is a need to satisfy requirements both for agile business processes and for stable and reliable provision of IT systems.

Policies, then, are a vital tool to let an organization make changes to the behaviour of a system or subsystem in a controlled way, and, by so doing, to let it respond to changing requirements and challenges.
