# 2.2 Identifying Roles

A community is, in essence, a collaboration; the objects fulfilling its roles agree that their objective is to achieve a stated business purpose. In simple cases, the parties performing roles in the contract, and the behaviour expressed in it, can remain the same throughout its lifetime. Alternatively, the contract may define behaviour that modifies the community membership, or changes details of its behaviour. One way of modifying behaviour dynamically is to define mutable policies (see chapter 10) for the community. Note that the community concept can be used to model the structure of a particular organization, or a collaboration between different organizations stated in terms of business contracts, service level agreements (SLAs) or as a federation between different legislative domains (see chapter 11).
