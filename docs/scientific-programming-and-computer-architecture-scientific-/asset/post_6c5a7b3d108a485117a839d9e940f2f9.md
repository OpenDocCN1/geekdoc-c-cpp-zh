Before getting into it, we ask what we want to do. Of course, at one level, the answer is simple: we want to overlap network activity with copying to from buffers. The other member functions have been defined to facilitate this overlap by copy to and from buffers one block at a time and not at all. Similarly, the sending and receiving is done one process at a time and not at once.
