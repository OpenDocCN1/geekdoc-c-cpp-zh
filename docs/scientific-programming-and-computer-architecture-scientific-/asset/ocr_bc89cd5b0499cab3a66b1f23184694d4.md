The of operation counts much close the theoretical lower bound of $0.75 N \log_2 N$ cycles, although MKL gets quite close for $N = 32$. For large $N$, the deviation from the theoretical bound is greater. We may be tempted to conclude that this is because of the memory references at the outer iterations of the FFT. In fact, there is so much cache on the 2.2 GHz AVX machine that all the data for even the problem instance with $N = 1024 \times 1024$ can comfortably fit in L3 cache. It is unclear whether optimizations to hide the streaming items from L3 cache are possible or whether they have been attempted.
