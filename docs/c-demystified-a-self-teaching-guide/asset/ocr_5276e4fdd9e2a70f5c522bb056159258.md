# About the Author

Jeff Kent is an Associate Professor of Computer Science at Los Angeles Valley College in Valley Glen, California. He teaches one of programming languages, including Visual Basic, C++, Java, and when he's feeling masochistic, Assembly, but he teaches+. He also manages a network for a law firm whose employees are guinea pigs for his applications, and as an attorney advice to attorneys whether they want or not. He has also written books on computer programming, including the recent Visual Basic.NET A Beginner's Guide for McGraw-Hill/Osborne.

Jeff has a varied career—or careers. He graduated from UCLA with a Bachelor of Science degree in economics, then obtained a Doctor degree from Loyola (Los Angeles) School of Law, and went on to practice. During this time, when personal computers were a gleam in Bill Gate's eye, Jeff was also a professional chess master, earning a third-place finish in the United States Under-21 Championship, and an international talent.

Jeff finds time to spend with his wife, Devvie, which is not difficult since she is a computer science professor at Valley College. He also acts as personal chauffeur for his teenage daughter, Emily (his older daughter, now has her own driver's license) and in his remaining time enjoys watching international chess tournaments on the Internet. His goal is to resume running marathons, since otherwise, given his losing battle to lose weight, his next book may be Sumo Wrestling Demystified.

> I would like to dedicate this book to my wife, Devvie Schneider Kent. There is no room here to describe how she has helped me in personal and professional life, though I do not mention several ways in the Acknowledgments. She also has been my computer programming teacher in more ways than one; I wouldn't be writing this and other computer programming books if it wasn't for her.

—Jeff Kent
