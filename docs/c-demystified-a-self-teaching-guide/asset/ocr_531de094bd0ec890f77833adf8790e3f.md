# Chapter 14: The Road Ahead: Structures and Classes

# Overview

Late evening, at the end of the final session of a computer programming class I that left off the last chapters do, one of my students asked me: “Where do I go from here?” My suggestion was: “Home.”

My answer, while technically accurate, was not very helpful, so my student attempted to follow up: “No, I mean, what can I expect from the next class?” My prediction was, quoting from Mr. T when he asked his prediction for the outcome of his upcoming rematch with Rocky Balboa III: “Pain.”

That answer too might have been accurate (depending on which teacher he took next) but it also was not very helpful, so I spared my student further stale humor and described to him what I am about to tell you in this chapter. Of course, the student getting a much more condensed explanation since it was late in the evening. Before that to though, I have a question for you: “Why are you reading this book?”
