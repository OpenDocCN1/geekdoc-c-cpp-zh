# Chapter 1: How a C++ Program Works

# Overview

You probably interact with computer programs many times during an average day. When you arrive at work and find out your computer doesn't work, you call tech support. At the other end of the telephone line, a computer program forces you to navigate a voicemail maze and threatens you while you are on perpetual help with repeated insincare messages about how important your computer is, along false promises about how soon you will get through.

When you're finally done with tech support, you decide to take a break and log on to your now-working computer to do battle with giant alien insects from the planet Megazoid. Unfortunately, the network administrator catches you goofing off using another computer program which monitors employee usage. Assuming you are still employed, an accounts payable program generates your payroll check.

On your way home, you decide you need some cash and stop at an ATM, where a computer program confirms (hopefully) you have enough money in your bank account and then instructs the machine to dispense the requested cash and (unfortunately) deducts that amount from your account.

Most people, when they interact with computers as part of their daily routine, don't need to consider what a computer program is how it works. However, a computer programmer should know the answers to these related questions, such as what is a programming language, and how does a C++ program actually work? When you have completed this chapter, you will know the answers to these questions, and also understand how to create and run your own computer program.
