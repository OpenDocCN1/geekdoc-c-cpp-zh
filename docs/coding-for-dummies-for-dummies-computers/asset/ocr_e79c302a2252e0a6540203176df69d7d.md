# *Designing your app*

Your app's visual design incorporates all of your research and describes exactly how your users will interact with every page and feature. Because your users will be accessing your site from desktop, laptop, and mobile devices, you'd want to make sure you create a responsive (multi-device) design and carefully consider how your site will look on all these devices. At this stage of the process, a general web designer, illustrator, or user interface specialist will help create visual designs for the app.

![](bbox=[0.12, 0.34, 0.24, 0.42])

Many responsive app designs and templates can be found on the Internet and used freely. For specific examples, see Chapter 8, or search Google using the query *responsive website design examples*.

There are two types of visual designs (see Figure 3-2):

+   - **Wireframes:** These are low fidelity website drawings that show structurally the ways your content and your site's interface interact.
+   - **Mockups:** These are high fidelity website previews that include colors, images, and logos.
