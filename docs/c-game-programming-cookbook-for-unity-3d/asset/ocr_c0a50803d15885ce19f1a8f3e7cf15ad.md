# 4.6.1 Third-Person Camera

A third-person camera sits behind the player. It is not quite the same as a follow camera; a third-person camera usually orbits the player rather than simply following it. To visualize this, imagine that the camera is tied to a pole attached to the target player. As the player moves, the camera remains to the length of the pole away from but it is free to move the player in a circle (see Figure 4.1).

The position of the camera is usually somewhere behind the player, with a little damping on its horizontal movement applied to it, for a good sense of movement when the player turns. In some third-person camera setups, the camera may be allowed to move in or out of the target player. In some setups, the camera may also be offset from the player a little (e.g., in the Microsoft Studios game Gears of War, the camera is positioned over the right shoulder of the player rather than directly behind).

Below is the Camera_Third_Person script:

```
public class Camera_Third_Person : BaseCameraController
{
    public Transform myTransform;
    public Transform target;
    public float distance = 20.0f;
    public float height = 5.0f;
    public float heightDamping = 2.0f;

    public float lookAtHeight = 0.0f;

    public float rotationSnapTime = 0.3F;

    public float distancesSnapTime;

    public Vector3 lookAtAdjustVector;

    private float usedDistance;

    float wantedRotationAngle;
    float wantedHeight;
```
