For our case study, both top-down and bottom-up approaches will be used at various stages of the design. I find it practical to begin decomposition in a top-down fashion until bulk modules and their interfaces are defined, and then actually design these modules from the bottom up. Before tackling the decomposition of our calculator, let’s first begin by examining the elements of a good decomposition.
