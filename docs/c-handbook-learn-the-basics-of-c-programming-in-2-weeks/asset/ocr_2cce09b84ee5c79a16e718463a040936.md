# **Yogurt Parfait with Chia Seeds**

*Ingredients:*

+   - 2 cups full fat yogurt
+   - 4 Tbsp. chia seeds
+   - 1/2 tsp cinnamon
+   - 1/2 cup unsweetened almond milk
+   - 4 Tbsp. sliced almonds

*Instructions:*

In a bowl, combine the almond milk, chia seeds, and yogurt.

Pour 1 layer of the mixture into 2 glasses, then add half of the almonds and cinnamon to each. Repeat until you have 3 layers each.

Place the glasses inside the refrigerator for about 12 minutes to thicken the parfait. Serve chilled.
