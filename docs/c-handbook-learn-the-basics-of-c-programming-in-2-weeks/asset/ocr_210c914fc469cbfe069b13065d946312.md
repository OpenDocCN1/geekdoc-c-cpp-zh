Oregano: 1 teaspoon

+   - Chili powder: 1 or 2 tablespoons
+   - Chipotle Tabasco sauce: 1 tablespoon
+   - Kidney beans and pinto beans: 1 can each or 14 oz. each
+   - Cornmeal: 1/4th cup
+   - Water: half cup
+   - Fritos: 1 packet

# Method:

Although the list of ingredients is fairly long, the method is very simple. The first step is to cook the beef in a pot until it gets brown and then add garlic to it after draining the beef. After mixing it for 2 minutes, shift the ingredients in a crock pot and add the rest of the ingredients except cornmeal, Fritos and water. The crock pot will take around 8 hours to cook, but before 30 minutes of the cooking time, you may add water and cornmeal in the dish. Once the dish is ready, garnish it with ingredients such as cheddar cheese, Fritos and sour cream.
