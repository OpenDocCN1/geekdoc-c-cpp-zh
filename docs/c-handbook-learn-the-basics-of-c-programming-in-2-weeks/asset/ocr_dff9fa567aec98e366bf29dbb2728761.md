# Recipe # 4 - Kale Green Smoothie Surprise

The ingredients include:

+   - 1 cups kale
+   - ½ cup coconut milk
+   - 1 ½ cups pineapple
+   - 1 ripe small banana

In place of the banana, an apple can be used. It will yield the same health benefits. At the same time, the taste will also be delightful.
