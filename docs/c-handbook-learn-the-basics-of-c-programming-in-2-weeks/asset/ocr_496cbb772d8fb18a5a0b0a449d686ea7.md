# Chapter 1: What is SEO and Why is it Important to your Website?

The internet is an ocean of information. It is a network of interconnected computers from all around the world. It allows us to access information from computers halfway around the world.

By itself however, the internet is a big mess. It is full of webpages that cater to the needs to billions of individuals. Early on, engineers have been looking for a tool that can organize all the information in the web and help people find what they are looking for in the least amount of time.

The search engine is that tool.

This is the function of the search engine. They “crawl” through as many webpages as they can by making artificial intelligence visit webpages and collect information. They add information about the crawled pages in an index. When a person types a search query, the search engine runs through all the crawled pages and brings back a list of pages that fits the query. It then organizes these pages according to their relevance to the query of the user.

Billions of people use search engines monthly. Some of them may be interested in your website. In order to drive traffic into your website, you want your webpages to rank high in the search results of your target visitor.
