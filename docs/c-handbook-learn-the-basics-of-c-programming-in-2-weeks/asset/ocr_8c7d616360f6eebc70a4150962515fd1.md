# Day 2

For the second day, we have recommended an even easy recipe. The simple recipe is Russian Apricot Chicken which is not only mouth-watering and appealing in taste, it is also fairly simple and quick to make. The best part about the recipe is the number of ingredients you will require. It may be hard to believe but you can create a super tasty recipe with just four easy ingredients. Low ingredients with an even lower cooking time makes this dish an all-time favorite one. The recipe is desired by all those women who want tasty homemade dinners but do not have enough time to make a tough dish.
