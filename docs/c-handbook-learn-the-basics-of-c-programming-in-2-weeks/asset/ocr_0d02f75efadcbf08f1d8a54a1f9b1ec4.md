# Recipe # 8 - Collard Greens Smoothie Delight

Ingredients include:

+   - 2 cups collard greens
+   - 2 tablespoons freshly squeezed lime juice
+   - 1 cup green grapes
+   - ½ cup mango

Do not over blend the ingredients. In case collard greens are not available, spinach can be used. Add more water to make it less thick. Chill and serve cool.
