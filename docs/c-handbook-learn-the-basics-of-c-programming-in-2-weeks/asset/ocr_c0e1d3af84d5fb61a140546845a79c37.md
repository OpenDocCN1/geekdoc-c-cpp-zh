# Chapter 2: Incredible Ingredients

Here I will provide you with a list of ingredients that you will need to stock up on. Some may be new to you, but not for long:

+   - Frozen broth (Chicken, Beef, or Vegetable)
+   - Dried and fresh herbs and spices (my favourite are oregano, turmeric and cinnamon)
+   - Coconut milk and cream
+   - Coconut oil, olive oil, avocado oil
+   - Fresh lemons
+   - Fresh garlic and ginger
+   - Avocados
+   - Dijon mustard
+   - Honey
+   - Crushed tomatoes or tomato puree
+   - Greek yogurt (great source of protein)
+   - A bar of 100% dark chocolate (for your cheat snack)
+   - Plenty of good quality butter
