There are 2 important parts to remember here …

+   - You MUST only recommend products that you believe in and will benefit others, and not just to make money. You must always put your visitors first, so only recommend products and services that you feel very comfortable with.
+   - Don ’ t over promote to your list. You must provide an an even balance of value and promoting. You need to give enough value to your visitors so they will want to keep coming back, and the number one way to lose a visitor is by just selling them stuff.

The main lesson here is, the most important part of building a list is generating trust and a relationship with your visitors. You must look out for them as much as, or even more than yourself.
