# Method:

Although the list of ingredients seems pretty long, the method of cooking is fairly simple. The first step is to add beef broth with sesame oil, garlic, brown sugar and soy sauce in the cooker and whisk it. Then add the beef and whisk it again. Next step is to leave the ingredients in the cooker for around 6 hours after which you have to add water and corn starch and let the dish cook for another half an hour so the sauce gets cooked properly. In the end just add broccoli, cook it for few minutes and finally enjoy the delicious Chinese dish with rice. The dish only requires a few minutes in the beginning and a few minutes in the end of the cooking time. The time in between is all there for you to relax and complete your unfinished jobs.
