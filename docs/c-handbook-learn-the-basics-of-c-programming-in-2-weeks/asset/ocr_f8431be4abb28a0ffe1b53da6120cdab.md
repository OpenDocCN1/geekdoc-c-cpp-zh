# ***Strawberry Spinach Salad***

Ingredients

+   1. 1 bunch young or baby spinach leaves
+   2. 2 cups strawberries — very thinly sliced
+   3. 2 cooked chicken breasts
+   4. 1/2 cup very crispy and crumbled bacon
+   5. 1 Tbsp honey
+   6. 1 Tbsp olive oil
+   7. 1 Tbsp lemon juice

Instructions

Combine all the ingredients. Serve immediately to avoid the salad getting soggy. Looks beautiful with fresh pansy flowers to garnish — ideal for a summer barbecue.
