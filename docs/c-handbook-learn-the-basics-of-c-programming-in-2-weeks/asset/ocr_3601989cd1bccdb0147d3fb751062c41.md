# Green Frappe

Want to indulge yourself with a drink that will make you feel like you’re sipping at your leisure in a high end coffee shop? Then whip up this green frappe and get your daily dose of rich green tea flavor, without the tedious process of brewing tea bags and waiting for your drink to cool down. The matcha powder is packed with the same health benefits, minus the prep time.

Fun and tasty ingredients:

+   - 1 teaspoon green tea matcha powder
+   - ½ tablespoon organic honey
+   - 1 cup coconut milk
+   - Ice (optional)

Blend all the ingredients until you get a creamy frothy drink. Since you’ll only be using a few key ingredients, you can enjoy the richness of this drink at just about any time that you please. The green frappe is best enjoyed alone in the hot summer months or with friends as you talk about your amazing weight loss journey so far.
