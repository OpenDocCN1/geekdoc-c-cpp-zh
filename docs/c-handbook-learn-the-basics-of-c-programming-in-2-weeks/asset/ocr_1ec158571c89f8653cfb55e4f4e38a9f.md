# Cheese Chips

Serves: 1

200 grams of shredded cheese

Method:

Cut some non-waxed baking paper, into the same size as the turntable in your microwave.

Now place the paper on the turntable and add small piles of cheese.

Turn the microwave onto full power for about a minute and a half, or until the cheese has turned crispy. Allow to cool, and serve.
