# *Recipe # 5 – Fat-Burning Green Tea Smoothie*

Ingredients are:

+   - ¾ cup green tea
+   - 2-3 tablespoons of lemon juice
+   - 2 teaspoons of agave nectar
+   - 1/8 teaspoon of cayenne pepper
+   - 1 small pear with the skin on
+   - 2 tablespoons of plain yogurt
+   - 6-8 ice cubes

Expect to lose weight faster with this smoothie. Green tea, the main ingredient, is known to boost metabolism hence increasing its fat-burning abilities.
