# # Swedish Meatballs

Makes: 30

# Ingredients:

+   - 500 grams of lean beef
+   - 1 tablespoon of butter
+   - 1 cooked egg
+   - Half a teaspoon of black pepper
+   - 1 and a half teaspoons of allspice
+   - 1 and a half teaspoons of nutmeg
+   - 1 and a half teaspoons of salt
+   - 200 grams of ricotta
+   - 114 grams of Swiss cheese, grated

# Method:

Add the butter to a pan and heat, then sauté the onions until they are clear. Allow to cook for about 10 minutes. Now take the Swiss cheese and place in a blender, and blend until they resemble bread crumbs.
