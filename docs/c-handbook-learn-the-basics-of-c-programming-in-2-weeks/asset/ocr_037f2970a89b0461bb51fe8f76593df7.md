# Chapter 5: Super Snacks

# Fried Avocado Wedges

_Ingredients:_

+   - 2 eggs
+   - 2 avocados
+   - 2 Tbsp. heavy cream
+   - 1/2 tsp sea salt
+   - 2/3 tsp freshly ground black pepper
+   - 1/3 cup ground pork rinds
+   - 1/2 cup shredded Parmesan cheese
+   - 1/2 tsp garlic powder
+   - 1/3 tsp onion powder
+   - Olive oil, for frying

_Instructions:_

Pour about 1 1/2 inches of oil into a fry pan. Place over medium low heat and preheat until 375 degrees F.

Meanwhile, beat the eggs and heavy cream together in a bowl.

On a plate, combine the pork rinds, garlic and onion powders, and Parmesan
