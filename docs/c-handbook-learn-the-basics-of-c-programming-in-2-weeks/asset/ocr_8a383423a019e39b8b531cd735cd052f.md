# Drinking Plenty Of Water

Drinking plenty of water is an integral part of the tea cleansing program. We should all drink more water anyway, but it is especially important now. Drink eight glasses of water each and every day. Water is ideal for helping to flush out those nasty toxins, but it can also help to reduce stress and promote a good nights’ sleep. What’s more is if you’re constipated, drinking water can help to relieve the issue.

A great way for you to make sure you get enough water every day, is to have a glass of water next to or near you, so you can get to it easily.
