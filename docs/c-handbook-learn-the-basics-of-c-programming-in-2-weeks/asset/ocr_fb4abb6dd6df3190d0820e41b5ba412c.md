# If You Want To Do The Plan Again

Once you have undertook the Paleo diet plan for 14 days, you will no doubt be feeling and looking better. Chances are you’ve lost a bit of weight, but you’re likely to have more energy and you’re probably sleeping better too.

The great thing about the Paleo diet plan is that it’s completely sustainable in the long-term. This is because it’s a very healthy diet that your body will love, and you won’t be missing out on any nutrients.

My advice is to enjoy taking part in the Paleo diet plan, but don’t forget to treat yourself from time to time. Treating yourself will make you feel like you’ve been rewarded, but it will also stop you from getting bored too.
