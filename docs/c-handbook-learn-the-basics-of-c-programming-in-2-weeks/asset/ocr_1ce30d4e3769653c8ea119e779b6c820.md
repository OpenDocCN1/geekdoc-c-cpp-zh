# Baths

For healthy, soothing and relaxing baths, essential oils are added to dispersing agents before added to warm bath water. Generally, aromatherapy baths are prepared by mixing 2 to 12 drops of essential oils to 1 teaspoon of whole milk, honey or vegetable (carrier) oil.

Aromatherapy baths are great for:

+   - Reducing anxiety/stress
+   - Soothing physical or mental fatigue
+   - Alleviating muscular pains, tension and aches
+   - Enhancing circulation of lymph
+   - Stimulating better blood circulation
+   - Reducing stiffness and pain
+   - Improving health and skin tone
+   - Supporting skin detoxification
