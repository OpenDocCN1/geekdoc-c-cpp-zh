# Beef Roast

This recipe of beef roast is known for its good taste and its ease of cooking. It will give you a lot of flavor gravy once it is ready so you should not expect the dish to be all dry and boring. You may also enjoy the dish with mashed potatoes because the potatoes will enhance the taste of the dish even more. Following are the ingredients that you will require to make this dish:

+   - Shoulder roast: 4 lbs.
+   - Cream of mushroom soup: 1 can
+   - Cream of potato soup: 2 cans
+   - Onion soup mix: 1 packet
+   - Dr. Pepper: 2 cups
+   - Creole: around 1 teaspoon for seasoning.

# Method:

The method is extremely simple. You just have to mix all the ingredients in a crock pot and forget about the dinner for 6 to 8 hours. The crock pot will cook on low and will return a mouthwatering dish once you are back to home after a tiring day at work.
