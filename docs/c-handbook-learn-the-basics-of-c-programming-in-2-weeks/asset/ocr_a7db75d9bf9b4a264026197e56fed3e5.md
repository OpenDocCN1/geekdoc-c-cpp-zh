# Peppermint tea

Ingredients: (Serves 4)

+   - Half a cup of peppermint leaves
+   - 4 cups of water
+   - 3 tablespoons of honey

# Method

+   - Boil the water in a pan
+   - Add the peppermint leaves and turn off the heat
+   - Allow to steep for approximately 5 minutes
+   - Strain the tea into a cup or mug
+   - Divide the honey between the cups
+   - Serve
