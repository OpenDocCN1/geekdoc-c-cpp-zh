# Sugar

Unless you can say for sure that you’re able to work off the sugar you consume, try to avoid it. If you don’t work off the sugar, it’s going to be stored as fat, and that’s the last thing that you want.

# Salt

We all eat too much salt, without realizing how bad it is for us. Salt can aid fluid retention, and it’s also well-known for raising your blood pressure. Cut your salt intake in half, and have no more than 6 grams of it per day. We all need a little salt in our diet, just try not to have too much of it.
