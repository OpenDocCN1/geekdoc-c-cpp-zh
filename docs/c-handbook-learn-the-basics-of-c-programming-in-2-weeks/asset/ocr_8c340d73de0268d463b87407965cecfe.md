# Coconut, Banana and Chocolate Smoothie

Serves 1

Ingredients:

+   - 1 teaspoon of grated coconut
+   - 240ml of coconut water
+   - 1 teaspoon of cocoa powder
+   - A handful of spinach
+   - 1 banana

Method:

Place all of the ingredients into a juicer or blender, and blend until they have all mixed together. Pour into a glass, serve.
