# Chapter 8 – DASH Diet Dessert Ideas

# Recipe 1: Tropical Skewers

This is a delicious and healthy sweet treat to calm down your sweet cravings.

You will need:

+   - 10 pieces strawberries
+   - 25 pieces blackberries
+   - A cup of pineapple, cubed
+   - A cup of kiwi, cubed
+   - Skewers
+   - 1 cup zero fat yoghurt
+   - 240 grams zero fat cream cheese
+   - 2 spoons honey

How to Prepare:

+   - Simply place the fruits in the skewers. Be creative.
+   - Combine the cream cheese, honey and yoghurt. Dip the fruits and enjoy!
