# Introduction

If you are looking for ways to lose weight, reduce fat, and increase energy levels, you are in the right place. The Paleo diet is a low carb diet, with a high amount of protein and a lot of vegetables. This diet is sometimes referred to as the “Caveman Diet” because it is basically anything that was eaten by caveman. This diet is so effective because it forces your body to burn fats for energy, instead of glucose from carbs.
