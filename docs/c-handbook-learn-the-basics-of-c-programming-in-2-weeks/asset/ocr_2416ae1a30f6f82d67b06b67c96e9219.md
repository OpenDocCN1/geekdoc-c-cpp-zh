# Essential Oil Blends that Energizes

*To increase energy levels*

Mix equal amounts of energizing earthy essential oils like ginger and frankincense oils
with green herbal oils such as rosemary, peppermint and basil.

*To invigorate the mind and body*

Mix 4 drops of wild orange essential oil and 4 drops of peppermint oil. Inhale by placing a
few drops of the blend to a handkerchief or to a diffuser.

*To increase performance and vigor*

Mix 2 drops each of grapefruit and peppermint essential oils.

*To boost overall energy levels within you and the environment*

Mix 8 drops of grapefruit oil, 4 drops of lavender oil, 4 drops of lemon essential oil, and 2
drops of basil essential oil.
