# Recipe 2: Shredded Pork and Chile

This is an incredible pork recipe, which is similar to what you would eat in a Mexican diner.

You will need:

+   - 1 kilogram pork loin, cut into pieces (about 2 inches)
+   - 1 cup green chili, diced
+   - 4 cups water
+   - 2 cups rice
+   - Half a cup chipotle sauce
+   - 3 tablespoon lime juice
+   - A handful of chopped cilantro
+   - 4 garlic cloves, minced
+   - A pinch of salt
