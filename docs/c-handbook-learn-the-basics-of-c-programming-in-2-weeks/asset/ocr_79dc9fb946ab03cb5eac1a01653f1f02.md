# How to Prepare:

+   - Season the pork with salt and put in the crock pot. Add in the garlic and peppers on top of the meat.
+   - Pout half a cup of water and chipotle.
+   - Cook the pork for 7 hours under low heat.
+   - In another pot, cook the rice using the remaining water, add in the cilantro and lime juice. Simmer for 20 to 25 minutes on low heat.
+   - Shred the cooked pork using 2 forks. Put back the pork in the crock pot to let it absorb the sauce. Serve over the cooked zesty rice. Enjoy!
