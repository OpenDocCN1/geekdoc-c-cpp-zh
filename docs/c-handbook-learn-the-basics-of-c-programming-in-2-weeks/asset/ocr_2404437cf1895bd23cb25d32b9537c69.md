# Day 11

For the 11th day, try something traditional and different. For the 11th day we suggest you go for Hungarian goulash which is a traditional yet a delicious dinner for your family. The dish has a lot of traditional flavors and your taste buds will love a change from the usual chicken and beef dinners. The best part about the dish is that it takes around 10 hours to get completed. So you will have a lot of time to complete all your chores before the dinner gets ready. Once you try this dish, you will definitely fall in love with it.
