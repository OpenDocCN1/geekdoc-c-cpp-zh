# Recipe 2: Crockpot Choco Cake

Chocolate cake is a classic dessert, and you can easily make it using your crockpot.

# You will need:

+   - 2 cups all purpose flour
+   - 1 cup cocoa powder
+   - 2 cups sugar
+   - 2 teaspoons baking powder
+   - 2 teaspoons baking soda
+   - 1 spoon vanilla extract
+   - 1 ½ cup of milk
+   - 3 spoons vegetable oil
+   - 1 cup hot water

# How to Prepare:

+   - Combine all the dry ingredients in one bowl, and all the wet ingredients in another bowl.
+   - Pour the wet ones to the dry ingredients along with the hot water and whisk thoroughly.
+   - Pour the batter into the pot.
+   - Cook for 3 to 4 hours on low heat. Allow the cake to rest for 20 to 40 minutes. Enjoy!
