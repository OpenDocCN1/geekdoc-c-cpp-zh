# # Cucumber, lime and watermelon smoothie

+   - 2 cucumbers
+   - The juice of 1 lime
+   - A quarter of a watermelon

Juice the ingredients until they are smooth. Pour the smoothie into a glass and serve.

Depending on the type of smoothie maker or juicer that you use, you may need to chop up your ingredients first. This is to ensure you do not damage the blades, and/or that your smoothie is as smooth as it can be.

Admittedly, some green smoothie recipes may take a bit of getting used to. If you’re not a fan of green vegetables, then you may want to slowly introduce them into your day. Not everyone drinks green smoothies, some people avoid them all together.
