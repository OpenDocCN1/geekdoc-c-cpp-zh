# Saturday

If you’ve followed the schedule, by now you should be feeling a bit healthier and a little less famished. Celebrate the weekend by eating well balanced meals. Your meals should contain healthy servings of carbs, protein, and healthy fats. Settle for any less and you’re bound to feel hungry in a couple of hours. Satisfy your craving for meat and fat by having a healthy serving of white fish in a light olive oil sauce.

# Sundays

Get into the habit of planning your meals from this day on so that you have all your nutritional needs covered. Schedule your mealtimes so that you don’t skip meals. Skipping meals won’t help you lose weight, if anything; it will only make you hungrier for your next meal. And when your body starts to feel deprived, you’ll tend to eat more to make up for the meal you skipped.

Start a routine that will enable you to eat your main meals at the same time every day. You can also include small healthy snacks in the afternoon if you need something to keep your energy levels up, and don’t forget to finish your meal with a cup of your favorite detox tea.
