# **_Beefy Zucchini_**

# Ingredients

+   - 2 cups of water
+   - 1 pound ground beef
+   - 2 large zucchini
+   - 1/2 tsp of curry
+   - 1 can of no-salt tomato sauce

# Directions:

Brown the ground beef and remove the excess fat. Cut the zucchini into chunks and boil until soft. Mix this with meat and add water, spices and the can of tomato sauce.

Let it boil and reduce the heat. Let it sit for a few minutes to take in the spices then serve.
