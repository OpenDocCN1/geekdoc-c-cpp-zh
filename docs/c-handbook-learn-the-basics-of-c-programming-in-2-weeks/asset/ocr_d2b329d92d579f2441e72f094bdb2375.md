# Sesame Chicken

Ingredients:

+   4 Tbsp soy sauce, low sodium
+   1 Tbsp olive oil
+   2 chicken breasts
+   1 Tbsp of sesame seeds
+   2 Tbsp of honey
+   2 cups broccoli, cooked

Directions:

Mix soy sauce, honey and sesame seeds in a small bowl. Steam broccoli, set aside to drain well, cube chicken into bite sized pieces and fry in olive oil. Add broccoli to pan with chicken, then pour over sauce. Stir and serve with some potatoes.
