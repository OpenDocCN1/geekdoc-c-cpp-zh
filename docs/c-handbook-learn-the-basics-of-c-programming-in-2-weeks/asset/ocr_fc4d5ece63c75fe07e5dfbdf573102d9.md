# Recipe 3: Melon Coolers

This is best consumed when chilled. You may also use watermelon or honeydew.

You will need:

+   - 2 to 3 cups cantaloupe, cubed
+   - A cup of fresh orange juice
+   - A cup of zero fat yoghurt

How to Prepare:

+   - Place all the ingredients in a blender and pulse until smooth.
+   - Put in the fridge for at least 2 hours. Enjoy!
