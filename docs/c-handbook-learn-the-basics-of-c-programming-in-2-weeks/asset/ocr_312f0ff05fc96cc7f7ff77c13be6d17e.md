# Chorizo and Cauliflower Soup

Serves: 3

Ingredients:

+   - Half a cauliflower, chopped
+   - Half an onion, chopped
+   - Half a medium sized Chorizo sausage, chopped
+   - Half a spring onion, chopped
+   - Half a turnip, chopped
+   - A dash of salt
+   - 240mls of chicken stock
+   - 1 tablespoon of butter

Grease a soup bowl, with a tablespoon of butter, and add the onion and cook on a medium to high heat until it has browned. Add the cauliflower, and cook for another 5 minutes, while stirring.

Add the chicken stock to the cauliflower, and place a lid on the bowl. Now add half the turnip and half of the chorizo. Cook until it’s crispy (About 10 minutes). Now place half of the chorizo into a blender, and blend until smooth. Season with salt, and then add the rest of the turnip and chorizo. Serve.
