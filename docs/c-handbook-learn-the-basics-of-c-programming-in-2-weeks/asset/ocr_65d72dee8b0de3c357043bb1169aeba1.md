# ### Carrot seed Oil

Rich in carotenoids, carrot seed oil has great antioxidant properties. This boosts the body’s natural protective immune response against the harmful UV rays of the sun. This action promotes greater protection against sun damage. Carotenoids stimulate skin cell rejuvenation. These also support the detoxification process that clears the skin of any waste, dirt and toxins. These properties all contribute to the effectiveness of carrot seed oil in promoting skin health. With these properties, carrot seed oil can also help in the treatment of chronic skin problems like psoriasis and eczema.
