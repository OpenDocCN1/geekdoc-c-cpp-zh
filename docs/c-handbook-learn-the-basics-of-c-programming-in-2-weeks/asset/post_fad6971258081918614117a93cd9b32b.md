Whenever anyone begins a new weight loss or diet plan, they tend to lose a nice amount of weight within a short space of time. This is only natural, but it’s not sustainable. Your weight loss will slow down, but that does not mean you won’t lose any more weight.

Stick with the plan, even if you don’t appear to be losing any more weight, as it’s likely to be doing you some good.

Some people like to do the 14 day smoothie plan more than once. I hasten to add that during those few weeks, you may want to eat a healthy balanced diet, rather than going back to old ways of eating, if you consumed a lot of fats, sugar and salt.

Once you’ve stuck to a healthy balanced diet for a few weeks, try the 14 day smoothie plan again.

If you do decide to stick with the plan for longer than 14 days, please make sure that you’re eating proper meals, and your diet is a healthy one.
