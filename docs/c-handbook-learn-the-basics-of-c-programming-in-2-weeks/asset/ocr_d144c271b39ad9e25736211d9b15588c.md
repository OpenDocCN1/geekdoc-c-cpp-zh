Serves: 2

# Ingredients:

+   - 4 apples, sliced
+   - A dash of pumpkin spice
+   - A dash of cinnamon

# Method:

Place the apple slices on a cookie sheet, and sprinkle with cinnamon. Place in the oven and cook for 1 hour on 230 Fahrenheit. Turn the apple slices over, and sprinkle cinnamon on the other side, and cook at 200 Fahrenheit for 1 hour.

Now turn the oven off, and keep the apples in there for 1 or 2 hours until they are crisp. Remove the apples from the oven, sprinkle with the pumpkin spice and serve.
