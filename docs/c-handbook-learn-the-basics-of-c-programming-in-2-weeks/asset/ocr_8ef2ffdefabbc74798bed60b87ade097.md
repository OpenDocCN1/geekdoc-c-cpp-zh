# *Recipe # 9 – Protein Power Green Smoothie*

Ingredients include:

+   - ¼ cup frozen mango
+   - ½ cup red grapefruit juice
+   - 1 large apple (cored and chopped)
+   - 1 cup Kale
+   - 1 cup cucumber
+   - ½ cup celery
+   - 3 tablespoons hemp hearts
+   - 1/8 cup mint leaves (fresh)

Optional ingredients:

+   - ice cubes
+   - ½ tablespoon virgin coconut oil

Blend all ingredients until smooth. If the mixture is too thick, add a little bit of water. Pour into a glass and enjoy. This is a great smoothie drink after a strenuous exercise.
