# # Chapter 4: Ketogenic Breakfast Recipes

Enjoy a delicious and nutritious breakfast every single morning. If you are a little short on time in the mornings, making your breakfast the night before could help a great deal.

# ## Cream Cheese Pancakes

Makes: 2

Ingredients:

+   - 2 eggs
+   - 1 teaspoon of Stevia
+   - 2-3 tablespoons of cream
+   - Half a teaspoon of cinnamon
+   - 1 tablespoon of coconut flour
+   - A small slice of butter
+   - Maple syrup

Method:

Place all of the ingredients apart from the butter and maple syrup into a bowl, and stir until combined and smooth. Heat a pan or a skillet, and add the butter and melt on a medium to high heat. Cook on each side until they are golden brown.

Remove from the heat and serve with the maple syrup.
