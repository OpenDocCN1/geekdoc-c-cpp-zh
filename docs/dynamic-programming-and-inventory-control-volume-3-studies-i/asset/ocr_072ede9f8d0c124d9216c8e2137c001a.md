\(\int_R^{+\infty} m(x) dx \ge (z_R(x_0, t) - \rho^{\frac{1}{2}} C_{s_0, M, \delta}) \int_{x_0 - \rho}^{x_0 + \rho} m dx ,\)

and thus

We first prove the continuity of \(z(x,t)\) for \(t\) fixed positive, inside \((s, +\infty)\). It is a direct consequence of the estimate (15.6.27). Indeed for any fixed positive \(t\) and any compact \([s_0, M]\) \(z(x,t)\) has its partial derivative in \(x\) bounded in \(L^2(s_0, M)\).
