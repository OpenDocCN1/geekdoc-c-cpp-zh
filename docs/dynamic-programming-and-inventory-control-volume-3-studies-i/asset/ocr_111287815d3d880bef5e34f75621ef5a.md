This relation implies

$$ \lambda \ge \inf_V \limsup_{N \to \infty} \frac{1}{N} \sum_{n=1}^N E^{V,i}(y_n, v_n). $$

Collecting results, we obtain

$$ \lambda = \inf_V J_t(V) = J_t(\hat{V}). $$

Similarly from the same initial relations we have also

$$ \lambda \alpha^{n-1}(1-\alpha) + \alpha^{n-1}(1-\alpha) E^{V,i} z(y_n) \\ \le \alpha^{n-1}(1-\alpha) E^{V,i} l(y_n, v_n) + \alpha^n (1-\alpha) E^{V,i} z(y_{n+1}) \\ +\alpha^{n-1}(1-\alpha)^2 E^{V,i} z(y_{n+1}) $$

Adding up we obtain

$$ \lambda + (1-\alpha)(z) \le (1-\alpha) \sum_{n=1}^\infty \alpha^{n-1} E^{V,i} l(y_n, v_n) + \sum_{n=1}^\infty \alpha^{n-1}(1-\alpha)^2 E^{V,i} z(y_{n+1}). $$

Letting \(\alpha \to 1\) and using the fact that \(z\) is bounded, we deduce

$$ \lambda \le \liminf_{\alpha \to 1} (1-\alpha) \sum_{n=1}^\infty \alpha^{n-1} E^{V,i} l(y_n, v_n) = J_t(V). $$

On the other hand one has also

$$ \lambda = \lim_{\alpha \to 1} (1-\alpha) \sum_{n=1}^\infty \alpha^{n-1} E^{V,i} l(y_n, v_n) = J_t(\hat{V}), $$

which completes the proof of the desired result.
