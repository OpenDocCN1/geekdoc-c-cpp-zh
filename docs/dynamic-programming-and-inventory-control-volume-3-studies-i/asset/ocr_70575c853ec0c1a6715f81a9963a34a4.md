It follows that \( L'_{\varpi}(x) \) vanishes at a positive point \( \tilde{x} \) and

\( L'_{\varpi}(x) > 0, \forall 0 \le x < \tilde{x}, \quad L'_{\varpi}(x) < 0, \forall x > \tilde{x} \)

Since \( L_{\varpi}(0) = 0 \) and \( L_{\varpi}(+\infty) = -\infty \) the function \( L_{\varpi}(x) \) has a maximum at \( \tilde{x} \) and has a unique 0 at a point \( x^* > \tilde{x} \). For \( x > x^* \) it is strictly negative, and for \( 0 < x < x^* \) it is positive. We check easily that the right hand side of (17.5.8) is negative and smaller than \( L_{\varpi}(b) \). Therefore there exists one and only one \( a_{\varpi}(b) \) solution of (17.5.8).
