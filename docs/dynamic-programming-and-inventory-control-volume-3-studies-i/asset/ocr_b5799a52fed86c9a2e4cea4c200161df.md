We then call the functional \( J(V) \) as \( J_{x,\sigma,\eta}(V) \), to emphasize the initial conditions. We define

(13.4.20) \( u(x, \sigma, \eta) = \inf_V J_{x,\sigma,\eta}(V) \).

If \( M=0 \), then \( \sigma_t = 0, \eta_t = 0 \) and the problem reduces to the standard one with no delay in the information studied in Chapter 9, section 9.3.
