We define next successively \( T_{n,x} \) as follows:

\( T_{0,x} = \tau_x; \quad T_{n+1,x} = T_{n,x} + \tau_{n,x} \)

\( \tau_{n,x} = \inf \{t > 0 | D(T_{n,x} + t) - D(T_{n,x}) \ge Q \} \)

We define \( y_x(t) \) by the relations

\( y_x(t) = x - D(t), t < \tau_x, \)
\( y_x(t) = S - D(t) + \tau(T_{n,x}), T_{n,x} \le t < T_{n+1,x} \)
\( y_x(T_{n,x}) = S; x_i(T_{n,x} - 0) \le s \)

We consider

\( u_\alpha(x) = E \int_0^\infty \exp -\alpha t \phi(y_x(t)) dt. \)

In the above notation, we do not write explicitly \( s, S \). On the other hand we note explicitly \( \alpha \), since we will let \( \alpha \) tend to 0. Note also that

\( u_\alpha(S) = C_\alpha(s, Q) = C_\alpha. \)

In the same way we have obtained the formula for \( C_\alpha \), we can check the following formula

\( u_\alpha(x) = E \int_0^{\tau_x} \exp -\alpha t \phi(x - D(t)) dt + C_\alpha E \exp -\alpha \tau_x \)

From the preceding formula we notice that

\( x \le s \Rightarrow \tau_x = 0 \Rightarrow u_\alpha(x) = C_\alpha. \)

A key point now is to obtain the probability distribution of \( \tau_x \). To simplify a little we will assume \( x, s \) integers. We have

\( P(\tau_x \le t) = P(D(t) \ge x - s) = \exp -\lambda t \sum_{j=x-s}^\infty \frac{(\lambda t)^j}{j!}. \)

This probability distribution has a density given by

\( \frac{d}{dt} P(\tau_x \le t) = \lambda \exp -\lambda t \frac{(\lambda t)^{x-s-1}}{(x-s-1)!}. \)

This has sense whenever \( x - s \ge 1 \) If \( x = s \) then

\( \frac{d}{dt} P(\tau_x \le t) = \lambda \exp -\lambda t, \)

and the density is 0 for \( x < s \).

It is then possible to compute the function \( E \exp -\alpha \tau_x \). We leave as an exercise to show that

\( E \exp -\alpha \tau_x = \left( \frac{\lambda}{\alpha + \lambda} \right)^{x-s}. \)

Next we can write

\( E \int_0^{\tau_x} \exp -\alpha t \phi(x - D(t)) dt = E \int_0^\infty \exp -\alpha t \mathbb{I}_{\{\tau_x > t\}} \phi(x - D(t)) dt \)

\( E \int_0^{\tau_x} \exp -\alpha t \phi(x - D(t)) dt = E \int_0^\infty \exp -\alpha t \mathbb{I}_{\{D(t) < x - s\}} \phi(x - D(t)) dt. \)
