the cube represents one of the displayable colors. The cube's main diagonal connecting the points (0, 0, 0) and (1, 1, 1) is the locus of all the grayscale values. Brighter colors are located closer to the cube's outer faces (visible in the figure) whereas darker colors are located closer to the origin (hidden in the figure). RGB color representations are handy from an implementation perspective, as most graphics software, such as OpenGL and image storage formats, manipulate colors in this way.
