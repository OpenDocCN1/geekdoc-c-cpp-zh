+   Orientation: The orientation of an axis indicates the direction along which the variation of its attribute is maximal in the point cloud. In our example, we see thus that the *longitude* and *latitude* attributes vary mainly in the horizontal plane, while the *median house value* varies along the vertical direction.

This helps users understand what the plot can, and cannot, show. For instance, users should not try reasoning about the variance of the *#rooms* attribute in the plot in Figure 11.33, since this axis is simply not visible from the chosen viewpoint.

+   Length: The length value of an axis indicates how much variance of the respective attribute is visible in the plot. Long axes indicate thus attributes which have a large variance in the projection, while short axes indicate attributes having lesser variance. For instance, in our plot, we see that *median house value* has the largest variance.
