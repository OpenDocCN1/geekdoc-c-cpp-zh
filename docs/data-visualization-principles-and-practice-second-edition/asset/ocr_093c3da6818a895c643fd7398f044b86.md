The missing neighbors plots tell us a different story. By looking at the size and color of the depicted bundles, we see that LSP and Pekalska have many more important missing neighbors than PLMP, while LAMP has the fewest missing neighbors. In all cases, we see bundles that connect borders of the projected point-set. This confirms that all these projections focus on the accurate placement of close points and dedicate less attention to the placement of far-away points. We also see that the missing neighbors are spread differently over the data: For LAMP, there are no bundles connecting to the bottom-right point group. This shows that all points in this group are dissimilar from all other points. In contrast, LSP, PLMP, and Pekalska all have bundles connecting to this group. This shows that these projections place this group too far away to the remaining points.
