In some other cases, however, we want to visualize the scalar values of all, not just a few, of the data points of a 3D dataset. Volume-rendering techniques, presented in Chapter 10, are an answer to this demand.

Besides these fundamental techniques, many different scalar visualization methods exist. For example, specific methods exist for the visualization of scalar data defined over images and volumes, i.e., two-dimensional and three-dimensional uniform grids with scalar attributes, respectively. These methods are discussed further in more detail in Chapter 9 (image visualization) and Chapter 10 (volume visualization).
