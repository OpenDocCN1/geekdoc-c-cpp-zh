Cover Image: The cover shows the combination of scientific visualization and information visualization techniques for the exploration of the quality of a dimensionality reduction (DR) algorithm for multivariate data. A 19-dimensional dataset is projected to 2D point cloud. False-positive projection errors are shown by the alpha-blended colored dots surrounding the points. The five most important point groups, indicating topics in the input data set, are shown using image-based shaded cylinders colored by group identity. The bundled graph above the scatterplot highlights the all-pairs false-negative projection errors and is constructed by a mix of geometric and image-based techniques.
