# Defining business process as a service

*Business processes* are the steps you take or the activities you perform to facilitate the delivery of products or services to your customers or stakeholders. These business processes (such as managing email, shipping packages, or writing a marketing document) are delivered as a service when they’re delivered to you in an automated, standardized, and repeatable way from a cloud service provider.
