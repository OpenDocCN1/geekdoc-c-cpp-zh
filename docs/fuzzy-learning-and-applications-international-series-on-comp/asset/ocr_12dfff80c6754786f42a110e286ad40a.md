On average, GEFREX finds lower errors. Moreover, when R increases, the difference becomes more significant.

However, to compare both tools well the evaluation of the time required by both tools to reach the same error is necessary. With this aim, the time required by GEFREX to reach the error that FuGeNeSys reaches, once the number R has been fixed, was estimated. More precisely, the error that FuGeNeSys reaches in 10000 genetic iterations with R ranging from 3 to 9 (see Fig. 1.14) was considered and the mean time required by GEFREX to obtain the same error was calculated.
