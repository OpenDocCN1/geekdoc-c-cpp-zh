Figure 5-1. Bluetooth network topology

There are 37 data channels ranging from 2404 MHz to 2478 MHz.

Once the connection is established, the Central and Peripheral negotiate which of these channels to begin communicating over.

Because the Peripheral can only hold one connection at a time, it must disconnect from the Central before a new connection can be made.
