A low pass filter is a filter that allows the lower frequencies of an image to be retained. Now let us consider a filter which is complementary to this – a filter that will throw away the frequencies passed by the low pass filter and retain the higher frequencies that are thrown away by the low pass filters. Such a filter, as you can probably guess, is called an *high pass filter*. The question is how we can design a high pass filter? One way that probably has come to the mind of most of you is to subtract the low pass filtered image from the original image. And this is a perfect route to take.
