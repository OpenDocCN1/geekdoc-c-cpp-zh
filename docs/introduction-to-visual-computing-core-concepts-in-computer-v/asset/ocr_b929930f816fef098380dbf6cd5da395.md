Figure 8.1. This figure shows two 2D points, A and B, in homogeneous coordinates and how a point P lying in the plane OAB relates to A and B.

Therefore, B × A provides the coefficients of the equation of the plane OAB. In other words, P would satisfy the following equation.

P^T ⎛ yw − tv ⎞
      ⎜ tu − xw ⎟ = P^T (B × A) = 0.   (8.3)
      ⎝ xv − yu ⎠

Now, consider the line M_l on the plane Z = 1 formed by the normalized homogeneous coordinates A' = (x/t, y/t, 1) and B' = (u/w, v/w, 1). Therefore, the slope m and offset c of this line M_l is given by

m = (tv − yw) / (tu − xw)   (8.4)

c = (yu − xv) / (tu − xw).   (8.5)

Therefore, the equation of M_l is given by

(tv − yw)x_l + (xw − tu)y_l + (yu − zw) = 0   (8.6)
