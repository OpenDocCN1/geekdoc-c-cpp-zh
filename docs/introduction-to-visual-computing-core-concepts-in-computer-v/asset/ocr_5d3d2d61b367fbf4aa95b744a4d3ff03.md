Similar techniques can be used to detect other parametric entities like circles and parabolas. Lets us consider briefly one such case. Let us consider the circle whose equation is given by (x − cx)² + (y − cy)² = r² where (cx, cy) is its center and r its radius. Therefore, the Hough space is a 3D space spanned by (cx, cy, r). Therefore, binning and counting of the votes cast and maximas have to be detected in this 3D space to find the circles in the image. Also r = √((x − cx)² + (y − cy)²) is a conic. Therefore, a point in the image space corresponds to a conic in the Hough space.
