Figure 11.15. This figure shows the effect blending of two image via their laplacian pyramid. The left two rows shows the different levels of the Laplacian pyramid blended using different spline functions achieving the effect of a wider blending region of lower resolution levels and narrower blending regions of higher resolution levels. The images are composited to create the image on the right to create each level of the laplacian pyramid of the composited image. These are then composited to create the image of the apple-orange on top right.

11.17, the contribution should switch from the blue image to the red image as we move from left to right. If this switch is done at a point where the pixel to the left coming from the blue image has very similar color to the pixel on the right coming from the red image, then the composition will be seamless. The problem is formulated as a minimal error boundary cut problem. The goal is to find a cut through the overlapping area such that the neighboring pixels at any point has the minimum energy transition across the cut. To learn more about this, please refer to the work from Efros and Freeman [Efros and Freeman 01].
