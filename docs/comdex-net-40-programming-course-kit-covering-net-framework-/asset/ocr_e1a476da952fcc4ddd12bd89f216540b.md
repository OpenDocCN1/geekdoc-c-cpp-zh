As you can see in Fig.VB-5.11, the Design view has several design-related elements, such as the Zoom control to zoom in or out of the Design view and margin lines to set the margins of a control. Let’s briefly discuss the various elements of the Design view.

# Grid Rails, Gridlines, and Gridline Indicators

The grid rails, gridlines, and gridline indicators appear only for the Grid control, which is a WPF control that is automatically added to a WPF application at the time of its creation. The Grid control allows you to represent the WPF application as a grid, which is enclosed by grid rails that span horizontally and vertically on the top and left of the WPF window (Fig.VB-5.11).

By default, the grid has one cell, that is, one row and one column. You can create additional rows and columns in the grid by clicking the desired positions on the grid rails. When you click the grid rails, gridlines and gridline indicators appear at those positions (Fig.VB-5.11). If you click the horizontal grid rail, then a vertical gridline appears dividing the grid into two columns. Similarly, if you click the vertical grid rail, then a horizontal gridline appears dividing the grid into two rows. You can control the height and width of the rows and columns by moving the gridline indicators, which appear as triangles on the grid rails.

# The Zoom Control and the Zoom to Fit Button

You can find the Zoom control and the Zoom to Fit button at the upper-left corner of the Design view (Fig.VB-5.11). The Zoom control allows you to zoom in and zoom out of the Design view. You can drag the Zoom control slider up or down to zoom in or out. The current zoom level displays at the top of the Zoom control, as shown in Fig.VB-5.12.
