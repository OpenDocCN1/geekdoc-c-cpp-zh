# Distinguished Teaching Approach

The book employs a 3-Stage Self-learning System (Book + CD), which not only allows you to master theoretical concepts but also provides you with a practical learning environment in which you can create applications, evaluate your learning, and grasp concepts in the subject matter. The three stages of the book are:

**Explanation Stage:** This is the first stage of your learning, where you are required to read the book to understand all the essential concepts about the subject. Once the basic know-how of the subject is clear, you can switch over to the next stage of learning.

**Audio-Video Demo Stage:** This is the second stage of your learning, where you insert the accompanying CD in your computer and click on the topic you want to learn. The customized software automatically teaches you the concepts step-by-step through audio-video and graphic simulations. You do not have to do anything—just sit back and watch.

**Self-Testing/Practice Stage:** This is the final stage of your learning curve, where the self-testing software allows you to test your skills by practicing what you have learned. You can seek help by reading the instructions; else, it would point out your mistakes, show you the correct steps, and encourage you to try again.

# What is so unique about this book?

+   - **Chapter Preview:** Helps you to gain an insight of the content in each chapter before you actually start reading the chapter.
+   - **Notes:** Conveys additional information, tips, and relevant perspectives.
+   - **Procedures:** Describe the sequence of performing a task in a step-by-step and easy to understand manner, with relevant figures.
+   - **Figures Supplementing the Descriptions:** Help to understand the concepts in a better way.
+   - **Callouts:** Serve as pointers to the location at which an action is being performed in the figure. The callouts mostly correspond to the tasks performed in steps.
+   - **Self-Learning Tutorial CD:** Helps you to learn, practice, experiment, and master Microsoft .NET 4.0 and Visual Studio 2010.
