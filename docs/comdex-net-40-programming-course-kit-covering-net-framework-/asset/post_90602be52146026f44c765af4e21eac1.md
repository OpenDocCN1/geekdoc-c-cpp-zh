Let’s now discuss nested master pages.

## Nested Master Page

As the name implies a nested master page is a master page that is inherited from another master page. The concept of nested master pages is similar to the concept of simple master page. The only difference between simple master page and nested master page is that the nested master page inherits from the other master page while the simple master page does not inherit. The master page, which inherits a master page, is known as child master and from which it inherits is known as parent master. You can better understand the concept of nested master page with the help of Fig.ASP-8.11:

![](img/9862a98a1a2d3bb14334137965a1ec60_644_2.png)

As shown in Fig.ASP-8.11, **Company** is a master page from which two other master pages, Branch 1 and Branch 2, are inherited. This shows that these are nested master pages.

Let’s perform the following steps to create an application, which contains nested master pages.
