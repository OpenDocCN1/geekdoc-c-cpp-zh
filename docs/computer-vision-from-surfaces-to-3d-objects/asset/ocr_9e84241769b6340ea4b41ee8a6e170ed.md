3. Yamabe flow can adapt the connectivity to the target curvature automatically, which makes it valuable for practical purposes. During Yamabe flow, if the algorithm detects a degenerate triangle, where one angle becomes π, then the algorithm swaps the edge against the angle and continues the flow. Unfortunately, this important technique of adapting connectivity to the target curvature during the flow cannot be generalized to the Ricci flow directly.
