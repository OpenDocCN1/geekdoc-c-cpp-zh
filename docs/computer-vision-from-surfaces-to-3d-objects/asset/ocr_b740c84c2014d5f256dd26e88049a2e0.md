Our initial probabilistic boundary edge map is the output of the Berkeley edge detector (Martin et al., 2004). Martin et al. learned the color and texture properties of the boundary pixels from the labeled data (~300 images) and used that information to differentiate the boundary edges from the internal edges. See Figure 2.3b (the edge map of Figure 2.3a) as an example of a typical output of the edge detector. Unlike binary edge detectors like Canny, it successfully removes the spurious texture edges and highlights the boundary edges, but it still has some strong internal edges (BC, CD, CF) that are not the depth boundaries.
