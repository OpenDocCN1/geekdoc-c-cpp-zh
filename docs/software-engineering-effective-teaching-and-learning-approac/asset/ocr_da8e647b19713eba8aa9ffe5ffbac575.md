# Evaluation Results

The learning theory that was most clearly involved in every subject’s learning process was Discovery Learning. All subjects were able to recount at least a few lessons they learned from SimSE, and none of these lessons were ever told to them explicitly during their experience. Rather, they discovered them independently through exploration and experimentation within the game. Interestingly, although all subjects that played a model seemed to discover the same lessons (for the most part), no two subjects discovered them in the same way.
