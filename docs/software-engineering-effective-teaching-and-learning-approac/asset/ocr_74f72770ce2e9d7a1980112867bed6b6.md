evaluation also shows that students usually have difficulties when doing integration tests. They don’t know how to start the integration and how to progressively select new classes to be added to the integration test. Coaching is much needed at this point. Final evaluations also show that students are generally satisfied with their work and consider the methodology experimented to be useful in the long term.

Our current work and intention for the future is to update and enhance the learning environment by incorporating into it the most relevant software testing related trends and techniques among those that are continuously arising in the software testing world. In particular, we are currently working to incorporate into the course testing of database access and new ways to identify test anti-patterns.
