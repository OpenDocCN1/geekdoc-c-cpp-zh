We then constructed the object, character, and fabrication machine models and associated textures (skins) using tools such as Autodesk 3DStudio and Photoshop. Visualizing and simulating materials spills was a priority interest of our sponsors, as training technicians in diagnosing and cleaning up such spills was a critical challenge of interest to our sponsors. Similarly, we recognized that fabrication technicians were attired in full-body suits (bunny suits), and modeling and simulating how they put on such suits (gowning) became a separate follow-on game-based training simulation project with our sponsor. So once we acquired knowledge about semiconductor fabrication operations and diagnostic servicing, we were able to rapidly design and prototype a new game called *FabLab* through a mod of UT2K4.
