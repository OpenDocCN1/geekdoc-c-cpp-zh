# # Colossus

But the German High Command used their own code system: Lorenz, a cipher too complex for even Turing's machines to crack. Something else was needed. Before the war Turing had speculated about building a machine that could solve any kind of mathematical problem. Now Tommy Flowers and Max Newman built just such a machine. It was named Colossus and it used electricity and vacuum tubes to perform

Colossus Mark 1 was finished in December 1943 and could read coded messages at 1,000 characters per second from a paper tape. Mark 2, completed in June 1944, was five times faster.
