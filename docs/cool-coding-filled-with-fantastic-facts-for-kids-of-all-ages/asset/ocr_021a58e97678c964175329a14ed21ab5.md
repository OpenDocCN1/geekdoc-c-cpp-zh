# That's neat!

Supercomputers' speeds are measured in flops, short for floating-point operations per second - essentially how many sums they can do per second. When this book was written the most powerful supercomputer in the world was made of more than 40,000 individual CPUs, each with 256 cores. The computer runs at almost 100 petaflops, meaning it can do nearly a quadrillion (a million billion) calculations a second. And yet by the time you read this there may well be something faster . . .
