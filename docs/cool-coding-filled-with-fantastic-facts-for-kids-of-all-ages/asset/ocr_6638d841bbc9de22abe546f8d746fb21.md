# # Cloudy Weather

The most important trend in computing in recent years has been devices being constantly connected to the internet. Until recently, computer users would only go online when they needed to download something, or look something up. But now, thanks to smartphones and the growth of wireless availability, a lot of computers are online all the time.

> **That’s neat!**
> 
> The internet was born from a research project in the 1970s between American universities, which created a network of computers named ARPANET because ARPANET was designed to survive a nuclear attack: it had no central point of control, which is why even today the internet is decentralized, with no person or country in charge of it. In 1989 a British computer scientist named Tim Berners-Lee created the first web page of the World Wide Web, which is how we use the internet.

# ## What is the cloud?

Devices that are always online can use a new technique: cloud computing. This essentially means that tasks a computer would once handle locally, such as storing files and performing computation, can now be offloaded to computers on the internet. Because it doesn’t matter where in the world these computers are, they’re said to be a “cloud”.
