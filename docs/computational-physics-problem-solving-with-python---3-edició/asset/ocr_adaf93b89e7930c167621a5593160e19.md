A relation of the form (13.9) also occurs in quantum mechanics, where it is known as the Heisenberg uncertainty principle, with Δt and Δω being called the uncertainties in t and ω. It is true for transforms in general and states that as a signal is made more localized in time (smaller Δt) the transform becomes less localized in time (larger Δω). Conversely, the sine wave y(t) = sin ω₀ t is completely localized in frequency, and consequently has an infinite extent in time, Δt ≈ ∞.
