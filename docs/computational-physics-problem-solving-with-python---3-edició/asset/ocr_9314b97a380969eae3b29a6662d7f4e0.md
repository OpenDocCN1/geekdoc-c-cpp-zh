# Theory: Continuity and Advection Equations

The motion of a fluid is described by the continuity equation and the Navier–Stokes equation (Landau and Lifshitz, 1976). We will discuss the former here and the latter in Section 25.2. The continuity equation describes the conservation of mass:

