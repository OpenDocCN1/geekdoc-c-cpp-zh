# LVM with Prey Limit

The initial assumption in the LVM that prey grow without limit in the absence of predators is clearly unrealistic. As with the logistic map, we include a limit on prey numbers that accounts for depletion of the food supply as the prey population grows. Accordingly, we modify the constant growth rate from $a$ to $a(1 - p/K)$ so that growth vanishes when the population reaches a limit $K$, the carrying capacity:

```
\frac{\mathrm{d}p}{\mathrm{d}t} = a p \left(1 - \frac{p}{K}\right) - b p P, \tag{14.46}
\frac{\mathrm{d}P}{\mathrm{d}t} = \epsilon b p P - m P \quad \text{(LVM-II)}. \tag{14.47}
```

The behavior of this model with prey limitations is shown in Figure 14.9. We see that both populations exhibit damped oscillations as they approach their equilibrium values, and that as hoped for, the equilibrium populations are independent
