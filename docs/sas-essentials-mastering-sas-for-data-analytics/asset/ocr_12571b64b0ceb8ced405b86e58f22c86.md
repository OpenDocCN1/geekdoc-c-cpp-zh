The ancient Greek philosopher Socrates had a saying: “Know yourself.” In statistics, it is important to know your data. You need to have knowledge of the distribution and characteristics of your data before you can successfully perform other analyses. If your data are quantitative in nature (e.g., a measure such as height or the volume of liquid in a bottle), the SAS procedures in this chapter can help you glean a lot of information from the numbers.
