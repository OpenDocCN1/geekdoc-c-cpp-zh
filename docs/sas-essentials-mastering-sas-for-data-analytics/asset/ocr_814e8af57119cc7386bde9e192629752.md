# 16.5 GOING DEEPER: ASSESSING A MODEL'S FIT AND PREDICTIVE ABILITY

Once you decide on a final model, you should analyze that model for its predictive ability. One method of analyzing the predictive capabilities of the logistic equation is to use the Hosmer and Lemeshow test. This test is based on dividing subjects into deciles on the basis of predicted probabilities (see Hosmer and Lemeshow, 2000). SAS reports chi-square statistics based on the observed and expected frequencies for subjects within these 10 categories.
