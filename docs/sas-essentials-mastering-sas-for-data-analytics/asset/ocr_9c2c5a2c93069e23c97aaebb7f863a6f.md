4. Examine the ROC curve, shown in Figure 16.2. This curve, which is a plot of 1-Specificity versus Sensitivity, measures the predictive ability of the model. Note that the “area under the curve” (AUC) is 0.9718. When the AUC is close to 1.0, a good fit is indicated. The closer the curve extends to the upper left corner, the closer the AUC is to 1. AUC approaches 0.50 as the curve gets closer to the diagonal line would indicate a poor fit. The AUC statistic is often reported as an indicator of the predictive strength of the model. When you are comparing “final” models, the Hosmer and Lemeshow test and AUC (larger is better) are criteria often used.
