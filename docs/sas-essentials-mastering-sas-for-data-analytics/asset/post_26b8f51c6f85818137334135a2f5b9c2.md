Next, we examine the three factors that are obtained and are described by the Factor Pattern Matrix in Table 17.15.

As was the case for the unrotated solution for the Intelligence Data, it can be seen that Factor 1 has a positive coefficient, all of which are above 0.4 except for RUN500S, which has a coefficient of 0.17. A reasonable interpretation is that Factor 1 measures overall athletic ability, primarily related to the first nine events. Factors 2 and 3 are more difficult to interpret.

Based on the conflicting interpretations associated with the Three-Factor solutions given in Table 17.15, we use a rotation to produce more interpretable results. Using the ROTATE=VARIMAX results in the Rotated Factor Pattern given in Table 17.16. The first rotated factor seems to focus on 100-m long jump, 400-m run, and 110m hurdles that involve the legs (speed and spring). Factor 2 seems to be primarily an arm strength factor with high coefficients for shot put and long jump and to a lesser extent javelin, pole vault, and (surprisingly) high jump. Finally, the only event with a large coefficient in Factor 3 is the 1500-m hurdles. This is consistent with our initial look at the correlation matrix that suggested 1500-m run was “different” from the other events.
