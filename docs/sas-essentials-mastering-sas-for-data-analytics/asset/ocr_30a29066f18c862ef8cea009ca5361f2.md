Reporting Totals and Subtotals: As in PROC PRINT, you may want to display totals at the bottom of a column of numbers. To do this in PROC REPORT, use the BREAK and RBREAK statements. BREAK <BEFORE|AFTER> var/SUMMARIZE <SUPPRESS>;
