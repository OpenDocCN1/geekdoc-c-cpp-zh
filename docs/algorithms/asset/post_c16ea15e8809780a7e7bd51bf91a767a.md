```
$(x_1 \vee x_2) \wedge (x_1 \vee x_3) \wedge (x_1 \vee x_4)$
```

This instance has a satisfying assignment: $x_1, x_2, x_3$, and $x_4$ to true, false, false, and true, respectively.

* $G_I$ has $2n$ nodes, one for each variable and its negation.
* $G_I$ has $2m$ edges: for each clause $(\alpha \vee \beta)$ of $I$ (where $\alpha, \beta$ are literals), $G_I$ has an edge from the negation of $\alpha$ to $\beta$, and from the negation of $\beta$ to $\alpha$.

Note that the clause $(\alpha \vee \beta)$ is equivalent to the implication $\widetilde{\alpha} \Rightarrow \beta$ or $\widetilde{\beta} \Rightarrow \alpha$. In sense, $G_I$ records all literals in $I$.
