Raw cereal costs $1 per pound and raw meat costs $2 per pound. It costs $1.40 to package the Frisky Pup and $0.60 to package the Husky Hound. Needless to say, management would like to maximize profit.

- (b) Graph the feasible region, give the coordinates of every vertex, and circle the vertex maximizing profit. What is the maximum profit possible?
