In other words, $\alpha_A$ measures the factor by which the output of algorithm $A$ exceeds the optimal solution, on the worst-case input. The approximation ratio can also be defined for maximization problems, such as INDEPENDENT set, in the same way—except to get a number larger than 1 we take the reciprocal.

So, when faced with an NP-complete optimization problem, a reasonable goal is to look for an approximation algorithm $A$ whose $\alpha_A$ is as small as possible.
