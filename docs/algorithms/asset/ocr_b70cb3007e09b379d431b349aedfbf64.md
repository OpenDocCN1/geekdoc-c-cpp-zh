Duality theorem: If a linear program has a bounded optimum, then so does its dual, and the two optimum values coincide.

When the primal is the LP that expresses the max-flow problem, it is possible to assign interpretations to the dual variables to show the dual to be none other than the minimum-cut problem (Exercise 7.25). The relation between flows and cuts is just a specific instance of the duality theorem. And in fact, the proof of this theorem falls out of the simplex algorithm, in the same way as the max-flow min-cut theorem fell out of the analysis of the max-flow algorithm.
