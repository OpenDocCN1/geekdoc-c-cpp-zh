The benefits of simulated annealing come at a significant cost: because of the changing temperature and the initial freedom of movement, many more local moves are needed until convergence. Moreover, it is quite an art to choose a good timetable by to decrease the temperature, called an annealing schedule. In many cases where the quality of solutions improves significantly, the tradeoff is worthwhile.
