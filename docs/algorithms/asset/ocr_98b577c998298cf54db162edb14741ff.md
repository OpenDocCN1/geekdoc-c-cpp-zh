2.11. In justifying our matrix multiplication algorithm (Section 2.5), we claimed the following block-wise property: if X and Y are $n \times n$ matrices,

```
X = \begin{bmatrix} A & B \ C & D \end{bmatrix}, Y = \begin{bmatrix} E & F \ G & H \end{bmatrix}
```

where A, B, C, D, E, F, G, and H are $n/2 \times n/2$ submatrices, the product XY can be expressed in these blocks:

```
XY = \begin{bmatrix} A & B \ C & D \end{bmatrix} \begin{bmatrix} E & F \ G & H \end{bmatrix} = \begin{bmatrix} AE+BG & AF+BH \ CE+DG & CF+DH \end{bmatrix}
```

Prove this property.
