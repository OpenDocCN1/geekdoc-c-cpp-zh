More generally, an (s, t)-cut partitions the vertices into two disjoint groups L and R that s is in L and t is in R. Its capacity is the total capacity of the edges from L to R, and as argued previously, is an upper bound on any flow:

Pick any flow f and any (s, t)-cut (L, R). Then size (f) ≤ capacity(L, R).
