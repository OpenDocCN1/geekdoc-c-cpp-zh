# 10.3.4 Exercises

**Exercise 10.3.1.** Prove that the sets \(S_i\), \(T_i\) and \(U_i\) are antichains.

**Exercise 10.3.2.** Prove that if \(\pi : \mathscr{D}_h \to \mathscr{D}_h\) is an elementary embedding, then \(\pi\) is the identity map.

**Exercise 10.3.3** (Simpson [139]). Prove that the first order theory of hyperdegrees is recursively isomorphic to the second order theory of arithmetic.

**Exercise 10.3.4** (Slaman and Woodin [143]). Prove that Theorem 10.3.1 holds for Turing degrees.

**Exercise 10.3.5.** Prove that under the assumption \(V = L\), if \((\mathscr{D}_h, \le, \mathbf{a}) \equiv (\mathscr{D}_h, \le, \mathbf{b})\) then \(\mathbf{a} = \mathbf{b}\).
