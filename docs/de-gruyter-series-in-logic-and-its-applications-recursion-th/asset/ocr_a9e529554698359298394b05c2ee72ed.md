# Define

```
J_0[A] = \emptyset,\nJ_{\beta+1}[A] = \omega^\omega(J_\beta[A]),\nJ_\lambda[A] = \bigcup_{\beta<\lambda} J_\beta[A], \text{ if } \lambda \text{ is a limit ordinal.}\nJ[A] = \bigcup_\beta J_\beta[A]
```

If \( A = \emptyset \), then we write \( J \) for \( J[A] \). Intuitively and according to Jensen [55], \( J_{\beta+1} \) is the result of “stretching” the collection of the definable subsets of \( J_\beta \) upwards by \( \omega \)-levels in rank without adding new sets. The proof of the following proposition may be found in [22].

# Proposition 3.3.3. For all \(\beta\),

+   - \( J_\beta[A] \) is transitive.\n- There is a \(\Sigma_1\)-formula \(\varphi(u, w)\) such that for any two ordinals \( v < \beta \), \( (J_\beta, \in) \models \varphi(v, x) \) if and only if \( x = J_v \);\n- There is a \(\Sigma_1\)-formula \(\varphi_\prec(u, v)\) such that\n  (i) for any \(\beta\), \(\varphi_\prec(u, v)\) defines a well-ordering \( <_J \) on \( J_\beta \);\n  (ii) for any \( v > \beta \), if \( y \in J_\beta \) and \( J_v, \in) \models \varphi_\prec(x, y) \), then \( x \in J_\beta \) and \( (J_\beta, \in) \models \varphi_\prec(x, y) \).\n- Theorem 3.3.2 remains true for \( J_\beta \);\n- \( J_{\beta+1}[A] \cap \mathscr{P}(J_\beta[A]) = \text{Def}^\omega(J_\beta[A]) \).\n- \( L_\beta[A] \subseteq J_\beta[A] \subseteq L_{\omega\beta} \).\n- \( J_\beta[A] = L_\beta[A] \) if and only if \( \alpha\beta = \beta \).\n- \( L[A] = \bigcup_\beta J_\beta[A] \);\n- Theorem 3.3.2 remains valid for \( J \).

The Gödel operators provide an algorithmic approach to the construction of \( L \). It reveals the “black box” hidden within the reference to definability. Based on Proposition 3.3.3, we may think of \( L \) as being obtained by an effective transfine induction over the class of ordinals, analogous to the way hyperarithmetic sets are defined over \( \omega_1^{\text{CK}} \). This connection is useful as it suggests initial segments of \( L \) as models of computation in the general setting.

# 3.3.2 Condensation and Skolemization
