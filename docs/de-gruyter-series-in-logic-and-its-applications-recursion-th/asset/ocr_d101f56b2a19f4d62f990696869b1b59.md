If \(\pi\) is a permutation of \(\omega_1\), let \(\text{spt}(\pi) = \{ \alpha < \omega_1 \mid \pi(\alpha) \neq \alpha \}\). Let \(H_0\) be the set of all permutations \(\pi\) of \(\omega_1\) with \(|\text{spt}(\pi)| < \omega\), and set

\(H = \{ \pi \in H_0 \mid (\forall \alpha < \omega_1)(\pi(\alpha) < \alpha + \omega) \}.\)

For each \(\pi \in H\), let \(\hat{\pi}\) be the permutation of \(L^{\aleph_1}\) induced by \(\pi\). Let \(B\) be the set of \(\mathbf{P}_{\aleph_1}\)-names of reals, and define

\(\hat{A} = \{ (\tau, p) \in B \times \mathbf{P}_{\aleph_1} \mid (\exists \alpha \in E_1)(p \Vdash (\tau =_\mathbb{T} \tau_\alpha)) \}.\)

**Lemma 9.3.14.** If \(\pi \in H\) and \(y \in E_1\) then \(\vdash_\mathbb{T} \tau_y = \hat{\pi}(\tau_y)\).

**Proof.** Note that the canonical name \(\dot{y}\) of \(y\) is invariant under \(\pi\), and \(R_\gamma\) is definable in \(y\) as \(L\) is the minimal inner model. Then for any \(\mathbf{P}_{\aleph_1}\)-generic \(G\), \(\tau_y^G\) and \(\hat{\pi}(\tau_y)^G\) differ at most finitely many columns. The lemma follows immediately.

**Corollary 9.3.15.** If \(\pi \in H\), then \(\emptyset \Vdash \hat{A} = \hat{\pi}(\hat{A})\).

Hence the permutations in \(H\) are sufficient to show

\(N \Vdash \text{``There is no well-ordering of } 2^\omega\text{''}.\)

By Proposition 9.3.7, \(N \Vdash \neg \text{CH}\) and this completes the proof of Theorem 9.3.8.

# 9.4 \(\omega\)-homogeneity of the Turing degrees

**Definition 9.4.1.** \(\langle \mathscr{D}, \le \rangle\) is \(\omega\)-homogeneous if for any two \(n+1\)-tuples \((\mathbf{a}_0, \dots, \mathbf{a}_n)\) and \((\mathbf{b}_0, \dots, \mathbf{b}_n)\) in \(\mathscr{D}\), if \(\langle \mathscr{D}, \le, \mathbf{a}_0, \dots, \mathbf{a}_n \rangle \equiv \langle \mathscr{D}, \le, \mathbf{b}_0, \dots, \mathbf{b}_n \rangle\) then there is an automorphism \(\pi : \mathscr{D} \to \mathscr{D}\) such that \(\pi(\mathbf{a}_i) = \pi(\mathbf{b}_i)\) for each \(i \le n\).

Slaman and Woodin [144] showed:

**Theorem 9.4.2.** The \(\omega\)-homogeneity of \(\langle \mathscr{D}, \le \rangle\) is independent of ZFC.
