Proof. If \(\omega_1^\alpha = \omega_1^{CK}\), then by Corollary 5.1.4, for any real \(z, z \in \mathfrak{A}(\omega_1^{CK}, x)\) if and only if \(z \le_h x\). Now suppose that \(\mathfrak{A}(\omega_1^{CK}, x) \vDash (\forall n)((\exists z)\varphi(n, z) \leftrightarrow (\forall z)\psi(n, z))\). Then for each \(n\), let \(f(n)\) be the \(<_{\mathcal{O}^*}\)-least \(m \in \mathcal{O}_1^*\) such that \(\mathfrak{A}(\omega_1^{CK}, x) \vDash (\exists z^{[m]})(\varphi(n, z^{[m]})) \lor \neg\psi(n, z^{[m]}))\). \(f\) is a total \(\Pi_1^1\), and hence \(\Delta_1^1\), function. It follows that there is an \(n_0 \in \omega_1\) such that \(f(n) < n_0\) for all \(n\). Let \(y\) be a real such that \(n \in y\) if and only if \(\mathfrak{A}(\omega_1^{CK}, x) \vDash \exists z^{[m_0]}(\varphi(n, z^{[n_0]}))\). Then \(y \in \mathfrak{A}(\omega_1^{CK}, x)\) and

\(\mathfrak{A}(\omega_1^{CK}, x) \vDash n \in y \leftrightarrow (\exists z)\varphi(n, z)\).

Suppose that \(\mathfrak{A}(\omega_1^{CK}, x)\) satisfies the \(\Delta_1^1\)-comprehension axiom. For the sake of contradiction, assume that \(\omega_1^\alpha > \omega_1^{CK}\). Let \(n \in \mathcal{O}^*\) be such that \(n = 3 \cdot 5^e\) and \(|n|_k = \omega_1^{CK}\). Note that \(H_m^\alpha \in \mathfrak{A}(\omega_1^{CK}, x)\) for \(m <_{\mathcal{O}^*} n\). Let \(\varphi(n, z)\) be

\((\exists m)(\exists i)[\hat{H}(\hat{\varphi}_{e(m)}^x(m), z, x) \wedge n = \langle m, i \rangle \wedge i \in z]\).

If \(m <_{\mathcal{O}^*} n\), then \(\hat{H}_{\varphi^{[m]}}^x\) is the unique real \(z\) such that \(\hat{H}(\hat{\varphi}_{e(m)}^x(m), z, x)\). Thus

\(\mathfrak{A}(\omega_1^{CK}, x) \vDash (\forall n)((\exists z)\varphi(n, z) \leftrightarrow (\forall z)((\exists i)(\langle m, i \rangle = n \wedge i \in z) \to \neg\varphi(n, z))\).

Then by \(\Delta_1^1\)-comprehension, there is a \(y \in \mathfrak{A}(\omega_1^{CK}, x)\) such that

\(\mathfrak{A}(\omega_1^{CK}, x) \vDash (\forall n)(n \in y \leftrightarrow (\exists z)\varphi(n, z))\).

Since \(\hat{H}_{\varphi^{[m]}}^x \in \mathfrak{A}(\omega_1^{CK}, x)\) for \(m <_{\mathcal{O}^*} n\), we have \(y = \hat{H}_n^x\), contradicting Proposition 5.1.3.

**Remark 5.1.16.** The results above may be generalized. For example, one may introduce the language \(\mathscr{L}(\omega_1^x, x, z)\), define the structure \(\mathfrak{A}(\omega_1^x, x, z)\), and show that all the results above hold. Then \(\{(x, \ulcorner p \urcorner, z) \mid p \in \Sigma_1^x \wedge \mathfrak{A}(\omega_1^x, x, z) \vDash p\} \Pi_1^1\). Moreover, \(\mathfrak{A}(\omega_1^x, z)\) satisfies \(\Delta_1^1\)-comprehension if and only if \(\mathfrak{A}(\omega_1^x, x, z)\) is precisely the collection of reals hyperarithmetic in \(x \oplus z\), and if and only if \(\omega_1^{x \oplus x} = \omega_1^x\).
