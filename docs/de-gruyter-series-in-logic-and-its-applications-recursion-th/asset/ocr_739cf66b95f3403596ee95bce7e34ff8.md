Let \( f : \omega \to \omega^3 \) be a recursive bijection such that if \( f(n) = (n_0, n_1, n_2) \) and \( n > 0 \), then \( n > n_0 \). The construction of the sequences proceeds as follows:

Stage 0. Let \(\langle p_0, q_0 \rangle = \langle p_0, q_0 \rangle\) and \( v_0 = 0 \). Let \(\{\gamma_i\}_{i < \omega}\) be an enumeration of the countable set

\(\{ \alpha < \omega_2 \mid p_0(\alpha) \text{ is defined } \lor p_0 \Vdash \text{``}q_0(\alpha) \text{ is defined''} \}\).

Let \(\beta_{0(i,j)} = \gamma_i\) for \( i, j < \omega \). Each \(\gamma_i\) occurs infinitely many times on the list to ensure that it is diagonalized infinitely often.

Stage \( n + 1 \). We decompose this into \( 2^n + 1 \) many substages.

At substage \( j \le 2^n \), define \(\{v'_\sigma\}_{\sigma \in 2^{n+1}}\) and \(\{(p'_\sigma, q'_\sigma)\}_{\sigma \in 2^{n+1}}\) such that \( p'_{\sigma^\frown 0}(\alpha) = p'_{\sigma^\frown 1}(\alpha) \) for each \( \alpha \notin \beta[n] \) and \( \sigma \in 2^n \).

For \( \sigma \in 2^n \) and \( i < 2 \), let \( v'_{\sigma^\frown i} = v'_\sigma \). If \( \beta[n] < \omega_1 \), then

\( p^0_{\sigma^\frown i}(\alpha) = \begin{cases} p_\sigma(\alpha) & : \alpha \notin \beta[n], \\ \tau \in p_\sigma(\alpha) \mid \tau < t_{p_\sigma(\alpha)}(i) \lor \tau \succeq t_{p_\sigma(\alpha)}(i) & : \text{Otherwise.} \end{cases} \)

Let \( q^0_{\sigma^\frown i} = q_\sigma \). If \( \beta[n] \ge \omega_1 \), then \( p^0_{\sigma^\frown i} = p_\sigma \) and \( q^0_{\sigma^\frown i} \) is a name such that

\( p_\sigma \Vdash q^0_{\sigma^\frown i}(\alpha) = \begin{cases} \check{q}_\sigma(\alpha) & : \alpha \notin \beta[n], \\ \tau \in p_\sigma \land \tau \in \check{q}_\sigma(\alpha) \land (\tau < t_{q_\sigma(\alpha)}(i) \lor \tau \succeq t_{q_\sigma(\alpha)}(i)) & : \text{Otherwise.} \end{cases} \)

Hence \(\{p^0_\sigma\}_{\sigma \in 2^{n+1}}\) satisfies (2). There are now two cases to consider.

Case 1. \( \beta[n] \ge \omega_1 \). Since (4) only contains ordinals not less than \( \omega_1 \), let \(\langle p_{\sigma^\frown i}, \check{q}_{\sigma^\frown i} \rangle = \langle p^0_{\sigma^\frown i}, q^0_{\sigma^\frown i} \rangle\), \( \beta[n+1, i, j] = \beta[n, i, j] \), and \( v_{\sigma^\frown i} = v_\sigma \) for every \( \sigma \in 2^n \) and \( i < 2 \), and go to the next stage.

Case 2. \( \beta[n] < \omega_1 \). Fix an enumeration \(\{\sigma_j\}_{1 \le j \le 2^n}\) of \( 2^n \).

Substage \( j + 1 \). By Lemma 9.1.18, there exist an \( m > |v'_\sigma| \) and a \(\langle p'', q'' \rangle \le \langle p^j_{\sigma^\frown 0}, q^j_{\sigma^\frown 0} \rangle\) such that for any \(\langle p'', q'' \rangle \le \langle p', q' \rangle\), we have \(\langle p'', q'' \rangle \Vdash x(m) = i\). Define

\( p''(\alpha) = \begin{cases} p'(\alpha) & : \alpha \notin \beta[n], \\ p^j_{\sigma^\frown 1}(\alpha) & : \text{Otherwise.} \end{cases} \)

Note that \( p^j_{\sigma^\frown 0}(\alpha) = p^j_{\sigma^\frown 1}(\alpha) \) for any \( \alpha \notin \beta[n] \). We have \(\langle p'', q''_{\sigma^\frown 1} \rangle \le \langle p^j_{\sigma^\frown 1}, q^j_{\sigma^\frown 1} \rangle\). Let \(\langle r_1, s_1 \rangle \le \langle p'', q''_{\sigma^\frown 1} \rangle\) so that for some \( v^{j+1}_1 > v^j_1 \) in \( 2^{m+1} \), \(\langle r_1, s_1 \rangle \Vdash x(m) = 1\) and \(\langle r_1, s_1 \rangle \Vdash x \restriction m + 1 = q^{j+1}_{\sigma^\frown 1}\). Define

\( r'_0(\alpha) = \begin{cases} r_1(\alpha) & : \alpha \notin \beta[n], \\ p'(\alpha) & : \text{Otherwise.} \end{cases} \)

Then \( r'_0 \le p' \) and so \(\langle r'_0, q'' \rangle \Vdash x(n) = i\) for \( i < 2 \). Hence there is a \(\langle r_0, s_0 \rangle \le \langle r'_0, q' \rangle\) such that \(\langle r_0, s_0 \rangle \Vdash x(m) = 0\) and \(\langle r_0, s_0 \rangle \Vdash x \restriction m + 1 = v^j_{\sigma^\frown 0}\) for some \( v^j_{\sigma^\frown 0} > v^j_\sigma \) in \( 2^{m+1} \). Let \(\langle p^{j+1}_{\sigma^\frown 0}, q^{j+1}_{\sigma^\frown 0} \rangle = \langle r_0, \check{s}_0 \rangle\). Define

\( p^{j+1}_{\sigma^\frown 1}(\alpha) = \begin{cases} r_0(\alpha) & : \alpha \notin \beta[n], \\ r_1(\alpha) & : \text{Otherwise.} \end{cases} \)
