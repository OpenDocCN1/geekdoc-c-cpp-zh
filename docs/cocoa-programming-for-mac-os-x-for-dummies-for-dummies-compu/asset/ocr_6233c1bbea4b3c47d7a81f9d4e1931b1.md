As Xcode builds your application, the debugger window appears with lots of text flying by at a rapid rate. This process is normal. If anything goes wrong during the build (such as an error in your code), the debugger is the first place you'll want to look for clues as to what exactly did wrong. When the build is finished, the debugger tells you so (see Figure 2-20).

After your build succeeds, Xcode launches the completed application, as shown in Figure 2-21. Your next step in the programming process is to test the functionality of the application. Enter numbers in the first two text fields and then click the Calculate. Does it display the answer you expect?
