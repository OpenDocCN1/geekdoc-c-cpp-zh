> Do you speak the language?

Cocoa (like most kinds of programming) requires the use of a programming language. To create Objective-C, Python, Ruby, Java, or Script. This book uses Objective-C because it's the 'native language' of Cocoa. Objective-C is a superset of the traditional C programming language. If you have experience with C, you're well on your way to understanding Objective-C. All the C functions you know and love are available to

you in Cocoa. Objective-C, however, goes one step further and enhances C by adding oriented features to the language.
Objective-C is a syntax that may look a little foreign to you at first, unless you're familiar with Smalltalk. But after you get the hang of it, you'll find that it isn't hard to understand at all. Chapter 6 goes into the details of Objective-C, but you start using it in Chapter 2 to build your Cocoa project.
