Band structure calculations take the self-consistent charge density as an input and then compute the energy eigenvalues on a larger set of k-points, generally arranged in high symmetry paths. AIMD consists of a series of self-consistent calculations interspersed with the classical motion of the ions. Structural relaxation consists of a series of self-consistent calculations interspersed with small perturbations of the ionic structure.
