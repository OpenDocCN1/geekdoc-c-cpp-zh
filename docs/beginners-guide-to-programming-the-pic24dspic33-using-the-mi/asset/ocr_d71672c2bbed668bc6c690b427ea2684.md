PIC24H/PIC24J/dsPIC33 Microcontroller with full on board support and peripheral capabilities. These capabilities support an extensive prototyping circuit environment without the need to rebuild any electronics. Like the Microstick II the Experimenter plugs directly into a Solderless breadboard. The Experimenter is socketed allowing it to easily support a number of Microchip 16 bit SDIP parts like the PIC24FJ128GP502 (which is included with the Microstick II). The Experimenter comes with a PIC24FJGA002.

A photo of the 16 bit Experimenter situated in a Solderless Breadboard is shown. The Experimenter on-board support has 16x3 LCD. 4 user pushbuttons, on-board power regulation (with power on LED indicator and power on/off switch), and a 32KB EEPROM, power on reset button, ICSP (In Circuit Serial Programmer and Debugger Interface), and finally an I/O Expansion Connector.
