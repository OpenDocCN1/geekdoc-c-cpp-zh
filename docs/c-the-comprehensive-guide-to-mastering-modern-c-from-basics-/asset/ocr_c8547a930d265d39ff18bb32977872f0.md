The even larger value of 1 trillion also causes the int to overflow with prints(bill,...). The conversion to a float does avoid overflow, but the float does not have enough precision to store the exact value.

No integer type, such as short and int, can hold a decimal number like 3.75f. When converting in prints(three,...), the decimal places are simply truncated. However, a float can be converted to a double without loss. The other way around, information could be lost.
