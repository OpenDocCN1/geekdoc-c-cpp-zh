You have also seen that + as an operator for C++ is actually called operator+, and that is its identifier. The angle brackets for function and class templates are also always written together later, resulting in the entire identifier, such as vector<int> or numeric_limits<int>.

You don't need to memorize the exact language definition here. What is important is that you get a feel for what constitutes an identifier through practice.
