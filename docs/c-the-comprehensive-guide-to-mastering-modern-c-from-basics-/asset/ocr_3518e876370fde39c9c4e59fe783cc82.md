24.6.5 **`deque`**nThe double-ended queue is useful to the vector. It forgets the guarantee of placing elements consecutively, and thus it offers not data, capacity, nor reserve. However, to the vector, it can efficiently add and remove elements from the front in O(1) time: using push_front, replace_front, pop_front, and in C++23 also append_range and prepend_range.

One of the most obvious things you can do with a deque—and the reason for its name—is to add things while removing them at the other end. This is the first, first out (FIFO) principle.
