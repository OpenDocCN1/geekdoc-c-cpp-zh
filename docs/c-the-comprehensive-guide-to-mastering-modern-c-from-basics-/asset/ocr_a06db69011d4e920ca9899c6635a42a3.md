In addition to the static_cast shown here, there are also reinterpret_cast, const_cast, and dynamic_cast, which I will not cover further here as they are an advanced topic. These are all the built-in cast cast variants. The standard library adds some things with “cast” in their name, like any_cast and duration_cast, but I call these explicit type conversions to distinguish them from the built-in casts.
