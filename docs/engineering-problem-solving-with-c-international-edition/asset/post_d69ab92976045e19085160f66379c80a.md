### Noise Signals

In engineering simulations, we often want to generate a floating-point sequence of values with a specified mean and variance. The function developed in Chapter 4 allows us to generate numbers between limits a and b, but it does not allow us to specify the mean and variance. By using results from probability, we can derive the relationship between the limits of a uniform random sequence and its theoretical mean \(\mu\) and variance \(\sigma^2\):

\[
\sigma^2 = \frac{(b-a)^2}{12}, \quad \mu = \frac{a+b}{2}.
\]
