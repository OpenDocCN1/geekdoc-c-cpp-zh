# Building term structure trees using the Ho and Lee (1986) approach

# 25.1 Intro

In this section we build interest rate trees following the orginal paper of Ho and Lee (1986). We will follow the analysis in the paper, and use it to illustrate how yu can build trees of more complex term structures than the simple binomial trees of the interest rate. The selling point of the original paper was that one could fit an initial term structure and then specify an evolution of the term structure consistent with this initial term structure.
