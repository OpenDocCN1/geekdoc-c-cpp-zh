+   - **Scalability:** Choose a pattern that scales well with your application's growth. If you anticipate your application's data requirements will increase over time, ensure the chosen pattern can handle larger data volumes efficiently.
+   - **Complexity of Operations:** Consider the complexity of the operations your application needs to perform. If you're working with complex data relationships and need to perform complex queries, the ORM pattern might be the best choice.
+   - **Consistency and Maintainability:** Choose a pattern that aligns with your application's design and architecture. Ensure it is easy to maintain and doesn't introduce unnecessary complexity.

Choosing the right data access pattern is crucial for the success of your application.
